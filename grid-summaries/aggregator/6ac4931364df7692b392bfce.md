## Palestinian Education Ministry Reports Over 800 Teachers Killed Since Gaza War Began

The Palestinian Education Ministry says more than 800 teachers have been killed and over 3,300 injured since Israel's war on Gaza began in October 2023. It issued the statement ahead of World Teachers' Day.

### Casualties and Detentions

The ministry describes what it calls "comprehensive genocide" affecting the education sector and says educators have been systematically targeted. It reports that more than 170 teachers have been detained. It also cites restrictions on movement that make the work of teachers harder.

### Financial Strain and Timing

The ministry points to severe financial hardship among teachers, which it links to Israel withholding Palestinian tax revenues. It issued the statement around World Teachers' Day, observed on October 5, a date that draws global attention to the situation of educators. Palestinians view the pressures on the sector as an existential threat to their education system, and the ministry's figures are intended to focus international attention on that crisis.
