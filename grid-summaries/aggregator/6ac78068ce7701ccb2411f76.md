## FAA Steps Deeper Into How Airlines Schedule Flights at Crowded Airports

The Federal Aviation Administration is taking a larger role in airline scheduling at congested airports. Takeoff and landing rights during peak hours are highly valuable commercially, which puts the agency's growing involvement under close scrutiny.

### Safety and Competition Intertwined

The article presents slot allocation as two questions at once. It is a matter of managing safety and traffic at busy airports, but it is also a business issue that affects how airlines compete with one another. Decisions about who gets access to the most sought-after time slots can carry significant financial consequences for carriers.

### Why the Shift Matters Now

Air travel demand is straining crowded airports, and control over schedules is becoming a stronger tool for cutting delays and managing capacity. Any change in the FAA's authority could reshape airline competition and pricing, and it could also affect how easily passengers reach key routes.
