## Expert Witness Services Move Online as Legal Tech Widens Its Reach

A lesser-noticed corner of legal technology is getting a digital makeover. Companies such as Expert Institute, IMS, and GLG are modernizing the way lawyers find, vet, and manage specialists for litigation, and the piece argues this niche is bigger and busier than many people realize.

### What is changing

Expert witness services help attorneys locate qualified specialists, check their credentials, and handle the work that follows once they are hired. By digitizing these tasks, the firms named are turning a quiet corner of the legal industry into an active technology segment. The article suggests the field has been overlooked despite its scale and activity.

### Why it matters now

Legal tech investment is spreading beyond document review and AI drafting into operational workflows that law firms still handle by hand. As litigation becomes more complex, quicker expert discovery and smoother management may offer a significant competitive advantage to the firms that adopt these tools.
