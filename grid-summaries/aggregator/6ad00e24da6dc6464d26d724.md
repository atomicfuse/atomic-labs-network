## Diesel Relief Welcome, but Triumph CEO Sees Little Near-Term Impact

Triumph CEO Jordan Graft has weighed in on the newly announced diesel relief, calling it a significant development for the trucking and freight sectors while cautioning that it is unlikely to bring immediate change.

### What the CEO Said

Graft described the announcement as meaningful for trucking and freight businesses, recognizing that fuel is a central concern for companies in those industries. At the same time, he argued that the relief is unlikely to materially lower their costs in the short term. He also does not expect it to change how those businesses operate in the near future, suggesting that any benefits will take time to be felt.

### Why Diesel Still Matters

Diesel prices remain a major pressure point across transportation and logistics, and they continue to influence consumer prices as well. That keeps fuel policy at the center of ongoing debates about inflation and supply chains. Graft's comments highlight the gap between the announcement itself and any practical effect for businesses dealing with fuel costs today.
