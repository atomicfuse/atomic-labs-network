## Senior Travelers May Be Leaving Money on the Table

A new article highlights a range of travel perks available to older adults that major booking sites frequently fail to mention. With trip costs still elevated, the overlooked savings could make a real difference.

### Perks Worth Knowing About

The piece covers 13 benefits, including park passes, rail and bus discounts, TSA accommodations, reduced transit fares, and savings tied to memberships. Many of these deals never show up in standard search results on popular booking platforms, so travelers may not realize they qualify.

### Why Timing Matters

Several discounts require booking directly, asking in person, or meeting specific age cutoffs, which makes them easy to miss. The article notes that pricing changes in 2026 are making some senior passes even more valuable, adding fresh urgency for older travelers looking to cut expenses on upcoming trips.
