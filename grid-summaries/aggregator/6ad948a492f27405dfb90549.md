## Japan Pledges $21 Billion for Two New Chip Factories on US Soil

Japan is putting $21 billion toward building two semiconductor plants in the United States. The commitment points to a significant effort to increase chipmaking capacity and make supply chains more secure.

### What the investment involves

The money is earmarked for two separate plants, both to be located in the U.S. The scale of the pledge marks a major push to expand manufacturing capacity for semiconductors, which remain essential to a wide range of products and industries.

### Why the timing matters

Chips continue to underpin artificial intelligence, defense systems, automobiles, and consumer technology. At the same time, the U.S. and its allies are racing to cut their reliance on supply chains linked to China. The investment also fits a broader trend of reshoring, driven by industrial policy and geopolitical pressure that are encouraging production to move closer to home markets.
