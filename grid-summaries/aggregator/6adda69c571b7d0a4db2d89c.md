## Storms Force Cruise Ships to Change Course From the Bahamas to Europe

Severe weather is disrupting cruise schedules in several regions, among them the Bahamas and Europe. Ships are having to adjust their plans as conditions worsen.

### What Is Happening

Royal Caribbean's Harmony of the Seas and Independence of the Seas are among the vessels affected. Because of the rough conditions, ships have had to change the ports they visit or return early instead of completing their planned itineraries. The disruption is not limited to one area, with problems reported across multiple regions.

### Why Travelers Should Pay Attention

Storm systems are intensifying during peak travel periods, which means cruisers are facing more last-minute itinerary changes and greater uncertainty. That makes the situation especially relevant for people planning fall and winter sailings. Understanding weather-related risks before booking or departing can help passengers set realistic expectations for how their trip might unfold.
