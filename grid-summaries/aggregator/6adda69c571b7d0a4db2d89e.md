## Royal Caribbean Corrects Tip Typo and Dismisses Fake Gratuity Letter

Royal Caribbean has cleared up confusion about its gratuity policy after an error on its FAQ page and a bogus letter spread across social media.

### An FAQ Typo Raises the Rate

The cruise line fixed a typo on its FAQ page that wrongly showed gratuities at 20 percent. The real figure is 18 percent. The mistake prompted some travelers to wonder whether the company had quietly raised its tipping charges, but the correction restores the accurate rate and confirms nothing has changed.

### A Fake Letter Stirs Worry

A forged letter has also been circulating online, claiming tips would become mandatory and could not be removed from bills. The article debunks that claim. Because gratuities are a sensitive subject for cruisers, such misinformation can travel fast through forums and social feeds. The clarification matters now because it helps travelers avoid confusion as they book trips and plan their onboard spending.
