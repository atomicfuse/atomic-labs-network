## Travel Agent Academy Offers Cash Chance With Puerto Vallarta Course

Travel Agent Academy is promoting its Puerto Vallarta Specialist certification, and agents who finish the training before a set deadline can enter a prize draw worth up to $500.

### What the training covers

The certification focuses on building destination knowledge for Puerto Vallarta. Topics include the city's neighborhoods and beaches, as well as weddings and general travel planning. Agents who complete the course by Nov. 15 become eligible to enter for the chance to win up to $500.

### Why the timing matters

Puerto Vallarta continues to be a high-demand Mexico destination for leisure, romance, and group travel. That makes deeper destination expertise a timely advantage when selling trips there. The limited-time cash incentive also gives agents a reason to complete the training quickly rather than putting it off, since the opportunity ends on the November deadline.
