## Kew Scientists Settle 245-Year Mushroom Mix-Up

Researchers at Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew have resolved a long-running taxonomic confusion involving two fungi. The study separates Ganoderma lucidum, a European species, from Ganoderma lingzhi, the East Asian medicinal mushroom known as lingzhi or reishi.

### A Case of Mistaken Identity

For 245 years, the two species were muddled together. The Kew team argues that this misidentification has distorted research, product labeling, and quality control in a major market for traditional medicine and supplements. Clarifying which fungus is which gives researchers and producers a firmer foundation to work from.

### Why Accuracy Matters Now

Reishi and other mushroom supplements are booming in Western wellness markets, and the traditional Chinese medicine industry is worth hundreds of billions of pounds. Against that backdrop, correct species identification is increasingly urgent. It bears on consumer safety, the credibility of pharmaceutical research, and the authenticity of the products people buy.
