## Eight Haunted U.S. Hikes Where Scenic Trails Meet Ghost Stories

A new roundup highlights spooky hikes across the United States, pairing scenic routes with eerie backstories. Along the way, hikers encounter cemeteries, abandoned settlements, wartime ruins, and local legends.

### History and Atmosphere Over Belief

The piece argues that haunted hikes are about more than paranormal belief. Their pull comes just as much from history and mood, with each trail offering a look at the past through places that feel quiet, worn, and full of stories. Visitors do not need to believe in ghosts to appreciate what these sites reveal.

### Why Haunted Travel Is Rising Now

Haunted travel tends to spike around spooky season, and outdoor audiences are seeking experiences that feel immersive, local, and story-driven. These trails also reflect a broader trend of blending adventure travel with folklore, dark history, and the curiosity of wondering whether a legend might be real.
