## Travelers Warm to Biometric Checks at Airports, IATA Survey Shows

A new survey from IATA suggests that most air passengers are comfortable with biometric technology in airports. The finding points to growing acceptance of facial recognition and similar identity tools in the travel process.

### What the Survey Found

According to the survey, 87% of travelers are satisfied with using biometrics at airports. That high figure indicates rising comfort with systems that verify identity using physical traits such as faces. The article also notes that some worries remain, particularly around how personal data is secured and how privacy is protected.

### Why It Matters Now

Airports and airlines are speeding up their rollout of digital identity systems, aiming to cut friction, shorten queues, and modernize how passengers are processed. As these tools spread to more locations, convenience alone may not be enough. Earning and keeping passenger trust on privacy is becoming just as important as the speed and ease the technology promises.
