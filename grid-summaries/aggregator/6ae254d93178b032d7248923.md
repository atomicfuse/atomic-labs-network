## Internova Plans Airline Meetups to Boost Advisor Air Sales

Internova Travel Group is preparing a series of events built around its travel advisors and the airline industry. The gatherings will take place in New York City and Beverly Hills, running from late 2026 into early 2027.

### What the events offer

More than 25 airlines are set to connect with Internova advisors at the events. The company's aims are to deepen relationships with suppliers, give advisors insights straight from the airlines, and ultimately help those advisors grow their air sales.

### Why the timing matters

Air travel continues to be a major source of revenue and also a frequent source of frustration for clients. That puts pressure on advisors to have strong airline access and current product knowledge if they want to remain competitive. By hosting these events, Internova signals ongoing investment in advisor and supplier networking as a way to drive sales growth.
