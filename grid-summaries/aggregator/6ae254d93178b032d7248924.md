## Princess Cruises Reveals 2028-29 Australia and New Zealand Lineup and 2029 World Cruise

Princess Cruises has released its plans for the 2028-29 Australia and New Zealand season, along with a long-distance voyage set for 2029. The announcement gives travellers an early look at what the cruise line will offer over the coming years.

### The Season and the World Cruise

The Australia and New Zealand program includes more than 90 departures spread across three ships. Alongside it, the line has unveiled a 129-night World Cruise for 2029. That voyage is scheduled to call at 61 destinations, giving guests a lengthy journey for those seeking a once-in-a-lifetime trip.

### Why the Timing Matters

Planning for long-haul and bucket-list travel is starting earlier, which gives cruisers more time to secure major 2028-29 itineraries. Booking incentives begin on November 10, offering an additional reason for travellers to lock in their plans well ahead of departure.
