## UK updates travel advice for 16 Middle East destinations

The UK Foreign Office has refreshed its guidance for 16 Middle Eastern and nearby destinations, among them Turkey, Egypt, the UAE and Oman. The update comes as regional tensions rise over possible US action against Iran.

### What the advice says

Many tourist destinations covered by the guidance remain open for travel. However, the update points to continuing risks, including disruption to journeys, closures of airspace and the chance of a sudden escalation. Travellers are being reminded that conditions in the region could change quickly.

### Why it matters now

Millions of UK travellers visit places such as Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Turkey and Egypt, so fresh military warnings could affect holidays, flight routes, insurance and safety planning. The timing is notable because geopolitical tensions are climbing just as the popular winter-sun travel season approaches, leaving many people weighing up their plans.
