## Camino Partners Backs Intake Breathing With $62 Million Investment

Camino Partners has put $62 million into Intake Breathing, the company behind a magnetic nasal dilator designed to improve airflow and support easier breathing. The deal is presented as a significant wager on consumer health and breathing-performance products.

### What the Deal Involves

The investment centers on Intake Breathing's magnetic nasal dilator technology, which aims to help people breathe more freely. By committing $62 million, Camino Partners is making a sizable bet that products built around better airflow can find a strong place in the consumer health market.

### Why the Timing Matters

Interest keeps rising in sleep quality, athletic recovery, nasal breathing, and non-invasive wellness tools across health and fitness markets. Against that backdrop, a large investment like this one signals that breathing technology may be shifting from a niche product into a more mainstream consumer category.
