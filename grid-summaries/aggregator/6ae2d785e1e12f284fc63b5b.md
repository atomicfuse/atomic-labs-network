## HeartBot Tests Whether a Chatbot Can Sharpen Women's Heart Health Awareness

A chatbot developed within the University of California system is aiming to help women better understand heart disease. HeartBot covers personal risk, the signs of a heart attack, and the point at which emergency care is needed.

### How HeartBot Works

The tool offers personalized education written in plain language rather than medical jargon. Researchers are now testing whether this approach can raise awareness and help people remember what they learn. The goal is practical: women who can recognize symptoms and know when to seek urgent help may be better prepared to act.

### Why the Timing Matters

Heart disease is the leading cause of death for women, yet awareness has fallen sharply. The decline is especially notable among Black, Hispanic, and younger women. As AI health tools become more common, HeartBot offers a timely example of chatbots serving public health, with efforts to limit the risk of misinformation built into the work.
