## Report imagines hopeful climate futures for UK coastal communities

A new report from the University of York and WWF looks at how communities in Pembrokeshire, Norfolk and the Firth of Forth picture hopeful, climate-resilient futures for their own areas.

### What the report proposes

The authors argue that a "Good Anthropocene" is possible when innovation and tradition are combined with restoration and real community agency. Rather than treating local people as bystanders, the report places them at the centre of imagining and shaping what comes next in each of the three places.

### Why it arrives now

Climate coverage is frequently dominated by stories of crisis, while interest is growing in solutions journalism, local resilience and so-called positive tipping points. The report taps into that shift. It also lands as communities face real pressures from sea-level rise, biodiversity loss and the transition in energy.
