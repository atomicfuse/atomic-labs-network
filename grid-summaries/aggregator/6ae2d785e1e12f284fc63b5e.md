## New Guidelines Set Out 37 Recommendations for Treating Hidradenitis Suppurativa

The American Academy of Dermatology and the Hidradenitis Suppurativa Foundation have released evidence-based guidelines for managing hidradenitis suppurativa, a chronic skin disease. The document contains 37 recommendations for clinicians.

### What the guidelines recommend

Among the strong recommendations are several biologic drugs: adalimumab, bimekizumab, infliximab and secukinumab. The guidelines also strongly recommend that clinicians routinely assess pain in people with the condition, reflecting how painful the disease can be.

### Why the timing matters

Hidradenitis suppurativa is often underrecognized, and standardized guidance can influence treatment access, insurance coverage and what patients expect from care. The recommendations come as newer therapies that target the immune system widen the available options beyond antibiotics and surgery.
