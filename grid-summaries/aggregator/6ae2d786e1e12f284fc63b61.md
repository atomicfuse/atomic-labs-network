## Britain's Prospero Satellite Reached Orbit on a Home-Built Rocket

On Oct. 28, 1971, Britain launched Prospero, its first satellite, on a Black Arrow rocket. It remains the only British satellite sent to space on a UK-built launcher.

### The Mission and Its Setback

Prospero was designed with scientific goals in mind, and the launch gave the country a place in the history of spaceflight. After the satellite separated from the rocket, however, a collision damaged its antenna. The mishap is part of the mission's story, alongside its status as a national first for British space efforts.

### Why It Still Resonates

Prospero remains a symbolic milestone as the UK expands its modern launch ambitions through Scottish spaceports and startups such as Skyrora. The satellite is still in orbit, which links this piece of history to the growing focus on orbital debris and sustainable space operations. Its story now speaks to both Britain's past achievements and its future plans.
