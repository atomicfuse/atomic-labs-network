## New Targeted Drug Outperforms Chemotherapy in Advanced Breast Cancer Trial

A phase 3 trial has found that trastuzumab rezetecan significantly delayed tumor growth in patients with ERBB2-low advanced breast cancer, when compared with chemotherapy.

### What the trial found

Tumors shrank in 75.5% of patients who received the drug, roughly three out of four. The treatment held back disease progression for longer than chemotherapy did. However, side effects occurred more often among those taking the drug, and some of them were serious.

### Why it matters now

Breast cancer care is shifting quickly toward targeted therapies, particularly for patients whose tumors do not fit older treatment categories. These results point to a possible new option for people with advanced disease. They also raise open questions about whether the drug improves survival and how it affects patients' quality of life.
