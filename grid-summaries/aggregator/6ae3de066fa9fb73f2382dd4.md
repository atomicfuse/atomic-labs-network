## Rethinking the Glass Tower in a Warming World

A new piece challenges the global appeal of glass skyscrapers, asking whether their sleek, reflective look still makes sense as temperatures rise. It presents shining skylines as emblems of modernity that may carry heavy climate and energy burdens.

### The Case Against Glass

The article questions both the practicality and the sustainability of glass-heavy design. Rather than treating glittering towers as simple signs of progress, it suggests their appearance may hide significant costs tied to climate and energy use. The central concern is whether a style associated with a modern city can hold up in a hotter future.

### Why Design Now Matters

With cities facing more extreme heat, how buildings are made is increasingly a question of climate resilience, not just taste. Developers, architects, and city leaders are under growing pressure to reconsider materials that raise cooling demand and add to urban heat. The debate reflects a wider shift in how urban design is judged.
