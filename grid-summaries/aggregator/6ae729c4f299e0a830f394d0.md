## Herzog urges flexibility as Israel enters coalition talks

President Isaac Herzog has asked Israel's political leaders to show flexibility and work together as coalition negotiations begin after the recent elections. He cautioned that digging in could lead to yet another election cycle.

### Consultations begin Wednesday

Herzog is set to start consultations with party representatives on Wednesday. The Joint List and Ra'am are expected to recommend Gadi Eisenkot for prime minister during those meetings. The recommendations form part of the process that will shape which leader gets the chance to assemble a government.

### Why the appeal matters

Israel is now in a critical post-election phase, and the way a coalition comes together will determine the next government's makeup and stability. Herzog's public call for pragmatism hints at possible friction among party leaders. It also highlights how hard it can be to reach agreement in a fragmented political landscape.
