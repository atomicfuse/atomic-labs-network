## Retatrutide shows promise against obstructive sleep apnea in trials

An experimental weight-loss drug called retatrutide has reduced obstructive sleep apnea symptoms in clinical trials. Researchers reported that as many as half of participants improved to mild sleep apnea by the end of the study.

### How the drug seems to work

Retatrutide is a triple hormone receptor agonist. Its benefit for sleep apnea appears to come mainly from helping people lose weight, rather than from any direct effect on breathing.

### Why it matters now

Sleep apnea affects up to one-third of US adults, and much of it goes undiagnosed. Obesity rates keep climbing, which makes alternatives to the standard CPAP machine more urgent. The condition raises the risk of cardiovascular disease, dementia and diabetes. Retatrutide is still in phase 3 trials and has not been approved by the FDA, but it offers a new approach to treatment.
