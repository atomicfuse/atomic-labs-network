## Major Medical Societies Unveil Life Stage Framework for Women's Heart Health

Four leading U.S. medical societies have jointly issued the Female Life Stage framework, a single set of guidance tying women's cardiovascular risk to hormonal shifts across five stages of life.

### What the Framework Says

The guidance follows women from menarche through pregnancy and into postmenopause. It stresses that heart health is shaped by hormonal transitions, reproductive history, and psychosocial factors. Because of this, the societies call for integrated clinical care instead of fragmented approaches that treat each issue separately.

### Why It Matters Now

Heart disease in women is still underdiagnosed and undertreated, and hormonal and sex-specific risk factors are often overlooked in practice. As the first unified guidance of its kind from major societies, the framework points to a cultural and clinical shift. Menstrual patterns, pregnancy complications, and menopause transitions are recognized as cardiovascular risk markers that belong in heart health assessments.
