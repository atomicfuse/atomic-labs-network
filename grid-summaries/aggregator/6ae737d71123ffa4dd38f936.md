## Autistic Women Face Harder Menopause, Study Finds

New research in the journal *Autism in Adulthood* suggests that autistic women go through menopause with more severe symptoms than neurotypical women. They also report higher levels of depression and anxiety during this life stage.

### What the study found

Autistic women experienced significantly more severe menopause symptoms, along with greater depression and anxiety, compared with neurotypical women. The researchers point to two contributing factors. Autistic women tended to feel less in control of their symptoms, and they reported a weaker understanding of what was happening in their bodies.

### Why it matters

Menopause is already underdiagnosed and under-supported for many women. This research highlights an overlooked intersection, showing that autism can compound the challenge. The findings make it urgent for healthcare providers and content creators to address this gap and offer better support to autistic women going through the transition.
