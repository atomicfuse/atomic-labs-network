## Fifteen Possible Reasons You Feel Tired All the Time

### What the Article Covers

The causes span a wide range. Some are tied to everyday habits and conditions, such as allergies, a sedentary lifestyle, and poor sleep. Others involve emotional and mental strain, including stress and depression. Metabolic issues also make the list. For every cause, the piece offers guidance from experts on how to address it, giving readers practical starting points rather than just a list of problems.

Burnout and exhaustion are described as epidemic at the moment. Many people are balancing work, social demands, and mental health challenges all at once, which makes constant tiredness a widely shared experience.
