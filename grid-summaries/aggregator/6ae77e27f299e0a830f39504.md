## Record Number of Health Professionals Seek Office

More than 125 doctors, nurses, and public health experts are running in the midterm elections, a historic surge. The article looks at why these candidates are entering politics and what their presence could mean for healthcare policy.

### What Is Driving the Surge

Candidates' experiences during the pandemic and concerns about how science is treated under current leadership are the main forces behind the wave. Many health professionals appear to see politics as a place where their expertise is needed. The piece explores how their arrival could shape the role of expertise in government.

### Why the Timing Matters

Healthcare ranks alongside gas prices among voters' top household worries, and proposed cuts to health insurance coverage threaten millions of people. At the same time, federal science funding is being slashed and public health infrastructure is being dismantled. That combination has created urgency for science-backed candidates to step into the political arena.
