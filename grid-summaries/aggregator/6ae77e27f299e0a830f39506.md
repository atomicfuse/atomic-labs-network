## Georgia Republicans Keep Their Campaigns Local as Election Day Nears

Georgia Republican Governor Brian Kemp is steering his party's candidates away from national politics as voting day approaches. His message to voters is to weigh what has been achieved in the state rather than messaging driven from Washington.

### A Local Focus

Kemp is urging voters to look at local accomplishments, and he is distancing himself and other state GOP candidates from national debates. The approach reflects a wider effort among some Georgia Republicans to insulate their campaigns from national polarization. Democrats in the state are taking the opposite route, bringing in high-profile national figures such as Obama and Harris.

### Why the Timing Matters

Georgia is a perennial swing state, and Election Day is imminent, so the contrast in strategy stands out. It shows how Republicans are handling voter fatigue with partisan politics and the risk of being defined by Trump or Washington controversies. The localization approach could signal broader GOP tactics heading into a tight gubernatorial race.
