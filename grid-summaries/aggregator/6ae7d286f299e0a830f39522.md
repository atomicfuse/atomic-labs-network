## Belmont Donors Favor Brownsberger, Healey and Dionne

Belmont residents have directed $414,880 toward statewide campaign spending, and their giving shows clear favorites. As Massachusetts enters the last stretch before the Nov. 3 midterm elections, the pattern offers a view of where local money is going.

### Who Is Getting the Money

Incumbent state Sen. William Brownsberger stands out, drawing 30% of everything Belmont residents have given in statewide campaign spending. Gov. Maura Healey also has strong backing from the town's donors. Elizabeth Dionne, the Republican challenger for state treasurer, rounds out the group of top recipients.

### Why the Pattern Matters Now

With the midterms close, donation patterns serve as a crucial indicator of where local money and grassroots energy are flowing. They also point to which races are energizing their communities most. Belmont's giving includes notable support from the tech, real estate and biotech industries, adding another layer to the picture of who is funding these campaigns.
