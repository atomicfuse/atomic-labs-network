## Black Michigan Voters Stay Steady Amid Senate Race Turmoil

Michigan's Democratic Senate contest has been shaken by internal conflict in its closing days, but the voters said to be most affected by the fallout appear largely unmoved.

### The Endorsement Shake-Up

Rep. Haley Stevens reversed her earlier endorsement of Abdul El-Sayed and instead backed Republican Mike Rogers, a move that prompted national outrage. Jim Clyburn responded with a counter-endorsement. The back-and-forth has fueled wider feuding among national Democrats, who are divided over Israel-Gaza politics and questions about which candidate is viable.

### Black Voters Hold Their Ground

Despite the noise, Black voters in Michigan are largely unfazed and remain solidly behind El-Sayed. Their steadiness challenges the media narrative that he is weak with Black communities. With the race in its final days, the lack of division among this electorate stands in contrast to the turmoil among party figures and commentators.
