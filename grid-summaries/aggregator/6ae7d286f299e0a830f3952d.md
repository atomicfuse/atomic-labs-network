## USC Students Turn Frustration Into Engagement Before Midterms

Student voters at USC are paying closer attention to the midterm elections, with many saying they are fed up with a lack of political action while still holding onto hope for change.

### What Is Driving Student Engagement

Frustration is pushing these voters toward the polls. Students point to political inaction on artificial intelligence, job prospects and systemic change as key concerns. Even so, they remain hopeful that polarization can be bridged. Many see localized politics as one path forward, and they want leaders who put constituents ahead of party loyalty.

### Why Youth Turnout Matters Now

Young voters have historically turned out in low numbers, with just 23% voting in the last midterm. This cycle looks different, as early voting data shows a significant surge, with more than 48.5 million early ballots cast nationwide. That increase suggests a possible shift in youth political participation, one that could reshape election outcomes.
