## Hampton Roads races lead Virginia political ad spending

Virginia's 2026 congressional races have drawn more than $82 million in political advertising, and the Hampton Roads media market accounts for much of it.

### Where the money went

The competitive 2nd District contest between Rep. Jen Kiggans and Democrat Elaine Luria alone attracted $13 million in ads. That race helped make Hampton Roads the dominant area for spending in the state. Overall, Democrats outspent Republicans, and the analysis shows campaigns divided their budgets between traditional media and digital platforms. Democrats leaned on social media more heavily than Republicans did.

### Why it matters now

Advertising spending in this election year is at historic levels, and the figures show how campaigns are distributing large budgets across television, radio and digital outlets in the final stretch. The emphasis on data-driven platform choices, along with one media market's outsized share, points to how modern campaigns operate and how voter attention is increasingly fragmented and targeted.
