## Trump Says He Could Have Won Vietnam in a Week, Drawing Fire

President Trump said Sunday that he could have won the Vietnam War in a week. He pointed to military tactics such as Agent Orange to back up the claim, and he appeared to mix up key Vietnamese cities while doing so.

### The Criticism

The boast drew sharp reaction, in part because of Trump's draft deferments during the conflict. Critics seized on the contrast between his confident claim about winning the war and his own absence from military service at the time. The apparent confusion over Vietnamese geography added to the pushback.

### The Timing

The remarks come just days before the midterm elections, while Trump's approval rating is under pressure over the military engagement with Iran. That conflict has dragged on for nine months and sent oil prices climbing. Against that backdrop, his message about winning wars quickly may feel hollow to voters worried about escalation.
