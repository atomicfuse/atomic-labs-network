## Pritzker's Personal Political Spending Reaches $545 Million in a Decade

Gov. J.B. Pritzker has put $545 million of his own fortune into politics over the past ten years, according to the story's account of his spending. Most of that money has gone to his own campaigns, media advertising and Illinois Democratic organizations.

### Where the Money Has Gone

The spending has climbed in election years. Beyond his own races and state party groups, Pritzker has also made sizable contributions to national Democratic committees. That national giving has prompted questions about whether his political ambitions extend past the governor's office.

### Why It Matters Now

Pritzker is seeking a third term as governor, with the election set for Nov. 3. His outlay illustrates how much influence a wealthy candidate can wield over state and national politics. He has also declined to rule out a presidential run in 2028, which adds to the debate over what his spending might signal.
