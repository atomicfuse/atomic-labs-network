## Doctors Push Back on Viral Claim About Morning Coffee

Gastroenterologists have weighed in on a popular social media claim that drinking coffee before eating is harmful. Experts largely say the worry is unfounded for most healthy adults.

### The Claim Under Scrutiny

The claim circulating on TikTok and other platforms holds that coffee on an empty stomach spikes cortisol and damages the gut. Doctors who were asked about it largely dismissed the concern, saying it does not hold up for most healthy adults. In their view, the habit many people follow each morning is not the health threat the videos suggest.

### Why the Fact-Check Matters

Wellness misinformation spreads quickly online, and audiences are eager for expert guidance on trending health advice. Because the question involves an everyday routine that many people wonder about, the doctors' response gives viewers a clearer picture of what specialists actually think about it.
