## Murdoch Blames "Viewer Fatigue" for Fox News Ratings Slide

Rupert Murdoch says Fox News's recent drop in ratings comes down to "viewer fatigue" and not to any change in the channel's editorial approach. His comments arrive just before the midterm elections.

### What Murdoch Said

Murdoch rejected the idea that editorial shifts at Fox News are behind the decline. Instead, he pointed to audiences simply growing tired. The explanation stands out because rival networks are benefiting from heightened public interest in the midterm races, while Fox News is not seeing the same lift.

### Why the Timing Matters

News consumption typically rises during major elections, which makes Fox News's weaker numbers a notable anomaly. The slide arrives at a critical political moment, and it signals possible problems with viewer trust or engagement. With the midterms imminent, the channel's performance will be closely watched.
