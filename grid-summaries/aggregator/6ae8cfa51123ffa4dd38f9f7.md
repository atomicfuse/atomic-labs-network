## Trump Veers Into Sibling Tangent at Kansas Midterm Event

President Trump used a midterm campaign stop in Kansas to volunteer that he would never date his sisters, an aside no one prompted. He described it as an answer to questions people had supposedly put to him years earlier.

### The Tangent and Its Target

The remark appeared to be a setup for a return to a claim he has made repeatedly without proof: that Rep. Ilhan Omar married her brother. The sibling comments served as a bridge to that accusation, which remains unproven.

### Why the Timing Matters

Trump is campaigning ahead of the midterms, and his speeches often mix personal stories with attacks on political opponents. In this case, he aimed a false accusation of incest at Omar while her home is under investigation and her citizenship is being questioned, adding weight to the remarks beyond a simple rambling moment.
