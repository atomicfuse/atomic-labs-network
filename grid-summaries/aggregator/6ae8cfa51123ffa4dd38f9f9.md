## Political Rudeness Has a Long History, and It Has Never Moved in One Direction

Harsh words, personal attacks, and incivility in American politics are older than today's headlines suggest. A new article looks back to show that standards of political decency have never really moved in a straight line.

### Echoes from earlier eras

The article draws on examples from the 1820s and from the 1980s and 1990s to show that inflammatory rhetoric has surfaced repeatedly. It also explores how social media, shock jock culture, negative political advertising, and two-party polarization have helped make aggressive political discourse seem normal.

### Why the timing matters

With the 2026 midterm campaign season underway, incivility is dominating headlines, from Trump's profanities to threats of violence. More than 60% of Americans say both parties need to dial back their rhetoric. The article places current behavior within a longer historical pattern rather than treating it as a modern aberration, which makes understanding how the country got here more urgent.
