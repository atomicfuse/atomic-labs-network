## Zelenskyy Warns of Russian Strike Timed to US Midterms

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says Russia is preparing a large-scale attack to coincide with the American midterm elections. He is asking Washington to act to deter Moscow before it happens.

### What Zelenskyy Is Warning About

Zelenskyy described the plan as a "massive attack" that would target Ukraine's energy infrastructure and supply routes. He said the timing is meant to exploit a moment when global attention is focused elsewhere. He urged the United States to raise pressure on Moscow through economic sanctions and to keep providing military aid, with particular emphasis on Patriot missiles.

### Why It Matters Now

The warning links US domestic politics with the ongoing war between Ukraine and Russia. Election results could directly shape the level of American military and financial support for Kyiv. The reported timing also suggests Russia chooses moments of major geopolitical focus for its strikes, turning attention cycles into part of the conflict.
