## Voters Still Trust Elections as Midterms Near, Despite Legal Battles

Confidence in how U.S. elections are run remains strong heading into the midterms, even as high-profile legal cases challenge the system's integrity. Those include Justice Department suits alleging noncitizens voted.

### Trust Holds, but Concerns Differ

Most voters still say they trust the election system. That steadiness stands out given how heated the debate has become. Beneath the overall numbers, though, partisan divides appear on specific worries. Voters differ over whether ballot access is secure enough and whether safeguards against noncitizen voting are adequate. Skepticism also varies sharply depending on which candidate a voter prefers.

### A Polarizing Campaign Issue

Election integrity has been a divisive political topic since 2020, with competing narratives about fraud on one side and access on the other shaping much of the public conversation. The latest findings suggest that, despite this noise, broad faith in the process has endured as voters prepare to cast ballots in the midterms.
