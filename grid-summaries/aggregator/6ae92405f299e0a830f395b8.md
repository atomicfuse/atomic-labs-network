## US to Host Russia-Ukraine Peace Talks in Abu Dhabi

Russian and Ukrainian delegations are set to meet in Abu Dhabi on November 10-11 for talks convened by the United States. Special envoy Steve Witkoff will mediate the discussions.

### What is on the agenda

The talks will center on ending the war, security arrangements for the period after the fighting, and steps to de-escalate. Separately, American negotiators will meet European allies in Brussels. That meeting is meant to coordinate on security guarantees for Ukraine and support for its reconstruction.

### Why the timing matters

The effort comes as winter approaches and geopolitical tensions remain high. It is the first trilateral negotiation since February, which points to renewed American commitment to resolving Europe's deadliest conflict since World War II. Even so, Moscow and Kyiv remain deeply divided over the terms of any settlement.
