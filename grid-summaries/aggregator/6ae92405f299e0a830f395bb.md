## First-Time Voters at Northeastern Reflect on Casting Ballots in 2026

Ahead of the 2026 midterm elections, students at Northeastern University are describing what it feels like to vote for the first time. They discuss the emotions that come with joining the electorate and how they are preparing to take part.

The students say their political engagement has been influenced by several forces, including their families, their education, and current events. Together, these factors have shaped how they think about politics and how they approach their voting decisions. Their accounts offer a window into how young people form civic habits.
