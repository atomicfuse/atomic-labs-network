## Trump Drops South Africa From Key African Trade Program

President Trump has taken South Africa out of the African Growth and Opportunity Act, known as AGOA, a preferential trade program. The move follows earlier criticism of the country by his administration.

### The Decision

The administration pointed to alleged discrimination against white people as its justification for the removal. The step marks an escalation in how the White House treats South Africa, building on its previous public criticism of the country.

### Why It Matters

Trade policy is a central focus of Trump's second term, and South Africa is a major African nation that had benefited from a decades-old preferential trade agreement. Its removal signals a shift in U.S.–Africa relations and raises questions about how the administration will use trade tools going forward. The decision also reflects wider tensions over affirmative action and racial equity debates, which are now being carried into international policy.
