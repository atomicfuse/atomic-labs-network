## Endless Cruise Ship Odyssey Left Stuck in Cambodia as Auction Looms

The Odyssey, a vessel sold as an "endless cruise" experience, is still stranded in Cambodia. Efforts to secure funding for the venture have fallen apart, and the ship could now be put up for auction.

### Passengers in Limbo

With the money to keep the voyage going gone, people connected to the cruise are left unsure what comes next. The ship remains in Cambodia, and the possibility of an auction hangs over its future. No clear answer has emerged about how those on board or booked to sail will be affected.

### A Test for Extreme Cruising

The collapse stands out as a high-profile failure in experiential travel. It raises questions about whether ultra-long cruise concepts can work as a business. It also highlights the gaps in passenger protections when a travel company fails partway through a journey, leaving travellers exposed at a distant port.
