## Karl Bushby Ends 28-Year Walk Where It Began

Karl Bushby has finished a 31,000-mile walk that took him across four continents and lasted 28 years. His final destination was his mother's house in Hull, England.

### A Journey Full Circle

The trek is regarded as one of the longest continuous walks in modern history. Bushby's route carried him over vast distances and many landscapes across four continents. Rather than ending somewhere new, it closed in the same place where his adventure started decades earlier, bringing the story back to his family's doorstep in Hull.

### Why It Resonates Today

Extreme endurance journeys and long-form personal quests are drawing renewed cultural interest. Many people are looking for meaning beyond conventional life paths, and Bushby's walk speaks to that mood. The story also lands at a time when more people document and share their own transformative personal missions, making his homecoming feel especially timely.
