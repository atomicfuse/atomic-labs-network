## Rosé Makes Her CFDA Fashion Awards Debut in Gold Saint Laurent

Blackpink singer Rosé stepped onto the red carpet at the CFDA Fashion Awards in New York for the first time. Her look was one of several high-profile appearances in a busy week.

### The Look

Rosé wore a gold brocade crop top paired with a midi skirt, both from Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello. The outfit showed off her midriff and made the debut a memorable one. The awards night is only one stop on her schedule, as she is also set to perform at the LACMA Art+Film Gala this weekend.

### Why It Matters Now

Rosé is working to expand her solo career beyond K-pop, and she has a second album due in 2027. Her growing presence at major U.S. fashion and cultural events, along with her choice of premium designer looks, signals her positioning as both a style icon and a solo star in the Western market.
