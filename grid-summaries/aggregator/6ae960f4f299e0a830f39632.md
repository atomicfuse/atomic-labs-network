## Katie Holmes Brings the Leather Trench to Milan

Katie Holmes turned heads at Milan Fashion Week in a luxurious leather trench coat. A new shopping guide now rounds up 15 ways for readers to recreate the look.

### The Look and the Picks

The guide gathers leather and faux leather trench coats in a range of styles, price points and colors. That variety means shoppers can echo Holmes's outfit whether they want the real thing or a more budget-friendly alternative, and whether they prefer a classic shade or something different.

### Why the Timing Works

Leather trench coats are enjoying a resurgence, and seeing a relatable figure like Holmes wear one adds to the appeal. Celebrity style often drives fashion retail, especially when an unexpected trend gets spotlighted. The moment also lands as fall shopping season gets going, when outerwear becomes a wardrobe essential for many people.
