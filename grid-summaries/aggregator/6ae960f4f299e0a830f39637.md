## Justin Timberlake Preps Four-Part Apple TV+ Docuseries on Past Controversies

Justin Timberlake is developing a four-part docuseries with Apple TV+, directed by Fisher Stevens. The project is set to premiere in December and will tackle two of the biggest controversies tied to his career.

### The Super Bowl Moment

The series will address the 2004 Super Bowl halftime show wardrobe malfunction involving Janet Jackson, an incident that sparked national debate and led to increased FCC fines. Jackson is slated to be interviewed about the moment, which gives the project a notable second voice alongside Timberlake's own account.

### Telling His Side

The docuseries will also explore Timberlake's past relationship with Britney Spears. It arrives as Jackson and Spears have both already shared their own narratives, and as celebrity reckoning documentaries, including Jackson's 2022 Lifetime film, have proven commercially successful and culturally resonant. The release marks a pivotal moment in how Timberlake presents his story and his public image.
