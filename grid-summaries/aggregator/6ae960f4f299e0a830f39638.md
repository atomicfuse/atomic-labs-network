## Jimmy Turns the Spotlight on James Stewart's War Years

A new biopic, *Jimmy*, looks at a lesser-known chapter in the life of James Stewart. KJ Apa plays the screen legend in a film that centers on his World War II service and the aftermath that followed.

### From B-17 Cockpit to Colonel

The film follows Stewart's military career, during which he flew 20 combat missions as a B-17 pilot and rose to the rank of Colonel. It explores how the trauma of war shaped his life and career once the fighting was over, presenting a side of the icon that has largely gone untold.

### Returning to Acting

After the war, Stewart struggled psychologically to go back to acting, particularly during production of *It's a Wonderful Life*. The film views that beloved classic through a new lens. It arrives in November 2026 as awards season approaches, amid growing interest in biopics about overlooked chapters and in stories of veterans and the human cost of service.
