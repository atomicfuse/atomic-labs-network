## Korean Drama The Remarried Empress Arrives a Day Ahead of Schedule

Fans of Korean romance will not have to wait as long as expected. The Webtoon adaptation starring Shin Mina and Lee Jongsuk is moving its premiere up by one day, landing on Disney+ and Hulu on November 3.

### What to Expect

The first four episodes will be released at the same time, giving viewers plenty to watch from the start. The story centers on an empress who divorces her unfaithful husband and then marries a rival crown prince. That decision sparks a power struggle that puts an entire empire at risk.

### Why the Timing Matters

The early release builds on strong interest from fans. The trailer reached 13 million views within 48 hours, and screenings at the Busan Film Festival sold out. Moving the premiere forward lets the series tap into that enthusiasm while it is at its peak, which could boost word-of-mouth momentum as viewers begin watching and talking about it.
