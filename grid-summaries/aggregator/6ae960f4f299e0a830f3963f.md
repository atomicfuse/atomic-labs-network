## Renner and Brolin Board True-Life Ocean Survival Drama

Jeremy Renner and Josh Brolin are set to star in *A Speck in the Sea*, a survival thriller built around a real emergency in the Atlantic Ocean. The project brings two well-known actors together with an Oscar-winning filmmaker.

### A Fight for Survival at Sea

The film is based on the true story of Johnny Aldridge, a lobster fisherman who fell overboard in the Atlantic. It follows the desperate rescue mission launched by his friend, making one man's brush with the open water the heart of the drama.

### Talent Behind the Camera

Orlando von Einsiedel, an Oscar winner, is directing the thriller. His pairing with stars as high-profile as Renner and Brolin arrives as survival stories continue to resonate with audiences.
