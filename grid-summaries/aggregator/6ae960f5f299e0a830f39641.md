## McCarthy Wants Ramsey Miniseries to Reignite Cold Case

Melissa McCarthy stars in an upcoming miniseries about the JonBenét Ramsey case, taking on the role of JonBenét's mother, Patsy. She has said she hopes the project does more than entertain, and that it might draw fresh attention to a case that has gone unsolved for decades.

### A Hope for Renewed Investigation

McCarthy has expressed hope that the show will spark a new look at the long-running cold case. Her wish is that audiences who watch the miniseries will come away wanting answers, and that this interest might encourage renewed investigative effort into what happened to JonBenét.

### Empathy Over Entertainment

McCarthy also wants viewers to approach true crime with greater empathy rather than treating real tragedies as entertainment. Her comments land amid a broader cultural conversation about the ethics of dramatizing real cases and how such programs affect victims' families, at a time when true crime content continues to dominate streaming and entertainment.
