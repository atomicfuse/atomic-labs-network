## SGLT2 Inhibitors Show Benefits for Over-80s With Kidney Disease

A study presented at the American Society of Nephrology's annual conference reports that SGLT2 inhibitors offer meaningful protection to adults aged 80 and older who have chronic kidney disease.

### What the Study Found

Researchers analysed real-world data from nearly 24,000 patients. They found that people taking SGLT2 inhibitors had significantly lower rates of death, cardiovascular events and kidney complications. The results apply to a group that is rarely examined closely in drug research.

### Why It Matters

Older adults are typically left out of major drug trials, which has created a gap in knowledge about how safe and effective treatments are for them. This analysis helps fill that gap for a class of medication that is being prescribed more and more often to elderly patients with kidney disease, giving clinicians stronger evidence to draw on.
