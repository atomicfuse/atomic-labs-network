## AI Heart Scan Readings Track Survival Better Than Clinician Measurements

A large study in The Lancet Digital Health has found that AI-derived measurements of ejection fraction from heart MRI scans are more closely tied to patient survival than measurements made by clinicians.

### Speed and Survival Findings

The AI system analyzed each scan in two to three seconds. Human experts needed about 13 minutes for the same task. Despite the gap in time, the AI's ejection fraction readings showed a stronger link to survival outcomes than those taken by clinicians, according to the study's results.

### Why It Matters Now

Healthcare systems are under growing pressure to work more efficiently and improve patient outcomes. The research points to a concrete and scalable way AI could support clinical decision-making in cardiology. It suggests the technology can maintain accuracy while easing the workload on clinicians, offering a practical option for busy cardiac care services.
