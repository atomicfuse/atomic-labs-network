## Midterm Voters Decide Congress as Cost Worries Dominate

Americans are casting ballots in midterm elections that will determine which party controls Congress. The vote arrives at a pivotal time for Trump's Republican Party.

### What voters are weighing

Rising living costs are the leading concern, tied to tariffs and an unpopular war with Iran. That pressure is putting traditionally safe Republican seats at risk in states including Kansas, Texas and Iowa, areas where the party has typically felt secure.

### Why the result matters

Trump's approval ratings sit at record lows, while inflation and fuel prices are at historic highs. The outcome could reshape the political landscape heading into 2028. It may also show whether Trump remains an electoral asset or has become a liability for Republicans, particularly in key battleground regions.
