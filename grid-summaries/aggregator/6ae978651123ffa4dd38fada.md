## Wage Protests Shut Hundreds of Bangladesh Garment Factories

Hundreds of garment factories across Bangladesh have closed as protests over pay intensify, with the unrest turning violent in several places.

### Workers Demand Higher Pay

Workers are calling for a minimum monthly wage of 30,000 taka, or $242, which is more than double the current standard of 13,000 taka. The demonstrations have involved road blockades, vandalised factories, burned vehicles and clashes with police. Dozens of people have been arrested as the protests have spread.

### Pressure on Industry and Economy

The garment sector is worth $40bn a year and supplies major global brands including H&M, Primark and Uniqlo, so the closures are a worry for international supply chains and for the national economy. Rising living costs have pushed workers to breaking point. The government has set up a wage review board, but that step has not stopped the demonstrations from spreading across manufacturing hubs.
