## Reddit takes Australia's under-16 social media ban to the High Court

Australia's pioneering ban on social media accounts for under-16s now faces its first major legal test. Reddit has brought the challenge to the High Court as enforcement of the law gets under way.

### Reddit's case against the law

Reddit argues the legislation is ill thought out and too broad. It also contends that the ban restricts political speech while failing to protect minors in any effective way. Studies suggest that most Australian teenagers are still using social media despite the ban, a finding that adds weight to questions about how well the rules work in practice.

### Why the ruling could reach beyond Australia

The outcome is expected to set a global precedent, since countries including the United Kingdom and Malaysia are weighing similar age-restriction laws. A win for Reddit could encourage other technology companies to contest comparable rules elsewhere. It might also push policymakers to rethink blunt approaches to youth online safety.
