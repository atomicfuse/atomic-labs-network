## Tokyo Watch Thief Returns $292,000 Timepiece With Handwritten Apology

A thief who took an Audemars Piguet watch worth $292,000 from a store in Tokyo has sent it back, along with a handwritten note saying sorry. Police still do not know who the suspect is.

### A Surprising Return

The luxury watch disappeared from the Tokyo store, and for three days its whereabouts were unknown. It then arrived back at the shop by courier, accompanied by the handwritten apology. Investigators have been left puzzled, as they have identified neither the person responsible nor the reason behind the decision to give the watch back.

### A Pattern at High-End Shops

The case draws attention to continuing trouble with luxury watch theft at upscale retail destinations in Japan. Only months earlier, another major heist took place at the same shopping complex. The unusual return also raises questions about criminal psychology and strange crime patterns.
