## Democrats Flex to Win Back Young Men Ahead of 2026

Democrats say they have reversed heavy losses among young men, a group that backed Trump at 55% in 2024 and now trails badly for him. Two forces appear to be driving the shift: frustration with Trump's unkept promises and a coordinated effort by Democratic candidates to project strength.

### Why Young Men Are Swinging Back

Young men moved sharply right in 2024, but they are now swinging back to the left. The reasons given are Trump's broken promises on inflation and foreign wars, which have faded as pledges. That change opens a critical chance for a realignment heading into the 2026 elections, with a demographic once thought lost now back in play.

### The Gym-Culture Strategy

Democratic candidates have posted workout videos and taken up combative, masculine rhetoric. The aim is to signal strength and authenticity to young male voters. The approach is described as deliberate, using gym culture and masculine optics as tools to recapture a group that moved away from the party in 2024.
