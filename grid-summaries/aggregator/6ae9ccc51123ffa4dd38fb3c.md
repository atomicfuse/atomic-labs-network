## Drones Now Lead Cause of Civilian Deaths in Sudan

UN data shows that drones have become the main cause of civilian deaths in Sudan's ongoing war in 2024. They now account for more fatalities than any other weapon used in the fighting.

### A Crisis Already Under Strain

The rise in drone-related deaths comes on top of a humanitarian emergency that was already severe. Famine and drought have placed heavy pressure on the country's population, and the growing toll from drones adds another layer of danger for civilians trying to survive in difficult conditions.

### Why the Figures Matter

Sudan's war has largely slipped out of global headlines, even though it remains one of the deadliest conflicts in the world. The UN data serves as a pointed reminder of how much civilians are suffering. It also highlights how modern warfare is changing, with drones taking a central and deadly role in the violence.
