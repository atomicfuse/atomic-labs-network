## CPAC Brings Its Conference to Mar-a-Lago, With Revenue Flowing to Trump's Club

CPAC is holding a four-day "Gathering of Visionaries" conference at Mar-a-Lago, Donald Trump's for-profit resort. Ticket packages run as high as $95,000, and some Republican insiders have criticized the event.

### Money to the Club

The conference sends substantial revenue straight to Trump's club. Critics within the party describe the event as a financial scheme that has little to do with policy substance, and they argue that the money matters more than the ideas on offer.

### A Changed CPAC

The event comes after the 2024 midterm elections, and it reflects how Trump's political apparatus has become a money-generating ecosystem. It also signals a shift for CPAC, which has moved from being a serious conservative policy forum to a celebrity spectacle centered on Trump.
