## Russian Strike on Zaporizhzhia Aid Site Leaves at Least 31 Dead

A Russian military strike on a humanitarian aid site in Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia region has killed at least 31 people, and dozens more are still missing. The attack was one part of a much larger overnight assault across the country.

### The Overnight Barrage

Russia launched 289 drones and 76 missiles in the barrage. Beyond the aid site, the strikes damaged hospitals and power plants in several regions. The full extent of the damage and the final number of casualties have not been established, since dozens of people remain unaccounted for.

### Timing and Political Context

The attack came as the U.S. presidential election took place. Ukraine's leadership is openly presenting the strikes as a deliberate signal, timed to test American attention and resolve at a pivotal political moment. That framing comes from Ukrainian officials, who describe the assault as meant to gauge how Washington responds.
