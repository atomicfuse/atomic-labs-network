## Scandal Shadows Competitive Suburban Illinois House Race

Republican Gabby Shanahan and appointed Democrat Jessica Dixon Heitman are facing off in Illinois' 97th House District, a contest overshadowed by questions about how Speaker Chris Welch handled harassment allegations against the seat's former occupant.

### A Rare Purple District

The 97th District is a competitive suburban seat in a state where Democrats otherwise dominate. Heitman was appointed to the post after Harry Benton, the previous representative, faced sexual harassment allegations. The race is seen as a test of whether Democratic leadership's response to workplace misconduct will cost the party at the ballot box.

### What Is at Stake

Democrats hold supermajorities in the statehouse, but Welch's role as speaker could come under pressure if the party loses seats or if members rebel over his handling of the complaints. With a new General Assembly set to be sworn in next year, this suburban race is viewed as a bellwether for whether the scandal brings real electoral consequences.
