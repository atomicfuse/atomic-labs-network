## Ms. Archives Trace Five Decades of Women's Voting Power

Ms. Magazine has published a retrospective that looks back at how it covered women as a political force long before the term "gender gap" existed. The collection gathers six pieces from its archives and connects them to today's political moment.

### A Prediction That Began in 1972

The retrospective follows five decades of feminist political power, starting with Gloria Steinem's 1972 prediction that women voters would become a force in politics. The six archival articles show how that idea developed over the years and how the magazine tracked the growing influence of women at the ballot box.

### What the 2026 Midterms Reveal

The collection also reflects on the 2026 midterm elections, where women candidates are filing in record numbers. Even so, women hold only 28% of congressional seats. The timing gives the archive fresh relevance, as readers weigh how far the feminist political movement has come and how much distance remains.
