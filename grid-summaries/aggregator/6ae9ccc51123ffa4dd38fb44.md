## Nonvoters Could Hold the Key to Congress

A group of people who are not planning to take part in the upcoming vote may end up shaping who controls Congress. Their absence from the polls, rather than their ballots, is what draws attention to them in this story.

### Why They Matter

The central point is that these voters have real potential influence. Congress is closely balanced, and the choices made by people who decide to stay home could help determine which side holds power. Their decision not to participate is itself a factor in the outcome, even though it leaves no mark on a ballot.
