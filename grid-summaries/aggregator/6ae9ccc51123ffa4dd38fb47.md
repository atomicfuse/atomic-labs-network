## Uganda detains ex-deputy army chief Peter Elwelu over alleged arms stockpile

Ugandan authorities have arrested Peter Elwelu, a former deputy military chief, accusing him of hoarding weapons and organizing activities meant to destabilize the country. The arrest followed closely after he lost his military rank.

### The allegations and the rank removal

Elwelu is accused of stockpiling weapons and of organizing activities aimed at destabilizing Uganda. Only days before his arrest, defence chief Muhoozi Kainerugaba stripped him of his military rank. The sequence of events has placed a once-senior officer in the position of an alleged threat to state security.

### Why it matters now

The arrest points to possible rifts within Uganda's military and wider government hierarchy. With a high-ranking former official now cast as a danger to the state, the case raises questions about political purges and about the consolidation of power at the top of the country's leadership.
