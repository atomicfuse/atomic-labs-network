## Singles' Day Adds AI Tokens to Its Shopping Lineup for the First Time

China's Singles' Day shopping festival now features AI token packages as a major consumer product category for the first time. Leading e-commerce platforms are putting AI services alongside traditional festival goods.

### Lipstick King Leads the Sales

Top livestreamer Austin Li, known as the "Lipstick King," sold 100,000 monthly memberships of Alibaba's QwenWork AI service in just 30 minutes. Alibaba and JD.com are both offering AI credits from several providers, aiming to meet rising demand as more Chinese consumers use AI productivity tools.

### A Shift in Monetizing AI

The move points to a change in how e-commerce platforms make money from AI adoption. With usage of AI tools growing quickly among Chinese consumers, AI services have moved from niche offerings to mainstream shopping categories deemed worthy of promotion at one of the country's biggest retail festivals.
