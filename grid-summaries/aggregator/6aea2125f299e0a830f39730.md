## Voters Weigh In on 145 Ballot Measures Across the Country

Voters in multiple states are deciding on 145 ballot measures that cover a wide range of issues. The topics include abortion rights, congressional redistricting, transgender restrictions, voter ID requirements, and changes to election systems.

### A Test of Political Priorities

Taken together, the measures serve as a political test of Trump administration priorities. Some proposals also deal with questions specific to individual states, such as immigration enforcement and the use of traffic cameras. The sheer number of measures means voters are being asked to weigh in on both national debates and local concerns at the same time.

### Stakes for Midterms and Beyond

The votes arrive during a critical midterm cycle that will determine control of Congress and state offices. Abortion rights has emerged as a dominant issue since the Supreme Court overturned Roe v. Wade. The results will shape immediate policy and influence the political landscape heading into 2028.
