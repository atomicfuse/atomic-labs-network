## Megyn Kelly Blasts Michigan Democrat Over Senate Endorsement Flip

Rep. Haley Stevens, a Michigan Democrat, has drawn sharp criticism from commentator Megyn Kelly after endorsing Republican Mike Rogers for Senate. The endorsement came after Stevens had backed Democrat Abdul El-Sayed in the primary.

### Kelly's Criticism

Kelly called Stevens a "nutcase" and a "phony," pointing to the sudden change in her position. She also objected to what she described as demanding behavior by Stevens toward El-Sayed's campaign. The remarks put a high-profile voice on the side of those questioning the congresswoman's move.

### Why It Matters Now

The episode lands as Democrats appear divided heading into a competitive Michigan Senate race. Kelly publicly siding against a member of the party is presented as a sign of deep internal dysfunction. The split also opens the door for possible Republican gains in a key swing state.
