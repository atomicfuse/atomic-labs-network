## Bomb threats hit seven European flights, including two bound for Warsaw

Seven passenger planes across Europe received suspected bomb threats on a single day, and two of those flights set out from the UK. Every aircraft was inspected, and none was found to be carrying explosives.

### What authorities found

After the threats were received, each of the seven planes was checked. Inspectors found no explosives on any of them. One airport was briefly closed in connection with the incidents, while two of the affected planes had been heading to Warsaw. Authorities are now looking into whether the threats were coordinated.

### Why the cluster stands out

Such a sudden run of bomb threats, aimed at several airlines and spread across multiple countries within one day, is unusual. That pattern has raised concerns about possible coordinated disruption of European airspace. The worries come against a backdrop of recent geopolitical tensions and ongoing concerns about aviation security.
