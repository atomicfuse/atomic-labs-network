## GOP Sends Karoline Leavitt to Fox News for Final Midterm Push

The Republican Party turned to former White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt for a late appeal to voters on Fox News. She cautioned about a "far-left extremist" Democratic Party and encouraged Republicans to vote.

### The Pitch

Leavitt's message centered on Democrats, whom she portrayed as extreme, while urging Republicans to show up on Election Day. The appearance came amid worries that weak GOP turnout could give Democrats control of Congress. The party is treating the effort as a final get-out-the-vote push.

### The Stakes

The appeal arrived about 24 hours before a midterm election that could cost Republicans their House and Senate majorities. The party faces historically low presidential approval, with 60% disapproving, a trend tied to foreign wars and inflation. Those pressures have made turnout a central concern for Republicans heading into the vote.
