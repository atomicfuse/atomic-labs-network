## California Weighs First-in-Nation Billionaire Wealth Tax

California voters are being asked to decide on Proposition 40, a ballot measure that would place a one-time 5% wealth tax on billionaires. If it passes, the state would become the first in the country to do so.

### What the measure would do

Money raised by the tax would be set aside to help offset cuts to federal healthcare funding. The proposal has turned into one of the most expensive ballot fights in the state's history. It has also split people who are usually on the same side, including traditional Democratic allies. Some argue it would address inequality effectively, while others contend it could push wealth out of California.

### Why it matters now

The vote is a pivotal moment in the 2026 election and carries national implications. Its outcome could set a precedent for other states that are considering wealth taxes of their own. The debate also exposes deep divisions within the progressive movement over how best to tackle inequality.
