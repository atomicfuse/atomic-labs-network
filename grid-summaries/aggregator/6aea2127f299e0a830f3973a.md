## Ohio's Suburbs Become a True Toss-Up

Suburban voters in Ohio have moved from dependable Republican support to a politically split position. They now make up 52% of the state's electorate, and their leanings are close to even: 50% Republican and 47% Democratic.

### What Is Driving the Shift

The change reflects a demographic and political realignment. Suburban areas have grown, become more diverse, and reached higher levels of education. Voters there also hold differing views on economic and social issues, which has pulled the group away from its former Republican consistency.

### Why It Matters for 2024

As the largest electoral bloc in the state and a genuinely competitive one, suburban voters are the decisive battleground in the 2024 elections. The old assumption that suburbs are safe Republican territory no longer holds. Campaigns must now compete aggressively in communities once considered locked in.
