## Democrats Aim for Governor Majority for First Time in 20 Years

Democrats are trying to win a majority of the nation's governorships for the first time in two decades. Thirty-six states are holding gubernatorial elections, giving the party a wide field on which to compete.

### What is shaping the races

Voter dissatisfaction is expected to influence the results, with concerns about the economy, inflation and democracy all in play. Trump's low approval ratings are also weighing on Republican candidates in key contests, including races in populous states and swing states. That pressure could help Democrats as they try to flip offices and build a majority.

### Why it matters now

Governors help determine how their states administer elections and how they respond to federal policy during Trump's presidency. The outcome could therefore decide whether states resist or align with his agenda. The contests also carry national political weight, since some Democratic governors are positioning themselves as possible 2028 presidential candidates.
