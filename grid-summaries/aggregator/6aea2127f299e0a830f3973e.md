## Conservative Pollster Declines to Call Midterms for GOP Despite Praise for Trump

Pollster Matt Towery went on Fox News to talk through the latest midterm polling, offering a cautious assessment instead of a bold forecast with the election only days off.

### What Towery Said

Towery praised Trump's campaign efforts but stopped short of predicting Republican victories. He acknowledged that GOP candidates have improved their standing compared with earlier deficits. Even so, he noted that many races remain within the margin of error and depend heavily on turnout, leaving the outcome uncertain in those contests.

### Why It Matters Now

With voters heading to the polls on Tuesday, competing polling narratives are shaping final campaign messaging and public expectations.
