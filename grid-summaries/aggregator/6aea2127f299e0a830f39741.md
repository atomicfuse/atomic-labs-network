## Thick Ballots Jam Westchester Scanners, but Officials Say Every Vote Will Count

Voting machines in Westchester County, New York, failed to accept paper ballots on Election Day because the ballots were too thick for the scanners to read. Election officials say voters should not be discouraged by the problem.

### How officials are responding

Officials said all ballots will still be counted by hand. Ballots that the machines cannot take are being placed in emergency ballot bags for manual tallying. Voters were urged to stay in line and cast their votes despite the delays.

### Why it matters now

Westchester is the seventh most populous county in New York, so the problem touches a very large number of voters. The situation is unfolding on Election Day itself and raises immediate concerns about voting access, the reliability of voting machines, and public confidence in the electoral process during a high-stakes election cycle.
