## Fox chief concedes ratings dip at Fox News, blames "viewer fatigue"

Lachlan Murdoch, chief executive of Fox Corporation, has acknowledged that Fox News has seen its ratings decline. He pointed to "viewer fatigue" among its audience as the cause, while maintaining that the network remains the leader in cable news.

### What Murdoch said

The admission is notable for coming from the top of the company. Murdoch did not dispute that viewership has slipped, but he framed the drop as a matter of audience weariness rather than a loss of standing. In his account, Fox News still holds the top position among cable news channels despite the softer numbers.

### Why it matters

Fox News has long dominated cable news ratings, so even a modest acknowledgement of decline suggests the competitive landscape may be shifting. The comments arrive as cable news audiences spread across more platforms and political polarization continues to evolve. They also feed into wider questions about how sustainable cable news is and how loyal its viewers will remain.
