## King Charles Addresses Slave Trade Legacy at Commonwealth Summit

King Charles spoke at the Commonwealth summit in Antigua and Barbuda, describing the transatlantic slave trade as a "shameful chapter" in history. His remarks come during a Caribbean tour that has revived debate over Britain's colonial past.

### What the King said

The King said the slave trade fills him with "profound sorrow" and acknowledged that its effects are still felt across member nations. He did not offer a formal apology, but he expressed personal contrition. He also committed to addressing historical wrongs through partnership in the future.

### Why it matters now

The tour has reopened difficult conversations about colonial legacy and calls for reparations. Some Commonwealth nations and activists have demanded formal apologies and financial restitution. That makes this a high-stakes moment for the monarchy's relationship with former colonies, as his words fall short of what some of them are seeking.
