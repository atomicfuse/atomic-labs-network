## Andrew Summoned as Witness in High Court Fraud Trial

Prince Andrew has been served with a witness summons to give evidence in a High Court fraud trial due on or around 15 December. He is not accused of wrongdoing.

### What he may be asked

Andrew may face questions about his financial arrangements, including the £750,000 he received from a Turkish woman who says her financial adviser defrauded her. He is also expected to be questioned about disputed payments and a contested £768,000 loan allegedly linked to the purchase of a Swiss chalet.

### Why it matters now

The appearance would be Andrew's first public questioning since his 2019 BBC Newsnight interview. It is believed to be the first royal witness summons in nearly 200 years, a historically unprecedented moment for the monarchy. The case comes amid continuing scrutiny of his finances and a lack of transparency, offering a rare form of direct accountability.
