## Kiffin laments end of annual Alabama-LSU series

Lane Kiffin has voiced sadness over the end of the yearly Alabama-LSU rivalry, saying money is behind decisions that upend college football's historic matchups. He also had some advice for LSU fans heading into this game.

### A rivalry loses its annual slot

The coming game is the last of 63 consecutive annual meetings between the two programs. Conference realignment and scheduling changes have ended the tradition, even though the teams share a conference. Kiffin framed the shift as a case of finances taking priority over history, a view that reflects wider unease about what traditions deserve protection.

### An early kickoff and a request for noise

Unlike the usual night-game tradition, this matchup kicks off at 2:30 p.m. Kiffin told LSU fans to start drinking earlier so the crowd can generate noise. His comments come as realignment and the College Football Playoff keep reshaping schedules in a sport increasingly shaped by TV revenue and playoff positioning.
