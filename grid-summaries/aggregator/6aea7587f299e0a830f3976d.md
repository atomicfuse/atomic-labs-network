## Midterm Voters Name Economy and Democracy as Top Worries

An AP Voter Poll finds that U.S. midterm voters are deeply unhappy with the direction of the country. The economy and the state of democracy rank as their two biggest concerns.

### A Widely Sour Mood

Three-quarters of voters say they feel angry or dissatisfied. Economic strain appears to be a major factor, as about 80% report that inflation has caused severe or moderate financial hardship for their households. Voters also placed the condition of democracy near the top of their list of concerns, alongside the economy.

### Why the Findings Matter

Voter sentiment directly shapes the results of midterm and presidential elections, and the poll captures a moment of broad economic anxiety and worry about democracy. Inflation has persisted even though voters expected solutions, and the findings point to the priorities the electorate carries into 2026.
