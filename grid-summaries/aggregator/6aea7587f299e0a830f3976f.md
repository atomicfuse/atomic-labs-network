## Gutfeld Says Democrats Put Party Ahead of Members After Stevens Endorsement

Fox News host Greg Gutfeld has taken aim at the Democratic Party over its response to Rep. Haley Stevens. She endorsed Mike Rogers, the Michigan Republican Senate nominee, and Democrats attacked her for it.

### Gutfeld's Criticism

Gutfeld argued that the reaction shows a party more concerned with protecting its own interests than with respecting the independence of its members. In his framing, the backlash against Stevens reflects an organization that exists for itself rather than for the people it is meant to represent.

### Why It Matters Now

Party loyalty and internal discipline are becoming sharper flashpoints heading into the 2024 election cycle. High-profile defections and cross-party endorsements are drawing strong responses from party organizations. That pattern points to shifting dynamics in how parties police their own members.
