## Pardoned Capitol Riot Participant Arrested Over Threats Against Federal Judges

Cleveland Meredith, who took part in the January 6 Capitol riot and later received a pardon from Trump, has been arrested. He faces charges tied to alleged threats against federal judges who handled cases linked to the riot.

### What he is accused of

Meredith is charged with threatening to execute judges who oversaw January 6 cases. One Facebook post allegedly declared that all January 6 federal judges would be executed. Other posts reportedly called for firing squads once the midterm elections have passed.

### Why it matters now

The case spotlights the consequences of Trump's broad pardons for January 6 defendants. It raises questions about whether some recipients may continue to pose a danger to the judicial system and to public safety. Those concerns carry added weight while tensions over elections and legal accountability remain high.
