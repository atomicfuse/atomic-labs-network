## AP Voter Poll Finds Appetite for Tougher Congressional Oversight of Trump

An AP Voter Poll conducted on election day 2026 shows a restless electorate. Voters expressed deep frustration with the nation's course and a strong desire for Congress to take a firmer hand with the White House.

### Anger Over Direction and Prices

Roughly three-quarters of voters said they feel angry or dissatisfied with where the country is headed. Inflation was a central grievance, and voters largely pointed to Trump's policies as the cause. Around 70% said they want Congress to impose stronger checks on the presidency, signaling a clear preference for more oversight of the president.

### Disapproval Across Key Issues

The poll also found widespread disapproval of Trump's military actions in Iran and of his aggressive approach to immigration enforcement. Congress did not escape criticism, as many voters were unhappy with its overall performance. Because the findings capture opinions as ballots were being cast, they offer a snapshot of public mood heading into possible shifts in congressional power.
