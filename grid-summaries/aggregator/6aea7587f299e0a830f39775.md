## Colorado Repeat Offender Gets Five Years After 100 mph Chase

Zakary Dean Hirschfield, a Colorado man with a record of prior offenses, has been sentenced to five years in prison after a high-speed pursuit that began with a traffic stop and ended with his arrest.

### The Chase

Deputies say Hirschfield led them on a dangerous chase through residential areas at speeds above 100 mph. The pursuit ended when he hid under a porch in his underwear, where he was taken into custody. He later pleaded guilty to felony eluding and received the five-year prison term.

### The Debate Over Release

The district attorney argues Hirschfield should never have been on the street, pointing to earlier court decisions that repeatedly released him. The case has added to frustration among law enforcement and prosecutors over repeat offenders who go free despite several chances for incarceration. It also raises questions about bail reform and court accountability amid ongoing public safety concerns.
