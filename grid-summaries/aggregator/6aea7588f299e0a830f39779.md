## Becerra and Hilton face off in California governor's race

California's 2026 race for governor pits Democrat Xavier Becerra against Republican Steve Hilton. Becerra is a veteran politician and former U.S. health secretary, while Hilton is a Fox News host and political outsider. Trump's unpopularity, the state's cost-of-living crisis, and the question of experience versus change are shaping the contest.

### A contrast in backgrounds

Becerra brings long experience in government to the campaign, and his record anchors the case for steady leadership. Hilton presents himself as an outsider, coming from television rather than elected office. That difference frames a central question for voters: whether to favor a seasoned figure or seek something new, particularly as the high cost of living weighs on the state.

### Why the race matters now

The campaign unfolds as Trump's administration actively challenges California's election integrity and policies. That makes the outcome a barometer for Democratic resistance in the nation's most populous state. It is also a test of whether Republican anti-establishment messaging can reach a heavily Democratic electorate.
