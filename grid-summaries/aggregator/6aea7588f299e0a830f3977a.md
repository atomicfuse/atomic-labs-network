## Learjet Parts Found Miles From Palm Beach Crash Site

The NTSB says it has recovered key control components from a Learjet that went down in Palm Beach, Florida, on Sunday. The crash killed nine people, and the location of the parts is now central to the inquiry.

### What investigators found

Among the recovered items is the aircraft's left elevator, a control surface on the tail. It was scattered between 1.3 and 3.8 miles from where the jet crashed. That distance suggests the part separated from the aircraft before impact, rather than breaking apart when the plane hit the ground.

### What remains unknown

Investigators are now working to establish whether losing the elevator caused the fatal nosedive, or whether the part came off because of extreme forces acting on the aircraft. The finding is regarded as a significant step in understanding what went wrong, and the investigation remains active.
