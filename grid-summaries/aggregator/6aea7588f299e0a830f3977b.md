## Texas Voters Swamped by Record Wave of Political Ads and Texts

Texans are receiving more political ads and text messages than ever during the 2024 election cycle. The surge is tied to the most expensive Senate race in U.S. history, which has seen more than $573 million in ad spending.

### Why Campaigns Are Turning to Texts

Campaigns are sending large numbers of texts and digital ads because they are cheap to produce. Regulations also exempt political messages from Do Not Call requirements, which leaves voters with little ability to opt out of the flood of outreach.

### What the Saturation Means

Political ad saturation is reaching a tipping point as campaigns direct unprecedented budgets into direct messaging channels. The result is voter fatigue, yet the tactics appear effective enough to justify the spending. The trend signals how modern campaigns are moving beyond traditional media and highlights gaps in consumer protection laws.
