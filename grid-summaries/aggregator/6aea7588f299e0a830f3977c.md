## Jones Wants More Balls Thrown to Pickens After Overtime Loss

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones has publicly questioned the team's offensive approach following a 33-30 overtime defeat to Arizona, saying George Pickens should be targeted far more often.

### Usage Under Scrutiny

Pickens was on the field for just 42 of Dallas's 74 snaps and saw only four targets in the game. That is a modest workload for one of the team's top receivers, who is playing on a $27.3 million franchise tag. Jones's comments put the game plan squarely in the spotlight.

### A Struggling Team Looks Inward

Dallas sits at 3-5, and the mismatch between Pickens's salary and his involvement has become a talking point in the locker room and the media. Jones's public criticism suggests friction inside the organization and raises questions about the offensive strategy, and about whether the situation can be repaired.
