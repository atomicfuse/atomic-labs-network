## Christa Pike Seeks Court Order Blocking Return to Old Prison Unit

Christa Pike, a Tennessee death row inmate who survived a failed lethal injection, has filed an emergency motion asking a court to stop her transfer back to the housing block where she was previously held.

### Her Safety Argument

Pike contends that her weakened physical condition leaves her vulnerable to assault. She also argues that the unit houses dangerous inmates with violent histories, and she wants the court to intervene before she is moved there.

### Why the Case Matters

The motion follows the failed execution attempt in September, which has created an unprecedented legal and ethical situation. Her filing raises urgent questions about the safety of prisoners, the protocols used in executions, and what obligations the state has toward inmates who survive an attempt at capital punishment.
