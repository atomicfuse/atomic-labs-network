## Trump Says Tarlov's Voice Drove Him Away From Fox News

President Trump has publicly criticized Fox News host Jessica Tarlov, saying he switched off the network during election night coverage because of her. He made the remarks on Truth Social, and the episode adds to his uneasy history with the channel.

### The Attack on Tarlov

On Truth Social, Trump described Tarlov as "dumb as a rock" and said her voice forced him to turn off Fox News while it covered election night. His complaints were not limited to the post. He also sent text messages to Fox host Greg Gutfeld, objecting to her commentary on inflation and other policy issues.

### Why It Matters Now

The incident reflects Trump's unpredictable relationship with Fox News and his readiness to attack media figures and networks in public. He does so even though he relies on the outlet for coverage and has previously hired people from its roster. The flare-up highlights continuing tension between Trump and the Murdoch-owned network at a critical political moment.
