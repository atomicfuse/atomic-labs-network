## Trump Made the Midterms About Himself, and Voters Took Him Up on It

President Trump set out to turn the midterm elections into a referendum on his own presidency. According to the article, that strategy backfired badly and helped produce a historic Democratic sweep.

### How the Strategy Unraveled

Trump pushed to center the contest on himself through rallies, endorsements, and a steady stream of messaging. The article argues this deliberate approach miscalculated the mood of voters, who responded by delivering a wave of Democratic victories. The result ends the free pass he enjoyed on legislation and leaves his final two years in office looking very different.

### What Comes Next

Congress has flipped, so Trump loses the rubber-stamp Republican support he relied on. That shift arrives as he faces possible legal jeopardy and serious investigations. He also has unfulfilled campaign promises on inflation and energy costs, leaving him with fewer allies and more pressure as the political landscape changes sharply.
