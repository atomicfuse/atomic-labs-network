## Trump Lashes Out at Fox News Over Midterm Coverage

The relationship between Trump and Fox News continues to be a flashpoint in conservative media politics. Any open rift between the two is seen as a sign of shifting dynamics inside the Republican coalition and the wider media landscape as the party looks ahead to 2024.
