## Democrat Bob Harvie Unseats Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in Pennsylvania's 1st District

Democrat Bob Harvie has defeated Republican incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick in Pennsylvania's 1st congressional district, a closely contested suburban seat north of Philadelphia that backed both Kamala Harris and a Republican House member in 2024.

### How the race played out

The contest pitted Fitzpatrick's bipartisan record against Harvie's message about economic strain, including inflation, housing costs and tariffs. Fitzpatrick, for his part, went after Harvie over an FBI grand jury appearance.

### Why the result matters

The flip points to how large Democratic gains were in this cycle. It also shows how economic anxiety and foreign policy, including Trump's Iran tariffs, can sway even moderate Republican-held districts. The outcome reshapes the House map and suggests suburban, highly educated districts remain vulnerable for Republicans, even with their party in control.
