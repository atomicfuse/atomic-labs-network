## Trump credits Democrats and faults GOP candidates after midterm defeats

Republican candidates handpicked by Trump lost heavily in the midterm elections, and Trump has responded by saying Democrats "did a good job" while pointing the finger at his own party's nominees.

### A shift in message

Democrats flipped Senate seats in seven states that Trump carried in 2024. Trump had first presented the vote as a referendum on himself, but afterward he blamed Republican candidates and the way Democrats ran their campaigns. Voters, meanwhile, named his economic policies and his tariff war as major concerns.

### What it means for Washington

With the Senate now in Democratic hands, the party gains the power to investigate and to block Trump's Cabinet and judicial nominees. That change limits his political capital and sets up immediate conflict with Congress as his second term begins.
