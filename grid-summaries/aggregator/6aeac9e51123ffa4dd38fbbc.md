## Democrats Win Congress in Historic Landslide

Democrats have won control of both chambers of Congress in an electoral victory described as a generational landslide and a rebuke of President Trump. The result reshapes the political map.

### How the Wave Unfolded

Democrats flipped long-held Republican Senate seats in Kansas, Texas, and South Carolina. They also swept statewide races in Texas for the first time in 30 years. Voter anger over high gas prices and inflation was the main driver, along with frustration over broken campaign promises on tariffs and foreign wars.

### Why It Matters

This is the largest Democratic wave since the 1974 Watergate backlash. It also defies the usual midterm pattern, in which the sitting president's party loses seats. The outcome signals that economic grievance, especially over prices, outweighed other measures such as unemployment and GDP growth. It sets up a major power shift in governance.
