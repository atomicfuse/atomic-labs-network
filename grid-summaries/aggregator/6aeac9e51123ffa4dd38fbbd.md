## Talarico Flips Texas Senate Seat Blue in Stunning Upset

Democrat James Talarico has defeated Republican Ken Paxton in the Texas Senate race. He is the first Democrat to win a U.S. Senate seat in the state since 1988, ending a long stretch of Republican control.

### How Talarico won

Talarico's campaign leaned on messaging about cost-of-living concerns, and it was boosted by energized Democratic turnout. The combination carried him past Paxton in a contest that few expected him to take, making the result one of the biggest upsets in recent Texas politics.

### What it means for Texas and beyond

The outcome points to a major shift in American politics, especially among Hispanic voters and those frustrated with inflation and Trump's policies. Democrats also made gains nationwide, and Republican Governor Greg Abbott was ousted. Together, these results suggest that Texas, the second-most populous state and a longtime Republican stronghold and policy incubator, is no longer safely red.
