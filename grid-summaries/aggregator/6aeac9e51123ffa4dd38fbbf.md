## Trump links Republican midterm defeat to Iran war

Donald Trump says the Iran war and the fuel price spikes that followed were behind Republicans' midterm losses, even as he defends the decision to go to war.

### Blame and defence

Trump attributes the defeat to the conflict and higher fuel prices, yet maintains the military action was justified and says he would authorize it again. He also argues that Democrats won partly because he was not on the ballot himself, pointing to his absence as another reason for the result.

### A divided Washington

Democrats have now taken control of both chambers of Congress, reshaping the political landscape for the final two years of Trump's presidency. Trump has signalled he will use his veto power to block Democratic legislation. The result marks the start of divided government and sets the stage for major policy battles, with Trump governing as a more constrained executive.
