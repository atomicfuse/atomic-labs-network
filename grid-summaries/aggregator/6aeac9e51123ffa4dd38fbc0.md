## Incoming Oversight Chair Warns Trump Team to "Lawyer Up"

Rep. Robert Garcia, set to chair the House Oversight Committee, has sent a blunt message to President Trump and congressional Republicans after Democrats won control of the House.

### A Warning With Investigations Behind It

Garcia told Trump and Republicans to get lawyers, a signal that Democrats plan aggressive oversight. The targets he pointed to include the Trump administration itself, corporate corruption, alleged abuses by DHS and ICE, and what he described as cover-ups tied to the Jeffrey Epstein files.

### A Shift in Congressional Power

The House majority hands Democrats subpoena power and investigative authority for the first time since Trump took office. That changes the balance of power in Washington and sets the stage for high-stakes hearings. Administration officials could also face potential legal exposure as the committee begins its work.
