## Fayetteville's Election Night Tradition Fades From View

A column looks back on Election Central, the long-running election night gathering that once drew much of Fayetteville's political world together. The event has now become a memory, with its last in-person edition held in 2022.

### A Gathering Place for Results

Election Central began at the courthouse and later moved to the Holiday Inn Bordeaux. Candidates, members of the media and community leaders would assemble there to follow the returns as they were written onto chalkboards and whiteboards. The column treats the event as a kind of who's who of local politics, a place where people could see one another and watch the outcome unfold together in a single room.

### Why the Ritual Declined

As results became available online, there was less reason to attend in person, and crowds steadily thinned. The column presents this as part of a wider change in election night culture, as digital tools replace face-to-face gatherings. It also reflects a moment when local political engagement is more fragmented and increasingly takes place online.
