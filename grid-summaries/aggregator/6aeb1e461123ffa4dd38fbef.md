## Drudge Report's "Orange Crush" Headline Captures GOP Midterm Rout

Conservative-leaning media outlets have framed Republicans' 2026 midterm losses as a direct rebuke of Donald Trump. Among them, one two-word Drudge Report headline stood out as the sharpest summary of the defeat and of who was seen as responsible.

### A Headline That Became Shorthand

Drudge Report's "Orange Crush" quickly became the shorthand for the Republican setback. The phrase pairs the party's electoral defeat with Trump himself, reflecting how the coverage tied the losses to the president rather than treating them as a broader or incidental outcome.

### Why the Backlash Matters

Democrats are projected to regain control of both chambers of Congress, and the midterms are being treated as a major political turning point. What makes the reaction notable is its source: the criticism is coming not only from liberal critics but also from outlets that once supported Trump or often favored him.
