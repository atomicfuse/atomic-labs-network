## Israeli Businessman Extradited to US in Psychic Mail Fraud Case

Mael Chemla, an Israeli businessman, has been extradited from Germany to the United States, where he faces accusations tied to a $4 million psychic fortune-telling scheme. The case is now before a U.S. court.

### What prosecutors allege

Prosecutors say Chemla helped process payments in a mail fraud operation that targeted thousands of Americans. Many of those who received the solicitations were elderly or otherwise vulnerable. Detention and fraud proceedings are now underway, making this an active legal matter as the court begins its work.

### Competing accounts and wider concern

Supporters of Chemla take a different view. They argue he was simply a service provider and say he is being unfairly pursued. The case also reflects growing concern about cross-border scams that exploit older adults through personalized, emotionally manipulative messages, a worry that gives the proceedings added attention beyond the individual defendant.
