## Schoen: Democrats Win Big as Trump and GOP Stumble in 2026 Midterms

Commentator Doug Schoen argues that the 2026 midterms produced a clear victor in the Democratic Party and a clear loser in Donald Trump and the Republican Party, whose standing he describes as having collapsed.

### Backlash more than enthusiasm

Schoen reads the results as voters pushing back rather than rallying behind Democrats. He points to the Iran war, high gas prices, inflation, concerns about corruption, and a combative style of governing as the forces that drove people away from the Republican side.

### A shift in power and a test ahead

Democrats are projected to take control of both chambers of Congress, a major change in the political balance. Republicans now face pressure to reconsider their strategy. For Democrats, the immediate question is whether they can govern effectively while tensions persist between their establishment and progressive wings.
