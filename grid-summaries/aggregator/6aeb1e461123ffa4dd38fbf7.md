## Israel's President Accepts Election Results, Opening Talks on Next Government

Israel's president has accepted the certified results of the national election and is set to start consultations on who is able to form the next government. Neither of the leading contenders emerged with a majority.

### A Divided Result

The vote left both Benjamin Netanyahu and Gadi Eisenkot short of the majority needed to govern outright. Election officials rejected fraud claims made by allies of Netanyahu, clearing the way for the certification to go ahead. The president's consultations will now determine which figure has a realistic path to assembling a coalition.

### What Is at Stake

Israel now enters a tense period of coalition-building that could change the country's political direction or lead to yet another election. The certification also sharpens attention on disinformation, the gains made by Arab-led parties, and the level of public trust in democratic institutions as negotiations begin.
