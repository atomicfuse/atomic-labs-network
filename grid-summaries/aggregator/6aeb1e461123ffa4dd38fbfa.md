## Israel's President Accepts Election Results, Opening Talks on Next Government

Israel's president has accepted the certified election results and will start consultations to determine who is able to form the next government. The step opens a closely watched period of political bargaining after a contested vote.

### Majority lost, fraud claims dismissed

At the center of the story is the fact that Netanyahu's bloc no longer holds a majority. Claims of fraud surrounding the election were rejected, and the certified results stand. With the outcome now official, attention turns away from the ballot count and toward the arithmetic of coalition building, which will largely decide who governs.

### What the talks could decide

The government formation process carries high stakes. Depending on how the coalition math plays out, the result could reshape Netanyahu's political future, increase the influence of Arab-led parties, or push the country toward yet another election. The president's consultations are the first formal move in that process.
