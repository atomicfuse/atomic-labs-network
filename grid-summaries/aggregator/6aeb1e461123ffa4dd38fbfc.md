## Bonta Plans State Observers to Shadow Federal Election Monitors

California Attorney General Rob Bonta says the state will send its own election observers to work alongside monitors from the Department of Justice. He argues that a large federal presence at polling places could intimidate voters, and the dispute centers on election integrity and access to the ballot.

### What California is planning

Bonta's plan is a direct response to the DOJ's push to monitor polling places across the country. The state's observers would be present alongside federal personnel, with the stated aim of protecting voters from intimidation. The disagreement highlights competing views of how best to safeguard elections and keep voting accessible.

### The federal operation behind it

The DOJ is carrying out its largest-ever election monitoring effort, with personnel in all 50 states and specific deployments in major California cities. The clash comes amid legal fights over alleged noncitizen voting and heightened scrutiny of how elections are administered, which has put both federal and state officials under a brighter spotlight.
