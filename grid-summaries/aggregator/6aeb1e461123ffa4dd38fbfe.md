## El-Sayed Uses Victory Speech to Take Aim at Trump, Vance and Rogers

Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan's Senate race and marked the moment with a speech that ridiculed Donald Trump, JD Vance and his Republican opponent, Mike Rogers. He cast the result as a rebuke of identity-based attacks and of Republican messaging.

### What shaped the race

The contest was influenced by affordability concerns, foreign policy debates and progressive politics. El-Sayed framed his victory as a repudiation of the attacks aimed at him, presenting voters' choice as a rejection of the approach Republicans had taken throughout the campaign.

### Why the win matters

The result is a significant progressive breakthrough in a high-stakes Senate contest and a setback for the Republican midterm strategy. His combative tone in the speech suggests he may arrive in the Senate as a prominent voice on culture-war issues and foreign policy, though how he will operate there remains to be seen.
