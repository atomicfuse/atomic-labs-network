## Thune Plans Bid for GOP Minority Leader After Democrats Take Senate

Sen. John Thune plans to run for Republican minority leader, Fox News reports, after Democrats flipped and expanded their control of the Senate in the midterms.

### The Backdrop for Thune's Bid

The report frames his candidacy against a backdrop of Republican losses and frustration over policy in the Trump era. It also points to possible backlash from conservatives who blame Senate leadership for agenda items that stalled.

### Why the Fight Matters Now

Republicans are heading into a leadership contest while they absorb major Senate losses and argue over who is to blame. Disputes touch on Trump's agenda, Iran, immigration, gas prices, and turnout. Thune's move could become a flashpoint in the wider struggle between establishment Senate Republicans and more combative members aligned with Trump.
