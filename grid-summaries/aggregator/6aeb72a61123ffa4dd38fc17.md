## Midterm Wins Raise Canadian Hopes for Tariff Relief

Democratic victories in US midterm races are being welcomed in Canada as a possible opening for relief from President Trump's tariffs. The results may not change policy right away, but they are seen as a potential boost for Ottawa.

### What the results mean

Democrats won in states hit hard by tariffs, including Michigan and Maine, and Canadians are reading those outcomes as a sign of shifting political winds. While the wins may not immediately alter US trade policy, they could strengthen Canada's position as talks between the two countries resume.

### Why the timing matters

Canada and the United States are locked in a high-stakes trade dispute touching autos, steel, aluminum, forestry, dairy, alcohol and other goods. With a new Congress on the way and a possible negotiating window before January, Canadian leaders see a rare chance to push for a better deal.
