## Hancock County Voters Give Sitting DA a Landslide Win

Hancock County's incumbent district attorney has been reelected by a wide margin, winning by roughly three votes to every one cast for the opposing side. The result points to firm voter backing for the current prosecutor.

### A Decisive Verdict

The 3-to-1 margin leaves little doubt about the outcome. Voters in the county chose to keep the current district attorney in office rather than turn to a different leader, and the scale of the win is being read as a sign of strong support for how the prosecutor has led the office so far.

### Why the Result Matters

District attorney races have a direct effect on local criminal justice priorities, including how cases are prosecuted and how public safety policy is shaped. A landslide like this can indicate that a community feels confident in its current direction, or that it has little appetite for change in how the office operates.
