## Poll shows voters wary of socialism even as left-leaning Democrats win

A Fox News Voter Poll finds most registered voters view socialism negatively and capitalism positively. That comes as left-wing Democratic candidates claimed victories in major races.

### How winning candidates framed their message

The article argues that candidates such as Abdul El-Sayed and James Talarico succeeded in part by refusing the socialist label. Instead, they directed their attacks at monopolies, billionaires, and economic inequality, presenting their agendas as pro-worker capitalism. Opponents tried to brand them as radical, yet they still won on Election Day.

### Why the results matter now

The findings point to a growing political lane. Voters may dislike socialism as a brand while still backing aggressive economic populism and tougher regulation. The wins suggest that how a candidate frames an economic agenda can matter as much as its substance, especially after contests where the radical tag failed to stop left-leaning contenders.
