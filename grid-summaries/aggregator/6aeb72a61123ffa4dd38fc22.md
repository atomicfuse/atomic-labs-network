## Court Fight Begins Over Michigan's New Political Donation Limits

Michigan voters have approved Prop 2, known as the "Money out of Politics" initiative, and a legal challenge followed almost immediately. The measure limits political donations from certain powerful interests, and a federal lawsuit now seeks to stop it from being enforced.

### What the measure does

Prop 2 restricts political contributions from regulated utilities, major government contractors, and parties connected to them. Voters passed it quickly on election night. Soon afterward, the Mackinac Center filed a federal lawsuit arguing that the rules violate the First Amendment, and it is asking the court to block enforcement.

### Why the case matters

The initiative's future now depends on a court battle over campaign finance rules, political speech, and donor privacy. The dispute arrives during continuing national tension over how far states can go in limiting the influence of money in politics, so the outcome in Michigan may draw attention well beyond the state.
