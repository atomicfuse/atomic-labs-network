## Southwest Getaways Knocks $250 Off Winter Ski Packages

Southwest's vacation arm, Getaways, is running a promotion aimed at skiers and snowboarders heading into the cold-weather season. The offer trims $250 from select winter ski vacation packages and adds baggage perks.

### What the Deal Includes

Travelers who book a winter ski vacation through Getaways can take $250 off the package price. Savings also come from the baggage policy, which allows two free checked bags per traveler. That allowance covers skis and snowboards, so gear can travel without the extra charges some trips involve.

### Why It Matters Now

Winter travel planning is picking up, and many people are searching for ways to keep ski trip costs down. Baggage and gear fees can add up quickly on these vacations, so a package discount combined with free checked bags may appeal to those budgeting for the season.
