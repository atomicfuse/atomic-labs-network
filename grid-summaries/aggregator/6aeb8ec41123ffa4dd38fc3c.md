## Royal Caribbean Rolls Out Cyber Season Sale With Up to $1,000 Off

Royal Caribbean has announced a Cyber Season promotion that will run from November 6 to December 7, offering travelers a range of discounts on select cruises.

### What the Sale Includes

Shoppers can save up to $1,000 on chosen sailings during the promotional window. The deal also features free fares for additional guests, onboard credits to spend during the trip, and reduced prices on add-ons such as drinks and excursions. Together, the perks are designed to lower both the upfront cost of a booking and the spending that often comes with a cruise vacation.

### Why the Timing Matters

Cyber-themed offers are becoming an important booking window for people planning vacations in 2025 and 2026. Cruise lines are competing to capture early holiday spending, and this sale gives budget-conscious travelers a reason to lock in plans before the year ends. The window closes December 7, so interested customers have a defined period to act.
