## A Smarter Booking Timeline for Your January Getaway

The advice starts with flights, which should be booked in advance rather than left to the last minute. Anyone eyeing Martin Luther King Jr. weekend is urged to lock in plans as soon as possible. For hotels and rental cars, the article recommends choosing refundable rates. Even with January's deal-friendly reputation, some options can still sell out or jump in price. Warm-weather escapes, holiday weekends, and popular cruises are the main examples.
