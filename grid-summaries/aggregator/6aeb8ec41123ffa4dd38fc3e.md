## Scenic Group Takes Its Luxury Cruises to CNBC in New 10-Part Series

Scenic Group is bringing its high-end river and ocean voyages to television with a new 10-part series on CNBC. The show premieres on November 8 and is designed to showcase what the company offers to travelers who want a premium experience on the water.

### What viewers will see

The series spotlights Scenic's luxury river and ocean cruise experiences across several destinations. Those include Europe, Antarctica, the Caribbean and the Mediterranean, giving audiences a broad look at the range of journeys the brand provides. Episodes will also be available on YouTube, so people can watch outside the television schedule.

### Why the approach matters

Luxury travel brands are increasingly turning to premium video storytelling to inspire bookings and to connect with audiences beyond traditional travel advertising. By pairing a CNBC broadcast with on-demand viewing on YouTube, Scenic's campaign draws on both the credibility of television and the ease of online discovery.
