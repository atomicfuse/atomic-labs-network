## Fora Adds Built-In Cruise Booking for Its Travel Advisors

Fora has rolled out integrated cruise booking tools inside its travel advisor platform. The addition is meant to make selling cruises simpler and faster for the advisors who use the service.

### What the new tools do

Advisors can now compare, quote, and book cruises from within the platform, with access to 55 cruise lines. The company presents the move as an efficiency upgrade, one that supports its fast-growing cruise sales and keeps more of the selling process in a single place.

### Why the timing matters

Cruise demand remains strong, and advisors are looking for tools that cut friction when selling complex itineraries. Consolidating features on one platform is increasingly seen as a competitive advantage, as agencies race to modernize their booking workflows and handle growing interest in cruises.
