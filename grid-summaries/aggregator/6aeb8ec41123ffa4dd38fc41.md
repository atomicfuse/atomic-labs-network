## MSC Poesia Sailing Scrapped After Coast Guard Detains Ship in Miami

MSC Cruises has called off a planned MSC Poesia voyage departing from Miami. The cancellation followed the ship's detention by the U.S. Coast Guard, which cited regulatory issues.

### What passengers can expect

Travelers booked on the canceled sailing are being offered full refunds along with assistance from the cruise line. The company has not been described as rescheduling the trip in the details available, so refunds are the main remedy on the table for those affected.

### Why the timing matters

Cruise disruptions draw close attention, especially when travelers count on smooth holiday and winter-season sailings from major ports such as Miami.
