## Wirecutter Distills 44 Travel Tips From Years of Testing

Wirecutter has published a collection of 44 practical travel tips, drawn from its journalists' years of gear testing and reporting. The advice is meant to help people get ready for trips with less hassle.

### What the Tips Cover

The guidance centers on four areas: packing more smartly, choosing better luggage, preparing technology and travel documents, and making flights more comfortable. Rather than offering a single checklist, the piece pulls together lessons the team has gathered while evaluating products and covering travel topics over time.

### Why Preparation Matters Now

Travel is booming again, and the conditions around it are demanding. Fees are rising, airports are crowded, carry-on rules are stricter, and using a phone or data abroad can be a headache. Those pressures make advance planning more valuable. The collection also fits a current appetite, including among creators, for practical systems that reduce travel stress.
