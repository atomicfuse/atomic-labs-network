## Three Google Flights tweaks to help cut holiday airfare costs

A new article lays out three tactics on Google Flights for finding cheaper holiday flights. Its central point is that small changes to how you search can surface lower fares that standard booking flows often hide.

### The three tactics

The first tip is to use the calendar view, which lets travelers compare prices across different dates instead of fixing on one day. The second is to check the "Cheapest" tab, which brings the lowest-priced options to the front of the results. The third is to set price alerts, so you can track a route and get notified as fares change rather than checking repeatedly by hand.

### Why it matters now

Holiday travel is one of the most expensive and stressful times to book, and prices can shift quickly as demand spikes. Travelers are looking for simple ways to avoid overpaying without spending hours comparing different sites. These tactics are presented as easy steps that fit that need.
