## Mother and Daughter Killed in Bella Coola Grizzly Attack, Officials Say

Canadian authorities are investigating a fatal grizzly attack in Bella Coola that killed art therapist Jenny Balcarras and her daughter, Selah Reed. Officials say more than one bear may have been involved.

### Investigation and Community Response

Authorities are working to determine exactly what happened, and efforts to capture the bears are continuing. The community is grieving the loss of the two women. Local concern has also grown over the possibility of repeated conflict between people and bears in the area, as residents and officials watch for further risks while the investigation moves forward.

### Why It Matters Now

The attack comes less than a year after another serious grizzly incident in Bella Coola, which has renewed public worry about safety in bear country. It also arrives amid broader questions about habitat loss, drought and climate pressure, and the increasing overlap between people and wildlife. These issues are drawing fresh attention as the community mourns and authorities continue their work.
