## Tennessee Midterms Show a Blue Tilt Despite Republican Wins

A WKNO political analysis argues that Tennessee's 2026 midterms revealed a notable Democratic shift, even though Republicans won the major contests.

### What the Results Showed

The analysis points to Marsha Blackburn's governor's race, which was closer than expected, and to gerrymandered congressional districts that produced narrow outcomes. It also notes a surprise blue flip in District 4. The piece frames these results as evidence that even voters in deep-red Tennessee may be souring on Trump-aligned state leadership.

### Why It Matters Now

Tennessee has long been treated as reliably ruby red, but tighter margins suggest its political map may be less settled than many assumed. With 2028 approaching, turnout, backlash over redistricting, and shifts in suburban and rural areas could become major storylines in the state's politics.
