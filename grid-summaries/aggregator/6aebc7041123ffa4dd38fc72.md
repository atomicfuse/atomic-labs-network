## Record El Niño Fuels Wave of Powerful Typhoons Across East Asia

A record-warm El Niño is energising the Pacific and driving stronger, more unusual typhoons across East Asia. Countries from Taiwan and Japan to China and the Philippines are affected.

### How the Ocean Is Changing Storms

The article argues that several shifts are combining to raise disaster risks across the region. Warmer seas provide more fuel for storms, wind patterns are changing, and typhoons now have longer windows in which to develop. Together, these factors are making the storms stronger and less predictable than usual.

### Why Preparation Is Urgent

This El Niño is forecast to rank among the strongest in living memory. Pacific sea surface temperatures have reached record highs, and typhoon energy is projected at nearly double the 30-year average. Governments are already scrambling to prepare for more storms, heavier rainfall, evacuations and disruption to food production.
