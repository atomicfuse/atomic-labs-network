## Fox News Era Fades as Online Entrepreneurs Reshape the Right

A new analysis argues that Republican politics, long organized around Fox News, is giving way to a different structure. In its place, it says, is an ecosystem of online political and financial entrepreneurs.

### A change in how influence is organized

The piece presents the shift as a major one, touching how conservative influence, messaging, and money are arranged. Rather than flowing mainly through a single dominant cable network, those elements are increasingly tied to a wider group of internet-based figures who build their own audiences and operations.

### Why attention is moving power

Media consumption is moving away from cable television and toward platforms, podcasts, newsletters, and creator-led networks. Political power, the story contends, tends to follow attention. That means the next phase of Republican politics may be shaped less by legacy gatekeepers and more by operators who are native to the internet and who understand how to work within these newer spaces.
