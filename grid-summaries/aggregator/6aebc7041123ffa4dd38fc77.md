## Arizona Democrats Cheer Gains as Republicans Warn of Sharp Shift

Democrats in Arizona are celebrating major wins in statewide offices, while Republicans express dismay over a changing political landscape. Democrats may also be on track to control both legislative chambers for the first time in 60 years.

### Democratic Priorities

Democrats say they plan to focus on affordability, education, water and access to elections. Party members are describing the results as a "blue tsunami," pointing to the scale of the gains and the opportunity they create to set the policy agenda across state government if the outcome is confirmed.

### Republican Concerns and Stakes

Republicans, for their part, are cautioning that the state could move sharply in a different ideological direction. Arizona has long been a key battleground, so the shift signals a possible realignment in its politics. If confirmed, unified Democratic control could reshape policy after decades in which Republicans held significant influence over the state's direction.
