## Text Reminders Boost Bowel Cancer Screening in London

A University of Surrey-led study has found that simple automated messages and targeted phone calls can get more Londoners to complete bowel cancer screening after they first fail to return their test kits.

### What the study found

Automated text reminders tripled uptake among people who had not returned their kits, and they did so at very low cost. Targeted phone support also made a significant difference, proving especially effective for men. The findings suggest that inexpensive follow-up steps can nudge people who might otherwise skip screening.

### Why it matters

London's screening rate stands at 63.8%, eight points below the national average. Early detection greatly improves outcomes, and bowel cancer remains one of the biggest cancer killers in the UK. Low-cost interventions like these could help narrow a major public health gap in the capital.
