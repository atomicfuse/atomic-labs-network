## Pediatricians Flag Vaping Dangers but Struggle to Help Teens Quit

A new survey from Australia suggests pediatricians widely see teen vaping as both addictive and risky. Yet many say they cannot easily turn that concern into practical help for young patients who want to stop.

### A gap between warning and treatment

Doctors in the survey broadly recognized the harms of adolescent vaping, but many reported lacking referral pathways, time, and resources to support quitting. Only one in five said they provided cessation support. That points to a divide between cautioning teens about vaping and actually guiding them toward treatment.

### Why the findings matter now

Youth vaping remains a major public health concern, and schools, parents, and clinicians are seeking practical responses that go beyond awareness campaigns. The survey's findings highlight a timely need for clearer clinical pathways and accessible quit supports designed specifically for adolescents, so that concern can translate into real help.
