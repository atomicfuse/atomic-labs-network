## Experts say fireworks pose an underrated public health threat

Writing in The BMJ, experts caution that fireworks are a hazard to public health that has not been taken seriously enough. They describe the harm as largely preventable.

### The dangers they describe

The authors point to injuries such as burns, blindness, and amputations, along with deaths. Beyond individual harm, they say fireworks also contribute to fires, pollution, and hazards in the workplace. To reduce the toll, they call for stronger regulation, better tracking of injuries, and public education campaigns that continue over time rather than appearing only briefly.

### Why the issue is pressing

Sales of fireworks to consumers have surged, while regulations remain inconsistent from place to place. Injury rates are rising where rules are looser. Major celebrations bring seasonal spikes in harm, and the experts suggest this makes the public health argument for safer alternatives increasingly difficult to dismiss.
