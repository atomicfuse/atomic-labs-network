## Montana Voters Back "The Montana Plan" to Reshape Political Money

Montana voters have approved Initiative 194, known as "The Montana Plan," a measure that changes how money can be used in state politics. Supporters framed it as a way to rein in corporate influence and dark money.

### What the measure does

Initiative 194 aims to curb the role of corporate spending and untraceable funds in state campaigns. It is also designed to push candidates toward engaging more directly with voters, rather than relying on outside money and messaging to carry their campaigns.

### Why the timing matters

The law takes effect in January 2027, which sets up a significant shift in how campaigns in Montana are run in future elections. Its passage comes amid broader public frustration with political ads, outside spending, and opaque influence in elections. Candidates and campaigns now have time to prepare for the new landscape before the rules apply.
