## Sterling Climbs to Its Strongest Level Since the Brexit Vote

The pound has reached its highest overall level since the 2016 Brexit referendum, marking a notable milestone for the UK currency. Several factors are lifting it, according to the article, and attention is now turning to what the move means for the wider economy.

### What is driving the rise

Four main influences are credited with pushing sterling higher. Markets expect an interest rate rise, UK growth looks stronger, and the political climate is seen as stable. Relations with the EU have also become warmer, which has added to confidence in the currency.

### Why it matters now

The Bank of England is expected to raise interest rates for the first time in three years. Meanwhile, households are still under pressure from energy costs, and consumer confidence remains shaky. A stronger pound could help ease inflation and boost purchasing power, particularly when buying imports or travelling abroad.
