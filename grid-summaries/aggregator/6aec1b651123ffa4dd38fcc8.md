## Jeffries Draws Fire Over Response to Trump Impeachment Question

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries is facing criticism after declining to discuss impeaching President Trump. He said his caucus is concentrating on affordability and accountability instead.

### The Backlash

Critics say his answer misreads the mood among Democratic voters, who they argue want firmer confrontation, stronger oversight, and real consequences. They point to the party's major wins in the 2026 elections as evidence that the base expects a more aggressive posture, not a cautious pivot away from the question.

### Why the Moment Matters

Democrats are positioning themselves to lead the House after a significant electoral rebuke of Trump's agenda, which makes their governing strategy a flashpoint. The dispute reflects a wider tension within the party between kitchen-table messaging focused on costs and a base demanding aggressive accountability. How Jeffries balances those pressures may shape how the party presents itself going forward.
