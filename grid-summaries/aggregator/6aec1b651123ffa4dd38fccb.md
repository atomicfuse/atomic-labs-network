## North Korea Reports New Air Defense Missile Test and Cruise Missile Drill

North Korea says it tested a new air defense missile system and carried out a cruise missile strike drill against a virtual airport target. The announcement adds to a run of recent launches that has kept attention on the Korean Peninsula.

### What Pyongyang Says It Tested

According to North Korea's account, the air defense missile test and the cruise missile drill were separate demonstrations of its growing arsenal. The report points to a focus on better guidance and target tracking. It also highlights possible cluster-warhead capabilities, suggesting the country wants weapons able to hit targets in more than one way. The airport target used in the cruise missile exercise was virtual.

### Why It Matters Now

This is North Korea's fourth weapons launch in a month, which heightens regional security concerns. The new air defense system also appears to resemble Russia's S-400. That similarity raises questions about possible technology transfer and a deepening military alignment between the two countries.
