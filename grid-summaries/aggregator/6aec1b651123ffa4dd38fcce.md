## Bob Dylan Meets Pope Leo XIV at the Vatican

The encounter drew attention to the common ground between the two men. Dylan is known for songwriting that speaks out against war, and Pope Leo has himself referred to Dylan's work. That overlap gave the short audience a cultural resonance beyond the exchange of presents.
