## Paris Jackson Seeks $600M Transfer in Dispute Over Michael Jackson's Estate

Paris Jackson has asked a Los Angeles court to move $600 million from her father Michael Jackson's estate into the family trust. Her request sets her against John Branca, the estate's longtime executor, in a escalating legal fight.

### What Paris Jackson alleges

Paris Jackson accuses Branca of mismanagement, retaliation, and improper spending. Her petition asks the court to shift the large sum out of the estate and into the trust that benefits the family. The estate rejects the claims. It argues that it has generated billions of dollars, has already distributed major funds, and remains limited by legal and tax issues that affect what it can do.

### Why the timing matters

Michael Jackson's estate is still among the most lucrative celebrity estates ever, and projects such as the biopic keep his legacy commercially active. A Dec. 4 court hearing could influence how control, money, and legacy rights are handled for Jackson's children.
