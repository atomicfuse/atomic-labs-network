## DeSantis Says Unfinished Conservative Agenda Cost Republicans at the Polls

Ron DeSantis has offered his explanation for the Republican setback in the midterms, pointing to the party's own record rather than outside forces. His remarks arrive as the GOP absorbs a major electoral loss.

### Blaming a Stalled Agenda

DeSantis argues that Republicans lost ground because they did not pass aggressive conservative legislation, with immigration singled out as a key example. He describes the Democratic sweep as a rebuke of the party's Trump-era leadership. In his telling, it also reflects frustrated Republican voters who chose to stay home instead of turning out.

### A Party Debate Takes Shape

The comments put DeSantis back in the national conversation as Republicans begin positioning for 2028. They also point to an internal fight over how the party should respond to the defeat: whether to moderate its approach or move further to the right.
