## Kenyan Pharmacists Sound Alarm Over Viral Allergy Pill Trend

Pharmacists in Kenya are cautioning women against a social media trend that promotes inserting cetirizine allergy tablets into the vagina for supposed "tightening." Experts describe the practice as unsafe and untested.

### The health concerns

Specialists say there is no evidence the method delivers any real tightening. Instead, they warn it could lead to dryness, irritation, injury, or infection. Cetirizine is an allergy medicine, and using it this way falls outside its intended purpose, which adds to worries about unpredictable harm to users who follow the advice they see online.

### Demand and official response

The trend has circulated widely on TikTok and has reportedly pushed up demand and prices for cetirizine in Nairobi and other areas, with possible shortages also reported. Kenyan health authorities are now considering how to respond to medicine misuse driven by viral misinformation, as pressure grows to address the spread of such claims online.
