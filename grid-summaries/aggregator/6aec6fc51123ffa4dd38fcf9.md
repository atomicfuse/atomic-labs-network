## Judge Lets Crichton Estate Pursue Lawsuit Over The Pitt's Likeness to ER

A US court has decided that HBO Max's *The Pitt* resembles *ER* closely enough for a lawsuit against Warner Bros. to move forward. The suit was brought by the estate of Michael Crichton.

### The core dispute

At the heart of the case is a question about whether *The Pitt* amounts to an uncredited reboot of *ER*. The Crichton estate argues that if so, the show would violate contractual rights held by Crichton. The ruling does not settle that question; it simply allows the estate to keep pressing its claim.

### Why it matters now

Studios are leaning heavily on reboots, legacy properties and so-called spiritual successors. This case could influence how closely a new series may echo an older hit without permission or payment. It also arrives during wider industry arguments over creator rights, estates, credits and ownership.
