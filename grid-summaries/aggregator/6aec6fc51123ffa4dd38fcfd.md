## France Prepares for Possible Record Day of Protests Amid Economic Anger

France is gearing up for what could turn into its biggest day of street demonstrations in years, with frustration over the economy fuelling the unrest.

### What authorities expect

Officials are anticipating a very large turnout. Their estimates put the number of protesters somewhere between 500,000 and 700,000 people. Preparations are under way for a sizeable wave of demonstrations, reflecting how widespread the discontent has become and how seriously the authorities are treating the scale of the planned mobilization.

### Why it matters

The expected turnout points to growing public anger over economic pressure. If crowds reach the levels authorities are bracing for, the day could stand out as one of the most significant mobilizations France has seen in years. That makes it an important moment for gauging how deep the frustration runs among people across the country.
