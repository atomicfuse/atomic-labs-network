## Court of Appeal to review sentence for killer of Henry Nowak

Senior judges are reconsidering the sentence handed to Jaswant Singh Digwa for the murder of Henry Nowak. The review pits two opposing arguments against each other, with both sides challenging the original outcome.

### Competing arguments over the sentence

The Attorney General argues that the sentence was too lenient. Digwa's lawyers take the opposite view and say it was too long. Part of the legal debate centres on aggravating factors, which can affect how severe a sentence should be. Nowak's family has expressed anger over the case. The killing involved a Sikh kirpan dagger.

### Why the review matters

The judges are weighing the case now, making it a live test of how courts respond to public concern over serious violent crime while keeping sentencing fair. It also touches on sensitive issues, including religious exemptions, knife crime, the impact on victims and their families, and the right of offenders to appeal.
