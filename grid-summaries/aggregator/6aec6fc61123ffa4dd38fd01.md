## St. Louis Recycling Drive Gives Campaign Yard Signs a Second Life

With the election behind us, many St. Louis-area households are wondering what to do with leftover political yard signs. A post-election recycling drive offers a local answer.

### How the drive works

The Missouri Botanical Garden, Republic Services, and OneSTL organized the drive for the region. Both the signs and their metal frames can be recycled, but they must be separated before drop-off. Residents should also know that these items cannot go into regular residential recycling bins.

### Why the timing matters

As households clear campaign signs from their yards, disposal becomes an immediate question. The drive provides a practical, local option that helps keep thousands of pounds of campaign materials out of landfills. Separating signs from frames is the key step for anyone planning to take part.
