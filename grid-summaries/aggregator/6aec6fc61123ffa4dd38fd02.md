## Harris Urges Democrats to Be Ruthless After Projected Midterm Sweep

Democratic leaders are weighing in on how to wield power after a projected sweep in the 2026 midterms. Kamala Harris, Hillary Clinton and Bill Clinton each offered a different view of what comes next.

### Harris Calls for an Aggressive Approach

Harris urged Democrats to use their power aggressively, pressing for economic relief for voters and for accountability for Donald Trump. Her message treats the projected win as a mandate to act forcefully rather than cautiously. Hillary Clinton framed the outcome as the start of a "post-Trump era," signaling a turning point in national politics.

### Bill Clinton Favors Conciliation

Bill Clinton took a more conciliatory tone, calling for a focus on rebuilding trust and addressing costs, health care and debt, alongside accountability. The contrast highlights an early debate within the party over whether to govern through confrontation, accountability or moderation, with Trump-era politics still shaping Democratic priorities and messaging.
