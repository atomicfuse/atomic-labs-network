## Serbia Arrests Ten Current and Former Police Officers Over Alleged Protester Abuse

Serbian authorities have detained ten officers accused of abusing and torturing demonstrators during last year's anti-corruption protests. Nine of those arrested are serving officers, and one is a former member of the force.

### What the allegations involve

The officers are accused of mistreating protesters in a garage inside a government compound in Belgrade. The case links police accountability directly to the student-led movement that helped bring an end to Aleksandar Vucic's 12-year presidency, giving the arrests significant political weight beyond the criminal allegations themselves.

### Why the timing matters

Serbia is currently in a post-election transition, and protesters are pressing for reform of its institutions. The EU has signaled that accession talks could be renewed if democracy is strengthened. The arrests may therefore become a major test of whether the new government is willing to confront alleged abuses by security forces.
