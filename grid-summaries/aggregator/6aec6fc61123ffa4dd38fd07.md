## Lee Carter: Persuasion, Not a Party, Won the Midterms

Writer Lee Carter says the biggest winner of the 2026 midterms was not a candidate or a party. In his view, the real victor is persuasion itself, the idea that voters can still be won over.

### The Argument

Carter contends that voters are not permanently bound to partisan identities. He argues that campaigns can still change minds when they connect with the real concerns people have, rather than assuming loyalties are fixed from the start.

### Why It Matters Now

Years of talk about polarization have shaped how many people view elections. Surprising results in states such as Michigan, Texas and Kansas suggest that political coalitions may be more fluid than assumed. The point is timely as parties and creators examine what the midterms reveal about independents, crossover voters and what motivates people to turn out.
