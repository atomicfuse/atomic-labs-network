## Scarborough Fires Back at Vance Over Midterm Loss Explanation

Joe Scarborough has sharply criticized JD Vance for pinning Republican midterm losses on the Iran war. The broadcaster dismissed that explanation as ignorant and argued it misreads what happened at the ballot box.

### Scarborough's Argument

Scarborough contended that the defeats had other roots. He pointed to Vance's own rhetoric, the politics surrounding immigration, the party's economic messaging, and Republican failures in several key states. In his view, blaming a foreign conflict lets the party avoid looking at its own choices and its own conduct during the campaign.

### Why It Matters Now

The exchange comes as Republicans absorb major losses and trade blame, while possible 2028 contenders work to reposition themselves. It also reflects wider voter frustration over affordability, immigration, corruption, and political accountability, issues that continue to shape how each side interprets the results and what lessons it draws.
