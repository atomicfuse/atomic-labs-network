## Harmeet Dhillon Steps Down as Head of DOJ Civil Rights Division

Harmeet Dhillon is leaving her post leading the Justice Department's Civil Rights Division. She says she decided to go weeks before Republican losses in the midterms, and that the White House asked her to hold off on announcing it until after Election Day.

### A Conservative Overhaul

Dhillon's time in the role is framed as a major conservative reworking of the division's priorities. Under her leadership, its focus covered DEI, election integrity, religious liberty and women's sports, marking a sharp shift in how the division defines its mission.

### Why the Timing Matters

The resignation arrives right after Republican midterm defeats, at a moment when congressional scrutiny of Trump administration officials is likely to intensify. It also underscores the continuing dispute over what civil rights enforcement should prioritize, a question that can change considerably from one administration to the next.
