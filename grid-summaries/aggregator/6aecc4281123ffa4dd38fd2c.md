## Harmeet Dhillon Steps Down From DOJ Civil Rights Division

Harmeet Dhillon has resigned as head of the Justice Department's Civil Rights Division. She is the first senior DOJ official to leave since Democrats swept the midterm elections.

### A Controversial Tenure

The article describes her time leading the division as a MAGA-aligned overhaul. That period saw many staff departures and lawsuits tied to elections. It also brought fewer hate-crime prosecutions and rollbacks of police reform efforts. Supporters and critics of her approach have sharply different views of what the changes meant for the division's work.

### Why the Timing Matters

Her exit comes during a political transition, with fresh attention on how the Justice Department might change course after Democratic gains. It also reopens a wider argument over whether civil rights enforcement was weakened or turned into a political tool under Trump-era leadership.
