## SpaceX and Tesla Slide as AI Stocks Sell Off After Democratic Wins

Shares in SpaceX, Tesla and major AI-linked technology companies dropped sharply after Democratic victories in the midterms. Investors worried that the results could bring tougher scrutiny of data centres and AI infrastructure.

### Investor anxiety grows

The article argues the sell-off reflects rising unease that the AI boom could run into political, community and return-on-investment headwinds. Rather than a one-off reaction, it frames the fall as a sign that confidence in the sector's rapid expansion is being tested.

### Data centres become a political issue

Opposition to data centres has turned into a live issue for both politics and markets, particularly after Democrats won in key states such as Texas. As AI companies race to build more infrastructure, they must now persuade local communities as well as investors that their plans are worthwhile.
