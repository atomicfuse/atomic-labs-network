## Paraguay's President Peña Steps Down Over Ueno Bank Collapse

Santiago Peña has resigned as president of Paraguay following a political crisis set off by the failure of Ueno Bank, a lender tied to him through shareholdings he held in the past.

### What led to the resignation

The collapse of Ueno Bank left accounts frozen for millions of people, prompting public protests and mounting pressure for impeachment. The article presents Peña's departure as an effort to preserve stability in the country while the situation unfolded. His past link to the bank placed him at the center of the anger over the failure.

### Why it matters now

The crisis brings together political trust, financial stability and people's access to their savings. Ueno Bank reportedly served a very large share of Paraguay's adult population, which magnifies the impact of the freeze. Peña's exit also creates sudden uncertainty for the ruling Colorado Party and for the country's democratic institutions.
