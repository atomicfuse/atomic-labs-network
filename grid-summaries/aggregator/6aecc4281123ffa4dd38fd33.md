## Hoax Bomb Threats Disrupt Over 20 European Flights in a Single Week

A wave of hoax bomb threats has disrupted more than 20 flights across Europe this week, leaving travellers facing delays and prompting emergency responses in the skies.

### What happened

The threats, which turned out to be false, caused operational chaos for air travel across the continent. Passengers faced delays as flights were affected, and fighter jets were scrambled in response to some of the incidents. In total, more than 20 flights were disrupted over the course of the week.

### Why it matters now

Air travel security is under heightened scrutiny as repeated false threats put pressure on airlines, airports, passengers and national defence resources. The scale and coordination of the disruptions make this a timely concern for European travel and public safety, raising questions about how well the system can absorb such repeated strain.
