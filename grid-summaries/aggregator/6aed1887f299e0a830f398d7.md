## South and Southeast Asia Urged to Claim Bigger Role in Pandemic Rulemaking

An article examining negotiations over the Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing annex argues that South and Southeast Asia should have greater influence over how pathogen sharing, benefits, and health sovereignty are handled in future rules.

### What the Article Argues

The piece centres on the annex talks and makes the case that the region must be more assertive at the table. It says rules governing how pathogens are shared and how benefits flow back should reflect the interests of South and Southeast Asian countries, including their claim to control over health matters within their own borders.

### Why the Timing Matters

Pandemic preparedness frameworks are currently being rewritten, and the article warns that the region could stay reliant on external powers for vaccines, diagnostics, and medical countermeasures. It presents global health governance as an increasingly important arena where equity, sovereignty, and geopolitical power are contested, making the current debate especially timely.
