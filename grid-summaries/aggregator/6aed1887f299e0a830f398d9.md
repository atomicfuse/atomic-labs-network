## King Charles embraces a politically charged Caribbean tour, aide says

A senior Palace aide says the King relishes taking on sensitive diplomatic duties for the UK government. His Caribbean tour, shaped by debates over slavery reparations, apologies and republican feeling, shows this approach in action.

### A "secret weapon" in diplomacy

The aide describes Charles as a "secret weapon" for the government, suggesting he actively welcomes politically charged statecraft rather than merely tolerating it. The visit unfolded against a backdrop of calls for reparatory justice and for constitutional change in the region, making it a diplomatically delicate undertaking.

### Why the tour matters now

The monarchy faces growing Caribbean pressure on reparations and on the future of its constitutional ties, while the UK government's stance on apologising appears to be shifting. Charles is still defining his reign, with health concerns and post-Brexit diplomacy in the background. In that context, royal tours are increasingly becoming tests of Britain's soft power.
