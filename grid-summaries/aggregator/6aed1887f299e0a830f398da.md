## Young Granite Staters Are Stepping Up as Lawmakers, Not Just Voters

A profile of several Gen-Z state representatives in New Hampshire looks at what pulled them into politics and what they say they contribute to public office.

### Why They Ran

The article describes a mix of motivations among these young lawmakers. Some were drawn by a desire to affect their own communities directly, while others focused on representation, civics education, or advocacy at the local level. Its central argument is that younger legislators bring lived experience, comfort with digital tools, and a fresh sense of urgency to state government.

### Why It Matters Now

Gen-Z is increasingly appearing on ballots as candidates, not only casting votes. That shift is especially notable in local and state races, where one person's participation can carry outsized weight. As debates over education, housing, LGBTQ+ rights, and civic engagement intensify, youth representation is becoming a practical political force.
