## Canada's 2026 budget channels oil windfall into sovereignty and housing

Canada's federal government has unveiled its 2026 budget, which draws on a windfall in oil revenue to pay for C$142bn in new spending. Prime Minister Mark Carney is presenting it as a way to reduce reliance on the United States while easing financial strain at home.

### What the budget targets

The new money is aimed at several priorities: sovereignty, affordability, homelessness, housing, and diversifying trade. It also supports industries that have been hit by tariffs. Together, the measures are meant to strengthen Canada's economic independence and respond to pressures on household budgets.

### Why the timing matters

Canadians are dealing with high housing costs, record food bank use, wage pressure, and uncertainty caused by US tariffs under Donald Trump. Voters are looking for tangible relief, and the budget arrives as Ottawa tries to redefine Canada's economic independence and its stance in the world.
