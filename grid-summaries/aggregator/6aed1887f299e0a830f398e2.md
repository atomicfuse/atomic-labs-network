## Belichick Back in the Hall of Fame Mix as a 2027 Finalist

Bill Belichick has moved forward once more as one of five coaching finalists for the Pro Football Hall of Fame's 2027 class, following his controversial omission on the first ballot in 2026.

### A Résumé That Fuels the Debate

The argument for Belichick rests on his record. He won six Super Bowls as head coach of the Patriots and two more as defensive coordinator of the Giants. With that many titles, his continued wait has become one of the NFL's biggest talking points, and the case for his induction is a central part of the discussion.

### A Test for the New Process

The Hall of Fame changed its selection process after the snub, so Belichick's candidacy now serves as a test of whether the new system can restore confidence. His legacy conversation also overlaps with his current job coaching North Carolina, an unexpected college football chapter.
