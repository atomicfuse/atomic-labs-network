## Colom's Viral Ads Put Mississippi's Senate Race in the Spotlight

Democrat Scott Colom's creator-style campaign videos have helped turn Mississippi's Senate contest against Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith into an unexpected battleground. Ballots are still being counted, and Colom is narrowly ahead.

### A Race With Historic Stakes

If Colom's lead holds, Mississippi would elect its first Democratic senator in more than 40 years. He would also become the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction. The article argues that short-form political content may be influencing a razor-thin result, and that possibility is drawing attention well beyond the state.

### Campaigning in the Feed Era

The race also reflects a wider change in how campaigns reach voters. Memes, movie references, and ads built for specific platforms can now outperform traditional endorsements. Colom's approach is presented as an example of that shift, and opinions on how much his viral ads really mattered are still coming in alongside the votes.
