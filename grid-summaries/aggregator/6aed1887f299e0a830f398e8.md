## Delhi Braces for Toxic Winter Air With New Action Plan

Delhi is heading into its most polluted season, and the government has rolled out a Winter Action Plan to limit the damage. Experts see progress but doubt it goes far enough.

### What the plan includes

The plan seeks to cut emissions through vehicle restrictions, construction bans, work-from-home measures and tighter enforcement of the rules. Experts say it arrives earlier and is better structured than previous responses. However, they argue it still falls short because it lacks long-term emission cuts that reach across the wider region and all major polluting sectors.

### Why the stakes are high

Colder weather, smoke from crop burning, traffic, construction dust and festival firecrackers combine to make the air hazardous at this time of year. Residents face serious health risks and daily disruption as a result. The season also serves as a major test of the government's pollution promises, showing whether its new measures can deliver real relief.
