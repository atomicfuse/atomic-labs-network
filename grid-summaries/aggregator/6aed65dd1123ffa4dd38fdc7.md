## New Documentary Will Trace BioWare's Journey From Edmonton Start to Closure

A film called "Massive Effect: The BioWare Story" is currently in production, looking back at the history of the celebrated RPG developer.

### The Studio's Story

The documentary follows BioWare from its beginnings as an Edmonton studio founded by doctors to its position as one of the most influential names in role-playing games. It will also cover the studio's final chapter, after Electronic Arts announced that BioWare would close.

### Why the Timing Matters

BioWare's closure signals the end of a significant era for narrative-driven RPGs, which gives the project added weight for longtime fans and the wider games industry. Because filming was already underway before the shutdown, the documentary may provide a rare behind-the-scenes view of a studio's legacy as it comes to an end.
