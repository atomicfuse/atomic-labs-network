## Democrats Dominate Minnesota Vote as Republicans Regroup in Minority

MPR's Politics Friday discussion looks at the state's new political landscape after an election in which Democrats swept the top offices and won legislative majorities. Amy Klobuchar was elected as Minnesota's first woman governor, while Republicans now face life in the minority.

### Democrats Take Broad Control

The DFL enters a new governing phase with command of the major levers of state government. High turnout, driven by frustration with President Trump, gave the party what is described as a mandate to act. How Democrats use that position will help define the coming policy agenda and set expectations for the party at the Capitol.

### Republicans Reorganize

With the loss of power, Republicans are regrouping under new Senate Minority Leader Jordan Rasmusson. The balance of power at the Capitol will shape policy fights, party strategy and the tone of state politics heading into the next election cycle, making the GOP's response a key part of what comes next.
