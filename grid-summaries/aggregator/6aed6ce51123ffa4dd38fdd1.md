## Is "Polarization" the Wrong Word for America's Political Crisis?

A new article questions whether polarization properly describes what is happening in American politics. Drawing on research by Lilliana Mason, it proposes a different reading of the country's divisions.

### A Fight Over Equal Citizenship

The piece suggests the central conflict is less a simple split between two opposing camps and more a struggle over equal citizenship, power, and democratic rights. Seen this way, the dispute concerns who gets a full and equal voice in the political system, rather than just where people sit on a spectrum of opinion.

### The Problem With "Both Sides"

The article also criticizes media coverage that treats both sides as equivalent when one movement challenges democratic norms or minority rights. It argues that the words used to describe division shape how the public understands it and which solutions seem reasonable. That matters now as debates over democracy, rights, and Trump-era Republican politics remain central to American political coverage.
