## Holocaust survivor shot in Bondi Beach attack dies aged 90

Eva Geller, a 90-year-old Holocaust survivor, has died almost a year after she was shot during the attack at Bondi Beach. The shootings killed 15 people and injured dozens of others.

### A life marked by resilience

Coverage of her death focuses on her resilience, as a survivor of the Holocaust who endured a second act of violence late in life. It also points to the legal fallout that continues after the attack and to the lasting impact the shootings have had on Australia's Jewish community.

Her death reopens national grief over what is described as Australia's worst mass shooting in decades. That tragedy led to gun law reforms and crackdowns on hate speech.
