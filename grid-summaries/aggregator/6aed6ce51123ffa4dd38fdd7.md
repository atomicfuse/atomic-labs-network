## House Republicans Want Closed-Door Nvidia Briefing on Chinese Spy Claims

Senior House Republicans are asking Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang for a private briefing, according to a Reuters report on a letter. The request centers on allegations tied to a Chinese businesswoman and a company called Megaspeed.

### What lawmakers are asking about

The allegations involve possible access to restricted AI chips. At the heart of the matter is whether Nvidia's relationships and security protocols adequately shield sensitive U.S. technology from Chinese espionage. Lawmakers want Huang to address that question behind closed doors.

### Why the scrutiny matters

AI chips sit at the center of U.S.-China tech tensions, export controls, and national security debates. Congressional attention on Nvidia points to growing pressure on major technology firms to show they can keep restricted technology from reaching Beijing.
