## Macao Ex-Lawmaker Gets 12½ Years in City's First National Security Case

Au Kam San, a former pro-democracy lawmaker in Macao, has been sentenced to 12½ years in prison. His is the first known national security prosecution in the city.

### What the Case Represents

The article describes the verdict as a major test of Macao's national security law. It also presents the outcome as a sign that the room for dissent in the city is shrinking, with a prominent opposition figure now facing a lengthy prison term under the legislation.

### Why It Matters Now

The sentence points to national security enforcement reaching beyond Hong Kong and into Macao, a place where opposition politics has historically been limited. It also raises fresh concerns about trials held behind closed doors and about access to legal representation. More broadly, the case feeds worries about Beijing's intolerance of pro-democracy voices and how that stance is applied across the territories it oversees.
