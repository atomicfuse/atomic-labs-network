## Readers Reveal the Worst Job Interviews, and What They Say About Employers

A collection of candidate stories shows how strange and disrespectful hiring can get, from being kept waiting 40 minutes to being asked, "are you a crier?" The article argues that such experiences are more than awkward anecdotes.

### Red flags in the room

The piece contends that bizarre, biased, or disrespectful interview behavior can expose serious problems inside a workplace. A long unexplained wait or an intrusive personal question may hint at how staff are treated once they are hired. Candidates who share these stories are effectively describing early warning signs that they noticed before ever accepting an offer.

### A two-way evaluation

As workers grow more alert to toxic workplaces and unfair hiring practices, interviews are increasingly viewed as a chance for candidates to assess employers, not just the reverse. The article also highlights continuing concerns about illegal bias in recruitment, particularly relating to age, gender, and family plans.
