## Judge pauses Trump's 250ft Washington arch after planning panel signs off

A federal judge has temporarily halted construction of Donald Trump's proposed 250ft "triumphal arch" in Washington, just after a federal planning commission approved the structure. The project is part of the president's wider push to remake the capital.

### Opposition from several fronts

Veterans, Democrats and plaintiffs in lawsuits are challenging the arch. Their objections cover what the monument symbolizes, how it would affect sightlines, whether it is safe, and whether the president has the legal authority to build it.

### Why the timing matters

The arch is linked to America's 250th anniversary, and it arrives as arguments over public monuments, presidential legacy-building and the use of federal space grow more heated. The dispute is also politically charged following Republican midterm losses, and other Trump-backed construction projects in Washington are already facing legal challenges.
