## Trump's 250-Foot Arch Clears Key Hurdle Near Arlington

Approval has been granted for President Trump's proposed 250-foot triumphal arch near Arlington National Cemetery, even as legal and safety objections continue. The monument would feature a golden winged statue, an eternal flame and floodlights, and it may also have military uses.

### A Key Planning Hurdle Is Cleared

The project has passed an important planning step and could see early construction begin within days. That timeline puts pressure on opponents, including Virginia and Washington, D.C., which have filed lawsuits seeking to stop the arch before work moves forward.

### Objections and a Wider Fight

Critics raise concerns about the arch's legality, aviation safety, traffic and public security. The dispute reflects a larger struggle over who gets to shape Washington's public spaces, symbols and security infrastructure, setting Trump's ambitions for redesigning the capital against those challenging the plan.
