## British Doctor With Ebola Flown Home for Specialist Care

A British doctor who caught Ebola during the outbreak in DR Congo has been evacuated to the UK. He is now receiving treatment in a specialist isolation unit at London's Royal Free Hospital.

### Public health response

Health authorities have set out their response to the case and say the risk to the UK public is low. The doctor's transfer to a dedicated isolation unit is part of that response, which is designed to treat him while keeping others protected.

### Scale of the outbreak

The outbreak in DR Congo has passed 10,000 cases and 5,000 deaths, making it the second deadliest Ebola outbreak on record. The case arrives as new candidate vaccines targeting the Bundibugyo variant are being prepared for clinical trials.
