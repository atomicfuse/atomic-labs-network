## Giants Pay Tribute to Rachel Apa, Runner Who Died at New York City Marathon

The New York Giants have honored Rachel Apa, a 24-year-old runner who died after collapsing near the end of the New York City Marathon. The tribute recognizes a young woman remembered for her athletic drive, her faith and her desire to help others.

### A Life of Purpose

Apa was a former Division I athlete who ran the marathon for a charitable cause, raising support for Parkinson's research in honor of her grandfather. Coverage of the tribute highlights her faith and her commitment to giving back. A foundation has been created to carry her mission forward, so the work she began can continue.

### Why the Story Resonates

Her death happened during one of the world's most visible marathons, drawing national attention and renewing concern about safety in endurance racing. It has also prompted wider reflection on purpose, grief and legacy, as people consider how a loss tied to a loving gesture for family has become a lasting call to serve others.
