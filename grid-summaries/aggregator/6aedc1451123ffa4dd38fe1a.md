## Cruz's Best Buy Tearful-Voter Tale Draws Online Mockery

Ted Cruz told Fox News that shoppers at a Best Buy were nearly in tears over Texas "turning blue" after big Democratic midterm wins. The anecdote quickly spread online, and many people were not convinced.

### The Claim and the Backlash

Critics on social media treated the story as exaggerated and hard to believe. Some compared it to the kind of tall tale Donald Trump is known for telling, and the jokes piled up fast. The idea of customers almost weeping among the electronics displays became the main target of ridicule.

### Why It Matters Now

Democrats flipping key statewide offices in Texas would be a major political shift, so how Cruz frames the result has become a flashpoint in the post-election conversation. The reaction also reflects wider weariness with dramatic cable-news anecdotes and displays of political grief that feel staged. Cruz's emotional framing, whatever its intent, has become the story itself.
