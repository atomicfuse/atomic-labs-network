## Eating fat before sugar may soften blood sugar spikes, mouse study finds

New research in mice hints that the order of foods in a meal could influence how the body handles sugar. Experts urge caution, saying the results need testing in people.

### What the study suggests

In the mouse study, eating fat such as olive oil before sugar appeared to slow how quickly the stomach empties. That slower pace may blunt the early rise in blood sugar after eating. Experts stress that this is not a quick fix, and they note that human trials are needed before anyone draws firm conclusions about the effect.

### Why it matters now

Blood sugar control, insulin resistance and metabolic health are major wellness topics, and many people want simple habits that avoid restrictive dieting. The findings add scientific interest to the growing advice known as meal sequencing, which encourages eating vegetables, protein or healthy fats before carbohydrates.
