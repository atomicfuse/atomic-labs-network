## Indonesia Rocked by Alleged Wiretapping of the President

Indonesia is grappling with a scandal over the alleged illegal surveillance of its own president. The arrests of President Prabowo Subianto's chief of staff and senior police figures have put the spotlight on how the state's monitoring tools may have been used.

### What happened

Those arrested are accused of illegally using the police surveillance system. The case is being framed as a possible treason investigation. It is also seen as evidence of a deepening split between Prabowo and his predecessor, former president Jokowi, adding a personal and political dimension to the legal matter.

### Why it matters now

The scandal arrives during a turbulent period for Prabowo's presidency, with pressure over free school meals, wildfires, market turmoil and public protests. It raises urgent questions about state surveillance and rivalries among the country's political elite. It also prompts concern over whether Indonesia's governing coalition is starting to come apart.
