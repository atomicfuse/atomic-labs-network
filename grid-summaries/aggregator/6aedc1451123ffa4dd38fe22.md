## Montana Voters Back Novel Limit on Corporate Election Spending

Montana voters have approved Initiative 194, known as the "Montana Plan," an unusual effort to rein in corporate money in politics. The measure is framed as a direct constitutional challenge to Citizens United and could ultimately reach the Supreme Court.

### How the Montana Plan works

Rather than simply capping donations, the initiative targets the state-granted power that lets certain entities spend in elections. It strips that power from corporations, unions, trade groups, and nonprofits. That approach is what makes it a novel attempt to curb corporate political spending and sets up the potential clash with existing constitutional precedent.

### Why the timing matters

Political ad spending is at record levels, and corporate election money has grown dramatically since Citizens United. Similar laws are being explored in dozens of states, and Hawaii already faces lawsuits. With that backdrop, Montana could become the next major test case in the ongoing fight over money in politics.
