## Australia turn to Mike Hussey as long-term batting consultant

Australia have brought in former Test batter Michael Hussey as a long-term batting consultant, with a crowded schedule ahead that includes the 2027 Ashes, the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and major white-ball tournaments.

### Why the appointment was made

The decision follows a run of poor red-ball batting performances and some tough recent Test results. Hussey's role is designed to run over the long term rather than for a single series, covering a cycle that spans several formats and competitions.

### What it signals for Australia

Australia are heading into a defining stretch, with heavy Test commitments, an Ashes series, World Test Championship ambitions and cricket's return to the Olympics all on the horizon. Hussey's arrival points to concern over batting depth and consistency at a moment when the team's standards are being tested.
