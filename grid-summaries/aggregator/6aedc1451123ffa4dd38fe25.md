## Wisconsin Infant's Measles Death Is State's First Since 1990

A Wisconsin infant has died of measles, marking the state's first death from the disease in more than three decades. The death comes during a large outbreak that has reached 431 cases spread across 13 counties.

### What Happened

The infant's death is the first measles-linked fatality in Wisconsin since 1990. The outbreak behind it is substantial, with hundreds of confirmed cases in a wide range of counties. Coverage of the death stresses how serious measles can be and points to vaccination with the MMR shot as the main way to prevent infection.

### Why It Matters Now

Measles was rare for decades, but cases and deaths are showing up again in several states. The Wisconsin death follows recent child fatalities in New York and Pennsylvania, turning the disease into a national story once more. The trend has sharpened public health concerns about vaccination rates and how well communities are prepared to handle outbreaks.
