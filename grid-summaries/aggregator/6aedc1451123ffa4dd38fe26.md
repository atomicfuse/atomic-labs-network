## Trump's Red "VETO" Hat on Resolute Desk Draws Online Mockery

President Trump has put a new red "VETO" hat on the Resolute Desk, a move that critics online have seized on and ridiculed. The prop appeared after projected Republican losses in the 2026 midterms.

### The Prop and the Backlash

The hat is being read as a sign of a combative stance toward a Democratic-controlled Congress. Many online critics have mocked it as lame-duck theater. They argue it shows he intends to obstruct priorities that voters backed at the ballot box, and some have called the display unhinged.

### Why the Timing Matters

The hat arrives right after a major electoral rebuke, so it has become a symbol of a post-midterm power struggle rather than a show of strength. It also points toward a likely governing clash over affordability, healthcare, corruption, tariffs, and other items on the Democratic agenda.
