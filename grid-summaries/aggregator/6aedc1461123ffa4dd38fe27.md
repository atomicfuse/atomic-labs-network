## Fox News Rides Along With ICE in Brooklyn as Officials Attack NYC Sanctuary Policies

Fox News has published a report from an embedded ICE ride-along in Brooklyn, following agents as they arrested undocumented migrants on city streets. The piece also carries ICE officials' criticism of New York City's sanctuary policies.

### What the report describes

The account details street arrests carried out during the ride-along. It argues that because local authorities decline to cooperate, ICE must make riskier public arrests rather than take custody of people through transfers from jail. ICE officials featured in the story are sharply critical of the city's approach.

### Why it matters now

Immigration enforcement is once again a flashpoint in New York City. Mayor Zohran Mamdani has publicly challenged ICE, while federal officials have threatened sanctuary-city funding. The report arrives amid heightened tensions over raids, protests, claims about migrant crime, and ongoing power struggles between local and federal governments.
