## Harmeet Dhillon Steps Down From Justice Department Civil Rights Post

Harmeet Dhillon has resigned as head of the Justice Department's Civil Rights Division, ending a tenure defined by a sweeping conservative overhaul of the office.

### A Division Remade

Under Dhillon, the division changed course sharply. It saw major staff departures, pursued enforcement actions aimed at diversity, equity and inclusion programs, opened investigations into universities, and pulled back from its work on police reform. Together, these moves marked a break from the priorities the division had previously followed.

### Timing and Stakes

Her exit arrives just after Democrats swept the midterms, which makes the division's direction a central point of conflict. The debate reaches beyond one office, touching wider disputes over Trump-era legal priorities, DEI, voting rights and the reach of federal enforcement power. What comes next for the division is now a significant flashpoint.
