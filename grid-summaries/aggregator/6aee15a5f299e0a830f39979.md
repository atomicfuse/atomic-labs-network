## Progressive Democrats Won Some 2026 Races but Trailed the Wider Party

An analysis of the 2026 midterms finds that far-left Democrats, including Abdul El-Sayed, may have claimed victories in certain contests. Even so, they ran behind more moderate members of their party.

### The Performance Gap

The article's central finding is that ideological progressives lagged the broader Democratic field. This held even though the midterm environment was favorable to Democrats. In other words, the climate was not the obstacle: candidates like El-Sayed still performed worse than their more moderate colleagues, according to the argument presented.

### What It Means for Democrats

The results are likely to fuel a debate within the party over whether 2026 validated a more progressive direction or strengthened the case for moderation. That gap in performance could influence how Democrats recruit candidates, shape their messaging, and build their coalition as they look ahead to the next election cycle.
