## Nithya Raman Ousts Incumbent Karen Bass in Los Angeles Mayoral Upset

Los Angeles City Council member Nithya Raman, a democratic socialist, has defeated Mayor Karen Bass in a major upset. The result is being framed as part of a wider left-wing surge in municipal politics.

### Voters Push Back

The win is presented as a backlash against the city's direction. Frustration centered on homelessness, public safety, basic services, and how leaders have handled crises. Raman, who sits on the City Council, was able to channel that discontent against a sitting mayor.

### Why It Matters Now

Los Angeles is moving toward the 2028 Olympics while residents remain deeply frustrated over housing, encampments, infrastructure, and governance. Raman's victory also points to growing momentum for democratic socialist candidates in major American cities, even as Democratic establishments have resisted them. The result gives that movement a high-profile win in one of the country's largest cities.
