## Syria sends military advisers to Saudi Arabia amid escalating Houthi attacks

Syria has dispatched military advisers to Saudi Arabia, according to sources cited in the report. Their role is to train Saudi and Yemeni forces as Houthi missile and drone attacks grow more intense.

### A shift in Syria's regional role

The article argues the deployment marks a major change in how Syria operates in the region. It deepens Damascus's ties with Riyadh, but it also raises the risk that Syria could be drawn into the war in Yemen.

### Why the timing matters

The Yemen conflict is escalating again against a backdrop of wider tensions across the Middle East. Saudi Arabia is looking for new partners to counter the Iran-backed Houthis. Syria's post-Assad leadership, meanwhile, is trying to present the country as a Gulf security partner while also securing Saudi economic support for reconstruction.
