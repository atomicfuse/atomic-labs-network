## Josh Bishop Targets Killer Kross in Title Showdown

Josh Bishop says he will be "the last man standing" when he challenges Killer Kross for the MLW World Heavyweight Championship at Saturday Night SuperFight. The two meet in a Last Man Standing match.

### Why Bishop Believes He Is Ready

Bishop points to his recent momentum as proof he can take the title. He also cites a past pinfall victory over Kross and says the two have unfinished business. In his view, those factors add up to a challenger who is prepared to become champion.

### What Is at Stake in Chicago

The feud reaches its peak this weekend in Chicago, with MLW's top championship on the line. Kross has held the belt for more than 250 days, and Bishop is presented as one of the biggest threats to that reign. The match also frames Bishop as a rising heavyweight star while MLW works to build buzz around major events and new talent.
