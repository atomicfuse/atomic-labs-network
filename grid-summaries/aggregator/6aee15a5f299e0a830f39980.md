## Leaked Documents Point to Larger Russian Role in Arming Iran

Leaked Russian defense industry records suggest Moscow readied 90 precision-missile launchers for Tehran under a contract labeled "K-10," according to a new report. The preparations reportedly came before Iran's war with the United States.

### What the records suggest

The documents indicate that Russia's contribution to Iran's missile capabilities may have been bigger than the Trump administration has publicly acknowledged. The report argues that the K-10 arrangement points to deliberate preparation of a sizable batch of launchers meant for Iran, though it rests on leaked paperwork rather than official confirmation.

### Why it matters now

The findings arrive as tensions between Washington and Tehran continue to escalate and as scrutiny grows over Russia's military partnerships with American adversaries. They also challenge official claims that Russian support has not materially affected the conflict, raising fresh questions about how much help Iran actually received.
