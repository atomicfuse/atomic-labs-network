## China Says Talks Are Underway on a Possible Xi-Trump-Putin Meeting

China has confirmed it is discussing a potential three-way meeting between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin. The gathering would take place on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen.

### What is being discussed

According to the report, such a meeting would be historically significant. However, it would probably be more symbolic than substantive, since each of the three leaders is said to be pursuing different goals. No outcomes or agreements have been described, and the discussions are still at an early stage.

### Why the timing matters

The prospect arises while U.S.-China trade tensions remain unresolved, Russia seeks diplomatic leverage over Ukraine, and major powers compete to shape the global order. Even a photo opportunity involving the three leaders could send a strong signal about influence, legitimacy and alignment, which is why the idea is drawing attention now.
