## Havilland Hall, Guernsey Estate With Hollywood Ties, Listed at £65m

A historic private estate in Guernsey, Havilland Hall, has come onto the market with a £65m price tag. Its story stretches back nearly 200 years and takes in a remarkable cast of owners and associates.

### A Property Steeped in Heritage

Havilland Hall has links to lieutenant-governors, the Keiller marmalade family and the de Havilland dynasty. It is also connected to Hollywood stars Olivia de Havilland and Joan Fontaine. The article presents the estate as an exceptionally rare Channel Islands trophy property, notable for its heritage and its scale.

### Why It Draws Attention Now

Ultra-prime heritage homes are attracting international interest as wealthy buyers look for privacy, provenance and lifestyle assets. Havilland Hall combines Regency architecture, a celebrity lineage and the scarcity of such estates in the Channel Islands. The article suggests those qualities give it global buyer appeal.
