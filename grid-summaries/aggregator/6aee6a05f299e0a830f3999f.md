## Burack Pushes Back on Clark's ESPN and OutKick Claims

An article by Bobby Burack takes aim at Ryan Clark's assertion that ESPN interviewed Sophie Cunningham about transgender athletes to curry favor with OutKick. Burack argues the claim is mistaken and that Clark's departure has a simpler explanation.

Burack rejects that reading and describes Clark's exit as a matter of performance and behavior, not a conspiracy involving ESPN, OutKick, or race.
