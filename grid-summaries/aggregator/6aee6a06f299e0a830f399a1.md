## Baier Reads Miller Promotion as a Sign of Conflict Ahead

Fox News anchor Bret Baier says President Trump's decision to replace Susie Wiles with Stephen Miller points toward a combative approach after the midterms, rather than an effort to strike bipartisan deals.

### How the Shake-Up Is Being Framed

Baier argues that Miller's appointment prepares the White House for several fights at once. These include expected Democratic investigations, a style of governing that leans on executive orders, and a sharper clash over immigration. In this reading, the move is about bracing for battle, not building bridges across the aisle.

### Why the Timing Matters

Democrats are set to take majorities in Congress after the GOP's midterm loss. That puts Trump on the defensive against oversight and narrows his options for passing legislation. Miller's rise comes as immigration enforcement and political chaos were reportedly key voter concerns in the election rebuke.
