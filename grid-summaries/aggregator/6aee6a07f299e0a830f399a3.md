## Cornell Faculty Rebuke Leadership in Symbolic Vote Over Assault Allegations

Cornell faculty have passed a symbolic no-confidence vote criticizing how university leadership handled sexual assault concerns. The vote follows allegations from a former student, identified as "Jane Doe," who says she was gang raped at a fraternity house.

### What Led to the Vote

The allegations are at the center of a lawsuit, and Cornell has issued a response to the case. Faculty used the vote to criticize leadership's handling of the concerns raised. Cornell's trustees have voiced support for President Michael Kotlikoff, even as the faculty expressed disapproval. State investigations into the matter are ongoing.

### Why It Matters Now

The case has sharpened scrutiny of how campuses are held accountable for sexual assault, along with fraternity culture and the question of whether universities can police themselves. With New York officials now involved, Cornell is emerging as a flashpoint in a wider debate over institutional trust and support for survivors.
