## Halton-Pope Set for Albany After Running Unopposed in Buffalo Assembly Race

Leah Halton-Pope will represent Buffalo's 141st Assembly District after an uncontested election. She is set to succeed longtime Assemblymember Crystal Peoples-Stokes, and her move marks a shift from local to state government.

### How she got the seat

The article centers on the party-led nomination process that put Halton-Pope on the ballot without an opponent. It also looks at her political background and the challenges she faces as she leaves Buffalo's Common Council for Albany.

### Why the timing matters

The race raises timely questions about ballot access, the influence of the local Democratic Party, and voter choice in heavily Democratic districts.
