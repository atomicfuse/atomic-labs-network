## Mississippi Senate Race Hangs on Uncounted Ballots and Missing Recount Rules

Democrat Scott Colom holds a narrow lead over Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's unresolved US Senate contest. Absentee and provisional ballots are still waiting to be counted, leaving the final result uncertain.

### No formal recount process

Mississippi stands out because it has no formal recount process for a race like this one. That gap leaves candidates and officials without a clear path if the margin stays razor thin once the remaining ballots are tallied. A legal fight is already taking shape around how ballots are being handled, adding further uncertainty.

### Why the stakes are high

A Colom victory could end nearly 40 years of Republican control of the state's Senate seats and could change the size of the Democratic majority. With certification deadlines approaching, the lack of clear recount rules turns the race into a high-stakes test of election law and ballot access.
