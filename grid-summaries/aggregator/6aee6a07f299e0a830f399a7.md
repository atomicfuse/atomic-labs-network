## Prince Harry and Others Pursue Appeal Over Mail Publisher Privacy Defeat

Prince Harry, Sir Elton John, Elizabeth Hurley and other public figures are asking for permission to appeal after the High Court dismissed their privacy claims against Associated Newspapers Limited. The group numbers six in total.

### The legal fight

The claimants are trying to overturn a High Court decision that went against them in their privacy case. Before any appeal can proceed, they need permission to bring it. The case also carries a significant costs order, which could leave the claimants facing a bill of up to £34.5m.

### Why it matters now

The bid keeps attention on celebrity privacy, the tactics used by the press and the influence of UK tabloids. It arrives during broader scrutiny of how public figures use the courts to challenge media intrusion, and it shows how high the financial stakes can be for those who take on newspaper publishers.
