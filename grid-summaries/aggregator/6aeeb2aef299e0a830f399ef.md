## Mother and Daughter Build a Comfort-First Activewear Label

Jennifer and Sydney Smith, the mother-daughter founders of ELV Active, are the focus of a new profile. Their label grew out of a shared background in fitness, fashion, and wellness, and it puts comfort at the center of its approach.

### How the Brand Is Made

The profile looks at how the pair design their pieces and how they use customer feedback in that process. Their combined experience across fitness, fashion, and wellness shapes what they create. It also covers their latest coastal-inspired release, the Pacific Form collection.

### Why It Resonates Now

Activewear is increasingly expected to work outside the studio, and shoppers want items that are flattering, versatile, and ready for everyday life. ELV Active fits the Pilates-to-coffee uniform moment. Its family-founded story and community-driven identity add another layer to its appeal.
