## Skepta Returns With a Statement Album

Skepta has released *The Fork and Knife*, his long-awaited sixth album. He presents it as a serious grime project with a strong social focus, and says he is "here to set levels" with what he hopes will be a classic.

### What the album tackles

The record takes on immigration, racism, Brexit, culture wars and what it means to be part of multicultural Britain. Skepta argues that the UK scene needs higher standards, and he frames the album as his answer to that.

### Why the timing matters

The release arrives during renewed debate about where UK rap is heading, the influence of TikTok-driven trends, and how grime portrays immigrant and multicultural Britain. Skepta is positioning himself against disposable viral music, favouring legacy, craft and substance instead.
