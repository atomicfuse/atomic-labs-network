## Michigan Campaign Signs Must Come Down Within 10 Days

Michigan law says campaign signs have to be removed within 10 days after an election. Getting rid of them responsibly takes more effort than placing them in a curbside recycling bin.

### Sorting Signs the Right Way

The article explains that signs are made of different materials and often have metal frames, so they should not be handled as one item. The pieces need to be separated. Some can be reused, while others should be taken to proper recycling locations instead of being left at the curb.

### Why the Timing Matters

With the 2026 midterms finished, yards and roadsides are full of signs, and residents and campaigns need to act quickly. The cleanup also serves as a reminder about "wishcycling," where people put items in the bin hoping they can be recycled. Those good intentions can create costly problems for recycling facilities.
