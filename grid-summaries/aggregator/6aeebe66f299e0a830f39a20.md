## Justice Department Abandons Lawsuit Over 20,000 Disputed California Ballots

The U.S. Justice Department has moved to dismiss its lawsuit that sought to stop California from counting about 20,000 ballots allegedly tied to noncitizens. The case was part of the Trump administration's wider effort to challenge how states run their elections.

### What the case was about

Federal lawyers had asked the court to force California to set aside the disputed ballots. Courts and California officials said the approach risked disenfranchising lawful voters. The dismissal ends the department's attempt to block the count of those ballots, which were allegedly linked to noncitizens.

### Why the timing matters

The move follows Republican losses in the midterms and comes just before a court review of the disputed voter list. That makes it a timely flashpoint in ongoing fights over election integrity, voting rights, and federal power. Millions of California ballots are still being processed.
