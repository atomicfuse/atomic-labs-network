## Houston mayor curbs police cooperation with ICE

Houston Mayor John Whitmire has directed city police to scale back their cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement, a notable step for a major Texas city.

### What the order changes

Police will no longer routinely notify ICE after minor encounters. Contact with the agency is now limited to arrests involving jailable offenses and cases involving administrative ICE warrants. The directive follows public outrage over a fatal ICE shooting and points to a wider Democratic shift on immigration policy in Texas.

### Why the timing matters

Texas recently delivered major Democratic midterm wins, including the governor's race, which has created momentum for cities looking to challenge hardline immigration enforcement. The policy arrives as debate is renewed over ICE practices, sanctuary-city politics, and how much control local governments should have over enforcement.
