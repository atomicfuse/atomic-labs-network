## Rams safety Lake says sorry to Herbert for "deli meat" jab

Rams safety Quentin Lake has apologized to Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert after describing Herbert's throws as "deli meat" on the St. Brown Podcast. Lake made the remark after intercepting Herbert, then sent him a text to say sorry.

### A jab seen as ordinary trash talk

The article argues the comment was harmless NFL trash talk, the sort of thing players say about opponents all the time. It also contends Lake had a partial point, citing the number of interceptions Herbert has thrown this season. In that view, the apology may have been more courteous than necessary.

### Podcasts and a struggling Chargers team

Athlete podcasts are producing more viral locker-room moments, and that blurs the line between candid player commentary and public controversy. Meanwhile, the Chargers are struggling badly, which is turning Herbert's performance into a larger talking point than usual game analysis would make it.
