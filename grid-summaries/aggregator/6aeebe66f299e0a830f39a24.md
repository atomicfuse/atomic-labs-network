## US Puts $20 Million Bounty on Iran Nuclear Intelligence

The U.S. State Department has announced a reward of up to $20 million for information about Iran's nuclear program. The offer is presented as one piece of Washington's push to counter any Iranian move toward nuclear weapons.

### What the Reward Covers

The department is seeking details on a broad range of subjects tied to the program. These include scientists, technologies, assets and the organizations associated with them. Anyone supplying such information could be eligible for the payment, which tops out at $20 million.

### Why the Timing Matters

The announcement arrives while nuclear talks between the United States and Iran remain stalled. Inside the Trump administration, there is also renewed discussion of possible military strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities. Amid rising tensions, embassy warnings and military preparations, the reward program looks like part of a wider escalation.
