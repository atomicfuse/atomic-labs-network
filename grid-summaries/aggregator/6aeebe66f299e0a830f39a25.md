## WTA chair apologises to Pegula over aired private remarks

WTA chair Portia Archer has apologised to Jessica Pegula after courtside microphones broadcast a private conversation. In it, Pegula made critical comments about Mirra Andreeva and Conchita Martinez, including a remark described as a "diva" comment.

### A debate over privacy

The incident has become part of a growing argument about player privacy as tennis broadcasts add more behind-the-scenes access. Pegula's case is framed as one example of how that access can capture moments players expected to remain private.

### Why it matters at Indian Wells

Tennis is increasingly experimenting with microphones, cameras and livestreams to bring fans closer to the action. Recent incidents involving Pegula, Coco Gauff and Iga Swiatek suggest players are pushing back. The controversy arrives at Indian Wells and renews questions about where fan access ends and surveillance of athletes begins.
