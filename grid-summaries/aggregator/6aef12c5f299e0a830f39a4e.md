## Oakland University Students Show America at 250 Through a Gen Z Lens

A student exhibit at Oakland University explores how Gen Z sees the United States as it approaches its 250th anniversary. The project looks at the country's political identity and how young people understand it.

### What the Exhibit Reveals

The exhibit reflects Gen Z perspectives on the nation's history and political character. According to the article, the project delivered the civic lessons it set out to teach, but it also produced unexpected insights. Those discoveries emerged as politics shift ahead of the midterm elections.

### Why It Matters Now

America's semiquincentennial is arriving during a period of intense polarization, strong youth political engagement, and a consequential midterm cycle. In that setting, Gen Z's views on patriotism, democracy, and national identity are increasingly shaping the broader political conversation, which gives projects like this one added relevance.
