## Republicans Eye a 2028 Senate Comeback, but the Path Looks Treacherous

Following sweeping Democratic gains, Republicans are looking at how they might reclaim a Senate majority in 2028. An analysis lays out competitive pickup chances, though it also warns that the party faces serious obstacles along the way.

### Opportunities and Obstacles

The piece describes several competitive seats Republicans could target, but notes that some GOP-held seats are vulnerable too. It argues a comeback is possible, yet statehood pushes, aging incumbents, and changing politics in red states could make the road much harder than it first appears.

### Why 2028 Matters

Democrats' wins have reshaped the Senate map and raised the stakes, with control of the chamber potentially resting on a handful of battleground states. The analysis also reflects wider uncertainty about party realignment and affordability politics, and about whether Democrats' recent gains in red territory will prove durable.
