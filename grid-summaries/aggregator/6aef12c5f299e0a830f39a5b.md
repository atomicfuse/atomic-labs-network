## Voters Put Corruption Alongside Inflation and Immigration

An article on the 2026 midterms argues that voters ranked corruption among their top concerns, next to inflation, immigration, and war. It says that focus helped Democrats win congressional majorities.

### How Corruption Was Defined

The piece takes a broad view of the term. It covers self-dealing, the influence of big money in politics, weak oversight, and a decline in trust that democratic checks and balances still work. In this framing, corruption is less a single scandal than a general sense that the system is not holding powerful people to account.

### What Comes Next

With Democrats now controlling Congress while Trump remains in the White House, the article expects corruption to become a central battleground. It points to investigations, subpoenas, campaign finance debates, and scrutiny of corporations as likely arenas. Voters, it says, are signaling that ethics and accountability are pocketbook and democracy issues, not abstract ones.
