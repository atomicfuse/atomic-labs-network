## Border Patrol Finds Nearly $10 Million in Suspected Cocaine at San Clemente Checkpoint

U.S. Border Patrol agents in Southern California seized more than 1,435 pounds of suspected cocaine, valued at $9.8 million, during two vehicle inspections that took place minutes apart at the San Clemente checkpoint.

### A Pair of Back-to-Back Busts

The seizures happened during consecutive stops at the checkpoint. Together, the two inspections turned up the large quantity of suspected narcotics. The article presents the haul as part of increasing drug interdiction efforts and a wider federal push against trafficking networks.

### Why the Timing Matters

Border enforcement and crackdowns on cartels remain major political and public-safety concerns, particularly along the California-Mexico corridor. Officials say fewer illegal crossings in San Diego have allowed agents to devote more attention to checkpoint operations and drug interdiction.
