## Michigan's El-Sayed Launches "Habibi PAC" After Historic Senate Win

Michigan Senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed has created a leadership PAC called "Habibi PAC," just days after becoming the first Muslim member of the US Senate. The move comes as he is known for a campaign message that opposed big money in politics.

### A reform message meets PAC politics

The article places the launch against El-Sayed's anti-big-money stance and the wider surge in spending by PACs, super PACs and dark-money groups. That backdrop makes the new committee a notable step for a politician who ran on cleaning up how campaigns are funded.

### Why the timing matters

El-Sayed's victory came during a record-breaking midterm cycle and followed intense Islamophobic attacks from national Republicans. The new PAC raises timely questions about how reform-minded politicians operate within a political system that still runs on money and influence.
