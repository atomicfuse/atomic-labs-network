## New Zealand Power Past Tonga to Reach World Cup Final

New Zealand beat Tonga 34-10 in the men's Rugby League World Cup semi-final, ending a wait for a final that dates back to 2013.

### Ponga Stars After Injury

Kalyn Ponga delivered a standout performance just 23 days after breaking his forearm. His quick return and leading role in the win turned the semi-final into a major comeback story for the Kiwis.

### Kiwis Master the Conditions

Brutal wind and rain made life difficult, but New Zealand handled the weather better than their opponents. They applied kicking pressure and finished their chances clinically. The result sends them to a Brisbane final against either Australia or Samoa, with the chance to disrupt Australia's long dominance of the men's World Cup.
