## Married at First Sight UK axed as cast react with relief and sadness

Channel 4 has cancelled Married at First Sight UK following serious safeguarding concerns and allegations involving former participants. Contributors have responded in very different ways.

### Mixed emotions from the cast

Some former cast members have expressed relief that the programme is ending, while others have spoken of tears and sadness at the decision. Beyond those personal reactions, contributors continue to demand accountability from both Channel 4 and the production company CPL, saying the end of the series should not close the matter.

### Wider scrutiny of reality TV

The cancellation comes as safeguarding in reality television faces intense scrutiny. Channel 4 bosses are due to appear before the DCMS committee, and questions are growing over whether high-conflict relationship formats can be made safely. The decision could also put international versions of Married at First Sight, along with similar shows, under fresh pressure.
