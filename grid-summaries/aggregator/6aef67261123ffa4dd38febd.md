## Tillis Slams Miller Promotion as Wrong Pick for a Divided Washington

Sen. Thom Tillis, a Republican, has sharply criticized Donald Trump's decision to elevate Stephen Miller to White House chief of staff. He called Miller the worst of all possible choices for the job.

### Why Tillis Objects

Tillis argues that Miller is too divisive and too partisan to help Republicans deal with Democratic majorities now in control of Congress. In his view, the role calls for someone who can build working relationships across the aisle, and Miller does not fit that need. The appointment is framed as a misstep following the midterms, tied to backlash over Trump's hardline immigration agenda.

### The Timing

The promotion arrives right after a major Republican midterm defeat, a moment when the party faces pressure to recalibrate and figure out how to govern alongside Democrats. Miller's rise suggests Trump may choose to double down on confrontation rather than shift toward moderation.
