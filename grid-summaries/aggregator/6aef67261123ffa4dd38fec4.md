## Why People Ghost, and the Regret That Can Follow

Ghosting remains a common frustration in dating, and a relationship coach says the behavior often says more about the person leaving than the person left behind.

### What drives the disappearing act

The article describes ghosting as avoidance, driven by discomfort, fear of hard conversations, or low investment in the connection. Relationship coach Monica Ricci notes that people who ghost may later feel guilt or remorse. New research adds that those who are ghosted may underestimate how much guilt the ghoster actually feels.

### A kinder way to end things

The piece offers a simple, direct script for ending early dating connections respectfully, giving people a practical alternative to silence. It also makes clear that cutting contact is appropriate when a situation is unsafe. With low-closure exits increasingly normalized in dating culture, the article responds to a clear appetite for useful communication tools and emotional reassurance.
