## Wall Street Slips After Democratic Midterm Victories

Stocks moved lower as Wall Street reacted to Democratic wins in the midterm elections. Heritage Foundation economist EJ Antoni weighs in on the turbulence and on what investors might anticipate in the coming period.

### Volatility and the jobs report

Antoni addresses the market swings that followed the election results, placing them alongside the October jobs report. The discussion connects political developments with fresh economic data, and considers what investors may face next as they digest both the vote and the employment figures.

### Why politics matters to markets

Elections can quickly change investor expectations about taxes, regulation, spending, and wider economic policy. Markets are already sensitive to inflation, jobs data, and uncertainty over interest rates, so political outcomes are becoming a major part of the broader economic story. That combination helps explain why the midterm results drew such a sharp response from Wall Street.
