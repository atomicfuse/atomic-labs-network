## Fox News' McEnany Show Leads Weekend Cable News Ratings

*Saturday in America with Kayleigh McEnany* has emerged as the most-watched weekend program on cable news, according to a report on its ratings performance.

### Leading the Pack

The show tops its time slot and is the only weekend program to appear in cable news' top 10. The report points to strong total viewership and solid results among younger demographic viewers, along with audience growth. It also notes that the program draws viewers beyond Fox's usual base, including independents and Democrats, which adds to its crossover appeal.

### Why It Matters

Weekend cable news is often a quieter battleground than weekday prime time. McEnany's numbers suggest Fox is turning Saturday mornings into a high-value programming slot. The show's growth also reflects how personality-driven political programming continues to shape viewer loyalty in cable news, even outside the weekday schedule where audiences are typically concentrated.
