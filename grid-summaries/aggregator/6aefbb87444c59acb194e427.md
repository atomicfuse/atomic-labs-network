## Washoe County Works Through About 7,000 Remaining Mail Ballots

Washoe County officials say roughly 7,000 mail ballots still await processing. Election workers are moving the batch through several steps as the county continues its vote count.

### Where processing stands

Staff are currently checking signatures, sorting ballots, extracting them from their envelopes, and scanning them. The county says its "Don't Wait Washoe" campaign helped cut the number of ballots dropped off on Election Day, bringing the total below what officials had expected.

### Why the timeline matters

Tight races could still change as the remaining ballots are tallied, so the pace of processing matters to both voters and candidates. The update also shows how mail ballot deadlines, signature verification, and ID requirements for provisional ballots can shape the final results.
