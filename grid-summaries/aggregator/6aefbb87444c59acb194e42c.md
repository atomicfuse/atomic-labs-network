## Earlier Bedtimes Linked to Greater Weight Loss in Prediabetes Study

A study of adults with prediabetes has found that going to sleep earlier was associated with losing more weight, pointing to bedtime as a possible factor in weight management.

### What the study found

Participants who fell asleep earlier tended to lose more weight. Each hour earlier was linked to roughly 1.3 pounds lost over eight weeks. The results suggest that when people sleep, and not only how long they sleep, may influence metabolism, hunger hormones, and late-night eating.

### Why it matters now

Prediabetes, weight-loss drugs, and metabolic health are widely discussed at the moment, and this finding offers a low-cost lifestyle approach people can try alongside diet and exercise. It also fits with growing interest in circadian rhythm, sleep quality, and the ways daily routines affect weight.
