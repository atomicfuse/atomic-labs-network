## Six Dead in Krakow Square Shooting; Three More Bodies Found at Suspect's Home

A mass shooting in Krakow's Main Market Square has left six people dead and at least 15 others injured. Polish leaders are treating the incident as a terrorist attack inspired by far-right beliefs.

### The attack and its aftermath

Police later searched the suspect's home and found three more bodies. These are believed to belong to members of the suspect's family. The discovery adds to a death toll that has already shaken the city, and the injured number at least 15.

### Why it matters now

Poland's leaders say terrorism on this scale has never been seen in the country, and the shock has been felt nationwide. The attack also arrives as concern grows across Europe about extremist violence and the security of public spaces. Foreign nationals were among the victims, which gives the story an international dimension, particularly for audiences in the UK.
