## Labubu Fever Cools as Pop Mart Sales Slide

The craze around Labubu appears to be fading, with Pop Mart reporting sharply lower sales. The toy's challenge is now whether it can turn a viral moment into a lasting following.

### What Is Behind the Slowdown

Scarcity, which once drove the frenzy around Labubu blind boxes, has faded. Resale hype has weakened, and collectors are becoming more selective about what they buy. The article argues that Labubu must move beyond viral blind-box excitement and build real loyalty to the character itself.

### A Test for Trend-Driven Collectibles

Pop Mart's global sales slump and falling share price point to a wider test for collectibles that thrive on trends. The market is now crowded with Sonny Angels, Smiskis, Miniso blind boxes and established properties such as Hello Kitty. The moment reflects a broader shift among consumers, from buying on hype to collecting with more curation.
