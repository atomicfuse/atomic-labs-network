## Rybakina Pulls Out of WTA Finals Defence With Viral Illness

Elena Rybakina has withdrawn from the WTA Finals, where she was due to defend her title, because of a viral illness. Her exit brings an early end to a dominant 2026 season.

### A season that reached the top

Rybakina finishes the year as world number one. She won both the Australian Open and the US Open, and she ended Aryna Sabalenka's long stay at the top of the rankings. The campaign ranks among the strongest in recent women's tennis.

### What the withdrawal changes

Her absence reshapes the WTA Finals field. It also ends her chance to become the first back-to-back champion at the event since Serena Williams in 2014. The season therefore closes on a health-related setback, even after such a successful year for the new top-ranked player.
