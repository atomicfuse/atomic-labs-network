## Tiny Residual Checks Reveal the Gap Between TV Fame and Pay

Several television actors have publicly shared how little they earn in residuals from hit shows, a problem sharpened as streaming changes the way performers are compensated. Even familiar faces from successful series can end up with pennies, or nothing, for continued reruns and streams.

### Streaming Changes the Payout

The article points to actors who have disclosed strikingly small checks for work that remains widely watched. Because ongoing viewing now happens largely on streaming platforms, recognizable stars can find that a show's lasting popularity does not translate into meaningful income for them.

### Why the Issue Persists

Residuals remain a contentious topic following the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strike, and more actors are now openly showing what legacy TV work pays in the streaming era. The story feeds into wider discussions about creative labor, platform profits, and the reality that fame does not always bring financial security.
