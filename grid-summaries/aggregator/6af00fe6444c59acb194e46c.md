## Israel's President Gives Opposition Leader Eisenkot First Shot at Forming a Government

Israel's president has asked opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot to try to assemble a governing coalition, after elections left Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc weaker. The move is a setback for the prime minister.

### A Difficult Road for Eisenkot

Eisenkot now holds the first opportunity to build a majority, but the article stresses that his route to power is hard. A central question is the role of Arab-led parties, which could end up as kingmakers if he is to succeed. Their position may decide whether a coalition can come together at all.

### Why the Stakes Are High

Israel remains politically divided after years of war following the Oct. 7 attacks, and Netanyahu's grip on power faces one of its biggest tests. Any coalition agreement could reshape wartime leadership and the influence of Arab parties. It could also affect the national debates over security, religion, and accountability.
