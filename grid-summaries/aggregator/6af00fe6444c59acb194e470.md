## Hinds County Officials Weigh Provisional Ballots in Tight Senate Race

CNN has taken a look inside the election office in Hinds County, Mississippi, where staff are working through a pile of provisional ballots. The work is drawing attention because the state's US Senate race is still too close to call.

### The review under way

Officials in the county are examining each provisional ballot to determine whether it should be counted. The process happens at the local level, inside the election office, and its outcomes feed directly into the overall tally. Because the Senate contest has not been decided, the decisions made in this review carry added weight for the final result.

### Why the scrutiny is intense

National politics often turns on narrow margins, and that has put ballot-counting procedures under close examination. Provisional ballots can prove decisive when a race is tight. As a result, local election offices like the one in Hinds County are playing a central role in shaping outcomes that reach well beyond the county itself.
