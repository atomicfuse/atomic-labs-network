## New Zealand Election Leaves Government Up in the Air

New Zealand's election has produced no clear winner, and the country now faces a stretch of uncertainty. Final special votes are still to be counted, and they could change the picture.

### A narrow path for National

Christopher Luxon's National Party says it has a slim route back into government, working with ACT and NZ First. That path is far from secure. With the result so tight, the smaller parties hold considerable leverage, and talks over a possible coalition could drag on for weeks rather than being settled on election night.

### Why the outcome matters

Voters have been dealing with cost-of-living pressure, high unemployment and wider frustration over the economy, which raises the stakes of the result. Because special votes remain outstanding and minor parties have gained influence, the next government may depend more on bargaining after the election than on the first tally of ballots.
