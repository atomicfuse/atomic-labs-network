## Vance Shapes a 2028 Identity After GOP Midterm Losses

Following Republican losses in the midterms, Vice President JD Vance is working to define a political brand of his own, according to Reuters. The effort comes as early positioning for 2028 gets underway inside the party.

### A brand built on four themes

Reuters reports that Vance is focusing on economic nationalism, foreign policy restraint, affordability, and public safety. The report argues he wants to inherit Trump's coalition while keeping distance from Trump's liabilities, especially the Iran war, which Vance has criticized publicly.

### Why it matters now

Republicans face a post-midterm reckoning with Trump's popularity weakened. Vance's criticism of the Iran war and his emphasis on affordability point to an early contest over what post-Trump conservatism will look like.
