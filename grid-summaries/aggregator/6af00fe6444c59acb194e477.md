## Bowling pioneer Ritsuko Nakayama dies at 84

Ritsuko Nakayama, the Japanese bowling icon who became the first woman to roll a professional perfect game, has died at the age of 84. She was widely seen as a key figure in Japan's bowling boom of the 1970s.

### From volleyball to bowling stardom

Nakayama began her sporting life in volleyball before moving into bowling, where she became a pioneering female professional. Her success on the lanes also made her a pop-culture figure in Japan, extending her fame well beyond the sport itself and helping bring bowling to a wider audience during its peak popularity.

### Why her legacy matters now

Her death closes a significant chapter in Japanese sports history, particularly in women's professional bowling, where she broke new ground. Her story also arrives amid renewed interest in the history of the sport and in community events such as National Bowling Day, which give fans a chance to look back on pioneers like her.
