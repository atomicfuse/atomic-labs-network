## Tennessee Routs Kentucky to Keep Playoff Hopes Alive

No. 13 Tennessee beat sixth-ranked Kentucky 34-6, giving its College Football Playoff chances a significant boost and ending the Wildcats' recent run of success.

### How Tennessee Won

The Volunteers leaned on a dominant defensive performance and pulled away with a surge in the fourth quarter. The result snapped Kentucky's six-game winning streak and left the Wildcats with far less room for error in the playoff chase. Tennessee controlled the game from start to finish by limiting Kentucky to just six points.

### What It Means

As the playoff race tightens, this is Tennessee's second signature win, which strengthens its case even after a recent loss to South Carolina. Kentucky, once viewed as a top-six contender, now needs a near-perfect finish to stay in the picture. The outcome reshapes the stakes for both programs in the weeks ahead.
