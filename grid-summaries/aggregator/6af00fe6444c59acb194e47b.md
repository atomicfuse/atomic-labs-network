## NASA Plans Ice-Hunting Rover for the Moon's South Pole

NASA has unveiled the PROMISE rover mission, which aims to land at the lunar south pole by late 2028. The rover will travel aboard a lander built by Blue Origin.

### What the mission will do

PROMISE is assembled from spare components left over from the Curiosity and Perseverance Mars rovers. Once on the surface, it will search for water ice and study conditions that could affect long-term human activity on the Moon.

### Why it matters now

The south pole has become a strategic destination in the new space race, since water ice could provide fuel, oxygen, and material for future habitats. The mission also shows NASA's push to reuse proven technology while working alongside commercial space companies.
