## Rhule Shakes Up Nebraska Offense After Loss to Illinois

Nebraska coach Matt Rhule has removed Dana Holgorsen as offensive coordinator following a 24-13 loss to Illinois. Rhule said the team was due for a change, and Glenn Thomas will now take over play-calling.

### Why the Change Was Made

Rhule said the Cornhuskers' offense "simply is not working," which prompted the decision to move on from Holgorsen. The defeat extended Nebraska's losing streak to four games, and Thomas now takes charge of the offense as the team looks to turn things around.

### Pressure Is Building

Frustration among Nebraska fans has been growing over poor offensive production, penalties, missed kicks and questionable play-calling. With important games still ahead, the coaching shakeup signals urgency and accountability from Rhule as he tries to keep the season from slipping further away.
