## Delta Shakes Up Hub Network, Trimming 15 Routes and Adding Summer 2027 Leisure Flights

Delta Air Lines is reworking the network that runs through its major hubs. The airline is dropping service to 15 cities while introducing new seasonal flights aimed at leisure travelers.

### What is changing

The cuts affect routes from hubs including Detroit, Atlanta, and Minneapolis, with 15 cities losing service in total. At the same time, Delta is adding seasonal weekend leisure flights for summer 2027. The moves reflect a deliberate rebalancing of where the airline puts its planes and seats, favoring vacation-oriented trips over some existing connections.

### Why it matters

Airlines are increasingly shifting capacity toward high-demand leisure routes as travel patterns change and pressure on profitability grows. Delta's choices signal where it expects travelers to head next. They also show which cities may lose convenient nonstop access, a change that could affect passengers who have relied on those direct links through Delta's hubs.
