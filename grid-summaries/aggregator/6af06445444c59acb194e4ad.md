## Column Says Sheriff Bianco, Not Taxpayers, Should Pay for Ballot Seizure

A column argues that Riverside County Sheriff Chad Bianco should personally bear the legal costs stemming from his handling of Proposition 50 ballots. It frames the matter as a question of accountability for a public official.

### The Argument Against Bianco

The column contends that Bianco unlawfully seized Proposition 50 ballots. It says he did so to advance his gubernatorial ambitions and to appeal to Donald Trump's narrative about voter fraud. Because, in the writer's view, the conduct abused public authority for political gain, the column maintains that taxpayers should not be asked to cover his legal expenses.

### Why It Matters Now

Election denialism remains a live political strategy, and courts are increasingly being asked to police abuses of power tied to false fraud claims. Bianco's fight over reimbursement tests whether public officials can shift the cost of politically motivated misconduct onto taxpayers.
