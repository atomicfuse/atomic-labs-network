## Trump Taps Miller, Signaling a Harder Line After Midterm Losses

Following Republican losses in the midterms, Stephen Miller is expected to become White House chief of staff. The article argues the move points toward confrontation and a reliance on executive power, not toward moderation or compromise.

### A Strategy Built on Executive Power

The piece describes Miller as a "wartime" adviser, and his expected elevation suggests the White House sees the political setback as a reason to dig in. Rather than seeking common ground, the administration appears ready to lean on presidential authority and escalate culture-war fights. It may also tighten its control over Republicans.

### Higher Stakes With Democrats in Charge

Democrats now control Congress, which makes Trump's path to governing harder. That raises the stakes on immigration, tariffs, investigations and foreign policy, all areas where a more combative White House could clash directly with lawmakers. Miller's appointment suggests the administration is preparing for that fight.
