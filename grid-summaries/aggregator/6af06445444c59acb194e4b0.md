## Open 2028 Primaries Could Serve Both Parties, Analysis Argues

An analysis contends that wide-open, hard-fought presidential primaries in 2028 could leave both major parties stronger. The central idea is that real competition pushes candidates, coalitions, and policy ideas to prove themselves under serious scrutiny rather than being settled in advance.

### The Case for Competition

According to the piece, a contested nominating process works as a stress test. Candidates must defend their records and visions, the groups that back them must show they can hold together, and ideas must withstand challenge from rivals. The argument is that this scrutiny can strengthen whoever emerges as a nominee and the platform that person carries forward.

### Why the Timing Matters

Although the election is still some way off, 2028 is already a topic in political strategy circles. Democrats and Republicans both face open questions about who should lead them, what each party stands for, and how to earn voter trust after years of polarization. The analysis suggests open primaries could be a way to work through those questions.
