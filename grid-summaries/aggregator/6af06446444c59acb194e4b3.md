## Australia Overpower Samoa to Reach Another World Cup Final

Australia beat Samoa 44-12 in Sydney to book a place in the men's Rugby League World Cup final for the 16th consecutive tournament. Reece Walsh scored a hat-trick in the win.

### How the match unfolded

The semi-final opened with Samoa's intense Siva Tau confrontation, which set a fiery tone. However, Samoa's mistakes, poor discipline and growing frustration let Australia take control of the contest and build a commanding victory.

### What comes next

Australia now chase a fourth straight world title, with a major final against New Zealand awaiting in Brisbane. The rivalry with Samoa, a refereeing controversy and the tension before kickoff added cultural and sporting heat to the result, as Australia's long run of dominance continues.
