## Australia Survive Fiji Fright to Reach Rugby League World Cup Final

Australia advanced to their fifth consecutive Women's Rugby League World Cup final with a 30-14 win over Fiji, though the tournament debutants made the reigning champions work hard for it.

### How the Jillaroos got through

Fiji gave Australia a major scare in the semi-final, putting real pressure on the defending champions. Abbi Church ultimately rescued the Jillaroos, scoring four tries to steer her side to the win and secure a place in the decider against New Zealand.

### What it means for the final

Australia are chasing a fourth straight World Cup title, but Fiji exposed a rare sign of vulnerability in the tournament favourites. The showdown with New Zealand in Brisbane is a classic pairing, and the close call adds tension and unpredictability to the build-up.
