## Khawaja Says He Has Not Thrown Anyone Under the Bus Over Sandpaper Scandal

Usman Khawaja has clarified remarks in his autobiography about Australia's 2018 sandpaper scandal, insisting he has not "thrown anyone under the bus." He argues that responsibility was shared across the wider team setup.

### Who was punished and who denied knowledge

The piece revisits which individuals faced punishment over the affair and which others said they had no knowledge of what happened. Khawaja's comments suggest the picture of blame was broader than the original outcomes implied. Cricket Australia, however, maintains that its original investigation remains sufficient.

### Why the issue has resurfaced

Khawaja's autobiography has reopened questions about accountability, silence and team culture in Australian cricket. The story carries fresh relevance because current stars Pat Cummins, Mitchell Starc, Josh Hazlewood and Nathan Lyon are all still connected to that era, keeping it firmly in front of fans and media.
