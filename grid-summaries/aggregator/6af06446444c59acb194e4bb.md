## Psychiatrist Flags Six Habits That May Worsen Depression

Psychiatrist Dr. Daniel Amen has identified six everyday habits that he says can make depression symptoms worse. The article behind this summary stresses that these are not a substitute for professional care.

### The Habits in Question

The list covers a range of behaviors, including alcohol use, social isolation, negative thought patterns, and eating ultra-processed foods. The piece also emphasizes that treatment should be individualized and guided by a proper clinical assessment, rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach.

### Why It Resonates Now

Depression rates among Americans ages 12 and older have risen sharply, and many people are searching for practical steps they can take alongside therapy or medication. The story reflects growing interest in lifestyle-based mental health support, with attention to sleep, diet, movement, and social connection.
