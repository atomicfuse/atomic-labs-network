## King Charles Leads Nation in Remembrance at the Cenotaph

King Charles III led the UK's Remembrance Sunday service at the Cenotaph, where royals, political leaders, veterans and dignitaries gathered to mark a two-minute silence and lay wreaths.

### A march of veterans

The ceremony included a march-past of around 10,000 veterans. Among them was a small number of World War Two veterans, linking the occasion to one of the conflicts it was created to honour. Wreaths were laid as part of the formal service, which followed the national silence observed by those present.

### Reflection and political significance

The service offered the country a moment of reflection amid global instability, reminding audiences of the human cost of war. It also carried political and symbolic weight. Prime Minister Andy Burnham attended for the first time in that role, and a record number of former prime ministers were present, making this year's gathering stand out.
