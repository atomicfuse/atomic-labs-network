## Pancreatic Cancer Vaccine Sparks Immune Response but Misses Survival Goal

An experimental pancreatic cancer vaccine called camupepimut has produced a near-universal immune response in a phase 2 trial. However, the results did not show a statistically significant gain in disease-free survival.

### What the trial found

Camupepimut is an "off-the-shelf" vaccine designed to target KRAS mutations. In the phase 2 study, it triggered immune responses in nearly 99% of vaccinated patients. Even so, the vaccine did not deliver a statistically significant improvement in disease-free survival, the measure of how long patients remain free of the disease.

### Why it matters

Pancreatic cancer remains among the hardest cancers to treat, and the risk of recurrence stays high even after surgery and chemotherapy. The findings add momentum to cancer vaccine research. They also highlight the gap between activating the immune system and demonstrating a proven survival benefit for patients.
