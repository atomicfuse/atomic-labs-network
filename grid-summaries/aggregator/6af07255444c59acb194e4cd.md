## Potassium-Enriched Salt Swap Could Spare Millions From Disease

A global modeling study suggests that swapping regular table salt for a potassium-enriched version could prevent millions of cardiovascular and kidney disease cases, along with nearly 3 million deaths each year.

### Who Gains and Who Should Be Careful

The article describes who would benefit most from the switch and how the substitute works. It also flags caution for people with kidney disease and for those taking medications that carry potassium-related risks, who should take the change seriously before making it.

### Why It Matters Now

High sodium and low potassium intake remain widespread causes of high blood pressure, and public health campaigns to reduce salt consumption have moved slowly. Potassium-enriched salt is emerging as a scalable, low-friction option that could improve health without requiring people to make major changes to their habits.
