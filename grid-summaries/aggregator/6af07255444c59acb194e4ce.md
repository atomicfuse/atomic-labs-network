## Surviving the Mountain Is Only the First Step, Article Argues

An article on outdoor accident survivors looks at what happens after the rescue, when the psychological fallout begins. It says that recovery has long been overlooked in adventure culture.

### The Hidden Toll

Survivors can face post-traumatic stress, grief, guilt, and a lost sense of who they are, along with a growing distance from the natural world that once defined them. The article contends that adventure culture has historically paid little attention to this stage. Women-led therapists and peer-support groups are now starting to fill that gap, offering help that fits the needs of people in these communities.

### Why Support Is Urgent

Participation in climbing, skiing, trail running, kayaking, and similar sports has surged. Mental health resources in mountain towns, however, remain limited. With more people heading into high-risk landscapes, the article says the need for culturally informed trauma support is becoming pressing.
