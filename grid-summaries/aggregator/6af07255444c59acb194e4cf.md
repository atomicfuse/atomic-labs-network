## Melissa Joan Hart Opens Up About Perimenopause and Midlife

Melissa Joan Hart is speaking candidly about her perimenopause experience as she turns 50. She describes moderate to severe hot flashes and encourages women to raise menopause symptoms with their doctors more freely.

### Her Message on Health and Reinvention

Hart urges women to talk openly with medical professionals about symptoms and the treatment options available to them. The piece presents midlife as a time for reinvention and health advocacy, and as a chance to push back against stigma tied to age. Her willingness to share personal details is central to that message.

### Why the Conversation Is Growing

Menopause is increasingly part of public and workplace discussion. Its symptoms can affect sleep, careers, relationships, and overall quality of life for millions of women. Hart's openness reflects a wider move away from taboo and toward better education, stronger medical support, and more representation of women over 50.
