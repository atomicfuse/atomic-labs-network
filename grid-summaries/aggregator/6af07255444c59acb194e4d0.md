## Longevity supplement shows early vascular benefits in small trial

A small six-month randomized trial has found that NOVOS Core, a multi-ingredient longevity supplement, improved several markers of blood vessel health compared with a placebo.

### What the trial found

Participants taking NOVOS Core showed improvements in endothelial function, systolic blood pressure and arterial stiffness relative to those given placebo. The article stresses that these are preliminary biomarker findings. They do not prove a reduced risk of heart disease, and they say nothing about long-term benefit.

### Why it matters now

Longevity supplements are booming, yet many claims outpace the clinical evidence. The study offers a timely example of what early human data can and cannot show. Cardiovascular health is also a major concern as people age, which makes vascular aging an accessible way into conversations about prevention.
