## Palestinian flag replaces German flag at Berlin's Reichstag monument

Pro-Palestinian activists took down the German flag from the "Flag of Unity" monument outside Berlin's Reichstag and put up a large Palestinian flag in its place. The action led to arrests and criminal investigations.

### A charged act of vandalism

The episode is framed as politically motivated vandalism. Its symbolism is sharpened by the timing, as it happened on the eve of the anniversary of Kristallnacht. Authorities have arrested people in connection with the incident and opened criminal investigations into what took place.

### Why the timing matters

The incident comes during an intense debate across Europe over pro-Palestinian protest, antisemitism, national identity and where the limits of public order lie. Its closeness to the Kristallnacht anniversary makes it especially explosive in Germany, where Holocaust memory and democratic values are central to the country's political culture.
