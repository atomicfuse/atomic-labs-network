## Houthi-Run Media Report 33 Dead After Saudi Strikes East of Sanaa

Media linked to the Houthis say Saudi air strikes east of Sanaa have killed at least 33 people, among them women and children. The reported attack comes as fighting in Yemen flares up again.

### An escalating confrontation

The strike is described as one episode in a wider escalation between the Saudi-led coalition and the Houthis. The recent fighting has involved missile attacks, strikes on airports and attacks on shipping in the Red Sea. Civilian casualties are also rising as the clashes continue.

### Why it matters now

Yemen's war had been relatively dormant but is intensifying once more, following regional tensions involving Iran, Saudi Arabia and Red Sea oil routes. The United Nations says more than 1,600 people have been killed and over 300,000 displaced since fighting resumed. That makes the situation a fast-growing humanitarian and geopolitical crisis, with consequences reaching beyond Yemen's borders.
