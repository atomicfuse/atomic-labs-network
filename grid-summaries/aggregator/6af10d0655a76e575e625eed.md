## Pope Leo XIV Tells Argentine Leaders to Put the Common Good First

In his first address in Argentina, Pope Leo XIV called on the country's leaders to place the common good above political interests and to see authority as a form of service.

### What the Pope Asked For

He urged leaders to strengthen democratic institutions, fight corruption, and pursue economic growth that includes vulnerable people. He also stressed opportunities for young people, protection of life, cooperation across the region, and responsibility toward the environment. He added a call for ethical governance of technology.

### Why the Timing Matters

The speech arrives during intense political and economic debates under President Milei, which gives his appeals for dialogue, inclusion, and trust in institutions added relevance. It also presents the Church as a moral voice in Latin America on democracy, migration, inequality, and artificial intelligence.
