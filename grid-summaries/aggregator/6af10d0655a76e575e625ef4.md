## Trump's Russia Deal Stirs NATO Fears Over Europe's Security Order

A proposed Russia-Ukraine peace deal from Trump is drawing concern within NATO that it could reshape European security without enough input from Ukraine or European allies. A former NATO commander has also weighed in on what Moscow may be after.

### Breedlove's Warning

Philip Breedlove, a former NATO commander, argues that Putin's wider aim is to divide global power into spheres of influence, with Russia dominating Europe. In this view, the proposed settlement matters well beyond the battlefield in Ukraine, because it touches on who gets to shape the continent's future.

### Talks Move to Abu Dhabi

Peace talks are heading to Abu Dhabi, and leaders in Ukraine, NATO and the EU worry about a possible "Yalta 2.0" moment, in which Europe's security order is decided over their heads. The story arrives amid sharper debate over U.S. foreign policy, NATO unity and how far negotiations with Putin should go.
