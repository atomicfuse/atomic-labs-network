## Teen Brothers Use Kayak and Paddleboard to Rescue Family From Florida Floodwaters

Two teenage brothers in Palm Bay, Florida, came to the aid of neighbors caught in rising water. They rescued a mother, her son and three dogs, using only a kayak and a paddleboard.

### The Rescue

The brothers saw that the family was trapped by powerful floodwaters and decided to act. With the two watercraft, they reached the stranded household and brought the mother, her son and their three dogs to safety. The story portrays the effort as a strong example of neighbors stepping in during a dangerous emergency, when quick action by people nearby can matter a great deal.

### Flooding in Brevard County

The rescue comes as severe flooding in Brevard County has disrupted homes, roads and daily life. At least one death has been reported after a vehicle became trapped in floodwaters. The account highlights ongoing concerns about extreme weather, emergency preparedness and how communities respond when conditions turn hazardous.
