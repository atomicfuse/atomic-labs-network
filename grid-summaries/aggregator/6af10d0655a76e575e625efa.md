## Iran drops missile-range cap and promises "astonishing" new weapons

Iran says it has lifted its self-imposed 2,000-kilometer limit on ballistic missile range and is threatening to unveil new weapons it claims will astonish the world. The article presents the announcement as a direct answer to a new U.S. task force.

### Pressure on supply routes

The task force sits under U.S. CENTCOM and targets Iranian weapons shipments, illicit trade routes and revenue streams. Washington is stepping up pressure on Iran's land routes and on its supply lines across the Caspian Sea. Tensions remain high over regional proxy activity, missile production and Iran's ties to Russia.

### Iraq as a flashpoint

Iraq's border crossings have become a point of contention. Washington is pushing Baghdad to restrict trade that could help Tehran evade sanctions. The article links this push to the wider U.S. effort to cut off Iranian revenue, and to Iran's latest warning about its missile capabilities.
