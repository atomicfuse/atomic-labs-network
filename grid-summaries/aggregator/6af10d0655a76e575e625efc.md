## Bush hosts 27 veterans at Texas ranch for 16th Warrior Ride

Former President George W. Bush welcomed 27 veterans to his ranch in Crawford, Texas, for the 16th annual Warrior Ride. The gathering honored wounded service members and the paths they have taken in recovery.

### A long-running commitment

The Warrior Ride is one part of Bush's work on behalf of veterans since he left the White House. That effort has also included cycling events, portraits, and public advocacy. The latest ride shows how consistently he has kept this cause in view over the years, with the focus placed on the veterans and their recoveries.

### Why the timing matters

The event arrives ahead of Veterans Day, a time when public attention turns to military service, sacrifice, and the support systems available to wounded veterans. It also offers a look at how a former president can shape his legacy through sustained civic work after leaving office.
