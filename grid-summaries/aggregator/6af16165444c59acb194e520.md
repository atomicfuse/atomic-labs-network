## New Zealand Waits as Tight Election Delays Coalition Talks

New Zealand's election has no clear winner after a vote so close that National and Labour are nearly level. Parties have put off coalition negotiations until special votes are counted.

### Minor parties gain leverage

Act, the Greens and the Opportunity Party all emerged from the vote in a stronger position. With the two largest parties almost tied, these smaller groups may have a decisive say in what kind of government eventually takes shape. Until the count is finished, the major parties are holding off on formal talks.

### Weeks of uncertainty ahead

Final results are still weeks away, and close to 400,000 special votes could change the balance of power. The wait comes at a difficult time for the country, which is dealing with a stagnant economy, a tough job market and record numbers of New Zealanders leaving.
