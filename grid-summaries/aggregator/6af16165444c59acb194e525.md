## Indiana Democrats Post Notable Gains Across Multiple Levels of Government

Indiana Democrats had a strong election, picking up ground in statewide, congressional, legislative and local contests. The analysis behind the story points to several forces at work, including voter worries about affordability, frustration with the economy, unease over immigration, and a backlash against national Republican politics.

### What Democrats Won

The party captured two statewide offices and flipped the 5th Congressional District. It also gained at least 16 seats in the Indiana House and strengthened its position in suburban local races. Taken together, the results mark a broad showing rather than a single isolated win.

### Why It Matters Heading Into 2028

Indiana has long been viewed as safely Republican, but these outcomes suggest the state could be more competitive than assumed. In 2028, the governor's office, a U.S. Senate seat and the Indiana House majority will all be on the ballot. Both parties now face pressure to rethink their messaging, turnout efforts and voter trust, especially on affordability and local quality-of-life issues.
