## Sanders Urges Democrats to Balance Trump Probes With Economic Agenda

Bernie Sanders says Democrats should look into corruption and alleged abuses from the Trump era, but warns that investigations must not push aside the affordability issues voters backed them on.

### The Economic Mandate

Sanders describes working-class economic pain as the party's real mandate. He points to housing, healthcare, wages, child poverty, seniors' income, and disruption from artificial intelligence as the problems Democrats should keep at the center of their work, even as they pursue accountability.

### A Strategic Tension

Democrats have just won control of Congress and are preparing to use subpoena power, which creates a tension between accountability politics and kitchen-table economics. Prices, fuel costs, healthcare, and housing remain central concerns for voters. Sanders is cautioning against repeating a resistance-first playbook, urging the party to keep its focus on those everyday pressures.
