## Rubio Heads to Paris for G7 Talks Amid Questions Over U.S. Direction

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio is travelling to Paris to meet G7 foreign ministers. The agenda covers Ukraine, Iran, energy, artificial intelligence and wider strains within the alliance. The gathering takes place against uncertainty about the course of Trump's foreign policy after heavy Republican losses in the midterm elections.

### Pressure to coordinate

Wars, fuel-price shocks and instability along key shipping routes are pushing Western governments to align their responses. The Paris meeting offers a chance for the allies to compare approaches on these overlapping crises, with energy and security concerns closely linked.

### Allies watch Washington

Partners are looking for signs of whether turmoil in U.S. domestic politics will alter Washington's position. Their attention is fixed on Ukraine, Iran, NATO and the International Criminal Court, where any change in American stance could affect how the group works together.
