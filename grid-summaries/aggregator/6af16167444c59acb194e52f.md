## Connecticut GOP Urged to Look Inward After Election Rout

Writing in an opinion piece, Timothy Herbst says Connecticut Republicans should treat their sweeping election loss as a call for internal change rather than a product of national conditions or Donald Trump.

### A Call for Reform

Herbst argues the party needs new leadership, a stronger organization and less infighting. He rejects the idea that outside forces explain the defeat, saying Republicans must take responsibility for their own performance. In his view, excuses will only delay the work of rebuilding the party's standing with voters across the state.

### Why the Timing Matters

After a major Democratic victory, Republicans face questions about why voter frustration over affordability did not turn into GOP gains. Herbst urges a kitchen-table economic message built on taxes, energy costs, schools and local control. The piece looks ahead to whether Democrats overreach and whether Republicans can regroup before 2028.
