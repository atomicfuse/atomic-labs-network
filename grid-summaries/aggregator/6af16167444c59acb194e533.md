## Denver Diner Calls Out 83-Cent Fee at Earl's Kitchen + Bar

A Denver customer has drawn attention to a small administrative fee added to a bill at Earl's Kitchen + Bar. The complaint set off a wider conversation about restaurant add-ons and how people tip.

### What Happened

The diner objected to an unexpected 83-cent administrative charge on the check. The amount was tiny, but it still struck a nerve and prompted debate online and among customers. The story shows how even minor surcharges can irritate people when they seem unclear or impossible to avoid, since the issue is often less about the cost than about the lack of transparency.

### Why It Matters Now

Diners are growing more sensitive to service fees, higher menu prices in the inflation era, and so-called tip fatigue. As restaurants try out extra charges, some customers are rethinking how much they are willing to leave as a tip. Threats to cut gratuities suggest that added fees may carry a cost of their own for restaurants.
