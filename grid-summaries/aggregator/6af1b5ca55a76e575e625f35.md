## COP31 Opens in Antalya With Pressure to Deliver on Climate Pledges

World leaders and negotiators have gathered in Antalya, Turkey, for COP31, arriving after a year of deadly extreme weather. They face growing pressure to turn earlier climate promises into real action.

### The Summit's Main Priorities

The talks focus on cutting methane emissions, expanding renewable electricity, building climate-resilient cities, and finding finance for poorer countries. These goals take on added weight as the 1.5°C warming target slips out of reach.

### A Difficult Backdrop

The summit unfolds amid overlapping climate and energy crises. Extreme weather is worsening, while disruption in the Strait of Hormuz is driving fossil fuel price shocks and prompting governments to rethink energy security. The United States is absent, and tensions persist between rich and developing nations. Together, these factors make COP31 a test of whether global climate diplomacy can still deliver results.
