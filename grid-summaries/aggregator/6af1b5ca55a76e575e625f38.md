## Scammers Posing as Federal Agents Target Seniors With Gold Bar Demands

Criminals pretending to be federal agents are telling older people their savings are in danger and pressing them to turn their money into gold bars. Couriers then arrive to collect the metal. Recent cases in Wyoming and Wisconsin show how the scheme works.

### How the Scheme Operates

The fraudsters impersonate government officials and apply steady pressure by phone. They use passwords and couriers to make the process seem official and secure. The reporting describes the approach as organized and constantly changing, part of a wider pattern of elder financial fraud rather than a set of isolated incidents.

### Why Gold Is Being Used Now

Gold has a long reputation as a safe haven, and scammers are turning that image against their victims. Worries about inflation, geopolitical uncertainty and fraud are already running high, which makes the pitch more believable. Seniors remain a prime target as these criminals grow more sophisticated.
