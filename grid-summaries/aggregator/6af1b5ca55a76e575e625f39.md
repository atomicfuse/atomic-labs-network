## Newsom's Mass Commutation Signals a Shift in Death Penalty Politics

California Governor Gavin Newsom has commuted the death sentences of 548 people. According to the article, the decision suggests that opposing capital punishment may no longer sink a presidential hopeful.

### A Changed Political Landscape

The article argues that the move shows how much "tough on crime" politics has lost its electoral punch. For decades, a candidate who appeared soft on crime risked serious damage at the ballot box. The piece frames Newsom's action as evidence that this fear has faded, and that taking a stand against the death penalty is no longer the liability it once was for national candidates.

### An Early Test for 2028

Newsom is widely viewed as a likely contender for the 2028 presidential race, so the commutation may serve as an early gauge of how crime and punishment issues will play in a Democratic primary and a general election.
