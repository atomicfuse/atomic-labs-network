## Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Named in Chinese Spy Plot Reports

Reports say Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang was targeted by a Chinese espionage effort. China has rejected the claims, calling them a smear campaign.

### Huang's Unusual Position

The allegations put a spotlight on Huang's place in both worlds. He is a key ally of President Trump, and he is also a business leader who has worked to keep U.S.-China ties on artificial intelligence open. That dual role makes him a distinctive figure in a tense relationship, and the coverage frames the claims around it.

### Why It Matters Now

Nvidia sits at the heart of the global AI race, so any suspected espionage involving its CEO becomes a major geopolitical flashpoint. The story arrives as Washington and Beijing compete over chips, AI infrastructure and technological dominance, with each side seeking an edge in a contest that shapes both economies.
