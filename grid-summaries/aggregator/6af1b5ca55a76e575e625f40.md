## Missouri GOP weighs whether to redraw congressional map again

Missouri Republicans are debating whether to try congressional redistricting a second time after voters rejected Prop A and the GOP-backed 2025 map, which targeted Democratic Rep. Emanuel Cleaver's district.

### Risks of another map fight

The piece argues that another attempt would be politically dangerous. Legal uncertainty, the risk of another referendum, and the potential for voter backlash all complicate any renewed push. Having just lost a statewide vote on the issue, Republicans would be returning to ground where voters have already signaled their opposition.

### A shifting legal landscape

The failed effort comes amid changing legal terrain around majority-minority districts following Louisiana v. Callais. Missouri GOP leaders now face a choice: revisit a priority backed by Trump, or move on in light of the clear rejection at the ballot box. The debate remains unresolved.
