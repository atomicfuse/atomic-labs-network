## Europe's Plastic Waste Is Washing Up on Turkey's Shores

Plastic waste exported from Europe is turning up on Turkish beaches and in the wider environment, according to an examination of the country's recycling industry. The findings arrive as Turkey presents itself as a climate leader.

### Recycling That Leaks

The article describes how imported plastic waste from Europe flows into Turkey's recycling sector, which is allowing trash to escape into natural surroundings. Beaches are among the places where the pollution is visible. This sits in sharp contrast with the country's "zero waste" branding, which promotes a clean, responsible image but does not match the realities tied to imported garbage.

### Why the Timing Matters

Turkey is hosting the U.N. global climate conference, which makes scrutiny of its waste system especially timely. The story also reflects growing concern that wealthy countries are outsourcing their pollution to markets with weaker regulation, leaving other nations to deal with the consequences.
