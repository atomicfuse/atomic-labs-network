## Opinion Piece Calls for Tougher Political Reporting in Buffalo and Western New York

A new piece argues that Buffalo and Western New York need deeper, more aggressive local political reporting. The author says such coverage is needed to expose entrenched power, inform voters and improve civic culture.

### The Case for Deeper Coverage

The article contrasts the work of Investigative Post with the shrinking political reporting capacity at legacy outlets. It holds that scrutiny of local power should not depend on a handful of newsrooms, and that the region's political culture suffers when fewer journalists are watching those who hold office.

### Why the Timing Matters

Election season has just ended, and major governance questions, including Buffalo charter revisions, are still on the table. The article frames political journalism as a civic necessity rather than a campaign-season add-on. It also arrives amid newsroom cuts that have left fewer dedicated reporters covering local power.
