## UK Firms Turn Textile and Leather Scraps into Fashion's Next Materials

British recycling companies are finding ways to convert discarded textiles and leather into lower-carbon materials. These are being offered to fashion, transport and consumer brands looking for alternatives to virgin inputs.

### Recycling technology takes shape

Project Re:Claim focuses on polyester-to-polyester recycling, aiming to turn old synthetic textiles back into new material of the same kind. Gen Phoenix, meanwhile, makes an upcycled leather fabric from waste leather. Its material is used by brands including Dr Martens, Nike, Coach and Jet2, showing that recycled options are reaching well-known names across several sectors.

### Pressure builds on brands

New EU sustainability rules and growing demand from Gen Z shoppers are pushing fashion companies to reduce waste, demonstrate recyclability and move away from virgin materials. The UK has promising technology to help, but industry adoption and legislation still lag behind the scale of the waste problem.
