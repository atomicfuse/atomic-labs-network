## Krakow Square Shooting Leaves Six Dead; Gunman Dies After Police Clash

A mass shooting in the Main Market Square in Krakow has killed six people and wounded 15 others. The 24-year-old gunman was shot by police and later died in hospital.

### What investigators know

Authorities are treating the attack as a possible terrorist offense. Officers also found three members of the suspect's family dead at his home. The investigation is still developing, and details about those deaths remain part of the inquiry. Attention has also turned to the shooter's far-right views.

The attack has shaken Poland and prompted urgent questions about extremist violence and the security of public spaces. It has also raised concerns about risks to tourists in major European cities.
