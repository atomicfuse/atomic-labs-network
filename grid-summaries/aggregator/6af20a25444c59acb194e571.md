## Women Missing from the Top in Israel's Knesset Politics

An article examines who is rising in Israeli politics and who is being left behind. It finds that religious Zionists hold an increasingly large share of Knesset seats and national leadership posts, while women remain largely shut out of the highest roles.

### A Record Number, but Not at the Top

The latest Knesset elections produced a record 33 women MKs, a sign of growing female presence in parliament. Yet none of the 11 elected party lists is headed by a woman. The article stresses this gap between representation in the chamber and access to real leadership power.

### Religious Zionism's Growing Influence

The article argues that the expansion of religious Zionism has been overwhelmingly male. As the movement extends its reach across Israeli public life, the question of who is promoted into leadership becomes more urgent, and the article asks where the women are.
