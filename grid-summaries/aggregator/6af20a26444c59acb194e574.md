## House Democrats Eye Healthcare and Iran War Powers as First Priorities

If Democrats win the House majority, they plan to open with a healthcare push and measures to rein in the Iran war. Their early agenda also covers tariffs, artificial intelligence, and cost-of-living concerns.

### The Agenda and the Obstacles

Healthcare tops the list, with bills to strengthen Medicaid and restore ACA subsidies. Democrats also plan legislation on Iran war powers, tariffs, AI, and everyday costs. Yet the path is steep. The Senate filibuster could block their bills, President Trump could veto any that pass, and some Democrats are pressing party leaders to act more aggressively.

### Why It Matters Now

Democrats want to turn a midterm win into visible policy results before the 2028 presidential cycle. Healthcare costs, war powers, AI regulation, and inflation are all high-profile issues, and how the party handles them could shape whether voters see the new majority as effective or merely symbolic.
