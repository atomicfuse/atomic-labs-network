## LePage Heads to Washington Before Maine Count Ends

Former Maine Republican Gov. Paul LePage is already in Washington after declaring victory in Maine's 2nd Congressional District, even though the ranked-choice count is still being finalized.

### A Rare Republican Pickup

The article describes LePage's likely win as an unusual bright spot for Republicans in a midterm cycle largely defined by Democratic gains. He has presented himself as ready to start, and the report notes he is "already at work" on Capitol Hill while officials complete the tally.

### Why the Race Matters Now

The contest could end up as the GOP's only flip from blue to red in a House seat that was not redistricted. That stands out as Democrats regain significant power in Congress. The race also keeps attention on the debate over ranked-choice voting and on the coming leadership fight inside the House Republican conference.
