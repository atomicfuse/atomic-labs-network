## Senate Republicans Push to Postpone Leadership Elections After Midterm Losses

A group of Senate Republicans led by Ron Johnson wants to delay the party's leadership elections following heavy midterm losses. They argue a serious review of what went wrong should come before any leaders are reelected.

### Thune Still Expected to Run

John Thune is still expected to seek another term as the top Senate Republican. For now, he appears unlikely to face a major challenger, which suggests the delay effort is aimed more at forcing a debate than at unseating him.

### A New Minority and Internal Blame

Democrats flipped the Senate, leaving Republicans in the minority. That shift has made leadership strategy and the question of who bears blame central to the party's internal fight. The push reflects wider Republican tensions over messaging, how candidates performed, and how to respond to an empowered Democratic majority.
