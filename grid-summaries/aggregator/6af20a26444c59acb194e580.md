## France Points Finger at Iran's Revolutionary Guards Over Supermarket Fridge Sabotage

France says Iran's Revolutionary Guards were behind cyberattacks that sabotaged refrigeration systems in supermarkets. The incidents caused fires, spoiled food and millions in losses.

### A Wider Pattern

The episode is framed as one part of a broader run of alleged Iranian-linked cyber disruption affecting Europe and the United States. French officials attribute this particular campaign to the Revolutionary Guards, placing responsibility on a state-linked force rather than on independent criminals.

### Diplomatic Stakes Rise

Tensions tied to the Iran conflict are spilling into civilian infrastructure, and everyday systems such as supermarket fridges, water plants and transit networks are becoming cyberwar targets. France has summoned Iran's envoy and threatened EU sanctions, which raises the diplomatic stakes. The move signals that attacks on ordinary commercial systems may now carry political consequences for those held responsible.
