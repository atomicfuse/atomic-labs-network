## College Football Playoff Set to Double to 24 Teams in 2027

An opinion piece argues that the College Football Playoff's official expansion to 24 teams in 2027 is driven mainly by money and will reshape the sport. It points to the likely end of most Power 4 conference championship games.

### A Deal Built Around TV Revenue

The article frames the agreement with ESPN as another case of tradition and competitive logic giving way to television dollars. In its view, the bigger field reflects what networks want rather than what is best for the game. The expected loss of most Power 4 title games is presented as one clear cost of that approach.

### Another Shift in a Changing Sport

College football is already absorbing NIL, revenue sharing, realignment, and expanded postseason formats. This change adds to that list and touches the sport's identity. The article notes how quickly the field is growing, from 12 teams to 24, and asks where expansion will stop and who truly benefits.
