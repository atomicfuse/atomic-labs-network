## Trump Says He'll Keep Fighting for Year-Round Daylight Saving Time

President Donald Trump has said he will keep pushing for permanent daylight saving time across most of the United States. His comments bring fresh political attention to the long-running debate over ending the twice-yearly clock change.

### What Trump is pushing

Trump's stated aim is to make daylight saving time the standard for most of the country, so clocks would no longer be moved forward in spring and back in autumn. The story centers on the renewed momentum his position gives to the idea, as a bill on the subject has stalled and time appears to be running short.

### Why the timing matters

Lawmakers are facing pressure from voters who are frustrated by the seasonal time changes. The wider debate also includes questions about health, safety and economic effects.
