## Trump Renews Push to Pardon Pete Rose, Saying Baseball's Hit King Was Mistreated

President Trump has again voiced support for pardoning Pete Rose over his past federal tax charges. He argues that MLB's all-time hits leader was treated unfairly in the aftermath of his gambling scandal.

### Rose's Legacy Still Debated

The article looks back at Rose's exclusion from the Hall of Fame and at MLB's decision to lift his ineligible status after his death. That move opened the door for his possible Hall of Fame consideration. Even so, his legacy remains the subject of debate, with people split over how his gambling should be weighed against his record on the field.

### Why the Timing Matters

Legalized sports betting has changed how fans and leagues think about gambling, and for some that makes Rose's lifetime ban feel newly controversial. Trump's comments arrive against that backdrop, and also after MLB's decision to allow Rose to be considered for the Hall of Fame following his death.
