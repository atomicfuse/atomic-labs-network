## Sand Adds Republican and Independent to Transition Team

Governor-elect Rob Sand has started the transition process for his incoming administration. He has brought a Republican and an independent onto his transition team as he prepares to take office in January.

### What the move signals

The additions point to an early emphasis on bipartisan, or cross-party, governing. By including people who are not Democrats at the very start of the transition, Sand is showing how he wants to approach the work ahead, even before he is sworn in.

### Why the timing matters

Transition teams help shape staffing, priorities and the overall tone of a new administration before its first day. Because those early decisions carry weight, the makeup of the team is closely watched. In a polarized political climate, Sand's choice to include a Republican and an independent offers a timely signal about how he may try to govern once in office.
