## Trump's mixed signals after midterm losses

After heavy Republican losses in the midterms, President Trump has struggled to settle on a clear message. An examination of his response finds him swinging between limited gestures toward compromise and the hardline denialism his supporters know well.

### A message that keeps shifting

The article points to inconsistency on several fronts: whether the election results can be trusted, immigration policy, the Iran war, and the economy. Trump has at times hinted at working across the aisle, then returned to familiar combative themes. Meanwhile, his allies are doubling down rather than adjusting course.

### Why this moment matters

Democrats are preparing to take control in Washington, so how Trump handles defeat could define the last two years of his presidency. Republicans now face a pivotal choice: recalibrate their approach, or keep defending policies that have proved unpopular with voters.
