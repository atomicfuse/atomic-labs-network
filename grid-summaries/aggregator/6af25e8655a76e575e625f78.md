## South Carolina Father Arrested After Leaving Sleeping Child in Line for Pokémon Cards

Police in South Carolina say a father left his 6-year-old daughter asleep outside a Target overnight so he could keep his spot in line for a new Pokémon card release. He was arrested over the incident, which has drawn attention to the growing frenzy around the trading cards.

### What Police Say Happened

According to police, the man left the sleeping girl outside the store while he held his place in line. The line had formed ahead of the new card release. The arrest followed, and the story links the episode to the intense demand surrounding Pokémon products.

### Why the Story Resonates

Pokémon cards have become a sought-after collector and resale item, with rare cards fetching thousands of dollars and new releases drawing overnight lines.
