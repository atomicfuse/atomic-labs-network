## Trump and Dana White Raise Possible Pardon for Pete Rose

President Donald Trump and UFC CEO Dana White discussed the idea of a posthumous pardon for Pete Rose during a White House press conference. The conversation put the late baseball star's legacy back in the spotlight.

### Rose's Legacy and the Hall of Fame

Rose is Major League Baseball's all-time hits leader, a record that sits at the heart of his reputation. Even so, he remains shut out of the Hall of Fame because of discipline tied to gambling. The pardon discussion brings fresh attention to that long-standing contradiction between his on-field achievements and his exclusion from baseball's highest honor.

### Why the Timing Matters

Sports gambling has become mainstream and is now connected to politics in ways that make Rose's lifetime ban feel newly open to debate. Having the topic raised at the White House adds urgency to one of baseball's longest-running arguments about how a player's legacy should be judged.
