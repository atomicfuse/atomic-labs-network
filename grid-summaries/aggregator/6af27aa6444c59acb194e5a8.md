## SKIMS Offers Refunds After Advent Calendar Backlash

SKIMS has begun refunding customers who bought its $148 holiday advent calendar, following a wave of online criticism about what was inside.

### What went wrong

Buyers said the calendar was filled with underwhelming extras such as keychains, scrunchies, and bra straps, which they felt did not justify the price. As the complaints went viral, SKIMS responded by offering purchasers either a full refund or store credit. The brand also took the listing down.

### Why it matters now

Advent calendars have become a big holiday retail showpiece, but shoppers are increasingly willing to call out brands when premium pricing does not match what they receive. The episode also highlights how fast TikTok-style consumer outrage can push a celebrity-backed brand into damage control, as it did here with SKIMS.
