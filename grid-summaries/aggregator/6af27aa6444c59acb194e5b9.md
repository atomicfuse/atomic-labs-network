## Kendall Jenner Strips 818 Tequila Back to Basics

Kendall Jenner is fronting a new campaign for her 818 tequila brand, called "Nothing Extra." It leans on a "Practically Naked" concept to highlight a product made with just three ingredients.

### A Softer, Nature-Led Look

In a profile, Jenner talks about the campaign's gentler, nature-driven creative direction. She also discusses her approach to confidence and boundaries, what running 818 involves, cocktails shared within her family, and the areas of life where she thinks less is more.

### Why Restraint Matters Now

Celebrity-founded alcohol brands are competing to seem more authentic and focused on the product itself. Jenner is positioning 818 around restraint, freshness and credibility instead of pure sex appeal. The campaign also reflects current lifestyle tastes, including natural luxury, soft minimalism and "quiet" branding that still has personality.
