## FDA Flags Rare Eye Condition Risk on GLP-1 Medication Labels

The FDA is updating the labels of all GLP-1 drugs to include a warning about a rare cause of vision loss. The change covers well-known products such as Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound and Mounjaro.

### What the warning covers

The new label language concerns non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy, a condition linked to reduced blood flow to the optic nerve. The FDA is applying the warning across the entire GLP-1 drug class rather than singling out one product, so every label in the group will carry it.

### Why the timing matters

GLP-1 drugs rank among the most popular medications for weight loss and diabetes, meaning even a rare risk can touch millions of current and potential users. The update follows new research and an FDA analysis that found a slightly higher rate of the condition among people taking these drugs.
