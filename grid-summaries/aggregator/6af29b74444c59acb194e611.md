## Reiner Children Honor Parents at Beverly Hills Gala

Jake and Romy Reiner made a rare public appearance at a Beverly Hills gala celebrating the legacy of their late parents, Rob and Michele Reiner. The event raised $4 million for children and families.

### A New Award Debuts

The siblings presented Jamie Lee Curtis with the first Michele and Rob Reiner Champion for Change Award. The honor was created in their parents' names, and the evening centered on remembrance and charitable giving rather than the circumstances surrounding the couple's deaths.

### Tragedy Remains in the Background

The gala took place nearly a year after the shocking deaths of Rob and Michele Reiner. Their son Nick remains jailed and charged in the case. Against that ongoing tragedy, the fundraiser put the focus on philanthropy and public remembrance, shaping the family's story around what the couple left behind.
