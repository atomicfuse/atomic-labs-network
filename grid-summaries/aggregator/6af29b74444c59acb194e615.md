## Deadliest Catch Season 23 Will Sail Without the F/V Wizard

The F/V Wizard, a longtime vessel on "Deadliest Catch," will reportedly not appear in the upcoming Season 23, which is currently in production, according to TMZ.

### What we know

TMZ reports that the Wizard was not removed from the series. Instead, the show is simply not filming aboard the boat this season. No further details about the decision have been shared, and the report does not say whether the choice came from the production or the vessel's side.

### Why fans are noticing

The Wizard joined "Deadliest Catch" in Season 3 and appeared continuously for 20 seasons, so its absence marks a significant change for devoted viewers. With production already underway, fans are talking about what the fleet lineup might look like this year and whether the vessel could eventually return to the show.
