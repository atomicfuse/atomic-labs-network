## Composer Francesco Le Metre on Scoring Judy Heumann's Story

An interview with composer Francesco Le Metre explores how he approached the music for Apple Original Films' *Being Heumann*. He discusses turning the humanity, urgency, and activist spirit of disability rights icon Judy Heumann into sound.

### Punk Energy and Unusual Strings

Le Metre describes drawing on punk-inspired drums to capture Heumann's relentless drive and the feel of the 1970s disability rights movement. He pairs them with unconventional string techniques, and he weaves several motifs together so the score reflects both the person and the cause she championed.

### A Moment for Disability Stories

*Being Heumann* has reached Apple TV following its premiere at TIFF, adding to ongoing conversations about disability representation, activism, and inclusive storytelling in Hollywood. Siân Heder's involvement, coming after *CODA*, places the project within a wider industry shift toward narratives centered on disability.
