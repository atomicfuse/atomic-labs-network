## Cheyenne Jackson Joins Six New Cast Members in Lionsgate's 'My Jesus'

Lionsgate and Kingdom Story Company have added six cast members, Cheyenne Jackson among them, to *My Jesus*, a music-driven coming-of-age film inspired by Anne Wilson's book.

### The Story Behind the Film

The movie follows Anne's rise after she posts a viral singing video. Along the way, it traces her journey through faith, family, fame, and purpose, placing music at the center of a personal story about growing up and finding direction.

### Why the Casting Matters

The announcement arrives as faith-based entertainment and country and Christian music stories continue to gain mainstream traction, and Lionsgate is expanding its slate in that space. The casting also ties the project more closely to Nashville culture, through the Grand Ole Opry and a special appearance by Carly Pearce.
