## Kris Bowers Scores a Musical Tribute to Ghana's Film Legacy

Composer Kris Bowers has written the music for *The Eyes of Ghana*, a documentary he describes as "a love letter to Ghana" and to film history. The score supports a story about preserving a nation's visual memory.

### The Story Behind the Film

The documentary centers on Chris Hesse, who preserved rare footage of Kwame Nkrumah and Ghana's decolonial era. He did so even though efforts were made to destroy that material. Bowers' music frames this act of safeguarding history as both a personal and a cultural devotion.

### Why It Resonates Now

Interest is growing in recovering suppressed Black and African histories, particularly through archives, documentary storytelling, and cultural preservation. The film sits squarely within that conversation. Its production was backed by Obama, and its screenings have sold out, which gives it timely momentum in both film and history circles.
