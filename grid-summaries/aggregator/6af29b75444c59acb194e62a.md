## Skydance Chiefs Take Swipe at Warner Bros. Film Lineup

David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz of Skydance presented the Warner Bros. merger as a big win for franchises. They also openly criticized the studio's weak film slate and the losses they expect it to bring.

### Losses and a Tougher Outlook

The leaders pointed to a projected $200M hit to fourth-quarter EBITDA and said the 2026 outlook looks harder. Their remarks put the studio's current performance under a spotlight just as the deal has closed, and they did not soften their view of what Warner Bros. is bringing to the combined company.

### A Plan for Discipline

Skydance intends to bring tighter greenlighting and stricter cost control to the studio. Wall Street is watching to see whether it can make Warner Bros. more profitable. With box office results unpredictable and franchise strategy central to Hollywood, the approach signals a more financially disciplined era for legacy studios.
