## Infographics and Number Choices Can Change How Risky Lyme Disease Feels

New research suggests that the way Lyme disease risk is presented can influence how seriously people take it. Both visuals and the type of numbers used appear to affect public perception and the actions people choose to take.

### What the research found

Infographics can raise people's perceived risk of Lyme disease and motivate them to take preventive action. The effect is especially strong among people who start with limited knowledge about the illness. The study also shows that numerical framing matters. When risk was expressed as percentages, Lyme disease seemed less severe than when it was presented as absolute numbers.

### Why it matters now

Lyme disease is becoming more common in the U.S., and 2026 is projected to be one of the worst tick seasons in nearly a decade. Public health messages compete for attention, so the way risk information is visualized can directly shape behavior.
