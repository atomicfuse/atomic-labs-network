## Childhood Trauma Linked to Gambling Harms in Adult Life

New research from the University of Bristol connects certain adverse childhood experiences, known as ACEs, with gambling harms later in life. The work adds to understanding of who may be more vulnerable and why.

### What the study found

Researchers report that some ACEs are associated with later gambling harms, and childhood sexual abuse showed the strongest and most consistent link. The study stresses that ACEs are one part of a wider network of risk factors. They are not a direct predictor for everyone, so a difficult childhood does not mean gambling problems will follow.

### Why it matters now

Gambling harms are increasingly viewed as a public health issue. This research moves the conversation beyond behaviour alone and toward trauma-informed support. The findings come as researchers and practitioners develop practical tools for counsellors, charities and NHS services.
