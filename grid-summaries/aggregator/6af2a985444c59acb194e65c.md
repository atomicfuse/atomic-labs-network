## Smartwatch ECGs Analysed by AI Show Early Promise for Spotting Weak Heart Pumping

Preliminary research suggests that artificial intelligence reviewing electrocardiograms recorded on smartwatches may help flag left ventricular systolic dysfunction, a hidden condition in which the heart pumps weakly.

### What the research found

Results were promising but not uniform. Performance varied across different devices and across individual recordings. Repeating the test reduced false positives, though that approach also missed some cases of the condition. The work is early, so the findings point to potential rather than a proven screening method.

### Why it matters now

Smartwatches are increasingly treated as mainstream health tools, and AI-enabled screening could extend early detection beyond clinical settings. The timing is notable because heart failure prevention, wearable diagnostics and consumer health AI are all moving forward quickly, making accessible checks for hidden heart problems a growing area of interest.
