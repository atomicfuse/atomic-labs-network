## Man's Brain Surgery Leaves Him Seeing Cat Eyes on Human Faces

A 28-year-old patient named Karl developed unusual visual disturbances after surgery on his right temporal lobe. Researchers describe how he experiences two conditions, palinopsia and prosopometamorphopsia, and how AI-generated visuals were used to depict what he sees.

### What Karl experiences

After the operation, Karl began seeing facial features from his cat, or from other people, appear on faces he looked at later. The conditions involved are palinopsia and prosopometamorphopsia, which together cause earlier visual impressions to carry over and distort the faces in front of him.

### Why AI imagery matters

Researchers used AI-generated visuals to represent his distorted perceptions. The case links neuroscience, artificial intelligence and patient experience, showing how surgery or brain injury can alter perception. It is timely because AI is increasingly used not only for diagnosis or research but also to help patients communicate subjective symptoms that are hard to put into words.
