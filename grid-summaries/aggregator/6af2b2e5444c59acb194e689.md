## Moscow summit tests Russia's waning grip on former Soviet allies

A CSTO summit in Moscow is putting a spotlight on Russia's weakening influence over former Soviet partners. Armenia, which has effectively frozen its participation in the bloc, is the clearest example of that strain.

### Armenia's drift from the bloc

Armenia stepped back from the CSTO after the alliance failed to back it against Azerbaijan. The article argues that Russia's war in Ukraine, along with sanctions and military setbacks, has cut Moscow's ability to discipline or rally regional partners. Yerevan is also pursuing closer ties with the European Union.

### Why the timing matters

Post-Soviet states are increasingly testing alternatives to Russian-led security structures, and regional players are recalibrating their alliances. Russia, meanwhile, is constrained by the conflict in Ukraine and faces rising security concerns around Afghanistan. That combination makes this summit a revealing test of how much sway Moscow still holds.
