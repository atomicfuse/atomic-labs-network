## US reportedly shifts refueling tankers away from UK base over Iran concerns

The Wall Street Journal reports that the United States has relocated aerial refueling tankers from RAF Mildenhall in Britain, citing worries about a possible threat linked to Iran.

### What the report says

According to the article, the tankers were moved to Germany and other locations. The report ties the decision to an earlier step in which B-1 bombers were withdrawn from RAF Fairford. That withdrawal followed intelligence about a suspected plot involving a drone attack backed by Iran.

### Why it matters now

The account arrives as Iran's regional and global proxy activity faces closer scrutiny, including the potential danger to Western military assets. It also shows how quickly American force posture in Europe can change when intelligence warnings emerge.
