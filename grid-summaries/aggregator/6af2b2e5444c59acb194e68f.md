## Poland's Bitter Rivals Walk Together in Krakow Silence

President Karol Nawrocki and Prime Minister Donald Tusk joined thousands of people in Krakow's "March of Silence," held to honour six victims of a mass shooting. The two leaders are usually at odds, which made their shared presence notable.

### A rare show of unity

The appearance stands out because Nawrocki and Tusk remain locked in ongoing clashes over reforms and the extent of presidential authority. Seeing the two men take part in the same solemn event, alongside the crowd, offered a moment of common purpose that has been uncommon in Polish politics.

### Why the moment matters

The march comes as concern over public safety is running high and political polarization in Poland runs deep. The leaders' joint presence suggests that a national tragedy can, at least for a time, change the tone of political conflict, even while the underlying disagreements between them remain in place.
