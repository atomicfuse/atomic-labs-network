## Democrats Win House Seats in Districts Once Seen as Safely Republican

An article looks at several House races that Democrats won even though few expected them to, including districts long considered secure for Republicans. The winners flipped seats in places where the party had little reason for optimism going in.

### How the Upsets Happened

The candidates highlighted in the piece shared a few traits. They took moderate positions, built local credibility, and ran against opponents closely aligned with Trump. Voters in these districts appear to have responded to that combination, and the backlash against Trump-aligned candidates played a role in the outcomes.

### What It Could Mean

The wins suggest Democrats pushed the map into deep-red or newly redrawn territory. That may change assumptions about how suburban, veteran, and working-class voters behave. The results also point to real costs for Republicans tied to Trump alignment, candidate quality, and local scandals.
