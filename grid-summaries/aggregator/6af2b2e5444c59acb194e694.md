## MacCallum Urges Parents to Trust Their Instincts Over AI

Martha MacCallum has a message for parents tempted to turn to chatbots: think twice before using ChatGPT or similar AI tools to guide how you raise your children.

### Her central warning

MacCallum's main argument is that parental instinct and human judgment remain essential in raising kids. In her view, these qualities cannot be replaced by technology, no matter how convenient an AI tool might seem when a parent is looking for quick answers.

### Why the conversation is timely

More families are experimenting with AI for everyday decisions, which has made parenting advice a new frontier for tools like ChatGPT. Her comments tap into wider anxieties about where artificial intelligence should, and should not, fit into personal life. As the technology spreads into more routine choices, the question of how much weight to give it in family matters is likely to keep coming up.
