## McVay Blames Himself as Nacua Injury Likely Sends Receiver to IR

Rams receiver Puka Nacua is expected to land on injured reserve after getting hurt on the final play of a win over Washington. Coach Sean McVay has taken responsibility for the decision that led to the moment.

### The Injury and McVay's Response

Nacua suffered MCL and PCL sprains along with turf toe on the closing snap. McVay publicly said he was "kicking himself" over the play call, putting the blame on his own choice rather than anyone else. The injury leaves Los Angeles with serious depth problems at receiver, particularly because Davante Adams is also hurt.

### What Lies Ahead for the Rams

The Rams sit at 5-4 and are currently outside the playoff picture. Their schedule turns difficult now, with games against the Cardinals, Packers, Chiefs, and 49ers coming up. With both Nacua and Adams hurting, the team may have to make a move before the NFL trade deadline.
