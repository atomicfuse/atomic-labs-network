## Europe's 2026 Elections Point to a Fraying Political Consensus

An article examining Europe's 2026 elections argues that they expose a broad loss of political consensus across the continent. It points to volatile voters, fragmented party systems, newly emerging movements, and declining trust in established parties.

### National Instability, Wider Effects

The piece draws on cases from Serbia, Bulgaria, Hungary, Slovenia, and other countries to illustrate its case. In its telling, instability at the national level is reshaping how decisions are made within the European Union, as governments change hands and new political actors gain influence.

### A More Unpredictable EU

The significance lies in the environment the EU now faces. Elections that alter governments and complicate coalition-building make European politics harder to predict. Issues such as support for Ukraine, sanctions, enlargement, and foreign policy consensus may become more difficult to manage as a result.
