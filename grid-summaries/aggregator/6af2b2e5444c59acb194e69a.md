## Hannity Frames GOP Midterm Losses as Business as Usual

Fox News host Sean Hannity has tried to play down the Republican Party's heavy midterm losses. Rather than treating the results as a failure, he described the outcome as a fairly typical turn in the political cycle.

### Hannity's Framing

Hannity addressed the Democratic takeover of Congress by presenting it as routine. His suggestion was that the result was "kind of more normal than not," which recasts a major setback for Republicans as an expected swing instead of a disaster for the party.

### Why the Spin Matters

Midterm fallout helps shape party narratives, voter confidence, and the way conservative media explains setbacks to its audience. How the losses are framed also influences whether Republicans see them as a warning sign that demands a response or dismiss them as ordinary politics. Hannity's approach points toward the second reading.
