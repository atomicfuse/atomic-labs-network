## Kushner joins Abu Dhabi talks as Washington links Ukraine's borders to its future security

Renewed Russia-Ukraine peace talks are under way in Abu Dhabi, with U.S. envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff taking part. The discussions center on territorial compromises and security guarantees for Ukraine.

### Borders and safeguards tied together

The article argues that Washington sees postwar safeguards and border questions as linked rather than separate issues. Any agreement, it suggests, depends on whether flexibility expressed in private turns into real progress at the negotiating table.

### Why the timing matters

The talks follow months without trilateral negotiations and arrive amid shifting geopolitical pressures. These include U.S. domestic politics, Europe's role in security planning, and Trump's upcoming diplomacy with Putin and Xi. A possible "NATO-like" guarantee for Ukraine could reshape the endgame of the war and Europe's wider security architecture.
