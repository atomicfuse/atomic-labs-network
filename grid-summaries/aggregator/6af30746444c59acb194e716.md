## Trump Backs Hegseth as Massie Pushes Impeachment

President Trump has publicly defended War Secretary Pete Hegseth after Rep. Thomas Massie called for his impeachment. The exchange has put the Pentagon chief at the center of a dispute among Republicans.

### Trump's Defense

Trump argues that Hegseth deserves praise rather than removal from office. His message frames the impeachment push as misguided and out of step with those results.

### Why the Timing Matters

Massie has warned that Democrats could mount a harsher impeachment effort if they win control of Congress. Hegseth has become a flashpoint in wider Republican infighting and in potential future impeachment battles.
