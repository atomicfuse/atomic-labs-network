## Richmond's democratic socialists build local political muscle

A grassroots democratic socialist movement in Richmond is gaining influence at the municipal level after helping elect three candidates. The group is now looking ahead to what comes next.

### What the movement has achieved

The movement's success in getting three candidates elected has given it a foothold in local politics. Having secured those wins, the group is entering a new phase.
