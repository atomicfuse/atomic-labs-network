## Democrats Rack Up Major State-Level Gains After Midterms

Democrats emerged from the midterms with sweeping wins in state legislatures. The gains include 549 flipped legislative seats, 12 chambers changing hands, GOP supermajorities broken, and at least six new Democratic trifectas.

### Built on Years of Organizing

The article's central argument is that these results did not come out of nowhere. Years of state-focused organizing by Democrats have now translated into real governing power outside Washington, giving the party a stronger foothold in statehouses across the country.

### State Capitals as the Main Arena

With federal power still constrained by Trump's veto and hard-right staffing choices, state governments may become the main arena for Democratic policy wins. The new trifectas give the party room to move quickly on redistricting, Medicaid expansion, abortion rights, voting access, housing, guns, and education.
