## Police Officer Dies After Backpack Explodes Following Teen Shooting in Oviedo

A police officer has been killed in Oviedo, Spain, after a suspicious backpack detonated. The blast followed an emergency response to the shooting of a 17-year-old girl.

### What happened

Officers arrived in Oviedo after the teenager was shot. A backpack at the scene then exploded, and the officer died as a result. Authorities are now investigating whether the shooting and the booby-trapped device were linked. A dangerous suspect remains at large, and a manhunt is underway.

### Why it matters now

The case combines an active search for a suspect, a targeted shooting and an explosive device aimed at first responders, making it a major public-safety story in Spain. It also raises urgent questions about police response protocols and the risks officers face when they arrive at crime scenes.
