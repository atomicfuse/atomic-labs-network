## Democrats Press Interior Department to Preserve Trump Construction Records

House Democrats are calling on the Interior Department to safeguard records connected to Donald Trump's construction projects in the Washington area. Their demand follows claims from whistleblowers that important documents have already been destroyed.

### What Democrats Are Asking For

The lawmakers want the department to preserve all records tied to the projects. Whistleblowers told them that key paperwork has been destroyed, and the concern is that further evidence could vanish before Democrats gain greater oversight power.

### Why the Timing Matters

The request arrives amid heightened scrutiny of Trump-related business dealings and of government accountability. Whether records are kept intact could shape future investigations, subpoenas, and the public's understanding of potential conflicts of interest.
