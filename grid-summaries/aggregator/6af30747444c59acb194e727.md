## Hewitt Urges Trump to Prepare for Looming Constitutional Showdowns

Commentator Hugh Hewitt argues that President Trump should act before January, when a new Democratic Congress takes power. His piece warns that several major legal confrontations are on the horizon and says the White House needs answers ready.

### The Proposed Task for Legal Counsel

Hewitt says Trump should direct the Justice Department's Office of Legal Counsel to resolve big constitutional questions in advance. He identifies four flashpoints: executive privilege, expanding the Supreme Court, D.C. statehood, and the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact. He frames them collectively as a coming clash between Article I, which covers Congress, and Article II, which covers the presidency.

### Why the Timing Matters

The article ties into expected Democratic oversight and subpoena fights following major Democratic wins. Trump's team is preparing for aggressive investigations. The piece also reflects wider disputes over institutional power, election rules, and possible structural changes to the courts and the presidency.
