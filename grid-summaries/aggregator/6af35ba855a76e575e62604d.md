## Rubio Presses G7 Allies to Help Secure Strait of Hormuz

Marco Rubio has called on G7 nations to do more to protect shipping in the Strait of Hormuz as Iranian drone attacks continue. He acknowledged that allies have not yet committed to providing support.

### Pressure on Allies

Rubio's appeal urges partner nations to step up and share the security burden in the waterway. The lack of firm commitments so far points to a gap between what Washington wants and what its allies have been willing to pledge. G7 talks also covered Ukraine, defense assistance, and diplomacy with Russia.

### Why the Strait Matters

The Strait of Hormuz is a critical global energy chokepoint, so instability there can quickly affect oil markets, shipping costs, and international security. The story also shows how conflicts in the Middle East and Ukraine are straining coordination among Western partners, while pressure grows on U.S. allies to carry more of the load.
