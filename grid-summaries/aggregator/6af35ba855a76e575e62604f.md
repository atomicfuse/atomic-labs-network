## Marjorie Taylor Greene Rejects Trump's Fresh Claims of Election Trouble

Marjorie Taylor Greene has publicly pushed back on Donald Trump's renewed suggestions that recent election results were questionable. She accuses him of reaching for the same "rigged" language he used in 2020 to avoid taking responsibility for Republican defeats.

### Greene's Explanation for the Losses

Greene argues the party's poor showing had little to do with fraud. In her view, voters had grown tired of Trump, the party's strategy was weak, and many Republican supporters simply stayed home instead of casting ballots. She places the blame squarely on the former president, saying it is "100% on him."

### A Widening Split in MAGA

Her comments matter because they come from a onetime loyalist, which points to a deepening divide inside the movement after major Republican midterm losses. Blame appears to be moving away from Democrats and voting systems and toward Trump himself, a shift that could shape how the party reckons with its setbacks.
