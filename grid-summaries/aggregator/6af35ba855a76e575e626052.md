## Thunberg Returns to UN Climate Talks at COP31 in Antalya

Greta Thunberg has gone back to the UN climate summit for the first time since 2021. Her appearance at COP31 in Antalya follows a period in which her public profile has been shaped largely by pro-Palestinian activism.

### A call to put people first

At the summit, Thunberg urged governments to give priority to people and the planet rather than to profit and geopolitical power. The coverage presents her return to climate advocacy alongside her recent activism for Palestinians and her confrontations with Israel, treating the two strands of her public work as connected.

### Why the timing matters

The summit arrives after another year of extreme heat and climate disasters. Global politics around Gaza and Israel also continue to influence activist movements and diplomatic spaces. Thunberg's return points to how climate advocacy is increasingly bound up with human rights, war and criticism of greenwashing.
