## Board of Peace Calls Israel's Gaza Aid Response Insufficient After Floods

The Board of Peace has made a rare criticism of Israel's handling of aid to Gaza after winter floods swamped tens of thousands of tents. The flooding has worsened displacement and shelter shortages that were already severe.

### A shelter crisis becomes an emergency

Heavy winter rains have turned Gaza's shelter shortage into an urgent humanitarian emergency. More than a million people are living in tents or makeshift shelters. Israel, Hamas, the UN and aid officials have each put forward competing claims about who is responsible for delays in getting supplies in.

### Why the criticism carries weight

The remarks are politically significant because they come from a US-backed body linked to Trump's Gaza peace plan. Implementation of the ceasefire remains stalled, and the same parties are trading blame over the lack of progress on that plan as the humanitarian situation worsens.
