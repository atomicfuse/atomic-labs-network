## Massie Pushes Impeachment of Hegseth Over Iran War and More

Rep. Thomas Massie is pushing to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth through eight articles. They cover the Iran war, civilian casualties, Caribbean boat strikes and retaliation claims. The House is preparing to vote on whether to advance them.

### What the articles allege

The push centers on whether Hegseth exceeded his legal authority and ignored congressional limits on military action. The allegations tie him to the conduct of the Iran war and the harm to civilians that resulted. They also address strikes on boats in the Caribbean and claims of retaliation.

### Why the vote matters now

The coming House vote raises the stakes for a party already split over the Iran war. Divisions among Republicans, along with political fallout after the midterms, add pressure. The story sits at the crossroads of war powers, executive overreach, civilian harm and open Republican infighting.
