## House GOP Leaders Sidestep Massie's Push to Impeach Hegseth

House Republican leaders stopped Rep. Thomas Massie from forcing a floor vote on impeaching Secretary of War Pete Hegseth. They did so by sending his resolution to the Judiciary Committee.

### A narrow procedural win

The referral is framed as a close victory for Republican leadership. Concerns about absences, divisions within the party, and instability following the midterms hung over the effort. By moving the resolution to committee, leaders kept it from reaching a direct vote on the House floor.

### Why it matters now

The outcome follows a difficult midterm cycle for Republicans and points to growing friction inside the GOP. That tension touches on Trump-era military actions, executive power, and tests of loyalty. It also shows how procedural tools can decide the fate of high-stakes impeachment efforts before they ever get to the floor.
