## Salazar Holds Florida's 27th District Seat for Republicans

Republican Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar has won another term in the U.S. House, taking Florida's 27th Congressional District. She defeated Democratic challenger Eliott Rodriguez in a contest that drew attention for its focus on immigration.

### A Race Shaped by Immigration

Immigration was a central theme of the campaign. Both Salazar and Rodriguez are former journalists, and both criticized Donald Trump's hardline approach to the issue. That shared stance made the matchup notable, as the candidates competed over how to address a policy area that resonates strongly in the district.

### What the Result Signals

Salazar's victory shows how Republican candidates in heavily Hispanic districts are handling backlash to immigration policy while still keeping GOP seats. It also underscores the ongoing political weight of Miami-area Latino voters in national elections, whose choices continue to draw attention well beyond South Florida.
