## Baldwin Bill Would Open Medicare Buy-In to Americans 50 to 64

Sen. Tammy Baldwin has introduced the Medicare at 50 Act, a bill that would let people between ages 50 and 64 buy into Medicare. The proposal is presented as a response to rising health care costs and gaps in coverage for older adults.

### What the bill would do

Under the legislation, participants could access benefits under Medicare Parts A, B and D, including Medicare Advantage options. The measure is framed around high premiums in the ACA Marketplace and the situation of older adults who are not yet eligible for Medicare.

### Why the timing matters

Health care affordability remains a major issue after the election. Premiums for older ACA Marketplace enrollees reportedly doubled in 2026, and enrollment dropped. The proposal feeds into wider debates over Medicare expansion and aging Americans, and it reflects how Democrats are positioning their health care policy after the midterms.
