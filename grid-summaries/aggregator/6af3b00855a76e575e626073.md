## Clashes Flare in Bangladesh as Hasina Loyalists Rally Nationwide

Police in Bangladesh used tear gas and rubber bullets against supporters of ousted prime minister Sheikh Hasina, who took to the streets in nationwide demonstrations calling for her to come home from exile in India.

### What Happened

The protests are the first major organised show of force by Hasina loyalists since she was removed from power in 2024. Demonstrators gathered despite bans on her party's activities and a death sentence handed down against her in absentia. Security forces responded with crowd-control measures, and violence broke out as the confrontations spread.

### Why It Matters Now

Hasina is seeking a passport to return, while the current government describes her as a fugitive and wants India to extradite her. The clashes underline tensions left unresolved since the 2024 uprising. They also add to concerns about violence, claims of repression and possible regional fallout, as Bangladesh faces renewed political instability.
