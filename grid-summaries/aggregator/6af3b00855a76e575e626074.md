## Democratic Gains Revive Push to Sideline the Electoral College

New Democratic control of several state governments has given fresh momentum to the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact, an effort that could effectively override the Electoral College without amending the Constitution.

### The Political Math

The article lays out how the compact could reach the threshold it needs as more Democratic-led states consider joining. Some governors are supportive, while others appear hesitant about committing. Republicans largely oppose the idea, and the piece anticipates constitutional challenges if enough states sign on and the plan moves toward real-world use.

### Why the Timing Matters

Recent Democratic wins in key swing-state governments have turned a long-running reform idea into a plausible near-term fight. With the 2028 race already on the horizon and public trust in election systems deeply polarized, any move to change how presidents are chosen would likely become one of the country's largest democracy debates.
