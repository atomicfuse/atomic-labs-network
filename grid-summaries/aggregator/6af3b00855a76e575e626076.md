## North Carolina Becomes a Democratic Bright Spot in the Midterms

NBC News examines how Democrats outperformed expectations in North Carolina during the midterms, even as Republicans pushed redistricting efforts meant to protect their position in the state.

### Democrats' Gains in the House

Democrats flipped five House seats in North Carolina and could pick up more. That performance stands out because Republicans had tried to reshape district lines in their favor. The analysis also notes leadership changes among Republicans after the election, including Jim Jordan's rise to become the House GOP leader.

### What It Means for 2028

After years in which Republicans held only narrow advantages, North Carolina now looks like a serious battleground for Democrats. That raises questions about strategy heading into 2028. The results point to possible shifts among young men, unaffiliated voters and inconsistent voters, groups whose movement could reshape how future campaigns are run in the state and beyond.
