## Sand Dune Collapses on Gaza Tents as Storm Batters Displaced Families

Heavy rain in Gaza set off a deadly collapse of a sand dune onto tents in Deir al-Balah, adding to the hardship of displaced Palestinians. A video report shows the aftermath of the storm.

### Shelters and Belongings Lost

The storm destroyed shelters and belongings for thousands of people, according to Gaza authorities and the U.N. The dune collapse onto the tents in Deir al-Balah was one of the deadliest effects of the downpour, which left families with even less protection than before.

### Why It Matters Now

Displaced people in Gaza face extreme exposure as winter weather compounds the humanitarian crisis. The story shows how temporary shelters and overcrowded camps are vulnerable not only to conflict but also to climate and infrastructure shocks. With so many people living in tents, severe weather can quickly turn into another emergency.
