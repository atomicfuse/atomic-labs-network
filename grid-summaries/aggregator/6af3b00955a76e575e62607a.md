## House Republicans Shelve Push to Impeach Hegseth

House Republicans have stopped an effort by Rep. Thomas Massie to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, sending the resolution to committee on a narrow procedural vote.

### What the Vote Showed

The House voted 211-204 to refer the resolution to committee, effectively blocking it. Massie's measure accused Hegseth of illegally carrying out President Trump's Iran war and of other abuses. Despite the accusations, Republican lawmakers largely stayed aligned with the administration and did not back the impeachment push.

### Why the Timing Matters

The vote arrives amid backlash over the ongoing Iran war, rising gas prices, and Republican losses in the midterms. Those pressures make war powers and party loyalty a live political fault line. The outcome also shows that internal GOP dissent can be contained when Trump's agenda is at stake.
