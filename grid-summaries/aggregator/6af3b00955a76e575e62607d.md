## Bob Boone, Baseball Champion and Family Patriarch, Dies at 78

Bob Boone, a World Series-winning catcher and a central figure in the first three-generation family in Major League Baseball, has died at the age of 78. He was also a manager, and his passing is being felt across the sport.

### A Career Behind the Plate

Boone was a four-time All-Star and won seven Gold Gloves as a catcher. He played for the Phillies, Angels, and Royals, and he later moved into managing. He also played a role in advancing player rights, a part of his legacy that goes beyond his on-field achievements.

### A Legacy Across Generations

His death links several eras of baseball history, from his father Ray to his sons Bret and Aaron. Tributes from the Phillies, Royals, Yankees, and the MLBPA point to his influence on the field and on the modern player experience. The Boone family's long connection to the game continues to stand out.
