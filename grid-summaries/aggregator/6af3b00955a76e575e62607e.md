## Oversight Democrat Says Four Former Trump Officials Remain Under Investigation

Rep. Robert Garcia, the ranking member on House Oversight, says the committee is already looking into alleged corruption involving several former Trump administration figures. He named Susie Wiles, Kimberly Guilfoyle, Corey Lewandowski, and Kristi Noem.

### The Accountability Argument

Garcia's central point is that leaving the administration does not protect former officials from scrutiny. In his telling, departing a government post does not put past conduct out of reach, and the committee is continuing to examine the people he listed despite their changed status.

### Why the Timing Matters

The remarks follow Wiles' resignation and new allegations that Trump insiders used government roles to benefit private industries or donors. They also arrive amid wider fights over accountability after the administration and a series of corruption probes, giving Garcia's warning added political weight as Oversight Democrats press their case.
