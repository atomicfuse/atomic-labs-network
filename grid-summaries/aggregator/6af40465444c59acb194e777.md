## El-Sayed Offers Trump Credit for Medicare-for-All

Abdul El-Sayed used an appearance on CNN to make a direct appeal to Donald Trump: back universal healthcare and attach your own name to it. The pitch put a personal spin on a long-running policy argument.

### The Offer

El-Sayed promoted Medicare-for-all and said the label matters far less than the outcome. Speaking straight to Trump on live television, he argued that getting universal healthcare in place should outweigh any concern over branding, so the president would be welcome to call it Trumpcare. The piece sets the invitation against Trump's years of vague promises to replace Obamacare.

### Why It Matters Now

Healthcare has returned to the political spotlight after midterm gains gave Democrats fresh leverage. Trump, meanwhile, still has no detailed alternative plan to point to. El-Sayed's "call it Trumpcare" line recasts the policy debate as a viral, personality-driven challenge, one aimed squarely at the president.
