## Climate Change Is Now a Political Fight, Not Just a Science Problem

An article on the covert politics of climate change argues that the issue can no longer be treated mainly as a challenge of explaining the science. It looks at how misinformation, polarization, unstable policy, and local environmental justice disputes shape the debate.

### From Denial to Doubt

As extreme weather becomes harder to ignore, the article says misinformation has shifted away from outright denial and toward creating doubt. At the same time, climate policy remains exposed to partisan swings, which makes lasting action difficult. The piece frames these problems as political and structural rather than simply matters of communication.

### Local Stakes

The article points to the Hennepin Energy Recovery Center as an example of how national climate arguments play out close to home. Fights like this one turn broad debates into concrete questions about public health and accountability for the communities affected.
