## Honduran Man Arrested After Alleged Cockpit Bid on United Flight

Rony Alexander Gomez-Alberto, a Honduran national who had previously been deported twice, was arrested after an incident aboard a United Airlines flight, according to the Department of Homeland Security.

### What Happened

DHS says Gomez-Alberto allegedly tried to enter the cockpit and claimed to have a bomb. He was arrested following the incident. DHS reports that Immigration and Customs Enforcement lodged a detainer after the arrest. United Airlines confirmed that the flight landed safely in New Orleans.

### Why It Matters Now

The case touches on aviation security, immigration enforcement, and public safety, subjects already central to political debate in the United States. It also arrives amid heightened scrutiny of disruptive passenger incidents and of how authorities respond to threats made during flights.
