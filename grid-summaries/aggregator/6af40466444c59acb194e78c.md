## Spain's Workers Walk Out in First General Strike Since 2012

Spain is holding its first general strike in 14 years, with workers and housing activists pressing for action on soaring rents, evictions, tourist apartments, and wage pressures. The walkout doubles as a major political test before a national election that could shift power to the right.

### Housing anger takes center stage

Frustration over housing has surged after the televised eviction and death of an elderly Madrid tenant, which has become a national symbol. Protesters are using the case to highlight how rising costs and displacement are affecting people's daily lives. Their demands cover rents, evictions, tourist apartments, and pay that struggles to keep pace.

### A test for the election campaign

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is trying to steer the campaign back toward social rights. Meanwhile, the far right is gaining momentum. How well the strike is observed, and whether any unrest breaks out, could help shape the story voters hear as the election approaches.
