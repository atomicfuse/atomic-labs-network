## Ex-West Point Cadet Faces Civil Suit Over Drugging and Abortion Pills

A civil lawsuit has been filed against Caleb Suh, a former West Point cadet who pleaded guilty to drugging his pregnant girlfriend and giving her abortion pills without her consent, which caused a miscarriage.

### The Case Against Suh

The lawsuit follows Suh's guilty plea in the criminal matter. It centers on his actions toward his then-girlfriend, who was pregnant at the time. Suh's sentencing has been delayed, and the new civil filing renews attention on a case that began with the original criminal proceedings.

### Questions About Accountability

While awaiting sentencing, Suh attended Cornell and Columbia. That fact has raised questions about accountability and about how institutions screen applicants. The case also arrives amid national debates over abortion pills, telehealth prescribing, campus safety, and how elite schools treat applicants with serious misconduct histories.
