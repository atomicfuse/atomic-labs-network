## Eswatini Moves to Shut Foreign-Run Shops Under New Empowerment Law

Eswatini is closing Asian-owned and other foreign-run businesses as it enforces a new Citizen Economic Empowerment law. The measure aims to transfer retail ownership to emaSwati citizens, and Parliament has backed a 12-month timeline for foreign operators to exit.

### Debate Over Readiness and Impact

The policy has prompted argument over whether local citizens are prepared to take over the shops being vacated. Concerns have also been raised about xenophobia, the livelihoods of those affected, and how the rules will be enforced in practice.

### A Wider Regional Pattern

The crackdown arrives during severe economic hardship, with poverty affecting much of the population. Pressure against foreign traders is rising across Southern Africa, and similar actions in South Africa and Kenya point to a broader regional flashpoint involving immigration, informal economies, and citizen empowerment.
