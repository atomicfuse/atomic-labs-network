## Democrats' Three Economic Promises May Not Fit Together

An article argues that Democrats could soon get a rare opening to pass a major economic agenda, but the party's central pledges may clash with one another.

### The Contradiction

The three promises are expanding the welfare state, cutting taxes for most Americans, and bringing down inflation and interest rates. According to the article, these goals are likely incompatible. Democrats may have to abandon or rethink one of them, unless they concentrate on boosting the country's productive capacity.

### Why It Matters Now

Following a major midterm win, Democrats are positioned to potentially control Washington in 2028, which makes these contradictions more pressing. Meanwhile, voters, even in Democratic-leaning electorates, are showing strong resistance to broad tax increases, narrowing the party's options for paying for its plans.
