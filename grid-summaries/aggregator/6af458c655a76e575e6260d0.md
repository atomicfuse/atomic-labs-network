## Rep. Madeleine Dean weighs 2028 Senate run, taking aim at Fetterman

Pennsylvania Democratic Rep. Madeleine Dean is considering a Senate campaign in 2028. Such a run could set up a Democratic primary contest against Sen. John Fetterman, whom she has openly criticized.

### Dean's criticisms

Dean has pointed to several areas of concern about the incumbent. They include Fetterman's health, concerns involving his family, and his voting record. She has also suggested he has drifted away from the Democratic Party, making his standing among fellow Democrats a central theme of her remarks.

### Why it matters now

Challengers are beginning to test the waters years ahead of the 2028 election, and Fetterman's place within the Democratic coalition is emerging as a flashpoint. The story reflects wider tensions over party loyalty, ideological identity, and electability in Pennsylvania, a key battleground state.
