## Michigan Utilities Challenge Voter-Approved Campaign Finance Limits

Michigan voters approved Proposal 2, a broad campaign finance measure that limits political donations from several groups. Two of the state's biggest utilities, DTE and Consumers Energy, have now taken the law to court.

### What the law does and why it is being challenged

Proposal 2 restricts political contributions from utilities, state contractors, senior employees, lobbyists, and family members. DTE and Consumers Energy argue that the measure violates constitutional rights to speech and political participation. Their lawsuit sets up a legal test of how far a voter-backed initiative can go in limiting donations tied to regulated companies.

### Why the timing matters

The dispute arrives as energy bills rise, data centers drive growing demand, and utilities propose new infrastructure spending. Scrutiny of utility influence in state politics is also increasing. Michigan is becoming a test case for whether voters can curb corporate political power in heavily regulated industries.
