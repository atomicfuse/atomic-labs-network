## Hundreds of Italian Schools Cleared After Identical Bomb Threats

At least 300 schools across Italy were evacuated after receiving the same bomb threat message. The scale of the incident triggered a nationwide security response and a police investigation.

### What happened

Officials said students were moved to safety as schools received the identical message. Police have opened an investigation into the threats. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni later stated that the threats posed no danger, though the evacuations had already disrupted classes in hundreds of locations.

### Why it matters now

The episode comes as anxiety grows across Europe over so-called hybrid warfare tactics, which target public infrastructure, media, transport and essential services. Schools are especially sensitive places, so even a false threat can cause major disruption and creates political urgency. The case shows how a single message, sent widely, can prompt a response on a national scale.
