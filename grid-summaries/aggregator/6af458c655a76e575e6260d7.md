## Bukele Bets on Haiti Gang Offensive Before Vote

El Salvador's president, Nayib Bukele, has sent Salvadoran troops to Haiti in an operation that serves two purposes: fighting gangs and shaping his political message. The deployment comes ahead of El Salvador's February election.

### A High-Risk Export of a Security Brand

The article argues the offensive is a risky effort to take Bukele's hardline security approach beyond El Salvador's borders. Results remain uncertain, and the operation must be carried out while he faces scrutiny over how openly it is run and over concerns about democratic standards.

### Why the Timing Matters

Haiti's gang crisis has become an international flashpoint, and Bukele is trying to present himself as the leader able to solve it. At home he faces criticism over transparency, human rights and democratic backsliding. He is expected to win the election, yet the move could still affect his legitimacy and his foreign alliances.
