## An Argument That Prop. 50 Was a Mistake Even in Victory

An article contends that California's Proposition 50, a Democratic redistricting measure, was the wrong move even though it helped Democrats win seats. Voters passed it in response to Republican gerrymandering in Texas and other states.

### The Core Objection

The piece's central claim is that partisan map-rigging undermines representative democracy. In its view, drawing districts for political advantage leaves millions of voters feeling politically erased, and that cost remains whichever party benefits. The article does not accept the argument that the measure's results justify it.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

Democrats have won big in the midterms and taken the House, which prompts the question of whether "fighting fire with fire" was necessary or damaging. The article arrives as redistricting and gerrymandering have become central political debates, alongside positioning around Gavin Newsom ahead of 2028.
