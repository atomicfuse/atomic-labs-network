## Dua Lipa Tapped to Make Coachella History in 2027

Coachella has revealed its 2027 line-up, and Dua Lipa will become the first solo female British artist to headline the festival. She will top the bill alongside Tame Impala and SZA.

### The Line-Up

Beyond the three headliners, the poster features a wide mix of performers. Zara Larsson, Phoebe Bridgers, Diljit Dosanjh, Jisoo, Kehlani, Weezer and Robyn are all among the acts announced, spanning pop, indie, rock and R&B and drawing artists from several parts of the world.

### Why It Matters

Dua Lipa's booking is a significant milestone for British women at one of the planet's most visible festivals. It also arrives amid continuing conversations about who earns top billing at major events, a debate that gained fresh attention after Charli XCX's "Miss Should Be Headliner" moment at Coachella 2025.
