## Trump Jr. Backs Russell Brand After Rape Sentencing

Donald Trump Jr. has publicly defended Russell Brand, who was sentenced to 10 and a half years in prison for rape and sexual assault.

### The Defense

Trump Jr. argues that Brand's prosecution is evidence of a "two-tier justice system." He links the case to Brand's move toward conservative views. His claim comes despite the jury's convictions and the judge's condemnation of Brand's conduct, which stand as the outcome of the legal process.

### Why It Matters

The case touches on celebrity accountability, partisan media narratives, and ongoing debates about justice systems in the United States and the United Kingdom. Trump Jr.'s remarks add to a wider conservative approach of portraying criminal prosecutions of allies as politically motivated. How such framing interacts with court verdicts remains part of that larger argument over fairness and politics.
