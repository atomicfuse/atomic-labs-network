## Witnesses Describe Hearing a "Bomb" as Learjet Falls Near Florida Road

911 audio has surfaced from people who saw and heard a Learjet crash in Palm Beach County, Florida. All nine people on board died, among them a young family flying from Texas to the Bahamas.

### What Witnesses Reported

Callers to emergency dispatchers described both the sound and the sight of the aircraft coming down. Some said the noise was like a bomb. The crash happened close to a busy road in South Florida, which made it especially visible and alarming to people nearby. The victims' youth has added to the attention the story is receiving.

### The Investigation

Federal investigators are examining the crash, with severe weather central to their inquiry. The cockpit voice recorder has been recovered, and the National Transportation Safety Board is now analyzing it. That work could soon bring new details about what caused the plane to go down.
