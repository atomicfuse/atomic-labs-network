## Chris Harrison Returns to Dating TV With Fox Nation's "The Vow"

Fox Nation is launching its first original dating show, "The Vow," and Chris Harrison is back in the genre as both host and executive producer.

### What the Series Is About

The show follows 30-year-old Jace Cates as he spends time with 10 women. Together they explore faith, family, communication and what it means to commit to marriage. The focus is on building lasting partnerships rather than chasing quick drama.

### Why It Matters Now

Dating shows are competing for viewers who have grown tired of influencer-driven romance and short-term drama. "The Vow" takes a faith-forward, marriage-minded approach, arriving at a time when conversations about modern dating, commitment and traditional values are especially visible.
