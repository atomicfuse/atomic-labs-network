## Richmond upon Thames named Britain's happiest place to live

Richmond upon Thames has topped Rightmove's annual "happy at home index," taking the title of Britain's happiest place to live. The survey points to a mix of everyday strengths behind the result, though living there carries a hefty price tag.

### What the survey found

The area scored highly across a range of measures, including green space, local services, shops, restaurants, jobs and civic pride. Together, these factors put it ahead of other places in the country. The trade-off is cost: the average asking price in Richmond upon Thames is above £1m.

### Why it matters now

With housing affordability under pressure, the findings highlight what people increasingly value in a place to live: nature, community, services and access to work. The result also feeds into the ongoing debate over whether Britain's best places to live are becoming accessible only to wealthier residents.
