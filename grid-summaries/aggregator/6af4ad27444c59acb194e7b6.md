## Sudan's Burhan Calls for Politics-Free Climate Finance

Sudan's Abdel Fattah al-Burhan has argued that money meant to tackle climate change should be easy for developing countries to obtain and kept separate from political demands or geopolitical rivalries.

### The Argument

Burhan's position centers on two points: access and neutrality. He says developing nations should be able to reach climate funds without facing obstacles, and that such funding should not depend on political conditions or be affected by disputes between powers. In his view, climate support should be treated as a practical tool for helping countries cope, not as leverage.

### Why It Matters Now

Climate finance remains one of the most contested topics in global climate negotiations. Vulnerable countries are pressing for funding that arrives faster and is distributed more fairly, so they can address climate damage and pay for adaptation. Burhan's remarks add Sudan's voice to that push as the debate over how money is delivered continues.
