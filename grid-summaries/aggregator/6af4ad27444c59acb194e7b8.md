## Indonesia's Vice-President Gibran Reportedly Steps Down

Indonesia's Vice-President Gibran Rakabuming Raka has reportedly submitted his resignation, according to an aide. The reported move follows court rulings that questioned whether his high-school equivalency credentials are valid.

### A Strained Political Alliance

Gibran is the son of former president Jokowi, and his departure threatens the alliance between Jokowi and President Prabowo Subianto. The partnership has been central to the country's political landscape since the last election, so the resignation could signal a significant split within the post-election power structure.

### Pressure Mounting on Prabowo

The news arrives while Prabowo already faces considerable turmoil. Challenges include cabinet changes, corruption concerns, student protests, wildfire crises and funding problems affecting his free school meals programme. Gibran's exit adds another layer of difficulty to an already crowded set of political problems for the president.
