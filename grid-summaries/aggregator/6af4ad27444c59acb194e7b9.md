## Florida Stays Red, but Midterms Show a Tighter Race Ahead

An analysis of Florida's 2026 midterm results finds that Democrats made gains both nationwide and within the state. Republicans still narrowly kept control of statewide offices and the Legislature, leaving the state's overall political leanings intact but less certain.

### A Red State With New Competition

The article argues Florida remains Republican-leaning but is now more competitive than before. Democrats flipped some state House seats, several congressional contests were close, and uncertainty surrounds redistricting. Together, those factors suggest the Republican advantage is narrower than it once appeared.

### What Comes Next

Strong Democratic showings have revived debate over whether Florida can still be counted as reliably Republican heading into 2028. Ashley Moody's Senate seat will return to the ballot soon, and coming property tax changes add another point of tension. The state could become a significant battleground for both politics and policy.
