## Browns Stick With Deshaun Watson as Starter, Keeping Shedeur Sanders on the Bench

Cleveland has confirmed there is no quarterback controversy. Deshaun Watson will take back the starting job even though Shedeur Sanders played well as a fill-in against the Saints.

### Why Watson Keeps the Job

The decision is framed around Watson's health, his contract status and the short run Sanders was given. Watson is coming back from injury, and the team has chosen to return him to the top of the depth chart rather than reward Sanders' strong outing with a longer look. Sanders' appearance was brief, but it drew plenty of attention.

### What It Means for Cleveland

The quarterback position is under close scrutiny. Sanders has shown flashes that could energize fans, and he is a potential future starter. The Browns must weigh the push for short-term wins against the sunk cost of Watson's contract and the long-term picture. That balance carries immediate team-building implications.
