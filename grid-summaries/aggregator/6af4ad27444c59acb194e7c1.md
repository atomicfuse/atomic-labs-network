## House Democrats Weigh New Impeachment Push Against Hegseth Over Ukraine Aid

Some House Democrats may seek fresh impeachment articles against War Secretary Pete Hegseth, according to Fox News. The possible move centers on allegations tied to Ukraine military aid and follows an earlier impeachment effort by Rep. Thomas Massie that did not succeed.

### What is driving the effort

The report presents the potential campaign as two things at once: an oversight fight over how Ukraine aid was handled, and a partisan contest over Trump administration officials. The allegations involving the aid are the stated basis for any new articles, though the effort has not yet moved beyond lawmakers' warnings.

### Why the timing matters

Democrats are preparing to take control of Congress in January, which could hand them subpoena power, committee leadership, and the ability to hold hearings. The story arrives amid renewed disputes over executive war powers and Ukraine aid, along with a broader debate over whether impeachment serves as accountability or as political deterrence.
