## Nonprofit Guides Veterans Toward Careers as Commercial Drone Pilots

Vets to Drones, a nonprofit organization, is helping former service members turn their military experience into certified careers as commercial drone pilots. The story presents drone piloting as a practical second career after military service.

### What the Program Offers

The organization focuses on helping veterans translate the skills they built in uniform into credentials that civilian employers recognize. By guiding them toward certification, it positions drone work as a realistic next step for people leaving the military and searching for stable, meaningful jobs.

### Why the Timing Matters

Demand for drone work is growing across construction, public safety, agriculture, and infrastructure. Many veterans want civilian roles that value technical discipline and mission-focused training, which makes the match a natural one. With Veterans Day approaching, the spotlight on veteran employment and transition support makes this story especially timely.
