## Stay Local This Holiday: Four Ways to Plan an NYC Staycation

New Yorkers hoping to unwind over the holidays without leaving town have some practical guidance to draw on. A new article lays out four tips for planning a staycation in the city, covering everything from mindset to lodging.

### The four tips

The advice starts with treating New York like a tourist destination rather than your home turf. It then suggests booking a hotel for the stay, keeping your plans flexible, and trying something new instead of defaulting to familiar routines. Together, the ideas aim to help locals feel as though they have truly gotten away.

### Why now

Holiday travel often means weather delays, crowded airports, and steep costs, so staying put can look appealing to city residents seeking a refresh. Seasonal markets, lights, performances, and hotel deals make the city especially well suited to a staycation at this time of year.
