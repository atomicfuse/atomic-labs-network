## Tauck Opens Bookings for 2028 Global Journeys & Safaris

Tauck has begun taking reservations for its 2028 Global Journeys & Safaris collection. The lineup adds new trips in Asia, week-long itineraries in Peru and Costa Rica, and a broader selection of Women-Only Departures.

The new offerings span experiential, wildlife, and small-group travel, pointing to fresh product development across those areas.
