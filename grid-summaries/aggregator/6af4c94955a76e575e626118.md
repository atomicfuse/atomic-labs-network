## Viva Resorts by Wyndham Rolls Out VIVA PROmotor for Travel Advisors

Viva Resorts by Wyndham has introduced VIVA PROmotor, a new tool built to help travel advisors promote and sell the resort brand with greater effectiveness. The launch is aimed squarely at the advisor community.

### What the Launch Signals

By releasing the tool, the company is positioned as investing more directly in advisor support and sales enablement. Rather than leaving advisors to build their own promotional efforts, the brand is offering a resource designed to make that work easier and more productive.

### Why It Matters Now

Travel advisors remain a key booking channel, particularly as travelers look for curated, trusted vacation planning. In that setting, supplier tools that simplify marketing can give advisors a competitive edge. Making it easier to promote a resort brand may help advisors turn customer interest into confirmed bookings.
