## Ten National Parks You Can Reach by Amtrak

A new practical guide lays out how travelers can get to 10 U.S. national parks using Amtrak, offering a car-light alternative to the classic road trip. It is built around real planning needs rather than inspiration alone.

### What the guide covers

For each of the ten parks, the article identifies the closest Amtrak stations and explains whether a transfer is required to complete the journey. It also points out the best times of year to visit, the main activities on offer, and options for where to stay. Together, those details are meant to help readers turn a train route into a workable itinerary.

### Why it matters now

The guide arrives as interest grows in slower, lower-stress and more sustainable ways to travel. Park crowds and road-trip fatigue continue to rise, and its logistics have been updated for 2026. That makes it timely for anyone planning a vacation around Amtrak routes while keeping car use to a minimum.
