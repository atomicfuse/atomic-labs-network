## Singapore Airlines Bets Big on Sky-High Comfort With Giant First-Class Beds

Singapore Airlines is putting $1.6 billion into a makeover of its long-haul cabins. The centerpiece is a set of first-class double beds that the carrier says will be the largest in the industry, backed by comfort upgrades in business and economy.

### What is changing

The investment reaches beyond the most exclusive seats. While the first-class double beds take the spotlight as the headline feature, the airline is also improving comfort in business class and in economy. The overhaul covers its long-haul cabins, the flights where passengers spend the most time on board.

### Why it matters now

Airlines are competing harder for premium travelers, and luxury flyers increasingly expect hotel-like comfort in the air. Singapore Airlines' spending points to a wider push to make long-haul flying more flexible and more high-end, with improvements felt across every cabin class.
