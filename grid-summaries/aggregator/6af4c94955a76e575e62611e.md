## Dulles Debuts Concourse E, Its First New Concourse in Two Decades

Washington Dulles International Airport has opened Concourse E, a $745 million, 14-gate facility used by United Airlines. It is the first stage of a modernization plan costing more than $20 billion.

### What travelers will find

The concourse is built around a better passenger experience. It connects to the AeroTrain, lets in plenty of natural light, and offers upgraded amenities along with dining from local names. Design touches pay tribute to architect Eero Saarinen, and the space includes a very large new United Club.

### Why the timing matters

Dulles is the fastest-growing airport among the nation's 50 largest, and crowding, confusing transfers, and outdated mobile lounges have frustrated many passengers. The opening points to a significant shift in how the airport hopes to compete for modern travelers, and it fits with United's expanding hub ambitions.
