## Railbookers and AmaWaterways Team Up on Combined River and Rail Journeys

Railbookers and AmaWaterways have joined forces to launch new European itineraries that pair river cruising with rail travel. The offering is positioned as a distinctive business-to-business product aimed at advisors and travel sellers.

### What the Partnership Offers

The new itineraries bring together two ways of seeing Europe: sailing along its rivers and riding its railways. Rather than a single-mode holiday, travelers can experience multiple destinations through one connected trip. The product is designed to be sold through travel advisors and other sellers, not directly pitched as a consumer brand launch.

### Why It Matters for Advisors

Travelers are increasingly looking for seamless, multi-modal European vacations, and suppliers are responding with more convenient ways to explore several destinations. For advisors, the pairing offers a fresh product angle that blends slow travel, luxury cruising, and the convenience of rail, giving them something new to present to clients.
