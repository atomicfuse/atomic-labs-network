## Army sentinel reprimands Hegseth and entourage at Arlington

Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth and the people travelling with him were reprimanded by an Army sentinel at Arlington National Cemetery. The rebuke came after the group appeared to break decorum rules near the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier.

### The Veterans Day setting

The incident took place around a Veterans Day ceremony that Trump, JD Vance and other officials attended. The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier is among the most sacred military sites in the country, and on Veterans Day public expectations of respect and symbolism are especially high. That is a large part of why the moment is drawing attention.

### Scrutiny of Hegseth

The reprimand arrives while Hegseth is already under political scrutiny. He faces continued controversy over Iran war powers, along with impeachment efforts. Against that backdrop, the encounter at the memorial has become another point of focus in debate over his conduct and standing.
