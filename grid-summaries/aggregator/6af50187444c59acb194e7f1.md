## Vance may front Iran talks as IRGC hard-liner clouds prospects

President Trump has authorized Vice President JD Vance to potentially lead a renewed diplomatic push with Iran, according to the article. Direct talks in Doha are among the possibilities being considered. The report argues the effort faces serious hurdles, including Tehran's increasingly firm demands and the sway of Ahmad Vahidi, the hard-line chief of the IRGC, who is described as a threat to any deal.

### Why the timing matters

The push follows an unpopular war and Republican losses in the midterms, which have raised pressure on Washington to find an off-ramp with Iran. The article suggests these domestic troubles shape how the talks may unfold and add urgency to the effort.

### Leverage and internal divisions

Tehran may view America's political weakness as an opening to strengthen its own bargaining position. Meanwhile, U.S. officials are described as split between those favoring diplomacy and those who want to maintain maximum pressure on Iran.
