## Scott Colom Edges Out Hyde-Smith in Historic Mississippi Upset

Democrat Scott Colom has narrowly beaten Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's Senate race. His win is a milestone for the state and a notable result in a deep-red stronghold.

### A Win With Few Precedents

Colom becomes Mississippi's first Black U.S. senator since Reconstruction. He is also the first Democrat to win a Senate race in the state since 1982. His campaign leaned on a "commonsense Democrat" message aimed at voters across party lines, and a viral humor ad helped raise his profile. National Democrats added support late in the race.

### What the Result Signals

The victory marks the eighth Senate seat Democrats have flipped this cycle, pointing to a major shift in Mississippi. Voting-roll issues delayed the call of the race, which raises timely questions about how voter-roll maintenance can affect outcomes. The result also highlights the potential of overlooked contests and viral campaign messaging.
