## Wrestling Champ Brings JTown SmackDown to Her Hometown High School

Ring of Honor women's pure champion Deonna Purrazzo is staging a wrestling event called JTown SmackDown at her hometown high school in Jefferson Township, New Jersey. The show aims to raise money for students while drawing attention to school funding inequities.

### The Funding Backdrop

The event is tied to the state's S-2 funding formula and to restrictions under the Highlands Act, both of which feed into the debate over how schools are paid for. Purrazzo has personal ties to the community, which helps explain why she chose to put the spotlight on Jefferson.

### Why It Matters Now

New Jersey's school funding debate is active, with the NJEA working toward a rewritten formula for the 2027-28 school year. Districts such as Jefferson have warned of cuts and larger class sizes. Purrazzo's event turns a local policy fight into a community-driven sports entertainment moment.
