## Democrat Scott Colom Upsets Incumbent in Mississippi Senate Contest

Democrat Scott Colom has won a US Senate seat in Mississippi, narrowly beating Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith. The result was a surprise in a state long dominated by Republicans.

### A historic first for Mississippi

Colom becomes the state's first Democratic senator since 1982. He is also the first Black senator elected from Mississippi in more than 140 years, which makes the victory significant well beyond the margin of the race itself.

### What it means for the midterms

The outcome reflects a wider Democratic surge in the midterms. That momentum has been driven by voter frustration over the economy, tariffs and the Iran war under President Trump. The win also strengthens the Democratic Senate majority and signals that even deep-red states may be competitive in the current political climate.
