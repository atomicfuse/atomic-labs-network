## Scott Colom Defeats Hyde-Smith in Historic Mississippi Senate Upset

Scott Colom has won Mississippi's U.S. Senate race, beating Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith. He becomes the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction, a result the article describes as a historic political realignment.

### What drove the result

The article credits several factors for Colom's victory: strong Black turnout, frustration over the economy, and weaknesses in Hyde-Smith's candidacy. It also points to Democrats broadening the Southern battleground map, putting contests in states once treated as out of reach.

### Why it matters now

The win is presented as a major midterm setback for Donald Trump's GOP and a challenge to the assumption that Mississippi is safely Republican. It also gives Democrats fresh evidence for investing in Southern states with large Black electorates and shifting rural and suburban dynamics.
