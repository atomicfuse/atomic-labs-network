## Spain Sees First General Strike in 14 Years

Workers across Spain walked off the job in a nationwide general strike, the first the country has seen in 14 years. The action comes as the country heads toward a national election in November.

### What Happened

Workers throughout Spain took part in the strike to protest two main concerns: rising housing costs and wages that have failed to grow. The walkout marks a significant moment in the country's labor history, given that it has been well over a decade since the last general strike of this kind. The timing, ahead of the November vote, places the demands squarely in the run-up to the election.

### Why It Matters Now

The strike pushes cost-of-living pressure to the center of Spain's political debate. It highlights how housing affordability and stagnant pay are shaping what voters care about most as the election approaches. With these issues now at the forefront, they are likely to remain closely tied to the national conversation about the country's direction.
