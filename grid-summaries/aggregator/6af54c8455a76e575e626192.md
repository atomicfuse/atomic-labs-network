## Famed Ocean Liner Set to Become World's Largest Artificial Reef

The SS United States, a celebrated retired ocean liner, is slated to be sunk off Florida's Gulf Coast. Once underwater, it would become the largest artificial reef formed from an intentionally sunken ship anywhere in the world.

### Preserving History, Building Habitat

The plan is presented as serving two purposes at once. It keeps the vessel's place in maritime history alive in a new form, while also offering the potential to boost marine habitat. Rather than disappearing entirely, the ship would take on a second life beneath the waves.

### Why the Timing Matters

The project arrives as concern grows over the collapse of natural coral reefs, and as interest rises in artificial reef systems as alternative homes for marine life. It also turns a famous Cold War-era liner into a story that blends tourism, diving and conservation, with cultural nostalgia built in from the start.
