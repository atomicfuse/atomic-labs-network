## Genetic Map of Bovine TB Reveals Distinct Strains Across Wales

A new study using whole-genome sequencing has charted the genetic diversity of bovine TB bacteria across Wales. The work offers a detailed picture of how the disease is changing in different parts of the country.

### What the study found

Researchers found that distinct regional strains of the bacteria are evolving differently from one another. The analysis also pinpointed strains that were likely brought into Wales through the movement of cattle from outside the country. Together, these findings show that the disease's genetic makeup varies from place to place and that some infections have origins beyond Welsh borders.

### Why it matters

Bovine TB continues to place a heavy animal health, economic and emotional burden on Welsh farming communities. Genomic surveillance of this kind could help shift disease control away from broad national measures and towards more precise interventions informed by local conditions.
