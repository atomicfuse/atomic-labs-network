## New Method Backs Up Famous Black Hole Image

A team at CSIRO has built an independent, AI-assisted imaging technique that checks the well-known 2019 Event Horizon Telescope picture of M87*. The work supports the original result.

### What the team found

Using their own approach, the researchers confirmed that the ring-like shadow seen around the black hole is genuine. It is not an artifact created during image processing. Because the technique works separately from the original method, it offers a fresh check on one of modern science's most recognisable images.

### Why it matters now

The finding boosts confidence in the image at a time when AI, data reconstruction and trust in scientific visuals face close scrutiny. It also points beyond astronomy. Methods developed for this kind of imaging could help improve other fields, including medical imaging and earthquake mapping, where reconstructing clear pictures from limited data is also important.
