## Brooklyn Building Collapse Leaves At Least Two Dead

At least two people have died and more than 17 others were injured after a three-storey building in Brooklyn fell into an open construction site on Flatbush Avenue, officials say.

### The Rescue Effort

More than 250 first responders took part in a search-and-rescue operation that officials described as dangerous. Authorities are investigating what caused the building to collapse. Rescue operations were still unfolding at the time of reporting, so the situation remains a developing one and the casualty figures could change.

### Why It Matters

The collapse raises urgent questions about safety on construction sites, the condition of aging buildings, and the risks facing urban infrastructure in New York City. For now it is a local tragedy still taking shape, though it carries wider implications for public safety. Investigators have yet to establish the cause, and further details are expected as the inquiry continues.
