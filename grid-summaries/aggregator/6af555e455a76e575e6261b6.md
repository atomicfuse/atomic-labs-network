## Soldier Charged With Rape of Servicewoman in Cyprus

A British Army lance corporal has been arrested and charged with raping a servicewoman while serving with British Forces Cyprus. The soldier belongs to The Highlanders.

### The case so far

The Defence Serious Crime Command is handling the investigation. The lance corporal has been remanded in custody ahead of court-martial proceedings, which will determine how the allegation is dealt with within the military justice system.

### Why it matters now

The charge adds to ongoing scrutiny of sexual offences within the armed forces and of how military justice systems deal with allegations involving service personnel. It is also notable because it concerns UK forces stationed overseas at sovereign bases in Cyprus, where the case will be managed through the military process rather than a civilian one.
