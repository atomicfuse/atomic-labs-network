## Deadly Brooklyn Building Collapse Leaves Two Dead, 17 Hurt

A three-story building collapsed in Brooklyn's Flatbush neighborhood, killing at least two people and injuring 17 others, among them children.

### Rescue Efforts Continue

Search-and-rescue operations are still underway at the site. Officials have voiced concern about unstable rubble and the possibility that more victims remain trapped. Construction activity near the building is also part of the concern as crews work. The FDNY, NYPD and Department of Buildings are responding, along with city leadership.

### Wider Concerns for the Community

The collapse is an active emergency in New York City, and it may prompt questions about construction oversight and building safety. Residents have been displaced, streets are closed, and people are worried about the safety of older buildings and neighboring structures.
