## Trump administration eyes sales of federal land to states, tribes and localities

The Trump administration wants to sell public lands held by the federal government, with states, tribes, and local governments named as the intended buyers. The proposal is part of a wider effort to reduce the amount of land the federal government owns.

### What the plan involves

According to the report, the administration's approach centers on moving federally owned acreage into the hands of other levels of government. The goal is a smaller federal estate overall. The article does not lay out here which parcels might be affected or how any sales would be carried out, so those details remain unclear.

### Why the debate matters

Public land policy has become a major flashpoint, with arguments over conservation, housing, energy development, tribal sovereignty, and state control all intensifying. Any move to shrink the federal estate could change how land is accessed and managed, and could alter environmental protections, in places across the country.
