## UK Government Weighs Forcing Entertainment Industry to Tighten Safeguarding

The UK government may compel the entertainment industry to strengthen safeguarding and whistleblower protections. The prospect follows allegations of sexual misconduct against David Walliams, which he denies.

### Mandatory participation under discussion

The article focuses on the possibility that taking part in CIISA could become compulsory rather than optional for organisations in the sector. Alongside that, pressure is growing to restrict the use of NDAs that silence people who wish to raise complaints. Together, these measures would mark a shift from voluntary commitments toward requirements set by government.

### Why the debate is changing

Recent allegations involving high-profile entertainers, combined with renewed scrutiny of Russell Brand and of power dynamics within the industry, have intensified calls for structural reform. The discussion is moving away from individual scandals and toward a broader question: whether the creative industries can be trusted to regulate themselves, or whether outside intervention is now needed.
