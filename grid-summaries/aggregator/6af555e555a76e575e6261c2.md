## White House Warns Aggressive Congressional Probes Could Be Treated as Crimes

The White House has cautioned Congress that forceful investigations might be handled as criminal matters. The warning comes even though its legal basis is unclear and lawmakers' legislative acts carry constitutional protections.

### What was said

According to the report, the administration signaled that investigative conduct by Congress could potentially cross into criminal territory. The legal footing for such a position remains uncertain, and the Constitution offers safeguards for actions lawmakers take as part of their legislative work. The report does not settle how those protections would apply, leaving the scope of the warning open to question.

### Why the timing matters

The warning arrives amid heightened tension between the executive branch and Congress over oversight powers, subpoenas, and investigations. By suggesting that aggressive probing could be criminalized, the claim raises significant separation-of-powers questions. It does so at a politically sensitive moment, when the boundaries of each branch's authority are already under close scrutiny.
