## Kennedy's Crude Analogy Becomes Senate GOP's Lame-Duck Rallying Cry

Sen. John Kennedy keeps using a blunt "urinating on our hands" comparison while pressing fellow Senate Republicans to move quickly. His argument is that the party should not shrug off its midterm loss as business as usual.

### What Kennedy Is Urging

Kennedy wants Republicans to treat the lame-duck session as a chance to show voters they care. Rather than coasting after the defeat, he says, the party should act before Democrats take control of Congress. He has returned to the vivid phrasing again and again, making it the centerpiece of his pitch to colleagues.

### Why It Matters Now

Republicans are working through a major midterm defeat, and the time left to pass their priorities is shrinking. They are set to lose Senate control in January. Kennedy's memorable wording has turned what might have been a quiet internal strategy debate into a meme-ready political moment, drawing attention to the party's choices in its final weeks of power.
