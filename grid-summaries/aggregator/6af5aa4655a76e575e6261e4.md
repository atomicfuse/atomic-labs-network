## Burnham Warns Climate Consensus Has "Gone" at COP31

Prime Minister Andy Burnham used his COP31 speech to deliver a stark message about global climate politics while also promising a "new era of British leadership."

### A speech that mixes warning and promise

Burnham told delegates that worldwide agreement on climate science has gone. At the same time, he pledged that Britain would take a stronger leading role. The coverage points to a tension between that ambitious language and recent UK policy choices, which are seen as having weakened the country's green credibility.

### Why the moment matters

The summit is happening as international climate cooperation falters. Burnham is the only G7 leader attending, and the US is absent from the process. Meanwhile, record UK heat, wildfires, effects on food supplies and rising sea levels are pushing climate security higher up the political agenda.
