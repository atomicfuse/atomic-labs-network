## Early 2028 Buzz Centers on Rubio, Vance, Ossoff and Buttigieg

A new Reuters/Ipsos poll suggests four names are already drawing attention for the 2028 presidential race: Republicans Marco Rubio and JD Vance, and Democrats Jon Ossoff and Pete Buttigieg.

### What the poll shows

No one has formally launched a campaign, yet voter impressions are taking shape. According to the piece, those views are being influenced by Donald Trump's sway over the party, each figure's name recognition, their ideology, and their recent electoral momentum.

### Why it matters now

The election remains far off, but the political backdrop is already opening lanes for potential successors. Trump's low approval and Republican divisions over his legacy sit alongside Democratic confidence after the midterms. Early polling also hints at how moderates, anti-Trump messaging, and foreign-policy fallout could shape both parties' primaries.
