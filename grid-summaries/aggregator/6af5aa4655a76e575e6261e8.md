## Vivir Quintana Gives the Corrido a Female Voice

Grammy-nominated Mexican songwriter Vivir Quintana is reshaping the corrido, a ballad form long associated with men. Her songs put women at the center, telling stories of survival, resistance, and justice.

### A Genre Reimagined

A profile of Quintana highlights her performance in Oaxaca and her growing visibility around the world as the Latin Grammy Awards approach. Rather than following the genre's customary path, she builds her ballads around the lives and experiences of women, turning a tradition once led by male figures into a vehicle for their stories.

### Why the Timing Matters

Corridos are enjoying a major cultural moment, yet the genre often dwells on male heroes, violence, and narco mythology. Quintana's work offers a contrast to that focus. Her Latin Grammy nomination, shared with Juanes, carries feminist Mexican folk storytelling onto an international stage and brings wider attention to her approach.
