## Opinion Column Calls for Ending the Electoral College

A new Patrick's Politics column makes the case that the United States should scrap the Electoral College and choose presidents by national popular vote. It uses renewed attention on the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact as its starting point.

### The Argument Against the Current System

The column says the Electoral College can override the popular vote and gives voters different levels of power depending on their state. It also contends the system makes swing states a priority over the rest of the country. In addition, it points to the system's roots in historical disenfranchisement as part of the reason it should go.

### Why the Debate Is Timely

Recent Democratic gains in state legislatures could move the compact closer to the 270 electoral votes needed to effectively bypass the Electoral College. With presidential races increasingly decided by narrow margins in a handful of states, the question of whether every vote should count equally feels newly urgent.
