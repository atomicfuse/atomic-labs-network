## Iran Targets Exiles' Property to Silence Critics Abroad

Iranian authorities are freezing and seizing assets inside the country to punish dissidents and journalists who speak out from abroad, according to a new report. The article argues that Tehran's repression no longer stops at its borders.

### Assets and Family in the Crosshairs

The report says officials are going after homes, bank accounts, and family property left behind in Iran. By targeting relatives and the financial ties that exiles still hold, the government is reaching critics who have already left the country. The article presents this as an extension of repression beyond Iran's borders.

### Why the Pressure Is Rising

Following heightened conflict involving Iran, the regime is intensifying pressure on critics outside the country while trying to deter public dissent online.
