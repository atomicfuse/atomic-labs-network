## Fox Host Urges Trump to Counter Democratic Probes With Affordability Push

Fox News host Kayleigh McEnany has offered advice to Republicans after heavy midterm losses. She says President Trump should answer Democratic investigations by taking the initiative rather than simply defending himself.

### McEnany's Proposed Strategy

McEnany argues Trump should go on offense by promoting a bipartisan agenda focused on affordability. She portrays Democrats as having shifted away from cost-of-living worries toward subpoenas and investigations. In her view, Republicans should "beat them at their game" by keeping attention on pocketbook concerns instead of letting the fight over probes dominate.

### Why the Timing Matters

Democrats are preparing to use their congressional power to investigate Trump and his administration, and the Republican midterm setbacks have sharpened the debate over how the party should respond. Affordability remains a defining issue for voters, which makes the contest over messaging especially urgent for both parties right now.
