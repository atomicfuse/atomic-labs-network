## Democrats weigh congressional moves against Trump-backed projects

A new report looks at how Democrats might use the tools available in Congress to challenge or slow projects backed by Trump, including an arch and a golf course. Several of these developments are also moving through the courts.

### The options on the table

The focus is on congressional levers rather than direct executive action. Chief among them are restrictions on funding and oversight efforts, which Democrats could use to push back on the projects and press for answers about how they are advancing.

### Why it matters now

The projects are turning into political flashpoints just as legal battles play out and Democrats search for ways to respond from Capitol Hill. The story ties into wider tensions over Trump's influence, the use of public land, federal spending, and the checks that institutions place on power.
