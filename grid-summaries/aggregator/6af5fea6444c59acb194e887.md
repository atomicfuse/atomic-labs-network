## Scott Colom's Senate Win Marks a Milestone for Mississippi

Democrat Scott Colom has won a U.S. Senate seat in Mississippi, becoming the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction. Black voters are reading the result against the long history of the state's racial past.

### A Win Set Against Mississippi's History

Coverage of the victory places it alongside the state's record of voter suppression and civil rights violence. It also looks at modern turnout and calls for political change, showing how a historic result connects to decades of struggle over who gets to participate in government.

### Why the Moment Resonates Now

The win ends a gap of nearly 150 years in Black statewide representation in a conservative state with deep symbolic weight. It comes during renewed fights over voting rights and redistricting, and amid frustration with Republican leadership on poverty, schools, health care, and the economy.
