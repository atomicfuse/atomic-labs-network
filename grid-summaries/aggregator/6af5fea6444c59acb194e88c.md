## Gym Shooting at Logan Circle Apartment Building Leaves One Dead, Four Hurt

A shooting inside the gym of a Logan Circle apartment building in Washington DC killed one person and wounded four others, according to police.

### The Incident

Police say the suspect barricaded himself inside the building and fired at officers who responded. He later died from what appears to be a self-inflicted gunshot wound. Investigators are still working to establish the timeline of events and the motive behind the attack. They are also looking into reports that a fire was set during the incident.

### Why It Matters

The shooting took place in a high-profile DC neighborhood roughly a mile from the White House. It has renewed attention on urban gun violence, building security, and how emergency crews respond in residential spaces. Mayor Muriel Bowser described it as another "senseless act of gun violence."
