## Fourteen killed as truck triggers 23-vehicle pileup in Fujairah

A truck that lost control in slow-moving traffic at the entrance to Fujairah in the UAE set off a chain-reaction crash involving 23 vehicles. A fire followed, and the collision left 14 people dead and 30 injured.

### What happened

The truck went out of control while traffic was moving slowly at the city's entrance, striking vehicles in a sequence that ended with 23 involved and a blaze breaking out. Authorities have opened an investigation into the cause. Because of the fire, victims are being identified through DNA testing. Officials are also coordinating support for the families of those affected, who come from several different countries.

### Why it matters now

The crash stands as a major mass-casualty incident for the UAE and raises pressing questions about road safety, the regulation of heavy vehicles, and emergency response on busy corridors. It also carries international weight, since the victims include Emirati, Indian, Omani, Brazilian, Pakistani, Jordanian and Egyptian nationals.
