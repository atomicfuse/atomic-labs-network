## Witkoff and Kushner Press Putin in Moscow to Bring Europe to the Table

Trump envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner traveled to Moscow to urge Vladimir Putin to include Europe in peace talks over Ukraine. The visit followed direct discussions between Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi.

### Moscow's Wider Demands

The piece argues that Russia is using cease-fire negotiations as a vehicle to advance broader demands about Europe's security architecture. In this reading, the talks are less about halting the fighting alone and more about reshaping the continent's security arrangements, which makes the question of European participation especially sensitive.

### Why the Timing Matters

The renewed diplomacy comes as Russia escalates attacks on Ukrainian cities ahead of winter. The Trump administration is also seeking a major foreign policy win after midterm losses. Europe's role is becoming a central fault line, and it may decide whether any Ukraine deal is seen as legitimate or durable.
