## AOC Hires Sanders Fundraising Strategist Amid 2028 Speculation

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez has brought on Aisle 518 Strategies, a firm led by Tim Tagaris, who worked as a fundraising strategist for Bernie Sanders. The hire comes as talk grows about her next move.

### The Hire

Tagaris is known for his fundraising work on the Sanders campaign, and his firm is now working with Ocasio-Cortez. Speculation has centered on two possibilities: a run for president in 2028 or a Senate challenge against Chuck Schumer. She has not been reported as confirming either path.

### Why It Matters Now

Democrats face a leadership fight after the midterms, and the party is debating its future direction. Progressives are looking for a national standard-bearer. Ocasio-Cortez's staffing moves suggest she may be building infrastructure for a much larger campaign, though what she will ultimately decide remains unclear.
