## Paul Barril, Celebrated French Gendarme Turned Mercenary Adviser, Dies

Paul Barril, once hailed in France as a "supergendarme," has died. An article on his life follows his path from elite law enforcement officer to a controversial figure in private security.

### From Elite Officer to Private Operator

After leaving the force, Barril became a private security adviser and a supplier of mercenaries to African leaders. His career drew scrutiny for arms sales and surveillance services. He was also linked, by allegation, to abuses, including events connected to Rwanda's 1994 genocide.

### Why His Death Resonates
