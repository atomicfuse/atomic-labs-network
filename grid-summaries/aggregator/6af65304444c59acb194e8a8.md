## Holscher Wins Kansas Governor's Race, Keeping Democrats in Charge

Democrat Cindy Holscher has won the open race for Kansas governor, defeating Republican Ty Masterson. Her victory means Democrats will hold the governor's mansion for a third consecutive election.

### A familiar split-ticket pattern

The result underscores how Kansas votes differently depending on the contest. Republicans continue to dominate presidential races in the state, yet Democrats have kept finding success in statewide campaigns, and the governor's office is the clearest example of that divide.

### What it means going forward

The outcome arrives as Democrats' gains draw wider attention, and it renews questions about why Republicans keep coming up short in Kansas gubernatorial contests despite the state's deep-red presidential record. It also sets up divided government, since Republicans still control the Legislature while a Democrat occupies the governor's office.
