## Harbaugh Blasts Broncos After Henley Trade Falls Apart

Chargers coach Jim Harbaugh publicly criticized the Broncos after a trade for linebacker Daiyan Henley fell through following the deadline. He called the way it unraveled a bad precedent for the league.

### What went wrong

The deal collapsed over a disputed medical evaluation and an attempt to change the draft-pick compensation that had been agreed. Harbaugh's complaint centers on the idea that a team can use a player's medical grade to reopen terms once a trade is already set. In his view, that gives clubs room to renegotiate after the fact.

### Why it matters

Harbaugh believes the NFL should close the loophole that allows this. The episode touches on deadline strategy, how teams assess player health, and front-office maneuvering. It also raises the question of how much power teams should have to change deals after a player has already gone through the uncertainty of being traded.
