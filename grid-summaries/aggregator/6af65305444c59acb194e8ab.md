## New Jersey Couple Say Towering Bigfoot Crossed Road in Front of Their Car

A New Jersey couple says they saw a tall, dark, humanlike creature walk across a rural road as they drove. They stand by their account, insisting they know what they saw.

### The Reported Encounter

The pair describe the sighting as happening on a rainy night in October. They say the figure was about eight feet tall and jet-black, and that it moved across the road directly ahead of their vehicle. Investigators who spoke with them called the couple credible witnesses. However, no photo or physical evidence was recovered, so the account rests entirely on what the two people say they observed.

### Why the Story Resonates

Interest in Bigfoot continues to grow as audiences are drawn to mystery, folklore, and paranormal claims backed by apparently credible witnesses. This report arrives during a renewed wave of attention to alleged Bigfoot evidence, including DNA claims, and to regional sightings reported across the Northeast.
