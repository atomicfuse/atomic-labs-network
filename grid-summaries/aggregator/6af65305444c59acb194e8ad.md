## Heritage Forum Turns Midterm Losses Into a Reckoning for GOP Leadership

At a post-midterm forum hosted by the Heritage Foundation, conservative leaders gathered to dissect Republican losses and weigh a path to recovery. David Marcus, who reported from the event, says Senate Majority Leader John Thune drew a share of the blame.

### A Call for More Fight

Marcus argues that the party must show more fight and take a hard look at its leadership. In his telling, the discussion went beyond criticism of Thune and into broader questions about whether current leaders can deliver for the movement. He also says Republicans need to court younger voters if they hope to rebuild their standing.

### Eyes on 2028

The piece arrives amid an internal conservative reckoning over strategy and the future of the MAGA-era GOP. Marcus contends the party should widen its 2028 presidential field, and notes that the primary conversation is already expanding as activists question whether the present leadership is up to the task.
