## Mamdani Moves Toward Dissolving NYPD's Strategic Response Group

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is getting ready to disband the police department's Strategic Response Group, according to the article. The unit handles protests, civil disorder, major events and counterterrorism.

### Campaign promise and open questions

The article presents the step as the delivery of a promise Mamdani made during his campaign. It also notes that questions remain about who will take over the group's responsibilities, including how protests are handled and how public safety duties are reassigned once the unit is gone.

### Why the timing matters

The decision arrives during continuing debates over policing, protest rights, counterterrorism and progressive governance in large cities. Mamdani faces pressure from his political base, while city officials and critics question what removing or restructuring the unit would mean for day-to-day operations.
