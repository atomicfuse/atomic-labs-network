## Camirale Launches as Queensland-Born Women's Golf Label

Camirale is a new women's golf and lifestyle apparel brand from Queensland, founded by Kylie O'Keefe and Sidone Thomas. It aims to deliver clothing that looks good, feels comfortable and welcomes a wide range of women, both on the course and away from it.

### The Launch Collection

The first range covers polos, knitwear, dresses, outerwear and bottoms, offered in sizes XS to 3XL. Practical details include pockets, built-in shorts and selected tall lengths, giving players more options than many traditional golf lines provide.

### Why Timing Matters

Women's golf is gaining visibility, and brands face pressure to fit more body types and design pieces that work beyond conventional sport settings. Camirale responds to growing demand for inclusive, lifestyle-friendly activewear. It also adds a community element through Club Camirale, a free membership for customers who want to connect with the brand.
