## State Fayre Books All-Female Headliners for 2027

State Fayre, a UK country music camping festival, has announced that its 2027 edition will be headlined entirely by women. The Chicks, Shania Twain, and Kacey Musgraves will top the bill, a first for the event that the festival says will make history.

### A Milestone for Representation

The booking is framed as a significant step for representation across country and Americana music, as well as the wider festival world. Having three women lead the lineup signals a shift in who gets the biggest slots at major live events, and it gives the festival a strong statement to build its 2027 identity around.

### Why the Timing Matters

Festival lineups are facing growing scrutiny over gender balance, while country music is enjoying a major mainstream moment in the UK. Against that backdrop, the announcement presents State Fayre as both a cultural statement and a serious contender in the live music landscape that has emerged since V Festival Essex.
