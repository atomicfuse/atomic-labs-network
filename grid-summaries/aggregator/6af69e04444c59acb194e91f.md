## Mother of two loses 18 kg in two years after second C-section

Manvi Mittal, 36, has shared how she lost 18 kg over two years following her second C-section. Her story centres on rebuilding strength and function after childbirth rather than chasing a pre-pregnancy body.

### What her recovery involved

Mittal describes a severe postpartum gap and a approach built on strength training, core rehabilitation, balanced nutrition and consistency. The article argues that recovery after birth should prioritise regaining strength and how the body works, instead of rushing to return to how it looked before pregnancy.
