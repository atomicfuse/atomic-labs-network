## Navy Dismissals Without Announcement Prompt Lawmakers to Question Transparency

The Navy has removed several senior officers and officials without the public announcements it has typically made. Lawmakers are now pushing back, and the central question is whether the service is stepping away from openness with the public and with Congress.

### Who Was Removed

The dismissals include submarine leadership following an underwater collision, the commander of a destroyer that was deployed at the time, and Congress' attending physician. In each case, the usual public notice was absent, which has drawn attention to a change in how the Navy handles such personnel actions.

### Why the Timing Matters

The removals arrive amid wider secrecy surrounding U.S. military operations, particularly in the Middle East. They could also complicate the upcoming confirmation process for acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao. As routine oversight practices appear to shift, lawmakers are increasingly challenging the Navy over what it shares and how it communicates with Congress.
