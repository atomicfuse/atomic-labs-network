## Seduction as a Spy Tool: The Long History of the Honey Trap

Foreign intelligence agents have long turned romance and sex into instruments of espionage. A new article examines this tactic, known as the "honey trap," and how it is used to manipulate people into giving up sensitive information.

### How the tactic works

The article presents seduction as an enduring method of spycraft. It describes how agents exploit emotional vulnerability, using intimate relationships to gain influence over a target. The approach blends personal feelings with serious national security risk, because a person who is emotionally entangled may be pushed toward betraying secrets they would otherwise protect.

Worries about foreign influence, digital surveillance, and insider threats are growing as geopolitical tensions intensify. The honey trap seems newly pertinent in this climate.
