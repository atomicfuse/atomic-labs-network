## DHS Claims Record ICE Arrests and Deportations in Fiscal Year 2026

The Department of Homeland Security says Immigration and Customs Enforcement reached its highest-ever levels of arrests and deportations in Fiscal Year 2026. The agency credits Trump-era enforcement policies for the results.

### What DHS Is Saying

According to the report, DHS attributes the numbers to Trump-era immigration enforcement and to Secretary Markwayne Mullin, who said the agency has been "unleashed." The department presents the figures as a sharp contrast with enforcement levels under the Biden administration.

### Why It Matters Now

Immigration remains a defining political issue heading into the next election cycle. Both parties are using enforcement data to argue over border security, public safety, and executive power. The record deportation claims are likely to fuel debate among voters, immigrant-rights groups, and law-and-order conservatives.
