## Outgoing Lt. Gov. Patrick Says Texas Has "Turned Purple" After Democratic Sweep

Texas Lt. Gov. Dan Patrick, who is leaving office, has acknowledged that the state has become politically competitive after Democrats won statewide races, took the governor's office, captured a U.S. Senate seat, and gained control of the Texas House.

### Patrick's Explanation

Patrick argues the results reflect less a change in ideology than a swing among independent voters. He points to frustration over affordability and to the effects of high fuel prices linked to the Iran war as key factors that pushed those voters away from Republicans.

### Why It Matters

Texas has long been viewed as a Republican stronghold, so a Democratic sweep suggests a significant political realignment, with possible national implications for 2028 and beyond. The episode also feeds a wider debate within the GOP after the election over whether economic hardship can outweigh cultural alignment in shaping how people vote.
