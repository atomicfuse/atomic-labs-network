## White House Tightens Rules on Lobbyist Access Under New Chief of Staff

Stephen Miller's office has issued a memo limiting how White House staff can engage with lobbyists. The step follows claims of influence peddling and comes just as Miller has taken over the top staff role.

### What the memo requires

Under the memo, White House staff may not meet with lobbyists about policy issues unless they first obtain approval. The move appears intended to show that the administration is independent of special interests, following claims that earlier leadership favored lobbyists and corporate donors.

### Why the timing matters

The restriction arrives right after Susie Wiles was ousted and Miller became chief of staff, making it one of his first efforts to define how he will lead. It also comes amid renewed scrutiny of influence, donor access, and lobbying power inside the Trump White House.
