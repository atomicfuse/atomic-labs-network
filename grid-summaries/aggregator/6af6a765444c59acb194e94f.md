## Democratic Group Backs $1.1 Million Research Effort on AI Messaging

A Democratic group called Tomorrow Together is starting a $1.1 million research project aimed at shaping how the party talks to voters about regulating artificial intelligence as the 2028 election approaches.

### Why Democrats See an Opening

According to the article, Democrats view guardrails on AI as an issue with growing bipartisan appeal. They also see it as a way to draw a clear political contrast with President Trump, whose stance is described as favorable to the industry. The research is meant to help refine the language that will resonate with voters.

### AI Moves Toward the Mainstream

AI is increasingly showing up as a campaign topic, driven by worries about data centers, safety risks, and companies building ever more powerful systems. Democrats, coming off midterm wins and preparing for the next presidential race, are now testing whether a message centered on regulating AI can succeed nationally.
