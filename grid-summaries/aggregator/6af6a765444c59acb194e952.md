## FBI Warns of Fake Video Call Scam Behind $400m Crypto Theft

North Korean state-linked hackers allegedly stole more than $400 million in cryptocurrency using a scam known as "FaceStealer," according to the FBI. The operation relied on fake video calls and malware to target people working in crypto and web3.

### How the scam worked

The FBI says the hackers impersonated conferencing platforms and exploited trusted contacts to lure victims. They mimicked familiar tools such as Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet, making the calls appear routine. Malware was then used to carry out the thefts. Crypto and web3 workers were the main targets of the campaign.

### Why it matters now

Crypto theft is being tied directly to North Korea's weapons funding. The warning follows Binance's disclosure of a record $6.3 billion hack attributed to North Korea-linked actors. Because remote work, open-source development, and video meetings remain central to web3, social engineering is becoming a major security threat for the industry.
