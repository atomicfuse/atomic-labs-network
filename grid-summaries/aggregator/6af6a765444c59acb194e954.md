## Indonesia's Vice President Steps Down Amid Eligibility Dispute

Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka has resigned, bringing an abrupt end to a period of legal and political pressure over his qualifications for the office.

### What happened

Raka stepped down while court disputes continued over whether his educational background made him eligible to hold the post. Calls for his impeachment were also growing. The resignation is presented as an effort to head off political turmoil, and it opens a constitutional process to choose his replacement.

### Why it matters now

The departure lands at the heart of Indonesian politics, raising questions about dynastic power, the legitimacy of the courts, and accountability within President Prabowo's government. It is also a historic first: Raka is the first vice president to leave office before the end of a term since Indonesia began its post-Suharto democratic reforms in 1998.
