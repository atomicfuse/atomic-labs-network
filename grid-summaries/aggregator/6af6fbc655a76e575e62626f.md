## Nvidia CEO Exit Puts US AI Strategy Under Pressure

An opinion piece argues that Jensen Huang's resignation from Nvidia, allegedly prompted by concerns over Chinese espionage, is a serious blow to American leadership in artificial intelligence. It also sets out a path for the U.S. to stay ahead.

### Four priorities for America

The article says Washington and industry should treat China's espionage threat with real seriousness. It also calls for backing companies that compete with Nvidia, keeping money flowing into AI investment, and fighting to hold market share for AI chips around the world.

### Why the stakes are high

AI chips have become a central arena in the U.S.-China rivalry, and Nvidia's dominance is closely linked to American strategic power. The piece presents instability at the top of the company as a national security matter rather than simply a shake-up within the technology sector.
