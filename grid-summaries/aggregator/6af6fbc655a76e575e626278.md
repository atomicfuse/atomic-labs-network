## Microsoft 365 outage disrupts services across the globe

A Microsoft 365 outage knocked out Teams, Outlook and Copilot in at least 74 countries, forcing a wide range of organisations to look for other ways to communicate.

### Who was affected

The disruption reached well beyond ordinary offices. Governments, courts, healthcare systems, financial markets and police forces all had to switch to backup communication channels. Businesses were caught up as well, and even spy agencies were forced to adapt. The scale of the problem shows how widely these tools are used across public and private life.

### Why it matters

The episode underlines how heavily public services, finance, politics and workplaces rely on a small number of cloud platforms. Because AI tools such as Copilot are now built into everyday workflows, a Microsoft disruption is no longer just an office annoyance. It can slow core civic and economic activity, which raises the stakes whenever one of these platforms goes down.
