## Affordability Politics Beat the Play-It-Safe Playbook

An opinion piece argues that Democrats' midterm success came from putting affordability and opposition to inequality ahead of cautious electability arguments. It treats the results as a sign that the party's old approach is giving way to something bolder.

### What the Article Argues

The piece credits wins by progressive, diverse, and labor-aligned candidates with showing that identity politics and working-class politics are not in conflict. In its telling, candidates who focused on everyday economic pressure did well, and those victories undercut the notion that Democrats must tread carefully to win.

### Why It Matters Now

Voters are reacting to rising costs, health-care insecurity, SNAP cuts, childcare expenses, and the politics of MAGA-aligned billionaires. The article positions affordability as the clearest path forward for Democrats. It also challenges consultants and pundits who remain stuck in 2016-era debates over moderation, identity, and who counts as working class.
