## Sanders Backs Van Hollen in Bid to Unseat Schumer as Democratic Leader

Sen. Bernie Sanders has thrown his support behind Sen. Chris Van Hollen, who is challenging Chuck Schumer for the post of Senate Democratic leader. Schumer maintains that he has the votes to keep his position.

### A Call for Bolder Leadership

Sanders frames his endorsement as a push for leadership that is bolder and more focused on working-class concerns. The contest adds to growing infighting among Democrats following their election wins, and it shows that tensions over the party's direction are surfacing even as the party celebrates.

### Why the Fight Matters Now

Democrats are preparing to lead a new Senate majority, so the choice of leader carries high stakes as a signal of where the party is headed. The challenge also reflects pressure from progressives, who argue that voters want economic populism and a break from establishment politics.
