## Granderson Says Schumer's Era Is Fading as Democrats Seek a New Kind of Leader

A column by LZ Granderson argues that Chuck Schumer's time as Democratic Senate leader is coming to an end. It points to recent insurgent victories as proof that voters are moving past the party's traditional gatekeepers.

### Evidence of a shift

The piece cites Scott Colom's historic Mississippi Senate win, along with other insurgent Democratic victories, as signs that voters are bypassing established party figures. It contrasts Schumer's skill at inside politics with a growing demand for public servants whose strength comes from grassroots momentum.

### A leadership fight ahead

Democrats now face a new leadership contest after breakthrough wins in red and swing states. That forces the party to weigh generational change, succession, and whether Washington power brokers still understand the electorate. The column ties this to a wider anti-establishment mood that is reshaping both major parties.
