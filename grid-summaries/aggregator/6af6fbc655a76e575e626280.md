## Some Trump-Era Republicans Question Whether MAGA Has a Future

Longtime Republicans who came of age politically during the Trump years are asking whether the MAGA movement can last. An article explores how some supporters are rethinking where they belong as the party's identity comes under pressure.

### Looking Elsewhere

Some of these Republicans and MAGA backers are beginning to look beyond the White House for direction. Others are drifting toward Democrats, while some are stepping away from politics altogether. The piece presents these shifts as signs of disillusionment among people for whom Trump's GOP was the only version of the party they had known.

### Why It Matters

The party is facing questions about what it stands for after Trump. Growing disillusionment inside MAGA could change how voters behave, who leads the party, and how political organizing unfolds in the 2024 era. How widely these feelings spread may shape the Republican Party's next chapter.
