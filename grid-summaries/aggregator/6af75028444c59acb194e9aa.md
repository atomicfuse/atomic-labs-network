## Deonna Purrazzo Eyes Janai Kai and Marina Shafir as Next Women's Pure Title Challengers

Deonna Purrazzo, the inaugural Ring of Honor women's pure champion, has looked back on a reign that is close to a year long. She also named two wrestlers she would like to see come forward and challenge her.

### A Dominant Run Nears One Year

Purrazzo has held the women's pure title since it was introduced, and the piece presents her as a commanding champion. With her reign approaching the one-year mark, the division looks ready for a fresh challenger and a new storyline. Her reflections focus on what she has done with the belt and on who might realistically threaten it.

### Kai and Shafir Get the Call

Janai Kai and Marina Shafir are the two names Purrazzo put forward as challengers she wants to see step up. Shafir's profile is also rising because she is about to take part in a high-profile AEW title match at Full Gear, which adds timely crossover interest to Purrazzo's comments.
