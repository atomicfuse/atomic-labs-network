## Miller Orders Legal Sign-Off for Lobbyist Meetings, Wrapped in Praise of Trump

Stephen Miller, newly installed as White House chief of staff, has issued a memo that requires staff to obtain legal approval before meeting with lobbyists. The directive is framed in notably deferential language toward Donald Trump.

### What the memo does

The mandate adds a legal checkpoint to any staff contact with lobbyists. According to the article, the memo's heavy praise of Trump reflects Miller's loyalty-driven, policy-first approach to running the White House. The article argues that this approach could sharply reduce the influence lobbyists have inside the building.

### Why the timing matters

The memo arrives after Democrats took control of Congress and amid scrutiny over alleged lobbyist influence in the Trump administration. The article reads the move as a sign of power shifting toward Miller's ideological control, and toward a more tightly managed White House operation.
