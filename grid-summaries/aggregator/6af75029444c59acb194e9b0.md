## Bessent Points Finger at Commerce Over Delayed Nvidia Chip Limits

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has publicly faulted the Commerce Department, led by Howard Lutnick, for not moving sooner to restrict Nvidia chips that could end up in China via Singapore-based Megaspeed.

### A dispute inside the administration

Bessent's criticism targets Commerce for delaying export controls tied to Megaspeed. The disagreement is linked to Nvidia's national security scandal, the resignation of Jensen Huang, and growing scrutiny of how well the United States enforces its chip restrictions. It places two senior Trump administration officials on opposite sides of a public argument over responsibility.

### Why the timing matters

AI chips sit at the center of technology competition between the United States and China, and the Nvidia fallout is turning export controls into a question of political accountability. Officials are now asking whether corporate relationships and lobbying slowed national security decisions, which makes the moment especially sensitive for the administration.
