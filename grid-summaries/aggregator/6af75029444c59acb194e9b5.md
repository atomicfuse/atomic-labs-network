## Quarterback Pay Surge Reaches New Heights in College Football

An article argues that the market for college quarterbacks is climbing out of control, pointing to Mississippi State's reported $7.5 million deal for Kamario Taylor as its latest example.

### A Risky Spending Spiral

The piece describes a "wild west" economy in which name, image and likeness money flows with few guardrails. In its telling, programs feel pressured to overpay, whether they are trying to keep an elite passer already on the roster or bring in a new one. Taylor's reported contract is presented as a sign of where things are heading rather than a one-off.

### Why the Timing Matters

NIL spending is becoming one of the main forces shaping how teams build their rosters, and quarterback is where it shows most. As players gain leverage through the transfer portal and top programs chase playoff-level talent, prices are rising faster than any clear regulation or salary structure can keep up with.
