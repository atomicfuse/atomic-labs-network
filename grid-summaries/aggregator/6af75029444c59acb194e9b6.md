## Sooner State Party Plans New Signature Drive for Official Recognition

A group calling itself the Sooner State Party says it will try again to win official status as a political party in Oklahoma. The effort centers on collecting enough signatures to qualify.

### What the group is doing

According to the article, the Sooner State Party intends to launch another round of signature gathering. If it reaches the required number, it would be recognized as Oklahoma's fourth political party. The report describes the move as a renewed push, which indicates the group has pursued this goal before.

### Why it matters now

Access to the ballot for third parties is a recurring point of contention. Some voters have voiced frustration with the two-party system and are looking for alternatives as upcoming elections approach. The Sooner State Party's new attempt arrives in that setting, and it will test whether the group can gather the support it needs for recognition.
