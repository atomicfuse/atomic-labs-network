## Ravens Give Lamar Jackson Return Window of 6 to 10 Weeks After Thumb Surgery

Baltimore has put an official timeline on quarterback Lamar Jackson's recovery from surgery on his broken and dislocated throwing-hand thumb. The Ravens say he will be out six to ten weeks.

### Season Could Play Out Without Jackson

The article argues that the Ravens may have to get through most or all of the regular season without their star passer. Jacoby Brissett is set to step in as the starter, and he takes over while the team is stuck in a four-game losing streak.

### Playoff Hopes Hang in the Balance

Baltimore sits last in the AFC North and outside the playoff picture, so the length of Jackson's absence could shape the whole year. With the NFL playoff race tightening, each game before mid-December could decide whether his eventual return has any impact at all.
