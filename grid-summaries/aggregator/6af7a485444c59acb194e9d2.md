## Starship Booster Lands in Gulf as Double Catch Plan Falls Short

SpaceX's newest Starship test did not complete its planned "double catch." The booster aborted its return and came down in the Gulf of Mexico, while the spacecraft carried on into orbit.

### What Happened in the Test

The goal was to catch both stages of the rocket, a key step toward full reusability. That did not happen, because the booster's abort sent it into the Gulf rather than back for a catch. The spacecraft was unaffected and continued its flight into orbit. Even without the double catch, the mission is still considered a major step toward recovering both stages.

### Why the Test Matters Now

Starship is central to SpaceX's plans for rapid launch scaling, Mars missions, and NASA's Artemis moon program. The test arrives as the company works to launch Starship from more sites. SpaceX is also trying to show that the vehicle can eventually replace its Falcon rockets over the long term.
