## Virginia Lawmakers Weigh Data Center Pause as Public Frustration Grows

Lawmakers from both parties in Virginia are increasingly lining up behind a pause or moratorium on new data centers. The push reflects worries about electric bills, water use, transparency, and environmental impact, and it is being shaped by both policy debate and political pressure.

### Growing concerns across party lines

Support for a temporary halt is no longer confined to one side of the aisle. Lawmakers cite the cost to ratepayers, demands on water supplies, a lack of openness about the facilities, and wider environmental effects. The article describes the debate as a policy fight and also as an election-year response to public frustration with big tech infrastructure.

### Why the timing matters

All 140 General Assembly seats will be on the ballot next year, making data centers a politically potent issue. Governor-elect Spanberger's proposed regulations also open a window for lawmakers to press for a temporary pause while new rules are developed.
