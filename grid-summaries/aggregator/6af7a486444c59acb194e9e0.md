## FAA ground stop disrupts college football teams' Friday travel

A nationwide FAA ground stop left several college football teams stranded or delayed as they tried to travel for important Saturday games. Programs including Texas, Tennessee, Ole Miss, Washington, Minnesota, Baylor, Tulane, Auburn, Arizona State, Oregon State, and Utah were scrambling to find alternate travel plans.

### Teams scramble for options

The stoppage affected a wide range of schools across the country, forcing staffs to rethink how and when their squads would reach game sites. With flights halted or delayed, the programs named in the report were working to arrange other ways to travel before Saturday's matchups.

### Why the timing matters

The disruption landed on a busy Friday, when teams typically fly out for their weekend games. Because major ranked teams and conference matchups are involved, the delays could carry competitive and logistical consequences. Preparation, readiness for kickoff, and fan attention could all be affected as teams work to get where they need to be.
