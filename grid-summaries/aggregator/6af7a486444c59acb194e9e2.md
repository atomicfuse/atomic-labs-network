## Most Americans Say Tech Platforms and News Outlets Wield Too Much Political Power

A new Pew Research Center survey points to widespread unease among Americans about who holds sway over politics. Large shares of respondents named both social media companies and news organizations as having excessive influence.

### What the survey found

According to the survey, 69% of Americans believe social media companies have too much political power. A smaller but still substantial majority, 55%, say the same about U.S. news organizations. Taken together, the figures show broad public concern about the role that online platforms and media institutions play in political life.

### Why the numbers matter now

Questions about trust in institutions, media bias, platform moderation, and influence over elections remain central flashpoints in politics. The findings offer a timely data point for anyone discussing how Americans see the power of information gatekeepers, particularly creators who cover media, technology, and public opinion.
