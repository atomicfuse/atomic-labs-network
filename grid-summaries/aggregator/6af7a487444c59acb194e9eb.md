## SpaceX Skips Booster Catch as Starship Flight Continues in Orbit

SpaceX's newest Starship test flight lifted off successfully, but the company called off its planned catch of the Super Heavy booster after problems appeared. The booster was sent down into the ocean instead.

### Ship Stays Aloft

The upper-stage Ship is still in orbit and is deploying Starlink satellites. SpaceX has not settled whether to try its first tower catch of the Ship, a step that would mark a milestone for the program. The decision remains under consideration as the flight goes on.

### Why the Test Matters

Reusability is central to Starship's promise of lowering launch costs, and it underpins NASA's ambitious plans to land on the Moon. Those plans unfold amid growing lunar competition between the United States and China. The flight also arrives while SpaceX faces scrutiny from shareholders and rivalry from Blue Origin, which is also racing to build lunar landers.
