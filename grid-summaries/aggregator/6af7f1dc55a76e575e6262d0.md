## Shane Gillis Calls His SNL Monologue "Controversy-Free" After Backlash

Shane Gillis has responded to criticism of his Saturday Night Live monologue, saying he believed the set would not stir controversy. The comments came during an appearance on Joe Rogan's podcast.

### What Gillis said

Gillis told Rogan he considered the monologue controversy-free, even though it included a joke calling women in gym clothes "whores." He graded his own performance a "solid C minus." He also made clear that SNL did not sign off on his exact jokes ahead of time, so the material was not pre-approved word for word.

### Why it matters now

The remarks arrive amid continued scrutiny of comedy and the limits of live television. They also highlight how performers with controversial reputations handle mainstream platforms like SNL. His comments revive questions about accountability when edgy material draws backlash after it has already aired.
