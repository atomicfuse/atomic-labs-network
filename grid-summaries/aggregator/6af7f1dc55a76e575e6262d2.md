## Nineties Tracks That Sound Like They Were Made for the Eighties

A new list makes the case that certain 1990s songs never quite fit their own decade. Ten tracks are singled out for production choices, melodies, or attitudes that feel closer to the 1980s than to the years in which they were released.

### The Argument

The piece suggests that style, not the calendar, defines a song's era. In its view, some records from the 1990s carried the polish, tunefulness, or outlook more commonly tied to the decade before. Rather than treating the 90s as a single, uniform sound, the list highlights songs that seem to have wandered in from another period and sat awkwardly among their peers.

### Why It Resonates Today

Nostalgia cycles are fueling fresh interest in both 80s and 90s pop, which makes sounds that blend the two decades especially timely. That overlap gives music commentators plenty to discuss and offers a handy angle for anyone building retro playlists. Songs that straddle eras may appeal to listeners who enjoy both periods.
