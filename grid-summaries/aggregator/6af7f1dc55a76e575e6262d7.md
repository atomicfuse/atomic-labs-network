## Dexter: Resurrection Episode 3 Leaves Dexter Juggling Too Many Threats

The third episode of *Dexter: Resurrection*, titled "Live Free and Die," keeps several storylines moving at once. A critic's recap calls it solid but less dynamic than it could have been.

### A Crowded Web of Complications

Dexter has plenty to manage: the Five Borough Killer, Wallace's trauma tied to the Ripper, and Quinn's ongoing investigation. He also takes on a tense mission with Charley involving the Sleepy-Eyed Stranger. Together these threads deepen the season's web of serial killers, while Quinn, Wallace, and Charley each emerge as growing complications for him.

### Connection Versus Isolation

The revival is trying to reframe Dexter through connection and chosen attachments, a sharp contrast to his earlier lone-wolf identity. The critic, however, finds the episode's theme of attachments versus isolation repetitive, which softens the impact of an otherwise capable installment.
