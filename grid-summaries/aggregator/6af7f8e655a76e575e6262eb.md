## Democrat Kreiss-Tomkins wins Alaska governor's race, ending a long drought

Alaska has elected Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins as its next governor. It is the first Democratic win for the office in the state since 1998. He defeated three Republican opponents, among them a candidate endorsed by Donald Trump.

### A campaign built on everyday issues

The coverage presents his victory as one piece of a wider Democratic wave in the midterm elections. His platform centered on affordability, education, taxes and infrastructure, along with a promise to govern in a bipartisan way.

### What the result signals

The outcome marks a significant political shift in Alaska and adds to the number of governorships Democrats hold nationwide, including several flipped in states that traditionally lean Republican. It also draws attention to how ranked-choice voting played out in Alaska and shows the limits of Trump's influence when the Republican field is fragmented.
