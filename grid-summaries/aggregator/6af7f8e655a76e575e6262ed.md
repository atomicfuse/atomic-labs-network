## Kreiss-Tomkins Takes Alaska Governorship in Upset Over Three Republicans

Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins has won Alaska's governor's race, beating three Republican opponents. Among them was the candidate endorsed by President Donald Trump.

### The Result

Kreiss-Tomkins' victory puts a young Democrat at the center of Alaska politics, in a state often linked with Republican strength. His win over a field of three Republicans, including the Trump-backed contender, marks a notable outcome in the race for the state's top office.

### Why It Matters
