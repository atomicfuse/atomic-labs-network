## Rogan: Podcasters Aren't White House Reporters

Joe Rogan says podcasters should not be treated like White House journalists or handed front-row spots at press briefings. His argument is that podcasting is about commentary, not reporting. The remarks come as "new media" figures have gained access during and after the 2024 "podcast election."

### What Rogan is arguing

Rogan draws a line between the work of reporters and the work of podcast hosts. In his view, commentary is a different job from newsgathering, so those who do it should not sit alongside real journalists at Trump press conferences or receive the same status in the briefing room.

### Why the debate matters now

Podcasts and influencers have become major channels for political communication, especially after Trump and other candidates turned to alternative media to reach voters. Rogan's pushback highlights a growing tension over who counts as press when an audience's influence can rival that of institutional journalism.
