## FAA Lifts Nationwide Ground Stop, but Flight Disruptions Are Expected to Continue

The Federal Aviation Administration has ended a nationwide ground stop that followed communications and radar instability affecting U.S. air travel. Even so, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said cancellations and major delays will keep going.

### Officials Blame an Aging System

Duffy pointed to an overloaded, aging air traffic control system as the cause of the trouble. He called for $50 billion in emergency funding to modernize it, framing the disruption as evidence that the current infrastructure is no longer up to the job.

### A Rare Event With Wide Impact

Nationwide ground stops are extremely rare, and this one stranded more than a million passengers. Airlines, international flights, and sports teams were all affected. The episode arrives amid growing scrutiny of U.S. aviation infrastructure, cybersecurity risks, and the reliability of outdated federal systems.
