## Europe's Protests Reveal a Widening Generational Divide

Aging populations and climbing pension costs are putting public budgets under pressure across Europe. That strain is fueling friction between retirees and younger people, a rift some describe as a generational clash.

### Where the Tension Comes From

As Europeans live longer, the cost of supporting retirees grows, leaving governments with tough choices about where to spend. Many younger people feel that schools, housing, and public services are being neglected while resources flow toward pensions. The article frames this as a contest over priorities, with each generation seeing its needs squeezed by the other's.

### Why It Is Coming to a Head

Protests in France and other European countries have pushed intergenerational inequality into the center of politics. With budgets tightening and life expectancy still rising, the trade-off between funding pensions and funding public services is becoming harder for governments to sidestep. The debate now looks set to shape political arguments across the continent.
