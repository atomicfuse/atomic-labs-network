## Nearly $20m in Cash Found in Probe of Former Hungarian Minister

Hungarian authorities say they have uncovered close to $20m in cash during a corruption investigation into Miklós Seszták, who served as a minister under Viktor Orbán. The money was reportedly hidden in several kinds of places.

### Where the cash turned up

Investigators say the cash was concealed in farms, warehouses, safes and forests. The discovery comes from a probe into Seszták, an Orbán-era former minister. The case forms part of a broader anti-corruption campaign led by Prime Minister Péter Magyar, which targets alleged graft during Orbán's years in power.

### Why it matters now

Magyar won office promising "regime change" and is moving quickly to dismantle networks tied to the Orbán era. That makes this case a defining test of Hungary's post-Orbán political reset. The find also arrives alongside wider arrests and investigations involving former senior officials and people in Orbán's own family circle.
