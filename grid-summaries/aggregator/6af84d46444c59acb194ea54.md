## Op-Ed Urges Congress to Shield CDC Science From Political Pressure

An opinion article contends that Congress should put the CDC's scientific independence into law. It points to what it calls unprecedented political intervention in the agency's vaccine advisory process.

### The Four Priorities

The piece sets out four areas for action. First, it wants the CDC's independence codified in statute. Second, it calls for rebuilding the agency's workforce. Third, it urges lawmakers to define clearly what the CDC is responsible for. Fourth, it says autonomy should be paired with accountability, so that greater independence comes with oversight.

### Why the Timing Matters

A new Congress is in place alongside a Republican administration, which keeps public health governance a live political battleground. The article presents CDC reform as a bipartisan matter of public trust, citing controversies from the COVID era and recent upheaval at the agency as reasons the issue deserves attention now.
