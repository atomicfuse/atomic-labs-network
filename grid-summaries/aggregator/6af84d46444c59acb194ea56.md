## Canadian Hockey Fans Face a Costly Maze of Ways to Watch

Following the NHL in Canada now takes more money and more effort than it once did. Games are scattered across several services, and fans must work out which one carries the matchup they want.

### A Patchwork of Platforms

Coverage is divided among Sportsnet streaming, Prime Video, TSN and NHL Centre Ice, while blackout rules add further complications. The article ties the rising cost of viewing to wider fan frustration, noting that attendance has fallen in some markets even as stars such as Connor McDavid post a historic scoring pace.

### Why the Shift Matters

Hockey sits at the heart of Canadian culture, so changes in access carry weight. Free Saturday broadcasts of Hockey Night in Canada have ended after 75 years, marking a major turning point. As leagues pursue streaming revenue, fans are being asked to pay more while navigating a confusing mix of platforms.
