## White House Readies Hard-Line Defense Against Democratic Oversight

The Trump White House is preparing an aggressive plan to push back on investigations from a Democratic-led Congress, according to the article. The strategy could include portraying some oversight efforts as criminal in nature.

### Building the Defense

Stephen Miller is described as playing a central role in shaping the approach. The Justice Department and the White House Counsel have also laid legal groundwork for it. At the heart of the dispute is how much access Congress can demand to the administration's internal decision-making, a question likely to be tested in the coming months.

### A Constitutional Clash Ahead

With Democrats back in control of Congress, a significant separation-of-powers confrontation is taking shape over subpoenas, executive privilege, and accountability. The article suggests Trump's second-term posture looks more confrontational than the stonewalling approach he used in his first term, raising the stakes for both branches of government.
