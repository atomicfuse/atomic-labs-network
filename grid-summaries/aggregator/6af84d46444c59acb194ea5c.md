## Teenager Vaibhav Sooryavanshi Becomes Youngest Captain in Men's T20 Cricket

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has added another record to his name, leading Bihar in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy at the age of 15 years and 232 days. That makes him the youngest captain in top-level men's T20 cricket.

### A Record-Breaking Rise

The captaincy is described as the latest milestone in a rapid climb. His earlier achievements include playing in the IPL, making his India debut and becoming the youngest T20 centurion. Each of those marks set him apart from his peers, and leading a state side now extends that run of firsts into a new area.

### What Comes Next

Indian cricket is seeing younger players take on major roles earlier than before, and Sooryavanshi is moving from teenage prodigy to leadership figure. His upcoming appearance at the Rising Stars Asia Cup offers a timely chance to follow his progress and see whether the hype around him can turn into lasting international promise.
