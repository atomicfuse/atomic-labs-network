## Boyle Weighs Budget Route for Democrats' Affordability Push

Rep. Brendan Boyle, a top House Democrat, is considering budget reconciliation as a way to pass an affordability package that could not be blocked by a filibuster. The plan would focus on several cost-of-living priorities.

### What the package would target

The proposal aims at health care, child care, housing and Medicaid cuts. By using reconciliation, Democrats would put the package before President Trump in a way that leaves him with choices: negotiate over the contents, sign the popular cost-of-living measures into law, or veto them. The approach is meant to create pressure on the White House.

### Why the timing matters

Democrats are preparing to use their new congressional majorities to deliver on the affordability promises that helped them win power. Living costs remain central to voter frustration. As a result, the fight over budget rules could become the first major test of divided government in the Trump era.
