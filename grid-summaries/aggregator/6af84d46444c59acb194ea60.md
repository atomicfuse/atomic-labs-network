## Zelensky says US floated Donbas withdrawal in return for Nato membership

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has claimed that American negotiators raised a possible deal in which Ukraine would pull out of its remaining positions in Donbas and, in return, receive a path into Nato.

### A notable shift in the debate

The article presents the idea as a major change in tone. Kyiv has so far refused to accept territorial concessions, while Russia keeps demanding that Ukraine stay neutral and that it hold control over the regions it has occupied. Zelensky's account suggests the US has been testing a trade-off that links land to security guarantees.

### Why the timing matters

The reported proposal arrives during renewed diplomacy involving the US, the EU, Ukraine and Russia aimed at ending the war. Nato membership appears to be back on the agenda after US officials had earlier dismissed it. Any bargain involving Donbas would be politically explosive inside Ukraine and could reshape the terms of a peace settlement.
