## Venezuela's Oil, Once a Source of Pride, Faces a Reckoning Over Identity

Venezuela's oil has long been viewed as a national birthright and a central part of how the country sees itself. A new article examines how that idea is being reinterpreted as the United States under Trump asserts control over a major share of the country's reserves.

### Old Assumptions Under Strain

The article argues that years of collapse, mismanagement, sanctions and hardship have weakened long-held nationalist beliefs about sovereign control of oil. What was once treated as a clear matter of national ownership and pride is now being viewed through the lens of economic survival and hard experience.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

Oil is again central to geopolitics, energy security and U.S. influence in Latin America. Economic desperation is reshaping how Venezuelans think about sovereignty, wealth and what oil can realistically deliver, making the question of who controls the reserves newly urgent.
