## Travelers Still Scrambling After Rare Nationwide Ground Stop

A rare nationwide FAA ground stop led to mass cancellations and delays at airports across the country, and passengers are still feeling the effects. A new article outlines four urgent issues facing affected travelers.

### Why Disruptions Could Linger

The ground stop was the largest nationwide of its kind in the United States in 25 years, and hundreds of thousands of travelers are still dealing with the fallout. Airlines now have to reposition planes and crews, a process that can keep delays and cancellations going even after normal operations resume. The article explains why those knock-on problems may continue for some time.

### Waivers, Rebooking and Refunds

Because rebooking windows and refund policies differ from airline to airline, passengers need quick, practical guidance. The article describes how airline waivers work and what refund rights travelers have, helping people decide what steps to take next as they try to get where they are going.
