## Democrats' Denver Gathering Previews 2028 Without the Fights

A major Democratic gathering in Denver gave potential 2028 presidential contenders a first stage to test early messages. Most of them steered clear of sharp policy clashes and ideological contrasts, leaving the primary's defining battles unsettled.

### A Party Riding Momentum

Democrats arrived in Denver enjoying the lift from a major midterm win. That success seemed to shape the mood, as hopefuls favored unity over confrontation. The 2028 field is already taking form, but the event offered few clear signals about how the contenders differ from one another or where each hopes to take the party.

### Tensions Still Unresolved

The party appears eager to move past progressive-versus-moderate labels, yet pressure remains for new leadership and a clearer direction. Questions about power, policy, and generational change were not settled in Denver. Democrats are trying to balance staying united against demands for fresh voices, and the coming primary will likely force those issues into the open.
