## Rubio Pairs Economic Empathy With Hardline Foreign Policy at Federalist Society

Marco Rubio delivered the keynote address at the Federalist Society National Lawyers Convention, speaking after Republican midterm losses. His remarks combined a message on household finances with a firm defense of Trump-era foreign policy.

### What Rubio said

Rubio argued that the economic pain Americans actually live through matters more than market metrics. He also defended the Iran war, immigration enforcement and strikes in Latin America. On the global stage, he contended that "international law" is being used to limit American sovereignty.

### Why it matters now

Voters punished Republicans over costs, tariffs, war and broken promises on prices, which makes economic credibility a defining issue heading toward 2028. The speech also reads as a positioning moment for Rubio as conservatives work out the party's post-midterm message and weigh future presidential contenders.
