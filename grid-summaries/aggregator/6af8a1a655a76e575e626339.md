## Wales Stun All Blacks in Cardiff to End 73-Year Wait

Wales beat New Zealand 35-28 in Cardiff, ending a run of 34 straight defeats against the All Blacks that stretched back to 1953. The result is framed as one of the finest performances in Welsh rugby history.

### How the win was built

Wales began quickly and scored five tries, setting the tone for a result few would have predicted. As New Zealand pushed late on, the home side held firm, with the closing defence described as heroic. That combination of early attacking sharpness and resilient finishing secured a victory that had eluded Welsh teams for more than seven decades.

### Why it matters now

Welsh rugby has been struggling both on and off the field, so this win feels like a reset moment for a generation of supporters and players. It also arrives within the Nations Championship context, shaking up momentum and national belief with one regular weekend still to come.
