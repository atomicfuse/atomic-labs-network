## Democrats Push ICE Reform After Midterm Gains, Though Voters Cared More About Money

Democrats are pointing to their 2026 midterm wins, particularly in Texas and districts with large Latino populations, as a mandate to press for changes to ICE and immigration policy. Polling, however, shows voters ranked the economy far above immigration as a priority.

### A Warning for Both Parties

The article frames the results as a caution to Republicans and Democrats alike. Affordability concerns appear to have driven turnout, yet aggressive immigration enforcement may have pushed away Hispanic voters whose support both parties want.

### Why It Matters Now

With Democrats claiming momentum and early 2028 positioning underway, Latino voter priorities are becoming central to each party's strategy. The tension between economic frustration and backlash over immigration could shape campaign messaging, policy promises, and how viable individual candidates look to voters.
