## Iran's Mine Claims Face Doubt as Nuclear Inspections Remain Blocked

Iran says tankers in the Strait of Hormuz are striking naval mines, but some analysts question that account. At the same time, concerns persist over Tehran's uranium stockpile and an Isfahan site that inspectors cannot reach.

### Doubts over the mine explanation

U.S.-based analysts argue Iran may be attacking ships directly instead of relying on mines. In their view, the aim could be to erode confidence in American escorts. The Strait of Hormuz is a vital oil chokepoint, so more tanker attacks could unsettle energy markets and raise the risk of a wider confrontation between Washington and Tehran.

### Nuclear access still unresolved

Inspectors remain unable to visit a secretive site at Isfahan or confirm the size of Iran's uranium stockpile. The lack of verification adds urgency to the situation, particularly as diplomacy remains stalled and tensions around the strait continue to build.
