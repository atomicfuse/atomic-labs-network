## Los Angeles Gang Member Indicted Over Alleged Help for Ex-Olympian Drug Boss

Federal prosecutors have indicted Roberto Gomez, a member of the 38th Street gang, for allegedly helping former Olympic snowboarder Ryan Wedding dodge law enforcement. Wedding is accused of running a cocaine-trafficking operation worth a billion dollars.

### The Allegations

According to the article, Gomez is alleged to have had ties to a cartel and access to sensitive law enforcement information. The indictment claims he used his position to help Wedding stay ahead of authorities while the trafficking operation continued. The report also traces Wedding's path from Olympic athlete to accused drug kingpin.

### Why It Matters Now

The case brings together sports, organized crime, cartel networks and federal law enforcement, at a time when transnational drug trafficking remains a major public concern. Wedding's recent time as a fugitive and his later surrender make Gomez's indictment one chapter in a larger crime story that is still unfolding.
