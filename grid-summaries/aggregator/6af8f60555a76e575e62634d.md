## TNA's Destination X Goes Ahead in Edmonton Despite Travel Chaos

TNA Wrestling's Destination X pay-per-view will still take place in Edmonton, though disruptions tied to an FAA ground stop have forced changes to the planned lineup.

### Talent Scrambling to Arrive

TNA president Carlos Silva said performers are working hard to reach the show. Some have offered to fly part of the way and then drive more than 600 miles to make it to Edmonton. The travel problems have left the company adjusting its card as it waits to see who can get there.

### A Wider Ripple Effect

The trouble stems from the longest nationwide FAA ground stop in 25 years, an episode that shows how major air travel disruptions can spill over into live entertainment and sports. The timing adds weight for TNA, since Destination X is returning for the first time since 2017.
