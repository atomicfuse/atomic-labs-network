## Ireland Dispatch Fiji With Seven Tries After Early Scare

Ireland beat Fiji 43-15 in Dublin in the Nations Championship, collecting a bonus point along the way. The hosts began poorly but found their rhythm after the break.

### A shaky start, then a surge

Fiji threatened an upset during a first half in which Ireland looked unconvincing. The second half told a different story. Ireland scored 26 unanswered points after the interval, crossing for seven tries in total and pulling clear of the visitors to finish comfortably ahead.

### What it means for the table

The result keeps Ireland in the race to finish top of the northern hemisphere table and reach the inaugural Nations Championship finale at Twickenham. Their next assignment is a high-stakes meeting with world champions South Africa next week, a game that now carries significant weight in the contest for a place in the final.
