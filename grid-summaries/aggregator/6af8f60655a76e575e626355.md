## Port of New Orleans sues tanker owners after cruise ship collision

The Port of New Orleans has filed a lawsuit against the owners and managers of the tanker Atlantic Asphalt. The vessel struck the cruise ship American Heritage, injuring 32 people and causing more than $1m in damage.

### What the lawsuit claims

The suit alleges negligence and asks for money to cover cleanup, inspections, repairs and related costs. Investigators are meanwhile looking into a possible steering failure aboard the tanker, though the cause has not been established.

### Why the case is drawing attention

More than 100 passengers had to be rescued from the Mississippi River after the crash, which officials described as a "miracle on the Mississippi." The incident raises immediate questions about port safety, oversight of commercial shipping and who bears liability when major river traffic goes wrong.
