## Trump Floods Social Media With Images Of D.C. Golf Course Plan

President Trump has posted more than 40 images on Truth Social promoting a redevelopment of East Potomac Park Golf Links in Washington, D.C., a site he calls "Trash Island." The posts arrive while a judge considers whether to block the park's closure.

### Competing Explanations For The Closure

The administration points to safety as its reason for closing the park. Preservation groups disagree, claiming the closure is linked to a wider overhaul of the golf course. That difference in rationale sits at the center of the legal dispute now before the court.

### Why The Fight Is Heating Up

The park faces imminent closure while the case is still active, making it a live flashpoint over public land, historic preservation, and presidential influence. Trump's unusually image-heavy posting has also pushed what might have stayed a local redevelopment argument into the national political spotlight.
