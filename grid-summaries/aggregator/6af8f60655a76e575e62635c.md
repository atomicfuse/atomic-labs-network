## Andreeva and Svitolina Set for WTA Finals Title Match

Mirra Andreeva beat Jessica Pegula to reach the final of the WTA Finals in Indian Wells. Elina Svitolina crushed Linda Noskova to join her in the title match.

### Two Routes to the Final

Andreeva's win makes her the youngest finalist since Maria Sharapova, a milestone that underlines her historic rise. Svitolina's path tells a different story. Since returning from maternity leave she has climbed back into the top 10, and her dominant semifinal result against Noskova carried her into the last match of the season.

### Why the Final Matters

The match pairs a teenage star accelerating toward the top of women's tennis with a veteran showing she can still beat the elite. Andreeva is set to finish the year as world number two, while Svitolina leads the tour in top-10 wins. With those credentials on both sides, the title match carries major end-of-season significance.
