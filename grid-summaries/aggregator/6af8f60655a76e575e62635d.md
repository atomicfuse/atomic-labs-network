## Security Expert Doubts Official Account of Four-Hour Airspace Shutdown

National security expert Richard Clarke has criticized the explanations officials gave for a four-hour FAA shutdown of U.S. airspace, saying many questions remain unanswered.

### Cyber Causes Not Ruled Out

Clarke argued that it is too early to dismiss a cyber cause for the disruption. He also asked whether new FAA technology had been properly tested before it was put into use, suggesting that the agency's rollout deserves closer examination before any conclusions are drawn.

### Communication and Wider Concern

Clarke was also harsh about how the government communicated during the crisis. The shutdown came shortly after a major Microsoft outage, which has heightened public worry about fragile digital infrastructure and aviation safety. It also arrives amid broader scrutiny of government technology modernization, AI systems, and transparency during critical disruptions.
