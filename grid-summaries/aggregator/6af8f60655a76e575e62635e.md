## Trump Jabs at DC Golf Course a Day Before Overhaul Starts

President Trump has taken another swipe at East Potomac Golf Links in Washington, D.C., one day before a major federally backed overhaul of the public course is set to begin. The project is framed as an effort to turn a struggling course into a championship-level venue.

### Scrutiny Around the Project

The redevelopment is drawing legal and environmental attention. A court inquiry is under way, and tree cutting has been halted. Those developments have fed a wider debate over how public land is used, how much influence a president should have over local projects, and what urban redevelopment should look like in the capital.

### Political Spectacle

Trump's sharply worded "Trash Island" comments have added a layer of political theater to what could otherwise have been a local infrastructure story. With work about to start, the remarks put fresh focus on the course's condition and on the disputes surrounding its future.
