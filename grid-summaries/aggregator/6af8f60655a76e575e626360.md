## Tanker Hits Docked Cruise Ship in New Orleans, Sending About 100 Into the Mississippi

A tanker struck the docked American Heritage cruise ship in New Orleans, and roughly 100 people ended up in the Mississippi River. All 191 people aboard survived, and at least 100 were pulled from the water.

### Recovery and investigation

Officials are now turning to several tasks, including removing fuel, monitoring the environment, and carrying out salvage operations. A lengthy salvage effort is expected. Investigators are also working to determine what caused the crash, and one possibility under examination is a steering issue.

### Why it matters now

The outcome is being called a "Miracle on the Mississippi" because a mass-casualty disaster was narrowly avoided. The crash also raises timely questions about safety for river traffic, how prepared cruise operators are for emergencies, and what risks exist for infrastructure at major U.S. ports.
