## Classic Twilight Zone Episodes Foresaw Today's Tech Anxieties

A new article looks back at the classic series and argues that several of its episodes anticipated modern debates. The topics range from AI companionship and beauty conformity to grief technology, surveillance, and authoritarian media spectacle.

### Old Stories, Current Questions

According to the piece, the show's writers imagined ideas that once seemed purely fictional but now echo real conversations. Episodes touching on artificial companions, pressure to look a certain way, and technology tied to mourning are presented as early takes on dilemmas people now face. Others are said to prefigure worries about being watched and about power staged as public entertainment.

### Why Serling Still Resonates

The article suggests these themes feel more pressing as AI partners, voice clones, cosmetic normalization, and public-facing punishment debates move from science fiction into daily life. In that light, Rod Serling's moral questions about human behavior and technology seem newly urgent for today's audiences.
