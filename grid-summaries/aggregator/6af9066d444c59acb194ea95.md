## NBC's "Real People" Helped Write the Reality TV Playbook

An article argues that NBC's 1980s series "Real People" laid the groundwork for modern reality television, arriving years before "The Real World" and "Survivor." Its mix of everyday folks and quirky stories set a template the genre still follows.
