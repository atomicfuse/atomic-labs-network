## Editorial Warns Against Religious Language Justifying Political Violence

An editorial condemns a rabbi who invoked *din rodef* against Jewish politicians weighing a government backed by Arab parties. It cautions that this kind of religious language echoes the justification used in Yitzhak Rabin's assassination.

### Coalition Disputes Are Legitimate

The editorial argues that disagreements over coalition arrangements are valid political questions open to debate. Such arguments, it says, must never become a license for violence, and religious framing cannot be used to excuse threats against elected officials.

### Why It Matters Now

Arab parties may play a decisive role in Israel's next coalition, which makes debates over legitimacy, security and campaign promises especially charged. Against the backdrop of Rabin's killing, renewed religious rhetoric about political violence carries urgent consequences for both democracy and security.
