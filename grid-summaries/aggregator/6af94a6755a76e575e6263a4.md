## Europe Looks Increasingly Like Italy, Article Argues

A new piece makes the case that European politics are becoming "Italianized," with Italy's long-familiar turbulence now showing up across the continent rather than staying a national peculiarity.

### What the Article Describes

The author points to several features of this shift: coalitions that are fragmented, voters who are frustrated with the political establishment, and a general sense of instability. Another feature is the normalization of far-right parties that were once kept outside the mainstream. In this reading, Italy was an early example of patterns that other countries are now experiencing.

### Why It Matters Now

Giorgia Meloni's rise in Italy, together with the growing strength of far-right parties in Britain, France, and Germany, gives the argument fresh weight. Italy appears less like an exception and more like a preview of where European politics may be heading, which invites a rethink of how the country is viewed.
