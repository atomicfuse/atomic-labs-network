## Kreiss-Tomkins Credits Statewide Reach for Alaska Governor Win

Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins is reflecting on his victory as Alaska's first Democratic governor in nearly 30 years. He says his campaign succeeded by covering every corner of the state and drawing broad bipartisan support.

### How the campaign won

Kreiss-Tomkins made rural outreach a central part of his effort, traveling to communities across Alaska. He also collected endorsements from across party lines, which helped him present a bipartisan brand. Education was among his stated priorities, and he has begun laying out plans for his transition into office.

### Why the result matters

His win flips the governorship after years of Republican control. It also adds to a wider Democratic hold on 30 governor's offices nationwide. The result suggests that in-person campaigning, attention to rural areas, and a bipartisan image can still reshape statewide races in red-leaning states.
