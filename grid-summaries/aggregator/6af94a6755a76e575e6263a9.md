## Banff's Famous Grizzly "the Boss" Faces a Risk Born of Fame

Banff National Park's best-known grizzly bear, called "the Boss," has become a local celebrity. A new profile follows the animal's life in the park and the unusual tension at its center.

### A Survivor in the Wild

The Boss has earned his legendary status by getting through encounters with trains, cars and rival bears. Those close calls are a large part of why people in the area speak of him with such admiration, and his reputation has grown alongside his popularity.

### When Admiration Becomes Danger

The article's central point is that his fame may now threaten him more than the wilderness does. The story arrives as wildlife tourism, social media fandom around animals and crowded national parks collide with conservation concerns. It raises a timely question: when does public affection for wild animals begin to create risk for both people and the animals themselves?
