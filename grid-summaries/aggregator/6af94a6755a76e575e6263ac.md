## Federal judge pauses Trump's plan to remake a DC municipal golf course

A federal judge has temporarily blocked President Trump's plan to redevelop a municipal golf course in Washington, DC. The order means the course must stay open, even though the administration had intended to shut it down for renovations.

### What the ruling does

The decision halts the closure for now, keeping the public course available while the matter stands. Because the block is temporary, it pauses the redevelopment effort rather than settling the dispute. The administration had planned to close the course so renovation work could proceed, and that step cannot go ahead under the judge's order.

### Why the timing matters

The ruling arrives amid heightened scrutiny of development projects linked to Trump and of federal control over public spaces in Washington, DC. It also raises a broader question about who gets to decide the future of municipal amenities that local residents use. That question now sits at the center of the dispute over the golf course.
