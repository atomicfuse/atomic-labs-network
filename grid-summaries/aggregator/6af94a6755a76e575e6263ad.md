## Judge Pauses Plan to Shut Historic DC Golf Course for Renovation

A federal judge has temporarily stopped the Trump administration from closing Washington DC's East Potomac Golf Links for a planned 15-month renovation, delaying a project that had already started.

### The Ruling

The judge found that the government's stated reasons for the closure appeared pretextual. The ruling also said the administration may have broken federal laws while trying to meet an arbitrary deadline. The order is temporary, so the course's long-term fate has not been settled. The decision puts the renovation on hold while the legal challenge continues.

### Why It Matters Now

The case touches several live issues: how public land is used, the influence of a president, the protection of historic sites, and whether government is held accountable. Timing adds to the urgency, since tree removal at the course had already begun. The court is now also examining whether Justice Department lawyers misled the judge during the proceedings.
