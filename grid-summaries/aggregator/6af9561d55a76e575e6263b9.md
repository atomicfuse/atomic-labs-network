## Travel Industry Presses Congress to Modernize Air Traffic Control After Ground Stop

The US Travel Association is calling on Congress to invest in modern air traffic control infrastructure following a nationwide ground stop that disrupted flights. The group says the episode points to a deeper problem in the nation's aviation system.

### A Warning About System Failures

The association's central argument is that aviation system failures should no longer be seen as isolated inconveniences. Instead, it describes them as signs of an urgent infrastructure problem that requires attention from lawmakers. Its message focuses on the need for funding and modernization of the technology that keeps flights moving.

### Why the Timing Matters

Air travel reliability is a high-stakes issue because passenger demand remains strong. When the system is disrupted, the effects ripple across tourism, business travel, and local economies. The ground stop gives travel leaders a timely opening to push Congress to act on funding and modernization.
