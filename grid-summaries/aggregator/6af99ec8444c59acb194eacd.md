## Sayers Backs Khawaja Over Cummins in Sandpaper Row

Former Australia bowler Chadd Sayers has entered the debate over the 2018 Cape Town ball-tampering scandal, offering a version of events that conflicts with Pat Cummins' recollection.

### Sayers' account

Sayers says he took sandpaper off the field during the Test before the Cape Town match. He supports Usman Khawaja's account rather than that of Cummins, and argues that "everyone knew" about the plan. He also recalls a long dressing-room conversation about collective responsibility after Cameron Bancroft was caught.

### Why it resurfaces now

Differing player memories are reopening questions about accountability, leadership and what the Australian team knew in 2018. The row also adds fresh tension around Cummins' public comments and the claims Khawaja has newly published, keeping the scandal firmly in the spotlight.
