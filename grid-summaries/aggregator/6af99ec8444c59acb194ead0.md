## Lebanon Braces for Possible Eisenkot Government as Israel Talks Stall

Negotiations between Israel and Lebanon have stalled while it remains unclear whether Gadi Eisenkot can form Israel's next government. Lebanese officials are preparing for the possibility that he would take a hard-line approach.

### Security presence in the south

Beirut is pushing to keep an international security presence in southern Lebanon, even as the mandate of UNIFIL may come to an end. That prospect arrives just as Israel goes through its own political transition, and the two developments are meeting at a tense moment for Lebanon's wider security arrangements.

### Pressure on Hezbollah

Hezbollah is also facing growing domestic pressure over its weapons and over arrests. The Lebanese state is trying to reassert control over arms and to reduce foreign influence, which has brought fresh scrutiny of the group at a delicate time for the country.
