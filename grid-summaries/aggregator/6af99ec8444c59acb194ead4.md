## Democrats Swap Culture-War Messaging for Class Politics

An article argues that US Democrats are moving away from "woke" politics and culture-war themes. After a strong midterm showing, the party is turning toward a sharper focus on class and economic issues.

### The Pivot

The central idea is that economic populism could become the party's new unifying strategy. Rather than leaning on cultural messaging, Democrats would build their appeal around class-based concerns, an approach the article presents as a deliberate change of direction following their midterm performance.

### Why the Timing Matters

Voters are still feeling pressure from inflation, housing costs, wages and inequality. The article suggests this makes class politics more electorally potent than symbolic cultural battles. The shift also points to a possible recalibration of Democratic strategy as the party looks ahead to the next national campaign cycle.
