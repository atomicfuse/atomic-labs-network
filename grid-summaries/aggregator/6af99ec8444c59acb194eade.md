## Biden's Memoir Delivers Blunt Verdicts on Rivals, Allies and His Own Party

Joe Biden's new memoir offers candid reflections on Benjamin Netanyahu, Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, Volodymyr Zelensky and the Democratic leaders who pushed him out of the 2024 race. He describes Netanyahu as having Trump-like tendencies and says he bent democratic institutions to serve himself.

### Defending a Presidency

The book's central thread is Biden's defense of his time in office. Alongside that defense, it reveals mistrust, regret and resentment behind major decisions on Israel, Ukraine and his decision to seek reelection.

### Why the Timing Matters

The memoir arrives as Democrats reassess the 2024 campaign, Trump's return and the political fallout from the Gaza war. Biden's comments reopen debates over U.S.-Israel policy, aging leadership and party loyalty, at a moment when both parties are redefining their futures.
