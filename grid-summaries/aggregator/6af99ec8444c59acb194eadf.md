## New Scrutiny of Ron Arad Case Questions Who Held Israel's Missing Airman

A Times investigation returns to Israel's long search for Ron Arad, the missing airman, and asks whether the blame placed on Iran told the whole story.

### The Hunt for Answers

For decades, Israel believed Iran held answers about Arad's fate. The investigation recounts a reported 2021 Israeli kidnapping of an Iranian commander, apparently carried out so he could be interrogated. It suggests other theories about what happened to Arad may have been overlooked during years of bloodshed, secrecy, and mistrust.

### Why It Resonates Today

Hostage politics are again central in Israel after the Hamas captivity crisis, which gives Arad's unresolved case fresh weight as a symbol of the state's promise to bring its people home. The story also arrives amid heightened tensions between Israel and Iran, along with renewed scrutiny of intelligence operations carried out beyond the country's borders.
