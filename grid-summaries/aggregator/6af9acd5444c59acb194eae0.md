## AI Matches and Slightly Beats Clinicians at Predicting Survival From Heart Scans

A large study in Lancet Digital Health found that AI-based measurements taken from cardiac MRI scans were slightly but significantly better than those of experienced clinicians at predicting survival risk.

### What the study found

The AI tool analyzed each scan in seconds, while clinicians needed roughly 13 minutes. Even so, the tool has a small error rate, so its results still require review by a human. The findings point to a supporting role for AI rather than a replacement for doctors.

### Why it matters now

Healthcare systems face rising demand for imaging and growing clinician workload, alongside strong interest in practical AI tools that can speed up care. This study adds evidence that AI may work best as a first-line assistant for routine medical measurements, with doctors still checking the output.
