## Review Points to Three Antidepressants for Lasting Relief

A new review in *The Lancet Psychiatry* suggests that escitalopram, agomelatine, and duloxetine may provide better sustained relief from depression symptoms than fluoxetine. The article also looks at how these medicines compare with one another.

### How the drugs compare

The review weighs the strengths and limits of each of the three antidepressants against fluoxetine, and it explains who may or may not be well suited to each one. Its central message is that the choice of medication still rests on a person's individual medical history, so no single drug fits everyone, even when long-term results appear stronger.

### Why it matters now

Many people take antidepressants for months or even years, which makes long-term effectiveness an important concern for both patients and clinicians. The findings could shape future treatment guidelines. They may also help patients hold more informed conversations with their clinicians about which option is right for them.
