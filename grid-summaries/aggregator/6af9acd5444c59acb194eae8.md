## Two-Drug Cholesterol Combo Outperforms High-Dose Statin on Artery Plaque

A trial published in JAMA Cardiology found that pairing rosuvastatin with ezetimibe did more to lower LDL cholesterol and reduce coronary plaque volume than high-dose atorvastatin used alone.

### What the trial found

Patients on the combination saw bigger drops in LDL cholesterol and a greater reduction in the volume of plaque in their coronary arteries. The results suggest that aggressively lowering LDL may help shrink and stabilize artery plaque. Researchers note that more study is needed to learn whether this translates into fewer heart attacks or strokes.

### Why it matters now

Heart disease remains a leading health concern, and the notion that atherosclerosis may be partly reversible marks a powerful shift in prevention messaging. With more patients and clinicians weighing combination cholesterol therapy, the trial adds timely evidence to the "lower LDL, lower risk" conversation.
