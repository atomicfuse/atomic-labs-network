## A Missed Deadline Prompts a Case for Truly Switching Off

Healthline's Editor-in-Chief has written a personal letter about missing a deadline just before a weekend away. The experience becomes a starting point for a broader look at why stepping back from work matters.

### The Case for Detaching

The piece centers on the idea of psychological detachment, meaning a genuine mental break from work during time off. According to the letter, practicing it can support well-being, lower the risk of burnout, and help people come back to their jobs feeling more restored. The missed deadline serves as a real-life prompt for exploring how hard it can be to let go.

### Why the Timing Fits

With Thanksgiving and the holiday season approaching, many people are balancing time off with family obligations, guilt about work, and digital habits that keep them always reachable. The article joins a timely conversation about burnout and boundaries, and about making rest feel like a legitimate choice rather than something to apologize for.
