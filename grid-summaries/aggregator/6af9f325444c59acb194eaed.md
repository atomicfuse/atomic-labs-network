## Poll asks who is to blame for a toxic political climate

A Marc poll carried out for Antenna TV examined who Greeks hold responsible for the toxicity in the political climate. The results rank four sources of blame, with the government at the top.

### What the poll found

The government received the largest share of responsibility for the tense political atmosphere. It was followed by the media, then the opposition, and finally social media. The ranking shows how respondents distribute blame among political actors, news outlets and online platforms.

### Why it matters now

Political polarisation and distrust toward institutions and the media remain central issues in public debate. The findings indicate where citizens see the main sources of tension. Because the media and social media appear alongside the government and the opposition in the ranking, the results suggest the public looks beyond party politics when assigning responsibility for the mood of the national conversation.
