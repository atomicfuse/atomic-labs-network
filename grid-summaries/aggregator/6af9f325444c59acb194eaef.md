## Trump slams judge's two-week pause on East Potomac golf overhaul, vows appeal

President Trump has criticized a federal judge's decision to halt renovations at East Potomac Golf Links in Washington, D.C., for two weeks. He says his administration will appeal the ruling.

### What the fight is about

The dispute turns on what the redevelopment would actually do. One view holds that the project would preserve a public, affordable golf course. The opposing view is that it would turn the site into a championship-level venue that fewer people could access.

### Why the timing matters

The pause arrived just as work was set to begin. It also joins a broader wave of legal challenges to Trump-backed redevelopment projects around the capital. The case touches larger debates over the use of public land, presidential influence, historic preservation, and who gets access to major civic spaces.
