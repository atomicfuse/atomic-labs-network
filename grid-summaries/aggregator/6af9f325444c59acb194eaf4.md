## Saudi-Backed Yemeni Forces Pull Back From Bab al-Mandeb

Saudi-backed Yemeni forces are leaving positions near the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, a move that effectively ends a six-week offensive against the Houthis. The forces say they are pulling back to defend Aden.

### Houthis Gain Ground on the Red Sea

The withdrawal hands the Houthis firmer control over strategic approaches to the Red Sea. That strengthens their hand and adds pressure on Taiz, Aden, Saudi Arabia and the global shipping routes that pass through the area.

### Why the Strait Matters Now

Yemen's war is intensifying again after a calmer stretch that followed the 2022 truce, while tensions involving Iran-backed groups spread across the region. Bab al-Mandeb carries global significance because disruption there can affect trade linked to the Suez route, oil flows, shipping costs and energy markets.
