## Rugby League World Cup 2026 Draws Praise and Criticism as a Benchmark

The 2026 Rugby League World Cup is being assessed as a possible turning point for the international game, with strong results in some areas and clear shortcomings in others.

### What the tournament got right

Supporters point to stronger crowds, profitability and elite-level performances as signs of progress. Polynesian momentum also stood out, adding energy and credibility to the event. Together, these positives suggest the sport can stage a commercially viable global tournament.

### Where it fell short

Critics take issue with the reduced men's format and uneven competitiveness between teams. Limited broadcast access and weak support for women's matches also drew complaints. With the 2030 World Cup in the USA ahead, the mixed reception highlights a tension between financial sustainability and the sport's claim to be truly international.
