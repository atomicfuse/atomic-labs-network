## DeMaio Urges Overhaul of California GOP and Eyes New Voter ID Push

Assemblyman Carl DeMaio is calling for sweeping changes to the California Republican Party in the wake of recent election losses. He also says he wants to bring back a voter ID ballot measure after Prop 39 failed.

### A Call to Rebuild the Party

DeMaio criticizes the party's leadership, its consultants and the way it sets spending priorities. His argument is that the losses demand a major restructuring rather than minor adjustments. The comments come as California Republicans face renewed pressure to explain their results and find a way to stay relevant in a state dominated by Democrats.

### Reviving Voter ID

After Prop 39 did not succeed, DeMaio plans to revive the voter ID effort through a new ballot measure. His push feeds into wider debates about party accountability and trust in elections, and it raises the question of whether voter ID can gain traction in California.
