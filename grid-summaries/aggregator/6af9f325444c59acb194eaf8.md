## Toy Soldier Line Turns Real Combat Veterans Into Collectible Figures

A new American-made toy soldier line called Real Plastic Heroes is drawing attention for basing its figures on actual combat veterans. The project is led by founder Tyler Donovan, with input from retired Air Force combat controller Eric Lionheart.

### What the Line Aims to Do

Real Plastic Heroes presents each figure as more than a plaything. Donovan and Lionheart describe the toys as a way to preserve the combat stories of the veterans who inspired them. By tying a physical object to a real person's service, the line seeks to keep those experiences visible and remembered.

### Why It Resonates Now

Patriotic, veteran-centered products are gaining notice as audiences look for tangible ways to honor service members beyond holidays and ceremonies. The made-in-America angle also connects with continuing interest in domestic manufacturing and values-driven buying, giving the line appeal both as a tribute and as a product with a clear story about where it comes from.
