## Garment Strike Shuts Factories Across Bangladesh Hubs

Bangladeshi garment workers are on strike over pay, and the walkout has closed close to 150 factories. Despite the original title's figure, the facts show the number of shutdowns is nearer 150, affecting major textile centres including Savar, Ashulia and Gazipur.

### What workers and owners want

Workers are demanding a minimum wage of 30,000 taka a month, saying living costs keep rising. Factory owners have warned that the closures could carry on. They point to "No Work No Pay" labour rules, which mean staff who do not work would not be paid for that time.

### Why the timing matters

Bangladesh is the world's second-largest exporter of ready-to-wear clothing and supplies big brands such as H&M, Primark and Zara. A drawn-out dispute could therefore disrupt global fashion supply chains. A government wage board is due to recommend a new pay structure by the end of November, which makes the coming weeks especially important.
