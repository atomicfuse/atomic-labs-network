## New AI Trap Aims to Spot Dengue Mosquitoes Faster

Florida is dealing with its largest dengue outbreak in decades, and researchers at the University of South Florida have developed an AI-powered mosquito trap that could help. In lab testing, the device identified disease-carrying mosquito species with more than 98% accuracy.

### How the Trap Could Help

The article argues that quicker detection of dangerous mosquito species could give public health teams a chance to act before outbreaks spread further. By recognizing which species are present, the AI trap could point officials toward the areas and insects that matter most, rather than leaving them to react after cases rise.

### Why the Timing Matters

Mosquitoes remain active well into November in warmer parts of Florida, so the risk has not ended with the summer months. Climate, travel and urban conditions are extending the period in which mosquitoes pose a threat. Against that backdrop, AI-powered surveillance could become a crucial public health tool for tracking and responding to mosquito-borne disease.
