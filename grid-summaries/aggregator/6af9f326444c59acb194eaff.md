## Duffy Says FAA Network Is Stuck in the 1980s and Needs Replacing

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy is calling for a new telecommunications network at the Federal Aviation Administration, arguing the current system is badly out of date. He says a modern replacement is needed and that more money will be required to build it.

### What Duffy Is Asking For

Duffy describes the FAA's existing network as built on architecture from the 1980s. He says AT&T and Verizon are ready to help create a more resilient system to take its place. However, he stresses that the effort cannot move forward without additional funding.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

Scrutiny of air travel infrastructure is growing as system outages, delays, and safety concerns highlight the risks of relying on aging federal technology. Duffy's push reframes aviation modernization as a question of funding and public safety, placing the cost of a new FAA network at the center of the discussion.
