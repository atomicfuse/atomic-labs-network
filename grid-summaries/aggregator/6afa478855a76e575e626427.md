## Haaland's historic rise contrasts with barriers for Native women at home

Deb Haaland is on a path toward becoming the first Native American woman governor. A new article looks at her breakthrough alongside the obstacles Native women still face in leadership roles within some Pueblo communities.

### A dispute over pay and power

The story centers on Ashley Sarracino and her pay-disparity dispute. Her case opens onto wider tensions over tradition, tribal sovereignty, gender roles and political representation. The article weighs how these competing ideas shape who is able to hold leadership positions in certain Pueblo communities.

### Why the contrast matters now

Native women are gaining visibility in mainstream politics, and Haaland's rise is part of that moment. Yet local tribal governance remains uneven in terms of who can lead. The piece highlights a gap between symbolic national progress and the lived realities inside some Indigenous communities.
