## Mexican Cartel Pioneer Félix Gallardo Dies at 80

Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo, who founded one of Mexico's first major drug cartels, has died in a hospital at the age of 80. He had been convicted in the 1985 killing of US Drug Enforcement Administration agent Enrique "Kiki" Camarena.

### A central figure in cartel history

Félix Gallardo was regarded as a key player in the rise of Mexico's modern drug-trafficking networks. His death closes the life of a man whose name remains tied to the origins of the country's cartel era and to one of the most closely remembered cases involving a US agent.

### Why attention returns now

His passing renews focus on how Mexico's cartels began, on cooperation between the United States and Mexico against drugs, and on the lasting impact of the Camarena case. It also arrives while public interest in cartel history, true crime and the roots of today's drug war continues.
