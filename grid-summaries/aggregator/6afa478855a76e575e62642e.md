## NTSB Cites Steering Failure Before New Orleans Riverboat Collision

Federal investigators say a "steering casualty" on a cargo vessel came before its collision with a riverboat in New Orleans. The crash sent 32 people to hospital, all with non-critical injuries.

### What investigators have found

The National Transportation Safety Board is examining the Atlantic Asphalt cargo vessel, which struck the American Heritage riverboat. Investigators say the cargo vessel's rudder appeared to be stuck to starboard. That position pushed the ship toward the east bank of the river before the two vessels collided. The board has not yet said what caused the steering problem.

### Why it matters now

The collision raises fresh questions about river traffic safety, vessel maintenance and emergency response on the busy Mississippi River. An NTSB preliminary report is expected in 30 days. Until then, the incident is likely to stay in the news as investigators work to determine what failed.
