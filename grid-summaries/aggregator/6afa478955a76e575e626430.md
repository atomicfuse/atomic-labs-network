## Rebellion Brews Against Trump's Pick to Lead the RNC

A growing revolt inside Republican National Committee leadership is targeting chair Joe Gruters, the man Donald Trump personally backed for the job. The challenge follows heavy midterm losses, with KC Crosbie emerging as the rival.

### More Than a Committee Post

The article argues the fight is not really about one party position. It frames the contest as a measure of how firmly Trump still controls the Republican Party as attention turns toward 2028. Even after the party's worst midterm showing in a generation, most Republicans have continued to defer to him.

### Why the Chair Race Counts

That makes the chair contest an early test of whether anyone in the party can challenge the president's authority. The stakes are practical as well. The committee will help shape the 2028 primary calendar, the debates, the national convention and the rules for choosing the nominee.
