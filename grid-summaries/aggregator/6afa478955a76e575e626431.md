## Canada Blocks Russian Under-20 Hockey Team From World Juniors

Canada will refuse entry to Russian Under-20 hockey players, which means Russia cannot take part in the 2026-27 World Junior Ice Hockey Championships. The move comes despite a recent Court of Arbitration for Sport ruling that overturned the International Ice Hockey Federation's ban.

### A Ruling Meets a Border

The CAS decision removed the federation's barrier, but Canada's entry policy now stands in the way instead. The case is presented as one chapter in a wider struggle over Russia's attempt to return to international sport following the invasion of Ukraine.

### Visas as a Sporting Weapon

Courts, federations, governments, athletes and sponsors are at odds over whether Russian and Belarusian athletes should be readmitted to global competition. With events coming up in Canada, Norway, Finland and elsewhere, visa policy is emerging as a powerful tool in sports geopolitics.
