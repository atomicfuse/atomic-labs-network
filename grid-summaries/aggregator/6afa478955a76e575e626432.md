## Few 2028 Campaigns Likely to Launch Before 2027, History Suggests

An analysis of past presidential races finds that early launches are rare, even as speculation about 2028 builds. Only 13 of 223 major-party, non-fringe candidacies since 1972 began in the midterm year or earlier.

### What the numbers show

The article looks at how seldom serious contenders declare during a midterm year or before it. Out of 223 candidacies from the two major parties since 1972, just 13 fit that description. Based on that record, it argues that most credible candidates for 2028 will probably hold off until 2027, despite the early chatter about who might run.

### Why the timing matters now

With the midterms behind the political world, attention is turning to the open 2028 race and who might move first. Early announcements can influence media narratives, donor attention, and party positioning well before voters begin paying close attention to the contest.
