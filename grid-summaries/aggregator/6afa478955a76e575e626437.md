## USC Legend Keyshawn Johnson Wants Lincoln Riley Out

Former USC star Keyshawn Johnson has publicly urged the school to move on from head coach Lincoln Riley. His comments came after the Trojans fell to Indiana, a result that has sharpened attention on the program's direction.

### The loss that sparked it

USC lost 27-13 to Indiana, dropping the team to 7-3. The defeat likely ended the Trojans' hopes of reaching the College Football Playoff. Johnson, a legend of the program, responded by saying it is time to find somebody else to lead the team.

### Why Riley's job is in focus

Riley's future is under scrutiny again after another season with three losses. USC has also struggled to build on its earlier upset of Ohio State. With the regular season winding down, pressure is growing around whether the program needs a reset.
