## Waiheke's Final Stoat Hunt Tests Island Conservation

Waiheke Island is working to wipe out stoats, an introduced predator that has devastated New Zealand's native birdlife. More than 280 have been trapped since 2020, but the remaining animals are proving the hardest to find.

### The Hunt for the Shy Survivors

The article follows the tactics used to track the island's last stoats and the community support behind the campaign. With so many animals already caught, those left are described as "shy," which makes trapping them a real challenge. Locals have backed the effort as it enters this difficult final stretch.

### A High-Profile Test Case

New Zealand is stepping up predator control as invasive species continue to kill millions of native birds each year. Waiheke could become the first urban island to eliminate stoats, giving it special significance. Because it is a populated tourist destination, its success or failure may shape how conservation is approached in similar places.
