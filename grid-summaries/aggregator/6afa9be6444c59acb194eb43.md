## COP31 in Antalya Tests Whether Climate Promises Can Reach Africa

An article examines what the COP31 summit in Antalya could mean for Africa, looking at climate finance, adaptation, implementation and the role of Türkiye as COP president. Its central argument is that the continent needs delivery, not fresh pledges.

### Africa's priority: delivery over declarations

According to the piece, Africa's main goal is to convert existing commitments into funding that countries can actually access, projects ready for investors, and workable cooperation on the ground. New promises matter less than making earlier ones function in practice.

### Why the timing matters

COP31 arrives as confidence in global climate diplomacy has weakened and after COP30 left questions about adaptation finance unresolved. For Africa, Antalya is a test of whether the so-called implementation COP can produce real progress on finance access, resilience and climate action tied to development.
