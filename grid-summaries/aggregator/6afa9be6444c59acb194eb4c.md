## Widow of Haiti's slain president joins race for the presidency

Martine Moise, the widow of assassinated former Haitian president Jovenel Moise, has registered to run in Haiti's delayed presidential election. Her candidacy comes as the country faces a political vacuum, gang violence and the unresolved trauma of the 2021 assassination.

### A candidacy shaped by loss

The article frames Moise's bid against the backdrop of her husband's killing in 2021, which remains unresolved in the national memory. Her entry into the contest brings a strongly personal element to a campaign already overshadowed by instability and the absence of settled political leadership.

### An election long overdue

Haiti has not held an election since 2016, and the next vote has been pushed back to July because of insecurity. Moise's registration adds emotional and political weight to a field that already includes former leaders and other major public figures, making the race one of the most closely watched in the country's recent history.
