## Intel shareholders file class action over 29% stock drop

Investors have taken legal action against Intel after the company's shares fell nearly 29% in a single day. The proposed class action targets the chipmaker and its leaders.

### What the lawsuit alleges

Shareholders claim Intel downplayed a slowdown in PC sales before the plunge. According to the complaint, the company's earlier messaging inflated its share price. That changed when disappointing revenue guidance exposed weaker demand, along with pressure tied to AI-driven data centre investments.

### Why it matters now

The case arrives as the chip industry splits in two directions: AI infrastructure demand is booming, while PC hardware sales are weakening and supply crunches persist. The lawsuit also reflects growing investor scrutiny of how large technology companies communicate risks during volatile semiconductor cycles.
