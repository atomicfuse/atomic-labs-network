## William and Catherine land in India for climate-focused visit

Prince William and Catherine, the Prince and Princess of Wales, have arrived in India for a three-day visit that includes a newly added meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Earthshot Prize engagements in Mumbai form a central part of the trip.

### A climate trip with a diplomatic edge

The visit is framed as both a climate-focused royal tour and a sign of William's growing diplomatic role. Alongside the Earthshot events in Mumbai, the addition of a meeting with Modi gives the schedule a more political dimension.

### Why the timing matters

This is the couple's first official overseas trip together in four years. It also comes as Catherine returns to more public duties after cancer treatment. The destination carries weight too, as India has produced more Earthshot finalists and winners than any other country, highlighting its growing role in climate innovation.
