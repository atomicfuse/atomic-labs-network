## Trump Administration Seeks $50 Billion to Rebuild Aging FAA Air Traffic System

The Trump administration is asking Congress for $50 billion to overhaul the Federal Aviation Administration's outdated air traffic control system. The request follows a rare nationwide flight grounding tied to router and network failures.

### Democrats Press on Unspent Funds

Democrats are scrutinizing the proposal, questioning why modernization money Congress previously approved remains largely unspent. They also want to know why the new request has climbed so sharply compared with earlier figures, and they are pressing for answers on how the funds have been used so far.

### Why the Timing Matters

The grounding disrupted thousands of travelers and exposed long-standing weaknesses in critical aviation infrastructure. With control of Congress shifting and FAA spending drawing close attention, the reliability of air travel is turning into both a public safety concern and a political fight over how, and how quickly, the system gets fixed.
