## South Korea Moves to Review Treatment of Young K-Pop Performers

South Korea's culture ministry intends to look closely at how the K-pop industry treats performers who are minors. Officials also plan to revisit the standard artist contract used across the sector.

### What prompted the review

The move follows scrutiny of contracts tied to NewJeans. Those agreements reportedly left out protections found in the template contract for minors, including rights related to education and sleep. The ministry's plan to reconsider the standard contract suggests the template itself will be part of the discussion.

### Why the timing matters

K-pop's international success relies more and more on teenage trainees and performers. At the same time, public concern is growing over exploitation, mental health and restrictive contract terms. The NewJeans dispute has pushed industry labor practices into the spotlight, making them a significant issue for both policymakers and fans.
