## Five EU states set to unveil Rwanda return hub plan

Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands are expected to announce a pilot deportation centre in Rwanda. It would be intended for asylum seekers who have used up all their legal options to remain in the European Union.

### What the plan involves

The proposed "return hub" would be run as a pilot. The participating countries describe the arrangement as legally compliant. Human rights groups disagree and caution that it could endanger the rights of migrants. They also argue it could lend legitimacy to Rwanda's human rights record.

### Why it matters now

The announcement follows recent EU reforms that permit return hubs outside the bloc. It reflects a growing European push to reduce irregular migration, with implementation targets set around 2027. It also brings back the controversial Rwanda model after the UK scrapped its own scheme, returning migration policy, outsourcing and human rights to the centre of debate.
