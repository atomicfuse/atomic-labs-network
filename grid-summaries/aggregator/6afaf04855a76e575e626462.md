## Why School Anxiety Is Keeping Thousands of Children Out of Class

Severe anxiety about attending school is stopping thousands of children in England from going in at all. An article looks at the causes and at how long-term absence can lead young people to become Neet, meaning not in education, employment or training.

### What lies behind the absences

The article links severe school anxiety to several factors, including autism and ADHD, disruption that followed the Covid pandemic, and classroom environments that do not suit every child. Together, these are described as keeping many pupils away from lessons for extended periods, and persistent absence is tied to a growing risk of later becoming Neet.

### Why the timing matters

A government-commissioned review into the situation of nearly one million young people who are Neet is due. Meanwhile, absence rates remain well above their levels before the pandemic. The issue brings together education, mental health, neurodiversity and the future workforce, which is why the review and the absence figures are being watched together.
