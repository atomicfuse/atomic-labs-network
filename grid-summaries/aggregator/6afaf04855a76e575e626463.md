## Newsom Urges Tighter Rules on Undisclosed Political Spending

California Gov. Gavin Newsom is pressing for stronger limits and greater transparency around so-called dark money in American politics. In an interview, he described hidden political spending as a danger to both accountability and public trust in elections.

### What Newsom Is Calling For

Newsom wants tighter restrictions on money that flows into politics without disclosure of its source. He argues that when voters cannot see who is funding political efforts, it becomes harder to hold those players responsible, and confidence in the electoral system suffers as a result.

### Why the Issue Matters Now

Campaign spending and outside influence are intensifying ahead of major election cycles, keeping dark money at the center of ongoing arguments. The topic remains a flashpoint in broader debates over democracy, corruption, and political reform, and Newsom's comments add a prominent voice to that discussion.
