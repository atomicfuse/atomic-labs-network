## Sportsbooks pour at least $136 million into state races

An investigation finds that DraftKings, FanDuel, and other online sportsbooks spent at least $136 million trying to influence state-level politics during the 2026 midterms. The article argues the industry relied on opaque PAC networks to support candidates who favored its aims.

### What the money was chasing

According to the investigation, the industry backed candidates aligned with its goals on legalization, taxes, prop bets, online casinos, and competition. The article contends that the PAC networks made it hard to see who was behind the spending and what they wanted.

### Why state races matter

Online gambling companies are fast becoming major political players in state contests, which draw far less scrutiny than federal campaigns. As states debate expanding and regulating gambling, the spending shows how industry money can shape policy before voters even know what is at stake.
