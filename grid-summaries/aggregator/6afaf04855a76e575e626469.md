## Advocates Urge EPA to Slash Federal Nitrate Limit in Drinking Water

Environmental and public health groups have petitioned the EPA to tighten the federal nitrate limit in drinking water, arguing that the current standard no longer reflects what science now shows about health risks.

### What the Petition Seeks

The groups want the limit lowered from 10 mg/L to 1 mg/L. They point to newer research that links nitrate exposure to cancer, thyroid disease, and fetal abnormalities. The article highlights Illinois as a major nitrate pollution hotspot, with contamination tied to agricultural runoff.

### Why the Push Is Happening Now

The existing federal standard is decades old and was based mainly on blue baby syndrome. Advocates say newer findings point to broader dangers, and that millions of people could be exposed. Earlier EPA efforts to reassess the standard stalled, and the petitioners are pressing regulators to act as industrial agriculture's effect on water quality faces renewed scrutiny.
