## Two Mass Shootings Leave 17 Dead Across South Africa

Seventeen people died in two separate mass shootings in South Africa, adding to growing unease over violent crime and illegal firearms in the country.

### What Happened

In the first attack, eight men were killed inside a minibus near Pietermaritzburg. In the second, nine people died inside a shack west of Pretoria. Police have said the two incidents are unrelated to each other. The motives behind both shootings remain unknown, and no further details have been provided on the circumstances.

### Why It Matters Now

The killings have renewed concern about mass shootings, gang violence and organised crime in South Africa, even though murder statistics show a slight year-on-year decline. They follow other recent deadly attacks and come as the government moves to deploy the military in provinces with high murder rates. The shootings underline the wider national crisis of violent crime.
