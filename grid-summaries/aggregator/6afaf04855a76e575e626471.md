## Ukraine Stages Its Largest Overnight Drone Assault on Russia Yet

Ukraine has carried out its biggest overnight drone attack on Russia since the full-scale invasion began. Moscow says it brought down 1,336 drones across 16 regions.

### Part of a Wider Strategy

The piece argues the strikes reflect Kyiv's growing approach of targeting Russian infrastructure and disrupting supply routes. It also presents the attack as a way for Ukraine to show it remains capable despite mounting pressure.

### Why the Timing Matters

The escalation arrives as Ukraine faces a potentially brutal winter, with Russian attacks on its energy grid intensifying. It also comes while U.S.-mediated talks remain stalled and pressure grows on Kyiv to demonstrate its relevance on the battlefield and its strategic resilience.
