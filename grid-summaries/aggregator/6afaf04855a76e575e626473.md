## Atlanta Brunch Spot Turns to $10 Meals to Win Back Cautious Diners

Teneshia Murray Butler, who owns T's Brunch Bar in Atlanta, is betting on low prices to keep customers walking through her door. Her $10 meals are aimed at guests watching their budgets.

### Responding to a Shift in Diner Behavior

Butler's move comes as restaurants face declining traffic and changing spending habits. By setting a clear, modest price on meals, her restaurant is trying to give cost-conscious customers a reason to return rather than cut back on dining out.

### A Wider Challenge for Restaurants

Economic uncertainty is putting pressure on consumers, and that is pushing restaurants to rethink how they price their menus and present value. The story reflects a broader hospitality problem: owners must keep prices affordable for guests while also dealing with rising operating costs.
