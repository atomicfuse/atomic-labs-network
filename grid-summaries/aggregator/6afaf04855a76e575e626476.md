## Peggy Flanagan Makes History as First Native Woman Elected to the U.S. Senate

Sen.-elect Peggy Flanagan has become the first Native American woman elected to the U.S. Senate. An interview with her covers the historic win, her progressive campaign, and the priorities she plans to bring to Washington.

### Campaign and Convictions

Flanagan talks about being underestimated along the way and explains her opposition to ICE actions in Minnesota. She also says she backs the new Senate Democratic leadership. Her campaign leaned into progressive themes, and the conversation shows how she frames her own path to office and the message that carried her to victory.

### Priorities and Why They Matter

Flanagan says she will focus on Medicaid, SNAP, health care, labor, and rural issues. Her win marks a major representation milestone as Democrats regain Senate control and debate what leadership and agenda should define the party next. It also reflects a broader demand for populist, anti-corporate, pro-accountability politics amid immigration crackdowns and economic pressure.
