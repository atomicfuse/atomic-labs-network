## Allies and aides press Trump for a firmer line on China

As Trump travels through Asia, U.S. allies and administration officials are urging him to take a tougher and better coordinated approach to China's military, economic and technological assertiveness.

### Pressure points on the trip

The Quad meeting in Manila is central to the visit, with tensions in the South China Sea, rare earth supply chains and the risks of artificial intelligence all on the agenda. China's pressure on the Philippines, its dominance in rare earth minerals and its rapid advances in AI and military capability are adding urgency for allies.

### What comes next

Trump is due to hold talks with Xi Jinping, and he may also meet Putin and Kim Jong Un. His meetings with Quad leaders and with Xi could shape the next phase of Indo-Pacific security and economic competition.
