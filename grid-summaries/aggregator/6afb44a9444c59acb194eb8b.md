## Slater Survives Miguel Challenge to Retain TNA Title at Destination X

Leon Slater kept the TNA World Championship at Destination X, beating Trey Miguel in the main event of the pay-per-view after Miguel overcame significant travel disruption to reach the match.

### Main Event Drama

Miguel's journey to the show became a storyline of its own, as travel problems reshaped the card and raised the stakes for his performance. He still made it to the ring and challenged Slater for the title. Slater came out on top, completing his first successful defense of the championship.

### Ultimate X and What Comes Next

The night also featured Ultimate X matches, with KC Navarro and Tasha Steelz both winning their bouts. Those victories make them future title contenders. Slater's win adds to his run as the youngest world champion in TNA history.
