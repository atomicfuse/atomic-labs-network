## Trump keeps Kim Jong Un in play for Shenzhen summit talks

President Trump has left open the possibility that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un could join, or become a factor in, high-stakes talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.

### The backdrop to the remark

Trump's comment comes against a background of stalled diplomacy between the United States and North Korea, while Pyongyang's nuclear capabilities continue to grow. The summit agenda is also broad, taking in arms control, Iran, and Ukraine, so any role for Kim would sit alongside several other major issues.

### Why it matters now

Nuclear tensions, the war in Ukraine, and rivalry among the United States, China, and Russia are all converging at a single summit. Even a small signal about renewed dialogue between Washington and Pyongyang could reshape regional security expectations for South Korea, China, Russia, and the United States.
