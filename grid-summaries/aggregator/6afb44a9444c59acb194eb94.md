## Carville Pushes Back as Lemon Presses Him on Racism Claim

Don Lemon questioned James Carville about an accusation from Florida Democrat Angie Nixon, who has said Carville is racist and ought to retire. The exchange followed Carville's criticism of her Senate campaign.

### Carville's Defense

Carville responded defensively, pointing to Black candidates he has backed in the past as evidence against the charge. His original remarks had described Nixon's Senate bid as uninspiring and a poor fit for Florida. Lemon pressed him on how those comments landed and on the accusation that followed from Nixon.

### Why It Matters Now

The clash reflects wider Democratic Party tensions over race, generational leadership, electability, and how establishment pundits judge progressive candidates. It arrives as Democrats keep debating why candidates lose in key states such as Florida, and who gets to decide what counts as good political strategy.
