## EU Mission Urges Jordan to Keep Electoral Reform Moving

An EU Election Follow-up Mission to Jordan has reviewed how far electoral reform has advanced since the 2024 parliamentary elections, encouraging continued momentum in the country's political modernisation.

### Recommendations under review

The mission's assessment centres on 18 EU recommendations. Priority areas include voter education, campaign finance, and the cybercrime and defamation laws. The list also covers access for people with disabilities, the rights of election observers, and wider participation by women and youth in political life.

### Why the timing matters

Jordan is now midway through the electoral cycle, with legislative elections due in 2028. That makes the present a key window for legal and administrative changes. The report also arrives amid ongoing regional scrutiny of democratic standards, political participation, and freedom of expression.
