## Klopp Says Infantino Is Worse Than Blatter, Urges New Fifa Leadership

Jurgen Klopp has sharply criticised Fifa president Gianni Infantino, arguing that football governance has deteriorated since the era of predecessor Sepp Blatter. He has called for fresh leadership at the top of the world game.

### Pressure on Infantino

The comments land as Infantino faces re-election pressure. Controversy surrounds the abandoned Fifa Forward Enterprise proposal, which has become a focus of criticism. Uefa has meanwhile thrown its backing behind Philippe Diallo as a challenger, giving the opposition to Infantino institutional weight.

### Why it matters now

Fifa's leadership race is intensifying ahead of the March election, with prominent European figures openly questioning whether Infantino is fit to govern. The backlash draws on long-running concerns about transparency, commercialisation and trust in global football institutions, and it suggests the contest may be more heavily contested than expected.
