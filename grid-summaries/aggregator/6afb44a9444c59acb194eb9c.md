## Dakich Says Kiffin Is Falling Short at LSU Next to Kelly's SEC Record

A new installment of Don't @ Me with Dan Dakich takes aim at Lane Kiffin's time at LSU. The piece argues that Kiffin has underperformed expectations, particularly when set beside the SEC record of his predecessor, Brian Kelly.

### The Kelly Comparison

Dakich builds his criticism around roster investment. He points out that Kelly never took four SEC losses as LSU's head coach, and that Kelly managed this without a $50 million roster. The argument suggests that Kiffin, working with far greater financial resources, should be producing better results than he has so far.

### Why the Scrutiny Is Growing

LSU football carries championship-level expectations every season. As big-money roster building has become common, coaching results face sharper examination. NIL spending is turning into a major yardstick, and fans and media increasingly ask whether expensive talent actually converts into wins.
