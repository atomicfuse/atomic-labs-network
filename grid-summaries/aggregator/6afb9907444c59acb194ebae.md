## Paxton Abandons Suit Aimed at Barring CAIR From Texas

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton has dropped his lawsuit that sought to bar the Council on American-Islamic Relations, known as CAIR, from operating in the state. The suit rested on branding the group a terrorist and criminal organization.

### Why the Suit Was Dropped

The withdrawal comes after major Democratic election victories in Texas. Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa has pledged to remove the designation that Paxton's effort relied on. Paxton's retreat follows those results, though the facts provided do not detail his stated reasons for ending the case.

### Wider Significance

The case touches on civil rights, Islamophobia and the politics surrounding the Gaza war, all set against a shifting balance of power in Texas after a sizable electoral backlash. Paxton's decision also signals a broader rollback of controversial culture-war lawsuits in the wake of the election, marking a notable change in the state's legal and political direction.
