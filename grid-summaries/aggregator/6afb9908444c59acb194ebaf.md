## Peltola Leads Sullivan as Alaska Awaits a New Governor

Alaska's political map is shifting. Democrat Mary Peltola is ahead of Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in the state's U.S. Senate race, while Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins has won the governorship in a historic result.

### A Senate race still taking shape

The Senate contest has not been fully settled. Peltola holds the lead, but mail ballots and ranked-choice tabulation remain factors that could affect the outcome. Sullivan, the sitting senator, is still in the race while the counting process continues across the state.

### A turning point for the state

Democrats could end up winning both a Senate seat and the governor's office in a state long associated with Republican strength. The timing adds weight to the moment, since ballot counting, ranked-choice voting and a December 7 gubernatorial transition are all happening together, leaving Alaska in a period of change.
