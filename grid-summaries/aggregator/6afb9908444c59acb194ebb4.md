## Montana attorney general declines to defend voter-approved spending limits

Montana Attorney General Austin Knudsen has said his office will not defend Initiative 194 in federal court. The measure, which limits corporate political spending, now faces a challenge from business groups and conservative organizations.

### Why the state is stepping back

Knudsen says he believes the initiative violates the First Amendment under the Citizens United ruling, so his office will not argue in its favor. Supporters of the initiative say they will step in and defend it themselves as the federal challenge moves forward.

### What is at stake

Initiative 194 passed with about two-thirds of the vote and was designed as a direct challenge to Citizens United. That puts Montana at the center of a major fight over corporate speech, dark money, and election law. The case could become a high-profile test of whether states can restrict political spending by corporations, unions, and nonprofits.
