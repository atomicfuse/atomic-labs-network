## United Airlines Resolves Tech Outage That Delayed Hundreds of Departures

United Airlines has fixed a technology problem that held up hundreds of departing flights. The disruption arrived only days after a nationwide ground stop caused by an FAA malfunction.

### What Went Wrong

The outage was reportedly tied to a system used to calculate aircraft weight, a figure needed for safe takeoff. United said flights already in the air were not affected, and neither were United Express regional flights. The airline also said there was no sign of a cybersecurity incident, and the issue has now been resolved.

### Why It Matters

The episode adds to a string of high-profile technology failures hitting aviation, banking, and Microsoft services. Taken together, they highlight how heavily critical infrastructure depends on digital systems that are aging or fragile. The close timing with the FAA's ground stop makes the United disruption part of a larger pattern of concern.
