## Young Prisoners in Seoul Prepare for South Korea's Toughest Exam

At Seoul's Mandela Boys' School, young inmates are studying for the Suneung, the country's notoriously difficult university entrance exam, while serving time behind bars. The article offers a rare glimpse of exam culture in an unusual setting.

### Education as Rehabilitation

The report argues that schooling inside prison serves more than academic goals. Learning is presented as a route to rehabilitation and discipline, and as a way for these young people to imagine a different life once they are released. Preparing for such a demanding test is framed as part of that wider effort to change direction.

### Why the Timing Matters

The Suneung takes place on 19 November, with more than 551,000 applicants across South Korea. Against that backdrop, the story shows an extreme and less visible side of the pressure surrounding the exam. It also arrives amid ongoing debate about youth crime, rehabilitation and whether education can meaningfully reduce reoffending.
