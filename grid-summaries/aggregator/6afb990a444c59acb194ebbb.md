## Shenzhen's AI Showcase Hides a Strained Economy

Shenzhen is being presented to visiting world leaders as China's "city of dreams" for AI and robotics. An article on the event looks past that polished image to the pressures facing ordinary people and businesses beyond the tech displays.

### A Future That Is Real

The article accepts that China's high-tech future is not an illusion. Xi Jinping is pushing AI and robotics as engines of national growth, and Shenzhen serves as the proof of that innovation power it wants the world's leaders to see.

### The Costs Out of View

Alongside that ambition, the piece describes job insecurity, property losses and gig-work pressures affecting workers, small businesses and factories. Its central point is the tension in the timing: technological ambition is rising while confidence, employment and consumer spending remain under pressure.
