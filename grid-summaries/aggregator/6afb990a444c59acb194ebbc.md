## Major US banks hit by outages affecting online banking and payments

Thousands of customers of several large US banks, among them Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and US Bank, reported problems accessing their money. Issues eased by Monday evening.

### What customers experienced

Users reported disruptions across online banking, account balances, transfers, ATMs and payments. Chase said the trouble stemmed from a temporary software bug. The bank stated that the problem was not systemic and was not related to a cyber incident. By Monday evening, the problems appeared to be easing.

### Why it matters now

The outages came during a wider run of technology disruptions affecting US infrastructure, including a United Airlines system outage and a recent FAA ground stop. The episode highlights how heavily consumers and businesses rely on digital banking systems, and how quickly confidence can wobble when access to money is interrupted.
