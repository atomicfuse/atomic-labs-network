## Globe's Woodward comparison to Dianna Russini draws criticism

A Boston Globe column likened NFL reporter Dianna Russini's sourcing controversy to Bob Woodward's reflections on getting too close to a source. A new article argues that the parallel does not hold up.

### Why the comparison falls short

The critique centers on one key difference. Woodward acknowledged his own fault when looking back on his closeness to a source. Russini, by contrast, has not publicly taken accountability for the situation involving Vrabel. According to the piece, that gap undermines the Globe column's attempt to place the two journalists in the same frame, and the comparison ends up backfiring.

### Why it is back in the conversation

Russini has returned to public view with a new podcast after a seven-month hiatus. Her reappearance has kept the ethics scandal alive in sports media discussion. The article connects her case to a wider debate about where journalists should draw lines with their sources, how accountability should work, and how comeback stories are told.
