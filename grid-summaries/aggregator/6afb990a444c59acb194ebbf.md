## Jim Gaffigan Joins Push to End Sales Tax on New York Comedy Tickets

Comedian Jim Gaffigan is lending his support to New York lawmakers and comedy club owners who want the state to eliminate its sales tax on comedy club tickets.

### The Argument for Repeal

Backers of the effort say the added cost of the tax could discourage people from buying tickets. In their view, that could hurt attendance at clubs and make live comedy harder for people to afford and enjoy.

### Why the Timing Matters

Live entertainment venues are still competing for audience spending as costs rise, and small comedy clubs depend heavily on affordable ticket sales. The decision now rests with Gov. Kathy Hochul, which makes the question a timely one for both state policy and the live comedy scene.
