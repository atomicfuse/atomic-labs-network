## Missouri Touts Record Tourism Boost From World Cup Campaign

Missouri officials say the state's "Come Play" FIFA World Cup campaign helped produce record tourism and economic activity, with Kansas City at the center of the results.

### Kansas City's Strong Showing

According to officials, the Kansas City area drew 680,000 visitors, and hotels saw major revenue gains. The article points to the city's solid performance across its matches, along with strong attendance at the fan festival and a wide international reach. Together, these figures are presented as evidence that the campaign delivered for the region.

### Why the Numbers Matter

The results cast Missouri and Kansas City as winners from hosting World Cup events, with measurable gains in tourism, hospitality, and brand lift. They also show how a major sporting event can be turned into a longer-term economic development story for the places that host it.
