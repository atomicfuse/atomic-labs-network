## Dress Festive, Stay Office-Appropriate: Six Holiday Party Looks

A new style guide rounds up six outfit ideas for the office holiday party, aiming to strike a balance between seasonal flair and workplace good sense.

### The Small-Statement Approach

The article's central argument is that a look does not need to be extravagant to feel celebratory. Instead, it points to modest statement pieces, such as sequins, interesting textures, scarves, or bold color, as ways to add a festive touch. Each of the six outfits relies on this idea, keeping the overall effect polished rather than over the top.

### Why the Timing Fits

Office holiday parties are returning to calendars, and many people are working out how to dress for events that sit between work and socializing. The goal is to avoid looking too corporate or too ready for a night out. The guide also lands during peak season for party dressing, holiday shopping, and end-of-year style coverage.
