## Brad Pitt Leads Red Carpet for Netflix's Cliff Booth Sequel Premiere

Los Angeles hosted the world premiere of Netflix's *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth*, with Brad Pitt returning as the character he played in *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*. Photos from the red carpet are the focus of the coverage.

### The Film and Its Creators

The sequel is directed by David Fincher and written by Quentin Tarantino. Set in 1977, it follows a Hollywood crime story built around Pitt's Cliff Booth. The project extends one of Tarantino's most popular recent characters and pairs his writing with Fincher's direction.

### Who Turned Up

Pitt was joined on the carpet by Elizabeth Debicki, Courteney Cox, Tarantino, Fincher, and Inés de Ramón, along with other guests. The event gathered major Hollywood names around a high-profile release.
