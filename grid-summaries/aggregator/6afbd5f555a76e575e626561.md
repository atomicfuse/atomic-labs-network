## Spider-Man Swings Back to Theaters With an Extended Cut

Sony is rereleasing *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* in cinemas under a new title, *Spider-Man: Brand New Brand New Day*. The extended version promises fresh scenes and "a couple more surprises."

### What's changing

The new cut adds footage that wasn't in the original theatrical run, along with a few unspecified extras. Coverage of the rerelease points to the film's enormous box office performance and to likely speculation about whether the added material might tease *Avengers: Doomsday*.

### Why the timing matters

The return to theaters arrives just before the movie's home-video debut, handing Marvel and Sony one more big-screen push for what is described as 2026's biggest film. With excitement for *Avengers: Doomsday* growing, even small new scenes could become major fuel for fans.
