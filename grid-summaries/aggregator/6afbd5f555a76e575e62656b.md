## Could The Good Daughter Get a Second Season on Peacock?

Peacock's The Good Daughter is now streaming, and viewers are already asking whether more episodes are coming. The show was designed as a limited series, which makes its future an open question.

### Built to Stand Alone

The series adapts Karin Slaughter's standalone novel, and the current season was created as a complete story that doesn't depend on a follow-up. That limited-series design is the main reason a second season isn't a given. Producers, however, haven't closed the door on returning, saying that more episodes are not ruled out.

### Why a Return Could Happen

One possible route is a prequel focused on Charlie, which producers have pointed to when discussing the show's potential. Limited series are also increasingly being extended when audiences connect with the characters, as happened with Big Little Lies. With the show newly available, viewers are looking for any clues about what comes next.
