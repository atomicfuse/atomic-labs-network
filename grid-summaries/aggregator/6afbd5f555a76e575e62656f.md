## Loafers Step Ahead of Boots as Fall's Go-To Shoe for Jeans

One fashion writer says she is already tired of boots and is reaching for loafers instead. A new roundup presents 13 elevated styles meant to be worn with denim this season.

### The Loafer Lineup

The article argues that loafers are now the polished, versatile choice for jeans, taking over from boots. Its 13 picks span several looks: timeless classics, preppy designs, animal prints, suede finishes, colorful pairs and hybrid styles that blend different shoe types. Prices begin at $10, so the list includes options for a range of budgets.

### Why the Shift Matters

Fall outfits are moving away from the default pairing of jeans and boots toward softer, more refined flats. Loafers are described as working with wide-leg, baggy, cropped and barrel jeans. The trend also fits with the "rich mom," quiet luxury and East Coast prep aesthetics, which continue to shape accessible fashion content.
