## Nicolas Cage and Chris Pine Attached to A24 Vegas Drama *American Epic*

Nicolas Cage and Chris Pine are set to headline *American Epic*, a new drama series from A24 written by B.J. Novak. Major buyers, including HBO and Amazon, are showing interest in the package.

### The story

The series follows a Vegas casino mogul and a Midwestern pastor who try to build something "eternal" in Las Vegas. Cage and Pine are attached to star, while Novak is the writer behind the project. A24 is the studio developing it, and the combination of creative names has already drawn attention from several of the biggest players in television and streaming.

### Why it matters

The project arrives in a hot market for prestige television packages built around A-list film talent, especially those coming from A24. Amazon's reported interest stands out, since Cage recently criticized the company as "AI-friendly" and suggested he might not work with the studio again. Whether that history affects where the series lands is not yet known.
