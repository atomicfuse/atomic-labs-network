## Flu Activity Climbs Early as Two More Children Die

Influenza is picking up across the United States sooner than is typical, with illness levels now sitting above the national baseline. Two additional deaths among children have been reported.

### What the numbers show

The CDC says the overall severity of the season remains low so far. Even so, several indicators are moving upward, including hospitalizations and the share of tests coming back positive for flu. Certain regions are also emerging as hot spots, with activity rising faster there than elsewhere.

### Why the timing matters

Flu season seems to be getting properly underway while COVID-19 and RSV stay relatively low. That makes this a pertinent moment for families, schools, and workplaces to pay attention. An early start does not mean the season will turn out severe, but it adds urgency to prevention, vaccination, and awareness of symptoms.
