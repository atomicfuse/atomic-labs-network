## SpaceX Catches Starship Second Stage After Orbital Flight

SpaceX has caught a Starship second stage out of orbit for the first time, using its launch-tower arms to snag the returning vehicle.

### The catch

The vehicle, known as Ship 42, was caught by the "Mechazilla" arms of the launch tower after a record 22.5-hour orbital flight. The feat moves SpaceX closer to its goal of a fully reusable orbital-class rocket system. That system is intended to deploy Starlink satellites, support NASA moon missions, and eventually carry travelers to Mars.

### Why it matters now

The result is a significant proof point for reusable spaceflight, arriving as NASA's Artemis moon program and commercial demand for satellites both gather pace. It also recasts Starship from an experimental spectacle into operational infrastructure, with geopolitical, scientific, and consumer-space implications.
