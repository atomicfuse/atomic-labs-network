## Roadmap maps the road to ultrathin flat optics

A new roadmap on optical meta-devices describes how metasurfaces and metalenses could take the place of bulky lenses and mirrors. These ultrathin components can also perform several functions at once.

### Advances covered

The article highlights progress in AI-assisted design, tunable flat optics, and sensing. It also covers uses in AR and VR and in quantum imaging. A recurring theme is the push toward manufacturing methods that can scale up, so these devices can be produced in volume rather than only in research settings.

### Why the timing matters

Flat optics is moving from laboratory research toward real-world systems as nanofabrication, AI design tools, and semiconductor-compatible production mature. That shift matters because smaller, lighter imaging and sensing hardware could reshape phones, wearables, autonomous systems, medical devices, and communications equipment.
