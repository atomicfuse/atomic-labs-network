## Daily Iron Supplements Cut Anemia Risk in Young People, Review Finds

A major Cochrane review concludes that giving children and adolescents iron every day lowers rates of iron deficiency anemia. It analysed 114 studies covering more than 50,000 young people.

### What the review found

Daily supplements reduced iron deficiency anemia, while having little to no effect on common illnesses such as cough, fever, diarrhea or respiratory infections. Side effects were mostly mild. The authors stress that supplementation should be targeted at children who actually need it, rather than given universally.

### Why it matters now

Anemia is still a heavy burden on child health worldwide, affecting about 40% of children under 5. Global health leaders are currently updating their strategies to meet the 2030 maternal and child health goals. The findings help address evidence gaps in World Health Organization guidance, which was last updated in 2016.
