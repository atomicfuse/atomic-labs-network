## Kangaroo care may cut deaths and infections in small babies

An updated Cochrane review has concluded that kangaroo care probably lowers the risk of death and serious infection among low-birth-weight and preterm infants. The approach combines skin-to-skin contact, breastfeeding support, and early discharge when appropriate.

### What the review found

The evidence is particularly strong in low-resource settings, where the benefits for fragile newborns appear clearest. Even so, open questions remain. Researchers have not settled on the ideal duration of kangaroo care, and how best to put it into practice is still unclear.

### Why it matters now

Neonatal care systems face pressure to improve outcomes using low-cost interventions that involve families, especially where hospital resources are limited. The review strengthens the evidence behind WHO guidance on the practice. It also points to gaps in knowledge, including how it applies in high-income neonatal intensive care units and whether caregivers can realistically provide it in everyday conditions.
