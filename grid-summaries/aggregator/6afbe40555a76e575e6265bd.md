## Capped Nanovials Give Scientists Tiny Sealed Labs for Cells

UCLA researchers have upgraded nanovials, which work as cell-scale test tubes, by adding hydrogel caps. The caps hold cells and their secretions inside for longer periods.

### What the upgrade makes possible

With the caps in place, the system supports colony growth, sorting, secretion measurement, and studies of how cells interact with one another, all at massive scale. The study shows it can be used with mammalian cells, bacteria, and yeast, and it relies on common laboratory equipment rather than specialised machines.

### Why it matters now

Biology is shifting toward high-throughput experiments suited to AI, which require huge, clean datasets drawn from thousands to millions of parallel cell tests. This technology could speed up drug discovery, antibody screening, biomanufacturing, antibiotic testing, and synthetic biology.
