## Holiday Hotel Suites Turn the Room Into the Trip

Hotels are rethinking what a seasonal stay can be. Rather than offering a simple place to sleep, a growing group of properties is dressing up guest suites as immersive holiday experiences that draw visitors in on their own merits.

### Themes From Storybook to Y2K

The suites on display lean hard into design. One in Charleston delivers pastel, storybook-style décor, while another in New York leans on Y2K nostalgia. Each transforms an ordinary guest room into a fully realized seasonal setting, making the suite itself the main attraction of a holiday getaway.

### Why Experience-Driven Travel Fits

Holiday travelers increasingly want stays that feel special enough to justify the journey. Guests are looking for photogenic, family-friendly, and highly themed spaces, and these suites tap into several trends at once: nostalgia, local design, immersive hospitality, and the appeal of the destination hotel. For many travelers, the room is now part of the reason to go.
