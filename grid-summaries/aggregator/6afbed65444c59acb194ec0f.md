## Optimizely flaw turned major news sites into malware carriers

A vulnerability in Optimizely's Experimentation platform let attackers slip malicious JavaScript into customer projects. As a result, visitors to well-known sites were exposed to malware.

### What the attackers could do

The flaw sat in the Experimentation signup flow. Through it, attackers planted code that sites including CNN, Fox News, the BBC, Zoom, Salesforce and GitLab then loaded. According to the account, the script could redirect cryptocurrency transfers, skim payment card details, gather cookies and screenshots, and deliver iOS exploit kits to visitors.

### Why it matters

The episode shows how a single compromised third-party web tool can quickly turn trusted, high-traffic sites into channels for spreading malware. Supply chain attacks are increasingly aimed at SaaS scripts and at marketing and analytics tools. Creators can use this case to help audiences understand the hidden risks behind everyday browsing.
