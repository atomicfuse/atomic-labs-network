## Sooner State Party restarts push for Oklahoma ballot status

The Sooner State Party has launched a fresh petition drive aiming to win official recognition on Oklahoma ballots in time for the 2028 elections. Organizers are starting early as they work to qualify as a recognized party in the state.

### What the party is doing

According to the party, it needs roughly 33,483 signatures to meet the requirement for recognition. To collect them, it is organizing a statewide volunteer effort, relying on supporters across Oklahoma to gather signatures and help the campaign reach that threshold.

### Why it matters now

The drive marks another attempt to widen voter choices beyond the two-party system, and the early start shows how soon 2028 ballot access efforts are getting underway. The campaign taps into broader voter frustration with partisan politics and interest in independent or alternative political movements.
