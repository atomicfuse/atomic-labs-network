## Trump's Late Arrival in Manila Deepens ASEAN Doubts About U.S. Reliability

President Trump will miss most of the ASEAN summit in Manila, arriving very late, and an article argues the timing reinforces existing doubts in Southeast Asia about whether Washington can be counted on.

### What the Article Argues

The piece contends that the late arrival adds to a perception of American unreliability among ASEAN members. It describes the bloc as increasingly forced to hedge, weighing Washington's role in regional security against the economic pull exerted by Beijing. In this framing, each missed engagement matters because it shapes how member states judge long-term U.S. commitment.

### Why It Matters Now

U.S.-China competition is intensifying in the South China Sea, while Trump's tariffs and inconsistent diplomacy are straining regional trust. ASEAN leaders are seeking stability, and the article suggests Washington's mixed signals could push them toward a balancing strategy that keeps distance from both major powers rather than leaning firmly on the United States.
