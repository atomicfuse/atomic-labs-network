## Alito Cautions That Court Changes Could Erode Judicial Independence

Justice Samuel Alito has publicly pushed back against efforts to reshape the Supreme Court, arguing that some of the most discussed reform ideas carry risks for the judiciary's autonomy.

### What Alito Said

Alito warned that proposals to enlarge the Supreme Court or set term limits for its members could weaken judicial independence. He described these ideas as part of a wider campaign to put pressure on the court in response to rulings that have proved unpopular with critics. His comments present the reform push less as a neutral effort at institutional improvement and more as a reaction to specific outcomes.

### Why the Timing Matters

The remarks arrive as Democrats prepare to take control of Congress, while public trust in the Supreme Court remains low. Together, those conditions make court reform a live political fight as the next legislative term approaches. How lawmakers respond to Alito's warning, and to the broader debate, may shape the conversation in the months ahead.
