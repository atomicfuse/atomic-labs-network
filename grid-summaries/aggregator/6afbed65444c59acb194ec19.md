## BBC Verify tests claim that Alaska voters mixed up two Sullivans

BBC Verify has looked at claims that Alaska voters confused Republican incumbent Dan S. Sullivan with another candidate, Daniel J. Sullivan Jr., in a Senate race where Democrat Mary Peltola leads. Donald Trump has amplified the suggestion that the contest was "rigged" through a spoiler.

### What the voting data shows

The numbers do not support the confusion theory as an explanation for the result. Even if every vote for both Sullivans were added together, the total would still not erase Peltola's lead. Many voters who backed Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. also did not rank the Republican incumbent second on their ballots, which weakens the idea that they meant to vote for him.

### Why it matters now

Counting is still under way after the midterms, so the claims are circulating while the outcome is unsettled. The dispute feeds into wider arguments over ranked-choice voting, trust in elections and campaign tactics.
