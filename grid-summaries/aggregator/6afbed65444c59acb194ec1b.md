## Khanna Floats $1,700 Refund Checks Over Iran War Costs

Rep. Ro Khanna has proposed sending $1,700 refund checks to American families to help cover higher gas, food, and electricity bills linked to the Iran war.

### The Proposal

Khanna's idea is presented as part of a Democratic push on affordability following the party's midterm wins. The checks would act as a direct refund to households squeezed by rising everyday expenses. The coverage also points out that similar cash-payment promises from Trump have stalled, leaving that approach without clear progress so far.

### Why It Matters Now

Frustration over the cost of living helped shape the midterms, and energy price spikes tied to the war are becoming a significant political liability. Direct payments are returning to the debate as both parties look for simple, voter-friendly responses to inflation and household strain. Khanna's plan puts a specific figure on that conversation.
