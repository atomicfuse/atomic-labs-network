## US Basketball Player Arrested at Lagos Airport Over Alleged Cannabis Bags

A U.S. basketball player, Greene Da'monte Khiree, has been arrested at Lagos' Murtala Muhammed International Airport. Nigerian officials say they linked him to two checked bags that allegedly held 75 pounds of cannabis.

### What Officials Say

Authorities say Khiree claimed his trip was for basketball networking. They allege that phone forensics, luggage tags and tracking devices instead pointed to drug trafficking. The headline's claim that he abandoned the bags is part of the reported account of how officials connected him to the luggage.

### Why It Is in the News

The case arrives amid heightened global airport drug enforcement and growing attention to international cannabis trafficking routes involving Thailand, whose cannabis laws and exports have drawn scrutiny. It also fits a wider news cycle about athletes, travel risk and cross-border criminal investigations.
