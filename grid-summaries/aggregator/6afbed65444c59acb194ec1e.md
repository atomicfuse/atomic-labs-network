## United Airlines Tech Outage Slows Hundreds of US Flights

A computer outage at United Airlines delayed hundreds of flights across the United States, interrupting several systems that airlines rely on to get planes off the ground.

### What went wrong

The problem disrupted flight planning, communication with crews, weather data and the calculations used to work out aircraft weight. United said the issue was fixed after roughly an hour and that it affected departures only. The airline also said it had found no evidence of a cyberattack.

### Why the timing matters

The outage arrived while U.S. aviation was still recovering from a major FAA ground stop and a weekend of widespread delays. That has made the reliability of airline technology a fresh concern for the public. It also raises broader questions about aging aviation infrastructure and how heavily flight safety now depends on digital operations.
