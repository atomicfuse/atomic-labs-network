## Critics Question CFP Committee Ahead of New Rankings

A fresh set of College Football Playoff rankings is about to be released, and one argument making the rounds says the selection committee has a credibility problem. The piece contends the group has leaned toward the SEC in recent weeks.

### The Indiana and Alabama Contrast

The article's central complaint is that the committee dropped Indiana during a bye week, a move it says looks hard to square with how Alabama and other SEC teams have been treated. In its view, similar or even bigger résumé flaws for those programs were brushed aside, which it presents as evidence of favoritism toward the conference.

### Explanations Under Scrutiny

The committee's stated reasoning is now being questioned, particularly its comments on strength of schedule, what counts as a quality win, and the effect of injuries. The article suggests brand-name bias may also be shaping decisions, and it argues that these explanations have done little to reassure skeptics as the new rankings approach.
