## Head-On Train Crash Near Prague Station Leaves 46 Hurt

Two passenger trains hit each other head-on on a bridge close to Prague's main rail station during the evening rush. Dozens of people were hurt, and an investigation into the cause is now underway.

### What happened

The trains collided at low speed, injuring 46 people in total. Both drivers were among the casualties and suffered serious injuries. Officials say early indications point to one train possibly running a red light, though the inquiry has not yet reached a conclusion.

### Why it matters now

The collision disrupted travel through a major European transport hub, and it prompts urgent questions about rail safety, signalling systems and how emergency services cope during rush hour. No one died despite the number of injured. That outcome highlights how much train speed and infrastructure conditions can influence the severity of such accidents.
