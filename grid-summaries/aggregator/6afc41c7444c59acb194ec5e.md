## Kornacki examines how Josh Turek flipped an Iowa Senate seat

Steve Kornacki has taken a close look at Democrat Josh Turek's victory in a U.S. Senate race in Iowa. The state backed Donald Trump in all three of his presidential runs, which makes the result stand out.

### A test case for Iowa's direction

Kornacki presents Turek's win as a way to ask a larger question about the state. Is the result a temporary upset, or does it mark the start of another political swing? The analysis weighs those two possibilities without declaring which one is correct.

### Why the result matters now

Iowa has come to symbolize Republican strength in the Trump era, so a Democratic Senate pickup there complicates assumptions about the 2028 map. Both parties will be watching to see whether the outcome points to broader voter movement in red states, and that attention gives the result significance beyond Iowa itself.
