## Iran Ends Two-Month Pause With Drone Strike on U.S. Radar Site in Bahrain

Iran has launched drones at a U.S. radar facility in Bahrain. It is the third recent Iranian strike on American targets in the country, and it comes after a two-month lull.

### A widening confrontation

The attack is presented as one piece of a broader escalation. The United States has deployed carriers to the region, and sanctions pressure on Iran continues. Negotiations between the two sides have stalled, and key shipping routes are under threat. Together, these developments have raised tensions well beyond a single incident.

### What is at stake

The U.S. has expanded its military presence in the region, while Iran faces growing economic strain from sanctions and blockade pressure. With diplomacy stalled, both sides appear to be testing whether escalation can shift the balance in their favour without tipping into full-scale war. The Bahrain strike shows how close that line may be.
