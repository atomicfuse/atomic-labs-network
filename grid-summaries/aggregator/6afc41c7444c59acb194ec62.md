## Anime Edits Are the Latest Chapter in Political Propaganda

An article argues that TikTok and Instagram edits pairing anime girls with political symbols are a modern form of propaganda, one that fits into a long lineage of persuasion through popular culture.

### A Familiar Tradition in a New Look

The piece places these edits alongside Civil War songs, World War posters, and icons like Rosie the Riveter. In its framing, they are culture-specific tools. Recognizable characters and aesthetics help make political messages feel relatable to the people who see them.

### Why the Trend Matters Now

Political content is increasingly consumed through short-form, meme-driven social media, especially among Gen Z audiences. As anime becomes more mainstream in American culture, both creators and political groups are using its imagery to grab attention and shape how messages are interpreted.
