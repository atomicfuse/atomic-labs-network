## US confirms probe into Singapore firm Megaspeed over Nvidia chips

The US government has confirmed it is investigating Singapore-based Megaspeed. The company is accused of helping divert Nvidia's advanced AI chips to China, which would violate American export controls.

### What the investigation involves

According to the article, Megaspeed allegedly played a part in moving restricted semiconductors to China. The probe is linked to broader political scrutiny of Nvidia and to congressional testimony. It also sits within the wider US-China contest over who can access advanced chips.

### Why it matters now

AI chips have become a central battleground in the technology rivalry between Washington and Beijing. The US has tightened controls to protect national security and its technological advantage. The investigation arrives as pressure grows on both Nvidia and policymakers over whether those export rules are being enforced effectively.
