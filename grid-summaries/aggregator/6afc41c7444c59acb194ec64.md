## Texas voters shift state's top courts toward Democrats

Texas voters have sharply moved the state's highest courts toward Democrats, breaking a decades-long Republican push to shape judicial power. The change touches the Texas Supreme Court, the Court of Criminal Appeals and the 15th Court of Appeals.

### A slow turn, not an instant one

The article stresses that the shift does not guarantee immediate results. Legal outcomes may change gradually rather than overnight, even though the makeup of these courts has been transformed by the election.

### Why the stakes are high

Texas courts sit at the center of battles over abortion, LGBTQ rights, redistricting, death penalty appeals, corporate liability and the reach of state power. After a major Democratic sweep, judicial elections are likely to become a far larger battleground for money, activism and partisan strategy.
