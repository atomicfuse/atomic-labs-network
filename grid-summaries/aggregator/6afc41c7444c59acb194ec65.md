## Remembering FIFA's World XI exhibition matches

A look back at FIFA's old World XI games recalls a time when top stars gathered simply to play a showpiece friendly. The piece centres on a 1997 meeting between a Europe XI and a Rest of the World side.

### A 1997 showcase of stars

That match featured names such as Ronaldo, Batistuta and Zidane. Through the recollections of Soren Colding, the article argues these events captured a more communal and less commercial era of football, one that now seems hard to picture. The games are presented as a different kind of occasion from today's fixtures.

### Why the nostalgia lands now

Modern football is shaped by crowded schedules, worries over injury risk, strong clubs and billion-pound business interests. Against that backdrop, the idea of elite players assembling for a friendly spectacle feels increasingly unlikely. The reflection arrives as fans debate whether the sport has lost some of its fun and spontaneity.
