## Dutch Government Survives Tax Row After Four Days of Crisis Talks

Prime Minister Rob Jetten says his three-party minority government has stayed together following a tense dispute over a tax package. The disagreement had threatened to split the coalition.

### How the talks ended

Jetten says four days of crisis negotiations were enough to keep the coalition intact. To strengthen the government's position going forward, it will bring in former minister Sybrand Buma, who is tasked with helping to secure more stable support in parliament.

### Why the moment matters

The row comes less than nine months into Jetten's time in office. It underlines how fragile coalition politics can be in the Netherlands, where parliament is fragmented. It also puts a spotlight on the prime minister himself. As the country's youngest premier, Jetten has promised to build bridges across party lines, and the episode tests whether he can deliver on that pledge.
