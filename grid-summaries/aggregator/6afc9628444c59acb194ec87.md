## Jinkins Re-elected House Speaker as Democrats Gain Supermajority

Washington House Democrats have re-elected Rep. Laurie Jinkins as speaker, following a major midterm win that gave the party a 69-seat supermajority. Her leadership was challenged by Rep. April Berg.

### The Leadership Contest

The vote centered on Jinkins' challenge from Berg and on how the caucus wanted to proceed after its election success. The result signals whether Democrats preferred continuity under established leadership or a more disruptive direction in the wake of the midterms.

### What the Majority Means for 2027

With 69 seats, Democrats hold unusual power in the Legislature. They can advance constitutional amendments, override vetoes, and shape budget negotiations without needing any Republican votes. The 2027 session will be the first test of how the party uses that expanded control under Jinkins' leadership.
