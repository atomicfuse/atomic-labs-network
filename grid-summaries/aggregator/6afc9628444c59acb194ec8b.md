## Ingraham presses Vance on FAA outage

Fox News host Laura Ingraham pushed back on JD Vance over a recent FAA outage that led to a nationwide ground stop. Her challenge came as Vance appeared to shift responsibility for the failure.

### The exchange

Ingraham questioned attempts to deflect blame, pointing out that Republicans have held power for the past two years. Her remark, in effect, told Vance that his side has been in charge and cannot easily point elsewhere when air travel systems break down.

### Why it matters

Flight disruptions are among the most visible public failures, and accountability for infrastructure is becoming a sharper political flashpoint. The moment stands out because the criticism came from within conservative media rather than from Vance's usual opponents, which makes the pushback harder to dismiss as routine partisan attacks.
