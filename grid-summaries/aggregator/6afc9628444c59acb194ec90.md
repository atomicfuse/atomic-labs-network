## Buttigieg Rejects Trump's Blame for Nationwide Flight Shutdown

Former Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg has pushed back after Donald Trump pointed the finger at him for an FAA ground stop that disrupted 11,000 flights across the country.

### Buttigieg's Response

Buttigieg argues that aviation performance improved during his time in office. He says the Trump administration should now explain why its own modernization work set off the breakdown, rather than assigning responsibility to his tenure.

### Why the Dispute Matters

The shutdown affected 1.5 million passengers and landed amid growing concern about fragile infrastructure, cyber threats, and the competence of government. It also comes as funding for aviation modernization and political blame over federal systems have become major flashpoints for the public, giving the exchange added weight beyond the immediate disruption.
