## World Rugby bans Springboks coach Rassie Erasmus for six matches

South Africa head coach Rassie Erasmus has been given a six-match ban by World Rugby. The sanction follows comments he made after South Africa's defeat to France, which suggested possible bias or improper influence by match officials.

### The sanction and the background

The governing body handed down the suspension over those remarks. Erasmus has a history of controversy involving officiating, which adds context to the latest punishment. South Africa Rugby is now pushing for a full misconduct hearing rather than accepting the sanction as it stands.

### Why the timing matters

The ban arrives during a crucial international window. If it is upheld, Erasmus would miss major fixtures, including the match against Ireland and the start of the Rugby Championship. The case also tests World Rugby's new sanction process for abuse of match officials, at a time of growing scrutiny over player safety laws and the consistency of officiating.
