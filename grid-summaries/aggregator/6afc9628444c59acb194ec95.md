## Jones Says Cowboys Weighing Changes to Defensive Coordinator Role

Jerry Jones says the Cowboys are actively discussing changes to the role of defensive coordinator Chris Parker. The talks follow another poor showing from Dallas' defense, which has become a recurring concern for the team.

### A Defense Wasting a Strong Offense

The article argues that Dallas' high-scoring offense is being squandered by a defense that gives up explosive plays and makes questionable calls. Those problems have turned the unit into a weekly storyline, and Parker is the focus of the criticism as the discussions continue.

### Why the Pressure Is Rising Now

Pressure intensified after a 46-40 loss to the 49ers, a game in which Dak Prescott threw five touchdowns and Dallas still came up short. With Mike Pettine already on the coaching staff, a shakeup could happen quickly, though nothing has been announced about what any change would look like.
