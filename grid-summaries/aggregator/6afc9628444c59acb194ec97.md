## Adviser Pitches Melania Trump's Prime Video Series as a Piece of History

Adviser Marc Beckman has described "Melania: The Series" as part of American history. The two-part Prime Video project is being previewed as a behind-the-scenes portrait of the first lady.

### What the Series Covers

Beckman presents the production as free of politics. It is positioned as a look at Melania Trump's family life, the circle of people she trusts, and her philanthropic work. It also follows her return to the role of first lady.

### Why the Timing Matters

The series arrives as public attention on the Trump White House is renewed. It also lands in a polarized media environment, where political figures are increasingly turning to streaming platforms to shape their own narratives and present themselves on their own terms.
