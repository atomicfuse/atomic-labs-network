## Bruce Buffer Thinks Conor McGregor Isn't Finished Yet

UFC ring announcer Bruce Buffer has added fuel to talk of another McGregor return after the star's latest setback. McGregor tore his ACL just 69 seconds into his comeback bout against Max Holloway.

### Why Buffer Sees More to Come

Buffer believes McGregor still has a "fighter mentality," which he suggests could keep the former champion from walking away. He also points to McGregor's huge drawing power as a reason the UFC legend may not be done. Those two factors form the core of his argument that another comeback remains possible.

### A Comeback Story Full of Twists

The injury is the newest turn in a return already marked by long layoffs, physical setbacks and questions over whether the UFC has entered a post-McGregor era. McGregor has one fight left on his UFC contract, which keeps speculation about his future firmly in the combat-sports conversation.
