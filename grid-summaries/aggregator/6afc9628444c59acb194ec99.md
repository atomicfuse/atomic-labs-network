## NSA's Classified Internal Network Hit by Intermittent Outage

The National Security Agency is dealing with an intermittent outage on NSA Net, its classified internal communications network. Critical communications and backup systems are still working.

### Part of a Wider Run of Failures

Reporting places the disruption alongside a string of recent technology breakdowns across U.S. infrastructure. Those affected include the FAA, banks, airlines, and Microsoft. Taken together, the incidents have sharpened questions about how well essential systems hold up under strain.

### Timing and Cyber Concerns

The outage arrives as public worry grows over the resilience of critical infrastructure following several high-profile failures. It also coincides with warnings from federal agencies about faster, AI-assisted cyber threats. No cyberattack has been linked to the NSA disruption, and the cause has not been tied to any outside actor.
