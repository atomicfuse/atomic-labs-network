## Dr. Marc Siegel Turns to Faith and Healing in New Book

Fox News medical analyst Dr. Marc Siegel has released a book called *The Angels Among Us*. It gathers stories that touch on faith, healing, and divine intervention, and he discusses it in a recent piece.

### What the Book Explores

The book presents spirituality as a powerful force during illness, recovery, and hope. Through personal accounts, Siegel looks at how belief can shape the way people face difficult health moments and the road back from them. The piece frames these stories as examples of comfort and meaning arising when people are most vulnerable.

### Why It Resonates Now

Conversations about mental health, resilience, and holistic healing remain active, and many people are looking for meaning beyond traditional medicine. The book taps into that interest, along with a growing appetite for personal testimony and spiritual comfort. Its focus on faith and reassurance arrives at a time when many are navigating uncertainty.
