## Pakistan's Household Solar Surge Tests the Grid

Pakistan is experiencing a fast solar boom led by ordinary households, according to a new examination of the trend. The shift is playing out as COP31 pushes nations to move away from fossil fuels.

### What is driving the boom

Soaring electricity bills, cheaper Chinese panels and unreliable grid power have pushed households toward solar. The article argues the shift is cutting costs and emissions, and that the momentum looks hard to stop.

### The strain on the grid

The same surge is putting serious pressure on the national grid. The article warns it could trigger a "utility death spiral." Pakistan's choices now may show whether mass solar adoption strengthens a clean-energy future or leaves poorer consumers stuck with a failing, expensive grid.
