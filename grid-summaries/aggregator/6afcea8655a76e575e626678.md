## North Carolina's Gas Tax Set to Return as Pause Lapses

North Carolina's 41-cent-per-gallon gas tax will come back after lawmakers decided against extending a temporary suspension that ends Nov. 30.

### Why the pause is ending

Legislative leaders say the move reflects falling fuel costs. Gas prices have dropped about 50 cents since last month, which they argue lessens the need for consumer relief. With prices easing, lawmakers are turning their attention away from helping drivers and back toward budget concerns.

### The funding dispute

Democrats counter that the pause has already cost the state $300 million in transportation funding. The decision arrives just after the election and during a lame-duck session, which keeps fuel costs and infrastructure money in the political spotlight. The disagreement shows how the tradeoff between lower pump prices and road and transit funding remains a live issue at the statehouse.
