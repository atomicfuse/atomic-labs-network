## Meta Takes Washington Ad Disclosure Law to the Supreme Court

Meta has asked the U.S. Supreme Court to strike down a Washington state campaign finance law. The measure requires sellers of political advertising to share information about the ads they run.

### Meta's argument

The company contends that the disclosure rules chill or restrict important spaces for political speech. Its petition asks the justices to invalidate the requirements rather than leave them in place. The law applies to those who sell political ads, and Meta says its effects reach well beyond paperwork by limiting where and how political messages can appear.

### Why the timing matters

The case arrives as online political advertising, transparency and platform responsibility face heightened scrutiny ahead of major election cycles. A Supreme Court ruling could reshape how states regulate digital political ads. It could also affect how platforms handle campaign content, making the outcome relevant well beyond Washington.
