## Hillsborough voters choose to elect their school superintendent

Hillsborough County voters have approved a referendum that will make the school superintendent an elected office beginning in 2028. The change moves the decision away from the School Board and places it with the public.

### Arguments for and against

Supporters say an elected superintendent would be more accountable to residents and would have greater autonomy in running the district. Teachers and board members have raised concerns, however, pointing to the influence of campaign money and the risk that schools become more politicized.

### What comes next

The district is one of the nation's largest, and it is moving toward a partisan superintendent election, which prompts wider questions about how education leaders should be chosen. With 2028 approaching, educators, candidates, and voters are already preparing for a new political landscape in local schools.
