## Hochul Toasts the Post That Has Often Skewered Her

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul has paid a gracious and funny tribute to the New York Post for its 225th anniversary. She did so even though the paper has run harsh covers aimed squarely at her.

### A Tribute That Landed

The piece argues that Hochul's response worked because she recognized the Post's messy, loud place in New York culture. She did not take the bait. Rather than answering past attacks, she joked her way through the occasion and gave the paper credit for what it is, which made the gesture feel generous and good-humored.

### Why It Resonates Now

In a polarized media environment, politicians who can joke across hostile lines tend to stand out and often earn public goodwill. The story also touches on New Yorkers' complicated love-hate relationship with the Post, whose sensational headlines and tabloid identity inspire both affection and irritation across the city.
