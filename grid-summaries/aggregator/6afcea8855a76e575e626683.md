## Democrats eye 2028 but say they must first prove they can deliver

Democrats who gathered in Denver after their midterm wins are already positioning for the next presidential race, NBC News reports. Even so, potential contenders warned that the party has to show it can deliver results quickly if it hopes to win the White House.

### What the hopefuls say Democrats need

Several possible 2028 candidates argued that the party should focus on tangible economic wins that voters can feel. They also called for accountability on Trump policies and a pragmatic willingness to work across the aisle when it helps get things done.

### Why the timing matters

The party is entering a post-midterm window of power, but voter trust remains fragile, and anti-GOP sentiment may not turn into lasting Democratic support. With early 2028 maneuvering already underway, this is a key moment for Democrats to define their message and showcase their bench of potential leaders.
