## Opinion: Swinney must adapt for Clemson to contend again

An article argues that Dabo Swinney's promise of another national championship at Clemson will sound empty unless he changes how he operates. It frames the coach as standing at a crossroads.

### The case for change

The piece points to Clemson's slide, Swinney's resistance to the transfer portal, and a costly buyout situation as the forces that have brought him to this moment. It contends that embracing a tougher, more ruthless approach, a so-called villain era, is the route to restoring the program's standing, and that his confidence alone is not enough.

### Why the timing matters

College football is increasingly driven by NIL spending, heavy roster turnover, and aggressive recruiting through the portal. Against that backdrop, the article says the distance between Clemson's established way of doing things and the sport's new reality is getting harder to overlook, which adds weight to Swinney's bold title pledge.
