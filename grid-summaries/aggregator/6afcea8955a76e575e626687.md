## NSA Classified Network Reportedly Suffers Temporary Outage

The US National Security Agency is said to have experienced a temporary outage or degradation of an internal classified communications network, reportedly called NSANet. The agency says the problem was not a cyberattack.

### What the agency says happened

According to the report, the disruption occurred during routine maintenance work. The NSA states that it was not the result of a cyberattack and that mission-critical national security operations were not affected. The account describes the incident as either an outage or a reduced level of service, and it does not go beyond what the agency has said about the cause.

### Why the timing matters

The incident comes amid a wider run of major technology disruptions across the United States that have touched banks, flights, and infrastructure, which has raised public concern about digital resilience. Even with no evidence of an attack, a problem inside the country's largest intelligence agency shows how much national security relies on complex internal systems.
