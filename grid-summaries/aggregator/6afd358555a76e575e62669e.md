## Parents Face Sports-Gambling Addiction With No Guidance

A new piece explores the experiences of parents trying to help their children through sports-gambling addiction. It argues that families are dealing with a fast-growing crisis while having little guidance or support to lean on.

### How phones changed the problem

Smartphones and always-on betting apps have made the problem more intense, according to the piece. Gambling no longer requires a trip anywhere, and the apps are available at any hour. That constant access is a central reason parents say they feel unprepared, with one theme standing out: there is no handbook for this.

### Why it matters now

Legalized sports betting, mobile apps, and nonstop sports media have made gambling more accessible to young people than ever. As betting becomes a normal part of culture, parents are increasingly confronting harms they may not recognize until the damage is severe. The story puts a spotlight on families caught in that gap.
