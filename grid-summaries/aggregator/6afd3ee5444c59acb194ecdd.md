## A 19-Year-Old Student Unseats a Veteran Massachusetts Lawmaker

Daniel Darris-O'Connor, a 19-year-old university student, is headed to the Massachusetts State House after narrowly beating an eight-term state representative. He also serves on the Billerica Select Board, a role that shaped his bid.

### How He Won Over Doubters

Darris-O'Connor faced skepticism about his age from the start. Rather than make youth the centerpiece of his message, he ran an issue-driven campaign grounded in local government. His work on the Select Board gave him a record to point to, letting voters judge him on what he had done rather than how old he was.

### Why the Result Resonates

His victory points to voter interest in generational change and local accountability. It also suggests an appetite for candidates who speak directly about cost-of-living concerns such as housing, energy, healthcare, and taxes. The result arrives amid wider debate over young people in politics, and whether experience should be measured by age or by results.
