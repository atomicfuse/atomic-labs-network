## Jeffries Faces First Test on Road to Speakership

Hakeem Jeffries heads into a closed-door Democratic leadership vote that will gauge his path toward becoming the first Black speaker of the House. His bid is unopposed, which makes the moment part coronation and part pressure point.

### A Party Demanding Fresh Leadership

Democrats say they want new leaders after defeating Republicans, and Jeffries' run carries that expectation. Though no one is challenging him, the vote puts a spotlight on whether he can satisfy a party eager for change.

### Expectations Amid Wider Unrest

Democrats are preparing to take power with a large House majority. Activists and newly elected members are pressing for clarity on what the next generation of leadership will actually deliver. The vote also arrives during broader Democratic unrest, including a Senate leadership challenge to Chuck Schumer.
