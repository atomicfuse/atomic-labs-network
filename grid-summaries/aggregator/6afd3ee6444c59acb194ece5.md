## AI Uncovers Critical Java Flaw Nicknamed "Java4Shell"

A cybersecurity firm has used artificial intelligence to find a critical vulnerability in Java, the platform behind much of the internet. The flaw has been dubbed "Java4Shell."

### What was found

The vulnerability could potentially let attackers execute commands remotely by exploiting Java's memory system. The piece argues the stakes are high because Java underpins banks, hospitals, governments, aviation, telecoms, and Fortune 500 companies. A flaw that stayed hidden for so long in such a foundational technology could have massive systemic impact.

### Why it matters now

AI is quickly changing cybersecurity by locating deep, legacy bugs faster than human researchers can. Attackers may soon use similar tools to discover and exploit such weaknesses themselves. Recent outages, bank access problems, and earlier PHP vulnerabilities show how fragile much of the internet's critical infrastructure may be.
