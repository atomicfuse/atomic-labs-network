## Europe Bets on Light-Based Chips to Gain Ground in Semiconductors

Europe is putting significant money into photonic chips, which process data using light rather than electrons. The aim is to carve out a stronger role in a chip market that the US, Taiwan, and China currently dominate.

### What Europe Is Building

Photonic chips are designed to work faster while producing less heat and using less energy than conventional designs. The article argues that initiatives such as PIX Europe and a new pilot line in Eindhoven could help Europe gain influence as this technology develops into a larger market. These projects form the core of the continent's push to secure an early position.

### Why the Timing Matters

Demand is rising for faster, more energy-efficient chips, driven by AI, data centres, 6G, and advanced sensing. At the same time, governments are racing to secure their semiconductor supply chains. Europe views photonics as a rare opportunity to lead in an emerging field instead of trying to catch up in one that is already established.
