## AOC Laughs Off 2028 Question in First Fox News Return Since 2020

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez sat down with Fox News for the first time in five years, and the interview closed with a question about her political future.

### The final question

Anchor John Roberts ended the conversation by asking whether she plans to run for president in 2028. Ocasio-Cortez laughed and brushed the question aside. She said her focus is on delivering results, and that talk of elections can wait until after that work is done.

### Why the speculation matters

Chatter about the 2028 Democratic field is already picking up speed. The Democratic Socialists of America has urged her to run, and polls place her among the leading potential contenders. Fox News is also treating a possible candidacy as a major test case for how Republicans message against socialism.
