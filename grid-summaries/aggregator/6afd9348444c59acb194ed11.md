## Trump heads into China summit with trade promises still unmet

President Trump is set to meet Xi Jinping in China, where earlier trade pledges on soybeans, agriculture, tariffs and rare earth minerals remain stalled. The article argues that broad promises made between leaders are unlikely to produce results without detailed negotiations.

### Why broad pledges fall short

The central argument is that headline commitments from a summit do not translate into action on their own. Progress, the piece suggests, depends on item-by-item trade talks that settle specifics, rather than general assurances from the top.

### What is at stake now

Both the United States and China face economic pressure as the meeting approaches. Billions of dollars in agricultural purchases and the supply of strategic minerals hang in the balance. Trade tensions, inflation concerns and dependence on rare earths mean any concrete agreement would carry real political and economic weight.
