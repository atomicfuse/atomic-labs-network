## GTA 6 Arrives in the UK After a 13-Year Wait

Grand Theft Auto 6 is about to launch in the UK, following a 13-year gap and two delays. Early reactions suggest the game is living up to its huge expectations.

### Early Praise from Players and Critics

Players in New Zealand, where the game arrived first, have been sharing their first impressions. They single out its graphics, realism, scale, and level of detail. Critics have also responded warmly, handing the game near-perfect reviews and historic scores. Fans have been preparing for launch, and Twitch has been buzzing with hype.

### A Launch Built on Hype

The game is being positioned as one of the biggest entertainment launches ever. Millions are watching streams, and UK fans have booked time off to play. Because the release is staggered across the world, many people are now scrambling to avoid spoilers, which has amplified launch-day culture online.
