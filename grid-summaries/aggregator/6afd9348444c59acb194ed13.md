## Putin Gains Symbolic Win From Planned Summit With Xi and Trump

A planned three-way summit between Vladimir Putin, Xi Jinping and Donald Trump is being described as an early success for the Kremlin, according to analysis by Steve Rosenberg.

### A Return to the Centre Stage

The article argues that the meeting already restores Putin to the middle of global power politics, even as the war in Ukraine continues. It stresses that the optics may favour Moscow, whether or not the talks change anything on the battlefield. Simply being seated alongside the leaders of China and the United States carries symbolic weight for the Russian president.

### Why the Timing Matters

Nearly five years after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, Putin's return to a high-profile stage challenges the idea that Western isolation of Russia has worked. The moment is also sensitive because Ukraine and Europe fear that decisions about the war could be discussed without them in the room.
