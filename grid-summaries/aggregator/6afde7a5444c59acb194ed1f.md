## Zelenskyy says Trump and Xi hold the leverage to push Putin toward peace

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has argued that Donald Trump and Xi Jinping could press Vladimir Putin to end the war in Ukraine if they chose to. His remarks come ahead of a summit in China.

### The sanctions argument

Zelenskyy's central claim is that the United States and China could change the course of the war if they jointly imposed sanctions on Russia, or even threatened to. In his view, pressure from two major powers acting together could shift the war's trajectory quickly, rather than leaving it to drag on.

### Why the timing matters

The comments land as Ukraine's future is increasingly tied to relations among Washington, Beijing and Moscow. Sanctions, diplomacy and Trump's approach to Putin are all central to the debate over how the war might end, which gives the upcoming China summit added weight for Kyiv and its supporters.
