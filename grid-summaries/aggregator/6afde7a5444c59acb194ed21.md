## Wisconsin Democrats Eye School Funding and Lower Costs as They Prepare to Take Charge

Wisconsin's incoming Democratic leaders in the Assembly and Senate say their new trifecta will focus on fully funding public schools and easing the cost of everyday life. They are preparing to govern with full control of the state Capitol.

### Priorities for the new majority

The leaders say they want to fully fund public schools and cut the state's dependence on local referendums. They also plan to bring down costs for families, naming childcare, housing, groceries, and prescription drugs as areas of focus.

### A rare opening for change

Democrats will hold control of the Capitol for the first time in 16 years, which gives them an unusual chance to reshape school funding and affordability policy. The moment arrives as a lawsuit over school funding continues and as local referendums multiply, with districts asking voters for billions of dollars.
