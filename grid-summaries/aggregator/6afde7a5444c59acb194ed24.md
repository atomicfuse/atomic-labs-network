## Affordable housing rents helped fund San Diego County campaigns

An investigation finds that National City Park Apartments, an affordable housing complex controlled by San Diego labor leaders, has used tenant rent and laundry revenue to support political campaigns and ballot measures.

### How the money was used

The article argues the complex has become a major financing vehicle for labor-backed causes in the region. It also notes that some tenants have reported poor living conditions at the property, raising questions about how the money collected from residents is being spent.

### Why it matters now

Spending rose sharply in the latest election cycle, with more than $521,000 directed toward local measures and races, many of which appear to have succeeded. The story arrives amid growing scrutiny of campaign finance, the governance of affordable housing, and the influence of organized labor over local infrastructure decisions.
