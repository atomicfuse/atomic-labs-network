## Intelligence Chief Tells Congress Recent Outages Were Not Linked

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton has told Congress that a recent run of technology outages was the result of separate technical problems rather than a single coordinated effort. He said they were not a state-sponsored cyberattack.

### What Clayton Said

Clayton described the disruptions as "unrelated." The outages hit a wide range of organizations, including the FAA, the NSA, banks, airlines, and major technology companies. In his testimony, he characterized them as ordinary technical disruptions and did not attribute them to a hostile actor or a shared cause.

### Why It Matters Now

Several major systems failed within days of one another, which has fueled public concern. Some political figures have also voiced skepticism about whether the government is being fully open about cyber risk and the vulnerability of the country's infrastructure. The timing of the failures has kept those questions in focus.
