## Ukrainian Troops Battle Russians Inside Old Soviet Gas Pipeline

Near Kupyansk, Ukrainian forces are fighting Russian troops in an unusual setting: a disused Soviet-era gas pipeline. The underground route lets soldiers avoid skies saturated with drones.

### Fighting in cramped tunnels

According to the report, Ukrainian forces have pushed forward 15km through the pipeline. Conditions are described as brutal and cramped, with combat taking place in a confined space far from the open ground above. The article presents this kind of underground warfare as an emerging tactic in the war.

### A shift in how wars are fought

Drones are making movement on the surface increasingly deadly, and both sides are adapting with unconventional methods. The story shows how existing infrastructure, such as a pipeline, can become a front line. It points to a broader change in modern warfare, where avoiding detection from above shapes where and how troops fight.
