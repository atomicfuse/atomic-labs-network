## A Letter Accuses U.S. Leaders of a Double Standard on the ICC

A letter to the editor argues that American political leaders treat the International Criminal Court differently depending on who is under investigation. It presents the dispute as a question of whether international justice rests on principle or on politics.

### The Argument

The writer says U.S. leaders have welcomed the court's investigation of Russia but condemned it when its attention turns to Israel. In the letter's view, this selective support undermines the idea that the court should apply the same standards to everyone. The controversy, it suggests, shows how easily international justice can become a political tool.

### Why It Matters Now

The letter lands amid fresh debate after Trump's 2025 sanctions on the ICC, which followed arrest warrants for Israeli leaders over alleged crimes in Gaza. The episode feeds wider arguments about national sovereignty, accountability for war crimes, and whether U.S. foreign policy is applied consistently.
