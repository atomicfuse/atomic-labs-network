## Sen. Ron Johnson Says Social Security Should Be Shut Down

Republican Sen. Ron Johnson has described Social Security as a "Ponzi scheme" and said the program should be ended instead of fixed. His remarks land as Congress weighs how to handle the program's long-term finances.

### Johnson's Position

Johnson argues that Social Security is beyond repair and that the better course is to shut it down. His stance runs against the wider direction of congressional efforts, which have focused on addressing the projected funding shortfall rather than closing the program. The contrast makes his comments stand out in an ongoing policy conversation.

### Why the Debate Is Heating Up

Social Security is heading toward a major funding crunch by 2032, which has turned reform into a live political fight. Johnson's language sharpens that debate at a moment when lawmakers are floating competing plans. One of them is a bipartisan proposal that would tax higher earners more. With the deadline approaching, the differences between those approaches are likely to draw increasing attention.
