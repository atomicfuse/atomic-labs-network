## Megyn Kelly Slams DHS Secretary Mullin Over Comments on Tech Outages

Megyn Kelly has sharply criticized Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin after he suggested that "SI," or AI, might be behind a run of technology outages. The disruptions hit Microsoft, the FAA, JPMorgan Chase, Zoom, and other organizations.

### Kelly's Criticism

Kelly argued that Mullin came across as unserious and poorly informed. In her view, the moment called for a DHS leader who could inspire public confidence, and his remarks fell short of that. She said he "seems like an idiot."

### Why It Matters Now

The outages in major digital infrastructure are adding to public worry about cyberattacks, the risks of AI, and how ready the government is to respond. The exchange comes at a time when trust in institutions, tech platforms, and official crisis communication is already fragile, which raises the stakes for how officials speak about such events.
