## Business Travelers Reveal Their Biggest In-Flight Annoyances

A new survey from Navan looks at what irritates business travelers most while flying. It examines the biggest pet peeves people report and considers how everyday airplane annoyances shape the overall experience of being in the air.

### What the Survey Looks At

The Navan survey focuses on business travelers and the in-flight behaviors that bother them most. Rather than treating these irritations as minor, it explores how common annoyances can affect how travelers feel about a flight as a whole. The findings put a spotlight on the small moments that can turn a routine trip into a frustrating one.

### Why the Topic Resonates Now

Business travel continues to rebound, which has made traveler comfort and etiquette bigger points of conversation. Airplane behavior is also a highly relatable subject, one that tends to spark strong opinions online. With more professionals returning to the skies, the survey arrives at a moment when shared space and courtesy on planes are drawing fresh attention.
