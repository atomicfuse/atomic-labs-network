## Explora Journeys Puts Winter 2028-2029 Voyages on Sale

Explora Journeys has begun selling its Winter 2028-2029 sailings, giving travelers and the agents who serve them an early look at a wide range of upcoming voyages.

### What the Offer Includes

The collection spans 230 destinations, giving buyers plenty of itinerary choice. Savings of up to 40% are available on bookings made by December 2026. The company is directing much of its attention toward travel advisors and B2B partners, positioning them as key channels for selling the new voyages.

### Why the Timing Matters

Luxury travelers and the sellers who work with them are planning further ahead, particularly for premium cruise inventory and itineraries rich in destinations. The early-booking incentives give advisors a timely chance to secure value for clients years before departure. For those already thinking about winter trips well into the future, the opening of sales offers a concrete option to consider now.
