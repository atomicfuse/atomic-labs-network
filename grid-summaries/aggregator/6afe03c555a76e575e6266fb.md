## Sandals South Coast Welcomes Guests Back After Hurricane Melissa

Sandals South Coast in Jamaica has reopened its doors following Hurricane Melissa, unveiling refreshed accommodations and new places to eat. The relaunch forms one piece of a wider $200 million transformation designed to give the resort a fresh feel.

### What Has Changed

Guests returning to the property will find upgraded accommodations and new dining venues. These improvements are part of the broader investment program, which aims to refresh the overall resort experience rather than simply restore what was there before the storm.

### Why the Timing Matters

The reopening arrives as Caribbean travel rebounds and resorts commit significant money to post-storm recovery and luxury upgrades. For people planning trips to Jamaica, the return of this property signals renewed confidence in the destination's hospitality market, suggesting that investment in the sector is continuing after the hurricane.
