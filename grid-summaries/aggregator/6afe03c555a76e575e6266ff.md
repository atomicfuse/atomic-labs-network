## Sundance Mountain Resort Pairs Skiing With Art and a Fresh Look

Utah's Sundance Mountain Resort is the focus of a winter travel guide that stresses how the property blends skiing, art, preservation, and boutique hospitality. The guide points to several new additions as the resort enters a growth phase.

### What's New on the Mountain

Newcomers include The Inn, a ski-in/ski-out stay, along with more than 60 acres of added ski terrain. The Electric Horseman, a high-speed chairlift, rounds out the upgrades. Together they widen what the resort offers while keeping its intimate, design-minded character.

### Why It Matters for Winter Plans

Sundance is leaning into traveler demand for small-scale, nature-conscious mountain escapes with a strong sense of design. With fresh terrain, a new place to stay on the slopes, and continued recognition from Michelin, the resort is especially relevant for anyone planning a winter trip.
