## Frommer's New Zealand Authors Share Their Top Travel Advice

Naomi Arnold and Kate Evans, authors of the newly published Frommer's New Zealand, offer guidance for planning a trip to the country. Their tips cover how New Zealand stands apart from its neighbor, where to engage with Māori culture, and how to get around.

### Not Just an Australia Add-On

The authors stress that New Zealand deserves to be treated as its own destination rather than a quick extension of an Australian itinerary. They point to its distinct character and its emphasis on nature and Indigenous culture. Interest is growing among travelers who want meaningful, nature-rich trips instead of rushed stopovers.

### Māori Culture and Planning Your Route

Arnold and Evans highlight places to experience Māori culture, noting that modern Māori tourism now goes beyond traditional performances. They also advise travelers to plan carefully, since New Zealand is compact on the map but can be slow to drive, so distances may take longer than expected.
