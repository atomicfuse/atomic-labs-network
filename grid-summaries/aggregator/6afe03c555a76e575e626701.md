## San Diego Airport Braces for Near-Record Thanksgiving Crowds

San Diego International Airport is preparing for as many as 950,000 passengers during the 11-day Thanksgiving travel period, with Sunday, Nov. 29, projected to be the busiest day.

### What travelers should expect

Airport officials are advising passengers to prepare for longer security lines and heavier traffic at the curbside. Construction-related detours may complicate getting to terminals, and parking will be limited. Anyone flying during the holiday window is being encouraged to build extra time into their plans.

### Why planning matters now

Thanksgiving travel is packed into a short stretch of days, which tends to make airport congestion more intense than during other holiday periods. Passenger volume is expected to rise 8% to 10% compared with last year, adding to the pressure. Travelers and locals who plan ahead may find the trip to and through the airport easier to manage.
