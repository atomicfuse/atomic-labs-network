## Carnival Releases 2028-29 Voyages on Three Ships

Carnival Cruise Line has begun accepting reservations for its 2028-29 season, covering Carnival Sunrise, Carnival Sunshine, and Carnival Valor. The new schedule offers a mix of itineraries.

### What Is on Offer

The three ships will sail varied itineraries that depart from four U.S. homeports. Travelers can now look at the options and choose the ship, route, and departure that suit them, well ahead of the sailing dates.

### Why Booking Early Matters

Cruise travelers are planning further into the future, and demand for popular sailings and preferred cabins remains strong. The early booking window gives families, groups, and deal-seekers extra time to lock in their dates, routes, and pricing. Those who want a specific cabin or a certain week may find that acting sooner gives them more choice.
