## Anthropic Eyes Record-Setting Nasdaq Debut at $1.4 Trillion

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI model, is planning to list on the Nasdaq in an IPO that could value it at $1.4 trillion or more. If it goes ahead, it would become the first US public company devoted entirely to artificial intelligence.

### A test for investor appetite

The listing is being framed as a major gauge of how much investors want to back frontier AI developers. Questions hang over the sector, including how much revenue these firms generate, whether they can turn a profit, how heavily they must spend on infrastructure, and how exposed they are to debt.

### Why the timing matters

AI investment is booming, yet scrutiny is growing over whether the industry's economics can support its enormous spending needs. OpenAI is expected to follow with its own listing next year, so Anthropic's debut could set the benchmark for how public markets value companies built purely around AI.
