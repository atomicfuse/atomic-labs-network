## Peltola Tops 50% to Win Alaska Senate Seat

Former U.S. Representative Mary Peltola has won Alaska's U.S. Senate race, unseating incumbent Republican Dan Sullivan. She secured victory by passing the 50% threshold under the state's ranked-choice voting system.

### A Historic Result

The win is described as historic on two counts. It is the first time in 18 years that Alaska has elected a Democrat to the Senate, and Peltola becomes the first Alaska Native to serve in the chamber. Her victory came after she moved past the majority mark in the ranked-choice tabulation.

### Why It Matters Now

Alaska was the last Senate contest to be called after the midterms, and its outcome locks in a 56-44 Democratic majority. The result also puts a spotlight on Alaska's ranked-choice voting system. Attention has turned as well to a Department of Justice investigation connected to alleged tactics meant to confuse voters, the outcome of which remains uncertain.
