## Russian Man Arrested in California Over Alleged Spying on Ukraine-Linked Tech Firm

A Russian man named Igor D'iu has been arrested in California, accused of working as a foreign agent against a Silicon Valley technology and defense company tied to Ukraine.

### The allegations

Prosecutors say D'iu followed employees of the company and collected personal information about them. They also allege he used forged identification documents and tried to carry out surveillance on behalf of Russian handlers. The arrest centers on the claim that he operated inside the United States without acting openly as a representative of a foreign government.

### Why it matters now

The case comes amid heightened concern that American technology and defense firms are becoming front lines in a wider geopolitical conflict, particularly those that support Ukraine. It also reflects growing scrutiny of foreign intelligence activity within Silicon Valley, a hub for companies working on sensitive technologies.
