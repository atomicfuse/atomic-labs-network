## Democratic Doctors Gain Ground in Congress, Reshaping a Once-Republican Niche

A new piece examines the growing number of Democratic physicians entering Congress, a change from the long-standing image of the Republican doctor-turned-politician. It focuses on Abdul El-Sayed and Adam Hamawy and draws on a conversation with emergency physician Dr. Thaer Ahmad.

### Healthcare and Humanitarian Experience

El-Sayed and Hamawy bring healthcare platforms, foreign policy positions, and medical-humanitarian backgrounds to their campaigns. The discussion with Ahmad connects those priorities to the broader trend, showing how work in medicine and crisis settings informs their views on policy and public service.

### Why the Shift Matters Now

Following major Democratic midterm gains, doctors are becoming a more visible force in debates over Medicare for All, war, humanitarian aid, and federal spending priorities. The trend also reflects growing public interest in leaders who have frontline experience in healthcare and in crisis zones.
