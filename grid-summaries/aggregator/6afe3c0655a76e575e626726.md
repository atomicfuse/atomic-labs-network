## Flu Season Ramps Up Early as Six States Hit High Activity

Flu activity is climbing across the U.S. earlier than is typical, according to data from the CDC. Six states have now reached high levels of influenza-like illness.

### What the Data Shows

The CDC reports increases in the share of flu tests coming back positive, as well as more doctor visits, hospitalizations and deaths. Several states are recording high or very high levels of influenza-like illness. Health officials are encouraging people to get vaccinated and to begin antiviral treatment early if they become sick.

### Why It Matters Now

Experts say this season is starting sooner than usual, and Thanksgiving travel and indoor gatherings could help the virus spread faster. The CDC's warning arrives amid wider concern about respiratory viruses as winter approaches, making prevention and early treatment a timely priority.
