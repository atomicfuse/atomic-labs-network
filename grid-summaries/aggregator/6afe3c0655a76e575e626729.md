## Prince Harry Says He Would Serve Again If Russia Threat Demanded It

Prince Harry has said he would go back to military service to confront Russia if the situation called for it. He also took aim at the Trump administration's approach to Ukraine, describing it as at odds with the views of the American public.

### What he said

The prince made two points. First, he indicated he is willing to return to the armed forces should a confrontation with Russia require it. Second, he criticized the stance the Trump administration has taken on Ukraine, arguing it does not match where ordinary Americans stand on the issue.

### Why it matters now

His remarks arrive while debate continues over how strongly Western nations should back Ukraine. They also come at a time of close attention to political divisions within the United States over how to handle Russia, a subject on which public opinion and government policy are being openly compared.
