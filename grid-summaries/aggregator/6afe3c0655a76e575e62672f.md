## Texas Starts First Statewide Audit of Its U.S. Senate Race

Texas has begun its first statewide risk-limiting audit, examining paper ballots from the U.S. Senate contest between Democrat James Talarico and Republican Ken Paxton. The review is the first of its kind in the state.

### What the audit involves

State laws passed in 2021 require the audit, and the Texas Secretary of State is carrying it out. Officials describe it as a transparency measure, and it is not expected to change the outcome of the race.

### Why it matters now

Trust in elections remains a major political fault line, and Texas is testing a new audit system after a high-profile Senate contest. The process could reinforce confidence in the vote counts. It could also become another flashpoint in ongoing debates over election denial.
