## Sanders Eyes Senate Committee Gavel to Drive Medicare for All Push

Bernie Sanders is preparing to use his expected chairmanship of the Senate HELP Committee to advance Medicare for All. His plan centers on a four-year transition toward universal coverage, alongside a wider progressive policy agenda.

### The Healthcare Plan

Sanders would begin by lowering the age at which people qualify for Medicare and by expanding the benefits the program offers. Those early steps would open a gradual path toward covering everyone. He also wants cheaper prescription drugs, making affordability a key part of his healthcare push.

### Why the Timing Matters

Democrats are set to control the Senate, which gives Sanders a powerful platform to test how far the party will go on progressive economic and healthcare priorities. His wider agenda includes a $20 minimum wage, stronger union rights and limits on AI development. With healthcare costs, Medicaid cuts and the 2028 political landscape looming, the debate over universal coverage is becoming a party-defining fight.
