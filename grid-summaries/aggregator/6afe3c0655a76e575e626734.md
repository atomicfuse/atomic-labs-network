## IAEA Flags North Korean Nuclear Expansion Before Possible Leaders' Summit

The International Atomic Energy Agency has raised alarm over North Korea's nuclear activity as a potential meeting of Trump, Xi and Putin in China draws closer.

### Expansion at Yongbyon

The IAEA warns that North Korea is expanding nuclear facilities at Yongbyon, a development that raises concern about greater production of weapons-grade material. The article links this buildup to the possible summit in China, where denuclearization could be on the agenda. A meeting with Kim Jong Un might also be discussed.

### Pressure on the Summit

North Korea's nuclear work is speeding up just as major powers prepare for high-stakes talks on global security and nuclear arms. The timing adds pressure on Trump, Xi and Putin to address Pyongyang's growing arsenal. Diplomacy has stalled, and leverage for pushing denuclearization is weakening, which makes the issue harder to ignore at the table.
