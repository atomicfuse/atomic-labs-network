## Fox News Host Brian Kilmeade Hits Out at JD Vance Over Iran War Warning

Fox News host Brian Kilmeade has launched a sharp attack on JD Vance, a public clash between a prominent conservative media figure and the vice president that centers on how the United States should handle Iran.

The dispute began after Vance cautioned against an open-ended war with Iran. Kilmeade responded forcefully, saying Vance "never should have been vice president." The remark was a notably personal rebuke, aimed at Vance's standing in the role rather than only at his position on the issue. The coverage frames the exchange as an eruption of anger from the Fox host, directed at a senior figure from his own political side.

### Why It Matters

The confrontation points to widening divisions among Republicans and in conservative media over foreign policy. As tensions with Iran bring fresh attention to the topic, the old argument between interventionism and restraint is returning to the foreground. Kilmeade and Vance landing on opposite sides shows how unsettled that debate remains on the right.
