## Study Backs Two-Track Approach to Preventing Teen Obesity

A large Norwegian study of nearly 125,000 children concludes that stopping obesity in adolescence takes two strategies working at the same time. Researchers point to targeted help for young children who already have obesity, paired with broad prevention aimed at everyone else.

### Treatment and prevention together

According to the findings, young children with obesity need focused treatment. At the same time, population-wide measures matter for the wider group of children, rather than leaving prevention to those who are already affected. Neither approach alone is presented as sufficient.

### Why it matters now

Childhood obesity remains a major public health concern. The study challenges the idea that being overweight early in life is enough to predict who will have obesity later. It directs policymakers, schools and parents toward systems that make healthy choices easier for all children.
