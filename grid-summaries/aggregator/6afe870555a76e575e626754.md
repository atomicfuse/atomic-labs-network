## Ancient Algae Fossils in Portugal Rewrite Green Algae Timeline

Microfossils from the Algarve region of Portugal are changing how scientists view the history of modern green algae. Researchers identified charophyte algae fossils that are 115 million years old.

### An Earlier Start for Modern Genera

The fossils push the known timeline of modern green algae genera back by about 30 million years. They suggest the Characeae group diversified earlier than previously believed. This happened while the now-extinct Clavatoraceae were still dominant. The diversification may have taken place during a brief Cretaceous cooling event.

### Why It Matters Today

The discovery reshapes understanding of how plants and algae evolved. It also shows that climate shifts may have opened ecological opportunities long before researchers had assumed. The find links sea-level changes in the ancient greenhouse world with questions that feel newly relevant in today's climate context.
