## Rare Rhino Calf Born at Chester Zoo, Birth Caught on Camera

A greater one-horned rhino calf has been born at Chester Zoo in England, and CCTV footage recorded both the birth and the newborn's first steps.

### A Carefully Monitored Birth

Zookeepers closely tracked the reproductive health of the calf's mother, and that careful monitoring is a key part of the story. The calf's arrival also forms part of a wider conservation breeding program, which aims to support the species through managed births in zoo care.

### Why This Birth Matters

Only around 4,000 greater one-horned rhinos remain, and concerns about genetic diversity are growing, so each successful birth counts toward the species' long-term survival. The story also carries emotional weight. The calf continues the legacy of its father, Beni, who died shortly before the birth.
