## Two Drugs Fail to Boost Spontaneous Birth After Induction

A large randomized trial from Norway has found no clear sign that hyoscine butylbromide or sodium bicarbonate raises the rate of spontaneous vaginal birth in first-time mothers whose labor was induced. The findings appear in the BMJ.

### What the Trial Concluded

The researchers say neither drug should be used routinely to improve the chances of spontaneous vaginal delivery after induction. They add that small benefits cannot be fully ruled out, so the result is not an absolute verdict against either treatment.

### Why It Matters

Labor induction is common, and first-time mothers face higher odds of instrumental or cesarean delivery, so any intervention that might help draws close attention in maternity care. This trial challenges smaller earlier studies and could influence hospital protocols as well as conversations between clinicians and parents-to-be about birth plans.
