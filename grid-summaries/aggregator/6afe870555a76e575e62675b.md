## Webb Confirms a Rare Stellar Merger in Andromeda

NASA's Webb telescope has helped confirm the first observed merger between a smaller, sun-like star and a massive supergiant in the Andromeda galaxy. Astronomers reconstructed the system, nicknamed Bao, before, during, and after its eruption.

### A Rare View of Common Envelope Evolution

The observations give astronomers a real-time look at common envelope evolution. This key process is linked to some supernovae, black holes, neutron stars, and sources of gravitational waves. Seeing it unfold offers a rare chance to study how such events begin.

### Why the Timing Matters

The result arrives as time-domain astronomy picks up speed. Webb, SPHEREx, and the upcoming Roman Space Telescope are positioned to catch many more fast-changing cosmic events. The discovery also shows how rapid-response observations can turn a brief stellar outburst into a detailed replay of stellar death and transformation.
