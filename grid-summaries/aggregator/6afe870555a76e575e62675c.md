## Study Links Morphea to Elevated Cancer Risk

A new study in JAMA Dermatology finds that people with morphea face a higher overall incidence of cancer. Researchers examined more than 6,000 morphea patients and compared them with both the general population and patients with systemic sclerosis.

### What the Study Found

Morphea patients showed an elevated risk of solid tumors. The increase was especially notable for female reproductive cancers and breast cancer. The relative risk was highest among younger patients, a detail that stands out within the broader pattern of heightened cancer incidence across the group.

### Why It Matters

Morphea has historically drawn less oncologic attention than systemic sclerosis. The new research suggests that cancer screening and clinical vigilance may deserve a larger role in caring for people with the condition. The findings also add timely nuance to ongoing conversations about cancer risk in autoimmune and inflammatory diseases.
