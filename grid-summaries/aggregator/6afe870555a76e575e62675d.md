## Calcareous Grasslands Key to Europe's Wild Bees, Study Finds

A European study has identified calcareous grasslands as crucial habitats for wild bees. The research points to the way land is used as a major factor in how many bee species thrive there.

### What the research found

Bee diversity was highest where land-use intensity was low to moderate. The study argues that managing the grasslands themselves is only part of the answer. Connected habitats nearby also play an essential role, so protecting individual sites in isolation is unlikely to be enough. The authors add that conservation measures must be tailored to regional conditions.

### Why it matters now

Wild bees face pressure from habitat loss, agricultural intensification and land abandonment, which makes practical conservation strategies increasingly urgent. Europe is currently debating biodiversity-friendly farming and land-use policy. The findings suggest that simply calling for more conservation falls short unless actions fit the local context.
