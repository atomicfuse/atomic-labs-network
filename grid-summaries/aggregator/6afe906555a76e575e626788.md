## Ex-GOP Strategist Says Fox Is Turning on JD Vance

Former Republican strategist Tim Miller argues that "Fox & Friends" has started positioning against Vice President JD Vance in the wake of GOP midterm losses. He says the tactics echo the old establishment playbook once used against Donald Trump.

### The Core Claim

Miller contends that Fox is portraying Vance in two ways at once: as ideologically suspect and as a weak electoral prospect. At the same time, he says, the network is floating other names as possible alternatives, including Ron DeSantis and Ted Cruz.

### Why It Matters Now

Republican power brokers appear to be jockeying early over the party's post-Trump future, particularly after the recent election setbacks. Fox's tone toward Vance could point to a wider conservative media effort to shape the next GOP primary before it officially begins.
