## Thousands in Haiti rally behind Salvadoran leader Bukele after gang crackdown

Mass rallies have taken place in Haiti in support of Salvadoran President Nayib Bukele, who sent troops and backed operations against powerful criminal groups. The coverage presents him as a foreign security figure whose popularity is growing among Haitians.

### A foreign leader gains a following

The rallies come as Haiti struggles with severe gang violence and an unstable state. Supporters have turned out in large numbers to back Bukele and the anti-gang operations he has supported, casting him as a source of hope in a country where official institutions have been weakened.

### Why the moment matters

Gangs now control much of Port-au-Prince, and thousands of people have been killed this year while more than a million have been displaced. Against that backdrop, Bukele's hardline approach is turning into an international flashpoint. Publics desperate for order are drawn to it, while the United Nations warns about the need to protect civilians.
