## Big Tech's Trump ties may become a 2028 campaign target

An analysis of the next presidential cycle argues that Donald Trump's once-visible partnership with major technology companies could turn into a political burden. Executives who drew close to power may now be exposed as convenient targets.

### Why the alliance could backfire

The piece suggests that the closeness between tech leaders and the Trump orbit may give opponents an easy line of attack. Being seen near power can raise a company's profile, but it can also tie its fortunes to a single political figure and make its leaders look like part of the establishment that voters distrust.

### Why it matters now

Public skepticism toward powerful tech firms remains high. AI, data privacy, platform moderation and billionaire influence are becoming major political flashpoints. As early positioning for 2028 begins, candidates may find that criticizing Big Tech serves them better than courting it.
