## Mosque Raid Deepens Scrutiny of Berlin's Die Linke

Police have banned and raided a Berlin mosque association accused of supporting Hezbollah, and reports that senior Die Linke figures visited the site have pulled the far-left party into a growing controversy.

### What the scandal covers

The article presents the raid as the latest in a series of questions about the party. It points to alleged ties to Hamas sympathizers, anti-Israel activism, and figures linked to organized crime. The reports of visits by senior party members to the mosque are central to the new criticism.

### Why the timing matters

Die Linke recently performed strongly in Berlin's state elections and is taking part in coalition talks, so questions about its security stance and its contacts are politically pressing. The raids also come as Germany is highly concerned about antisemitism, Islamist networks, and polarization over Israel.
