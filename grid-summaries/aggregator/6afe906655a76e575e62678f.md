## Calves Pay Surprise Hospital Visit to Ailing Lancashire Farmer

A dairy farmer from Lancashire who is critically ill received an unexpected visit in hospital from three calves. One of them had been born on the day he was admitted.

### A reunion with farm life

Hospital staff said seeing the calves lifted the farmer's spirits and helped his recovery, both emotionally and physically. They explained that the visit reconnected him with the farm life he knows well, giving him a link to the world outside the critical care unit. The calves' arrival came as a surprise to him, and the occasion stood out as a warm moment on the ward.

### Why the story resonates

The visit reflects growing interest in healthcare that puts patients at the centre, in what motivates people to recover, and in the part animals can play in wellbeing. It also offers a welcome piece of uplifting local news at a time when coverage of hospitals and the NHS is often difficult and downbeat.
