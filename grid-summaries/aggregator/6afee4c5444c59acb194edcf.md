## Florida Pastor Convicted Over Role in Haitian President's Killing

A Miami jury has found Christian Emmanuel Sanon, a Florida pastor with dual US and Haitian citizenship, guilty on eight charges linked to the 2021 assassination of Haitian President Jovenel Moïse.

### The case against Sanon

Prosecutors said Sanon took part in a plot to remove Moïse from power and possibly make himself president. They described a conspiracy built around security contracts and the use of mercenaries. The verdict was handed down in Miami, where the charges were heard.

### Why the verdict matters now

The ruling arrives while Haiti remains stuck in a political vacuum and faces worsening gang violence in the wake of Moïse's killing. It also comes ahead of a presidential election scheduled for next year. Moïse's widow, Martine, has announced that she plans to run in that vote.
