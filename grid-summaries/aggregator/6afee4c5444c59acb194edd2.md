## Democrats Win Big, Then Fight Over What the Victory Means

After their major electoral success in 2026, Democrats are now debating what actually produced it. The disagreement splits the party along familiar lines, and the answer could guide what comes next.

### Two Competing Explanations

Moderates say the wins came from centrist candidates who kept their distance from the party's left wing. In their telling, that positioning made Democrats appealing to a broader group of voters.

Progressives see it differently. They argue that economic populism drove the results, and that the party succeeded because it spoke to voters' financial concerns.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

The question is which lesson becomes the party's accepted story. Democrats are weighing whether to govern cautiously or pursue larger structural reforms. The winning narrative could shape policy choices, how the party recruits candidates, and the platform it carries into the 2028 presidential race.
