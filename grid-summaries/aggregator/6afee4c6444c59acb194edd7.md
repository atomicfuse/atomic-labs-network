## Review of Reagan book argues defense buildup holds lessons for today

An article reviewing Roger Zakheim's book on Ronald Reagan makes the case that the president's "peace through strength" approach helped win the Cold War. It then applies that history to the present, calling on lawmakers and 2028 presidential hopefuls to rebuild America's defense capacity.

### The Reagan argument

The review focuses on Reagan's military buildup and credits that investment with helping bring the Cold War to a close. Drawing on Zakheim's account, the piece presents strength as the foundation for peace rather than an alternative to it. It uses that record to argue that sustained defense spending can shape the outcome of a long rivalry between major powers.

### Why it matters now

The article arrives amid renewed debate over Pentagon spending, nuclear modernization, China's military rise, and Russia's hostility toward the United States. It frames this moment as a Reagan-style test of political will, particularly as Congress weighs its defense funding priorities.
