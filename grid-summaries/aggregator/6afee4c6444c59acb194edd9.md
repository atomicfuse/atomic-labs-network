## Two Storylines Could Turn The Rock's Survivor Series Return Into More Than a Cameo

An article imagines how WWE might use The Rock after his Survivor Series: WarGames appearance. Its argument is that the company has two "real" storyline paths if it wants the visit to mean more than a nostalgia moment.

### Roman Reigns or Cody Rhodes

The first option is a long-awaited Rock vs. Roman Reigns setup, a matchup fans have wanted for some time. The second would see The Rock steer Cody Rhodes toward a corporate heel turn. Both are presented as fantasy booking rather than confirmed plans, and the article offers them as the biggest possibilities.

### Why the Timing Matters

Survivor Series arrives just as WWE begins its road toward the Royal Rumble and WrestleMania, so any Rock tease could feel especially big. His unresolved interactions with Roman, Cody, and John Cena from 2024 and 2025 also leave fans hoping for some kind of payoff.
