## Lawmakers Drop Plan to Pause Roadside Mowing for Campaign Signs

North Carolina legislators have backed away from a proposed rule that would have kept state crews from mowing along highways during campaign season. The reversal appears in a budget corrections bill that is now headed to Gov. Josh Stein.

### What changed

The earlier plan would have barred NCDOT from mowing in highway rights of way during political campaign seasons, with the goal of protecting political signs placed there. The budget corrections bill removes that mowing ban, so the restriction will not take effect if the bill becomes law.

### Why the timing matters

The change arrives ahead of future election cycles, when roadside campaign signs are a visible and sometimes contested part of political messaging. The decision also highlights the tension among campaign visibility, public maintenance of roadsides, and roadway safety.
