## NSA Outage Reportedly Disrupts Georgia Intelligence Hub During Iran War

A major technology failure at the National Security Agency has reportedly disrupted classified communications at an intelligence hub in Georgia. The facility supports U.S. cyber operations and activity across the Middle East, and the outage struck while the United States is at war with Iran.

### What Officials Say About the Cause

According to the report, officials state the failure was not a cyberattack. They indicate it may be linked to security patches applied to fix software vulnerabilities that were discovered with the help of artificial intelligence.

### Why the Timing Matters

The disruption arrives during active conflict and alongside a broader run of technology outages. It raises pressing questions about whether aging government systems can keep up with the pace of AI-driven cybersecurity demands. It also underscores a different danger: defensive updates meant to protect systems can themselves become an operational threat.
