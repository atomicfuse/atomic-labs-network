## Putin Takes Centre Stage in China's Power Display

A new piece examines how Vladimir Putin was positioned alongside Donald Trump and Xi Jinping during an appearance in China, treating the placement as a statement in itself.

### A Symbolic Return to Visibility

The piece frames Putin's prominent spot next to the American and Chinese leaders as a symbolic comeback to global diplomatic view. It suggests he is relying on high-profile imagery to project influence and legitimacy internationally, using the visual setting to signal that Russia remains a significant presence among major powers.

### Why the Image Matters

Great-power politics is increasingly shaped by public spectacle and strategic alliances, and the piece argues that Putin's appearance in China reflects a change in diplomatic messaging. By placing Russia, China and the United States within a single visual narrative, the image points to power competition and possible realignment among the three countries.
