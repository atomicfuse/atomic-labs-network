## DJ Lag Joins Burna Boy and Asake on GTA 6 Radio

South African producer DJ Lag has called his place in Grand Theft Auto VI a "dream come true." He appears alongside a lineup of African artists on the game's AfroBank FM station.

### African Sounds Take the Spotlight

AfroBank FM features Burna Boy, Asake, Pat Thomas, Ebo Taylor, Robot Boii and Kamo Mphela, alongside DJ Lag. The station gives a major platform to Afrobeats, amapiano, gqom, highlife and 3-step, placing these genres inside one of gaming's biggest franchises.

### Why the Soundtrack Matters

GTA 6 ends a 13-year wait and is expected to rank among the biggest entertainment releases in years. That makes its music choices culturally significant. The prominent placement of African music shows how global gaming is becoming a powerful discovery platform for African artists and genres.
