## Trump's FDA Pick Pulls Out, Catching Officials Off Guard

Heidi Overton has withdrawn as President Trump's choice to lead the FDA, and sources say the decision blindsided officials. A key senator is reportedly furious about how it unfolded.

### How the withdrawal unfolded

According to the report, Overton's own team apparently learned of her exit through Axios rather than directly. That detail, along with the senator's frustration, points to disorder inside the nomination process.

### Why the FDA post matters

The FDA oversees drugs, vaccines, food safety and wider health regulation, so its leadership carries political and commercial weight. A messy withdrawal signals uncertainty in how the administration staffs its health policy roles, and it could slow progress on regulatory priorities.
