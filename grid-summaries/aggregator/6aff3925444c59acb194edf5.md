## Jon Stewart Says Nvidia Scandal Goes Well Beyond an Alleged Affair

Jon Stewart contends that the headline-grabbing claim about Nvidia chief Jensen Huang and a Chinese spy is a distraction from a more serious question. His focus is on who allowed advanced chips to flow to China, and who gained from it.

### Stewart's Larger Argument

Stewart argues the alleged affair is not the heart of the matter. In his view, the central issue is whether Nvidia, President Trump, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick enabled chip exports to China while profiting from the arrangement. He points to Trump and Lutnick as figures who deserve scrutiny alongside the company itself.

### Why the Timing Matters

The story arrives as competition between the United States and China over artificial intelligence is intensifying. House Democrats have also renewed their scrutiny of possible conflicts of interest involving Nvidia, Lutnick's family firm, and Trump's stock holdings, which gives Stewart's argument added relevance at this moment.
