## Billingham revamp delayed as councillors demand closer scrutiny

A £30m plan to regenerate Billingham town centre has been put on hold after Conservative councillors called in Stockton Council's £13m purchase deal for extra scrutiny. The move has sparked a political row over how the project should proceed.

### Warnings over derailment

Labour MP Chris McDonald says the call-in risks derailing long-awaited redevelopment of the town centre. He has accused opponents of playing politics with a scheme that supporters hope will help revive the area. The delay means work cannot yet move ahead while the process runs its course.

### Calls for proof of value

Conservatives argue that taxpayers deserve evidence the £13m deal represents good value. The scheme is backed by major public funding and is now at a decision point, with scrutiny set to determine whether it can progress. The dispute also feeds into wider local debates about town centre decline, public spending, and who earns credit or blame for regeneration.
