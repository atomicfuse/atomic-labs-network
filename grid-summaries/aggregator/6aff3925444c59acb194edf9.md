## Florio apologizes in one sentence after Vrabel mustache joke draws NFL rebuke

### What drew the criticism

The joke compared a coach's facial hair to that of Hitler, a reference tied to antisemitism and the Holocaust. It was made on air while the show was based in Germany, which added to the sensitivity. The league's rebuke and the shortness of the hosts' apology became central parts of the reaction.
