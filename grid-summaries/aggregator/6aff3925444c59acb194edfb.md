## Hegseth Stays in the Spotlight Despite Blocked Impeachment Vote

A vote to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth was blocked, but critics say that outcome does not clear him. He still faces intense scrutiny over accusations linked to the US-Israel war on Iran and other military actions.

### Who is accountable for the war decisions

The article describes Hegseth as a central figure in the controversial decisions surrounding the war. It argues, however, that responsibility does not rest with him alone. Accountability also extends to President Trump, to Congress, and to the broader system of executive war powers that allows such decisions to be made.

### What comes next for Congress

Democrats have just won control of Congress after midterm elections shaped in part by backlash to the Iran war. That shift sets up possible investigations and renewed impeachment efforts. The story also feeds into a larger fight over presidential war-making and civilian casualties, and over whether Congress will reassert its constitutional authority.
