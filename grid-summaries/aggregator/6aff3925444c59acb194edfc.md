## Apple Photos can now watermark real pictures edited with AI

Apple Photos has begun adding invisible SynthID watermarks to genuine photos that have been changed with certain Apple Intelligence tools. The affected features include Clean Up, Extend, Reframe, Image Playground and Image Wand.

### What triggers the watermark

The report explains which edits cause the mark to be applied and how a person can check whether a picture carries one. The key point is that the watermark signals an image was AI-assisted. It does not automatically mean the picture is fake, since the underlying photo may still be a real capture that was only adjusted.

### Why it matters now

AI editing is increasingly built into everyday phone photography, making it harder to tell an authentic capture from a modified image. Apple's step reflects mounting pressure for transparency around AI media, particularly as edited images circulate across social platforms, news coverage and personal sharing.
