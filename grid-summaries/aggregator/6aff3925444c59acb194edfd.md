## DOJ Brings in Essayli for Probe of Mar-a-Lago Search

First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli, a Trump loyalist based in Los Angeles, has been tapped to help with a Justice Department investigation into the 2022 search of Mar-a-Lago. The probe examines whether officials broke the law during that operation.

### A Broader Reframing Effort

The article describes the appointment as part of a wider Trump-aligned push to recast earlier investigations into the former president as politically motivated. The inquiry into the search is presented as one piece of that larger effort, rather than a standalone review.

### Legal Questions Over Essayli's Role

Essayli's own authority is under legal challenge. Courts are questioning whether he can lead the Los Angeles U.S. attorney's office without Senate confirmation, and the Supreme Court could act. The move also arrives amid continuing political fights over DOJ independence, election enforcement, and claims of retaliation tied to investigations of Trump.
