## Army to Detonate Five Tons of Unstable Chemicals at Iowa Plant, Prompting Evacuations

The U.S. Army plans to destroy five tons of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant on Nov. 20, 2026. The operation will lead to temporary evacuations in Middletown and plume warnings for nearby communities.

### Why it is being destroyed on site

Officials say the lead azide has become too unstable to move, so it must be destroyed where it sits. The EPA has given emergency approval for the detonation. Residents should expect noise, vibrations, and dust that contains lead.

### What it means for the area

Because the date is set, the event is an immediate public safety and environmental concern for residents, schools, and towns in the plume's path. It also raises wider questions about how hazardous materials are stored at military sites and how communities are notified when risks arise.
