## Two lawsuits challenge Michigan's new campaign spending limits

Two federal lawsuits have been filed against Michigan's newly passed Proposal 2, a "Money Out of Politics" ballot measure. The measure restricts campaign spending by utilities, major government contractors, and entities connected to them.

### What the lawsuits claim

Both suits argue that Proposal 2 violates the First Amendment. They say its reporting rules are vague and that it unfairly targets certain speakers, singling them out for restrictions that others do not face. The challenges put the measure's constitutionality squarely before the federal courts.

### Why the timing matters

Voters approved Proposal 2 with more than 80% support, and it is set to take effect by Dec. 3, 2026, unless courts intervene. The outcome could help define how far states may go in limiting corporate political spending, especially when voters have clearly called for campaign finance reform. The cases will test that balance between public demand and free speech protections.
