## Inside the Rohingya Camps as a Genocide Ruling Looms

A New York Times video takes viewers into the Rohingya refugee camps of Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. Correspondent Hannah Beech guides the report, which was made ahead of an International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.

### Life in the Camps

The video focuses on the sheer scale of displacement and the everyday reality of more than 1.2 million refugees living in the settlements. Beech's reporting from inside the camps puts the human dimension of the crisis at the center of the story.

### Why the Ruling Matters

The court's ruling brings fresh legal and global attention to atrocities that many Rohingya survivors have described for years. It also raises pressing questions about accountability for those responsible, whether and how refugees might return to Myanmar, and what the future holds for one of the world's largest refugee populations.
