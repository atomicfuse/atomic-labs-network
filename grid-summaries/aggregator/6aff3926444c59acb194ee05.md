## South Carolina Parole Board Again Rejects Susan Smith's Bid for Release

Susan Smith, who was convicted of drowning her two young sons in 1994, has been denied parole for a second time. The decision by a South Carolina parole board was unanimous, coming 32 years after the crime.

### What the board heard

Smith appeared before the board and said she had changed and felt remorse for what she did. Members were not persuaded, and every one of them voted against granting her release. The outcome means she remains in prison, with no change to her status following this latest hearing.

### Why the case still resonates

The killings remain among the most notorious child murder cases in the United States. Because Smith becomes eligible for parole every two years, her case returns to public attention on a regular schedule. Each review reopens debate over punishment, remorse, mental health and justice for the victims, keeping questions about the crime and its consequences alive well after three decades.
