## McMillan Defends NFC South Against Media Mockery

Carolina Panthers wide receiver Tetairoa McMillan has pushed back on the way NFL media treats the NFC South. Every team in the division has a losing record, and the jokes have followed. McMillan says the division deserves more respect.

### Competitive Despite the Records

McMillan argues that the NFC South is competitive even though no team sits above .500. The division comes across as chaotic and underwhelming, yet oddly compelling, because all four teams still have a shot at winning it. In his view, that open race counts for something.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

As the NFL season tightens, the wide-open playoff race makes the division an easy target for debate, jokes, and hot takes. McMillan's comments tap into a familiar tension in sports media: whether a so-called bad division deserves respect if the race itself is entertaining.
