## Trump Holds Off on Iran Strike Decision Until After New Year

President Trump is in no hurry to decide on renewed major strikes against Iran, pushing any choice to after the new year even though he had earlier hinted that action could come after the midterms.

### Mixed Signals and Political Fallout

The administration has sent conflicting messages about its intentions, while Republicans are dealing with the aftermath of losing both chambers of Congress. JD Vance publicly tied the war to the party's defeat, underscoring how sensitive the issue has become. Claims about threats from Iranian weapons are also disputed, adding uncertainty to the debate.

### Why the Delay Matters

The Iran conflict is now a live political liability in Washington. The postponement suggests military strategy is tangled up with domestic politics, sanctions pressure, and a broader recalibration after the election, rather than being driven by military considerations alone.
