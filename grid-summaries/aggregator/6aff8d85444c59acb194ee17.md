## California Opens Inquiry Into Nvidia Over Possible China Chip Access

California's attorney general has issued a subpoena to Nvidia as part of an investigation into whether the company's actions helped China obtain advanced AI chips or confidential information. The probe also examines whether investors or the public were misled.

### What prompted the probe

The inquiry follows Jensen Huang's departure from the company amid scandal. It also comes as scrutiny grows over Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer whose alleged links to chip diversion have drawn attention. Investigators are looking at whether Nvidia's conduct played a role in technology reaching China.

### Why the stakes are high

Nvidia sits at the center of the AI boom and of U.S.-China technology restrictions. Any suggestion of export-control breaches or investor deception therefore carries major market and geopolitical weight. The company's stock drop and leadership crisis add to the pressure, making this a live story about trust, governance, and national security.
