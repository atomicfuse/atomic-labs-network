## Trump Grants Posthumous Pardon to Baseball Great Pete Rose

President Trump has issued a posthumous pardon for Pete Rose, the baseball legend who was convicted of filing false tax returns and spent years banned from MLB over gambling allegations.

### What the pardon does

The pardon is largely symbolic. It does not alter Rose's standing with the Hall of Fame, which remains a separate matter. MLB, however, has already opened a path that could lead to his possible induction, so the pardon arrives as the sport's own position on him is shifting.

### Why it matters now

Rose's legacy is being debated again after MLB softened its stance following his death, and Hall of Fame eligibility could come as early as 2028. By stepping in, Trump has turned a decades-old sports controversy into a fresh political and cultural flashpoint, adding another layer to an argument that fans and the league were already revisiting.
