## Cumin Sodas Take On Cola Giants in India

Cumin-flavoured fizzy drinks such as Lahori Zeera, Campa Jeera and Jeeru are rising quickly in India. These so-called ethnic beverages are now challenging Coca-Cola and Pepsi in the mass-market soft drinks segment.

### What is driving the rise

The article argues that the carbonated drinks market is being reshaped by a mix of factors. Nostalgic Indian flavours appeal to buyers, while low pricing keeps the drinks accessible. Wider distribution has also helped these products reach more shoppers and compete directly with the established cola brands on the shelf.

### Why it matters now

India's non-alcoholic beverages market is expanding fast, and online demand for cumin sodas has surged. Local brands are gaining share from global cola giants. The shift points to a broader consumer move toward homegrown, culturally familiar flavours that many see as more authentic or healthier.
