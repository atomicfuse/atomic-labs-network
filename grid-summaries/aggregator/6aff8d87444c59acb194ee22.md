## Judge Extends Pause on Construction of Proposed Washington Triumphal Arch

A federal judge has lengthened a temporary order that stops the Trump administration from beginning work on a planned 250-foot Military Triumphal Arch in Washington, D.C. The extension runs for 14 days.

### The legal and political fight

The case centers on court challenges to the proposed monument, along with political backlash against it. A key dispute is whether the project must receive approval from Congress before construction can proceed. The article lays out the arguments on each side of that question and the pressure the plan has drawn.

### Why it matters now

The arch is one of several high-profile projects Trump is pushing to reshape public spaces in the capital, and many of them now face court challenges. With Democrats set to take control of Congress soon, the arch and related efforts could come under heightened scrutiny.
