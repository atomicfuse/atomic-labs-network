## Pakistan Says 22 Militants Killed in Border Raids

Pakistani security forces say they have killed 22 militants in intelligence-led raids on hideouts in the northwest, close to the Afghan border. The military alleges the fighters were backed by India.

### What the operations involve

The raids are presented as part of Pakistan's wider campaign against the TTP and allied groups. Authorities describe them as a response to rising attacks on police and security forces. The claims about the death toll and Indian backing come from the Pakistani military's account of the operations.

### Why it matters now

Militant violence in Pakistan has surged in recent years, and relations with both Afghanistan and India remain highly sensitive. The latest raids follow another recent military claim that 74 militants had been killed, which points to an intensified security push across the border regions.
