## Women's Health Retreats Offer Whole-Body Care for Hormones and Fertility

A growing number of wellness retreats are built around women's health, covering hormones, fertility, perimenopause, burnout, and reproductive wellness. A new roundup highlights five programs that take an integrative approach to care.

### Why Women Are Turning to Retreats

The article argues that many women look beyond conventional medicine because it often splits hormonal concerns into separate problems or brushes them aside. The retreats it features respond by blending diagnostics with Eastern medicine and restorative therapies, aiming to treat the body as a connected whole rather than a set of isolated symptoms.

### A Shift in Luxury Wellness

Conversations about hormonal health, fertility, PCOS, endometriosis, and perimenopause are moving into the mainstream of wellness and healthcare as more women ask for personalized, whole-body care. Luxury wellness is changing alongside that demand, moving away from generic spa escapes and toward medically informed programs designed around the female body.
