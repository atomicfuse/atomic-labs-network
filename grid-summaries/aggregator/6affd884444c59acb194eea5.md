## SEASE Pushes Into Womenswear With 2026 FW Collection

Italian luxury lifestyle brand SEASE is stepping beyond its menswear origins by launching an official women's collection for the 2026 fall-winter season, alongside a new winter capsule.

### What the Brand Is Launching

The women's line is built around functional materials, Italian tailoring, and clothing meant to move easily from city streets to outdoor settings. SEASE has also introduced a winter "Ski Kit" capsule. The brand aims to lift womenswear from 15% to 40% of its total sales, a sizeable shift in its business mix.

### Why the Timing Matters

Luxury labels are increasingly courting "performance lifestyle" consumers who want refined clothing that works for travel, outdoor activity, sport, and urban life. SEASE's move fits that trend. It also points to potential growth in Korea, where premium golf, ski, and lifestyle categories remain strong.
