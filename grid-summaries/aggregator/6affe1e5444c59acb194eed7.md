## Leavitt's Trump Meeting Boast Draws Sharp Pushback

Karoline Leavitt's claim that Donald Trump "dominates" his meetings with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin has prompted a wave of criticism. Opponents say the record doesn't match her description.

### The Claim and the Criticism

Leavitt asserted that Trump always walks away from such talks with a "deliverable." Critics counter that the meetings with Xi and Putin yielded no concrete outcome, only vague statements and friendly imagery alongside two authoritarian leaders. They argue the gap between her framing and the visible results is wide.

### Why the Debate Matters

The exchange puts Trump's foreign policy image under fresh scrutiny. The high-profile encounters produced plenty of warm rhetoric but little visible substance. That fuels a broader argument over whether personal diplomacy with strongmen achieves real results or is mostly political theater.
