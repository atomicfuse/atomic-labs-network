## Retired Sergeant Warns Over Plan to Disband NYPD Unit Before Parade

A retired police sergeant is raising concerns about Mayor Zohran Mamdani's plan to dissolve the NYPD Strategic Response Group. The mayor intends to replace parts of its role with new protest-response and de-escalation structures.

### The Warning

The retired sergeant argues the change could weaken the department's ability to control crowds, coordinate on counterterrorism, and secure major events. The criticism centers on what the unit currently does and whether the planned replacements can take over those duties.

### Why the Timing Matters

The debate is heating up ahead of the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, when New York City expects huge crowds and heavy security demands. It also feeds into a wider national argument over policing, protest rights, public safety, and the use of civilian-led de-escalation.
