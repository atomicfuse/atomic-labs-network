## Senate Passes Bipartisan Permitting Bill Aimed at Faster Energy Projects

The Senate has approved a major energy permitting bill with support from both parties. It is designed to speed approvals for power plants, transmission lines, pipelines and other infrastructure, while requiring data centers to pay for the energy they use.

### A Clash Over Priorities

The bill is framed as a contest between two sets of concerns. Supporters stress affordability and the need to expand the grid. Critics point to environmental safeguards, tribal sovereignty and worries about fossil fuels. The debate shows how hard it is to balance faster construction against protections that opponents want to keep in place.

### Why the Timing Matters

Rising electricity demand from data centers, higher utility bills and political backlash over energy costs are pushing lawmakers to act. The vote also comes after the midterm elections, making it an early test of how both parties will weigh the energy needs of the AI era against climate commitments.
