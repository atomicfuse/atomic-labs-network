## Houthi Strike Damages Riyadh Tower Housing Saudi Sovereign Wealth Fund

PIF Tower, the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund and the tallest skyscraper in Riyadh, was damaged in a strike attributed to the Houthis. No injuries were reported. The attack is described as part of a renewed and widening conflict in Yemen.

### A Symbolic Target

The building is the home of the fund at the heart of Saudi Arabia's push to diversify its economy, which makes the strike notable beyond the physical damage. The article presents it as one sign that the Yemen conflict is increasingly putting Saudi financial, oil and transport infrastructure at risk.

### Why the Timing Matters

Riyadh expects the Yemen campaign to drag on, and tensions across the Gulf are rising. The strike also shows the conflict moving away from border zones and oil sites toward high-profile urban and financial targets, widening the range of places exposed to attack.
