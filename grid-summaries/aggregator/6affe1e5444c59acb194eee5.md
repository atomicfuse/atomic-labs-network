## Cyber director Sean Cairncross removed as outages mount

National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross has been fired, and Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies is now serving as acting director. The change arrives during a run of significant tech disruptions across the United States.

### Why he was removed

According to the article, Cairncross was removed after concerns were raised about his cybersecurity credentials. Davies, who serves as the Pentagon's chief information officer, has stepped in on an acting basis. The report places the leadership change alongside a recent wave of major outages that have hit air travel, banks, Microsoft, Apple, Marriott, weather systems and the NSA.

### Why it matters now

Public anxiety is growing over how fragile the country's digital infrastructure may be, and over whether such outages stem from cyberattacks, outdated systems or ordinary technical failures. The administration is trying to project control during the turmoil, while insisting there is no evidence of a coordinated foreign cyber operation behind the disruptions.
