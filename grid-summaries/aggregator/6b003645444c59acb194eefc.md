## Gen Z Rebrands Obama-Era Hope as "WhimZ"

Following Democratic midterm wins, young liberals online are bringing back Obama-era aesthetics and the "millennial optimism" that went with them. But they are putting their own spin on it.

### A more ironic kind of hope

The article describes the trend as "WhimZ," a battle-tested and humorous version of optimism. Gen Z's political joy rests less on faith in institutions and more on organizing, skepticism and comedy. Members of the generation also expect to see actual results, not just inspiring words.

### Why it matters now

Electoral victories are turning into a nostalgia-driven meme cycle, while younger voters set their political identity apart from that of millennials. As Gen Z grows into a larger voting bloc, its blend of cynicism, humor and cautious hope could reshape campaign messaging and progressive politics.
