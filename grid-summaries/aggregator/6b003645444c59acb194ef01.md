## Food Babe Takes Aim at the "Holiday Death Aisle" Before Thanksgiving

Food activist Vani Hari, known as the "Food Babe," is criticizing ultra-processed holiday foods as Thanksgiving approaches. She is also promoting a new cookbook filled with "real food" alternatives.

### Her Case for Real Food

Hari argues that Thanksgiving and other celebrations can keep their nostalgia and joy without leaning on additives, dyes, preservatives, or packaged shortcuts. Her cookbook offers substitutes meant to let families enjoy traditional gatherings while skipping the heavily processed products that fill store shelves during the season.

### Why the Timing Matters

Thanksgiving shopping is at its peak, and shoppers are paying closer attention to what goes into their food. Debates over additives are growing, and the FDA is reviewing chemicals such as BHA and BHT. The story highlights a tension between holiday indulgence, health-conscious eating, and distrust of processed food marketing.
