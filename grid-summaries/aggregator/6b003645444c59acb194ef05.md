## Defeated Republicans skip votes, angering party leaders

Republican lawmakers who lost reelection or are leaving office are missing votes during the party's last weeks in power, and colleagues are increasingly irritated. Their absences are getting in the way of the GOP's own legislative plans.

### Anger over discipline

Rep. Greg Murphy has voiced open frustration over the no-shows, and his reaction reflects wider worries among party leaders. They are concerned about discipline and accountability among members who no longer face voters. When outgoing lawmakers stay away, the party struggles to hold its ranks together on the measures it most wants to pass.

### Why the timing matters

Power is about to shift, so Republicans have a short window to advance their priorities before they lose leverage. Every vote counts in that effort. The absences also expose internal weakness just when the party hopes to appear in command and competent, making the attendance problem both a practical obstacle and a political embarrassment.
