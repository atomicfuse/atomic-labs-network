## Hundreds of Women Killed in South Africa in Just Three Months

New crime figures from South Africa show that 581 women were killed between July and September. The total includes several high-profile cases around Johannesburg that prompted national outrage.

### The scale of the violence

The statistics reveal how widespread gender-based violence remains in the country, even though murders overall edged down slightly during the period. The number of women killed in only three months underlines the problem, and the cases near Johannesburg drew particular public attention and anger.

### Why the pressure is growing

The data arrives as public fury over femicide is rising again, following this week's killing of five women and a toddler in Free State. South Africa continues to have one of the highest murder and gender-based violence rates in the world. As a result, police and political leaders face mounting pressure to take action.
