## Ex-Secret Service Investigator Warns AI Is Making Scams Harder to Spot

A former Secret Service cyberfraud investigator says artificial intelligence is making scams more convincing, and he argues that Americans need to become more skeptical as fraud grows harder to detect.

### How AI is changing fraud

According to the investigator, AI is improving the quality of familiar tricks. Phishing messages now look more polished, scammers can imitate voices, and fake delivery alerts are easier to produce. His central advice is that people should build habits of doubt and verification, rather than trusting messages or calls at face value.

### Why the concern is rising now

Recent tech outages, holiday shopping, travel, and a surge in package deliveries are creating an ideal moment for scammers and for public anxiety. The financial stakes are also climbing, with cited FBI figures pointing to major losses tied to AI-related scams.
