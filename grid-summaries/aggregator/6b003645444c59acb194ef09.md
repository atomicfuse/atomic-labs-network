## Orange County's Republican Era Gives Way to Democratic Control

Orange County has long stood as a national emblem of suburban conservatism, but one article argues that its Republican identity has effectively collapsed. Democrats have now won across a wide range of contests there.

### How the Shift Happened

According to the piece, Democrats swept key statewide, congressional, legislative, local, and school board races in the county. The article attributes the change to several forces working together: MAGA politics, demographic change, and pressures in the housing market. It also says Republicans denied local realities, which helped turn a once-reliable conservative stronghold blue.

### Why It Matters Now

Because Orange County has symbolized suburban conservatism for decades, its change is presented as a broader warning for Republicans in diverse, high-cost suburban regions. The article arrives amid post-midterm analysis of where MAGA politics helped Democrats consolidate power, and it offers the county as one clear example of that pattern.
