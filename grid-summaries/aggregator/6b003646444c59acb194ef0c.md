## South Korea Catches 111 Suneung Candidates With Unauthorised Smart Devices

South Korea has caught 111 candidates at the Suneung exam carrying unauthorised smart devices, following the introduction of urgent anti-cheating measures. Those steps included nationwide emergency phone alerts.

### A Growing AI Cheating Concern

The article argues that cheating enabled by artificial intelligence is becoming a serious threat to fairness in one of the country's most consequential exams. Anxiety has grown around AI glasses, microcameras, smartphones and other covert tools being used in high-stakes testing, and the new measures reflect that pressure.

### Why the Stakes Are So High

Suneung can shape access to university, careers and social status, so fears about cheating reach well beyond the exam hall. The issue has turned into a national debate about trust, opportunity and the future of exams, with the detection of 111 candidates adding urgency to those questions.
