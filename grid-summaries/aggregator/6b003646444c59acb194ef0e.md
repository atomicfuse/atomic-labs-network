## Tinubu Asks Parliament to Scrap Jail Terms for Attempted Suicide

Nigeria's President Bola Tinubu has formally asked parliament to change the country's approach to attempted suicide, moving it away from the criminal justice system and toward health care.

### What the proposal would change

Under the plan, attempted suicide would be decriminalised. Instead of facing jail terms, people would receive medical treatment, rehabilitation, and support. The request reflects a shift from punishment to mental health care, and it follows years of advocacy by psychiatrists and reform campaigners who pushed for the change.

### Why the timing matters

Nigeria is reconsidering its suicide laws while other African countries, including Ghana and Kenya, move away from criminalisation. The debate also arrives as public attention grows around mental health stigma, access to care, and the way justice systems treat vulnerable people.
