## Georgia Man Charged Over Alleged Crypto Transfers to ISIS-Linked Recipients

Federal authorities have arrested Younes Moustafa Younes of Georgia, accusing him of sending more than $26,000 in cryptocurrency to individuals and groups tied to ISIS. He faces charges of international money laundering and destruction of evidence.

### What prosecutors allege

Prosecutors say Younes used aliases, several accounts and encrypted communications to carry out the transfers. They also claim that before his arrest he tried to destroy phones and documents. The charges rest on those allegations, and the case now moves forward through the legal process.

### Why the case matters

The arrest reflects a growing law-enforcement focus on cryptocurrency as a way to finance terrorism, and on the digital trails investigators follow to trace such payments. It also arrives during heightened scrutiny of online radicalization, encrypted communications and domestic connections to foreign extremist networks.
