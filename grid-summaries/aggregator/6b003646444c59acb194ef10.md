## Young Europeans Walk Out in Coordinated Protest Over Costs and Conflict

Tens of thousands of young people in more than 25 European countries are taking part in a coordinated day of action. Their focus is the rising cost of living and war, and the protests present youth activism as a response that crosses national borders.

### What is happening

Students and other young people are joining strikes in many countries on the same day. The action ties economic pressure to geopolitical instability, treating both as issues that shape everyday life for their generation. The breadth of participation across so many nations marks it as a cross-border effort rather than a series of separate local demonstrations.

### Why it matters now

Inflation, housing costs and war are reshaping daily life across Europe, and young people are emerging as a visible political force. The scale and coordination of the strikes suggest growing frustration with how leaders are handling affordability and security, two concerns now at the center of the protesters' message.
