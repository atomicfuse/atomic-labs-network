## Pistons' Muslim Night Draws Double-Standard Criticism

The Detroit Pistons are set to host American Muslim Night, an event that has drawn a critical article questioning how the team approaches faith-themed programming.

### What the Night Includes

The evening is to feature halal concessions, a prayer space and a shoutout to Senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed. Ticket-linked donations are also part of the plan and may support Gaza-related causes. The article reports on these details as part of its broader argument about the team's event choices.

### The Double-Standard Argument

The piece contends the Pistons apply a double standard, pointing out that the team hosts Muslim and Jewish heritage nights but offers no broader Christian-focused event. It arrives as faith, politics and sports branding increasingly overlap, particularly around Gaza-related advocacy and community heritage celebrations. The debate centers on whether teams are practicing inclusion or selectively elevating certain religious and political identities.
