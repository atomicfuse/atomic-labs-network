## Santa Clara's Next Mayor Wants 49ers Out of Local Politics

Santa Clara's incoming mayor has drawn a clear line between City Hall and the San Francisco 49ers. The mayor says the team "should stay out of city politics," a stance that puts local power and sports influence at the center of the city's governance debate.

### A Pointed Message to the Team

The comments highlight tension over how much sway a professional sports franchise should have in the city where it plays. The 49ers have been politically active in Santa Clara, and the incoming mayor's remarks signal a desire for a firmer separation between the team's interests and the decisions made by city leaders.

### Why the Timing Matters

Cities that host stadiums are increasingly negotiating not only with teams themselves but also with their political spending and public influence. Santa Clara's change in leadership could reshape the city's relationship with one of its most powerful business stakeholders, making the new mayor's early position an important signal for what comes next.
