## US Court Hands Indian National Over 19 Years in Plot Against Sikh Separatist

An Indian national has been sentenced in the United States for his role in a failed murder-for-hire scheme aimed at a prominent Sikh separatist figure.

### The sentence and the case

Nikhil Gupta received a prison term of more than 19 years for the attempted plot targeting Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a Sikh separatist leader. Prosecutors argued that Gupta was directed by a figure linked to the Indian government. India denies that claim, and the sentencing does not change that disagreement over who was behind the effort.

### Why it matters now

The outcome increases scrutiny of alleged transnational repression, a term for efforts to target critics or activists beyond a country's borders. It arrives amid continuing diplomatic tensions involving India, the United States and Canada over Sikh separatist activists living abroad. The case also keeps the Khalistan movement and diaspora politics firmly in the international spotlight.
