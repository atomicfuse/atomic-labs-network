## Shenzhen summit offers the look of peace, critic argues

An article examining Trump's summit in Shenzhen with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin contends that the meeting projected peace and cooperation but produced little of substance. It presents the gathering as one more example of Trump showing deference to U.S. adversaries.

### A pattern of deference

The piece places the summit within a broader habit it says Trump has of treating accommodation toward rivals as a form of peacemaking. It points to escalating Russian aggression and to Chinese efforts to get around American technology controls as developments that make that approach look questionable.

### Why the timing matters

The meeting comes as the war in Ukraine intensifies and as the U.S. and China compete more fiercely over AI and chips. U.S. allies are increasingly anxious about Washington's reliability, while nuclear rhetoric, Taiwan tensions, and great-power diplomacy remain central to global security.
