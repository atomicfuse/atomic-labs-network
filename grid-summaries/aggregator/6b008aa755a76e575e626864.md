## Inside the Factory Where F1 Helmets Are Made by Hand

A look inside Schuberth's Formula 1 helmet factory in northern Italy shows how each carbon-fibre helmet is built by hand. The finished product has to deliver extreme safety, driver comfort and a distinctive visual identity all at once.

### From Leather Caps to Life-Saving Systems

The story traces how head protection has evolved from simple leather caps to modern helmets. Today's designs are engineered to withstand projectiles, fire and crushing forces, while also meeting the specific demands of racing. That progress reflects how far safety thinking has moved in the sport.

### Why It Matters Now

Safety in Formula 1 remains under constant scrutiny as the sport becomes faster, more global and more commercially visible. In that setting, a helmet is no longer just protective gear. It works as a high-tech, life-saving system and also serves as a personal branding tool for elite drivers.
