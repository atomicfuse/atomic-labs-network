## Iraqi Troops Enter Long-Sealed Militia Stronghold in Jurf al-Sakhar

Iraqi forces have entered Jurf al-Sakhar, a stronghold that Kataib Hezbollah had kept closed off for more than 12 years. The move followed a compromise that was reportedly brokered with Iranian involvement.

### What the Entry Signals

The piece argues that the deal lowers immediate fears of civil war in Iraq. At the same time, it says the episode exposes how limited the Iraqi state's ability is to disarm powerful Iran-aligned militias. Entering the area is a notable step, but the way it came about points to the constraints Baghdad still faces.

### Why the Timing Matters

The standoff comes as pressure grows on Iraq to bring militias under state control, following the withdrawal of the U.S.-led coalition and rising regional tensions around Iran's "axis of resistance." Baghdad is trying to assert its sovereignty without setting off an armed confrontation, which makes the outcome in Jurf al-Sakhar especially relevant now.
