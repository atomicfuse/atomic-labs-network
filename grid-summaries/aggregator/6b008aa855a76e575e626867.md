## Most Republican Voters Doubt Midterm Fairness Despite Trump's Early Acceptance

New polling from UC San Diego finds that fewer than half of Republican voters regard the 2026 midterms as "free and fair," even though Trump at first accepted his party's losses.

### What the polling shows

According to the article, distrust in elections remains deeply embedded in the Republican base. That skepticism persists even after the president's initial acceptance of the results, and the article argues it continues to fuel demand for claims of fraud. In other words, a formal concession of defeat has not translated into broad confidence among GOP voters.

### Why it matters now

Democrats' midterm wins, along with Trump's quick return to conspiracy language, have made election trust a live political issue heading into the next cycle. The data suggests election denial may be less visible among winning candidates, yet it still carries real power among voters. That gap could shape how the issue plays out as the next campaign season approaches.
