## Poll Finds Public Trust in Midterm Fairness Has Rebounded

A new University of California San Diego poll shows that about two-thirds of Americans consider the midterm elections "free and fair," a notable improvement over what people expected before voters went to the polls.

### Who Drove the Rebound

The recovery in confidence comes mainly from Democrats and independents, whose views of the election's integrity rose sharply. Republicans, by contrast, showed a modest dip in trust. That decline came as claims of fraud continued to circulate and as debates persisted over whether the federal government or the states should control elections.

### Why It Matters Now

Questions about election legitimacy remain a central fault line in American politics, particularly following Trump-era efforts to reshape voting rules and challenge voting systems. The findings suggest public confidence can recover when elections go smoothly. Still, partisan "winner-loser" effects and fraud narratives continue to influence how much people trust the process.
