## Underdog Promo Offers $50 in Bonus Entries for NBA Cup Games

Underdog is promoting its FOXNEWS promo code for new users, tied to two NBA Cup matchups on Friday's slate: Raptors vs. Pistons and Rockets vs. Nuggets.

The NBA Cup group stage is nearing its decisive stretch, which gives Friday's games added weight for standings and knockout-round positioning.
