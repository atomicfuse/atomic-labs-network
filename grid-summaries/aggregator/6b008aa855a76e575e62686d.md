## Whitmer Wavers on School Tax Credit, Rules Out 2028 Run

Gov. Gretchen Whitmer is hesitating over whether Michigan should join the Trump administration's Education Freedom Tax Credit before she leaves office. She also firmly denied that she will run for president in 2028.

### A Deadline That Lands on Benson's Day One

Michigan has until Jan. 1 to decide whether to opt into the controversial federal school tax credit program. That is the same day Gov.-elect Jocelyn Benson takes office, so Whitmer may leave the choice unmade. The question feeds broader arguments over school choice, public funding for education, and how Democrats should respond to Trump-era federal policy.

### Reflections on Trump and Legacy

Whitmer also looked back on her dealings with Trump and her legacy as governor, and she offered advice to the Democrats who will soon take the reins. Her denial of any presidential ambitions was unambiguous.
