## Police Tell GTA 6 Fans to Keep the Crime in the Game

US police departments are taking to social media with a simple message for players of the newly released GTA 6: do not copy the game's crimes in real life. The warnings arrived as the title's launch drew huge attention.

### A Record-Breaking Launch

GTA 6 arrived after a wait of 13 years, and it has reportedly brought in more than $2.36bn in sales on its first day. The excitement was big enough that some fans skipped work and school to play. The release has turned into a major cultural moment well beyond the gaming world.

### An Old Debate Returns

The game's debut has also revived a long-running argument over violent video games and real-world behavior. Discussion has again turned to crime, guns, and whether violent media can influence the way people act. The police messages have added a fresh, public-facing angle to that ongoing disagreement.
