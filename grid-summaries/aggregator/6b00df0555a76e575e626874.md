## Valdez Breaks With Democrats Over Jeffries Speaker Vote

Rep.-elect Claire Valdez voted against Hakeem Jeffries for House speaker, even though most Democrats supported him in a historic nomination. Her choice has drawn attention to a divide inside the party.

### A Split on the Left

Valdez belongs to a small group of incoming progressive lawmakers aligned with the Democratic Socialists of America. Her vote against Jeffries set her apart from the large majority of her Democratic colleagues and highlighted a gap between party leadership and this new bloc of members.

### Why the Vote Matters

Jeffries' elevation would make him the first Black speaker, which gives the moment added historic weight. At the same time, dissent from the left points to continuing tensions within the Democratic Party. The vote also shows progressives testing how much independence they can assert against establishment leadership.
