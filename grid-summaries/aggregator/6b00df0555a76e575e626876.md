## Nepal's New PM Heads to India for First Foreign Visit

Nepal's new Prime Minister, Balendra Shah, is travelling to India for talks with Narendra Modi. It is his first trip abroad since taking office, and it is seen as his first major diplomatic test after Nepal's Gen Z-driven political shift.

### What is on the table

Shah hopes to reset a relationship shaped by many overlapping issues. These include trade, hydropower, border disputes, air routes and disaster aid. The wider rivalry between India and China hangs over the talks as well, since both countries are competing for influence in Kathmandu.

### Why the timing matters

Nepal is recovering from devastating floods and needs major reconstruction support. At home, Shah faces growing scrutiny over governance and concerns about press freedom. With India and China both seeking influence, his first moves abroad carry added weight for Nepal's direction.
