## Farce, Not Tragedy: An Argument About American Decline

A new piece casts American politics as farce rather than tragedy. It contends that the country's real troubles run deeper than anything happening in Washington or on the campaign trail.

### The Argument

The article holds that America's crisis is rooted in social and civic decay, a problem politics alone cannot repair. Its central claim is that the spectacle of political life pulls attention away from the country's own part in creating its instability. In this view, the show itself becomes a distraction from the underlying condition.

### Why It Resonates

As American politics becomes more polarized and performative, many people are asking whether elections, parties, or institutions can truly address the nation's deeper problems. The article taps into a broader mood of exhaustion with political theater and growing distrust of the idea that politics can serve as salvation.
