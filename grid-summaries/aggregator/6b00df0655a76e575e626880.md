## Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude Helped Prove the Riemann Hypothesis

An Anthropic employee who is also a mathematician says he has used an unreleased version of Claude to produce a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, long counted among mathematics' most famous unsolved problems. The claim has not been verified.

### What is being claimed

The mathematician says the proof runs to roughly 250,000 lines written in Lean, a language used to formally check mathematical arguments. The article describes the claim as potentially historic, but stresses that it remains unconfirmed. It also places the announcement within a growing run of AI companies touting breakthroughs in advanced mathematics.

### Why the timing matters

The claim arrives just after Anthropic's stock market debut, and while AI labs are publicising major scientific and mathematical results. That has fed an ongoing debate over whether AI is genuinely speeding up discovery or turning science into a contest over credibility and marketing.
