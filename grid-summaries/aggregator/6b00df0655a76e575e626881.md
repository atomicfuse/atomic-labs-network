## Trump Defends Putin as a "Friend" but Stops Short of Speaking for America

Donald Trump has defended his personal relationship with Vladimir Putin after being asked whether the Russian leader is a "friend to America." His answer drew criticism and quickly spread online.

### What Trump said

Trump insisted Putin is his friend, praised the way Putin has treated him, and said he "can't speak to America" when it came to the broader question. Critics argued that the response made him appear more sympathetic to Russia than to American concerns, and the exchange became the focus of a wave of backlash.

### Why it matters now

The remarks arrive as Trump's foreign policy stance faces heightened scrutiny following his meetings with Putin and Xi Jinping. They also come against the backdrop of Russia's war in Ukraine and its ties to Iran. The viral reaction shows that Trump's rhetoric on Russia remains a major political flashpoint.
