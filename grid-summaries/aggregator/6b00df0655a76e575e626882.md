## Hundreds of Pets Meant for Cremation Found Dumped Near Manila

Around 170 dead dogs and cats were discovered dumped near Manila, even though their owners believed they had paid to have the animals cremated. The case came to wider attention after the bodies appeared on a livestream, and it has since become a scandal in the Philippines.

### What the investigation found

The article follows rescuers, grieving owners and authorities as they respond to the discovery. Criminal complaints are now being aimed at people linked to pet cremation businesses. For the owners, the news added a second shock to the loss of their companions, since the farewell they had arranged and paid for did not happen as promised.

### Why the case matters now

The scandal comes as "fur parent" culture booms in the Philippines, with pet ownership and end-of-life pet services expanding quickly since the pandemic. The case raises urgent questions about regulation, consumer fraud and animal cruelty laws. It also asks how grieving owners can be confident that memorial services will do what they say.
