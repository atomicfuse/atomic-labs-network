## Fire Destroys Barns at Major US Foie Gras Farm

A large fire at Hudson Valley Foie Gras in upstate New York has destroyed two barns and killed ducks and geese. The farm is counted among the biggest foie gras producers in the country.

### What happened at the farm

The blaze wiped out two barns, and the number of ducks and geese that died has not been established. Hundreds of young birds were rescued from the fire. The incident has also drawn fresh attention to the wider argument over how foie gras is produced, along with the legal fights still under way over efforts to ban it.

### Why it matters now

Foie gras is under growing political and cultural pressure. Voters in Washington DC and Denver have recently backed bans on its sale. Against that backdrop, the fire adds urgency to debates about animal welfare, farming practices, food ethics and the role of local regulation, all of which are drawing increasing public scrutiny.
