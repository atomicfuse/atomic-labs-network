## Iraqi Forces Enter Kataib Hezbollah Stronghold Under Negotiated Deal

Iraqi security forces have deployed in Jurf al-Sakhar, a stronghold of the Iran-backed Kataib Hezbollah militia. The move came through a negotiated arrangement rather than a confrontation with the group.

### What happened

The deployment is intended to reassert state control over the area while avoiding a direct clash with the militia. It follows accusations that Kataib Hezbollah ran a massive oil-smuggling network. The operation also comes amid pressure on Baghdad to rein in militias that operate outside the government's authority.

### Why it matters now

The timing is volatile, as tensions between the United States and Iran spill into Iraq. Washington is pressing Baghdad to disarm militias that have targeted U.S. forces and Gulf facilities. The deployment also tests whether Iraq's government can challenge powerful Iran-aligned groups without provoking armed retaliation.
