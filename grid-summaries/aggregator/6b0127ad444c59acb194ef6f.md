## Cruise lines trade sun loungers for padel courts and Pilates studios

Cruise companies are widening their wellness offerings, and the change is reshaping what a holiday at sea looks like. The focus is moving away from simply lounging and towards more active, personalised self-care.

### What ships are adding

Onboard, the options now stretch well beyond the traditional spa. Larger spa facilities and medi-spa treatments sit alongside Pilates classes, thermal rooms and sports such as pickleball and padel. Wellness has also moved onto dry land, with excursions designed around healing and restoration. The article's central argument is that the old "flop-and-drop" style of cruising is giving way to something more energetic and tailored to the individual traveller.

### Why the shift is happening

Wellness travel is booming, and stressed holidaymakers increasingly want a trip that leaves them restored rather than worn out. Cruise brands in the luxury, premium, budget and expedition categories are racing to meet that demand. Their responses include upgraded spas, fitness programmes and experiences linked to the destinations they visit.
