## Jury Convicts Man of Arson in Deadly Palisades Fire

A Los Angeles jury has found Jonathan Rinderknecht guilty of arson for starting the Palisades fire, one of the deadliest blazes in California's history. The fire killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,800 buildings.

### How the fire began

Prosecutors argued that the disaster started as a brush fire. They said it smouldered underground before reigniting into one of the most destructive wildfires the state has seen. The flames tore across Pacific Palisades and Malibu, leaving widespread devastation in both communities.

### What comes next

Sentencing is set for 17 March. The verdict arrives as California continues to grapple with the human, political and environmental consequences of increasingly catastrophic wildfires. The case raises fresh questions about accountability, emergency response and rebuilding after climate-amplified disasters.
