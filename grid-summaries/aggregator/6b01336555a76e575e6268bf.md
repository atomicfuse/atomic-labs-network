## Trump Order Tells Federal Contractors to Alert White House Before Sharing With Congress

President Trump has signed an executive order directing federal contractors to notify the White House before they hand information to Congress. The order also tells them to hold back material considered confidential.

### An Effort to Control Information

The article presents the order as an escalation in the administration's push to restrict Democratic oversight and manage how information flows out of the executive branch. Contractors would effectively have to route their communications with lawmakers through the White House first, giving the administration a say over what reaches Capitol Hill.

### Oversight Fight Looms in January

Democrats are due to take control of Congress in January and are preparing investigations. Their targets include the administration, Trump's business ties, federal contracts, immigration enforcement, and the Epstein matter. The order could set off a major legal battle over executive privilege, subpoenas, and the scope of Congress' oversight powers.
