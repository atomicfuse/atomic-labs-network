## Missouri Judge Signs Off on Bayer's $7.25bn Roundup Settlement

A Missouri judge has approved a nationwide settlement worth $7.25bn from Bayer, aimed at people who have developed non-Hodgkin lymphoma or may develop it after using Roundup.

### What the deal covers

The agreement comes after years of lawsuits alleging that glyphosate, the active ingredient in Roundup, causes cancer. It applies to Roundup users across the country, including those who have not yet become ill but could in future. Bayer has continued to deny that Roundup causes cancer, even as it moves to resolve the claims.

### Why the timing matters

The judge's approval arrives shortly after a US Supreme Court ruling that strengthened Bayer's legal position on lawsuits over warning labels. Together, the developments keep attention on the long-running dispute over pesticide safety and corporate liability. They also sustain debate over whether federal regulation protects companies from claims brought under state law.
