## Trump Signs Law Ending U.S. Penny Production

President Trump has signed the bipartisan Common Cents Act, formally halting the minting of the penny after nearly 250 years. The law responds to the coin's cost, since each penny takes almost four cents to produce.

### What the Law Changes

Production of new pennies stops, but existing ones remain legal tender and can still be spent. Cash-rounding rules now apply, so businesses begin rounding totals to the nearest five cents. The law also brings changes affecting the nickel. The measure is projected to save tens of millions of dollars each year, and coverage of the move also revisits the penny's long design history.

### Why It Matters Now

The decision turns a long-running argument over waste, inflation, and the role of cash into federal law. Beyond the government savings, it reaches into everyday life, because shoppers paying with cash will start seeing rounded totals at the register.
