## Chad Smith Set to Drum Alongside Wu-Tang Clan at Lions Halftime

Red Hot Chili Peppers drummer Chad Smith will team up with Wu-Tang Clan for the Detroit Lions' Thanksgiving halftime show. The Lions face the Chicago Bears in that game.

### The Thanksgiving Lineup

Smith's appearance is one piece of a wider slate of NFL Thanksgiving entertainment. Miranda Lambert is booked for the Cowboys-Eagles game, while Goo Goo Dolls will perform at Bills-Chiefs. Together, the acts give each of the holiday's games its own musical moment.

### Why It Stands Out

Thanksgiving NFL games are big cultural events on television, and halftime shows are increasingly treated like smaller versions of the Super Bowl stage. Pairing a rock drummer with a hip-hop group adds a cross-genre surprise. Eminem and Paul Rosenberg continue to shape Detroit's holiday showcase, which keeps the Lions' production a notable part of the day.
