## Trump Floats Ban and Possible Tax on Overseas Data Centers

President Trump has threatened to stop American tech companies from building data centers in other countries, according to a new report. He also suggested the firms could face a tax if they go ahead with such projects.

### What was said

The report says Trump raised the idea of blocking U.S. tech companies from constructing data centers abroad and indicated that a tax might apply if they build them anyway. How either step would actually work remains unclear, and no mechanism for a ban or a levy has been laid out.

### Why it matters

Demand for artificial intelligence is driving a worldwide boom in data center construction. Any attempt to limit where American companies can build that infrastructure could reshape how the tech industry expands, influence energy strategy, and affect the competitiveness of the United States. For now, the threat is only a suggestion, and the details will determine its real impact.
