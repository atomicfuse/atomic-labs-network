## Saban Leaves Alabama Out of His Title-Contender Trio

Nick Saban has picked Georgia, Utah and Notre Dame as the college football teams with the "right stuff" to be genuine national championship contenders, and Alabama is not among them.

### Why those three

Saban's case rests on balance. He argues that Georgia, Utah and Notre Dame best combine a strong run game, a reliable defense and good quarterback play. In his view, that mix sets them apart from the rest of the field. He describes Alabama as still dangerous but flawed, suggesting the Crimson Tide lack the full package he looks for in a true champion.

### Why it matters now

The remarks arrive as College Football Playoff rankings and debates over who can win it all grow louder. Alabama remains in the conversation, though questions about its consistency linger. Because Saban made his assessment after leaving coaching, and because he is a legend of the very program he is critiquing, his words challenge the hype around Alabama with added weight.
