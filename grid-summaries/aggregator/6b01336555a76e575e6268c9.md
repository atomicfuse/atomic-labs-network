## Harvard Professor Examines Shifting U.S. Role in World Affairs

Harvard professor Mathias Risse visited the University of Utah to discuss how American foreign policy and human rights commitments have changed from one presidency to the next.

### A Role That Keeps Changing

Risse's talk, as described in the article, portrays America's place in the world as unstable. The country has moved from international leadership to a stance of exceptionalism, then to withdrawal, and now to renewed debate over how much weight human rights should carry in its dealings abroad.

### Why the Talk Resonates Now

With U.S. politics increasingly polarized and foreign policy swinging sharply between administrations, students and citizens are being pushed to understand how global affairs touch daily life. The piece ties current arguments over Trump-era foreign policy, the UN and human rights to the question of what American leadership will look like in the future.
