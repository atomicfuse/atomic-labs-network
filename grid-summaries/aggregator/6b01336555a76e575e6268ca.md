## Mapping the Peru Earthquake: Epicenter and Reach of the Shaking

A new map visualizes an earthquake in Peru, pinpointing where the quake originated and outlining the surrounding region where people felt the shaking. The graphic offers a quick geographic picture of the event.

### What the Map Shows

The piece centers on two key elements: the epicenter, the point on the map where the earthquake began, and the wider area in which shaking was felt. Together they help readers understand how far the tremors extended from their source and which parts of the country were within reach of the event. The map serves as a visual guide rather than a detailed account of effects.

### Why Peru Draws Attention

Peru lies along the Pacific "Ring of Fire," a zone known for intense seismic activity. Because of that setting, earthquakes there prompt immediate interest. Such events can affect communities and infrastructure, and they can place demands on emergency response, which is why a clear picture of where the shaking occurred matters right away.
