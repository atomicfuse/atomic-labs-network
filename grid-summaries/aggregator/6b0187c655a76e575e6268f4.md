## Chicago Mayor Johnson Battles to Keep Place on 2027 Ballot

Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson is working to stay on the 2027 mayoral ballot while election officials review challenges to his nominating signatures. The fight comes as City Hall faces a wider crisis over leadership and finances.

### A Ballot Challenge and Political Pressure

### Turmoil Inside City Hall
