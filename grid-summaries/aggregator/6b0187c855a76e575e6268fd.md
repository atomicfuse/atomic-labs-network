## Mexico Reports Arrest of Cartel Boss Wanted by FBI as Violence Rocks Sinaloa

Mexican officials say they have arrested Fausto Isidro Meza-Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro," a cartel leader who appeared on the FBI's Top 10 most wanted list. The operation took place in Sinaloa.

### Chaos in the streets

News of the operation was accompanied by gun battles, roadblocks and fires across the area. Military helicopters were active overhead, and a baseball game was suspended as civilians took cover. The scenes showed how quickly a security operation can disrupt ordinary daily life.

### Why the arrest matters

Meza-Flores is accused of trafficking fentanyl, methamphetamine, cocaine and other drugs into the United States. His arrest is therefore significant for Mexico's security efforts and for ongoing U.S. debates over drug policy. The violence that followed also highlights how crackdowns on cartels can spill into public spaces where civilians are going about their lives.
