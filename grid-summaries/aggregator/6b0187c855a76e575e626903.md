## Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, Trailblazing South African Leader, Dies at 71

Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, who served as South Africa's first female deputy president and later led U.N. Women as its executive director, has died at the age of 71.

### A Career in Politics and Activism

Mlambo-Ngcuka was active in the struggle against apartheid before rising to senior political office in South Africa. Her time as deputy president marked a historic first for women in the country's leadership. She later took her work to the international stage, where she became a prominent advocate for women's rights and gender equality through her role at the United Nations.

### Tributes Follow

News of her death has brought tributes from South Africa's government, the ANC, the U.N. and U.N. Women. Their messages point to her influence in both liberation politics and global efforts on gender equality. Her passing comes at a time when women's leadership and rights remain central issues around the world.
