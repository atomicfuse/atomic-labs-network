## Denmark Says It Foiled Russian-Linked Plot Against Rapper Kyivstoner

Danish authorities say they stopped a plan tied to Russian intelligence to assassinate Ukrainian American rapper Kyivstoner. The alleged target was the artist's upcoming concert in Copenhagen, and the response has included arrests and a diplomatic expulsion.

### What Denmark Did

Officials say two people described as alleged members of a gang were arrested in connection with the plot. Denmark also expelled Russia's defense attaché, a step that signals the government links the scheme directly to Russian state interests. The accusation is that the plan was meant to be carried out on Danish soil, just before the performance was due to take place.

### Why It Matters

The case emerges at a time of growing tension between Europe and Russia. Officials are presenting it as one example of a wider pattern of Russian hybrid attacks and intimidation on EU territory. That framing places the Copenhagen allegations within a larger European security concern rather than treating them as an isolated incident.
