## Trump Directs Contractors to Clear Congressional Responses With White House

President Trump has signed an executive order that changes how companies doing business with the federal government handle requests from Congress. Contractors and subcontractors must now consult the White House before answering congressional inquiries, and they are told to withhold communications considered privileged.

### Executive privilege and oversight

The order is presented as a way to protect executive privilege. It also puts the administration in a stronger position to push back against oversight from incoming Democrats, who are expected to examine federal contracts and business interests tied to Trump. Contractors would be expected to route their replies through the White House first.

### A looming separation-of-powers clash

Democrats are preparing to take control of Congress and open investigations into federal spending, contractors, and possible conflicts of interest. The order intensifies a coming fight over the limits of congressional oversight and executive privilege. It also raises questions about what private companies must disclose when lawmakers ask for information.
