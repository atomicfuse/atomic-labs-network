## California school board votes to bar transgender girls from girls sports, defying state law

A California school board has approved a policy that bans transgender girls from girls sports, locker rooms, showers, and certain overnight accommodations. The measure directly challenges state law, and the article describes it as a major escalation in the dispute over Title IX, parental rights, and sex-based eligibility in school athletics.

### What the policy covers

The policy goes beyond athletic teams. It also applies to locker rooms, showers, and certain overnight accommodations, extending the restrictions to several parts of school life. By adopting it, the board has set itself against California's legal framework on the issue.

### Why the vote matters now

The decision arrives during intensifying legal and political battles over transgender student policies. California continues to defend protections based on gender identity, while some districts point to federal law and recent court decisions in support of their positions.
