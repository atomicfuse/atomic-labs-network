## Trump Offers Jeffries "Congratulations" Despite Miller Racism Charge

Donald Trump has publicly extended praise toward House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries, even though Jeffries recently attacked Trump adviser Stephen Miller as racist. The remark came during a conversation with Fox News host Kayleigh McEnany.

### What Trump Said

Speaking with McEnany, Trump said he gives Jeffries "a lot of congratulations." The comment arrived after Jeffries criticized Miller in sharply personal terms, labeling him a racist. Trump did not appear to answer the criticism with a matching attack, and instead chose to offer the unexpected acknowledgment of the Democratic leader.

### Why It Stands Out

The exchange comes during intense partisan fighting over immigration, race, and the people closest to Trump. Jeffries and Miller have become prominent symbols within that debate. Against a backdrop of escalating rhetoric between Democrats and MAGA-aligned figures, Trump's words create a notable contrast and have drawn attention for that reason.
