## Republican Leaders Drop Plan for Final Party-Line Spending Bill

Republican leaders are stepping back from pushing one last spending bill through on party lines before Democrats take over Congress. Instead, they are turning toward Democrats to secure votes for military funding.

### A Shrinking Window

Republicans have less than two months left before they lose their congressional majorities. That short lame-duck period has become a major obstacle to advancing priorities aligned with Trump, and it raises the stakes over what can still pass before the handover of power.

### A Shift Toward Bipartisan Talks

The move toward bipartisan negotiations signals how quickly the balance of power in Washington is changing. With a party-line route fading, Republican leaders now appear to need Democratic cooperation to get even core items, such as defense money, across the finish line.
