## Disputed Gaza Strike: Israel Cites Militants, Palestinians Cite Aid Workers

An Israeli military strike in northern Gaza has prompted conflicting accounts of who was killed. Israel says the dead were three Palestinian Islamic Jihad terrorists moving supplies, while Palestinian sources first described them as aid workers.

### Competing Claims Over the Victims

The Israeli military maintains that the three men targeted in the strike belonged to Palestinian Islamic Jihad and were transporting supplies at the time. Palestinian sources initially presented the victims differently, calling them aid workers. World Central Kitchen, the organization named in connection with the incident, denied that the vehicle struck had any affiliation with it.

### Why the Dispute Matters

Aid delivery in Gaza remains one of the most politically sensitive issues of the war, particularly after earlier incidents involving humanitarian groups. This episode lands amid ongoing arguments over protecting civilians, claims that militants use aid infrastructure, and a wider contest over information.
