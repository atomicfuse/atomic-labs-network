## Swimmer Crosses Hormuz Waters to Flag War's Toll on the Sea

British endurance swimmer Lewis Pugh has finished a three-day, 15km swim through the Strait of Hormuz. His aim was to draw attention to the environmental damage caused by conflict involving Iran.

### A Swim With a Message

Pugh argues that the human cost of war rightly stays at the center of attention. He says, however, that marine ecosystems are frequently an overlooked casualty, and he used the long swim to put that issue in front of a wider audience.

### Why the Location Matters

The Strait of Hormuz is both a critical global oil route and a fragile marine habitat, so fighting there raises worries about energy security as well as the environment. Recent attacks on fuel vessels near the area of Pugh's swim underline the immediate risk of spills, fires and long-term contamination of the water.
