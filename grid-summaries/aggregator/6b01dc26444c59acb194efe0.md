## Hackers Reportedly Breach FBI Crime Database in Second Recent Cyber Incident

The FBI is investigating a reported breach of the National Crime Information Center, a core law-enforcement database. Sources describe it as the second major cyberattack involving the bureau in recent months, and investigators are still working out what the intruders reached.

### What the database does

The National Crime Information Center is used to track fugitives and sex offenders, and it also holds records of failed gun background checks, among other information. Because police and public-safety officials rely on it routinely, it sits at the center of everyday law-enforcement work. The extent of any access remains unclear while the investigation continues.

### Why the timing matters

The report arrives amid heightened concern about cyberattacks on critical U.S. systems. It also follows a recent FBI-linked breach attributed to ShinyHunters. With the scope still undetermined, the uncertainty alone raises serious questions about trust in the system and about its security.
