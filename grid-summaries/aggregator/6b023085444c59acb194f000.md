## Virginia Confirms First Measles Death of 2026 in Young Child

A child under 5 has died after testing positive for measles, Virginia's first measles death this year. The state has recorded 220 cases in total, and the death comes as the disease continues to spread across the country.

### A Death Within a National Surge

The article ties the child's death to a broader rise in measles nationwide. The United States has lost its measles elimination status, and most of the cases have occurred among people who were not vaccinated. Virginia's figures sit within that larger pattern, and the loss of a young child underscores how serious the illness can be.

### Why the Timing Matters

The outbreak is intensifying during peak holiday travel and gathering season, which raises concern that the virus could spread faster. Newly issued CDC guidance on the risks measles poses during pregnancy adds further urgency, making the timing of vaccination and the clarity of public health messaging especially important right now.
