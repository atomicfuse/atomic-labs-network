## Trump Pledges to Team Up With Democrats on Credit Card Rate Cap

President Trump says he will work with the incoming Democratic-controlled Congress to limit credit card interest rates. The pledge revives a campaign promise that went nowhere while Republicans held power.

### What the proposal involves

The idea is often described as a 10% cap on interest rates. Supporters say such a limit could save consumers billions of dollars. Banks and credit card companies, however, are expected to fight it fiercely, so any effort will face a serious challenge from the industry.

### Why the timing matters

Credit card debt has reached record levels, with Americans carrying roughly $1.23 trillion and paying median rates above 22%. Democrats are set to take control of Congress, and figures such as Elizabeth Warren back rate caps. That combination gives the policy a clearer path forward than it has had before.
