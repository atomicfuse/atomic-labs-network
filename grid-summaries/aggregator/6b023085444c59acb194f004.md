## Ex-FBI Agent: Altered NCIC Records Could Endanger Officers

A retired FBI supervisory special agent is cautioning that a reported breach of the National Crime Information Center could put police at risk. The concern centers on what might happen if hackers changed criminal records that officers rely on during traffic stops.

### What Investigators Will Examine

The piece lays out what NCIC does and what investigators will be looking for as they assess the reported breach. According to the former agent, the greatest safety worry is not simply stolen data. It is data manipulation, since altered records could mislead officers in the field about the people they encounter.

### Why the Timing Matters

The reported breach follows a string of cyberattacks on FBI systems. It also arrives amid broader warnings that many federal agencies are falling short of cybersecurity requirements. As artificial intelligence speeds up hacking attempts, critical law enforcement databases are becoming higher-stakes targets.
