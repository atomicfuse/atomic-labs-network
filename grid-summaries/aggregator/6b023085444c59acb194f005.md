## Sports academy tied to Alpha School leads Texas voucher payouts

Texas Sports Academy, a sports-focused school linked to the AI-driven Alpha School network, has become the largest recipient so far of money from the state's new voucher program, receiving $6.5 million.

### A rejection, then rapid growth

The article points to a notable tension in Alpha's story. The network was earlier turned down when it sought to run a public charter school. It has since expanded quickly as a private-school operator funded through vouchers, which sends public money to families who choose private options.

### Why the timing matters

Texas launched its voucher program in 2025, and it is already influencing where public education dollars go, including toward AI-based and sports-specialized private schools. The story arrives amid wider debates over school choice, oversight of voucher spending, the use of AI in classrooms, and the public funding of private education.
