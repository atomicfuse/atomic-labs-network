## Jones Says Cowboys Had No Record of Fight Before Fan's Death

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones says the team had "no record" of an alleged fight at AT&T Stadium that police link to the death of fan Mario Narro. The incident followed a home loss to the 49ers.

### Conflicting accounts

Jones' statement stands in contrast to police reports, which say a fight took place before Narro died. The difference between the team's version of events and the account from authorities is central to the story. Meanwhile, Narro's family is working to bring him home so they can make funeral arrangements.

### Why it matters now

The case raises pressing questions about safety at major NFL venues, including surveillance, how incidents are reported, and who is held accountable. It also comes at a time of heightened scrutiny of fan behavior and security at live sporting events, which adds weight to the debate over how stadiums respond when something goes wrong.
