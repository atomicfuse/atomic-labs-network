## Trump Says Cornyn Stalled Judicial Nominee Over Hurt Feelings

President Trump has accused outgoing Sen. John Cornyn of blocking a judicial nominee because his "feelings were hurt." The remark follows Cornyn's criticism of the pick and his statement that he had recommended a candidate of his own.

### What Trump Alleged

Trump said Cornyn's opposition to the nominee came down to personal pique rather than any objection of substance. The accusation came after Cornyn publicly criticized the selection and noted that he had put forward his own recommendation for the judicial post.

### Why the Dispute Matters

The exchange highlights continuing tensions within the Republican Party over who gets nominated to federal courts, how much loyalty Trump expects, and how much sway senators hold over those choices. It also lands at a time when judicial appointments remain a high-stakes political battleground, making any friction over a single nominee politically significant.
