## Cat videos find a big-screen audience at UK cinemas

CatVideoFest, a 75-minute compilation of cat videos, animations and memes, has spread from the US to UK venues, including Newcastle's Star and Shadow Cinema. Audiences are even turning up in cat ears.

### More about the crowd than the clips

The article argues that the appeal lies less in the footage itself and more in the way the event changes how people watch. Clips that would usually be viewed alone online become something shared, with a room of strangers laughing together. That communal, community-driven feel is presented as the real draw, rather than the videos on screen.

### What it says about cinema now

As cinemas look for programming built around experience, CatVideoFest shows online culture being repackaged into real-world gatherings. Its growth in the UK, local charity partnerships and audience dress-up culture all tap into demand for communal, low-stakes joy.
