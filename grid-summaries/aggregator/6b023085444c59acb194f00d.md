## Trump Says He'd Team Up With Democrats on 10% Credit Card Rate Cap

President Trump says he is willing to work with the incoming Democratic Congress on capping credit card interest rates at 10%, bringing back a promise he made during his 2024 campaign.

### What's on the table

The proposal has drawn support from both parties, while the credit card industry opposes it. The article also outlines how much money Americans who carry credit card debt could potentially save if a 10% ceiling were put in place. Trump's willingness to cooperate across the aisle gives the idea a new push after it first surfaced on the campaign trail.

### Why the timing matters

Democrats are set to take control of both chambers of Congress, which makes a rate cap more politically viable than it was under Republican control. Credit card debt has reached a record high, and Americans are paying steep interest rates while concerns about affordability continue to weigh on households.
