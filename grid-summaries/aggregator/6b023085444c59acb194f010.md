## George Washington Hair Lock Fetches $4,000 at Auction

A framed lock of George Washington's hair has sold at auction for $4,000. Its provenance connects it to John Gadsby, who hosted Washington shortly before the first president's death.

### The sale

The framed keepsake changed hands for $4,000, with its documented history tracing back to Gadsby. The article puts the result in the wider market for presidential memorabilia, where other Washington hair has sold for higher prices and signed documents are also traded. The Gadsby link gives this particular lock a specific connection to Washington's final days.

### Why it matters

Historical collectibles keep drawing attention as buyers pay for tangible ties to major figures, especially from the founding era. The sale also reflects a renewed fascination with odd, intimate relics. Such items are a reminder of how people preserved memory before photography became common, and of why physical objects still carry so much weight for collectors today.
