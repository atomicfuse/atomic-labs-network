## France beat Argentina 45-17 to reach Nations Championship final

France secured a place in the Nations Championship final with a 45-17 victory over Argentina in Paris. The bonus-point win was what Les Bleus needed to finish top of the northern hemisphere standings, and it sets up a meeting with South Africa.

### How the match turned

The contest changed when Argentina's Mayco Vivas received a 20-minute red card. France took advantage of the extra man and pulled clear with a series of second-half tries, turning the game into a comfortable victory and sealing the bonus point they were chasing.

### What comes next

France now face South Africa in a high-profile rematch only days after edging the world champions 21-19. The final takes place next Sunday, and the result gives France a significant storyline heading into it, with attention on their momentum, squad depth and credentials as title contenders.
