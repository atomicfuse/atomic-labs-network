## Ohio State's Playoff Hopes Fade After Road Loss at Nebraska

Ohio State's chances of reaching the College Football Playoff were effectively ended by a 31-28 defeat at Nebraska, even though Jeremiah Smith delivered a huge performance.

### A Setback for the Buckeyes

The loss deepens the troubles of an Ohio State season that has continued to unravel since Julian Sayin's injury. Smith's standout effort was not enough to prevent the road defeat, and the result changes the late-season playoff picture. The Buckeyes now face added pressure heading into The Game against Michigan.

For Nebraska, the victory is framed as a major breakthrough for coach Matt Rhule and may mark a turning point for the program. The Cornhuskers can cap their best season in years with a win over Iowa, giving them a chance to finish on a high note after beating one of the sport's biggest names.
