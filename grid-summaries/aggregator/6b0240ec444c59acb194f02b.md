## Jamie and Corinne Foxx Developing Horror Series "Buried" at Fox

Jamie and Corinne Foxx are working on "Buried," a supernatural horror series, as part of an extended first-look deal between their company and Fox Entertainment Studios.

### The Project

"Buried" draws on reported hauntings in a Texas neighborhood built over a historic Black cemetery. It is being developed under the extended first-look agreement held by Foxxhole Productions with Fox Entertainment Studios. Chad and Carey Hayes, the writers behind "The Conjuring," are attached to the series.

### Why It Matters

Horror grounded in real places and buried history continues to perform well, particularly when it connects to underrepresented cultural stories. The extension of the Fox deal also points to a larger effort to build scripted and unscripted projects around Jamie Foxx's creative brand. That arrangement now runs through 2028.
