## Apple's New Siri Remote May Be the Smarter Upgrade

Apple has released a fourth-generation Siri Remote alongside the latest Apple TV 4K. The article examines what the new remote offers and whether owners of older Apple TV models can use it.

### What the remote adds

The headline feature is an upgraded Precision Finding capability, which helps users track down a misplaced remote. The article also highlights the remote's broad compatibility, meaning it is not limited to the newest box. At $59, it costs far less than the $299 Apple TV 4K, and the article argues that many people may be better served by buying only the remote.

### Why the timing matters

Apple is promoting the new Apple TV 4K around Siri AI and smarter home theater features. Some of those upgrades are also coming to older Apple TV 4K models, which weakens the case for replacing the whole device. That leaves many Apple users weighing a simple question: do they really need to upgrade?
