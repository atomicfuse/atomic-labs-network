## Deadly fire tears through Indonesian candy factory, dozens unaccounted for

A huge blaze at the Milkita candy factory in Indonesia's Banten province has left at least three people dead and four injured, while 36 workers remain missing.

### Search continues amid uncertainty

Crews are combing through the wreckage in a rescue effort, with fears growing for workers who may be trapped. The cause of the fire has not been established, leaving open questions about how the disaster unfolded. Families are still waiting for word on their missing relatives as the search goes on.

### A wider safety concern

The tragedy arrives at a time of renewed scrutiny of industrial safety in Indonesia. Deadly factory fires have previously raised concerns about workplace protections and emergency preparedness. Beyond being a breaking emergency, the Milkita fire is now also seen as a labor-safety issue, with attention on how workers are protected on the job.
