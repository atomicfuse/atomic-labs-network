## Watchdog Finds Most Major Federal Agencies Behind on Zero Trust Security

A new Government Accountability Office report says 17 of the 22 major civilian federal agencies have not fully met cybersecurity planning guidance for zero trust architecture. The watchdog warns that this increases the risk of cyberattacks and operational disruptions.

### What the Report Recommends

The GAO issued 40 recommendations to address the shortfalls. Among the agencies flagged are the departments of Health and Human Services, Commerce, Homeland Security, Justice, State and the Treasury. The findings point to uneven progress across the civilian government.

### Why the Delay Stands Out

Federal agencies have been under orders to adopt zero trust since 2021, yet the report suggests implementation is still lagging years later. Government systems face persistent threats from hackers and foreign adversaries, so the gaps could affect public services, sensitive data and national security.
