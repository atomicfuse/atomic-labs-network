## Georgia Rolls Past South Carolina, Eyes Top Playoff Seed

Georgia took a big step toward the No. 1 seed in the College Football Playoff, beating No. 21 South Carolina 34-6 on the road.

### Defense Sets the Tone

The Bulldogs' defense controlled the game from the start, smothering the Gamecocks and holding them to just six points. ### What Comes Next

The playoff race is tightening late in the season, and this win leaves Georgia in prime position to secure the top seed. That makes its upcoming rivalry game against Georgia Tech especially significant, as the matchup now carries major postseason stakes for the Bulldogs.
