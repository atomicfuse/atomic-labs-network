## COP31 Talks Run Into Overtime as Fossil Fuel Wording Divides Nations

Negotiators in Antalya are working past their deadline as countries clash over whether the final climate agreement should name a transition away from fossil fuels. The disagreement now threatens to undo the wider deal.

### What the latest draft says

The most recent text softens its language on fossil fuels and accepts that the world is on course to miss the 1.5C warming goal. It has not settled the dispute, though. Developing nations remain unhappy with how the draft handles money for adaptation, leaving another area of disagreement alongside the argument over fuels.

### Why the stakes are high

If delegates cannot reach consensus, negotiations could be suspended until next year, which would be a rare breakdown in the UN climate process. Climate-vulnerable countries are pressing for stronger action, while fossil fuel producers are resisting wording that might speed the decline of coal, oil and gas.
