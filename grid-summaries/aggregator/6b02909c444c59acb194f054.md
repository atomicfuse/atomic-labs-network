## Holland America Widens Its 2028-2029 Asia Season With Cherry Blossom Voyages

Holland America is growing its 2028-2029 Asia cruise season, with itineraries built around Japan's spring cherry blossoms and calls at five new ports. The line presents the season as a broader and more varied way to see Japan and other Asian destinations by sea.

### What is changing

The expanded season centers on sailings timed to coincide with the spring bloom in Japan, alongside visits to five ports the line has not previously included. Together, the additions are meant to give travelers more choice when exploring Japan and other parts of Asia from the water.

### Why it matters now

Cherry blossom travel remains a bucket-list experience in high demand, and cruise lines are planning further ahead to win travelers booking premium, once-in-a-lifetime trips. The new ports also reflect rising interest in less-crowded, more diverse Asia itineraries that go beyond the usual gateway cities.
