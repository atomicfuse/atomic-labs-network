## Yashar and Likud Teams Meet to Explore Unity Government

Negotiating teams from Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party and Benjamin Netanyahu's Likud have met to discuss a possible unity government. The talks follow the October election, which shifted the balance of power in parliament.

### Openness meets resistance

Yashar has said it is willing to "turn over every stone" in its search for a workable coalition. That stance sits uneasily with Eisenkot's allies, who reject any unity arrangement led by Netanyahu. The gap between Yashar's openness to talks and its partners' firm opposition is the central tension in the negotiations.

### What is at stake

Eisenkot holds the mandate to form Israel's next government. Netanyahu, whose bloc lost its majority, is working to remain politically central. The outcome of these discussions could determine whether Israel moves toward a broad unity coalition or a narrower government built in opposition to Netanyahu.
