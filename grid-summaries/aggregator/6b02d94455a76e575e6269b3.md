## Oath Keepers Founder Stewart Rhodes Turns on Trump After Years of Loyalty

Stewart Rhodes, who founded the Oath Keepers, says he is "absolutely done" with Donald Trump. A new profile traces his sharp turn from devoted supporter to bitter critic.

### From Loyalty to Betrayal

Rhodes was convicted over the Jan. 6 attack and even got a prison tattoo honoring Trump. He now calls the former ally a traitor. His grievances include the absence of a full pardon, reversals on foreign policy, the Epstein files, and what he describes as corruption.

### A Crack in the Base

The story points to a visible fracture within Trump's far-right support base. Post-Jan. 6 politics, pardons, and anti-establishment anger continue to shape the right. Rhodes's case also shows how loyalty built on conspiracy thinking can flip into claims of betrayal when supporters feel abandoned.
