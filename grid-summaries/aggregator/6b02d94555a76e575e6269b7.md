## Daphne du Maurier's grandson opens the family archive

Paul de Zulueta has shared previously unseen family photographs of his grandmother, Daphne du Maurier. He found them while researching a book about the du Maurier artistic dynasty, and they offer a more personal look at the novelist.

### Cornwall, marriage and private letters

The article explores du Maurier's deep connection to Cornwall and her marriage to Tommy Browning. It also describes how her private letters changed her grandson's understanding of her life and work, adding detail to a figure he thought he already knew.

### Why the story resonates now

Du Maurier's novels remain culturally enduring, and *Rebecca* is still widely read and adapted. Interest in literary estates and hidden family archives continues to grow, and the newly released photos arrive amid that curiosity. They present an intimate view of an author often framed as mysterious or reclusive.
