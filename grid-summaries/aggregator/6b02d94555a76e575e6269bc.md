## Texas Turns Blue, and the 2028 Map Shifts

An article argues that Democratic wins across Texas statewide races have changed how the state is viewed. Once dismissed as a "blue mirage," Texas is now presented as a genuine presidential battleground heading into 2028.

### A Potential Historic Map Shift

The piece frames Texas as possibly the biggest change to the electoral map since California became reliably Democratic in the 1990s. In its telling, the sweeping statewide victories mark a real transformation, not a passing blip, and move Texas from long-shot territory into serious contention for the White House.

### Why It Matters Now

Texas holds 40 electoral votes, a cornerstone of Republican presidential calculations. Even making the GOP defend the state could alter spending, strategy, and the route to 270 electoral votes. The timing is significant because 2028 campaigns will soon decide where to build infrastructure and which candidates can credibly compete there.
