## Israeli opposition leaders denounce West Bank settler arson as "Jewish terrorism"

Israeli opposition politicians have condemned overnight arson attacks by settlers on Palestinian villages in the West Bank. Several described the violence as "Jewish terrorism," and the reaction has sharpened political criticism of the government.

### Blame falls on government leaders

Critics are directing growing blame at Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, Itamar Ben-Gvir, and Bezalel Smotrich. They accuse the trio of allowing lawlessness to spread, and they warn that the situation poses security risks for Israel and could bring international consequences. The article presents the dispute as part of a wider argument over how the country is governed.

### Fears of a wider explosion

The attacks come amid warnings that the West Bank may be approaching a broader eruption, with some voicing fears of a third intifada. The row also reflects mounting pressure on Israel over settler violence and its handling of the territory, as well as concern about the country's growing global isolation.
