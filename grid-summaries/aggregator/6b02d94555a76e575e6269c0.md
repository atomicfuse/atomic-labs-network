## Verstappen Wins Chaotic Las Vegas Grand Prix as Russell Crashes Late

Max Verstappen came out on top in a wild Las Vegas Grand Prix, a race defined by repeated lead changes and heavy slipstreaming. The result also carried a personal edge, given his renewed feud with George Russell, who crashed late on.

### Spectacle or "Mario Kart"?

The race has reopened debate over Formula 1's current energy-management rules. Some see thrilling, unpredictable action, while others argue the battery-driven passing feels artificial, likened to the video game Mario Kart.

### Why it matters now

The discussion comes as F1 weighs entertainment against sporting purity. Rule changes planned for 2027, 2028 and 2030 aim to reduce this kind of battery-driven overtaking. Meanwhile, Las Vegas delivered the high-drama, casual-fan-friendly show that F1's global growth strategy relies upon.
