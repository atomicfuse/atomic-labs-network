## Jacks Ruled Out of England's Australia T20 Tour With Broken Hand

England all-rounder Will Jacks will miss the remainder of the T20 tour of Australia after breaking his hand in the opening match in Melbourne. His absence arrives alongside other changes to the touring group.

### Captaincy change for the finale

Tom Banton will lead England in the final T20 of the tour. Harry Brook is leaving early so he can prepare for the Test tour of South Africa, which means the side will be without both Jacks and Brook for the last game.

### Pressure on squad depth

The setback disrupts England's leadership and the balance of the squad during a busy winter schedule. The South Africa Test series begins on 17 December, and with that date approaching, England's player management and strength in depth are facing immediate scrutiny.
