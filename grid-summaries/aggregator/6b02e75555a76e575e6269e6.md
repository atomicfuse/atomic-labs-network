## Frequent short walks beat one long walk for blood sugar control

A randomized trial has found that interrupting long stretches of sitting with several brief, brisk walks improved blood glucose regulation more than a single continuous 45-minute walk covering the same total time.

### What the study found

Participants who spread their walking across the day in short bursts showed better blood sugar regulation than those who did all 45 minutes in one session. The findings suggest that when and how activity is distributed may matter as much as the total amount of exercise for metabolic health.

### Why it matters now

Sedentary workdays and diabetes risk continue to rise, and many people want practical habits that fit into daily routines. The research supports a low-cost, accessible approach: move more often, not just for longer stretches at once.
