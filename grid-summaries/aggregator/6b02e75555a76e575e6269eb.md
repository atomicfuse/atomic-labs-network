## Most Popular TikTok Dementia Advice Falls Short, New Data Shows

A new survey suggests that the majority of widely viewed dementia advice on TikTok cannot be trusted, raising fresh concerns for families looking online for answers.

### What the Data Found

According to the survey data, 66.4% of popular TikTok videos offering dementia advice contain misleading information. Nearly half of the videos were judged potentially harmful. The article behind the findings argues that families should not treat viral clips as a source of guidance. Instead, it says they should check dementia advice with qualified clinicians and trusted medical organizations.

### Why It Matters Now

More people are turning to social media for health information at a time when dementia cases are rising worldwide. Alzheimer's care is also becoming more complex, with new diagnostics and treatments entering the picture. In that setting, inaccurate content can delay diagnosis, stoke fear, waste money, or steer families toward solutions that do not work.
