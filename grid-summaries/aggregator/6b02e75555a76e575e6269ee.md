## Stopping a GLP-1? Study Says Plan Ahead to Keep the Weight Off

A Cleveland Clinic study suggests that people who come off GLP-1 medications tend to hold on to far less of their weight loss than those who switch to another GLP-1 or have bariatric surgery. The accompanying article says stopping drugs such as semaglutide or tirzepatide calls for a long-term medical plan rather than reliance on willpower.

### What the study found

Patients who discontinued GLP-1 treatment maintained noticeably less weight loss than two other groups: those who moved to a different GLP-1 and those who underwent bariatric surgery. The article takes this to mean that quitting these drugs without a structured strategy is a risky approach to keeping weight down.

### Why it matters now

GLP-1 use is growing, and more people are dealing with side effects, lost insurance coverage, shortages, or doubts about whether they can stop. The study frames obesity care as ongoing management, with medication, surgery, nutrition, and clinical support working together.
