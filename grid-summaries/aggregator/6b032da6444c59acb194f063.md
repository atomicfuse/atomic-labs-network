## Eisenkot Calls West Bank Settler Attacks "Jewish Terrorism"

Opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot has condemned a wave of settler violence in the West Bank, using the phrase "Jewish terrorism," according to Reuters. The reported attacks targeted Palestinian villages.

### What Was Reported

Israeli militant settlers attacked Palestinian villages, setting vehicles on fire and spraying racist graffiti. Reuters links the incidents to a broader escalation in settler violence. Far-right ministers Itamar Ben-Gvir and Bezalel Smotrich have faced criticism in this context.

### Why the Timing Matters

Israel is in a fragile government-formation process after Netanyahu lost his majority following the election, leaving the country politically deadlocked. Eisenkot may need Arab party support to govern. The violence also sharpens scrutiny of West Bank settlement policy and Israel's security posture, amid regional instability that has persisted since 2023.
