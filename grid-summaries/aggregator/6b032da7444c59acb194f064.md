## House Progressives Back $25 Minimum Wage in Affordability Push

House progressives are advocating a $25 federal minimum wage as a centerpiece of a broader affordability agenda. Their argument is that a single full-time job should be enough to pay for basic living costs.

### The Proposal and the Pushback

Supporters present the higher wage floor as a direct answer to the squeeze many households feel. Conservative economists, however, have warned that such a mandate could drive up prices, put pressure on businesses, and lead to higher unemployment. The article sets the progressive plan against those concerns, highlighting a sharp divide over how best to help workers.

### Why the Debate Matters Now

Affordability is emerging as a central political battleground as Democrats shape their economic message ahead of the next Congress. The discussion draws on voter frustration over wages and inflation, and it raises a larger question: whether government mandates help or hurt working-class households.
