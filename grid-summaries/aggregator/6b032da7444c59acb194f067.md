## Lincolnshire farmer says rural crime is "out of control" after field damage

A Lincolnshire farmer has spoken out after suspected hare coursers drove across his fields, leaving deep ruts in land that was being readied for a major farming event.

### What happened

The farmer says the vehicles tore up ground prepared for The Cereals 2027, an agricultural event expected to attract thousands of visitors. He described rural crime as out of control. Police regard hare coursing as a serious rural and wildlife crime, one that is often tied to wider offending, intimidation and damage to land.

### Why it matters

The incident feeds growing worry in rural communities about repeat offending and deterrents seen as weak. Farmers also face the financial burden of repairing the damage themselves. The timing adds pressure, since the affected land was being prepared for one of the sector's key gatherings, which is expected to draw large crowds.
