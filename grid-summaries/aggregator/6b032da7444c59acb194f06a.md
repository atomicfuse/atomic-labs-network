## South Korea's Education Minister Resigns Amid AI Exam Cheating Scare

South Korea has been gripped by panic over AI-assisted cheating on its university entrance exam, and the fallout has now cost Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin his job.

### A confession sets off a crackdown

The alarm began with a confession describing the use of hidden cameras, smartphones and AI-generated answers. In response, authorities introduced extreme anti-cheating measures for the Suneung, the national university entrance exam. A total of 111 suspected violations were reported, and public criticism of the handling of the situation continued to grow.

### Why the case matters beyond Korea

As AI tools become easier to access and harder to detect, institutions running high-stakes tests are being pushed to rethink security, fairness and trust. South Korea's intense exam culture makes it a closely watched example of how a system reacts when AI collides with life-defining exams.
