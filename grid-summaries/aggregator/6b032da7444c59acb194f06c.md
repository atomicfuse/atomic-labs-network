### A Trip Through Roadside Americana

The article connects every stop to the nostalgia and roadside culture that made the route famous. It points readers toward the kinds of places that defined the golden age of American road trips, including vintage diners, neon motels and small towns that interstates later bypassed. ### Why the Centennial Matters

Route 66's 100th anniversary is fueling renewed interest in slow travel and Americana. The story reflects a wider cultural appetite for nostalgic, experiential road trips, in which the drive and the stops along the way matter as much as the destination.
