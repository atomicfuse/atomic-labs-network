## Laura Brand pledges to follow prison rules after row over message

Laura Brand has said she will "honour" the rules of HMP Littlehey after criticism of her reading out a prison message from Russell Brand in which he claimed innocence.

### The clip and the response

The row has centred on the recorded message and efforts by the government to have it taken down. Google has refused to remove the clip. The episode has renewed debate over the distress such messages may cause victims, the influence celebrities hold, and the safeguards the media should apply when sharing them.

### Why the debate matters now

Russell Brand's conviction and imprisonment have prompted fresh scrutiny of how famous men, their online platforms and their supporters handle accountability after convictions for sexual violence. The dispute also raises timely questions about how social media is moderated, what prisoners are allowed to communicate, and how victims' rights should be weighed against public messaging.
