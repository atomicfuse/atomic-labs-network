## Freshman Democratic senators face an early leadership test

A large class of newly elected Democratic senators is being drawn into a leadership contest between Chuck Schumer and Chris Van Hollen. The article presents the vote as a measure of whether candidates who ran on change will confront established party power once they arrive in Washington.

### What is at stake

Democrats have just won a new Senate majority, and a secret-ballot leadership vote is set for Dec. 1. The outcome could shape how the party approaches Donald Trump, responds to progressive demands, and handles internal reform. Because the ballot is secret, each senator's choice will not be openly recorded.

### Why the newcomers matter

The timing is especially charged because many of the freshman senators won in red or swing states by promising independence. Their decisions in this first major internal contest may show how far that pledge extends now that they are inside the Senate and part of its leadership structure.
