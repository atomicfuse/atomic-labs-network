## Burnham calls new EU deal a "landmark" for growth

Andy Burnham is due to tell MPs that his new deal with the EU is a landmark growth measure. He says it could add £17bn to the UK economy and reset Britain's post-Brexit course.

### What Burnham is promising

Burnham will present the agreement as a way to improve the country's prospects over the coming decade. The case he makes centres on economic growth, and he frames the deal as a turning point after years of post-Brexit trade-offs. The £17bn figure is the headline claim he will put to Parliament.

### Criticism from rivals

The proposal has drawn sharp attacks from Kemi Badenoch, Nigel Farage and former prime ministers. Their objections focus on sovereignty, regulation and the deal's economic claims. The row comes as Europe returns to the centre of political debate, with growth, food prices and opportunities for young people all weighed against Brexit's costs. Party leaders are using major business and parliamentary moments to stake out their positions before the next phase of UK-EU negotiations.
