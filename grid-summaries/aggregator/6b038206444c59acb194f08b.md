## Cornyn Confirms Heated Floor Clash With Cruz Over Judicial Pick

Sen. John Cornyn has acknowledged a heated exchange on the Senate floor with fellow Texas Republican Sen. Ted Cruz. The row concerns T. Elliot Gaiser, a judicial nominee put forward by President Trump. Cornyn also took a jab at his colleague, describing Cruz as "a fragile thing."

### The blue slip dispute

At the center of the argument is Cornyn's decision not to return a blue slip for Gaiser. He has pointed to concerns that the nominee is not from Texas and has no trial experience. The blue slip is the tool that lets home-state senators weigh in on judicial nominees from their states.

### Why it matters now

The clash puts a public rift between two Texas Republicans on display while Trump's judicial appointments and Senate confirmation norms face scrutiny. It also underscores a continuing struggle over blue slips, home-state influence, and who gets to shape the federal courts.
