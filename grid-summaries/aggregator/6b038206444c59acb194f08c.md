## Boy, 7, Rescued After 14 Hours Under Rubble Near Kyiv

A seven-year-old boy has been pulled alive from the ruins of a residential building near Kyiv, rescuers say, after a Russian strike that killed six people and injured others.

### The rescue effort

The boy spent 14 hours beneath the rubble before emergency teams reached him. Rescue operations at the site have continued, and other strikes were also reported across the Kyiv region. Renewed air raid alerts have added to the danger for emergency crews, who are working in an area that could be hit again while they search the damaged building.

### A wider pattern of attacks

The strike came as Russia keeps up widespread drone and missile attacks across Ukraine, with civilians and residential areas again bearing much of the war's toll. Multiple regions have been hit, pointing to a broader escalation. The boy's rescue offers a human focal point within that larger picture, set against the deaths and injuries from the same attack.
