## Democrats Win Biggest House Majority in More Than 30 Years

Democrats have secured 258 House seats, giving them their largest majority since 1992. They flipped 44 seats and defeated Republicans in several key races. The article presents the outcome as both a mandate to govern and a significant test of what the party can accomplish.

### A Test of Delivery

The win comes with pressure to act on affordability, healthcare, immigration, and oversight of President Trump. Democrats now have to show they can turn a large majority into results on the issues that shaped the campaign, rather than simply celebrating the numbers.

### Divided Government, High Expectations

Democrats control Congress while a Republican president remains in the White House, a rare arrangement. Voters cited inflation, economic anxiety, and corruption concerns, so expectations are high for swift action and visible accountability from the new majority.
