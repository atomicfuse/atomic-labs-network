## COP31 Climate Talks End Without a New Deal

Negotiations at the COP31 climate summit have collapsed, with countries unable to reach agreement. The breakdown came amid deep divisions over the role of fossil fuels, leaving the gathering without a new deal.

### What Happened

According to the report, the talks fell apart after delegations failed to find common ground. Fossil fuels proved the central point of contention, with countries sharply split on how to approach them. No agreement emerged from the summit, and the outcome is described as a collapse of the negotiations rather than a modest or partial result.

### Why It Matters Now

Climate diplomacy is increasingly focused on whether nations should phase down fossil fuels and how quickly they should do so. Against that backdrop, a failed summit points to growing geopolitical friction at a critical moment for emissions targets. The lack of a deal underscores how contested the question of fossil fuels has become among the countries involved in the talks.
