## Editorial faults Chikli for invoking Aner Shapira's death against his mother

An editorial condemns Diaspora Affairs Minister Amichai Chikli for his treatment of MK Shira Shapira, whose son Aner Shapira is regarded as a hero of October 7. The piece says the line was crossed when Chikli sent her a post that used her son's death against her political choices.

### The argument

The editorial accepts that elected officials are legitimate targets for criticism. It insists, however, that bereavement and the memory of fallen soldiers must never be turned into political ammunition. In its view, Chikli's message did exactly that.

### Why it matters now

Israeli political discourse remains deeply fractured amid the continuing trauma of October 7, bereavement, the plight of hostage families, and debates over coalition-building with Arab factions. The piece reflects a wider call for leaders to model restraint, responsibility and civic decency at a moment of national exhaustion.
