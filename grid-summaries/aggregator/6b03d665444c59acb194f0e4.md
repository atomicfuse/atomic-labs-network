## Ball State Program Supports Indiana Teachers as Civics Becomes a Political Flashpoint

Ball State University's CREATE250 program is helping Indiana educators build stronger civic education, even as the teaching of U.S. history faces growing political pressure.

### What the program offers

CREATE250 gives Indiana teachers primary documents, ideas for classroom projects, and professional support. The aim is to deepen how students learn about civics and American history. The article also looks at how civics instruction has become contested, with disputes over so-called "patriotic education," race, history, and what students should be taught about the country.

### Why the stakes are high

Indiana now requires a sixth-grade civics course, while national and state politics continue to reshape how history and citizenship are taught. Low youth voter turnout and declining civic knowledge add urgency, and culture-war pressures make the classroom a high-stakes arena. Together, these factors place programs like CREATE250 at the center of a broader argument over civic learning.
