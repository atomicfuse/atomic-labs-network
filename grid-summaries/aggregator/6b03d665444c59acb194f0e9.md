## Presidential turkeys check in to a Washington luxury hotel

Two Thanksgiving turkeys have arrived at the Willard InterContinental in Washington, ahead of President Donald Trump's yearly White House turkey pardon. The birds were greeted with red carpet photos and the kind of treatment usually saved for hotel guests of a different sort.

### A naming vote and a farm origin

Americans are being asked to vote on names for the pair, which turns the pardon run-up into a public contest. The birds come from a farm in West Virginia, a detail that gives the story a regional touch alongside its ceremonial one. Their stay at the hotel is part of the buildup to the event at the White House.

### Why the tradition draws attention

The pardon has a long history as a presidential custom, and it returns during Thanksgiving week, when seasonal rituals and political pageantry tend to get notice. It offers a light, easily shared moment at a time when heavier news often dominates the day's headlines.
