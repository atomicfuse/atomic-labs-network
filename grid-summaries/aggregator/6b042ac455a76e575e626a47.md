## Ukraine says naval drones sank a Russian corvette near Libya

Ukraine reports that it has carried out a strike far from the main battlefield, using naval drones against two Russian vessels off the coast of Libya. The attack is being presented as a notable widening of Kyiv's campaign.

### What Ukraine says it hit

According to Ukraine, its naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury and also struck the transport ship Angara. The article describes the operation as a major expansion of Ukraine's efforts to target Russian naval logistics, reaching well beyond the Black Sea where such actions have been concentrated.

### Why it matters

The strike suggests Ukraine is taking the war into new theaters and going after Russian supply routes across the Mediterranean. It also brings diplomatic fallout in Libya and raises questions about how vulnerable Russian military assets may be around the world.
