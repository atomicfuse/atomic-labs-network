## Right-Wing Anger Grows Over Haredi Draft Talks With Eisenkot

Frustration is building inside Israel's right-wing bloc after haredi parties began negotiating an IDF draft-law deal with Gadi Eisenkot's camp. A senior right-wing official has warned that the discussions could signal the end of the long-standing alliance between the religious and right-wing camps.

### The Source of the Anger

Right-wing figures are upset that the haredi parties, traditionally their partners, are talking with Eisenkot's camp about how to handle military conscription. The senior official described the talks as potentially marking a collapse of the alliance that has long defined this political bloc.

### Why the Timing Matters

Israel is still working to form a government after the October 28 election, and haredi conscription has become a central obstacle to building a coalition. The dispute reveals a significant fault line within Netanyahu's traditional bloc, at a moment when political loyalty could decide what the next government looks like.
