## Kennedy Downplays 2028 White House Bid but Won't Close the Door

Sen. John Kennedy says a presidential run in 2028 is "not likely," though he has not ruled it out entirely. His comments arrive as political attention begins turning toward the next national race.

### What Kennedy Said

Kennedy described a 2028 presidential campaign as not likely, yet he continues to leave open the possibility of changing course. He indicated he might reconsider if he grows frustrated with the Senate or with the Republican field of candidates. For now, his stated plan is to seek re-election to the Senate in 2028 rather than launch a bid for the presidency.

### Why It Matters Now

Donald Trump is constitutionally barred from serving another term, so both parties are already positioning themselves for the 2028 presidential contest. Kennedy's remarks add another name to the growing speculation about who might shape the next Republican primary, even as he points toward staying in the Senate.
