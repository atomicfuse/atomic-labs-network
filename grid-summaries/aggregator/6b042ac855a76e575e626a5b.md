## Belgian Strike Grounds Hundreds of Flights Over Austerity Plans

Hundreds of flights were cancelled and public transport was disrupted across Belgium as unions held a major strike against planned austerity measures.

### What the strike targets

The action is aimed at budget talks led by Prime Minister Bart De Wever. Unions oppose proposed cuts to pensions, healthcare, holidays and social protections, and they used the walkout to show the strength of that opposition. The impact reached beyond air travel, affecting public transport as well.

### Why it matters now

Belgium is caught in a tense budget standoff. The government is trying to reduce its borrowing costs while facing resistance from unions and from coalition partners. The strike shows how quickly austerity debates can turn into public disruption, with effects on travel, schools and daily services. For many people in Belgium, the budget argument is now being felt directly in their everyday routines.
