## Joe Milton Turns to His Daughter's Name to Settle Deep Throws

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton has shared an unusual trick for staying calm before launching a deep pass. He says his daughter's name, Maleia, to himself in those moments.

### The Habit Behind the Throw

Milton explained that repeating Maleia's name reminds him to "show a little love," which helps him avoid putting too much power on the ball. The routine came up in the context of a missed deep touchdown attempt to DeVonta Smith during Philadelphia's 19-16 win over Pittsburgh.

### Why the Spotlight Is on Him

With Jalen Hurts sidelined, Milton has stepped into the starting quarterback role, so details about his development and performance now carry more weight. The Eagles sit at 3-7 but remain technically alive in the NFC East race, which makes Milton's poise and growth a genuine storyline.
