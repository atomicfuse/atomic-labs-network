## Trump to Speak at a Justice Department Critics Say He Has Remade

President Trump is set to deliver a speech at the Justice Department, an agency that critics argue has been reshaped to advance his political agenda and target his adversaries. The appearance arrives amid sharp debate over how the department now operates.

### Concerns Over Independence

Critics point to an erosion of the department's independence from the White House. They also cite politically charged prosecutions and say the agency has been bent toward pursuing the president's opponents. Firings inside the department and cases against political rivals that ultimately failed are central to that criticism.

### Scrutiny on the Horizon

The speech comes as Democrats prepare to investigate how the Trump administration has handled the Justice Department, following Republican losses in the midterms. That oversight will land within a wider argument over whether law enforcement institutions are being used as tools of political retaliation.
