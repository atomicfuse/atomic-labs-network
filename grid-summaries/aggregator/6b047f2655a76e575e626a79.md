## Universal Healthcare Moves to the Center of the Democratic Agenda

Proposals once dismissed as politically unrealistic, including Medicare for All, are now central to Democratic planning as the party prepares to control Congress in 2027.

### What Democrats Are Proposing

The article outlines a range of ideas now gaining traction. Democrats want to reverse cuts to Medicaid and restore the tax credits under the Affordable Care Act. They also aim to lower the age at which people become eligible for Medicare, add dental, vision and hearing benefits, and expand health coverage for children. Medicare for All, once seen as a long shot, is part of this broader shift in the conversation.

### Why the Fight Matters Now

Healthcare affordability was a major concern for voters in the midterms, leaving Democrats under pressure to turn campaign promises into legislation. Obstacles remain, since Trump still holds veto power and the Senate filibuster could come into play. The coming battle could shape the next phase of U.S. healthcare politics.
