## CNN's Harry Enten Says Trump Risks Sliding Into Irrelevance

CNN analyst Harry Enten contends that Donald Trump may be drifting toward the outcome he dreads most: no longer being the center of attention. His case rests on several signals of fading influence.

### The Evidence Enten Cites

Enten points to a drop in Google searches for Trump, soft approval ratings, and doubts within the Republican Party about his agenda. He also draws comparisons to earlier second-term presidents who grew unpopular, suggesting Trump's position fits a familiar historical pattern rather than standing apart from it.

### Why the Timing Matters

The analysis arrives soon after a Republican midterm wipeout, which has raised questions about how much political leverage Trump holds over the next two years. It also reflects a wider change in how he is viewed, from a figure who constantly commanded attention to a possibly weakened lame-duck president whose pull may be diminishing.
