## US Army reschedules detonation of toxic explosives at Iowa plant

The US Army plans to detonate five tonnes of degraded lead azide at an Iowa ammunition plant. The move comes despite lawsuits, public concern and questions about environmental safeguards.

### The Army's case and the objections

Army officials argue the degraded material poses an immediate risk of spontaneous explosion, which they say justifies acting now. Environmental groups see it differently. They accuse officials of bypassing legal review and of failing to be transparent about the plan. Lawsuits have been filed, and questions remain over whether adequate protections for the environment are in place.

### Why the timing matters

The detonation is imminent and is linked to freezing weather, which officials say could raise the chance of an uncontrolled blast. The decision also arrives during heightened public scrutiny of toxic exposure, environmental justice, military accountability and the use of emergency government powers, so the outcome is likely to be watched closely.
