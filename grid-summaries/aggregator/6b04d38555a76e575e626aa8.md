## COP31 in Antalya Ends Without a Deal, a First in Seventeen Years

Climate negotiators left Antalya without a final agreement, the first COP failure in seventeen years. Disputes over fossil fuels, electrification and adaptation finance proved impossible to bridge, and the consensus-based format of the talks added to the deadlock.

### What went wrong

Atlantic Council experts point to several fault lines: disagreement over how to treat fossil fuels, differing views on electrification, and arguments about money for adaptation. They also stress the limits of a system that requires everyone to agree before anything can be decided.

### What comes next

The same experts argue that Paris Agreement obligations still stand despite the collapse. They suggest the COP process may need to better reflect economic realities and allow more flexible decision-making. The failure raises questions about whether climate diplomacy can keep pace with energy security concerns, industrial competition and geopolitical fragmentation, while countries face pressure to turn earlier pledges into concrete action.
