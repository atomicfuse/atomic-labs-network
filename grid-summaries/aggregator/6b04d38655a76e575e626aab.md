## Neeley Marks Exit From Flint's Top Job With Farewell Luncheon

Flint Mayor Sheldon Neeley hosted a farewell luncheon as his time in office draws to a close. He lost his re-election bid to Councilwoman Tonya Burns and used the occasion to look back on his years leading the city.

### Reflecting on a Legacy

Neeley described his tenure as a period that moved Flint "from crisis to recovery," presenting that shift as the core of what he leaves behind. His remarks at the event centered on how he hopes his time as mayor will be understood as the city heads toward a new chapter.

### A Handover Ahead

Burns is set to take office Dec. 1, which puts questions of continuity, public trust and the city's direction in focus. Neeley said he expects the transition to go smoothly. His comments also speak to wider themes of governing after a crisis and of serving the public after an electoral defeat.
