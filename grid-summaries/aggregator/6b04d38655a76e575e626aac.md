## VAR upgrade sends off Tarkowski in first Premier League red

James Tarkowski has been shown the first red card of his Premier League career, and the decision has left David Moyes with a frustrating night. The central defender was originally booked, but a VAR review changed the outcome.

### The challenge and the reaction

The yellow card was upgraded to red because of a forceful follow-through on Brentford's Yehor Yarmoliuk. Pundits and fans have been split. Some see serious foul play that justified the punishment, while others view it as another case of VAR overreach on a challenge that looked like a hard tackle in real time.

### Why the debate matters

VAR remains one of the Premier League's biggest weekly flashpoints, particularly when slow-motion replays turn robust tackles into sending-off offences. The call also feeds into wider discussion about player safety and consistency, and about whether Tarkowski's past challenges influenced the decision.
