## Nvidia Soars Over 30% as Huang Returns to CEO Role

Nvidia's stock jumped by more than 30% after co-founder Jensen Huang was reinstated as chief executive. He had briefly resigned amid concerns tied to misconduct and national security. The move lifted the wider tech market.

### Rally Leaves Questions Unanswered

Despite the strong market reaction, several issues remain open. Uncertainty continues around regulation and corporate governance at the company, and there are reports of scrutiny from the SEC. Investors cheered the return, but the underlying concerns have not gone away.

### Why Nvidia's Leadership Moves Markets

Nvidia sits at the center of the AI boom, so instability in its leadership can sway the entire tech sector. The rebound arrives while investors are anxious about AI regulation and accounting concerns. Political pressure for stronger oversight is also adding to the tension surrounding the industry.
