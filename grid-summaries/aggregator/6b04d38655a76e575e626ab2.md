## UK Sanctions Myanmar Military Leaders After Genocide Ruling

The UK has announced sanctions on senior Myanmar military leaders following an International Court of Justice ruling that found Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017.

### Further UK Measures

Beyond the sanctions, the UK is taking several other steps. It is blocking supplies of aviation fuel, expelling Myanmar's defence attaché, and raising the issue at the UN Security Council. Together, these moves signal a broader diplomatic and economic response to the court's finding, rather than a single punitive measure aimed at individuals.

### Why It Matters Now

The ICJ ruling gives renewed legal and diplomatic weight to years of Rohingya demands for accountability. It also lands at a time when the situation in Myanmar remains volatile. Ongoing air strikes, continuing violence, and problems with humanitarian access mean this is not only a case about a historic atrocity but a live international crisis that still demands attention from governments and international bodies.
