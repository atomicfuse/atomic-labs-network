## US Moves to Blacklist Singapore AI Firm Megaspeed Over Chip Concerns

The US Commerce Department plans to add Megaspeed, a Singapore-based AI data centre company, to its blacklist. Officials suspect the firm violated export controls involving Nvidia chips and China.

### The Nvidia scandal behind the move

The planned blacklisting is tied to a wider scandal around Nvidia. That controversy involves alleged espionage, relationships between executives, and national security worries about advanced AI hardware. Megaspeed's suspected export violations place the company within that broader dispute over where Nvidia's chips end up.

### Why it matters now

AI chips have become a geopolitical flashpoint, as Washington tries to keep China from reaching cutting-edge computing power. The scandal also lands at a delicate time for Nvidia, one of AI's most influential companies. Leadership turmoil, stock volatility, and government scrutiny are all converging around the firm at once.
