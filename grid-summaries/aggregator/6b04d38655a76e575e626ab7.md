## Fox News Host Adds His Voice to Conservative Pushback on GTA VI

Fox News host Joey Jones has become the latest right-leaning commentator to criticise *Grand Theft Auto VI*, as the game's release stirs political debate.

### What Jones said

Jones argued that the game is a waste of time, that it "emasculates" men, and that it could be harmful to young Americans. His remarks are presented as part of a wider split on the right. Some critics lean on the older moral panic over violence and sex in games, while others focus on newer complaints about "wokeness."

### Why it matters now

*GTA VI* is dominating both culture and commerce after a record-setting launch, which has made it a flashpoint for political commentary and generational anxiety. The reaction shows how quickly major entertainment releases can be drawn into culture-war narratives, sometimes almost as soon as they arrive.
