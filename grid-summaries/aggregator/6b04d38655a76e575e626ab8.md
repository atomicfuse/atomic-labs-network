## Nursing Student and Mother Arrested After Man Found Stabbed in Ravine

Police in California have arrested 22-year-old nursing student Alexia Nieves Solache and her mother, Veronica Rangel, in connection with the death of Marcelino Perez Jimenez. His body was discovered in a ravine near Livermore, and he had been stabbed to death.

### What Police Allege

Investigators say Jimenez was killed inside an apartment in San Jose and that his body was later moved to the ravine where it was found. The arrests came months after the discovery, marking a delayed development in the investigation. Many details remain unclear, including the motive and the nature of the relationship between those involved.

### Competing Account and Open Questions

Rangel has said her daughter acted in self-defense, offering a different account from the one police have described. The case is still developing, and questions remain about what led to the killing and the alleged effort to conceal it. The arrests have drawn attention because family members are involved and because the breakthrough took months.
