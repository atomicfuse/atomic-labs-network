## Trump pauses Iowa lead azide detonation plan pending court and governor-elect approval

President Trump has ordered the military to hold off on a planned controlled detonation of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. The blast can go ahead only if both a federal appeals court and Gov.-elect Rob Sand give their approval.

### The legal and safety dispute

The order comes amid a legal fight over the plan. The Army argues that a controlled detonation is safer than risking an uncontrolled explosion, because the explosives have become unstable. Residents and environmental groups have raised concerns about the plan and its possible effects.

### Why the timing matters

The issue is urgent because of the material's instability. Local residents and environmental groups fear lead-contaminated dust and health risks. The dispute brings together military safety, environmental regulation and political accountability, making it a high-stakes controversy at the state level.
