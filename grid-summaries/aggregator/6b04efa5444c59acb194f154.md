## Report Says William Weighs Title Limits for Non-Working Royals

A new report claims Prince William is considering a significant restructuring of the monarchy. Under the idea, royal titles and HRH styles would be kept for working royals only.

### What the Proposal Could Mean

According to the article, the change could remove titles from Prince Harry, Meghan, and their children, Archie and Lilibet. It would also give William's own younger children a clearer route toward private lives, rather than expecting them to take on public roles as they grow older.

### Why the Timing Matters

The report arrives as public scrutiny of royal costs, privilege, and relevance is growing, particularly around non-working royals and scandals linked to the wider family. It also comes amid continuing tension between William and Harry, and wider questions about how the monarchy should modernize for the next generation.
