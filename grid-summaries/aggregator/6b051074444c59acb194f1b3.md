## Zosia Mamet Identifies Mad Men Creator as Boss Behind Her Early Exit

Zosia Mamet has publicly named Matthew Weiner, creator of Mad Men, as the "horrible" boss she says led her to leave the acclaimed series after just four episodes.

### What She Alleges

Mamet describes a set culture driven by fear. She says threatening comments were made during a table read, and she recalls being dressed down in front of others during a rehearsal, an experience she describes as humiliating. Her account places responsibility for the atmosphere on Weiner.

### Why It Resurfaces Now

The naming arrives as scrutiny of toxic workplace behavior in Hollywood continues. It also draws fresh attention to Mamet's 2025 essay collection, in which she described the experience without identifying anyone. Her account invites a new look at a prestige-TV classic through the lens of behind-the-scenes power dynamics.
