## Devin Ratray Says He's Unaware of Any "Home Alone" Sequel but Would Gladly Play Buzz Again

Devin Ratray, the actor who played Buzz in "Home Alone," says nobody has told him anything about a newly reported sequel. According to TMZ, he is open to coming back to the role if asked.

### Left Out of the Loop

Ratray told TMZ that he has heard nothing about the project. The report adds that Christopher Columbus, who directed the original film, is also said to be uninformed about the reported sequel. Neither man has apparently been brought into any discussions so far.

### Why Fans Are Watching

The timing of the sequel talk is notable, since "Home Alone" remains a holiday favorite and Christmas-season viewing is picking up. Fans are curious whether familiar faces from the original cast, such as Ratray and Macaulay Culkin, might be part of any new film. For now, Ratray's willingness to return is the main detail on the table.
