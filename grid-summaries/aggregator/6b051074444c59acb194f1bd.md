## Antoine Fuqua's Team Rejects Civil Claim of Armed Bar Confrontation

Representatives for director Antoine Fuqua have denied a civil complaint accusing him and his brother of assaulting a Hollywood bar manager with handguns. The filing says the confrontation followed the refusal of entry to Fuqua's daughter.

### The Allegations and the Response

The complaint alleges assault and battery and asks for $50,000 in damages. Fuqua's representatives say the claims are not true. Law enforcement reportedly investigated the incident, which dates to February, and took no further action.

### Why the Timing Matters

The case emerges while Fuqua enjoys major commercial success with *Michael*, which has reportedly passed $1 billion worldwide and is set to get a sequel. The claims also touch on wider concerns in Hollywood, including celebrity conduct, security at nightlife venues, allegations involving weapons, and the reputational risks that come with such disputes.
