## Hooters Origin Story Film Starts Shooting in Tampa Bay With Joyner, Clark and Trullinger

Hooters: The Movie has begun filming in the Tampa Bay area. The project tells the story of how the Hooters restaurant chain was founded, with Owen Patrick Joyner, Kyle Clark and Audrey Trullinger in the lead roles.

### Cast and Approach

Alongside its three leads, the film features a wider ensemble cast. Current and former Hooters Girls are also involved in the production. The movie aims to present the brand's early days as a nostalgic comedy-drama, driven largely by the personalities behind the company.

### Why It Matters Now

Films about the origins of well-known brands and nostalgia-heavy biopics remain a strong Hollywood trend, particularly when they involve familiar consumer names. Hooters carries plenty of cultural weight, which makes the project timely for conversations about retro Americana, workplace image and gender. It also raises the question of how controversial brands choose to retell their own histories.
