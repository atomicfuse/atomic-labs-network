## Only Murders in the Building Heads Home to New York for Season 7

After a trip abroad in Season 6, the hit mystery series is expected to bring its trio back to New York for its seventh outing.

### What We Know

Season 6 takes the central trio to London, but Season 7 is expected to return to New York. Showrunner John Hoffman and stars Steve Martin and Martin Short stressed that the series remains deeply tied to the city. They also left the door open for future travel, so the show has not ruled out more trips away from home.

### Why It Matters Now

Season 6 premieres December 8, which makes talk about the show's future and setting especially timely. The London season widens the world of the series and raises a question for fans: will Only Murders become a globe-trotting mystery franchise, or will it settle back into its Arconia roots?
