## Yellow Dress at Miley Cyrus's Black-Themed Wedding Was an Honest Mistake, Guest's Sister Says

A guest who wore a bright yellow dress to Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando's all-black wedding has become a talking point online, and her sister has now explained what happened.

### A Dress Code Mishap, Not a Statement

According to the guest's sister, the bright outfit was a genuine mix-up over the dress code rather than a deliberate choice to stand out. The sister said the guest simply got it wrong, and the clarification came after the moment spread widely on social media and prompted plenty of chatter.

### How Miley Responded

The sister also said Miley handled the situation graciously. Rather than making things uncomfortable, she reportedly complimented the guest on her dress. The episode has fed ongoing online debate about wedding dress codes, public shaming, and how much a guest's outfit really matters, especially when a small fashion choice is framed as breaking the rules.
