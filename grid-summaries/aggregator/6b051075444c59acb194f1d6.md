## Billie Jean King Reflects on Chris Evert's Health Battle

Billie Jean King has spoken about her long friendship with fellow tennis great Chris Evert, who is facing a third ovarian cancer diagnosis. King also tied her comments to her new documentary, *Give Me the Ball!*.

### A Friendship Tested

King said Evert has been through hell and back during her health struggles. She spoke of their bond, built over many decades, and of the support the wider tennis community has offered Evert as she confronts the diagnosis again.

### Why the Story Resonates

Evert's ongoing fight draws fresh attention to ovarian cancer awareness and survivorship, and to the emotional strength that lasting friendships can provide. The conversation also arrives as women's sports icons are increasingly honored through documentaries and legacy storytelling, a trend King's new film is part of.
