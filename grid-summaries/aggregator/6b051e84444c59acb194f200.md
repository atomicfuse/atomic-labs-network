## Wolbachia mosquito release cuts childhood dengue, Zika and chikungunya infections in Brazil

A school-based trial in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, has found that releasing mosquitoes carrying the wMel strain of Wolbachia sharply lowered infections spread by Aedes aegypti.

### What the trial found

Researchers report that releasing Wolbachia-infected Aedes aegypti reduced dengue, Zika and chikungunya infections among children by 68.6%. The study argues that Wolbachia could serve as a sustainable and scalable tool for preventing diseases transmitted by mosquitoes, offering a method that can be extended to more places.

### Why the timing matters

Brazil recently endured its largest recorded dengue outbreak, which fits into a growing global dengue burden linked to climate, urbanization and the spread of mosquitoes. Public health systems urgently need options beyond insecticides and source reduction, and these results arrive at a moment when that search has become pressing.
