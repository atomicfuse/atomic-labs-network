## Scientists Decode Taxol's Full Recipe and Grow It in Tobacco

Researchers have mapped the complete biosynthetic pathway for Taxol, a major chemotherapy drug. They also showed that tobacco plants can be engineered to produce the medicine.

### What the researchers found

The team uncovered every step in the natural process that builds Taxol, effectively writing out its full recipe. Using that knowledge, they modified tobacco plants so the plants could make the drug. The result could offer a manufacturing route that is cheaper, faster, and easier to scale than the current approaches of extracting it from plants or converting related compounds through chemistry.

### Why it matters now

Cancer diagnoses are rising worldwide, while access to affordable chemotherapy remains uneven, particularly in low-income regions. A dependable biological production pathway for Taxol could lower costs and widen the availability of treatment for patients who currently struggle to obtain it.
