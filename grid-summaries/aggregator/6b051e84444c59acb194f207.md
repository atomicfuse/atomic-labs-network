## Cryo-EM Maps the Command Center Inside Poxviruses

A study in Nature has used cryo-electron tomography to reveal the three-dimensional architecture of the vaccinia virus core. The work shows how poxviruses start an infection from a protected "headquarters" before they release their DNA.

### Inside the viral core

The researchers describe dormant, paired RNA polymerases within the core, along with flower-shaped export portals. Together, these structures help explain how the virus organizes its machinery for launching infection while keeping that machinery shielded from the outside environment.

### Why it matters now

Mpox clusters and other poxvirus outbreaks keep these viruses relevant for public health. Vaccinia is also being engineered for biomedical uses, including cancer therapy. Understanding how the core's machinery works could point to new antiviral targets and to safer strategies for engineering the virus.
