## Personalized music tied to calmer patients during heart angiography

A randomized clinical trial has found that personalized music therapy, played before and during coronary angiography, was linked to better readings on several measures. The article behind the story suggests that continuous music may ease stress and make this invasive heart test easier for patients to bear.

### What the trial found

Patients who received personalized music therapy showed lower systolic blood pressure and a lower heart rate. They also reported less pain, and their troponin levels were lower. The article argues that playing music throughout the procedure, not just beforehand, may help reduce stress during invasive heart testing.

### Why it matters now

Healthcare systems are looking for low-cost, non-drug ways to improve the patient experience and cut down on sedation use, which gives music therapy fresh relevance in clinical settings. The study adds cardiovascular evidence to a practice already used in cancer care, psychiatry, and other areas.
