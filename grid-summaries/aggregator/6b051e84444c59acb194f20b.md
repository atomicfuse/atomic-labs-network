## Putting a Number on "Food Noise": New Inventory Measures Intrusive Food Thoughts

Researchers have developed a way to measure "food noise," the unwanted, intrusive rumination about food that is distinct from physical hunger. The tool is called the RAID-FN Inventory.

### What the tool does

The RAID-FN Inventory is a validated questionnaire designed to capture two things: how often food-related thoughts occur and how disruptive they are. Framing food noise as a persistent preoccupation rather than a primal urge to eat gives clinicians and scientists a clearer target for study.

### Why it matters now

The concept has gained wide attention alongside GLP-1 weight-loss drugs, with many patients saying the mental chatter around food has quieted for the first time. A clinical measure could influence obesity care, eating disorder research, studies of food insecurity, and nutrition support that takes cultural context into account.
