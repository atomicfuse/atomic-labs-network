## Hairy 99-Million-Year-Old Snail Gets Named for The Prodigy's Keith Flint

Researchers have identified a new species of land snail preserved in Myanmar amber, dating to about 99 million years ago. It has been named *Archaeospirostoma flinti*, a nod to the late lead singer of The Prodigy.

### Why the Name Fits

The snail's shell is covered in fine hairs, and the scientists say they resemble Keith Flint's iconic hairstyle. That visual echo inspired the tribute. The pop-culture link gives a fossil find a hook that can reach readers well beyond the usual science audience, making paleontology feel more approachable and easier to share.

### Scanning Without Damage

The study also shows how micro-CT scanning can reveal fragile fossil details without harming the specimen. This imaging technology lets researchers see tiny, delicate fossils in three dimensions, and it is helping bring renewed momentum to the study of ancient amber. Finds like this one show how much detail can be recovered from specimens once thought too small or fragile to examine closely.
