## Parents Embrace Intestinal Ultrasound for Children With IBD

A study in *JPGN Reports* finds that parents of children with inflammatory bowel disease strongly accept intestinal ultrasound as a way to monitor their child's condition. Approval was especially high among families who had already experienced the scan.

### What Parents Valued

Parents described intestinal ultrasound as fast and reassuring. They also appreciated that it is noninvasive and said it was less uncomfortable for their children than cross-sectional imaging. Those who had gone through the procedure were the most positive, suggesting that firsthand familiarity strengthens acceptance of the approach.

### Why It Matters Now

Pediatric IBD care is increasingly focused on cutting back burdensome testing while keeping families engaged in ongoing monitoring. The findings support wider use of point-of-care ultrasound as a family-friendly alternative in routine care, offering a way to track disease that parents are comfortable with and that fits this shift in priorities.
