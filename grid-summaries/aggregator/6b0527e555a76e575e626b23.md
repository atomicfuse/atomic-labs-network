## Cockroach Janta Party Leader Says Delhi Airport Blocked Oxford Trip

Saurav Das, a leader of India's youth-led Cockroach Janta Party, says he was prevented from boarding a flight to the UK. He was due to attend events at the Oxford Union and the university, and says his passport was "flagged."

### What happened at the airport

According to the report, Das was stopped at Delhi airport before he could travel for the Oxford engagements. He attributes this to his passport being flagged. The article places the incident within broader worries about freedom of expression, youth activism, and the way activists and journalists can be barred from international travel.

### Why it matters now

The episode comes as Gen Z-led political movements gain visibility in India and as scrutiny grows over how dissenting voices are treated. Leaders of the Cockroach Janta Party have recently faced detentions, arrests, and limits on protests linked to allegations about electoral irregularities.
