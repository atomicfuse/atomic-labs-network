## CFP Committee Faces Another Chance to Fix Its Inconsistencies

An opinion piece argues that the College Football Playoff selection committee has applied uneven standards and now has a fresh opportunity to correct course. A new top-25 ranking is about to be released, and the piece treats it as a test of the committee's approach.

### The Indiana and Alabama Contrast

The article says the committee has penalized Indiana because of weak results from the Hoosiers' opponents. It argues that Alabama has been shielded even though its résumé has suffered similar damage. The author frames this as evidence of a perceived bias toward the SEC and big-name programs, suggesting reputation may be outweighing what teams have actually accomplished on the field.

### Why the Timing Matters

Playoff positioning is tightening, and strength of schedule and ranked wins are drawing closer scrutiny. The piece also reflects growing fan frustration over how transparent the committee is, how much influence the media has, and whether the playoff field is decided by merit or by brand.
