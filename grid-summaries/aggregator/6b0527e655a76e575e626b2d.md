## Unsealed Warrant Papers Describe Decades-Long Probe Tied to Omar Home Search

Newly unsealed warrant documents connected to a federal raid on Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington, D.C. home are heavily redacted but offer a first glimpse of what prosecutors say they are examining. They describe an investigation that spans decades and involves alleged immigration-related offenses.

### What the documents show

Prosecutors portrayed the inquiry as long-running, with the allegations centered on immigration matters. Much of the text remains blacked out. The release followed a legal fight over whether the papers should become public, and the filings also address the items that were seized during the search. Omar has denied any wrongdoing.

### Why it matters now

The search of a sitting member of Congress's home is unprecedented, and it has sharpened scrutiny of federal investigative power. Claims of political targeting under the Trump administration add to the debate, which comes amid heightened public interest in immigration enforcement, Justice Department transparency, and election-year political investigations.
