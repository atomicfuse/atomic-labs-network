## Newsom Fires Back at Trump Over Death Row Commutations and Pardons

California Governor Gavin Newsom has answered Donald Trump's criticism of the state's decision to commute 548 death row sentences to life in prison. Newsom says the president is misrepresenting what California did.

### Newsom's Argument

Newsom contends that Trump is distorting the commutations and, in doing so, drawing attention to what the governor calls a "pay-to-play" approach to presidential pardons. In his telling, the president's own use of clemency deserves far more scrutiny than California's decision to spare people from execution.

### Why the Clash Matters

The exchange brings together capital punishment, clemency, and the power of presidential pardons in a highly visible political fight. Trump and Newsom are sharpening their national contrasts on justice, and the dispute also touches wider worries that the legal system treats people unequally depending on race, wealth, and political access.
