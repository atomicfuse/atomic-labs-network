## COP31 Ends Without a Deal as Global Order Frays

The collapse of COP31 without a consensus agreement is read in one article as more than the work of petrostate obstruction. It points instead to a wider breakdown in the global order, with war, failed climate finance and North-South distrust all playing a part.

### Progress Beyond the Negotiating Room

The article stresses that climate action has not stopped just because formal talks stalled. Work continues through finance programs, electrification efforts and the expansion of regional policy. These tracks show that implementation can move forward even when diplomats cannot agree on a common text, though they do not replace a functioning multilateral process.

### A Warning Sign for UN Climate Diplomacy

The failure came during energy shocks, rising military spending and declining trust in multilateral institutions. That backdrop makes diplomacy harder just when implementation is most urgent. The article treats the summit as a warning sign for the UN climate process, but argues it is not a reason to abandon it.
