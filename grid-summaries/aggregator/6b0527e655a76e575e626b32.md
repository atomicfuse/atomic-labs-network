## Judge Unseals Heavily Redacted Records in Probe Involving Rep. Ilhan Omar

A federal judge has made public a set of court records tied to a possible criminal case and search warrant involving Rep. Ilhan Omar. Much of the material remains blacked out, but it offers a view of how prosecutors proceeded.

### What the records show

The documents indicate that prosecutors weighed how to move forward while accounting for the constitutional protections that apply to members of Congress. The article describes the investigation as legally delicate and politically charged. Omar has denied any wrongdoing and has called the probe an act of retaliation.

### Why the timing matters

The release comes as the Justice Department faces close scrutiny over its investigations of critics of President Trump, alongside a wider anti-immigration push by the administration. Omar is a prominent progressive, a Muslim and a Somali-born lawmaker, which has made her case a flashpoint in debates over political prosecution, immigration enforcement and congressional immunity.
