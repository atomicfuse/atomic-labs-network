## Outdoor recreation adds $704 billion to the U.S. economy

New data from the Bureau of Economic Analysis shows outdoor recreation contributed $704.2 billion to the American economy in 2025. Fishing, boating, hunting, shooting and trapping were named as major drivers of that total.

### More than a pastime

The report makes the case that these activities are pillars of the economy rather than simply hobbies. They support jobs, sustain rural businesses, back conservation work and help keep local economies running, according to the analysis behind the figures.

### Why the numbers land now

The industry has kept growing despite inflation and changing consumer habits, which gives the new totals added weight. They arrive during ongoing debates over conservation funding, land access and the future of rural communities. For creators, the data offers a timely way to discuss the real economic importance of hunting, fishing and public lands.
