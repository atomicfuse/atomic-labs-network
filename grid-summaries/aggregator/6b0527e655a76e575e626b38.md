## California Voters Say No to Billionaire Wealth Tax

California voters have turned down Proposition 40, a first-of-its-kind one-time wealth tax aimed at billionaires. The measure was meant to pay for healthcare, food assistance, and education.

### Why the Measure Failed

According to the article, several factors sank the proposal. Many voters felt uncertain about how it would work, while Democrats were divided over it. Opposition funded by billionaires also played a role, and there were worries that wealthy residents might leave the state if the tax passed.

### What It Means for Other States

With federal funding cuts looming and economic anxiety running high, states are looking for ways to support public services without leaning on middle-class voters. The result in California shows that even in a liberal state, "tax the rich" ideas can falter when their design seems risky or unfamiliar to voters.
