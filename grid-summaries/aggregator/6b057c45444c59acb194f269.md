## Pope Francis' Posthumous Diary Describes Three Sightings of the Devil

Newly published extracts from Pope Francis' private diary reveal a side of his inner life rarely seen by the public. In the entries, he writes that he saw the devil three times, and also describes seeing "the gates of hell."

### Reflections on the Chile Abuse Scandal

The diary also contains his thoughts on the clerical abuse scandal in Chile. Francis writes with regret that he underestimated how severe the scandal was, a piece of self-criticism that adds to the personal tone of the extracts.

### Why the Release Matters

This is the first papal diary made public since the 1960s, giving readers a rare look at a pope's private spirituality, fears and self-assessment. Its publication comes after Francis' death, which has heightened public interest in his legacy and in the hidden inner lives of modern popes.
