## Triple killer pleads guilty to murdering Ian Huntley in prison

Anthony Russell, who is already serving a life sentence for three murders, has admitted killing Soham child-killer Ian Huntley inside HMP Frankland. He had first denied murder before changing his plea at Newcastle Crown Court.

### The attack and the plea

The article describes the assault as planned, carried out with a metal bar. It also sets out earlier tensions between the two inmates. Russell's change of plea means the case now moves to sentencing, which is due.

### Why it matters now

The case is unfolding in court at present and has revived public attention on Huntley, one of Britain's most notorious child killers, as well as on violence inside high-security prisons. It also raises questions about how inmates are managed, the position of so-called natural lifers, and how prisons deal with conflicts between extremely dangerous offenders.
