## West Virginia Farmer Readies Two Turkeys for Presidential Pardon

Scott Rightsell, a turkey farmer from West Virginia, has spent recent weeks preparing Freedom and Feathers, the pair of birds chosen for President Trump's Thanksgiving turkey pardon.

### From Training to the White House

The article follows Rightsell as he trains the birds for their big moment, covering their preparation, their debut at the White House, and their arrival at a luxury hotel ahead of the ceremony. Their journey is framed as part of a long-running holiday tradition that turns a farm-raised pair into temporary celebrities.

### Why It Draws Attention

The pardon arrives just before Thanksgiving, mixing tradition, politics, agriculture, and lighthearted media spectacle. This year Trump hosts the ceremony on the South Lawn helipad, and West Virginia supplies the birds. After the pardon, Freedom and Feathers are set to retire at Texas A&M.
