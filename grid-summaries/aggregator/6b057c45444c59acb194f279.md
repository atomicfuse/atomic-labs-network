## Meta's Muse AI agent arrives in the UK, useful but awkward

A reviewer spent a week testing Muse, the new AI agent Meta has launched in the UK, and came away with mixed feelings. It proved genuinely handy for some tasks, yet the experience was not entirely smooth.

### A helpful assistant with rough edges

The review found Muse valuable for life admin, the everyday chores that eat up time. However, it also felt awkward and at times intrusive, and its usefulness was limited by the access it had to other apps. The reviewer argues that Muse's success hinges on whether people will trust Meta with deeply personal data.

### Why the stakes are rising

AI agents are shifting from chatbots that simply answer questions to tools that act on a user's behalf. That makes privacy, autonomy and the cost of mistakes far more serious. Meta is positioning Muse as a possible mass-market assistant just as UK regulators and consumers are growing warier of AI and of Big Tech's data practices.
