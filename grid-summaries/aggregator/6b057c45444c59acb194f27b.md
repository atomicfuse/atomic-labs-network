## California Man Charged Over Alleged Online Threats Against ICE Agents

Leonard Michael Fierro of California has been arrested and charged with interstate threats, according to the Department of Justice. Prosecutors allege he posted online calls for armed attacks on ICE agents and government facilities.

### What investigators say they found

Prosecutors say investigators recovered weapons, ammunition, and tactical gear connected to the case. They also report finding planning materials and videos that, in their account, suggest an intent to target ICE operations. The charge stems from the alleged online posts and the material discovered during the investigation.

### Why the case matters now

The case arrives amid heightened tensions over immigration enforcement, political violence, and online radicalization. It also underscores how federal authorities are treating violent threats against law enforcement as criminal conduct rather than protected speech. The charge is an allegation, and the case against Fierro is being pursued by federal prosecutors.
