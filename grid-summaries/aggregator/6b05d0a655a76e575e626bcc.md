## Jesse Watters Draws Ridicule After Outlining an Impeachment Case Against Trump

Fox News host Jesse Watters has been mocked online after a warning about Democratic plans ended up sounding like a blueprint for impeachment.

### What Watters said

Watters cautioned that Democrats might treat Trump's intervention in the Nvidia and Jensen Huang controversy as grounds for impeachment. In trying to portray the attacks coming from Democrats, he laid out the argument in terms that many viewers found clear and persuasive, which led to a wave of mockery on social media.

### Why it matters now

The moment arrives as scrutiny grows over Trump's relationships with big tech, foreign influence, national security, and access for donors. It also illustrates how pro-Trump media personalities can sometimes end up strengthening the strongest arguments against the president while attempting to defend him or dismiss his critics.
