## Gen Z Daters Weigh Partner Potential Over Present Status, Hinge Report Suggests

A new Hinge report suggests young daters may care more about who a partner could become than who they are today when they think about marriage. A Gen Z panel discussed the findings, and the conversation also turned to gaps in young people's knowledge of Thanksgiving history.

### Potential Over Present

The report points to a mindset in which long-term goals, self-improvement language and "growth potential" shape how young people approach relationships. Rather than judging a partner only by their current standing, some daters appear willing to "mold" someone toward a future they envision together, according to the panel's discussion of the findings.

### A Wider Debate

The topic arrives amid a broader cultural conversation about maturity and education. At the heart of it is a question about whether partners should be accepted exactly as they are or encouraged to change over time. The panel's remarks on Thanksgiving history added to that wider discussion of what young people know.
