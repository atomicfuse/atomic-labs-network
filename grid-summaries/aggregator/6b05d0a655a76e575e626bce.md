## Raskin Widens Probe Into Trump Jr.'s Venture Firm

Rep. Jamie Raskin is expanding a House Democratic investigation into 1789 Capital, the venture firm tied to Donald Trump Jr. The move centers on whether political connections played a role in the firm's business dealings.

### What the expanded probe seeks

Raskin is requesting records from four companies that received investments from 1789 Capital before they benefited from federal contracts, approvals, or policy shifts. The central question is whether Trump Jr. used political access or insider knowledge for financial gain. Both Trump Jr. and the firm deny that.

### Why the timing matters

Democrats are preparing aggressive oversight of the Trump administration as they take control of Congress, with corruption and conflicts of interest at the center of their agenda. The probe also arrives as the administration is trying to limit how federal contractors respond to congressional inquiries, a effort that could bear on how companies handle requests like these.
