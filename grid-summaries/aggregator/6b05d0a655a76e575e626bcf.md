## Cubs reportedly land Japanese slugger Teruaki Sato on six-year, $120 million deal

The Chicago Cubs are reportedly signing Japanese slugger Teruaki Sato to a six-year contract worth $120 million. The deal adds a powerful left-handed bat to a lineup already led by NL MVP Pete Crow-Armstrong.

### What the deal brings

Sato arrives with a reputation for dominant production in Japan. He is expected to play in Chicago's corner outfield, giving the lineup another source of power from the left side. The signing also reflects the Cubs' aggressive spending this offseason.

### Why it matters now

The move suggests the Cubs are pushing hard to contend after an 89-win season that ended in a Wild Card sweep. It also extends a growing pattern in MLB, where Japanese stars are landing major contracts with big-market teams.
