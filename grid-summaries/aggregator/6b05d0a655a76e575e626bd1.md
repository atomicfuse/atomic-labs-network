## Apple Narrows AI Agent Access on Macs With macOS 27.3

Apple's upcoming macOS 27.3 brings security changes meant to limit how much access AI agents and other apps have to Mac files, folders and system controls. The article urges users to look at their privacy settings before the update arrives.

### Old Approvals Stay Active

According to the article, permissions granted in the past will not disappear when macOS 27.3 installs. Any app or agent that already has access will keep it. That is why the piece recommends reviewing existing approvals now, rather than assuming the update will tidy them up automatically.

### Why the Timing Matters

AI agents are growing more capable of acting across several apps at once. That makes sweeping permissions, such as Full Disk Access, riskier than before. Recent concerns about AI apps and sensitive data have also pushed privacy controls into the spotlight, giving the new restrictions added relevance for everyday Mac users.
