## Draft Smithsonian Overhaul Plan Reportedly Glimpsed on Staffer's Laptop

A commuter reportedly photographed a White House staffer's laptop screen showing a draft titled "Smithsonian Institution Renewal Plan." The document appears connected to the Trump administration's wider effort to confront what it describes as "ideological capture" at the Smithsonian.

### What the draft covers

According to the report, the plan is aimed at reshaping the National Museum of American History. Its areas of focus include the museum's leadership, its exhibits, its curriculum and its diversity guidelines. The draft is described as a work in progress, and the report does not say whether it has been finalized or adopted.

### Why it matters now

The Smithsonian has become a flashpoint in the culture-war fight over how American history is taught and displayed. The administration has applied pressure through reports, hearings and executive orders. The leaked draft offers a concrete look at how political power could influence museum leadership, public history and education.
