## Recovery Commander Recounts Five-Day Search After JFK Jr. Crash

Captain W. Russell Webster and Theresa M. Webster have shared their account of the search and recovery effort that followed John F. Kennedy Jr.'s plane crash. Their book, "The Last Flight," covers the mission from start to finish.

### Inside the Five-Day Mission

The Websters describe the operational challenges that shaped the search, which ran for five days. Their account also covers the specific requests the Kennedy family made during the recovery. The article presents the effort as a procedural, firsthand narrative, focusing on how the work was carried out rather than on the spectacle that surrounded it.
