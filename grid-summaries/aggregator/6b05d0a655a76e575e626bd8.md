## Disney's *Hexed* Earns Praise as a Colorful Witch Adventure

A new review celebrates *Hexed*, Disney's animated tale of witches, as a vibrant family fantasy. The critic compares it to *Turning Red* for its emotional mother-daughter story and to *Harry Potter* for its magical world, while noting it avoids the problematic politics of that older franchise.

### What the Review Highlights

The review points to the film's bright visuals and its emotional core. Themes of self-love, empathy and generational trauma run through the story, alongside a message about fitting in without losing yourself. The writer sees these ideas as giving the magical setting real heart.

### Why It Matters Now

*Hexed* arrives over Thanksgiving, giving families a fantasy option when audiences are eager for magical worlds free of the cultural baggage tied to older franchises. Its focus on identity, parental pressure and acceptance should resonate with younger viewers and their parents alike.
