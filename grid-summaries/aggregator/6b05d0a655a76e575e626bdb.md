## Remains on Oregon coast identified as former Newport councilor lost in plane crash

Human remains found on the Oregon coast have been identified as Ralph H. Busby III, a former Newport city councilor. He disappeared in September after his small plane crashed into the Pacific Ocean.

### The crash and the search

Busby's plane went down in the Pacific in September, and he was reported missing afterward. Search efforts followed, and the article describes the conditions that shaped the recovery of his remains along the coast. The identification ends a missing-person case that stretched across several months.

### Why it matters now

The finding brings closure to a local coastal community affected by the loss, and it highlights Busby's civic legacy in Newport. It also arrives amid continued public attention on small-plane safety, coastal search-and-rescue operations, and the challenges that weather can pose to recovery efforts.
