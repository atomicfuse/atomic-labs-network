## Pakistani Astronaut Set to Make History Aboard China's Tiangong Station

China's Shenzhou 24 mission will carry Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to the Tiangong space station. He will be the first foreign national to board the orbiting outpost.

### A Scientific and Diplomatic Milestone

The mission is presented as a notable scientific achievement, but also as a political signal. It points to China's expanding space diplomacy, with a foreign astronaut joining a station that Beijing operates and controls.

### Why the Timing Matters

The launch arrives as China works to establish Tiangong as an international research platform and an alternative to the US-led space order. For Pakistan, the flight is a historic moment of national pride and a significant step made possible by its close partnership with Beijing.
