## Utah Makes Its Midterm Results Official, Ending a Decades-Long GOP Supermajority

Utah has formally certified the results of its 2026 midterm elections, locking in an outcome with major political significance for the state.

### What the certification confirms

With the results now official, Republicans no longer hold a supermajority in the Utah House of Representatives. That marks the first time in decades the party has been without one in the chamber. The certification settles the count and confirms the shift as a historic change in the state's legislative makeup.

### Why the timing matters

The certification arrives while the country is engaged in disputes over voting access, mail-in ballots, and federal scrutiny of state election systems. Utah stands out in that debate because more than 90% of its voters cast ballots by mail. That makes the state an important case study in how elections are run and administered, particularly when mail voting is under such close national attention.
