## Players Counter Salary Cap Push With Revenue Sharing Offer

An opinion piece argues that Major League Baseball players have challenged commissioner Rob Manfred's position by answering owners' calls for a salary cap with a different idea. MLB appears headed toward a lockout, and the coming labor fight could affect the 2027 season.

### The players' counter

According to the article, players responded to the owners' salary-cap push by agreeing to broader revenue sharing. The writer sees this as a way of testing ownership's stated reasoning. If the real concern were competitive balance, the argument goes, sharing revenue more widely would address it without capping what players earn.

### What it reveals about ownership

The writer contends that the response exposes the owners' true aim: moving money from players to owners rather than fixing competitive balance. The timing matters because owners have framed their salary-cap proposals around competitive balance and gaps in media revenue. The next collective bargaining agreement is at stake as a likely lockout nears.
