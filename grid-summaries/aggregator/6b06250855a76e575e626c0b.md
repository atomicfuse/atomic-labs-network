## Tuberville Invites Disaffected Texans to Relocate to Alabama

Sen. Tommy Tuberville is courting conservative Texans and businesses after Democrats scored major wins in Texas, offering Alabama as a new home.

### The Invitation

Tuberville's message that "Alabama is open for business" is aimed at Texans who may be unhappy with the political direction of their state. The article presents his pitch as a response to LGBTQ advocacy and to shifting political power in a state long seen as reliably Republican. He is encouraging both individuals and companies to consider moving.

### Why It Matters

Texas' midterm results point to a dramatic political and cultural shift. Democrats won high-profile statewide races, while Republicans lost ground even after redistricting efforts meant to help them. Tuberville's offer taps into a wider culture-war narrative about migration, business relocation and so-called "woke" politics, casting a change in election outcomes as a reason for people and companies to pick up and move.
