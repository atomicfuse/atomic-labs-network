## Broncos suspend running back J.K. Dobbins for one game

Denver coach Sean Payton has suspended running back J.K. Dobbins for one game, citing conduct "detrimental to the team."

### What we know

Payton gave no further details about the behavior behind the decision. The suspension arrives as Dobbins' role in the offense has shrunk sharply, while rookie Jonah Coleman has taken over the Broncos' backfield.

### Why it matters now

At 8-2, the Broncos sit near the top of the AFC and next face the Steelers on Black Friday. That timing raises the stakes for any locker-room discipline and roster change. The suspension also signals a possible turning point in Denver's playoff push and in its running back pecking order.
