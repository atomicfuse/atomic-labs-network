## Nvidia Controversy Sparks Rare Bipartisan Alarm in Congress

President Trump's push to reinstate Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, despite alleged national security concerns tied to China, has unsettled lawmakers from both parties and deepened the divide between Trump and Republicans in Congress.

### Lawmakers Demand Answers

Members of Congress are calling for hearings, tougher export controls on chips, and investigations into possible corruption linked to Trump's financial interests. The unusual show of unity across party lines reflects how seriously many view the allegations and the president's role in the matter.

### Why the Timing Matters

AI chips sit at the heart of the U.S.-China technology rivalry, which makes Nvidia's exposure to China a national security flashpoint. The scandal also arrives as Democrats prepare to take control of Congress, lending momentum to bills on export controls and stock-trading reform.
