## North Korean Christians Describe Surveillance, Beatings and Prison in New Report

A new investigation by Korea Future documents alleged abuses against North Koreans suspected of practising Christianity. The report relies heavily on survivor accounts, including that of Kim Bo-bin, to show how the authorities treat unsanctioned faith.

### What the investigation describes

The report details alleged surveillance, torture, imprisonment and social punishment aimed at people suspected of being Christian. Survivor testimony sits at the centre of the findings, with Kim Bo-bin's story used to illustrate the experience. According to the investigation, the regime views unauthorized religious belief as a political threat rather than a private matter of conscience.

### Why it matters now

The findings suggest religious repression in North Korea remains active despite years of international scrutiny, with alleged abuses continuing as recently as last year. The report also arrives amid wider concern about Pyongyang tightening its control over foreign influence, media and border-crossing networks.
