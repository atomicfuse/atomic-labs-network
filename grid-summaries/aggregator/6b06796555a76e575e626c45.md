## Solomon Islands Swaps China Security Pact for Australian Treaty

The Solomon Islands has agreed a new security and economic treaty with Australia, and reports say it will end the controversial 2022 security agreement it signed with China.

### What the Treaty Does

Under the deal, Australia becomes Honiara's "security partner of choice." The treaty also restricts foreign military access and limits security arrangements made outside Pacific frameworks. That effectively closes the door on the kind of bilateral defence ties that the earlier pact with China represented, while keeping Honiara's security cooperation anchored within regional structures.

### Why It Matters Now

China, Australia and the United States are competing intensely for influence across the Pacific, and this move is a major strategic win for Canberra. It could also reshape regional security norms, particularly around sovereignty, infrastructure and military access, as other Pacific nations watch how the arrangement develops.
