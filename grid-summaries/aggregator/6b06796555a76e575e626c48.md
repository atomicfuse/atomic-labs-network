## Trump Hints at Shrinking Window for Possible Iran Strikes

President Trump has warned that his room to continue or widen military action against Iran could narrow if Democrats take control of Congress. He made the point as Israel presses for renewed strikes tied to Iran's weapons capabilities.

### Funding Deadline Looms

Trump said war funding is in place through the end of January, which sets up a political deadline for U.S. military strategy in the Middle East. His comments suggest that any further action may depend on what Congress looks like after that point. The timing also lands amid Israeli pressure and ceasefire efforts that have collapsed.

### Nuclear Goal, Not Regime Change

Trump maintained that the U.S. objective is preventing Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon, not toppling its government. The discussion unfolds against wider tensions involving Iran, North Korea, Ukraine and Russia, which add to the strategic backdrop for decisions on the region.
