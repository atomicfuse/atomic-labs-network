## Young Texas Republicans Tell Their Party It's Time to "Grow Up"

Young Republicans in Tarrant County, Texas, are debating why the GOP struggled in the midterms and whether Trump-style politics has become a liability. Many say the party needs a more mature approach.

### What they are saying

The young conservatives followed in the article argue that the party should "grow up." In their view, that means concentrating on affordability rather than meme-driven conflict. They also want Republicans to move beyond politics built around personal loyalty, and to focus on issues that touch voters' daily lives.

### Why it matters now

Texas Democrats' statewide wins, along with a formerly conservative county flipping blue, have intensified GOP soul-searching ahead of 2028. Younger conservatives are signaling frustration with performative politics at a time when housing, family formation, and economic anxiety are shaping how people vote. Their message points to a generational push inside the party to rethink its tone and priorities.
