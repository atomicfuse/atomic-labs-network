## Solomon Islands Picks Australia as Preferred Security Partner

Australia and Solomon Islands have signed a new security pact that makes Canberra the Pacific nation's preferred defense partner. The agreement signals a move away from the country's earlier security alignment with China.

### What the deal changes

Under the pact, Australia takes the leading role in Solomon Islands' defense arrangements. That position marks a notable change for a nation that previously tied its security interests to Beijing. The agreement formalizes a closer relationship with a US ally and suggests a reordering of the country's strategic priorities.

### Why the timing matters

The signing arrives as the United States and China compete more intensely for influence across the Pacific. In that contest, security agreements can reshape regional power dynamics and affect which countries gain military access. The new deal therefore carries weight beyond the two signatories, as it adds to the shifting balance of partnerships in the region.
