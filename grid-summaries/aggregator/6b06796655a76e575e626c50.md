## Meyer Picks Oregon as the Big Ten's Best Ahead of the Playoff

Former coach Urban Meyer has named Oregon the strongest team in the Big Ten as the College Football Playoff approaches. His ranking places Indiana second, followed by Iowa and UCLA, though he still considers the SEC the better conference this season.

### Why Meyer Likes Oregon

Meyer points to the Ducks' speed, athleticism and size, saying those traits resemble what teams in the SEC bring to the field. That physical profile, in his view, sets Oregon apart from the rest of the Big Ten, with Indiana next in line and Iowa and UCLA further back in his order.

### A Muddled Playoff Picture

Rivalry week and the newest CFP rankings have left the Big Ten's playoff outlook unsettled, following key losses and injuries affecting UCLA and Indiana. The conference has produced the past three national champions, so Meyer's remarks add to the debate over whether it still has a true title contender.
