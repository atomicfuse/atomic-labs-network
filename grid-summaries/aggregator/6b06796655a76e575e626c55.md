## Retired FBI agent revisits the fraud case she never shook

Jane Collins spent decades with the FBI, but one investigation stayed with her after she retired. It involved Michael Donovan, Richard Moore and Libre by Nexus, and their exploitation of vulnerable people, among them Zach Cruz, the brother of the Parkland shooter.

### A case about human cost

Collins reflects on how white-collar crime can inflict deep personal damage on victims. The piece argues that this harm remains real even when criminal accountability arrives slowly or only in part, and it frames the case as a lasting lesson in how fraud affects people in crisis.

### Why it resurfaces now

Donovan and Moore were recently found guilty of stealing Cruz's inheritance. The verdict has renewed scrutiny of their wider history of alleged scams aimed at migrants and others in difficult circumstances. The story arrives amid ongoing public concern about immigration detention, consumer fraud, and whether wealthy operators escape meaningful consequences.
