## Florida attorney general seeks license revocation for Miami-area OB-GYN

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier is urging that North Miami Beach OB-GYN Dr. Sylvester Braithwaite lose his medical license. The call centers on allegations that the doctor carried out at least 149 illegal abortions.

### The allegations and the law

The case is presented as a matter of enforcing Florida's Heartbeat Protection Act. Uthmeier's push to strip the physician of his license comes alongside the possibility of criminal prosecution, though the account provided does not say whether any charges have been filed. The figure of 149 is described as a minimum, and the claims against Braithwaite remain allegations.

### Why it matters now

Abortion enforcement continues to be a major political and legal flashpoint as states test the limits of restrictions in the post-Roe era. This case could become a high-profile example of how aggressively Florida officials pursue doctors accused of violating the state's abortion law.
