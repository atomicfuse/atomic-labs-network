## Raheem Sterling Handed Suspended Sentence Over Dangerous Driving

Former England footballer Raheem Sterling has been sentenced after a crash in which he was sleep-deprived and had used nitrous oxide. The judge described his decision to drive as "deliberate and entirely selfish."

### The sentence

Sterling received a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months. He was also given a two-year driving ban and ordered to complete 100 hours of unpaid work. The court additionally required him to pay costs. The judge's remarks focused on the choice he made to get behind the wheel in that condition.

### Why it matters now

The case arrives as scrutiny grows over nitrous oxide use, celebrity accountability and road safety risks involving high-profile athletes. Sterling's standing as a former England footballer and public role model gives the sentencing cultural weight that reaches beyond sport.
