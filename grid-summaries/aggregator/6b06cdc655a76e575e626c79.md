## EA Brings Back "Motty": AI Voice of John Motson Heads to FC 27

EA Sports FC 27 will feature an AI-generated version of the voice of John Motson, the late BBC football commentator. The recreation is built from archive recordings and has the permission of his family.

### A tribute with a catch

The addition is framed as a nostalgic tribute to a commentator many football fans remember fondly. At the same time, it has become a flashpoint in the wider argument over AI voice cloning, consent, and the value of creative labor. The use of his archive material makes the project a closely watched example of the technology in action.

### Why the debate matters now

Generative AI is becoming more common across entertainment and games, and this is a high-profile case of resurrecting a beloved public figure through synthetic media. It arrives amid growing concern over who should control a person's voice and likeness after death: families, companies, or the creators themselves.
