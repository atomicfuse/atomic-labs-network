## Trump Administration Reportedly Debates Striking Iran's Top Leaders

The Trump administration is discussing whether to go after senior figures in Iran's regime, according to a report. The talks are part of a broader push to bring the war to a close.

### What is being considered

Officials are weighing whether targeting Iran's top leaders could help end the conflict. The aim is also to stop Iran from advancing nuclear or other unconventional weapons threats. The report describes the matter as a discussion under way, not a decision that has been made.

### Why the timing matters

The debate arrives as the war enters its ninth month. U.S. officials are balancing the risks of escalation against claims that Iran's leadership, economy and military posture are under severe pressure. How that balance is struck may shape the next phase of the conflict.
