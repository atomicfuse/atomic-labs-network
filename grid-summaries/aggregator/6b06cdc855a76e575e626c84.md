## Trump Administration Renews Public Threats Toward Cuba's Leadership

CNN reports from Havana that the Trump administration has stepped up its public threats against Cuba's leadership following the midterm elections. Some residents in the Cuban capital say they doubt that U.S. intervention is actually likely.

### Reaction in Havana

The report, filed from Havana, captures how the renewed warnings are being received by ordinary Cubans. Rather than alarm, some residents voice skepticism, suggesting they see the rhetoric as more talk than a sign of coming action. Their doubts stand in contrast to the forceful tone coming from Washington.

### Why the Timing Matters

The threats arrive amid heightened political messaging after the midterms and renewed attention on U.S.-Cuba relations. Hardline rhetoric of this kind can shape diplomacy, migration, and regional stability, which is why the exchange between Washington and Havana is drawing notice now.
