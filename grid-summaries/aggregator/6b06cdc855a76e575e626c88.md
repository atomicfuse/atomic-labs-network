## Burmese Pythons Edge Northward Across Florida as Hunters Fight Back

Invasive Burmese pythons are spreading farther north in Florida, and the shift is raising concern about whether the problem can still be kept in the south of the state.

### A Range on the Move

The snakes are surviving colder temperatures by sheltering in animal burrows, which helps them push beyond their usual territory. Meanwhile, they continue to devastate native wildlife in the Everglades. Tens of thousands are already established and breeding quickly, putting native mammals, reptiles, and local habitats at growing risk.

### Hunters and the Push for Early Action

Professional python hunter Amy Siewe is at the center of the story, which stresses why early detection, public education, and removal efforts matter. Her work shows how people on the ground are trying to slow the snakes' advance before the ecological damage spreads even further.
