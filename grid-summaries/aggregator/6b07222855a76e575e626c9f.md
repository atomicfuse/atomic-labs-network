## Trump's accounts traded Nvidia shares dozens of times this year

Financial disclosures show that Donald Trump's investment portfolios bought or sold Nvidia shares at least 44 times this year. The records have drawn attention because of his public role in the reinstatement of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, which followed a major drop in the company's stock.

### Conflict-of-interest concerns

The pairing of frequent trading with presidential involvement in the company's leadership has raised questions about potential conflicts of interest. The White House says the portfolio is independently managed.

### Why the timing matters

Nvidia is one of the world's most valuable companies and central to the AI boom, so any presidential involvement in its leadership or in events that move its stock carries large market and political stakes. The timing is especially sensitive because Nvidia's share price surged 31% after Huang returned to his post.
