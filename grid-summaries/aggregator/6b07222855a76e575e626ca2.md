## Senate Democrats Seek Leadership Seat for Newest Members

A group of Senate Democrats is lobbying to bring a freshman senator into the party's leadership team. Supporters say the incoming class has earned a voice at the table.

### The Case for Representation

The senators behind the effort argue that the new class of lawmakers played a part in powering the party's November victory. In their view, that contribution justifies giving freshmen a formal place among those who steer the caucus. They are pressing the case that newcomers should not be left on the sidelines when leadership roles are shaped.

### A Broader Push Within the Party

The campaign reflects wider pressure within the Democratic Party to lift up newer and more diverse voices. As Democrats work out their direction and power structure after the election, the question of who sits in leadership has become a visible test of that demand.
