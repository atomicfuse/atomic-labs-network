## Eight Years of Sánchez: Is "Sanchismo" an Ideology or Just an Insult?

An article looks back on Pedro Sánchez's eight years in power and asks what "Sanchismo" really means. It concludes the term is less a coherent ideology than a label, used mostly by his opponents to attack how he leads.

### What defines his leadership

The piece points to Sánchez's pragmatism and his skill at building coalitions as the core of his approach. It also weighs his policy record and his role as a barrier against Spain's right and far right, which supporters see as central to his time in office.

### Why the label matters now

Sánchez remains at the heart of a polarized political scene. Debates over the Catalan amnesty, corruption, housing costs, NATO spending and the far right are all intensifying. With upcoming polls set to test his survival, "Sanchismo" has become shorthand for Spain's wider political divide.
