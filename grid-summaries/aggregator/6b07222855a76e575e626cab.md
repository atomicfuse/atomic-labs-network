## Switzerland Shuts Pyongyang Office After North Korea Row Over Sanctions

Switzerland says it has had to close its diplomatic office in North Korea. The closure followed a dispute over a Swiss sanctions decision targeting a children's camp, and it came only weeks after Bern had reopened the mission.

### How the dispute unfolded

Bern imposed sanctions on Songdowon International Children's Camp, citing alleged links to the transfer of Ukrainian children from Russian-occupied areas. The situation escalated quickly. Pyongyang responded by declaring the head of the Swiss office persona non grata, and Switzerland says North Korea forced the office to close.

### Why it matters

The episode points to a harder North Korean line toward Western governments, at a time when Pyongyang is deepening its ties with Russia during the war in Ukraine. It also removes one of the few remaining Western diplomatic channels into the country. That loss comes as concern grows over sanctions, child transfers and military cooperation.
