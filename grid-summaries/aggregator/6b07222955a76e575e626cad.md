## Schiff and Trump Ally Trade Blows Before Oversight Fight Begins

Sen. Adam Schiff has openly clashed with U.S. Attorney Aaron Reitz over the nature of coming Democratic investigations into President Trump and those close to him. The dispute shows both camps staking out positions before any formal probe has started.

### Competing Narratives

Schiff and fellow Democrats are preparing an aggressive approach to oversight, and they describe their plans as legitimate congressional work. Trump-aligned officials, including Reitz, are pushing a different framing. They are trying to cast the investigations as legally suspect and as partisan retaliation rather than proper scrutiny.

### What Is at Stake

Democrats are about to take control of Congress, which sets up a major confrontation. Likely flashpoints include subpoenas, claims of executive privilege, and inquiries into Trump's family, allies, and business dealings. Because each side is already working to shape public opinion, the argument over legitimacy may influence how the investigations are received once they formally begin.
