## Watchdog Data Shows 44% Rise in Anti-Christian Incidents Across Europe

A Fox News report points to figures from the watchdog group OIDAC indicating that anti-Christian hate crimes and incidents across Europe climbed 44% in 2025. The report argues the problem receives too little attention.

### What the Data Shows

According to the OIDAC figures cited, attacks increased sharply against clergy, visibly practising Christians, churches, and sites linked to the pro-life cause. The report contends that anti-Christian hostility is underreported and given lower priority than hate crimes aimed at other religious groups.

### Why the Debate Is Heating Up

The report arrives as European institutions discuss how to classify, track, and respond to religiously motivated attacks. The OSCE has issued new guidance, and advocates are calling for an EU coordinator focused on anti-Christian hatred. Tensions over migration, secularism, pro-life activism, and religious identity make the subject politically and culturally charged.
