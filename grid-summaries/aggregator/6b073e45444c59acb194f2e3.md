## Oceania Marina Adds Chef's Studio for Hands-On Cooking at Sea

Oceania Cruises is rolling out a new Chef's Studio on board Oceania Marina, giving guests a more active way to explore food during their voyage. The addition aims to make the ship a more immersive, food-focused cruise experience.

### What the Chef's Studio offers

The studio features hands-on culinary classes priced at US$49, along with chef-led tours in the destinations the ship visits. Together, these options let passengers cook on board and then connect with local food culture ashore. The launch positions Marina as a vessel where dining and learning go hand in hand.

### Why it matters now

Culinary travel remains a major driver for premium cruise guests who want more than traditional sightseeing. By pairing affordable onboard classes with local food excursions, Oceania gains a timely advantage in the growing field of experiential travel. The Chef's Studio speaks directly to travelers looking for deeper, more participatory journeys.
