## Experts Share Holiday Travel Advice for Families Caring for Someone With Dementia

Specialists have put together practical guidance for families traveling during the holidays with a loved one who has dementia. The advice centers on keeping trips supportive and low-stress, and on considering whether to skip travel altogether if it may not bring joy or comfort.

### What the experts recommend

The tips stress holding on to routine as much as possible, planning with safety in mind, and keeping medications organized and on schedule. Experts also suggest finding quieter spaces where a person can rest away from busy gatherings. Flexible planning is another theme, so families can adjust when needs or moods change.

### Why it matters now

Holiday travel and family get-togethers can upend familiar routines, which may increase confusion or stress for people living with dementia. With Thanksgiving and the wider seasonal travel rush getting under way, caregivers are looking for practical, compassionate ways to plan trips that are safer and easier for everyone involved.
