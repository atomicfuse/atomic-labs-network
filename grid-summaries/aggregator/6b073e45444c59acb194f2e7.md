## AAA Offers Holiday Travel Guidance as Cruise Interest Grows

AAA has released a batch of advice for people heading out over the holidays, along with Thanksgiving travel forecasts and a look at where vacation habits are going. The guidance arrives as families begin planning trips for one of the busiest times of year.

### Preparing for Peak Travel Stress

Thanksgiving and other holiday trips are among the most stressful moments for travelers. Crowded roads and busy airports can make getting to a destination harder, and shifting vacation habits are changing how people approach their planning. AAA's tips and forecasts are meant to help households think through those challenges before they set out, rather than reacting once they are already in the middle of the rush.

### Why Cruises Are Drawing Attention

Among the emerging trends AAA highlights is the expectation that cruises will be a major travel choice in 2027. That interest points to a broader move toward bundled trips that are easier to plan, where many pieces of a vacation come together in a single booking instead of being arranged one by one.
