## Harry Reid Airport Prepares for a Busier Thanksgiving Rush

Las Vegas' Harry Reid International Airport is gearing up for a crowded Thanksgiving week, and a new guide offers practical ways for travelers to cope. Passenger volume is expected to climb 5% from last year, even though holiday travel nationwide is slightly lower.

### Tools and Options for Travelers

The advice covers TSA wait-time tools that help passengers plan their arrival, along with parking alternatives, valet services, and tips for arranging pickups. Using these options in advance may make the trip to and from the terminal smoother during one of the year's busiest travel periods.

### Why Timing and Parking Matter

Peak departure days are Tuesday and Wednesday, while return crowds are expected Sunday and Monday. Right now, parking pressure appears to be a bigger headache for Harry Reid travelers than TSA delays, so planning where to leave a vehicle may be just as important as allowing time for security.
