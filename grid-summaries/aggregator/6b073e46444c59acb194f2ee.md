## Uber Adds Train Tickets to Its App in France

Uber is extending its service beyond car rides in France by letting people search for and buy rail tickets without leaving the app. The move is presented as a convenience feature that pushes the company into wider travel planning.

### What is changing

Users in France can now look up train options and complete their purchase inside the Uber app itself. Rather than switching between separate services to organize a journey, travelers can handle the rail portion in the same place where they already request rides. The addition positions Uber as more than a ride-hailing tool.

### Why the timing matters

Travelers increasingly expect a single app to cover their mobility needs, and Uber appears to be responding by trying to own more of each trip, not just airport pickups and city rides. France's strong rail culture makes it a strategic place to test how well app-based, multimodal travel works.
