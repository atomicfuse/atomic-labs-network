## Check Caltrans QuickMap Before Hitting the Road This Thanksgiving

California drivers heading out for the holiday can use Caltrans' QuickMap to look up road conditions before they leave. The tool is meant to help travelers plan routes and avoid surprises on the highway.

### What QuickMap Shows

According to the article, QuickMap lets drivers check road closures, chain controls, winter weather impacts, and general highway conditions. Looking at these details ahead of time can help motorists decide which route to take and whether they need to prepare for restrictions along the way.

### Why Timing Matters

Thanksgiving is one of the busiest travel periods of the year, and drivers in California may run into congestion, mountain snow, chain requirements, and unexpected delays. Because conditions can change, checking the map before departing gives travelers a clearer picture of what to expect and a better chance of planning around trouble spots.
