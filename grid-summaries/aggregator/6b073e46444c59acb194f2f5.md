## Washington Drivers Brace for a Busy Thanksgiving on the Road

Thanksgiving ranks among the busiest travel times of the year, and a new guide lays out what Washington motorists can expect. AAA projects nearly 82 million travelers nationwide, with heavy road traffic likely on Tuesday, Wednesday, Sunday, and Monday.

### Where Congestion Is Expected

The article identifies peak congestion windows and highlights trouble spots across the state. Drivers should anticipate slowdowns on I-5, I-90, and US 2, as well as on the mountain passes. Planning departure times around the busiest days may help travelers avoid the worst of the delays.

### Preparing for Winter Conditions

Beyond traffic, the guide offers safety preparation tips for winter road conditions. Because mountain routes are among the areas of concern, drivers heading across the passes are encouraged to get ready before leaving home. The advice is aimed at helping Washington residents reach their holiday destinations safely during one of the year's most crowded travel periods.
