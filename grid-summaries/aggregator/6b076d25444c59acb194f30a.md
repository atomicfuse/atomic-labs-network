## Nvidia's Slide Drags Tech Lower as Tariff Worries Grow

Technology stocks are under heavy selling pressure, and a decline in Nvidia shares is unsettling the wider market. Investors are increasingly concerned about the risk of tariffs escalating.

### What is driving the selloff

The market pressure comes from two sources combined: anxiety about AI-related stocks and renewed uncertainty over trade policy. Nvidia's drop has become a focal point, spreading nervousness beyond the chipmaker to the broader market as traders weigh the possibility of tougher tariff measures.

### Why it matters now

Nvidia is treated as a bellwether for the AI trade, so weakness in its shares can quickly erode confidence across many other stocks.
