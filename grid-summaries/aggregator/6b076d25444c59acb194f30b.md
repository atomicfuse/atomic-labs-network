## Nvidia Shares Slide as Jensen Huang Prepares for Washington Spotlight

Nvidia's stock dropped ahead of an expected appearance by CEO Jensen Huang on Capitol Hill. The decline points to investor unease about how closely lawmakers may examine the company.

### What Moved the Stock

The sell-off reflects worry over political scrutiny, policy toward AI chips, and Nvidia's exposure to regulators. Investors appear to be weighing what Huang's visit could mean for the company's standing in Washington, even before any discussion takes place.

### Why Washington Matters Now

Nvidia sits at the center of the AI boom, which means pressure from policymakers on exports, competition, or national security can swiftly shift markets. The timing is notable because officials increasingly view advanced chips as both economic assets and geopolitical tools. That dual role keeps the company in a sensitive position as its leader heads into the political arena.
