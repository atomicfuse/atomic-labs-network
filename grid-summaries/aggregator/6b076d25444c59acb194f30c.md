## Yardeni Blames AI Panic for Stock Slide

Market watcher Ed Yardeni says a wave of investor anxiety about artificial intelligence is behind the latest stock market sell-off, pointing to jitters that have unsettled trading.

### What Yardeni Is Saying

Yardeni attributes the decline to panic surrounding AI. In his view, worries about how richly AI-related companies are valued, along with concern over how concentrated the market has become in a small group of stocks, are fueling the volatility. He frames the sell-off as a reaction to those fears rather than pointing to some other trigger.

### Why It Matters Now

AI has been one of the biggest drivers of recent market gains, which means the sector carries outsized influence over how stocks perform overall. When confidence in it wobbles, the effects can spread quickly through technology shares and then into the broader indexes. That makes sentiment around AI a key factor for investors to watch as the market swings.
