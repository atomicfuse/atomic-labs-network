## Wang Fuk Court Fire: A Renovation Meant to Improve Homes Ended in Disaster

A mandated renovation of Hong Kong's Wang Fuk Court became a catastrophic fire that killed 168 people. A new examination of the case argues the tragedy was preventable.

### What went wrong

The article points to a chain of failures: unsafe materials, disabled alarms, complaints that went ignored, and alleged bid-rigging. It says contractors, property managers and the authorities all fell short. Rather than a single mistake, it presents the disaster as the result of many lapses that together left residents exposed.

### Why it matters now

A final independent report has sharpened public scrutiny of Hong Kong's building safety regime, how renovations are overseen, and who is held accountable for ageing residential blocks. The tragedy also raises wider concerns about elderly residents, the risk of corruption, and safety shortcuts in the city's densely packed housing.
