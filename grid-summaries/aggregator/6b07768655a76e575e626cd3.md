## Steinberg Warns of Fallout From Rodgers-Steelers Split

Agent Leigh Steinberg believes Aaron Rodgers' public break with the Pittsburgh Steelers and coach Mike McCarthy carries real risks for everyone involved. His comments come as the dispute has grown from a team matter into a league-wide storyline.

### Risk to Rodgers and the Steelers

Steinberg argues the rupture could harm Rodgers' legacy. He also says it may raise red flags for future free agents weighing Pittsburgh as a destination. The Steelers, he says, must look into Rodgers' claims while guarding their long-standing reputation for stability and class.

### Why the Story Is Escalating

Rodgers' benching, a reported waiver, and his explosive remarks on "The Pat McAfee Show" pushed the conflict into public view. The fallout may shape not only Rodgers' future but also how agents and players view the Steelers as a place to play.
