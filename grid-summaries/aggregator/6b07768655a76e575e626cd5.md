## Duran Duran Mourn Guitarist Andy Taylor, Dead at 65

Andy Taylor, guitarist with Duran Duran, has died at 65 after a battle with prostate cancer. His family and bandmates have paid emotional tribute to his talent, his courage and his devotion to those closest to him.

### A Career That Shaped a Sound

Taylor played a key part in shaping the sound that made Duran Duran famous. Remembrances also look back at his solo work, his reunions with the band and the group's place in the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, which cements a legacy that still resonates with fans.

### Why His Loss Resonates

His death removes a central figure from one of the defining bands of the 1980s and has prompted fresh reflection on Duran Duran's influence on pop, rock and music-video culture. It also draws attention to prostate cancer and to the reality of artists continuing to create while living with serious illness.
