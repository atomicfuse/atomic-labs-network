## Fulton County judge pauses order to hand 2020 ballot copies to state election board

A Fulton County judge has put on hold her own earlier order that would have required the county to give Georgia's State Election Board unredacted digital copies of its 2020 election records.

### What happened

The judge paused the directive she had issued herself, so the county does not have to hand over the records for now. The dispute is rooted in long-running efforts by allies of Donald Trump to revisit Fulton County's 2020 results, along with their claims of voter fraud.

### Why it matters now

The decision arrives as political and legal fights over how elections are run continue, particularly in battleground Georgia. It shows that disputes over the 2020 election are still shaping arguments about oversight as the state looks ahead to future elections.
