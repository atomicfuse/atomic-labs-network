## North Korea Expels Swiss Diplomat and Orders Office Closed

North Korea has expelled Switzerland's diplomatic representative and told the Swiss office in Pyongyang to shut down. The decision came after Switzerland imposed sanctions on a children's camp accused of involvement in transferring children from Russian-occupied Ukraine.

### A Sharp Diplomatic Setback

The article presents the expulsion as a significant blow to relations between Pyongyang and Switzerland, one of the few Western countries that had still kept up dialogue with North Korea. The Swiss sanctions on the camp appear to have triggered the response, ending a channel that had remained open despite wider Western isolation of the country.

### Why the Timing Matters

The dispute arrives as North Korea's backing for Russia's war in Ukraine faces growing scrutiny, including troop deployments and alleged missile use. It also follows a rare Swiss diplomatic reopening in Pyongyang. That makes the expulsion a timely sign of how sanctions are clashing with fragile engagement channels.
