## Israeli air strike in Gaza City kills Hamas security official and his family

An Israeli air strike in Gaza City has killed Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, a senior Hamas internal security official. His wife and two teenage daughters also died in the attack.

### Targeting security leadership

The strike is presented as part of Israel's continuing campaign against Hamas security leaders. Hamas condemned the attack, describing it as an attempt to destabilize Palestinian society. The deaths of the official's wife and daughters add to the weight of the incident, which unfolded in one of Gaza's main urban centers.

### Why the timing matters

The attack came even though a ceasefire was agreed last year. It also followed, by a day, the killing of Hamas's internal security commander. Taken together, the two strikes highlight the continuing intensity of Israeli operations in Gaza and a growing focus on Hamas's internal security apparatus.
