## Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie Resolve Château Miraval Dispute

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have ended their four-year legal fight over the sale of Château Miraval, the French winery they once owned together. The settlement closes a case that drew wide attention.

### The settlement

Both sides released a joint statement saying the agreement was reached in the best interests of the family. They also said neither party will comment any further, so no other details have been shared.

### Why it matters now

The case was closely followed because it prolonged the fallout from the former couple's high-profile divorce. It also touched on earlier allegations linked to a private plane incident. With the settlement, one of the most public legal chapters of their post-split conflict may now be finished.
