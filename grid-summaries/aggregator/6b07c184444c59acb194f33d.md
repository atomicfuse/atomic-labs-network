## Peregrine falcons could see habitat expand by 2080

A new UK study has modelled how climate change might alter the habitat available to 16 breeding birds of prey. Among them, the peregrine falcon, often described as the world's fastest animal, stands out as a potential winner.

### What the study found

Researchers estimate that peregrines could gain up to 73.5% more suitable habitat by 2080. The central finding is that climate change will not affect all raptors in the same way. The work also stresses that more suitable land does not automatically translate into larger populations.

### Why it matters now

As climate impacts accelerate, conservation planning needs to move past blanket assumptions that all wildlife will lose habitat in the same manner. The study points to a tension: some species may benefit geographically while others decline, which complicates how future protection strategies are designed.
