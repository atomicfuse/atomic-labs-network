## Large study finds no link between ADHD medication in pregnancy and child developmental disorders

A big Scandinavian cohort study has found that children exposed to ADHD medication in the womb were not at higher risk of neurodevelopmental conditions. The authors describe the results as broadly reassuring.

### What the study found

Researchers looked at children whose mothers took ADHD medication during pregnancy and found no increased risk of autism, ADHD, intellectual disability, learning disorders, or other neurodevelopmental conditions. Based on this, the authors say the findings do not support stopping ADHD medication solely because of worries about developmental risk to the child.

### Why it matters now

ADHD medication use is rising among women of reproductive age, and concerns about safety in pregnancy often lead patients to discontinue treatment. The study adds timely evidence to that discussion. It may help pregnant patients and their clinicians make more informed, shared decisions about whether to continue medication.
