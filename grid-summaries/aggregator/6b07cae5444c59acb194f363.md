## Birthday Boy Blake Corum Powers Rams Past Packers on Thanksgiving Eve

Blake Corum celebrated his 26th birthday with a career-high 184 rushing yards, leading the Rams to a 26–13 victory over the Green Bay Packers on Thanksgiving Eve.

### Ground Game Makes the Difference

Los Angeles leaned on its running attack and controlled the clock, which proved to be the deciding factor. Corum's big night came against a Green Bay defense that struggled to stop the run. The Packers were also hurt by injuries and turnovers, problems that pointed to deeper issues for the team.

### Playoff Picture and Primetime Spectacle

The win was the Rams' fifth in a row and moves them firmly into the NFC Wild Card conversation. Green Bay, meanwhile, continues to slide into messy contender territory. The game was also the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve broadcast on Netflix, and U2 performed the halftime show, adding extra spectacle to the night.
