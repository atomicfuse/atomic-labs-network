## BBC probe links German boat dealer to Channel smuggling

A BBC investigation alleges that a German company, MaReBoote, and its managing director, Marvin Reuter, supplied boats and engines tied to English Channel smuggling gangs. Reuter is contesting extradition to France.

### What authorities suspect

Investigators believe the equipment was used in nearly a third of the crossings detected early this year. They also suspect it was involved in two fatal incidents. The claims come from the BBC's findings and the suspicions of authorities, and Reuter is fighting the move to send him to France.

### Why the timing matters

The case follows Germany's decision to close a post-Brexit legal loophole that had made it harder to prosecute those who support smuggling to the UK. Small-boat crossings remain politically explosive in both the UK and Europe. The investigation shows how supply chains, and not only smugglers at sea, are increasingly becoming targets for enforcement.
