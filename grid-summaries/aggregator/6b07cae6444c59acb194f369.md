## Hand recount ahead in tight Western Washington legislative race

A legislative contest in Western Washington will go to a hand recount after the two candidates ended up just 80 votes apart.

### What happened

The narrow gap between the candidates triggered the recount. Both candidates have accepted the election results, which indicates the recount is a procedural step and not the product of a disputed outcome. Neither side is contesting how the vote came out, but the margin is slim enough that a hand tally will take place.

### Why it matters now

Extremely close races show how small margins can influence state politics and the balance of power in the legislature. The recount also connects to continuing public interest in how transparent elections are and how much people trust the counting of votes. A hand review of ballots in a race this tight puts that process in the spotlight.
