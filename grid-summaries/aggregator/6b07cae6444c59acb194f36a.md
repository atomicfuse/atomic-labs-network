## Fox News segment flags rising Thanksgiving dinner costs

A Fox News segment reported that the cost of a Thanksgiving dinner has climbed about 6% compared with last year. The increase ends a streak of three years in which prices fell.

### What the segment found

The report pointed to higher prices for several holiday staples, including turkey, milk, stuffing, sweet potatoes, and cranberries. It also noted that farmers may not see much benefit from the higher prices at the checkout, since their own costs are rising as well.

### Why it matters now

Thanksgiving prices are a highly visible gauge of household inflation, which makes the numbers politically sensitive for the Trump administration. The segment carries added weight because Fox News reaches a conservative audience, and the network chose to air a blunt look at the cost of living.
