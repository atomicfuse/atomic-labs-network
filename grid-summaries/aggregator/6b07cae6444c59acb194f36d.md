## A Riviera Mayor Eyes Milei's Chainsaw as France's Right Churns

France's right wing is in flux, with one Riviera mayor looking to Argentina for inspiration while Marine Le Pen's party faces fresh questions. The story draws together several strands of a political landscape that is shifting ahead of a major national vote.

### Chainsaw politics comes to the Riviera

A mayor on the French Riviera is embracing the anti-state style associated with Argentina's Javier Milei, whose "chainsaw" approach centers on sharply cutting back government. The mayor wants to bring that pro-austerity message into French politics, adding a new voice to the country's crowded right-wing field.

### Scrutiny over the National Rally and Ukraine

Marine Le Pen's National Rally is under growing scrutiny over its stance on Ukraine. That attention comes as France heads toward a high-stakes presidential race, where populist, nationalist and pro-austerity messages are colliding with the war in Ukraine and concerns over Russian influence.
