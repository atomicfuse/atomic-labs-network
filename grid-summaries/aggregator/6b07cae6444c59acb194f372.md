## Historic First: Macy's 100th Thanksgiving Parade Pushed to Saturday

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed from Thursday to Saturday because a severe nor'easter is forecast for New York City. Organizers have never before rescheduled the event because of weather, making this a landmark moment for the milestone edition.

### Behind the Scenes Scramble

The delay creates a long list of logistical problems. Giant balloons must be deflated, performers need to be coordinated for the new date, and inflation night has been canceled. Officials are also expanding public safety plans to account for the changed schedule and the conditions expected in the city.

### Why the Delay Matters

The postponement lands where holiday travel, large public gatherings, and extreme weather overlap. That makes it both a cultural moment, since the parade has never been moved before, and a safety story, as planners weigh the risks of a major event during a powerful storm.
