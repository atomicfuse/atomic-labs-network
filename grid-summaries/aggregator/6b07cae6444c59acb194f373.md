## Kushner Under Fire for Praising Putin as a Political "Grandmaster"

Jared Kushner is facing backlash after describing Vladimir Putin as a "grandmaster of politics." He also suggested that Russia showed restraint during the war in Ukraine by choosing not to use nuclear weapons.

### Critics Push Back

Diplomats, Ukraine experts and commentators have objected to the way Kushner framed the conflict. They argue that his remarks downplay Russian aggression and treat the absence of a nuclear strike as a sign of moderation rather than as a baseline expectation. The criticism has been broad, coming from several quarters at once.

### Why the Timing Matters

Kushner is reportedly involved in U.S.-led efforts to negotiate an end to the war, which gives his words added political weight. The comments arrive during an ongoing debate over how Washington should deal with Russia and Ukraine, and over the shape of peace talks under Trump-aligned diplomacy.
