## UK Set to Publish Two New Post-Brexit Treaties With EU

The UK government is publishing the texts of two new treaties agreed with the EU, part of a wider post-Brexit reset in relations. The agreements cover food trade and emissions trading.

### What the Treaties Cover

The texts are expected to benefit exporters, particularly in food trade. They also set out legal mechanisms through which the UK would align with EU rules. Supporters point to the trade gains, while critics dispute the costs involved and raise concerns about sovereignty and the direction of the UK-EU relationship.

### Why the Timing Matters

The publication comes amid renewed debate over Brexit's long-term economic impact and whether closer ties with the EU are becoming politically unavoidable. Food exporters, carbon markets, border infrastructure and parliamentary scrutiny are all in play, and the treaties could shape the next phase of UK trade policy.
