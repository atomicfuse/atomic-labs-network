## Deadly Tanker Explosion on Cairo's Ring Road Kills at Least 31

A petroleum tanker overturned and exploded on Cairo's Ring Road, leaving at least 31 people dead and 21 injured. The blast destroyed 27 vehicles, and authorities are now investigating what caused it.

### What We Know So Far

Early reports suggest a high-voltage cable may have fallen, and that the tanker driver may have lost control before the vehicle overturned. Investigators have not confirmed a cause, and the inquiry is continuing. The toll of 31 dead is described as a minimum, so the figure could change as more information emerges.

### Why It Matters Now

The explosion comes as Egypt faces a worsening road safety crisis, with traffic deaths up more than 10 percent compared with the previous year. The disaster raises urgent questions about how well infrastructure is maintained, how hazardous cargo is transported, and how effectively emergency services can respond on major urban highways.
