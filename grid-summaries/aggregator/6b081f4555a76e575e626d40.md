## Democrats Eye Reform Agenda After "Blue Tsunami" Win

Following an election that expanded their power in Congress, Democrats are pushing a package of anti-corruption and democracy reforms. The article presents these priorities as central to the mandate the party believes voters gave it.

### What Democrats Want to Pass

The agenda covers anti-corruption measures, voting rights, redistricting, campaign finance, and ethics reforms. Issues such as congressional stock trading, voter suppression, gerrymandering, and abuses of executive power during the Trump era are described as key to the party's platform and its sense of purpose after the win.

### Why the Moment Matters

Corruption and voting rights emerged as major concerns for voters in the election, particularly after Supreme Court decisions weakened protections and sharpened redistricting fights. Democrats view this as a rare chance to define a reform agenda. Obstacles remain, though, since Trump is still in the White House and the filibuster continues to loom over any legislative effort.
