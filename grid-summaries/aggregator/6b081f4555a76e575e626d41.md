## Judge Halts FCC's Early License Review of ABC Stations

A federal judge has stopped the FCC from fast-tracking license reviews for eight ABC-owned stations. The ruling backs ABC's First Amendment challenge in a dispute between the agency and the network.

### What the Court Decided

The judge sided with ABC and blocked the accelerated reviews from going forward. At the heart of the case is a question about the FCC's motives: were its actions lawful oversight of broadcasters, or retaliation tied to political tensions? Those tensions involve Disney, ABC, Jimmy Kimmel, and "The View."

### The Wider Fight

The decision arrives amid a larger argument over media regulation, political pressure on broadcasters, and how far government authority over speech can reach. It also follows signals from the FCC of a tougher posture toward networks facing scrutiny over their content, DEI practices, and political programming.
