## Carville Slams DNC's Proposed Money Rule for 2028 Hopefuls

Longtime Democratic strategist James Carville has publicly criticized a new Democratic National Committee rule that would require 2028 presidential candidates to reject super PAC and dark money involvement in order to qualify for debates.

### Carville's objections

Carville argues the rule goes beyond what the DNC should be doing, saying that policing campaign funding is not the committee's job. He also contends the requirement could backfire by giving an advantage to wealthy candidates who can fund their own campaigns, since they would have less need for outside money.

### Why the timing matters

The 2028 Democratic primary is already starting to take shape, and rules governing money, debates, and candidate access could affect who gains early traction. The dispute also highlights a wider tension within the party between anti-billionaire messaging and the practical realities of financing a national campaign.
