## Politician's Daughter's Lavish Wedding Sparks National Debate in India

The wedding of Shivangi Singh, daughter of Bihar politician Anant Singh, has become a major talking point across India. The celebrations drew huge crowds, political VIPs and showy displays of luxury, while also attracting intense public scrutiny.

### A Spectacle of Wealth

The event was framed as an extravagant show of affluence. Massive crowds gathered, prominent political figures attended, and luxury was on open display throughout the festivities. For many observers, the scale of the occasion made it stand out as one of the most talked-about weddings in the country.

### Why It Is Controversial Now

Critics have linked the wedding to Bihar's poverty and to Singh's criminal-political profile, fuelling wider debate about political wealth, inequality and so-called "bahubali" strongman culture. The timing adds weight, because Singh faces legal pressure, including a Supreme Court order to surrender by 4 December.
