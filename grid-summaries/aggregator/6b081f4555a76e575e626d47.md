## Bangkok Refinery Blast Leaves Seven Hurt, Four in Critical Condition

An explosion and fire at Bangchak Corporation's Phra Khanong oil refinery in Bangkok has injured seven people, four of them critically. The blaze was brought under control within 30 minutes.

### Response at the site

Authorities evacuated nearby residents and shut down production at the refinery while the incident was dealt with. An investigation into the cause of the explosion is now under way, and no explanation has been given so far.

### Why it matters now

The blast has raised immediate concerns about public safety, the environment and energy supply in one of Bangkok's urban industrial zones. Smoke was visible across the city skyline, and emergency alerts added to the attention the story has drawn locally. The incident also carries wider relevance for how refinery safety is viewed.
