## Veruschka, pioneering supermodel, dies aged 87

Veruschka, regarded as one of the first global supermodels, has died at 87. Coverage of her death looks back at a career that reached well beyond the catwalk and the magazine page.

### A career beyond modelling

She shaped fashion in the 1960s and 70s, appeared on Vogue covers, and took roles in film. She also worked in avant-garde art that used the body as its medium. The article presents her as more than a model: a shape-shifting performer who widened ideas of beauty, fashion, and feminist performance.

### Why her legacy matters now

Her death renews interest in where supermodel culture began and in how fashion, celebrity, and art can blur together. As today's creators revisit archival style and iconic image-making, her career offers a timely blueprint for personal branding as transformation.
