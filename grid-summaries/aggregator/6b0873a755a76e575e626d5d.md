## Netflix Drops Will Ferrell's Golf Comedy "The Hawk" After One Season

Netflix has canceled "The Hawk," the golf comedy series starring Will Ferrell, following its first season. The show is being framed as an expensive miss for the streamer.

### Why It Was Axed

The article points to weak reviews, high production costs, and a market already crowded with golf comedy. Ferrell's star power was not enough to turn the show into a hit. Audiences were somewhat kinder than critics, with a better audience score than critic response, but that did not change the outcome.

### The Bigger Picture

The cancellation arrives as streamers face pressure to justify costly originals, particularly comedies carrying major celebrity salaries. It also lands during a small wave of golf entertainment, which includes "Happy Gilmore 2" and Apple TV's "The Stick." That context shows how much competition the show faced for viewers.
