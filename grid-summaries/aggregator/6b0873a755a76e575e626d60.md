## Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Delayed by Severe Weather for the First Time

Severe weather has pushed back the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, the first postponement in the event's history.

### A First in a Century

The delay disrupts a holiday ritual that has marked Thanksgiving for a hundred years. Because the parade has never before been rescheduled, the change draws fresh attention to how long the tradition has endured and how often people have counted on it as part of the holiday.

### Only Wartime Cancellations

The parade has been fully canceled just three times, in 1942, 1943, and 1944. During World War II, organizers halted it to help conserve rubber, helium, fuel, and police resources. Those years remain a reminder of wartime sacrifice and of how public traditions bend during national crises, even as this postponement stirs nostalgia for the celebration.
