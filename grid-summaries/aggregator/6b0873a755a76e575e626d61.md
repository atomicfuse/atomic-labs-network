## Cassidy Presses for Social Security Fix as Deadline Nears

Sen. Bill Cassidy is making a late push to overhaul Social Security, with the trust fund projected to run short in 2032. The effort puts his ideas alongside rival Democratic proposals.

### Competing Proposals

Cassidy's plan would create a fund that invests in the market. Democrats favor a different route: raising or eliminating the payroll tax cap. The article compares the two approaches and weighs how each might address the looming shortfall.

### Why the Window Is Narrow

Urgency is growing because benefit cuts could reach 70 million seniors within six years, including nearly 1 million in Louisiana. A lame-duck Congress, shifting party control, and renewed interest from Trump and congressional leaders have combined to create a brief but consequential opening for action.
