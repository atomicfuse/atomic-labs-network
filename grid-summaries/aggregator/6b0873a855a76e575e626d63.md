## Travel expert offers tips for beating Thanksgiving storm delays

Brian Kelly of The Points Guy has shared practical advice for travelers dealing with flight disruptions as storms hit both coasts during the Thanksgiving rush.

### Rebook fast with the right tools

Kelly stresses that travelers should lean on airline apps and flight-tracking tools when delays strike. Acting quickly to rebook through these channels can help passengers avoid getting stuck. His message is that speed matters when weather scrambles schedules, and the technology already in your pocket is often the best way to respond.

### Why the timing matters

Thanksgiving ranks among the busiest travel periods of the year, and severe weather now threatens the plans of millions of Americans. People want fast, usable ways to avoid being stranded or paying too much during the holiday chaos. On the cost side, Kelly suggests waiting until Travel Tuesday to look for better airfare deals.
