## Russia Detains Senior Aerospace Forces Commander Over Alleged Corruption

Russian authorities have arrested Col. Gen. Yuri Grekhov, the deputy commander of the country's aerospace forces. The detention comes as questions mount over failures in Russian air defense.

### What Is Known About the Case

Grekhov is reported to be facing corruption allegations. Pro-war bloggers have connected the case to Ukrainian strikes on prominent Russian targets, among them the Crimean Bridge. The accounts of that link come from those bloggers, and the information provided does not confirm the connection.

### Why the Timing Matters

The war is approaching its fifth year, and both Russia and Ukraine are leaning more heavily on long-range strikes. That shift has exposed weaknesses in Russian air defenses and in military logistics. Grekhov's arrest also fits a wider Kremlin pattern of corruption crackdowns within the defense establishment, which has seen similar cases before.
