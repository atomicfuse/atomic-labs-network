## Ospreay and MJF Set for Ladder Match at Worlds End

AEW has booked Will Ospreay against MJF for the undisputed AEW World Championship, and the bout will be a ladder match at Worlds End.

### The Build

The match follows MJF's return, during which he called himself the "real world champion." Ospreay answered that claim, and the exchange is presented as the latest escalation in AEW's title picture. The ladder stipulation adds a further layer to a rivalry that has already grown heated.

### Why It Matters

Worlds End is being positioned as a major championship-reset moment for the company. MJF is chasing history, while Ospreay will be defending the world title in his first reign. The feud also lands amid a crowded title scene that includes Andrade and Kyle Fletcher, giving fans several storylines to follow and debate as the event approaches.
