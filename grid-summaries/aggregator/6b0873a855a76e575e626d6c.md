## Trump Labels Revised GDP Figures "Fake," Seeks Commerce Department Probe

President Trump has dismissed revised GDP figures as fake and called for an investigation into the Commerce Department, claiming politically motivated errors were made ahead of the midterms.

### A Pattern of Disputing Data

The article describes the move as part of a wider habit. Trump has repeatedly challenged economic data that reflects poorly on him, and he has done so without presenting evidence. Critics cited in the report worry that such claims chip away at public trust in official government statistics, which are meant to offer a neutral picture of how the economy is performing.

### Why the Timing Matters

The clash arrives after Democrats won control of Congress in the midterms, with voters unhappy about inflation and the economy. Attacks on federal numbers carry weight beyond politics, since businesses, financial markets, policymakers, and ordinary consumers all depend on that information when they make decisions.
