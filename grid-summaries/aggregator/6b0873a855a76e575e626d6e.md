## Labour MPs press prime minister to block Rosebank oil field

Nearly 100 Labour MPs, among them ministerial aides, have urged the prime minister to stop the Rosebank oil field from going ahead. Some junior government figures are reportedly weighing resignation if it is approved.

### A test of Labour's balancing act

The article presents Rosebank as a major test of how Labour weighs its climate commitments against energy security, economic growth and party unity. Pressure is coming from several directions: unions, climate-focused MPs and pro-growth voices inside government are all pulling on the decision.

### Decision expected within weeks

Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh is expected to rule within weeks, after an earlier approval was found to be unlawful. The outcome arrives as Labour works to define its industrial strategy while trying to keep its credibility on climate policy intact.
