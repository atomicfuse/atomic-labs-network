## Rosenthal readies for Railroad Commission seat after win

Democrat Jon Rosenthal has won election to the Texas Railroad Commission and is now preparing to take office. He has outlined several priorities for his time on the panel, which regulates oil and gas in the state.

### What Rosenthal wants to tackle

Rosenthal has pointed to energy production as a central concern as he gets ready to join the commission. He also plans to address abandoned "zombie wells" and the question of how the agency is funded. In addition, he has raised the possibility of renaming the commission, though that idea is only under consideration.

### Why the result matters

His victory is part of a broader Democratic sweep in Texas that shifted power even in lesser-known agencies with large economic influence. Because the Railroad Commission oversees oil and gas regulation, the change carries weight for energy policy, environmental policy, and communities across West Texas.
