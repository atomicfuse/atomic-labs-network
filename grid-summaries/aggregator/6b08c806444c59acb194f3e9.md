## Farm Costs and School Scholarships Take Center Stage in Trenton

The latest episode of *New Jersey Politics with Laura Jones* looks at two issues facing state leaders: a bipartisan proposal to help farmers cut expenses and a campaign to bring a federal scholarship program to New Jersey.

### Bulk-Buying Bill for Farmers

Lawmakers from both parties are backing legislation built around bulk purchasing, with the goal of lowering the cost of supplies for New Jersey farmers. The episode explores the proposal as farmers continue to contend with ongoing cost pressures. By pooling purchasing power, the bill aims to ease some of the financial strain that growers face as they buy the materials they need to operate.

### Push to Join Federal Tax Credit

The episode also covers an effort urging the state to opt into the new federal scholarship tax credit program. Governor Sherrill faces a year-end deadline to decide whether New Jersey will participate. That timeline gives the discussion added urgency, as supporters press the state to take part before the window closes.
