## Tommy Robinson's Ex-Wife Gets Suspended Sentence Over Missing Loan Records

Jenna Lennon, the former wife of Tommy Robinson, has been handed a six-month suspended sentence. The penalty relates to her failure to supply company records to liquidators about a £396,630 loan connected to Hope & Pride Limited.

### What the case involved

Liquidators requested documents explaining the loan but did not receive them. Without those records, they were unable to verify more than £1.1m of activity in the company's bank account. The sentence was suspended, so it is not an immediate custodial term.

### Why it matters now

The ruling arrives while company director accountability, insolvency rules and the finances of high-profile public figures remain under close scrutiny. It also underlines that poor record-keeping can lead to criminal consequences, even where fraud has not been proven.
