## Scotland's chief medical officer steps down citing mental health

Sir Gregor Smith, Scotland's chief medical officer, has resigned, pointing to the pressures on his mental health. He led through the Covid period and has faced scrutiny over avoidable deaths and patient safety failures at NHS Grampian.

### Interim appointment and review

Dame Anna Dominiczak has been appointed interim chief medical officer. Her appointment comes as an independent review looks at what officials knew about the problems at NHS Grampian and how they responded to them.

### Why the timing matters

The resignation arrives amid growing concern over NHS Grampian. A leaked review identified 19 avoidable deaths, and fresh allegations have emerged about surgical safety. The departure also raises questions about accountability and transparency, as well as the mental health toll on those who hold high-pressure public health leadership roles.
