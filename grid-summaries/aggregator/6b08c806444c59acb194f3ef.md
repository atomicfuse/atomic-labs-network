## Thanksgiving Storms Disrupt Holiday Across the Nation

Heavy rain, strong winds, coastal flooding and snow swept across much of the U.S. on Thanksgiving, causing power outages and major travel problems from the East Coast to California.

### What Happened

The storms led to the postponement of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. Widespread power outages left many without electricity, while flight delays and damage to infrastructure added to the disruption. Coastal areas faced flooding as the severe weather moved through different regions of the country.

### Why It Matters

The weather arrived during one of the busiest travel periods of the year, turning holiday plans into safety and logistics challenges for millions of people. The events also highlight how coastal flooding, king tides and extreme storms are increasingly interfering with major public events, transportation systems and local infrastructure.
