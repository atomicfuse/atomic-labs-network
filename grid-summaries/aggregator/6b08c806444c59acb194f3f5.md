## Zelensky Says Washington's Focus Has Drifted Away From Ukraine

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has told the BBC that the United States is too neutral toward Russia and is paying more attention to the Middle East than to the war in his country. His comments come as Russian strikes on Ukraine intensify.

### Criticism of US Policy

Zelensky accused Washington of delaying deliveries of Patriot interceptors, which Ukraine needs for air defense. He argued the US is not pressing Russia hard enough, and his remarks have renewed scrutiny of American priorities and of Donald Trump's posture toward Vladimir Putin. He also discussed stalled peace talks and restrictions on Starlink in occupied areas.

### Pressure Ahead of Winter

Civilian deaths in Ukraine are reportedly at an all-time high as Russia escalates its missile and drone attacks, and peace negotiations show little progress. Zelensky also spoke about Ukraine's long-range strikes and voiced fears of a harsh winter in Kyiv, underscoring the urgency of air defense.
