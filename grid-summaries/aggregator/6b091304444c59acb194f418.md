## A Leukemia Patient Turns Going It Alone Into a Home-Care Guide

Judy Martin, 78, faced leukemia treatment without a full-time caregiver. She has now turned that experience into a practical planning guide for others in the same position.

### Preparing for Treatment at Home

Martin's guide centers on preparation, independence and safety. It aims to help patients organize their homes and routines before exhausting treatment cycles begin, so daily life stays manageable when energy runs low. The advice is also designed to make things easier for friends, relatives or other helpers who step in to support a patient.

### Why the Gap Matters

More older adults are living alone while managing serious illness, and practical planning for care outside the hospital remains a gap in patient support. Martin's story touches on wider conversations about aging, independence, caregiver burden and the challenge of handling complex medical treatment at home.
