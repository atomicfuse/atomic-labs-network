## Study Finds Lifestyle Diseases Widespread Among Keralam's Elderly, Women Hit Harder

A study of senior citizens in Thrissur district has found that close to 70% of them live with lifestyle diseases. Elderly women were affected more than elderly men.

### What the study found

Hypertension, diabetes and high cholesterol were among the conditions seen in the older residents surveyed. The gap between the sexes stood out, with women carrying a heavier burden of these illnesses. The research also flagged broader health, financial and social concerns facing older people as Keralam's population continues to age.

### Why it matters now

Keralam is already dealing with the pressures of an ageing society, which makes elder healthcare and the management of lifestyle habits pressing matters for policymakers and families alike. The higher disease burden among women adds a women's health dimension to the wider conversation about growing older in the state.
