## Is There an Age When Women Can Stop Trying?

Columnist Tracey Cox asked women and men a pointed question: at what age can a woman afford to "let herself go"? The responses she gathered were sharply divided, and she concludes that no clear age cut-off exists.

### Effort, but for whom?

Most of the people consulted still expect some level of effort, whether that means grooming, dressing well or simply looking after oneself. The real disagreement is over motive. Some see effort as a form of self-respect or a courtesy to a partner, while others argue it should be only for the woman herself. Views on comfort, attractiveness and double standards between the sexes added to the split.

### A live cultural tension

The topic lands amid heated debate about beauty standards, ageing, cosmetic work and so-called tweakments. Many women are pushing back against the idea that they must look youthful at all costs, setting self-care against partner expectations and the right to age comfortably.
