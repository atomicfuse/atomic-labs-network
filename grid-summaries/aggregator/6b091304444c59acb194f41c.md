## Lifestyle Coach Pens Menopause Guide Aimed at Men

A lifestyle coach has written a practical book to help men understand and support partners going through perimenopause and menopause. The guide is pitched at readers who want to do more than watch from the sidelines.

### What the book offers

The book explains the symptoms that partners may experience and looks at the strain these changes can place on a relationship. It also sets out a 12-week plan for couples to follow together. The emphasis of that plan is on offering support rather than trying to "fix" what a partner is going through.

### Why it matters now

Menopause is becoming a bigger public conversation, particularly around gaps in medical support and the emotional toll it can take on relationships. Against that backdrop, the guide speaks to a timely need for clearer, more practical advice aimed at partners, who are often left unsure of how best to help.
