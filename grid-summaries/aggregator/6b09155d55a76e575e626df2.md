## Alpina Launches Galaga-Themed Startimer Pilot Solarmetre Watch

Alpina has introduced the Startimer Pilot Solarmetre Galaga, a limited-edition watch that pays tribute to the 1981 arcade classic. It brings retro gaming style to a timepiece in a way designed to catch the eye of collectors and players alike.

### Design and Details

The watch is built around playful Galaga design touches that nod to the original game. It runs on a solar quartz movement. The presentation adds to the fun, as the watch arrives in packaging styled like an arcade cabinet. Production is capped at 1,981 pieces, a number that echoes the year the game first appeared.

### Why It Matters Now

Gaming nostalgia is increasingly driving collectibles across fashion, watches, and tech, as brands tap into the culture of 1980s arcades. This release sits where luxury watchmaking meets gamer fandom, which makes it well timed for people who enjoy retro design and limited drops.
