## Venezuela Moves to Repeal Law Barring Opposition From Office

Venezuela's government plans to scrap the Simón Bolívar Law, which kept people who backed foreign sanctions out of public office, according to the National Assembly speaker. The step is presented as part of a negotiated democratic "route map."

### What is changing

The repeal follows the disputed 2024 election and the removal of Maduro. The National Assembly speaker says the government will do away with the measure, which targeted figures who supported sanctions imposed by other countries. Officials describe the move as one piece of a wider, negotiated plan for the country's political future.

### Why the timing matters

Venezuela is entering what could be a pivotal transition. Interim leader Delcy Rodríguez has promised free elections, while opposition figures are pushing for real guarantees. Repealing the law could reopen the political field for major opposition players, including María Corina Machado.
