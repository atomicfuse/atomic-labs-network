## Taiwan's Local Elections Seen as an Early Test for 2028

Taiwan's upcoming local elections are being examined for what they might say about the next presidential race and about relations across the Taiwan Strait. The contests are increasingly treated as a gauge of national political momentum.

### The Opposition's Alliance

The results could show whether the opposition Kuomintang and Taiwan People's Party, often called the "blue-white" alliance, can remain a workable partnership. How well the two parties coordinate in local races may shape the opposition's strategy as the 2028 presidential contest approaches, making these votes a proxy for broader political direction.

### Beijing's Interest

Beijing is watching the outcome closely, both the level of opposition coordination and the course of local diplomacy. Its attention is particularly focused on what the results could mean for future city-level exchanges between China and Taiwan. The outcome may therefore influence not only domestic planning in Taiwan but also the shape of cross-strait engagement in the years ahead.
