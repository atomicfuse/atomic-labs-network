## Nor'easter Brings King Tides, Fierce Winds and Outages to East Coast

A powerful nor'easter is hitting the East Coast, bringing extreme winds, king tides, coastal flooding and widespread power outages. Thousands of people are already without electricity as the storm moves through.

### What the storm is doing

The system is affecting vulnerable coastal areas in both the Northeast and the Southeast. FOX Weather's Bob Van Dillen is reporting on the impacts across those regions, where strong winds and rising water are combining to create hazardous conditions along the shoreline.

### Why king tides raise the danger

King tides can dramatically worsen storm surge and flooding, which makes this event especially dangerous for coastal communities. With thousands already in the dark, people in affected areas need timely, practical information on staying safe, getting around and protecting their property as the storm continues.
