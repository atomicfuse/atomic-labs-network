## Strobe Talbott, Diplomat and Brookings Leader, Dies at 80

Strobe Talbott, a former deputy secretary of state, Russia expert, journalist and longtime president of the Brookings Institution, has died at the age of 80.

### A career shaping Russia policy

Talbott played a significant part in shaping American policy toward Russia during and after the Clinton administration. His work touched on the expansion of NATO, diplomacy with the countries that emerged after the Soviet Union, and wider thinking about U.S. foreign policy. He combined careers in journalism, government and the leadership of a major research institution.

### Why his legacy resonates today

Tensions between Russia and the West, the readiness of NATO and the future of European security are again central global concerns. Talbott's death prompts a look back at the optimism that followed the Cold War about Russia's democratic future, and at how sharply that path has changed under Vladimir Putin.
