## Violence mars European football fixtures as Macron condemns clashes

Crowd trouble and security incidents were reported across several European football matches, drawing public condemnation from French President Emmanuel Macron and renewed attention to how games are policed.

### What happened at the matches

Clashes were reported at Marseille's game against Levski Sofia, while police confronted fans at Besiktas versus Hapoel Be'er-Sheva. Disturbances also occurred at Sunderland's match with Jagiellonia Bialystok. The reports mention injuries, security worries linked to UEFA, and disputes over who bears responsibility for the trouble. Political figures have voiced criticism of the violence.

### Why it matters now

European football is under fresh scrutiny over fan violence, policing, political tensions and stadium security in international competitions. With governments and federations now speaking publicly, the incidents raise broader questions about how clubs and UEFA handle high-risk fixtures.
