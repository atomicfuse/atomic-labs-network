## Pregnant Inmate Flees Transport SUV at Fast-Food Stop in New Mexico

A pregnant inmate named Rebecca Taylor escaped from a New Mexico corrections vehicle after a prenatal appointment, and a search for her is still underway.

### How the escape unfolded

Authorities say the transport stopped at a Whataburger following the appointment. When the driver got out of the SUV, Taylor drove away in it. She later abandoned the vehicle. Multiple agencies are now working together in an active effort to find her.

### Why the case is drawing attention

The episode has renewed public concern about how inmates are transported, particularly when medical needs and rules on restraining pregnant people make security more complicated. It also arrives amid a broader run of high-profile inmate escapes that have drawn national attention, adding to scrutiny of how such trips are handled.
