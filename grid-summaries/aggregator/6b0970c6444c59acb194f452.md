## Bennett urges Likud unity government as Liberman cautions over Netanyahu tactics

Coalition talks around Gadi Eisenkot's effort to form Israel's next government are intensifying. Naftali Bennett is pushing for a unity government that includes Likud, while Avigdor Liberman warns that Benjamin Netanyahu could use the negotiations to derail the process.

### Ministries and a fragile majority

Tensions are growing over key portfolios, particularly defense and finance. Eisenkot's backing stands at 67 MKs, a figure the article describes as fragile, which leaves little room for disputes among the parties he needs. How the top ministries are divided could decide whether his attempt holds together.

### Why the outcome matters

The next government remains unsettled, and the argument over whether to bring in Likud, haredi parties, or support from Arab parties could shape the country's political direction. Netanyahu's legal and political situation adds urgency. His rivals warn that he may prefer new elections to losing control.
