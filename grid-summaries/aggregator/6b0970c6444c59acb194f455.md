## Trump's Thanksgiving Remark on Iran War Draws Fierce Criticism

Donald Trump is facing backlash after telling deployed U.S. service members during a Thanksgiving call that the Iran war would end "whenever I want it to be." Critics have condemned the comment.

### Criticism of the Remark

Opponents framed the statement as callous toward troops and their families, arguing it showed little regard for those bearing the burden of a prolonged conflict. The remark was made to a service member on the call, and critics have described it in harsh terms, including as despicable.

### Why the Timing Matters

The comment landed during a holiday when many service members are separated from loved ones, which has sharpened scrutiny of Trump's handling of the war. It also feeds a broader debate over presidential war powers, troop deployments, and whether the conflict has a clear endgame.
