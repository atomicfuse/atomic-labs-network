## Russian sabotage in Europe rises 16%, with Ukraine aid routes in the crosshairs

A new report from the IISS says Russian sabotage incidents across Europe increased by 16% in the year to September 2026. Supply lines, logistics hubs and defense-linked infrastructure are increasingly the targets.

### What the report found

The IISS says many attacks still rely on low-skilled proxies. However, it argues that operations directed by Russia are becoming more sophisticated. The growing focus on logistics and defense-related sites suggests the effort is aimed at the networks that keep supplies flowing to Ukraine.

### Why it matters now

Europe's support network for Ukraine is turning into a frontline target, particularly in countries such as Germany and Poland that move military supplies. The warning arrives as Western governments raise alarms about drones, cyberattacks, arson and espionage aimed at disrupting aid to Kyiv.
