## Sánchez's Trump defiance may not save him at home

Spain's Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is heading into a major electoral test. He has cast himself as a leader willing to confront Trump on the world stage, yet he looks increasingly vulnerable among Spanish voters.

### Frustrations at home

Voters are weighing a series of grievances, including housing, corruption and migration. Alongside these concerns, Sánchez faces a serious challenge from the center-right People's Party, which stands to benefit if discontent with the government turns into votes against it.

### A vote with wider meaning

The coming election could reshape Spain's political direction. It also arrives amid broader European debates over immigration, the cost of living and trust in incumbents. Sánchez's predicament points to a wider problem for progressive leaders: global stature does not always translate into support from voters at home.
