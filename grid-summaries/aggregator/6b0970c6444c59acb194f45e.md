## Why Thanksgiving Tables Still Call for Hard Political Talks

A new column says holiday family gatherings remain politically charged after years of polarization tied to the Trump era. Its central claim is that avoiding the subject only delays the deeper conversations Americans need to have with one another.

### The case against staying silent

The writer argues that keeping quiet at the table does not resolve disagreements; it simply pushes them off. Thanksgiving is presented in two ways: as a civic ritual and as an opportunity to better understand relatives who sit on the other side of ideological divides. In that framing, the meal becomes a chance to engage rather than a hazard to be dodged.

### Why the timing matters

The column arrives during the holidays and in the wake of major political shifts. Families are once again weighing whether to steer clear of politics or to confront what recent elections reveal about their shared values. Because polarization still shapes homes, friendships, and policy debates, the dinner table has come to reflect the country's unresolved tensions.
