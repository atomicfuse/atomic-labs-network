## Uganda to pull troops from Lubero amid diplomatic row

Uganda has announced that it will withdraw its troops from Lubero town in eastern DR Congo within two weeks. The deployment there, aimed at ADF militants, has lasted nearly two years.

### What is changing

The withdrawal applies to Lubero town only. Uganda says Operation Shujaa will carry on elsewhere in the region. The announcement follows rising diplomatic tensions after controversial remarks by Uganda's Gen Kainerugaba about rebel activity in mineral-rich eastern Congo.

### Why the timing matters

Eastern DR Congo is experiencing renewed fighting and civilian insecurity, along with growing concern over links between armed groups and mineral resources. The ADF remains one of the deadliest threats in the region. The security partnership between Uganda and DR Congo is now under strain at a sensitive moment.
