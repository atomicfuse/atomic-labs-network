## DOGE Cuts Push Ex-Federal Workers Toward Congressional Runs

Elon Musk's DOGE-driven reductions to the federal workforce have had an effect few anticipated: they have nudged former civil servants into electoral politics, according to one article's argument.

### From Government Jobs to Campaign Trails

The piece points to candidates such as Zach Dembo and Sam Forstag, who left government roles and went on to flip congressional seats. It presents DOGE's efficiency agenda as a catalyst for a fresh wave of public-service candidates, many of them campaigning on accountability and on repairing institutions they say were damaged.

### Why the Backlash Matters Now

With the 2026 midterms reshaping Congress, anger over federal layoffs has become an electoral force in battleground states. The article ties this to wider voter concerns about government competence and the state of public services, and to the question of whether austerity-style reforms actually benefit ordinary people.
