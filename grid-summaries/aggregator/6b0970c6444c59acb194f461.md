## China Drills Use Civilian Vessels as Temporary Floating Bridge Near Taiwan

China is carrying out amphibious landing drills near Taiwan that rely on civilian vessels linked together to form a temporary floating bridge. The structure is used to move troops and equipment ashore.

### What the drills show

According to the article, the exercise suggests Beijing is refining low-profile logistics options. These could matter in a potential contingency involving the first island chain or Taiwan. Using civilian vessels in this way points to an effort to expand the means available for getting forces and materiel onto a shoreline without relying only on purpose-built military assets.

### Why the timing matters

The drills come during sustained tension across the Taiwan Strait, and scrutiny is growing over how China might use civilian shipping in military operations. Their timing near Taiwan's local elections, along with repeated exercises by the People's Liberation Army, makes the activity politically and strategically sensitive.
