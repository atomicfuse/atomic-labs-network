## Dragon Lee Promises Team Mexico Will Bring Fire to Survivor Series

WWE's Dragon Lee is previewing the five-on-five elimination match between Team Mexico and Team USA at Survivor Series: WarGames. He says Mexico's luchadores will deliver "fire in the ring."

### Pride and Leadership

Lee speaks about how proud he is to represent Mexico on one of WWE's biggest stages. He also points to Rey Mysterio's leadership of the team, and he sees the match as a chance to create a major moment for the company.

### Why the Match Stands Out

Survivor Series is one of WWE's biggest annual events, and the Team USA versus Team Mexico throwback format adds national pride and a spotlight on lucha libre. Rey Mysterio, Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Penta and Dragon Lee are all involved, which gives the bout strong crossover appeal for wrestling fans and Latino audiences.
