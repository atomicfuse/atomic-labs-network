## Israel's Right Urged to Trade Grievance for Responsibility

An opinion piece by Roy Folkman argues that Israel's Right stumbled in power because of how it governed, not because of what it wanted. Its aims were legitimate, the article says, but the methods undermined them.

### What the Article Argues

Folkman contends that the Right pursued its goals through force, a sense of grievance, and contempt for professional institutions. In his view, that approach cost it success in government. He calls instead for a political culture built on responsibility, compromise, and principled disagreement, in which opponents can differ without treating each other as enemies.

### Why It Matters Now

The piece arrives after a full term of fully right-wing rule, as debates over judicial reform, media law, and draft legislation continue. It ties these disputes to a wider crisis of governance and polarization in Israel. The next political challenge, it suggests, is not only ideological but also procedural and cultural, concerning how decisions are made and how institutions are treated.
