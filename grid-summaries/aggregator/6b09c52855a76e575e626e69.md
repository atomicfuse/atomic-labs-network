## GOP Doubts Over Tariffs Open a Door for Democrats

Some congressional Republicans are showing new willingness to revisit President Trump's tariffs after election losses linked to rising costs. That shift may give Democrats a possible bipartisan route to challenge parts of his trade agenda.

### Canada tariffs as an early test

The article points to tariffs on Canada as a likely first test of this emerging opening. Even so, Trump's veto power remains a major obstacle, which could limit how far any challenge to his trade policies can go.

### Why it matters now

Affordability has become a defining political issue, and tariffs are increasingly blamed for higher costs facing consumers, farms, factories, and small businesses. With Democrats retaking Congress and Republicans reassessing Trump's economic agenda, trade policy could quickly become an early flashpoint between the two parties and the White House.
