## Illinois Governor Draws Fire for Turning Down Federal School Choice Plan

Gov. JB Pritzker of Illinois has rejected a federal school choice program, prompting criticism from local advocates who wanted the state to take part.

### The Pushback in Illinois

Marsha McClary of Moms for Liberty is among those objecting to the governor's decision. She argues that families need more education options, particularly in areas where public schools are underperforming. Her criticism reflects the view of advocates who see the federal program as a way to give parents more say over where their children learn, and who are unhappy that Illinois families will not have access to it.

### A Wider National Debate

School choice remains a major political flashpoint as states clash over federal education policy, parental rights and public school funding. The Illinois decision feeds into a broader argument over whether education dollars should follow students or remain within traditional public school systems. That question continues to divide officials and advocates across the country.
