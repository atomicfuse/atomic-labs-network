## Post-Thanksgiving Storm Eases, but Outages and Flood Risks Linger on the East Coast

A major storm system that struck after Thanksgiving is winding down along the East Coast, though hundreds of thousands of people are still without power. The storm brought flooding, travel disruption and rescues, while other parts of the country dealt with different kinds of severe weather.

### East Coast Flooding and Outages

Heavy rain caused flooding across the East Coast, and widespread power outages continued even as the downpours eased. Travel was disrupted, and emergency crews carried out rescues. Coastal flood threats remain in place, so some areas may still face trouble despite the rain tapering off.

### Weather Elsewhere and Why It Matters

Other regions faced snow and freezing rain, and the West Coast saw flooding from king tides. The storm landed during one of the busiest holiday travel periods, adding strain for families, commuters and emergency services.
