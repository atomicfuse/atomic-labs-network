## Student List Picks Chemistry Professor for Serbian Presidential Race

Serbia's Student List movement has chosen its candidate for the early presidential election. The group, which won last month's general election, put forward Radomir Saicic, a chemistry professor at Belgrade University.

### The nomination

Saicic is an academic rather than a career politician, and he will carry the movement's banner into the early vote for head of state. The announcement comes shortly after the movement's success in the general election, giving the campaign a fresh burst of momentum as it moves to the next contest.

### Why it matters

The choice shows an effort to convert student-led political energy into control of the presidency. If the movement succeeds, the result could reshape Serbia's leadership and challenge the country's political establishment. The early presidential election will now be an important test of how far that momentum can carry.
