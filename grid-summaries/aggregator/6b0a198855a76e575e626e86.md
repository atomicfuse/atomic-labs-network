## Pakistan and Turkey Back Saudi Arabia Against Houthi Threat

Defense officials from Pakistan, Turkey and Saudi Arabia met in Islamabad to discuss security cooperation under the Joint Mecca Defense Agreement. The three countries reaffirmed that an attack on Saudi Arabia by Yemen's Houthi rebels would bring collective support.

### What the talks covered

The meeting centered on the pact, which the article describes as defensive in nature. Participants restated that Houthi strikes on Saudi territory would prompt a joint response. Some observers, however, worry the arrangement could draw Pakistan or Turkey further into conflicts across the Middle East.

### Why it matters now

Gulf security has become a live flashpoint. Houthi attacks, risks to Red Sea shipping, and tensions involving Iran, Israel and the United States all add pressure. Turkey's ratification of the agreement and Pakistan's pledge to use "whatever means are available" point to a more formal regional security bloc forming around Saudi Arabia.
