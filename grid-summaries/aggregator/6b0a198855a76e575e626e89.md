## NBC Withdraws Anti-Israel Ad After Backlash From Jewish Groups

NBC has pulled an advertisement that ran during a Thanksgiving "Saturday Night Live" special, following criticism from Jewish organizations and pro-Israel advocates.

### What drew the criticism

Critics objected to the ad's message, which blamed U.S. support for Israel for the suffering of Palestinians. They said it left out Hamas' Oct. 7 attack and the wider context of the Gaza war, and that the omission made the ad one-sided. The reaction from Jewish groups and pro-Israel advocates was strong, and NBC removed the spot after it aired.

### Why it matters now

The dispute arrives during heightened tensions over Israel, Gaza and antisemitism. It also puts a spotlight on media responsibility in political advertising, and raises timely questions about how major networks vet advocacy ads during such a volatile news moment.
