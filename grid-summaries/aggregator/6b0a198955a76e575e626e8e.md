## Activists Say China's Global Appeal Is Muffling Calls for Rights

Uyghur, Tibetan and other dissident campaigners say their voices are fading internationally as China's economic and cultural influence grows. An article examining the shift describes activists who fear that Beijing's rising profile is shifting attention away from their causes.

### Softer tactics, splintered networks

Activists argue that Beijing has moved toward gentler methods, including family visits, tourism messaging and incentives aimed at overseas communities. They say these approaches are fragmenting exile networks and easing the international pressure that once surrounded their campaigns.

### Rights slip down the agenda

As Chinese electric vehicles, technology, tourism campaigns and consumer culture reach audiences worldwide, human rights concerns are ranking lower on diplomatic and public agendas. Western governments are focused on trade, populism and geopolitical bargaining, and activists worry their causes are being overshadowed at a critical moment.
