## ACLU Asks Court to Stop Government Bids to Unmask Anonymous ICE Critics

The American Civil Liberties Union has gone to court to stop the Trump administration from using grand jury subpoenas to identify anonymous social media users who criticize ICE and DHS. The case puts online speech and government investigative power in direct conflict.

### The ACLU's Argument

The group says the administration's effort amounts to a bad-faith investigation. In its view, the subpoenas violate First Amendment protections and have a chilling effect on political dissent online. The ACLU is seeking a court order that would block the government from pursuing the identities of these critics through the technology platforms they use.

### Why the Timing Matters

The lawsuit arrives during heightened conflict over immigration enforcement, online speech, and government surveillance powers. A hearing on Jan. 6 could help shape how far federal agencies may go in compelling tech platforms to reveal who is behind anonymous criticism of the government.
