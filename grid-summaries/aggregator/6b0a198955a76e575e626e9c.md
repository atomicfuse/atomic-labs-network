## California Bill Ties Booster Seat Rules to Seat Belt Fit, Not Just Age

California has passed Assembly Bill 435, a law that could keep some teenagers in booster seats until they turn 16. Children between 8 and 15 would need to pass a seat belt fit test before they can ride without a booster or sit in the front seat.

### What the law requires

Under the measure, children ages 8 to 15 must pass a five-step test showing that a seat belt fits them properly. The law holds that fit, rather than age or height alone, is what matters most for protecting young passengers. The reasoning comes as crash injuries among children have increased.

### Why it matters now

The rule takes effect in the new year. Families with older kids and teens who once aged out of booster requirements may now need to reassess how those children ride. The change arrives amid renewed attention on child passenger safety and on the widespread misuse of car seats and boosters.
