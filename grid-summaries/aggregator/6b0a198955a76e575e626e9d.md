## New York Senate Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins Dies at 76

Andrea Stewart-Cousins, the New York state Senate majority leader, has died at the age of 76. She was the first woman to hold the post, and her death is a significant loss for the state's government.

### A Trailblazing Leader

Stewart-Cousins made history by becoming the first woman to serve as majority leader in the New York state Senate. Her passing draws attention to the role she held and to the barriers she broke while leading the chamber, marking the end of a notable chapter in New York politics.

### What Her Death Means Now

Her death raises questions about who will succeed her and how the Senate's leadership will move forward. It also puts a spotlight on the legacy of women who have broken barriers in state government, at a time when representation and institutional power remain central to political conversations.
