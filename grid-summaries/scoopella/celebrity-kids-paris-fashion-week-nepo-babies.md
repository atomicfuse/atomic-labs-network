## Celebrity Kids Steal the Spotlight at Paris Fashion Week

Paris Fashion Week this season was as much about who walked as what they wore. Several celebrity children made their runway debuts, reviving questions about whether fashion houses are scouting new talent or banking on famous surnames for publicity.

### The Debut Lineup

Apple Martin, daughter of Gwyneth Paltrow and Chris Martin, walked for Chloé. Ella Bleu Travolta, John Travolta's daughter, and Lily Brant also took their first official runway turns. Other famous offspring sat in the front rows.

### What Brands Gain

The column argues that casting a name like Martin's brings built-in media attention and social reach. Brands get guaranteed press, and families add a prestigious credit, while unknown models who have trained for years may struggle to get a casting director's notice. The writer stresses that the young women themselves are not the target of the criticism.

### Parents in the Background

Paltrow's decades as a film star and Goop founder, and Travolta's fame from Saturday Night Fever, Grease and Pulp Fiction, make comparisons between generations unavoidable.

### An Old Gatekeeping Problem

The piece says the industry has long had an access problem, and that casting famous children decorates it rather than fixing it. It calls the pipeline infrastructure rather than a passing trend, and says the real test is whether these debuts lead to lasting careers.
