## Two Standout Parties Define the Social Side of Paris Fashion Week

Paris Fashion Week isn't only about the runway. This season, some of the most talked-about moments happened at evening events around the city, with two gatherings drawing particular attention: a launch for an Elie Saab and H&M capsule collection, and an accessories celebration hosted by Vera Wang.

### Elie Saab x H&M at Palais Brongniart

The collaboration between Elie Saab and H&M was introduced at Palais Brongniart, with plenty of celebrities in attendance. A surprise reveal of a bridal gown added a fairy-tale touch to the evening.

### Vera Wang's Verafied Celebration

At Hôtel Barrière Le Fouquets, Wang hosted a smaller event for a select crowd, built around accessories that felt both new and nostalgic. The focus fell on conversation about design choices rather than spectacle, with Wang treating accessories as statements.

### Why the Events Matter

The article sees the Saab partnership as a sign of luxury blending with mass-market appeal, offering accessibility while keeping prestige. Wang's event, in contrast, points to the value of heritage storytelling in modern design.

### A Lasting Impression

Together, the parties show fashion as a narrative shaped by collaboration, accessible luxury and surprise, and they keep the style conversation going in Paris.
