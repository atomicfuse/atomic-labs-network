When faced with fertility challenges, couples often experience stress and uncertainty about the next steps. Recently, scientists at the University of Aberdeen and NHS Grampian introduced a new fertility calculator that estimates a couple’s chances of conceiving naturally before they dive into IVF treatments. This tool can empower hopeful parents with knowledge about their fertility odds and enable meaningful conversations with their fertility specialists.

## The Value of the New Fertility Calculator

The calculator is based on a study revealing that 13% of couples diagnosed with fertility issues managed to achieve a natural pregnancy leading to a live birth within a year. This statistic can be significant for couples considering their options and can influence crucial decisions regarding the pursuit of IVF.

The calculator works by taking into account individual circumstances, which can personalize the information provided to couples. By offering tailored predictions, it serves as a reliable starting point in determining whether to continue trying naturally or to explore assisted reproductive options like IVF.

## Key Questions to Discuss with Your Fertility Specialist

As you prepare to engage with your fertility specialist about potential IVF treatments, consider asking the following questions:

### 1. What is our estimated chance of conceiving naturally?

Utilize the insights from the fertility calculator to ask your specialist about your unique situation and chances for natural conception. Understanding this before jumping into IVF can help clarify whether additional attempts at natural conception are worthwhile.

### 2. How does our age affect our fertility prospects?

Age is a critical factor in fertility. According to data, the likelihood of natural conception decreases with age. Asking this question will help you comprehend the timeline and assist you in making decisions aligned with your reproductive goals.

### 3. What factors from our medical history should we consider?

Every couple's health history plays a role in their fertility journey. Engage in a conversation about how factors such as duration of infertility, past medical conditions, and lifestyle choices might influence your chances to conceive. This tailored approach helps in making informed decisions.

### 4. What is the emotional and financial impact of pursuing IVF?

Discussing the emotional and financial implications of IVF is crucial. Many couples face significant costs and emotional uncertainty. Understanding these aspects upfront will prepare you better for the IVF process.

### 5. What are the next steps if we decide to proceed with IVF?

If the fertility calculator and your specialist's insights indicate that IVF is the right course of action, ask about the typical timeline, required tests, and preparation steps for the procedure. Knowing what lies ahead can alleviate some anxiety surrounding the unknowns.

It’s essential to approach fertility issues with compassion and understanding.
