## The Sink Topper, Explained

Per BuzzFeed, the product is a foldable topper that sits over your sink and creates a flat surface where there wasn't one. That's the whole pitch, and it's a good one. In a small bathroom, the sink is often the largest unused horizontal area in the room, and it's trapped behind a basin shape that's useless for setting things down.

The idea is simple enough that it triggers the exact reaction the roundup's title is fishing for: why didn't I think of that? It's hard not to see the appeal. You get temporary counter space for a skincare routine, a hair appliance, or a stack of towels, and when you need the sink again, the topper folds away.

## How to Judge a "Genius" Product Yourself

A clever design is only clever if it survives daily use. Here's a quick test I'd run on the sink topper, and on anything else from a list like this:

- **Does it solve a problem you actually have?** If you never run out of counter space, a counter-space gadget is just clutter with good branding.
- **Does it store easily?** Foldable is the selling point, so make sure there's somewhere for it to live when it's folded.
- **Is it stable?** Anything that sits over a basin should be checked for wobble before you trust it with your good moisturizer.
- **Is it easy to clean?** Bathrooms are damp. A surface that traps water or grime defeats the purpose.
