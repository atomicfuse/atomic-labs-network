## What Is a Soft Launch, Anyway?

The "soft launch" is the idea of subtly revealing a relationship through social media rather than making it official: a hand in frame, a shared view, an elbow that is suspiciously not yours. The cropped photo does the work a caption used to.

It's easy to see the appeal, and this is my read, not anything either actor has said. A soft launch lets you share something without owning it. If the relationship is real, fans feel in on the secret. If it isn't, nobody has to issue a correction. Deniability, but make it aesthetic.

## The Home Detective Kit

Here's where this becomes weirdly practical. Fan sleuthing is basically a home organization project: you take a pile of loose items (photos, tags, timestamps, outfits) and sort them into a system until a pattern shows. Folders, labels, a corkboard with red string. The *Pinterest* of parasocial life.

A few ground rules for doing it well:

1. **Separate what's confirmed from what's inferred.** A shared location is a clue. It is not a verdict.
2. **Remember there are real people on the other end.** Two actors sharing a city, a fashion house, or a restaurant isn't an invitation to demand their dating status.
3. **Let the people involved tell their own story.** If a couple wants to keep things quiet, that's a perfectly good plan.
