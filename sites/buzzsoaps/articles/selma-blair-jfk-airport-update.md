## Bail, Decoded

The $20,000 cash or $60,000 bond figure sounds like a riddle, but the idea is simple. Bail is the amount set to secure a defendant's release while the case is pending. Cash bail means paying the full $20,000 up front. A bond is the alternative: a bond is set at $60,000, and the defendant typically works through a bonding service rather than putting up the whole sum. The reporting we have doesn't say which option, if either, Blair used, so we won't guess.
