The performance gap observed in the test can be attributed to several factors:
  
1. **Tread Design**: The Michelin CrossClimate 2 features an innovative tread pattern designed for both wet and dry conditions. It helps channel water away from the contact patch, reducing the risk of hydroplaning.
  
2. **Rubber Composition**: Premium tires often utilize advanced rubber blends that enhance grip and provide better longevity. The material used in Michelin tires is engineered for performance, particularly in unpredictable weather conditions.
  
3. **Handling Characteristics**: High-quality tires offer more responsive handling, allowing drivers to feel more connected to the road. This translates to greater confidence when cornering and reacting to sudden changes in driving conditions.
