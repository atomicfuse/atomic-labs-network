As Labor Day approaches, it symbolizes not just the end of summer but also the perfect opportunity to gather with friends and enjoy one last taste of warm-weather flavors. Whether you're hosting a backyard BBQ, a beach gathering, or a cozy evening at home, these 11 cocktails will help you celebrate the season's transition with flair. Each drink features a blend of summer fruits and spirits that you can whip up in no time, making them perfect for any occasion.

## 1. Hennessy Berry Mojito
The Hennessy Berry Mojito combines the refreshing essence of mint and citrus with the rich flavors of Hennessy. Muddle fresh mint leaves with berries and lime juice, add Hennessy and top it off with soda water. This cocktail is a vibrant tribute to the summer fruit bounty.

## 2. Royal Treatment
This drink provides a luxurious experience with a combination of gin, elderflower liqueur, and fresh lemon juice. Shake the ingredients together and serve over ice, garnished with a lemon twist. It’s perfect for a brunch gathering or a relaxed afternoon refreshment.

## 3. Campari Spritz
A quintessential summer spritz that can transition into fall nicely, this drink features Campari, prosecco, and a splash of soda water served over ice with an orange slice. Its bittersweet profile is ideal for those who appreciate a sophisticated cocktail while soaking up the last rays of summer.

## 4. Knob Creek Peach Old Fashioned
Elevate the classic Old Fashioned by adding a hint of peach. Muddle a peach slice with sugar and bitters, add Knob Creek bourbon, and a twist of orange. This cocktail melds summer fruit with the deeper flavors of whiskey, making it a perfect après-dinner option.

## 5. The Open Road
This refreshing concoction combines vodka, fresh grapefruit juice, and a splash of tonic for a light, zesty drink that captures summer’s end. Serve in a frosted glass with a grapefruit wedge for that extra seasonal touch.

## 6. Mango Margarita
Take a trip to tropical bliss with a Mango Margarita. Blend fresh or frozen mango, lime juice, and tequila, then serve it on the rocks with a salt rim. This cocktail is not only delicious but also visually stunning, bringing a bit of sunshine to your Labor Day celebration.

## 7. Aspen Dirty Shirley
For a playful spin on the classic Shirley Temple, this version incorporates vodka and cherry juice alongside the traditional ginger ale and maraschino cherries. It provides a nostalgic taste while still appealing to adults, making it a fun option for gatherings.

## 8. Haku Matcha Martini
For a unique twist, try the Haku Matcha Martini, which blends vodka, matcha, and a touch of simple syrup. It’s sophisticated yet approachable, and the bright green color is sure to impress your guests while providing a refreshing taste.

## 9. Roku Gin Fizz
Showcasing the floral notes of Japanese Roku gin, this cocktail combines gin, fresh lemon juice, and a splash of soda. It’s light and bubbly, perfect for sipping while enjoying a sunset on your patio or at the beach.

## 10. The Rémy V en Rose
Light, fruity, and oh-so-refreshing, this cocktail pairs Rémy Martin VSOP with fresh berries and a splash of sparkling rosé. The drink is as delightful as it sounds, and it serves as a beautiful centerpiece for any summer farewell party.

## 11. ALB (A Little Berry)
Last but not least, the ALB cocktail brings together vodka, berry-infused syrup, and lemonade. It's incredibly easy to make and tastes just like summer. Serve it in a mason jar for a rustic, charming look.

### Closing Thoughts
As you gear up for Labor Day, these cocktails are not just drinks but also a canvas for showcasing summer’s bounty and your hosting skills. With their vibrant flavors and easy recipes, you can enjoy the seasonal transition with style and taste. Cheers to the unofficial end of summer, and may these cocktails bring joy to your celebrations!
