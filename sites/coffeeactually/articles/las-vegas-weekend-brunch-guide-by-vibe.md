Vegas brunch has stopped being just eggs and a mimosa. Per a roundup from Las Vegas Weekly, four resorts and restaurants are pitching weekend brunch as a full outing, with everything from poolside Pilates to a prime rib buffet. This guide matches each one to the kind of morning you want, so you can book once and not regret it.

One note before we start: the original roundup is sponsored, and the details below are limited to what it reports. It doesn't list prices, hours, or reservation rules, so check each venue before you commit.

## If You Want a Wellness Reset: Resorts World

Resorts World leads with the most unexpected pairing: Poolside Pulse Pilates at AYU Dayclub, followed by brunch at FUHU. You move first, then eat, which is a pretty sensible order of operations on a weekend where the rest of your itinerary may not be so virtuous.

The resort also offers a second option, a Latin-inspired brunch at ¡VIVA!. If Pilates isn't your thing but you still want something with a theme, that's your alternative under the same roof.

**Best for:** Travelers who want to start the day feeling good, or anyone splitting a group between the active and the hungry.

## If You Want a Party Brunch: STK Las Vegas

STK's All You Can Brunch combines chef-driven bites, cocktails, and live DJs. That last part sets the tone: this is brunch with a soundtrack, closer to the start of a night out than a quiet morning with a newspaper.

**Best for:** Groups celebrating something, or anyone who thinks brunch should have some energy behind it.

## If You Want Brunch With Built-In Entertainment: Play Playground Las Vegas

Play Playground offers a brunch menu, bottomless mimosas and Bloody Marys, and more than 20 interactive games. The games solve a real brunch problem: the long, lingering table where conversation runs out of steam. Give people something to do and the meal stretches comfortably.

The roundup doesn't specify the terms of the bottomless drinks, so ask about time limits and what's included when you book.

**Best for:** Mixed groups, friends who like a little competition, and anyone who'd rather play than sit.

## If You Want the Classic Feast: South Point Garden Buffet

South Point's Garden Buffet goes the traditional route: a prime rib and Champagne brunch with global buffet stations. It's the pick for people who want to walk the line, sample a bit of everything, and circle back for the thing they liked best.

It's also the clearest contrast with the others. Where STK and Play Playground sell atmosphere, the buffet sells choice.

**Best for:** Big appetites, groups with different tastes, and anyone who values variety over vibe.

## How to Choose Between Them

Think about the one thing you want from the morning:

- **Movement and calm:** Resorts World
- **Music and cocktails:** STK Las Vegas
- **Games and bottomless drinks:** Play Playground
- **Prime rib and variety:** South Point Garden Buffet

Bottomless formats and buffets are different bets. Bottomless rewards how much you plan to drink, while a buffet rewards how much you plan to eat. Since the roundup gives no prices, compare the actual costs yourself before deciding which is the better value for your group.

## A Quick Planning Tip

Brunch sets the rhythm for the whole day. A Pilates-and-brunch morning leaves you energized for the afternoon, while a DJ brunch or bottomless session may make a late start tomorrow more realistic. Pick the one that fits what comes after.

And since this is a coffee site: none of the four are described as coffee destinations in the roundup, so if a proper cup matters to you, ask what's on the drink menu before you reserve. Decide what you want from the morning, then book the one that delivers it.
