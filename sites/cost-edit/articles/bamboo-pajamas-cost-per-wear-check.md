## The cost-per-wear formula

The math is simple:

**(Price after discount + shipping or tax) ÷ number of nights you'll wear them = cost per wear**

The discount changes the top number. The part most people skip is the bottom one: how many nights a set will realistically get worn.

## Why wear count matters more than the sticker

The reviewer's pitch rests on two claims: the fabric breathes, and it holds up. Both feed directly into your formula.

- **Breathability** affects how many nights you actually reach for them. Pajamas that make you overheat end up in the drawer.
- **Durability** affects the years in your denominator. Fox's review calls them durable, but that's one writer's account, so treat it as a claim to check against return policies and your own wash tests.

If you own a favorite set you wear every week, that's your benchmark. A new pair has to beat its cost per night or give you something it can't, like staying cool.

## Compare on the things that decide it

The angle that fits hot sleepers is comparing fabrics on what bothers you at 2 a.m. Judge each option on:

1. **Overheating:** Do you wake up warm? Note it for a week in your current pajamas first.
2. **Softness after washing:** Check reviews that mention several washes, not first-wear impressions.
3. **Fit:** The Cozy Earth set is described as stretch-knit, which matters if you dislike restrictive waistbands.
4. **Return window:** A generous one lowers your risk more than any coupon.

## If you're buying it as a gift

The review positions the pajamas as a giftable winter pick. Gifts skew the math, because you're guessing at someone else's wear count. Ask yourself whether the person already owns pajamas they love. If they sleep hot, a breathable set may be genuinely useful. If you're unsure of size or preference, confirm the return terms before you buy.

## Stack the savings carefully

- **Enter the code at checkout** and confirm the 20% shows before payment. The offer runs through Dec. 12 per Fox.
- **Don't buy just because it's discounted.** A 20% cut on something you won't wear costs you 100%.
- **Check for shipping and return fees,** which can eat into the savings.
- **Compare same-day prices** at other retailers for the cheaper set, so your table is apples to apples.

## The takeaway

Before you click buy, write down how many nights you'll wear the set this winter, then divide the final price by that number. If the cost per night beats what you're paying now, or solves a real sleeping problem, the code is a bonus. If not, skip it.
