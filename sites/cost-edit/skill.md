## Content Guidelines
Everyday spending. Cover groceries, takeout, subscriptions, and household essentials. Example: When Is the Bulk Pack Actually Cheaper?
Bills and recurring costs. Explain common charges and how to compare plans for internet, mobile service, and utilities. Example: What to Check Before Your Internet Promo Rate Ends.
The real cost of buying. Break down the upfront and ongoing costs of purchases like printers, coffee machines, and small appliances. Example: What a Budget Printer Costs After You Buy the Ink.
Make comparisons easy to verify. Use simple tables, show the math, and include the date and location for price examples. Use the same units across options and account for fees or renewal rates when they affect the total.
