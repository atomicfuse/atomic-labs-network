Forbes reports that around La Fortuna, Costa Rica, local ingredients and traditional Costa Rican cooking are increasingly becoming the kind of dining luxury travelers prefer. Restaurants there are taking regional food, the kind grandmothers have long made, and treating it as the main attraction.

You don't need a plane ticket to borrow the idea. The heart of it is simple: humble, local, well-made food is worth savoring. So here is a cozy, one-pot version of a Costa Rican staple, sopa negra (black bean soup), that serves four and takes about an hour.

The Forbes piece, as summarized for us, doesn't name specific dishes or recipes, so this one is our own home-kitchen take on a traditional style of cooking, not a recreation of anything served in La Fortuna.

## Why Black Bean Soup Fits a Chilly Evening

Sopa negra is the kind of dinner that rewards patience without demanding much effort. One pot, pantry staples, and a pot of gently simmered beans that fills the kitchen with smell of onion and cumin. It also stretches well, which makes it a friendly weeknight option and an easy one to double for leftovers.

## Sopa Negra for Four

**Time:** 15 minutes prep, 40 minutes simmer
**Serves:** 4 generous bowls

### Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 sweet bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 3 cans (15 oz each) black beans, undrained
- 3 cups broth (vegetable or chicken)
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and black pepper
- Juice of 1 lime
- Chopped fresh cilantro, for serving
- 4 eggs (optional, for poaching in the soup)

### Steps

1. **Soften the base.** Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt. Cook 6 to 8 minutes, until soft and just turning golden.
2. **Add the aromatics.** Stir in the garlic, cumin, and oregano. Cook 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. **Simmer.** Add the beans with their liquid, the broth, and the bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, partly covered, for 30 minutes.
4. **Thicken.** Remove the bay leaf. Use a potato masher or immersion blender to mash about half the beans right in the pot. You want a soup that is creamy but still has some whole beans.
5. **Finish.** Stir in the lime juice and season with salt and pepper. Taste it; lime is what makes the soup taste bright instead of flat.
6. **Optional eggs.** For a heartier bowl, crack the eggs gently into the simmering soup, cover, and cook 3 to 4 minutes until the whites set.
7. **Serve** with cilantro on top.

## Affordable Swaps

- **No fresh bell pepper?** Use a handful of frozen diced peppers, or skip it and add an extra half onion.
- **Dried beans instead of canned:** Use 1 pound dried beans, soaked overnight, and simmer about 1 to 1.5 hours in 6 cups of water or broth. It costs less and the flavor is deeper, but plan ahead.
- **No fresh cilantro:** Thinly sliced green onion or a squeeze of extra lime works fine.
- **No lime:** Use a teaspoon of vinegar, added at the end.
- **Make it meatier:** Add diced cooked ham or sausage when you soften the onion.

## What to Serve Alongside

Rice is the natural partner. A scoop of plain white rice in the bowl, or on the side, turns the soup into a full dinner. Add warm tortillas or a simple cabbage-and-tomato salad with lime and salt for crunch.

## Small-Batch and Slow-Cooker Options

**For two:** Use 1.5 cans of beans (or 1 can plus a splash more broth), 1.5 cups broth, half an onion, and 2 cloves of garlic. Simmer 25 minutes.

**Slow cooker:** Sauté the onion, pepper, and spices first if you have five minutes, then add everything except the lime and cilantro. Cook on low for 6 to 7 hours, mash partway, and finish with lime.

## Storing and Reheating

The soup keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days and freezes well for about 3 months. It thickens as it sits, so loosen it with a splash of broth or water when reheating. Hold off on cilantro and any eggs until you serve.

## The Takeaway

The idea behind the La Fortuna dining trend is that simple, local, traditional food deserves care. Try that tonight: pick one humble pot of beans, season it well, finish it with fresh lime, and put it on the table while it's hot.
