## Small-Batch Jeera Soda for Two

**Makes:** 2 tall glasses
**Time:** 5 minutes, plus optional 10 minutes of chilling

### What You Need

- 1 teaspoon whole cumin seeds (or 3/4 teaspoon ground cumin)
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon or lime juice (about 1 lemon)
- 2 tablespoons sugar, or 2 tablespoons honey
- 1/4 teaspoon salt, plus a pinch more to taste
- 1/4 cup hot water
- 1 1/2 cups chilled sparkling water or plain club soda
- Ice
- Fresh mint leaves, optional

### Steps

1. **Toast the cumin.** Warm a small dry pan over medium heat. Add the whole seeds and stir for 30 to 45 seconds, until fragrant and a shade darker. Don't walk away; cumin scorches quickly and turns bitter.
2. **Make the syrup.** Crush the toasted seeds lightly with the back of a spoon or a mortar. Stir them into the hot water with the sugar and 1/4 teaspoon salt until dissolved. Let it steep 5 minutes.
3. **Add the citrus.** Stir in the lemon juice. Taste it. It should be bright, a little salty, and distinctly cumin-forward, since the fizz will dilute it.
4. **Strain and chill.** Pour through a small sieve if you want a clear drink, or leave the bits in for a rustic look. Chill 10 minutes if you have time.
5. **Assemble.** Fill two glasses with ice, divide the syrup between them, and top each with about 3/4 cup sparkling water. Stir gently and add mint.

## Affordable Swaps and Fixes

- **No whole cumin?** Ground works. Use 3/4 teaspoon and toast it for just 15 seconds.
- **No sparkling water?** Use cold plain water for a still, lemonade-style cooler.
- **Lemon unavailable?** Bottled lemon juice is fine; start with 1 1/2 tablespoons and adjust.
- **Want it sweeter?** Add sugar a teaspoon at a time. A little salt makes the sweetness taste rounder, so try that before adding more sugar.
- **Want more kick?** A pinch of black pepper or chaat masala-style seasoning, if you keep it on hand, adds a savory edge.

## Make Ahead and Scale Up

The syrup (steps 1 through 4) keeps in a jar in the fridge for up to 3 days. Double or triple it for a family dinner, then add the sparkling water only when you pour so the bubbles stay lively. For four people, use 2 teaspoons cumin, 1/4 cup lemon juice, 1/4 cup sugar, and 1/2 cup hot water, topped with 3 cups sparkling water.

## What to Serve It With

This drink shines next to rich, warming food. Think a pot of lentil soup, a baked rice casserole, or a slow-cooker chicken curry on a chilly evening. The salt, citrus and toasted spice cut through heavy dishes and keep each bite tasting fresh.
