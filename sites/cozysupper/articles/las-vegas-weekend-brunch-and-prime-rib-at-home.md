## The four brunches, in brief

## Bring the prime rib brunch home

The buffet idea is the easiest to copy in your own kitchen. A small roast, a few sides, and something sparkling turn an ordinary Sunday into an occasion. Here's a tested-style plan for a small-batch version that serves four.

### Easy oven-roasted beef for four

Prime rib is pricey, so use a 2 to 3 pound boneless beef roast (rib-eye roast if you can find it on sale, or a top sirloin roast as the budget swap).

1. Pull the roast out of the fridge 45 minutes before cooking, and heat the oven to 450°F.
2. Rub with 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 tablespoon kosher salt, 2 teaspoons black pepper, and 2 minced garlic cloves. A teaspoon of dried thyme is lovely, too.
3. Roast 15 minutes at 450°F, then lower to 325°F.
4. Keep roasting until a thermometer reads 120 to 125°F for medium-rare. Expect roughly 45 to 70 minutes more, depending on size and shape. Sirloin is leaner, so pull it at the lower end.
5. Rest 20 minutes, loosely tented with foil. The temperature will climb about 5 to 10 degrees as it rests.
6. Slice thin against the grain.

A thermometer matters more than the clock. Ovens and roasts vary.

### Build your own "stations"

The Garden Buffet's appeal is variety. You can borrow that with a table of simple dishes, most made ahead:

- **Potatoes:** Toss 2 pounds baby potatoes with oil and salt, and roast alongside the beef for about 40 minutes.
- **Eggs:** A baked egg casserole (8 eggs, 1 cup milk, 2 cups cubed bread, 1 cup shredded cheese) bakes at 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes. Assemble it the night before.
- **Something green:** Roasted green beans or a simple arugula salad with lemon.
- **Something sweet:** Warm cinnamon rolls from a tube are perfectly respectable.

### The bubbly part

Sparkling wine works, but a pitcher of mimosas stretches further: one bottle of dry sparkling wine plus 2 cups orange juice makes about 6 glasses. For a no-alcohol table, use sparkling water with the juice. Cava or prosecco is a budget-friendly swap for Champagne.

## Match the brunch to your crowd

The roundup's four options map neatly onto four kinds of weekends, whether you're traveling or staying put.

- **Wellness reset (Resorts World):** At home, take a walk or do a short stretch session, then serve a lighter spread of eggs, fruit, and yogurt.
- **Party brunch (STK):** Put on a playlist, set out small bites, and keep the cocktails flowing.
- **Play-and-linger brunch (Play Playground):** Pull out a deck of cards or a board game and keep the table going for hours.
- **Classic feast (South Point):** Go all in on the roast and a sparkling toast.

## Planning tips if you do go to Vegas

Because the roundup doesn't cover costs or logistics, treat these as questions to ask before booking:
