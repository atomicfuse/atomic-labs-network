Bill Michaels of Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard recently appeared on "Fox & Friends Weekend" to share autumn apple cider ideas, per Fox News. The centerpiece was a hot mulled cider in a caramel-rimmed glass, topped with whipped cream and mulling spices, served alongside extra-sharp cheddar cheese.

By the end of this article, you'll have a way to make that kind of café-style mulled cider in your own kitchen, and a simple plan for serving it with cheddar on a chilly evening. The segment's elements are the caramel rim, whipped cream, mulling spices, and cheddar. The quantities and timings below are my own practical suggestions, not Michaels' recipe.

## What Michaels Put in the Glass

According to the segment description, the drink has four parts:

- Hot mulled cider
- Mulling spices
- A caramel-rimmed glass
- Whipped cream on top

The cheddar is served alongside. That's the whole idea: a warm drink that doubles as dessert, with something savory to balance it.

## Mulled Cider for Four

This is my own simple home version built on those elements. It's forgiving, so don't stress about exact measurements.

**Makes:** 4 mugs (about 1 quart)
**Time:** 5 minutes of prep, 15 to 20 minutes of gentle heat

### Ingredients

- 1 quart apple cider
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4 whole cloves
- 2 whole allspice berries (optional)
- 2 strips orange peel (optional)
- Caramel sauce, for the rims and drizzle
- Whipped cream, for topping

### Steps

1. Pour the cider into a medium saucepan and add the cinnamon, cloves, allspice, and orange peel.
2. Heat over medium-low until steaming, with tiny bubbles at the edges. Keep it below a boil so the flavors stay bright.
3. Reduce the heat to low and let it steep for 15 to 20 minutes. Taste at 10 minutes and decide if you want it spicier.
4. Strain out the spices, or ladle carefully around them.

## How to Rim a Glass With Caramel

Do this before the cider is ready so the caramel can set slightly.

1. Warm a few tablespoons of caramel sauce for 10 to 15 seconds in the microwave, just until pourable.
2. Spread it on a small plate.
3. Dip the rim of each heatproof mug or glass, turning to coat about a quarter inch.
4. Let it sit for a few minutes while you finish the cider.

Pour the cider in gently, so you don't wash the caramel off, then add a swirl of whipped cream and a thin drizzle of caramel over the top.

## Why Cheddar Alongside Makes Sense

Michaels paired the cider with extra-sharp cheddar, a classic fall combination. My take on why it works: the cider is sweet and spiced, and a sharp, salty cheese gives your palate something to reset on between sips. Sweet and savory in alternating bites is what keeps a warm drink from feeling too much.

A simple way to serve it:

- Slice about 4 to 6 ounces of extra-sharp cheddar into thin wedges or small cubes.
- Set it out at room temperature for 20 to 30 minutes first, since the flavor is stronger when it isn't cold.
- Add apple slices or plain crackers if you want something to fill out the plate.

## Budget-Friendly Swaps

- **No whole spices?** Use a pinch of ground cinnamon and a tiny pinch of ground cloves, stirred in just before heating. The cider will be slightly cloudier.
- **No caramel sauce?** Skip the rim, or use a spoonful of honey for a lighter drizzle.
- **No whipped cream?** A dollop of lightly sweetened cream, whipped by hand or with a mixer, takes about 3 minutes. Store-bought canned topping works too.
- **Store-bought cider is fine.** Fresh-pressed from an orchard is lovely, but any apple cider will do the job.
- **Orange peel optional.** A thin strip from the fruit bowl is enough, and the drink is good without it.

## Making It Work for a Crowd or a Cozy Night In

For a gathering, scale the batch up in a slow cooker. Set it to low, keep the spices in a cheesecloth bundle or tea infuser, and let guests ladle their own. Steep for about an hour, then switch to warm. Set out the caramel-rimmed mugs, a bowl of whipped cream, and a cheddar board, and the table is done.

For a night for two, halve the recipe: 2 cups of cider, one cinnamon stick, two cloves, and 10 minutes of steeping. Leftover cider keeps in the refrigerator for a few days and reheats gently on the stove.

## Your Next Step

On the next cool evening, warm a pot of cider with a cinnamon stick, rim two mugs with caramel, and put a few slices of sharp cheddar on a plate. That's the whole Michaels-inspired idea, and it takes less than half an hour.
