## Content Guidelines
Focus on hearty soups, casseroles, one-pot dinners, slow-cooker meals, and nostalgic desserts.
Favor practical stories: “Comforting Dinners for Chilly Evenings” or “Small-Batch Meals for Two.”
Keep the tone warm, reassuring, and approachable.
Include clear portions, realistic cooking times, affordable substitutions, and tested recipe instructions.
