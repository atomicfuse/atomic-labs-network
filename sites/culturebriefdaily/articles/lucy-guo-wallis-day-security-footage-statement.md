## Why Video Changes the Game

A few cautions, though, because this is where the story could easily get ahead of itself:

- We haven't seen the footage, and TMZ's report, as summarized, doesn't describe what it shows beyond Guo's claim that it contradicts the paramedics accusation.
- Guo is disputing one specific allegation with this evidence. The reporting we have doesn't say the footage addresses everything Day has alleged.
