Las Vegas brunch no longer means eggs and a mimosa. Per Las Vegas Weekly, which rounded up four weekend options in a sponsored feature, brunch in the city now comes with poolside Pilates, live DJs, arcade-style games, and carving-station prime rib. The easiest way to choose is to decide what kind of morning you want, then match it to a spot. Here are four styles and where each one fits.

One caveat up front: the roundup is sponsored, and the details below come from its summary. Check each venue for current days, hours, and pricing before you book, since none were provided here.

## If You Want a Wellness Reset: Resorts World

Resorts World pairs movement with a meal. Per the roundup, Poolside Pulse Pilates takes place at AYU Dayclub, with brunch at FUHU to follow. It's a structured way to start a Saturday or Sunday: stretch first, eat second, and skip the guilt about the second plate.

The same resort also offers a Latin-inspired brunch at ¡VIVA!, which suits you better if you'd rather skip the workout and go straight to the flavors.

**Good fit for:** travelers who want the morning to feel active and a little restorative before a long night out (or instead of one).

## If You Want a Party Brunch: STK Las Vegas

STK Las Vegas serves an All You Can Brunch, with chef-driven bites, cocktails, and live DJs, according to the roundup. The format matters here: all-you-can formats reward people who plan to linger, so treat it as the main event of your day rather than a quick stop.

**Good fit for:** groups celebrating something, or anyone who wants brunch to feel like the start of the night rather than the end of the morning.

## If You Want Brunch With Games: Play Playground Las Vegas

Play Playground Las Vegas combines a brunch menu with bottomless mimosas and Bloody Marys and more than 20 interactive games, per Las Vegas Weekly. The games give a table something to do between courses, which helps if your group has mixed energy levels or includes people who don't love sitting through a long meal.

The roundup doesn't specify age policies or family rules, so if you're bringing kids, confirm directly before you go.

**Good fit for:** friend groups who like a little competition with their cocktails.

## If You Want a Classic Feast: South Point Garden Buffet

South Point's Garden Buffet offers a prime rib and Champagne brunch with global buffet stations, per the roundup. The buffet is the least fussy option here. You choose your own pace, your own plate, and your own combinations, which makes it the easiest to satisfy a group with different tastes.

**Good fit for:** big appetites, big parties, and anyone who prefers variety over a set menu.

## Bottomless vs. Buffet: How to Decide

Two of these four options are built around "as much as you want," but they reward different habits.

- **Choose bottomless (STK, Play Playground)** if drinks and atmosphere are the draw. Settle in, pace yourself, and treat the entertainment as part of the value.
- **Choose the buffet (South Point)** if food is the draw. Walk the stations once before you fill a plate, then go back for what stood out.
- **Choose the Pilates route (Resorts World)** if you want the meal to feel earned and the morning to feel structured.

The roundup doesn't include prices, so compare current menus before judging value. A simple test: divide the posted price by what you'd realistically order a la carte. If the math is close, pick the vibe you'd enjoy most.

## Building a Saturday Around Brunch

Brunch works best as an anchor, not an add-on. A few practical moves:

1. **Book first, plan second.** Pick the brunch, then fit everything else around it.
2. **Eat lighter beforehand.** Skip breakfast at the hotel if you're headed for a buffet or an all-you-can format.
3. **Leave a gap afterward.** A long brunch often runs into the afternoon, so keep the next item flexible.
4. **Match the venue to your location.** The roundup spans Resorts World, STK, Play Playground, and South Point, so check where each sits relative to where you're staying before committing.

## A Quick Way to Choose

Ask yourself what you want to remember about the morning. Movement and a calm start point to Resorts World. Music and cocktails point to STK. Games and a lively table point to Play Playground. A plate stacked with prime rib points to South Point.

Pick the one that sounds most like your ideal Saturday, then book it before you build the rest of your trip.
