## Why a pizza historian is worth having

A guide who treats pizza as history gives you something a review site can't: context. When someone can explain why a local style developed and how a particular shop earned its reputation, the slices stop being interchangeable. You taste differences because you know what to look for.

If a guided tour appeals to you, ask these questions before booking:

1. Which pizzerias are on the route, and how many stops are there?
2. Is the tour walking, driving, or a mix?
3. Are slices or whole pies served, and is the food included in the price?
4. How do they handle dietary restrictions?
5. What days and times do the stops operate?

Those answers tell you whether you'll leave full, rushed, or somewhere in between.

## How to build your own pizza crawl

Even with a guide, a little planning helps. A pizza crawl works on a simple principle: you're sampling, not dining.

### Pick two to four stops

Pizza is filling. Most people hit a wall after a few stops, so choose a short list. Prioritize the places a local expert or recent coverage points to as essential, then fill any gaps based on location.

### Order the same thing everywhere

The fastest way to understand a regional pizza style is a control. Order the simplest pie on the menu at each stop, then compare crust, sauce, and char. Once you've set that baseline, add one specialty at your favorite.

### Share everything

Go with at least two or three people so you can split pies. Order small, and order more only if you're still hungry. Leftovers are fine, but a half-eaten stop three means you miss stop four.

### Check hours and wait times in advance

Famous pizzerias can have lines, and some have limited weekday hours. Confirm opening times on each shop's official channels the week you travel, rather than relying on old articles. Build a buffer so a long wait doesn't wreck your day.

### Leave gaps between stops

Walk between shops when you can. A 20-minute stroll does more for your appetite than any trick, and it lets you see the city you came to eat in.
