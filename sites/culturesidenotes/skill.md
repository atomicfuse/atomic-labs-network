## Content Guidelines
Cover local specialties, street food, food markets, and regional dining customs.
Publish practical guides: “What to Eat in Lisbon” or “A Food Lover’s Weekend in Sicily.”
Include approachable recipes inspired by featured destinations.
Keep the tone warm, curious, and informative. Avoid invented firsthand experiences.
