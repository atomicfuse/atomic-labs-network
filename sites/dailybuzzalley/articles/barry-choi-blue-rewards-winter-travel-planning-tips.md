## Why Timing Is the Whole Game

CP24's framing is that this is the stretch of the calendar when things move fast. Fares, availability and rewards options can change quickly as the holidays approach. It's hard not to read that as a nudge toward booking sooner rather than later, which is also the most common piece of travel advice on the planet. The difference is that year-end travel is where procrastination tends to cost the most.

A sensible seasonal checklist, in the spirit of the segment:

- **Decide your trip type first.** A fall getaway, a holiday family visit and a winter escape have very different budgets and booking windows.
- **Set a total number, not just a flight price.** Holiday trips tend to leak money in the extras.
- **Check your rewards before you book,** not after. Knowing what your points can actually cover changes which trip makes sense.
- **Book early where you can,** since availability and options can shift.

## The Everyday-Budget Reality

The segment speaks to households trying to make vacation dollars go further. That's the right instinct for the season. Year-end travel is emotionally loaded: people want the trip, the reunion, the beach in January. Budget discipline is the unglamorous part that makes the glamorous part possible.

A few practical habits that fit the segment's spirit:

1. Pick your dates with flexibility in mind, if your schedule allows.
2. Write down the full cost of the trip before committing.
3. Treat rewards as a bonus on a trip you can afford, not a reason to take one you can't.
