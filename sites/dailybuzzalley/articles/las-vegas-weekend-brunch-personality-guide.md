Las Vegas weekend brunch has officially outgrown eggs Benedict and a lukewarm mimosa. Per a sponsored roundup from Las Vegas Weekly, four spots are now pitching brunch as a full-blown event: poolside Pilates at Resorts World, live DJs at STK, bottomless drinks and 20-plus games at Play Playground, and a prime rib and Champagne spread at South Point.

That range is the whole story. The brunch you pick says something about how you plan to spend the rest of your Saturday, so here's a guide to the four, matched to four brunch personalities. (Note: the roundup is sponsored, and the details below are limited to what it reports. Check each venue for current menus, pricing and hours before you go.)

## The Wellness Reset: Resorts World

For the person who wants to feel virtuous before the day gets weird, Resorts World leads with Poolside Pulse Pilates at AYU Dayclub, paired with brunch at FUHU. It's a combination that sounds like a contradiction until you remember this is Las Vegas, where "earn your brunch" can happen next to a pool.

The resort also offers a second route: a Latin-inspired brunch at ¡VIVA!. So even if the idea of a Pilates class near a dayclub makes you want to lie down (a different kind of lying down), there's a more traditional table-and-plate option under the same roof.

It's hard not to see the appeal of the sequence. Move first, eat second, and you can tell yourself the rest of the weekend is balanced. That's spin, not science, but Vegas has run on worse logic.

## The Party Brunch: STK Las Vegas

If your idea of a morning meal involves a beat drop, STK Las Vegas is the pick. Per the roundup, its All You Can Brunch features chef-driven bites, cocktails and live DJs.

The phrase to notice is "chef-driven." This is brunch pitched less as a pancake situation and more as a dinner-reservation energy event that happens to start earlier. If you like your weekend to open at a volume above conversational, this is the lane.

## The Playful One: Play Playground Las Vegas

Play Playground Las Vegas takes the most literal approach to brunch as entertainment. According to the roundup, it offers a brunch menu, bottomless mimosas and Bloody Marys, and more than 20 interactive games.

That's a different proposition from the usual brunch routine of sitting, ordering and scrolling your phone while waiting for the check. Here the games are the point, which makes it a natural fit for groups who need something to do besides trade weekend plans. The roundup frames it as the interactive pick, and the combination of bottomless drinks and games is easy to understand: the table is never short on activity.

## The Classic Feast: South Point Garden Buffet

Then there's the old-school move. South Point's Garden Buffet serves a prime rib and Champagne brunch with global buffet stations, per the roundup.

This is the one for the person who doesn't want a theme, a playlist or a warm-up routine. Prime rib, Champagne, a spread of global stations, and the freedom to make three trips without anyone raising an eyebrow. In a city that keeps reinventing brunch, the buffet remains the confident choice: no gimmick required.

## So Which One Are You?

A quick cheat sheet, with the caveat that the roundup doesn't provide prices, so a value comparison will have to wait until you check the menus yourself:

- **Want to feel healthy first:** Resorts World, with Pilates at AYU Dayclub and brunch at FUHU.
- **Want a soundtrack:** STK Las Vegas and its live DJs.
- **Want something to do with your hands:** Play Playground Las Vegas and its 20-plus games.
- **Want to eat until the Champagne runs out of excuses:** South Point Garden Buffet.

## The Bigger Picture

The roundup's underlying point is that Vegas brunch now competes on experience, not just food. Wellness, nightlife, games and buffet indulgence all fight for the same Saturday-morning slot. For travelers, that's useful: brunch becomes an easy anchor around which to build a whole day on or off the Strip.

It's also a little funny. A city built on the idea that you can have anything at any hour has taken the most leisurely meal of the week and given it a fitness class, a DJ and an arcade. The one brunch that hasn't changed the formula, the prime rib buffet, may be the boldest move of all.

Pick the one that matches your mood, and leave the afternoon open. In Vegas, brunch is rarely the last decision of the day.
