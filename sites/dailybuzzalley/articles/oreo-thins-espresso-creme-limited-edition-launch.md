Sporked's headline frames the cookie as a pick-me-up. That could mean a flavor that tastes like your morning cup, or it could literally mean energy. Here's what I'd judge it on once a pack is in hand:

1. **Does it taste like espresso, or just like coffee-flavored candy?** Real espresso has bitterness. Creme fillings usually sweeten that away.
2. **Does the chocolate wafer help or fight?** Chocolate and coffee are old friends, so this should be the easy part.
3. **Does it survive a dunk?** If you're pairing it with an actual coffee, it had better hold up.

A fair prediction, and only a prediction: it'll land closer to a mocha-leaning dessert than a barista-grade shot. Coffee-flavored cookies tend to chase "cozy," not "jolt."
