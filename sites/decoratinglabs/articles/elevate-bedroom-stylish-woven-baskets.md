## Styling Tips for Your Woven Basket
1. **Placement**: Position your basket at the end of the bed for a cozy, inviting look. This not only utilizes space efficiently but also creates an effortless style focal point.
2. **Filling**: Fill the basket with throw blankets, spare pillows, or even seasonal linens. This approach ensures that you have easy access to comfort while maintaining a polished appearance.
3. **Complementing Decor**: Choose a basket that complements your existing décor. For example, if your bedroom features earth tones, a natural banana bark basket will seamlessly integrate, adding warmth.
4. **Layering**: If you have multiple baskets, consider layering them. A larger basket can hold larger items while a smaller basket can nestle inside for added texture and interest.
