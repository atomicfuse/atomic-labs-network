When it comes to creating a stylish, inviting home, many of us would love the luxuries of high-end design but feel constrained by budget. Thankfully, interior designers say that elevating your space doesn’t have to mean splurging on expensive decor. Instead, it’s all about avoiding common mistakes that can make a room look cheap. Here are three common decor pitfalls to steer clear of and simple solutions for achieving a more polished look at home.

## 1. Don’t Settle for Small Rugs

One of the biggest mistakes that can diminish the overall aesthetic of a room is choosing a rug that’s too small. A tiny rug can make a space feel disjointed and cramped. It’s essential to select the right size for your area — ideally, a rug should anchor your furniture, allowing at least the front legs of your sofas and chairs to rest on the material.

**Solution:** Measure your space and invest in a larger rug. If you’re on a budget, consider layering a smaller rug on top of a larger, neutral one. This creates visual interest and room definition without overwhelming your space.

## 2. Don’t Deprioritize Practical Elements

Sometimes, in the quest for style, practical details can be overlooked. Neglecting things like cable management, clutter, or outdated hardware can contribute to a cheap-looking environment. These details matter much more than you might think.

**Solution:** Focus on functionality coupled with aesthetics. For instance, use decorative cable covers to hide cords or choose stylish storage options that keep your space organized. Updating drawer pulls and cabinet knobs can also make a surprisingly significant difference in elevating your decor.

## 3. Don’t Go for Grommets

While grommet-top curtains may seem trendy, they can often come off as casual and inexpensive, detracting from the sophistication you’re going for. The way curtains hang can dramatically affect the feeling of your space, so there’s no need to settle for styles that undermine your design vision.

**Solution:** Opt for tailored curtain styles or rods that create a more streamlined look. Hanging your curtains higher than the window frame can also give the illusion of taller ceilings and a more elegant environment. Look for fabrics that flow and create a sense of luxury, like linen or rich silk blends.

## Conclusion

Achieving a refined, magazine-worthy look in your home doesn’t require a hefty budget — it’s about making intentional choices. By avoiding these three common decor mistakes, you can transform your space into one that feels both elevated and inviting. So next time you’re planning a room refresh, take a moment to consider the size of your rugs, the importance of practical details, and the style of your curtains. Your home will thank you for it!
