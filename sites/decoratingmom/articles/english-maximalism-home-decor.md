Maximalist interiors are making waves as homeowners seek vibrant, personal spaces filled with warmth and character. Leading this movement is Toronto designer Colette van den Thillart, known for her plush, layered aesthetic that fuses English charm with bold patterns and oversized elements. In this guide, we'll explore how you can bring the spirit of English maximalism into your own home, creating a haven that feels both collected and cozy.

## Understanding English Maximalism

At its core, English maximalism is about celebrating heritage-inspired design while embracing bold, fearless elements. It combines traditional decor with unexpected patterns and vibrant textiles, transforming spaces into lively retreats. This style isn’t just about filling a room with objects; it’s about crafting an environment that tells your personal story.

## 1. Embrace Bold Patterns

One of the simplest ways to introduce English maximalism is through the use of bold patterns. Think of wallpapers, upholstery, and throw pillows that incorporate striking prints. Instead of choosing a single pattern, consider mixing different designs in complementary colors. For instance, floral patterns with paisley or stripes can add visual interest and depth to a room.

### Tip:
In Colette van den Thillart's home, layered wallpapers create a dynamic backdrop that sets the tone for the entire space. Don’t hesitate to explore wallpaper combinations or try a feature wall to make a statement!

## 2. Opt for Oversized Furniture Pieces

Maximalist decor thrives on scale. Oversized furniture, such as large sofas or statement lamps, can create a dramatic focal point in a room. This approach not only fills the space visually but also invites comfort — larger pieces often come with more plush cushioning, perfect for the family-oriented home.

### Tip:
Look for oversized lighting fixtures like sculptural lamps that draw the eye upwards. They bring intrigue and whimsy to the space while also creating a cozy ambiance.

## 3. Layer Textures 

To achieve that warm, collected feel that's synonymous with English design, layering different textures is key. Combine fabric types such as velvet, linen, and wool for curtains, throws, and cushions. This tactile variety not only adds warmth and comfort but expresses a unique personality.

### Tip:
Incorporate velvet fabric as a cold-climate design tool — its plush softness provides warmth while enhancing color richness in your decor. You can use it in everything from cushions to accent chairs.

## 4. Curate Personal Collections

One of the distinguishing features of English maximalism is the idea of curation; it's about showcasing your favorite pieces. Display a mix of vintage finds, family heirlooms, and modern art. These elements together create a narrative in your home.

### Tip:
Rather than arranging everything symmetrically, try clustering items of varying heights and textures on a shelf or table. This curated chaos feels intentional and inviting, encouraging guests to explore and appreciate your collection.

## 5. Play with Color and Scale

Don’t shy away from vibrant colors! Mixing various hues can breathe life into any room. Consider a palette that allows for pops of color to flow naturally through the space. When it comes to art and decorative elements, choose pieces that feel grand. Larger artworks and accessories become statement pieces, enhancing the overall look.

### Tip:
Colette's use of unexpected color combinations is a great reminder that your home should reflect your personality. Experiment with bold colors throughout your home to find what feels right for you.

## In Conclusion

Bringing English maximalism into your home is about creating a space that tells your story, reflects your personality, and invites warmth and comfort. By embracing bold patterns, oversized furniture, varied textures, curated collections, and vibrant color palettes, you can transform your living space into a masterpiece of personality and comfort. So go ahead, get bold, layer up those textures, and let your creativity shine!
