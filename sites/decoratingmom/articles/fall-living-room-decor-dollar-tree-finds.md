Fall is the perfect season to refresh your living room decor without breaking your budget. With a little creativity and some precise picks from Dollar Tree, you can create a cozy, inviting space that reflects the warmth of the season. Here are 11 fantastic finds, all under $1.50 each, to help you create a stylish fall atmosphere.

## 1. Oval Woven Baskets
These versatile baskets not only add texture but can also help organize small items. Place them on a coffee table to hold remote controls or decorative items, giving your living room a neat and curated look.

## 2. Ceramic Fragrance Warmer
Warm up your space with a touch of style using a ceramic fragrance warmer. Choose a scent that resonates with fall—think spiced pumpkin or warm vanilla—to create a cozy aroma that enhances your home atmosphere throughout the season.

## 3. Diamond-Lid Glass Bottle
Add a hint of elegance to your decor with these chic diamond-lid glass bottles. They can serve as vases for seasonal flowers or simply stand alone as statement pieces, shimmering on your shelves or coffee table.

## 4. Round Mirror
Mirrors can enhance light and space, making your room feel larger. A round mirror from Dollar Tree is not just functional but decorative—hang it above a console table or position it within a vignette to add depth and interest.

## 5. Gold-Framed Chalk Boards
Personalize your fall decor with a gold-framed chalkboard. Use it to write seasonal messages, quotes, or even daily reminders, seamlessly integrating it into your living room decor.

## 6. Beaded Tray
Serve as the foundation for your fall display with a beaded tray. Fill it with candles, decorative pumpkins (without going overboard), or seasonal fruits to create a cozy centerpiece that invites warmth.

## 7. Fall-Themed Wooden Spheres
These wooden spheres are perfect for adding a natural touch to your decor. Group several in a bowl or display them on a shelf for a rustic autumn vibe.

## 8. Wooden Flower Medallions
Bring a handcrafted element into your space with wooden flower medallions. They can be used as wall art or displayed on shelves and tabletops, reinforcing nature-inspired decor without high costs.

## 9. Ceramic Designer Fragrance Warmer
Like its ceramic counterpart, this designer fragrance warmer brings sophistication and delightful scents into your living room. Opt for a unique design that enhances your decor style and invites guests to relax.

## 10. Lustre Glass Bottles
For a touch of glamour, incorporate lustre glass bottles into your decor. Perfect for showcasing seasonal greenery or fairy lights, they add a sparkling element to your living room’s ambiance.

## 11. Ceramic Buddha Incense Burner
Finally, for a meditative touch, a ceramic Buddha incense burner can elevate both your scent and decor. Light incense sticks to create a calming atmosphere, enhancing your cozy fall retreat.

## Styling Tips
Use these items to style your living room thoughtfully:
- **Coffee Table Decor:** Combine the beaded tray, fragrance warmer, and wooden spheres for an inviting coffee table display.
- **Vignettes:** Group several items like the diamond-lid glass bottle with the gold-framed chalkboard on a shelf to create visual interest.
- **Mantle Design:** Use the round mirror to reflect light, combined with beaded trays and seasonal floral arrangements for a stunning mantle vignette.
