Renovating your home can often lead to sleek, modern results that overlook the warmth and character of more traditional interiors. Designer Emily Ruff recently transformed a stripped-back 1926 Seattle home into a cozy sanctuary that harmoniously blends vintage charm with the comforts of modern living. Here’s how you can infuse your own renovated space with historic charm, drawing from her inspiring approach.

## Embrace Architectural Details

One of the most striking aspects of Ruff's design is her attention to small architectural details that evoke a sense of history. Consider incorporating arched openings, wood moldings, and details around doorways that may have been overlooked. These subtle touches can make your space feel immediately warmer and more inviting, as they draw the eye and create soft visual lines that soften the straight edges typical in modern design.

## Layer in Vintage Pieces

Mixing modern elements with vintage furniture and decor can create a uniquely layered aesthetic. Embrace secondhand finds, antique furniture, or even family heirlooms that introduce stories and charm into your space. Ruff’s project highlights the importance of curating collections that reflect your personality and values, adding depth to your home.

## Floral Wallpapers for Freshness

Floral wallpapers can be a striking design choice that adds both pattern and warmth, reminiscent of English country homes. Opt for muted tones and delicate patterns to ensure that the wallpaper enhances, rather than overwhelms, the room’s atmosphere. Ruff’s use of floral designs in her space illustrates how wallpaper can create a natural, cozy feel, invigorating walls while maintaining a timeless quality.

## Infuse Soft Yellow Tones

Ruff’s design brilliantly incorporates soft yellow hues that add lightness without dominance. When selecting paint colors, look for soft, understated yellows that can seamlessly blend with warm neutrals. This technique can create a sense of continuity throughout your home, making spaces feel curated rather than haphazard.

## Choose Warm Neutrals for Unity

A palette of warm neutrals can provide the backbone for any design scheme. Consider shades of beige, soft taupe, and warm whites to create a welcoming environment. These colors serve as a backdrop that highlights your vintage pieces and floral patterns, encouraging a cohesive aesthetic throughout your home. Ruff’s ability to balance these tones creates a serene atmosphere that invites relaxation.

## Small Details That Make a Difference

Finally, don't underestimate the impact of small details—lighting fixtures, cabinet hardware, and even the choice of window treatments can add significant character. Opt for brass or vintage-inspired fixtures to add warmth and sophistication, echoing the styles of bygone eras. These finer points enhance the overall vibe of the space and contribute to the feeling of history.

### Takeaway: Infuse Historic Charm into Your Home

By applying these ideas inspired by Emily Ruff's renovation, you can transform your newly renovated home into a warm, character-filled sanctuary. Focus on architectural details, layer in vintage pieces, and choose a harmonious color palette to foster a space that feels both welcoming and rich in history.
