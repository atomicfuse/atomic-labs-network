With the school year kicking off, families are adjusting from relaxed summer schedules to busy mornings and filled afternoons. This shift can be particularly stressful for dogs, who thrive on routine and companionship. To keep your furry friend comfortable and safe as everyone heads back to school, here’s an actionable checklist designed to help you prepare them for the changes ahead.

## Understand the Impact of Routine Changes

Dogs are creatures of habit, and abrupt shifts in their routine can lead to confusion and stress. Pay attention to the signs your dog may be showing due to these changes, such as barking, chewing, or showing signs of anxiety. Recognizing these behaviors early can help you address the issues before they escalate.

## 1. Introduce Separation Practice

As the mornings get busier, your dog will likely face new alone times. Start practicing short periods of separation now to help your pup adjust gradually.

- **How to Do It:** Begin with leaving your dog alone for 10 minutes while you step outside. Gradually increase this time as they become more comfortable. 
- **Tip:** Use a treat-dispensing toy or a favorite chew to keep them occupied while you’re gone.

## 2. Create a Comfortable Kennel Space

If you need to confine your dog during school hours, ensure their kennel is a cozy and inviting space.

- **How to Do It:** Add a soft mat or blanket, a few toys, and maybe even an item of your clothing so they feel secure.
- **Tip:** Encourage your dog to spend time in their kennel while you’re home by giving treats or feeding them there.

## 3. Door Manners Training

With more people coming and going at home — like family members or delivery drivers — teaching your dog door manners can prevent them from running out or jumping on guests.

- **How to Do It:** Train your dog to sit or stay when someone approaches the door. Reward them for staying calm and quiet.
- **Tip:** Practice with a family member at the door for a smooth experience when the school year starts.

## 4. Recall and Leash Control Skills

Being able to call your dog back to you and maintain leash control is crucial, especially with kids involved.

- **How to Do It:** Practice recall by calling your dog from a distance and rewarding them with a treat when they respond. Also, practice walking your dog on a leash, ensuring they don’t pull.
- **Tip:** Teach your kids how to safely walk the dog using a proper leash technique and encourage them to practice together.

## 5. Develop a Pre-School Routine

Get your dog accustomed to the new daily rhythm by practicing the routine you’ll follow once school starts.

- **How to Do It:** Mimic school-day scenarios by leaving the house at the same time each morning and returning later, to help prepare your dog for when everyone is out during school hours.
- **Tip:** Consider a daily walk or playtime in the evening to reinforce the new routine and provide your dog with ample exercise and mental stimulation.

## Concluding Thoughts

Adopting these simple routines will not only ease the back-to-school transition for your dog but also promote a safer, calmer environment overall. Make these changes gradually and be consistent; your dog will adapt to the new normal, ensuring both you and your furry friend enjoy the back-to-school season together!
