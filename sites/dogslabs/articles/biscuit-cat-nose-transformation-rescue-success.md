## Understanding Cryptococcosis
Cryptococcosis is a potentially life-threatening fungal infection that primarily affects the respiratory systems of given animals. In Biscuit's case, it resulted in severe swelling and long-term scarring of her nose. It can be caused by exposure to the fungus Cryptococcus neoformans, often found in pigeon droppings and decaying wood, which means stray animals can be at particular risk.
