If your cat is always in a box, a bag, or the laundry pile you just folded, you don't have to fight it. You can build a spot that gives them what they're after, and you can do it without turning your living room into a cardboard warehouse. Here's a simple way to set up a cozy cat corner, plus a few products to start your shopping list.

The prompt for this guide is a BuzzFeed Shopping roundup of 15 cozy cat products for cats who love hiding, burrowing, climbing, and curling up in enclosed spaces. The three standouts named in its description are a Scandi-style cat cabin, a cozy Lola blanket, and a mushroom-themed floor-to-ceiling cat tree. The full list isn't reproduced here, so this piece uses those three as examples and focuses on how to choose well.

## Start by watching what your cat already picks

Before you buy anything, spend a few days noticing where your cat goes. The spot they choose tells you what to shop for.

- **Boxes and bags:** they like enclosed spaces with walls on most sides. Look for a cabin or cave-style bed.
- **Laundry piles and blankets:** they like soft material they can push around and burrow into. A good blanket is your best bet.
- **High shelves and tops of furniture:** they like to climb and look down. A tall cat tree fits.

Most cats mix these. That's why a corner with a hideaway, a soft layer, and something to climb tends to work better than one expensive bed.

## Pick a hideaway with walls and a roof

The Scandi-style cat cabin from the roundup is a good example of the category: a small, enclosed house with a clean, simple look. For a cat who dives into every box, an enclosed bed is the closest upgrade.

What to check before you buy:

1. **Size.** Your cat should be able to turn around and curl up inside. Measure your cat curled up, not stretched out.
2. **Entrance.** Make sure it's easy to get in and out of, so your cat never feels trapped.
3. **Cleaning.** Look for removable or washable parts. Hideaways collect fur fast.

## Add a blanket they can burrow into

The cozy Lola blanket is the soft-layer option on the list. Cats who love laundry piles are usually after texture and warmth, so a blanket tucked into or draped near the hideaway gives them both.

A few tips:

- Put it where they already nap rather than where you want them to nap.
- Keep a spare so you can wash one while the other stays in rotation.
- If they ignore it at first, rub it on something that already smells like them or leave it near their current favorite spot.

## Give them vertical space

The mushroom-themed floor-to-ceiling cat tree is the most decor-forward pick named in the roundup. A tree that reaches the ceiling takes up little floor space and gives a climber somewhere to go that isn't your bookshelf.

Before you commit:

- Check your ceiling height against the product's stated range.
- Pick a spot away from heavy foot traffic but still in the room where you spend time. Cats like to watch their people.
- Make sure it's stable. If it wobbles, your cat will notice before you do.

## Make it look good in your home

Buyers increasingly treat cat furniture as part of their home decor, not just pet supplies. That's a fair point: you'll see this corner every day, so it's reasonable to want it to match your space.

Some easy ways to blend it in:

- Choose one or two colors that match your room and stick to them.
- Put the corner against a wall or in a nook so it looks intentional.
- Group the cabin, blanket, and tree together instead of scattering items around.

## Set it up for fall and winter

Cooler months send many indoor cats hunting for warm spots. Position the hideaway away from drafty windows and doors, ideally somewhere with a bit of sun during the day. Add the blanket and you've made a nest that suits seasonal nesting instincts, and it makes a good gift for another cat owner, too.

## Keep your expectations realistic

Cats are famously unpredictable about new furniture. Your cat may ignore the pricey cabin and sleep in the box it came in. That's normal. If so:

- Leave the box next to the new bed for a week or two.
- Move the new item closer to their favorite spot rather than forcing it.
- Give it time before deciding it was a miss.
