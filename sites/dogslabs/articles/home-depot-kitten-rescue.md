## What to Do If You Find Kittens

If you ever find yourself in a situation like this, here are crucial steps to take:

1. **Assess the situation safely**: Ensure you are safe and away from any immediate danger, especially in a busy public space.
2. **Listen for sounds**: Kittens often cry out when they are scared or in distress. Follow the sound to locate where they might be.
3. **Don't remove them without help**: Kittens may need professional help. Contact animal control or a local rescue organization for assistance.
4. **Provide information**: When contacting a rescue, provide as many details as possible about the location, number of kittens, and any visible health concerns.
5. **Be patient**: The rescue organization may need time to respond and arrange transport or care.
