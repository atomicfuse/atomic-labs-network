## Important Recall Announcement
Nearly 2,000 pounds of Northwest Naturals raw pet food have been recalled due to potential contamination with salmonella and listeria monocytogenes. This recall affects specific lots of two products distributed nationwide, prompting pet owners to check their supplies urgently.

## Products Affected by the Recall
The recall includes the following items:
1. **Northwest Naturals Frozen Chicken Recipe (2-pound)**  
   - **Lot Number:** B-5  
   - **Best-by Date:** January 26, 2028  
   - **UPC:** 087316384956  
   - **Contamination:** Positive for salmonella  

2. **Northwest Naturals Frozen Raw Diet for Dogs Chicken Recipe (6-pound)**  
   - **Lot Number:** B-19  
   - **Best-by Date:** January 25, 2028  
   - **UPC:** 087316380392  
   - **Contamination:** Positive for both salmonella and listeria monocytogenes  

If you have purchased these products, it's crucial to check if your supplies are included in this recall.

## How to Check Your Products
To determine if your pet food is part of the recall:  
1. **Locate the Product:** Find the frozen raw pet food in your freezer.
2. **Check the Lot Number:** Look for the lot number on the packaging. It should be displayed clearly. If yours matches B-5 or B-19, stop feeding it to your pet immediately.
3. **Review the Best-before Date:** Ensure the best-by date matches either January 26 or January 25, 2028.  
4. **Identify the UPC Code:** If you're in doubt, verify by checking the UPC listed above. This code can typically be found on the back or bottom of the packaging.

## Recognizing Symptoms in Pets
While no illnesses tied to this recall have been reported, it's essential to monitor your pets for signs of salmonella and listeria infection. Common symptoms include:
- **Vomiting**
- **Diarrhea** (potentially bloody)
- **Loss of appetite**
- **Lethargy**
- **Fever**
If you notice any of these symptoms, contact your veterinarian for guidance.

## Best Practices for Safe Handling
To prevent any risk of bacterial transmission:
- **Employ proper sanitation:** Wash your hands, utensils, and surfaces after handling raw pet food.
- **Avoid cross-contamination:** Keep raw pet food separate from human food.
- **Store pet food correctly:** Maintain proper storage temperatures and avoid thawing raw meat at room temperature.

## Steps for Disposing of Recalled Food
If your pet food is part of the recall:  
1. **Do not feed it to your pets.**  
2. **Dispose of the recalled product:** Place it in a sealed bag and throw it away in an outdoor trash can to avoid any risk to other animals.  
3. **Seek a refund:** Contact the store where you purchased the product or visit Northwest Naturals' website for more information on obtaining a refund.

## Conclusion
Pet food recalls can pose significant risks to your furry companions. By taking the time to check your Northwest Naturals products against this recall, you can help ensure your pets remain healthy and safeguarded from contaminated food. Stay proactive about pet food safety, and keep an eye on future recalls to protect your beloved pets.
