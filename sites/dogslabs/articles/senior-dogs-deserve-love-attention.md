## Why Seniors Make Great Pets

Many potential adopters mistakenly believe that younger dogs make better companions. However, senior dogs come with a wealth of experience and personality. They are often calmer, already house-trained, and just eager to find their loving families. Doppy, despite his health challenges, is still playful and brings happiness wherever he goes. Senior dogs like him are often less energetic than puppies, making them perfect for people who want a loving pet to cuddle with.

## The Role of Foster Care

Doppy's story is also a testament to the importance of foster-based rescues. He was taken in by Long Way Home Adoptables, where he received the medical care he desperately needed. Foster homes not only provide immediate safety for animals in trouble but also help in their rehabilitation. This often leads to a more personalized and loving approach to care that shelters alone might not be able to offer. Doppy’s journey from a shelter to a loving foster home illustrates this transformation beautifully.
