Swifties have a new fixation, and it comes with a 1985 soundtrack. According to Cosmopolitan, fans have spotted a pattern suggesting Taylor Swift's next song could be called "That's What Friends Are For," after the same phrase reportedly turned up in posts from Taylor, Cara Delevingne, and *Saturday Night Live*. The phrase is also the title of Dionne Warwick's 1985 hit.

To be clear up front: this is fan speculation. Nothing from Taylor or her team confirms a song with that title. But the timing is exactly why the theory is spreading.

## Why Now?

Taylor just released four new songs from *The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore*, per Cosmopolitan, and the fandom is in what the outlet describes as peak theory-crafting mode. When fresh music lands, every caption, timestamp, and punctuation choice becomes potential evidence. A new batch of songs is basically a starter pistol.

## The Case, Such As It Is

The core of the theory is repetition. Per the report, the same phrase, "That's What Friends Are For," appeared in three places: posts from Taylor, a post from Cara Delevingne, and SNL. Swifties see a thread. A skeptic might see a very common phrase. Both can be true, and that's the fun of the genre.

Then comes the numerology, which is where Swiftie theorizing tends to go from detective work to performance art. The theory, as reported, leans on:

- **2,121 days**, a number fans connect to the phrase's appearances
- **12:12 timestamps** on the relevant posts
- **12/12**, Taylor's birthdate

It's hard not to admire the commitment. Taylor's birthday is a number that fans already treat as significant, so any 12 or 21 that wanders into frame gets drafted into the case. Whether those numbers point to a song or just to the fact that numbers exist is, of course, the open question.

The Cosmopolitan writer's own verdict, per the article's description, is "I've never been more convinced or delusional." That's honestly the most accurate summary of Swiftie theory culture I've seen in one sentence.

## The Dionne Warwick Factor

The 1985 original is a famous song, so a nod would at least be a recognizable one. A Taylor track with that title, whether a cover, a reference, or something wholly original, would be a notable choice. That's my read, not a report, and there's nothing in the coverage indicating what such a song would sound like or whether it exists.

What's worth keeping in mind is that a title like this could just as easily be a coincidence. A friendship-themed phrase popping up around Taylor, a close-circle friend like Delevingne, and a sketch show is plausible without any hidden album plan. Plenty of Swiftie theories have been right over the years, and plenty have been gloriously wrong. This one hasn't been confirmed either way.

## The Verdict (Unofficial)

Should you bet on a new single called "That's What Friends Are For"? Probably not with real money. Should you enjoy watching an entire fandom turn three coincidental phrases and a birthday into a conspiracy board? Absolutely.

Until Taylor says otherwise, this stays in the "delightful but unproven" file. And if the song never materializes, the theory will still have done what the best Swiftie theories do: given everyone something to squint at while waiting for the real news.

In the meantime, there are four fresh songs to listen to. Maybe the answer is hiding in one of them. Maybe it's hiding at 12:12.
