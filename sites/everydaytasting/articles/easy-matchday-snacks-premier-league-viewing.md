Premier League games are on today, with Manchester United facing Bournemouth and Liverpool hosting Brighton, per ESPN's live coverage. If you're settling in to follow them, you don't need a complicated spread. You need food you can make in under 30 minutes, mostly from pantry staples, that you can eat with one hand while watching.

I can't tell you kickoff times or lineups here. For those, check the match coverage from Manchester United, BBC Sport (which is running Chris Sutton's Man Utd vs Bournemouth predictions), and ESPN. What I can do is get your snacks sorted so you're not stuck in the kitchen at the wrong moment.

The plan: make one snack before the first match, one you can assemble at halftime, and one you can bake and forget.

## Snack 1: Crispy Baked Wings With a Pantry Glaze (about 50 minutes, mostly hands-off)

This is the one to start first, since the oven does the work.

**You'll need:**
- 2 lb chicken wings (or drumettes)
- 1 tablespoon baking powder (not baking soda)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 3 tablespoons hot sauce
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 tablespoon honey

**Steps:**
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with foil and set a wire rack on top if you have one.
2. Pat the wings very dry with paper towels. Dryness is what makes the skin crisp.
3. Toss with the baking powder, salt, and garlic powder until evenly coated.
4. Bake 25 minutes, flip, then bake another 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden.
5. Whisk the hot sauce, butter, and honey. Toss the hot wings in the glaze and serve.

**Ingredient swap:** No hot sauce? Use 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon honey, and a squeeze of lime for a sweet-salty version.

## Snack 2: Quick Black Bean Nachos (15 minutes)

This is your halftime assembly job, and it costs very little.

**You'll need:**
- 1 bag (about 8 oz) tortilla chips
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1.5 cups shredded cheese
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Optional toppings: salsa, sliced pickled jalapeños, sour cream, chopped cilantro

**Steps:**
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Spread half the chips on a sheet pan. Scatter half the beans and half the cheese over them.
3. Add the remaining chips, beans, and cheese. Layering twice means every chip gets toppings.
4. Sprinkle the cumin over the top.
5. Bake 8 to 10 minutes until the cheese is fully melted.
6. Add your toppings after baking so they stay fresh.

**Tip:** Beans warm in the oven, so there's no need to cook them first.

## Snack 3: Spiced Chickpeas With a Global Twist (35 minutes)

This is the crunchy, bowl-friendly option that works as a flavor experiment. The spice blend borrows from Indian chaat-style seasoning, which is tangy, salty, and a little warm.

**You'll need:**
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon chili powder
- Squeeze of lemon or lime juice after baking

**Steps:**
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the dry chickpeas with oil and spices on a baking sheet.
3. Roast 25 to 30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway, until crunchy.
4. Squeeze citrus over the top while still warm.

They're best within a few hours. Left overnight, they soften.

## A Simple Timeline for the Day

Because kickoff times aren't something I can confirm here, work backward from whichever game you're watching:

- **60 minutes before:** Get the wings in the oven.
- **30 minutes before:** Roast the chickpeas alongside, since the oven temperatures are close (425°F for both).
- **At halftime:** Turn the oven to 400°F and bake the nachos while you stretch your legs.

If you're watching both matches back to back, make the chickpeas and wings first and keep the nachos for the second game.

## Budget Notes

All three snacks lean on inexpensive staples: canned beans, canned chickpeas, a bag of chips, and a family pack of wings. If wings are pricey where you live, bone-in chicken thighs cut into pieces work with the same method, though they need about 10 extra minutes.

## Your Move

Pick one snack and start it now. The wings take the longest, so if you only make one thing before the first whistle, make those.
