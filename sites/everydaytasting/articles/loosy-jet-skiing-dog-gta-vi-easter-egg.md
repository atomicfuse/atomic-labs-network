## A Florida-Style Snack for Game Night

If you are going to spend an evening hunting for Easter eggs, you want food you can eat with one hand. This citrus-lime shrimp and avocado toast is a beginner-friendly nod to South Florida flavors. It serves 2, takes about 15 minutes, and needs no special skills.

### Ingredients

- 1/2 pound (225 g) peeled, deveined small shrimp, thawed if frozen
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt, plus a pinch for the avocado
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 1 ripe avocado
- 4 slices sturdy bread, toasted
- Chopped cilantro, optional

### Steps

1. **Dry the shrimp.** Pat them with a paper towel so they sear instead of steam.
2. **Season.** Toss with the chili powder, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and half the lime zest.
3. **Cook.** Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook 2 minutes per side, until pink and curled. Remove from the heat.
4. **Mash the avocado.** Mix with a squeeze of lime juice and a pinch of salt. Leave it a little chunky.
5. **Build.** Spread the avocado on the toast, top with shrimp, and finish with the remaining zest and cilantro.

### Easy Swaps

- No shrimp? Use canned chickpeas, drained, patted dry, and cooked the same way for 4 to 5 minutes.
- No fresh lime? Bottled lime juice works for the avocado.
- Want more heat? Add a pinch of cayenne or a few drops of hot sauce.
