## Embrace Pilates for Core and Stability

Kelsie highlights the importance of Pilates as a cornerstone of her fitness routine. This method focuses on core strength, flexibility, and stability, all beneficial for anyone looking to enhance their physical fitness. Don’t worry about your skill level; you can adapt Pilates practices to your abilities.

### Beginner-Friendly Pilates Moves

Here are three beginner Pilates moves you can try to start building your core strength:

1. **The Hundred**: This classic Pilates exercise warms up your core. Lie on your back with your legs raised to a 45-degree angle. Lift your head, neck, and shoulders off the mat. Pump your arms up and down at your sides while breathing in for five counts and out for five counts. Repeat until you reach 100.

2. **Bridge**: Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor, hip-width apart. Press through your heels and lift your hips towards the ceiling. Hold for a moment at the top, then lower back down. This move targets your glutes, hamstrings, and lower back.

3. **Pelvic Curl**: Start on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Begin to roll your pelvis up off the mat, one vertebra at a time, until you form a straight line from your knees to your shoulders. This move promotes spinal flexibility and core engagement. 

Incorporate these exercises into your workout routine, aiming for two to three sessions a week. As you grow comfortable, consider increasing the number of repetitions.

## Prioritize Recovery and Hydration

Kelsie doesn’t just spend her time working out; recovery and hydration are equally important in her routine. With six performances a week, showgirls must prioritize recovery to prevent injuries and remain at peak performance.

Aim for at least 8-10 glasses of water daily, especially if you're engaging in vigorous activity. Hydration aids muscle recovery and overall performance. Likewise, consider adding stretching and foam rolling to your routine for muscle recovery.

## Walk Your Way to Fitness

Walking might seem simple, but Kelsie recommends getting in your steps to promote wellness. She aims for 10,000 steps a day, which not only contributes to overall health but also fits seamlessly into a busy lifestyle. 

To increase your daily count:
- **Take the Stairs**: Whenever possible, opt for stairs instead of elevators.
- **Walk While You Talk**: If you have phone meetings, try pacing while you chat.
- **Set a Timer**: Every hour, take a short walk to refresh your mind and get your body moving.
