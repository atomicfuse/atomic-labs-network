Feeling the weight of period pain, bloating, or mood swings? Instead of pushing through the discomfort, consider turning to restorative yoga. This gentle approach is designed to calm your body and mind, providing relief without the added strain of vigorous workouts. Here are six prop-supported yoga poses that can help ease period discomfort while allowing you to practice self-care during that time of the month.

## 1. Corpse Pose (Shavasana)
This foundational pose is excellent for relaxation and reconnecting with your breath. Lie flat on your back, arms at your sides, palms facing up. Allow your feet to relax, rolling slightly outward. Focus on your breath, inhaling deeply and exhaling slowly. Stay in this pose for 5-10 minutes, letting go of tension.

## 2. Reclining Bound Angle Pose (Supta Baddha Konasana)
This pose opens the hips and fosters a deep sense of calm. To practice, lie on your back and bring the soles of your feet together, letting your knees fall open to the sides. You can place cushions or blocks under your knees for added support. Take slow, deep breaths, and hold for 5-15 minutes.

## 3. Gentle Spinal Twist (Shava Udarakarshanasana)
A gentle spinal twist can relieve lower back discomfort and improve digestion. Lie on your back and draw your knees toward your chest. Gently lower your knees to one side while keeping your shoulders on the ground. Extend your arms in a T-shape and look toward the opposite side to deepen the twist. Hold for 3-5 breaths on each side.

## 4. Lying Abdominal Stretch Pose (Supta Udarakarshanasana)
This stretch helps alleviate bloating and discomfort in your abdomen. Lie on your back and extend one leg at a time, while keeping the other knee drawn toward your chest. Switch legs after a few breaths, holding the stretch for 5-10 breaths per side to encourage relaxation in your abdominal region.

## 5. Legs-Up-the-Wall Pose (Viparita Karani)
Positioning your legs against the wall reduces tension in the lower back and promotes relaxation. Sit next to a wall and gently lower back while swinging your legs up, keeping them straight against the wall. You can support your back with a folded blanket for added comfort. Stay in this pose for 5-15 minutes, breathing deeply.

## 6. Gentle Cobra Pose (Saral Bhujangasana)
This pose can help open up the chest and alleviate tension in the back. Lie on your stomach with your hands under your shoulders. Slowly press into your hands, lifting your chest off the floor while keeping your lower body grounded. Hold for 3-5 breaths, then slowly lower back down.

## Mindful Breathing Techniques
Incorporating mindful breathing can enhance the benefits of these yoga poses. As you settle into each pose, focus on slow, deep breaths, inhaling through the nose and exhaling through the mouth. This rhythmic breathing not only allows you to connect with your body but also calms your nervous system, helping to manage mood swings and anxiety that may accompany your cycle.

## Conclusion
Practicing restorative yoga during your period offers a gentle alternative to traditional exercise, allowing focus on relaxation and self-care. Embrace these six poses, take your time, and honor your body's need for kindness during these days. Remember, it’s perfectly okay to prioritize your comfort — your body deserves it!
