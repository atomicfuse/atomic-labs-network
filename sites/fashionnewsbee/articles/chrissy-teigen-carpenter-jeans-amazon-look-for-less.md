Chrissy Teigen stepped out in a pair of Darkpark "carpenter" jeans, and Us Weekly has already tracked down a lookalike on Amazon for about $20. Us Weekly's write-up pegs the designer pair at $780 in its description (the headline rounds the savings to $760), so the gap is roughly the cost of a very nice weekend trip, give or take a rounding error.

The jeans themselves are the real story. Us Weekly describes them as a hybrid: both straight and wide. That's a smart middle path in a year when, per the outlet, straight-leg and wide-leg denim are both trending in 2026.

## The Case for the Hybrid

Denim debates tend to split into camps. Team straight-leg wants clean lines. Team wide-leg wants drama and room to move. A carpenter-style cut that borrows from both is, it's hard not to think, the diplomatic solution to a fight nobody should have had to pick a side in.

Per Us Weekly, the appeal is that the style is flattering, wearable and easy to style. That's the outlet's framing, but it tracks with why hybrids tend to catch on: you get the structure of a straight leg with some of the swing of a wide one, without committing to either extreme.

## The $20 Question

Here's the math as reported: $780 for Teigen's Darkpark pair, around $20 for the Amazon alternative. Us Weekly positions the cheaper pair as a lookalike, not a twin, and that's the right way to read it. A dupe gives you the silhouette. It doesn't give you the label, the fabric or whatever else a four-figure-adjacent price tag buys.

But the silhouette is the whole point of this trend. When the news is a *shape*, a budget version can carry the idea surprisingly far. And with shoppers hungry for expensive-looking fashion at budget prices, per Us Weekly, the pairing of celebrity pick and bargain find is an easy sell.

## How to Wear Them Three Ways

The styling ideas below are my take, not Teigen's or Us Weekly's, so treat them as suggestions rather than gospel.

### Errands and school pick-up

Keep it easy: a fitted tee or a thin knit, plus sneakers. Relaxed jeans like these do the heavy lifting, so everything else can stay quiet.

### Casual-but-put-together

Try a tucked-in bodysuit or a crisp button-down with the sleeves pushed up. A defined top balances the volume below and makes the whole thing look deliberate rather than comfy by accident.

### Dressier plans

Swap in a sleek top, a structured blazer and heeled boots. A wider leg over a heel is a classic way to make denim read as evening-appropriate, and a hybrid cut keeps the line from swallowing your shoes.

## Why This Trend Has Legs

The carpenter label nods to workwear roots, which is part of why the style feels sturdy and unfussy rather than precious. A pair that works for pick-up and a night out is the kind of wardrobe workhorse that justifies a spot in a closet, whatever the price tag.

The celebrity-dupe cycle has a predictable rhythm: a famous person wears something expensive, and within days the internet finds the budget cousin. What makes this one tidier than most is that the original idea, straight meets wide, is genuinely easy to spot and easy to replicate.

Should you spend $20 or $780? Only one of those lets you test the trend on a Tuesday without a second thought. If it works, you've learned something about your denim taste. If it doesn't, you've lost the price of a few coffees, and Teigen's version is still there for dreaming.
