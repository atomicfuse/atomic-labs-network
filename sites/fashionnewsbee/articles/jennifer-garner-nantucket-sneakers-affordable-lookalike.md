## How to Wear Them Like You Own a Boat

The appeal of a tan sneaker is that it plays well with the usual fall coastal staples. Here's how to build around it, in the spirit of Garner's look:

- **Denim:** Jeans and beige sneakers are the easiest pairing here. Keep the wash classic and the fit relaxed.
- **A good sweater:** Cream, navy, or oatmeal knits push the look toward coastal-preppy. Drape one over your shoulders if you're feeling especially Nantucket.
- **Sunglasses:** The finishing touch for any boat day. Bigger and simpler tends to read more polished.
- **A dress:** A neutral sneaker softens a dressier piece, which is the sporty-preppy balance this season loves.

The formula is a little coastal grandmother, a little tennis club, and zero effort visible from the outside. Matching tones helps: when the shoe, sweater, and denim sit in the same calm color family, the outfit looks more expensive than the sum of its parts. That's my read, not a rule, but it's the quiet-luxury trick in a nutshell.
