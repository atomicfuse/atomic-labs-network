At 59, fitness trainer Jill Brown is proving that age is just a number—and she looks better now than she did in her 30s! ### The Weekly Workout Breakdown
Brown’s weekly routine is a mix of high-intensity workouts, strength training, and recovery-focused exercises that are designed to build strength while being gentle on the body. Here’s her approach laid out day-by-day:

1. **Monday:** HIIT workout with functional exercises, focusing on getting the heart rate up while building overall endurance.
2. **Tuesday:** Two **Body Sculpt** classes coupled with shoulder and hip physical therapy sessions. It's all about understanding your body's needs.
3. **Wednesday:** A rowing machine session followed by core strengthening exercises. After all, a strong core is essential for maintaining balance and stability, especially as we age.
4. **Thursday:** Pilates class, plus back and biceps resistance training, integrating flexibility and strength.
5. **Friday:** Cardio Sculpt Step class, using dumbbells and battle ropes for both strength and coordination. Talk about a killer combo!
6. **Saturday:** A Body Sculpt class, ending the week with a leg-focused strength workout to reinforce those key power zones.

By leveraging **lighter weights and higher reps**, Brown embodies a smarter strength formula. Instead of heavy lifting, which can increase the risk of injury—especially for those in their 50s and beyond—her routine encourages a focus on form and sustainable movement.
