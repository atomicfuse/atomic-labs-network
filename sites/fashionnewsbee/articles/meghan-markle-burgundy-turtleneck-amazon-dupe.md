Meghan Markle stepped out in a head-to-toe burgundy outfit this fall, and Us Magazine has already found the budget version of her sweater: a $13 slim-fit turtleneck on Amazon. The designer original, per Us, is a Joseph style. The dupe costs about what you'd spend on a latte and a croissant.

The broader pitch from Us is that burgundy is shaping up as a richer fall alternative to beige and the other quiet-luxury neutrals that have dominated closets. That's the angle worth chasing here: not just "look, a cheap sweater," but whether burgundy deserves a spot as your new default neutral.

## The Look: Monochrome, Polished, Fitted

According to Us, Markle's outfit centers on a fitted burgundy turtleneck in a polished, tonal look. The Amazon alternative the outlet highlights is a slim-fit top in the same family of color, positioned as an affordable stand-in for her Joseph piece.

A caveat, since honesty is part of the dupe game: a $13 top and a designer knit are not the same garment, and nothing in Us's report suggests otherwise. What you're really copying is the silhouette and the shade. That's most of what makes the outfit read as expensive anyway.

## Why Burgundy Might Be the New Beige

Beige has had a long, cashmere-soft run. It's hard not to see burgundy as the natural next move: still muted, still grown-up, but with enough depth to look deliberate rather than defaulted-to. Us frames it as a richer fall option, and that tracks with how the color behaves. It sits comfortably next to black, camel, cream and denim, so it doesn't demand a new wardrobe.

That's our read, not a rule, but the logic is simple. A neutral is a color that plays well with everything. Burgundy is a color that plays well with most things and looks better doing it.

## Three Ways to Wear a Fitted Turtleneck

The styling ideas below are our suggestions, not details from Markle's outfit.

### For Work
Tuck it into tailored trousers in black or charcoal and add a long coat. The slim fit means no bulk under a blazer.

### For the Weekend
Pair it with relaxed jeans and sneakers or flat boots. The fitted top keeps the outfit from tipping into sloppy.

### For Evening
Go tonal, in the spirit of Markle's monochrome approach: burgundy on burgundy, or a deep wine skirt or trouser with gold jewelry. Monochrome is the quickest way to look styled without trying hard.

## The Dupe Math

The appeal of this story is plain arithmetic. At $13, you can buy the sweater in more than one shade and still come out ahead. Celebrity-inspired buys and affordable Amazon finds stay popular as shoppers refresh their wardrobes for cooler weather, as Us's coverage reflects, and a basic turtleneck is about the lowest-risk way to join in.

One practical tip, again ours: with budget knits, the fit and the fabric hand are what separate chic from clingy. Check reviews for sizing and see-through complaints before you commit, and steam the neckline so it sits cleanly.

## The Takeaway

Markle's outfit is a reminder that the most convincing "rich" look often comes down to one well-chosen color worn head to toe. Beige says you're being careful. Burgundy says you meant it. For $13, that's a pretty cheap way to start meaning it.
