Naomi Watts has apparently retired the basic crossbody. Per Us Weekly, the actress was spotted carrying a slouchy suede hobo-style shoulder bag, a shape that sits somewhere between a shoulder bag and a bucket bag. Us also flagged a $40 lookalike on Amazon from the brand Bostanten, which is the part your wallet will care about.

## The Case Against the Everyday Crossbody

The crossbody is the sensible friend of the handbag world. It's hands-free, it's practical, and it's on every commuter in every city. Us Weekly's pitch is that the hobo is the more polished alternative for everyday carrying, and it's hard to argue with the logic: a bag that drapes from the shoulder tends to look deliberate, while a strap slashed across a puffer coat can look like you're on your way to a theme park.

That's our read, not a rule. But it explains why the hobo works as an upgrade rather than just a change.

## Why the Hobo Reads as Expensive

Per Us, the appeal is the "quiet luxury" feel: a soft, slouchy silhouette with no logo doing the talking. Suede helps. It has a matte, tactile richness that smooth faux leather often can't fake, and it's right on trend, as slouchy suede shoulder bags are having a fall moment, according to the outlet's framing.

There's also a pop-culture shorthand at work. Us ties the demand for elevated basics to celebrity street style and the "NYC rich mom" aesthetic, that universe of good coats, good boots and a bag that looks like it has seen a lot of school pickups. Watts carrying one off-duty fits that picture neatly, and it's hard not to wonder how many people will be hunting for the shape this week.

## The $40 Version

The stand-in Us points to is a Bostanten bag on Amazon, priced at $40. We can't speak to its leather, stitching or longevity beyond what the outlet reported, so treat that as a starting point and check the reviews before you buy. What the price does prove is the broader point: the look comes from the shape, not from a designer tag.

## How to Wear a Brown Shoulder Bag This Fall

Us positions the hobo as the easy accessory swap that makes coats, boots and sweaters look more expensive. Here's how we'd run with that:

- **Pair it with outerwear.** A slouchy bag softens a structured coat and keeps the outfit from feeling stiff.
- **Let it be the only statement.** Quiet luxury means the bag does its work without a logo, so keep hardware and prints minimal.
- **Stick with texture.** Suede next to knits and boots adds richness without extra effort.
- **Wear it on the shoulder.** The whole point is that drape, so resist the urge to shorten the strap and turn it back into a crossbody.

## The Takeaway

The best thing about this trend is how little it asks of you: one slouchy bag, one sensible price, and a fall wardrobe that suddenly looks like it has a budget it doesn't. Watts didn't need a logo to make the point, and neither will you.
