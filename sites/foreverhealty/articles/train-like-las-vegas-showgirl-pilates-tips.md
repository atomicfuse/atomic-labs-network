## Train Like a Las Vegas Showgirl: Pilates Tips from Kelsie McNutt

Las Vegas showgirls light up the stage with their dazzling performances, but behind the sparkle lies a rigorous fitness routine that keeps them in top shape. In honor of National Women’s Health and Fitness Day, let’s explore how Kelsie McNutt, a dancer in the renowned ‘FANTASY’ at the Luxor, utilizes Pilates, strength training, and daily movement to stay stage-ready for her six high-energy performances each week. By incorporating insights from her routine, you can embrace an accessible and effective wellness path.  

### 1. Embrace Pilates for Core Strength

Kelsie emphasizes the importance of Pilates as a core component of her training. It doesn’t just sculpt the body; it significantly enhances overall strength and stability. Here are some beginner Pilates moves you can try at home to strengthen your core and improve posture:  
- **The Hundred**: Lay on your back, raise your legs to a 90-degree angle, lift your head and shoulders, and pump your arms for 100 counts while breathing.
- **Pelvic Curl**: Lie on your back with your feet flat on the ground. Slowly curl your pelvis off the mat, articulating each vertebrae until you create a straight line from shoulders to knees. Roll back down gently.
- **Side Leg Lifts**: Lie on your side, stack your legs, and lift the top leg about 45 degrees then lower it back. This targets the hip abductors and glutes, crucial for those long dance routines.

These moves can help establish a strong foundation, which is essential for preventing injuries—an important aspect of her routine given the demanding nature of live performances.

### 2. Prioritize Recovery and Flexibility

Recovery is as vital as the workout itself. Kelsie integrates stretching routines into her day to promote flexibility and prevent muscle tightness. Here’s a simple post-workout stretch sequence:
- **Cat-Cow Stretch**: Alternately arch and round your back while on all fours to improve spinal flexibility.
- **Child’s Pose**: Sit back on your heels with arms extended to relax your lower back.
- **Standing Forward Bend**: With feet hip-width apart, hinge forward at the hips and let your head hang for a relaxing stretch.

These stretches can help mitigate soreness and make sure your body stays limber enough for daily demands.

### 3. Incorporate Daily Steps and Hydration

Staying stage-ready means more than just structured workouts. Kelsie reminds us of the importance of incorporating daily movement into our routines. Aiming for 10,000 steps each day can be an excellent way to stay active. Consider these strategies:
- **Walk During Breaks**: Utilize any downtime during your day to take a quick walk, helping you reach your step goal without much extra effort.
- **Active Commuting**: If possible, walk or cycle to work, or park further away to increase your daily movement.

Hydration also plays a crucial role in Kelsie's fitness. Aim to drink plenty of water throughout the day—keeping a water bottle with you can serve as a constant reminder to stay hydrated.

### 4. Make Fitness Fun

Fitness doesn’t have to feel like a chore. Kelsie highlights the connection between physical activity and confidence. Try to find joy in your movements:
- **Dance Classes**: Join a dance class to add enjoyment to your routine, just as she does.
- **Group Activities**: Find a workout buddy to make exercising feel more like socializing than a task.

Cultivating an enjoyable fitness routine can help you maintain motivation and stay consistent over time.

### Final Takeaway

Preparing for the limelight may seem a world away from the average fitness journey, but Kelsie McNutt’s routine sheds light on attainable wellness practices. By embracing Pilates, valuing recovery, stepping more throughout your day, and making fitness an enjoyable endeavor, you can experience the confidence and vitality of a Las Vegas showgirl. Start incorporating these tips into your daily routine, and watch how your wellness journey transforms!
