If your brain is fried and a ranked match sounds like homework, a cleaning game may be the reset you need. Vice recently spotlighted three cozy picks built around low-stress play, soothing routines, and satisfying organization: Bao Bao's Cozy Cleaning Service, Fresh Start Cleaning Simulator, and Unpacking. Below is what each one offers, based on Vice's coverage, and how to choose the one that fits your evening.

A note on labeling: this is a What to Play roundup built on Vice's reporting, not our own hands-on review. We haven't played these for this piece, and we haven't verified platforms, prices, or subscription availability here. Check your storefront of choice before buying.

## Why cleaning games work as a wind-down

Vice's central argument is that cleaning and tidying mechanics can be a calming alternative to more intense games. That tracks with a wider appeal: reset routines, satisfying makeovers, and the kind of content people call "oddly calming." You see a mess, you fix it, and you get a clear before and after. There's no timer to beat and no one to lose to.

Cozy gaming keeps growing, per Vice, as players look for relaxing, low-pressure experiences after work, school, or burnout-heavy days. These three games are the picks it highlights.

## Bao Bao's Cozy Cleaning Service: best for a lighthearted, low-stakes session

Vice's tagline for the group is "power-washing with a panda," and Bao Bao's Cozy Cleaning Service is where that image comes from. The pitch is simple: cleaning as a gentle, friendly routine.

**Pick it if:** you want a game that feels like a warm, easygoing chore, with a bit of charm to take the edge off a rough day.

## Fresh Start Cleaning Simulator: best for a classic scrub-and-reset routine

Fresh Start Cleaning Simulator is the most straightforwardly named of the three, and it sits squarely in the cleaning-sim lane. It fits the soothing-routine side of the genre: take a space that needs work and make it better.

**Pick it if:** the idea of methodically restoring a space is what relaxes you, and you don't need a story to go with it.

## Unpacking: best for organizing and settling into a new home

Vice describes Unpacking as being about unpacking a new home. Instead of scrubbing grime, you're finding a place for everything, which is the organization side of the cozy appeal.

**Pick it if:** you enjoy tidying, arranging, and decorating more than scrubbing. If a neatly organized shelf makes you happy, start here.

## How to choose tonight

- **Want something playful?** Start with Bao Bao's Cozy Cleaning Service.
- **Want a straight cleaning routine?** Go with Fresh Start Cleaning Simulator.
- **Want to organize and decorate?** Pick Unpacking.

There's no wrong order here. All three are designed around the same idea: a short session should leave you calmer than when you sat down.

## Tips for getting the most out of a cozy session

These are our suggestions, not claims from Vice:

- Set a loose stopping point, like one room or one level, so the game stays a break rather than another to-do list.
- Play with something low-key in your ears, like a podcast or music, if the game's own audio isn't doing it for you.
- Skip the completionist urge on your first night. The point is to decompress.

Pick one of the three tonight, play for 30 minutes, and see whether the before-and-after payoff does what Vice says it does.
