Bethesda's recent *The Elder Scrolls VI* teaser has fans buzzing again, per Vice. But no release window has been confirmed, so the wait continues. This guide helps you pick one of three fantasy RPGs based on what you miss most: Tamriel itself, a chosen-one adventure, or a story-driven quest.

The three picks, as recommended by Vice, are *Elder Scrolls Online*, *Dragon's Dogma*, and *The Witcher III: Wild Hunt*. Each offers a rich world, room to explore, and quests that matter. We haven't covered platforms, prices, or subscription availability here, so check your storefront of choice before you buy.

*This is a What to Play roundup based on Vice's reporting, not a hands-on review.*

## If You Miss Tamriel Itself: Elder Scrolls Online

The most direct way to stay in the franchise's world is the franchise's own online game. *Elder Scrolls Online* lets you keep exploring Tamriel and soaking up its lore while you wait for the next mainline entry.

**Pick it if:** the setting is what pulls you back to Elder Scrolls games. If you read the in-game books, chase side quests for flavor, and like simply being in this universe, this is the closest match you can play today.

**Keep in mind:** it's the online entry in the series, so expect a different rhythm from a single-player Bethesda game. Think of it as a way to revisit the world rather than a stand-in for the next solo adventure.

## If You Want a Chosen-One Adventure: Dragon's Dogma

*Dragon's Dogma* is the pick for players who love the classic fantasy setup: a hero with a destiny, a wide world to cross, and monsters to face along the way. Vice highlights it for its chosen-one fantasy and open-world adventure.

**Pick it if:** you enjoy setting out with a goal and letting the road surprise you. If your favorite Elder Scrolls moments are the ones where you wander off the path and stumble into trouble, this fits.

**Keep in mind:** it's a different world with its own identity, so don't expect Tamriel's lore. What you get is the sense of adventure.

## If You Want Story Choices and Lore: The Witcher III: Wild Hunt

*The Witcher III: Wild Hunt* earns its spot for lore-rich exploration and quests where your choices carry weight. Vice points to its story choices as the draw.

**Pick it if:** you care most about quests with meaningful decisions and a world that feels deep. If you're the type who reads every dialogue option before choosing, start here.

**Keep in mind:** this one leans toward a more directed story than a pure sandbox, so it suits players who want narrative payoff along with their exploring.

## How to Choose in 30 Seconds

- **You want more Tamriel:** *Elder Scrolls Online*.
- **You want to chase a destiny across open country:** *Dragon's Dogma*.
- **You want choices that shape the story:** *The Witcher III: Wild Hunt*.

All three fit the bill Vice set out: rich worlds, open exploration, deep lore, and meaningful quests. They just emphasize different parts of that formula.

## What We Know About Elder Scrolls VI

Very little, officially. Per Vice, Bethesda's recent teaser reignited anticipation, but no release window is confirmed. Treat any specific date you see floating around as unverified until Bethesda announces it. When it does, we'll cover platforms and launch details here.

## Your Next Step

Pick the one that matches what you miss most, not the one with the biggest reputation. Match the game to the itch, and the wait for *The Elder Scrolls VI* gets a lot shorter.
