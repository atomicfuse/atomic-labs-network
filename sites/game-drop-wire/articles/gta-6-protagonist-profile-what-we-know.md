## How to vet a GTA 6 claim in 60 seconds

Search interest in this topic is rising, which is exactly when rumors spread fastest. Run any claim through these checks:

1. **Find the source.** Does the post link to a Rockstar announcement, trailer or interview, or just to another post?
2. **Look for specifics with no origin.** Exact percentages, level counts and unlock lists with no named source are a red flag.
3. **Check for attribution.** Reliable coverage says who said what. "Reportedly" and "per" are good signs. Flat certainty about an unreleased game is not.
4. **Compare outlets.** If Red Bull, Forbes and others describe it the same way, that's stronger than one viral clip.
5. **Note the date.** Details about unreleased games change. Something accurate last year may not be now.
