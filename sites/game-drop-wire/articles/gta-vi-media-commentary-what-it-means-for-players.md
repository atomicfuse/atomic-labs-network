### The newer "wokeness" complaint

That tension is the real story. A single game being attacked for opposite reasons suggests the game is serving as a stand-in for something else.

## Why every big game becomes a morality test

This is our analysis, not reporting. Three patterns seem to explain it:

1. **Scale invites scrutiny.** Huge audiences mean huge stakes for anyone commenting.
2. **Generational anxiety is easy to channel.** Concerns about what young people spend time on attach neatly to any popular medium.
3. **Outrage travels.** A strong claim about masculinity or harm gets clicked and shared far more than a measured take.

None of this means criticism of a game is automatically illegitimate. Parents have real questions about content and playtime, and those deserve practical answers. But a pundit's verdict on a game is different from a hands-on assessment of what it contains.

## How to decide for yourself

If the noise has you wondering whether *GTA VI* is for you or for your household, skip the commentary and use these steps:

- **Check the official rating.** Look up the ESRB rating (or your region's equivalent) and its content descriptors on the storefront page.

- **Set your own time limits.** If "wasting time" is your worry, that's a scheduling decision you control. Decide how many hours a week you want to spend in any game.
