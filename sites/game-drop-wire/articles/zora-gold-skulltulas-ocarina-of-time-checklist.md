If you're chasing 100% in Ocarina of Time on Switch 2, the Zora region is where a Gold Skulltula hunt turns into a multi-trip errand. Per Videogameschronicle's guide, Zora's River, Zora's Domain, and Zora's Fountain hold eight Gold Skulltulas between them, and you won't grab all of them in one pass. This page gives you the full checklist, the gear to line up first, and a way to plan your visits so you don't backtrack more than you have to.

A note on sourcing: this is a roundup of VGC's guide, not our own playthrough. The guide's full walkthrough has the exact spots, so treat this as your planning sheet.

## The full checklist: all eight Zora-area Skulltulas

VGC's guide breaks the region into these eight entries:

1. **Zora's River: Magic Bean seller.** The first entry is tied to the Magic Bean seller in the river area.
2. **Near the Domain entrance.** One sits close to where you enter Zora's Domain.
3. **Magic Bean plant.** This one depends on planting a Magic Bean, so it can't be done on a first visit without beans.
4. **Zora's River as adult Link.** A second river Skulltula that only shows up when you return as an adult.
5. **Zora's Domain as adult Link.** A Domain Skulltula that also needs your adult form.
6. **Fountain tree.** A Skulltula tied to a tree in Zora's Fountain.
7. **Fountain at night.** A time-locked one. You need to be there after dark.
8. **Fountain with gauntlets.** The last entry requires the Silver Gauntlets.

That's three in the River and Domain area that you can plan around early, two that are explicitly adult-only, and three in the Fountain where timing and gear matter most.

## Bring these tools before you start

VGC's guide says the hunt calls for several items: bugs, Magic Beans, the Boomerang, the Silver Gauntlets, and the Lens of Truth. The guide's summary doesn't match each tool to a specific Skulltula, so check the full walkthrough for that. What you can do from the list above:

- **Magic Beans:** Needed for the plant entry (#3). Buy them before you head out.
- **Silver Gauntlets:** Needed for the Fountain entry (#8). Don't plan on finishing the Fountain until you have them.
- **Boomerang, bugs, and Lens of Truth:** Also on the guide's required list. Keep them in your inventory so you aren't forced into a return trip over one missing item.

## Child Link vs. adult Link: how to split your trips

The region needs multiple returns, which is the main reason it trips up completionists. Two entries are explicitly adult-only (#4 and #5), and the guide frames the others around different items and times of day.

A practical way to split it:

- **Child pass:** Handle whatever you can reach with the tools you have, including the Magic Bean seller entry and the Magic Bean planting, so the plant has time to be useful later.
- **Adult pass:** Return for the River and Domain Skulltulas that only appear as adult Link.
- **Fountain pass:** Save this for when you hold the Silver Gauntlets and can wait out the night cycle.

The guide doesn't specify which age handles every Fountain entry, so confirm that in the full walkthrough before you commit to a route.

## The easy-to-miss ones

Two entries are the likeliest to slip past you:

- **The Fountain at night (#7).** If you only visit during the day, you'll never see it. Nighttime Skulltulas are the classic cleanup miss because you can walk through the area three times and never meet the condition.
- **The Magic Bean plant (#3).** It depends on a decision you made earlier: whether you bought and planted a bean. If you skipped it, you'll need to circle back.

If you're short on patience, mark these two first in your notes.

## A cleanup route that saves backtracking

Here's a simple order based on the checklist:

1. Do the River's Magic Bean seller Skulltula and plant your bean while you're there.
2. Grab the one near the Domain entrance on the same trip.
3. Once you're adult Link, sweep the River and then the Domain for #4 and #5.
4. Finish at the Fountain: tree first, then wait for night, then use the Silver Gauntlets for the last one.

This keeps the Fountain, your most gear-dependent stop, for last, so you only go there once everything is in your pocket.

## Why bother now

VGC points out that players are revisiting Ocarina of Time on Switch 2, and Gold Skulltulas remain one of the game's classic 100% challenges. Because this region is time-locked and item-gated, it rewards a checklist more than wandering. Missed ones are easy to overlook after major story milestones, so a cleanup pass is worth planning.

Before you leave Hyrule Field, write down the eight entries above and check off each one as you get it. Then open VGC's full guide for the exact positions.
