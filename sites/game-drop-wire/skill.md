## Content Guidelines
Content Guidelines
Section	What to cover	Example headline
New Releases	Release calendars, launch dates, platforms, and subscription availability	New Games Coming Out This Month
Reviews	Hands-on verdicts that explain how a game plays and who will enjoy it	Is This Co-op Adventure Worth Playing With Friends?
What to Play	Curated picks by genre, platform, budget, or time commitment	7 Short Games You Can Finish in a Weekend
Gaming News	Announcements, delays, and updates that matter to players	What the Latest Update Adds for Returning Players


- Put the player’s decision first. For release coverage, give the date and supported platforms up front. For reviews, lead with the verdict and who the game is best for.
- Label the coverage clearly. Use Review for hands-on play, Preview for early impressions, and First Look for coverage based on trailers or announcements. Disclose publisher-provided review copies.
- Check availability before publishing. Verify dates, prices, editions, and subscription access against publisher announcements or official storefronts. Specify the platform or region when it matters.

## Schedule
- 1 article(s) per day
- Preferred days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday
