## Why Facial Performance Capture Matters

The 13wham report notes that the project involved facial performance capture. That detail matters for how you think about voice acting in a modern blockbuster.

In a traditional recording booth, an actor delivers lines into a microphone and the audio is matched to animation later. With facial capture, the face is part of the performance. Expressions, reactions, and small shifts in emotion are recorded alongside the voice. Here's what that means in practice for you as a player:

- **Characters can feel more natural.** Voice and facial movement come from the same performance rather than being stitched together.
- **Minor characters get more weight.** A side character with a believable face and voice can stick with you.
- **Actors need more than a good voice.** Acting for the camera and the microphone at once is a broader skill set.

This is our read on why capture matters, not a claim from the report. The report itself confirms only that facial performance capture was used.
