## Why "localized" and "persistent" are the words to watch

These two words carry most of the news.

**Localized** suggests that conditions can differ depending on where you are. If that holds, you could plausibly be in a downpour in one part of the map while conditions elsewhere differ. That's our reading of the term, not a confirmed example from Rockstar.

**Persistent** suggests a storm is something that exists and develops, rather than a scripted moment that starts and stops. Again, how that plays out in missions or free roam hasn't been spelled out in what we have.

It's reasonable to say these terms point toward weather that feels like part of the simulation rather than a backdrop. How far that goes is the open question.
