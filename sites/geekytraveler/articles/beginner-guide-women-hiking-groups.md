Hiking clubs are emerging as the new run clubs — especially all-women groups that provide a supportive and empowering environment for outdoor adventurers. ## Understanding All-Women Hiking Groups

The rise of all-women hiking clubs in Australia, such as the Cool Girls Club and Wild Women on Top, is changing the way women engage with the outdoors. These organizations prioritize safety and camaraderie, creating a space for women to build confidence while adventuring together. Unlike traditional run clubs, these hiking groups focus on community and enjoyment rather than performance, making them more approachable for beginners.

## Benefits of Joining a Women-Only Hiking Group

1. **Build Outdoor Confidence**: Women-only groups foster a supportive environment where you can develop hiking skills at your own pace, without the pressure often found in mixed-gender settings.
   
2. **Connect with Fellow Adventurers**: Exploring nature alongside other women provides an opportunity to bond over shared interests and experiences. This connection can often lead to lasting friendships.
   
3. **Mental Wellbeing**: Nature has been shown to enhance mood and alleviate feelings of loneliness, making hiking not just a physical activity but a crucial aspect of mental health care.

## Steps to Start Your Hiking Adventure

### Find a Local Group

Use a simple online search to find all-women hiking groups near you. Websites and social media platforms often list local chapters or upcoming events. Reach out to them for details on membership and upcoming hikes.

### Equip Yourself with Basics

Don’t worry about high-end gear; you don't need to invest a fortune to join a hike. Start with comfortable clothing suitable for temperatures and weather conditions. Here’s a basic checklist:
- **Footwear**: Good walking shoes or hiking boots.
- **Clothing**: Dress in layers to accommodate changing weather.
- **Backpack**: A small, comfortable bag for carrying essentials like water, snacks, and a first-aid kit.
- **Navigation Tools**: Bring a map or download a reliable hiking app to avoid getting lost.

### Join a Beginner-friendly Hike

Many hiking groups offer beginner or low-pressure hikes, perfect for those who may feel intimidated. Look for guided trails and ask group coordinators about the difficulty level. This step is crucial for building confidence and ensuring a pleasant experience.

### Engage and Connect

During hikes, take the opportunity to chat with fellow members. Shared experiences, encouragement, and shared challenges open avenues for friendship. Share tips, discuss your experiences, and engage with the group's social media to stay connected.
