## Understanding Forex and Liquidity Risk

Before diving into the implications of this AI tool, it’s essential to clarify what forex and liquidity risk really mean. Forex risk arises when changes in foreign exchange rates affect a company's financial performance. For example, if a multinational corporation conducts business in multiple countries, fluctuations in currency values can impact profits. 

Liquidity risk, on the other hand, involves the danger of not being able to quickly convert assets into cash without a significant loss in value. Essentially, banks need to ensure they have enough liquidity to meet their obligations while also maximizing their investment opportunities.
