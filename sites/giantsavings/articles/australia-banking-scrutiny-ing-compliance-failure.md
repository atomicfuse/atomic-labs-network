## What Happened?

The Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) discovered that ING had consistently overstated its liquidity position and, at times, failed to meet minimum regulatory standards. As a result, APRA imposed new license conditions requiring the bank to hold more liquidity and capital. This is a direct response to serious compliance failures in the bank's financial reporting obligations.
