## Details of Trump's Proposed Tax Cuts

These changes, he argues, aim to stimulate economic growth and expand the job market by providing individuals and corporations with more disposable income.

## Which Demographics Stand to Gain?
While Trump's tax cut proposals have been framed as beneficial to all, the reality is often more nuanced. Several groups potentially stand to benefit disproportionately:
- **High-income earners**: Historically, tax cuts tend to favor those at the top of the income bracket. Should these cuts be implemented, wealthier individuals may see substantial reductions in their overall tax burdens.
- **Businesses**: By lowering corporate tax rates, companies can retain more profits, which can lead to increased investments in growth and hiring. This could particularly benefit larger corporations that can absorb the benefits of such cuts more readily.
- **Service Industry Workers**: As mentioned, the 'no tax on tips' initiative can provide a significant boost to workers in hospitality and service sectors. For instance, servers who rely heavily on tips may find their after-tax income considerably enhanced under this proposal.

However, it's also essential to consider the potential downsides. Critics argue that such tax cuts could lead to reduced federal revenue, which may impact public services and social programs — often essential to lower-income populations. Without a broader economic growth to offset the loss of tax revenue, the long-term impacts of these cuts could raise significant concerns.
