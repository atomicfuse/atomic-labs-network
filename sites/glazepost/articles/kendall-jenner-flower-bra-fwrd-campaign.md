Floral appliqué is a rising trend, and a flower bra is the daring cousin of a floral dress. Florals read as romantic and soft, which lets a provocative silhouette feel playful rather than aggressive. Pop culture has a long history of fashion moments that turn on this tension between delicate materials and bold exposure, and the flower bra fits right into it.
