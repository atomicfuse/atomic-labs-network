The costume centered on Jenner's orange Chrome Hearts look. That matters. "Kylie Jenner" as a general concept is hard to costume, but one particular outfit is a visual shorthand people can clock instantly, even in a thumbnail. The signature pout gets its own mention in the report, and it's hard not to see why. A facial expression is the one element you can't buy. It takes commitment, and commitment is where a lot of look-alike costumes quietly fall apart.

If you're planning a last-minute celebrity costume before the holiday, Rinna's approach offers a simple template drawn from what The Cut described:
