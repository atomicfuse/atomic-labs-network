The appeal is simple visual comedy. In a sea of black, yellow is the one thing you can't miss. Any photo or clip with that contrast practically writes its own caption, and people love a "rule-breaking" moment, especially at a celebrity event where every detail is scrutinized.

It also touches a nerve. Most people have worried about reading an invitation wrong, or have strong opinions about guests who ignore one. A dress code is a small, clear rule, which makes breaking it feel like a clean verdict: guilty or innocent. The internet loves a verdict.
