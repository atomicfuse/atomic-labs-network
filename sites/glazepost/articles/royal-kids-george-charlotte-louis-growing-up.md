## What the Format Gets Right

There are a few reasons this kind of piece lands with readers:

1. **Built-in suspense.** You know where it ends, but you still click to see how you get there.
2. **Shared milestones.** Christenings and first days of school are things most families recognize, even when the setting is far grander.
3. **Three arcs at once.** George, Charlotte and Louis are close enough in age to be compared, and different enough to give each entry its own flavor.
4. **Low stakes.** It's a pleasant scroll, not a scandal.
