## The Oldest Costume Gag in the Book

Dressing up as yourself is a time-honored Halloween move, and it works for a simple reason: it's the lowest-effort costume that still lands as a joke. The humor lives in the gap between the idea of a costume (becoming someone else) and the reality (not changing a thing). Anyone who has ever shown up to a party in their work clothes and announced they came as "a person who's tired" knows the format.
