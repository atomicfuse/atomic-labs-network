## How to Judge a Viral UAP Clip Yourself

You do not need a lab to apply discipline to a sighting. Use these checks on any clip, this one included.

### 1. Find the original source
Who filmed it, and where did it first appear? A clip passed along through reposts loses its context quickly. Original files carry data that edited copies strip away.

### 2. Ask what else it could be
Experts urge caution on viral UAP footage for a reason: cameras distort. Lens effects, distance, compression, and unusual lighting can all make ordinary things look strange. A responsible investigator lists the mundane explanations first and tries to eliminate them one by one.

### 3. Look for a second witness
One camera is a clue. Two independent angles, radar data, or other records are far stronger. If a case rests on a single clip, treat it as unresolved.

### 4. Watch for the missing details
Time, location, direction, and duration. If those are vague, the case cannot be tested. Vague cases are the ones that stay mysterious forever, not because they are unexplainable but because they are unexaminable.

### 5. Notice your own reaction
If you feel certain within ten seconds, that is the nickname and the mood talking. Let the evidence catch up.
