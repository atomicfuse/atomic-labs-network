## Unearthing Dietary Patterns

The bones and teeth of individuals buried in this necropolis have provided researchers with a portal into the past, showing that men enjoyed a diet richer in animal protein compared to women. Isotope analysis—an advanced technique that examines the chemical composition of remains—indicates a marked disparity in food access tied directly to gender. This finding underscores a long-standing inequality, rooted deep in the social fabric of the community.

## The Gaps in Gender Access

According to the study, while men in this ancient society had greater access to animal proteins, women relied more heavily on plant-based diets. This sexual division of dietary resources reflects larger cultural attitudes towards gender roles. It is not only a matter of what they ate; it suggests underlying ideologies about women and their places in the social hierarchy. 

## Migration and Mobility: The Unequal Footing

## Gravestones vs. Lived Realities
