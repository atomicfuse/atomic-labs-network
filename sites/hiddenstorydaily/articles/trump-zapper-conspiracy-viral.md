2. **Algorithm-Driven Platforms**: Social media algorithms favor engagement, pushing controversial content to wider audiences regardless of factual accuracy. This means that provocative claims, such as the zapper theory, can go viral in a bid for clicks and shares.
3. **Fringe Narratives Becoming Mainstream**: In an environment devoid of gatekeeping, what starts on the fringes can quickly catapult to the mainstream discourse. The zapper theory isn't just a niche topic; it reflects a growing acceptance of absurd narratives among a significant number of users.

## Why Do People Embrace Such Bizarre Narratives?

The allure of conspiracy theories like the zapper stems from a complex interplay of psychology and culture. Here are some reasons why these narratives find willing audiences:

- **Community and Identity**: Believing in a conspiracy can create a sense of belonging among like-minded individuals. It often acts as an in-group bonding mechanism where shared beliefs reinforce community ties.
