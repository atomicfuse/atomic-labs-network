Texas is one of the most closely watched states in national politics, and NBC News has put together a hub for the 2026 Texas midterms covering key races, candidates, and past results. If you plan to follow along with friends or family, you'll want a room that works for a long evening of refreshing results pages.

Here's how to set up a comfortable watch space with what you already own, plus a few cheap upgrades. No special skills needed.

## Start With the Screen and the Sightlines

A long night of watching goes better when everyone can see. Before you buy anything, sit in every seat and check the view.

- Move the main seating so no one has to twist their neck.
- Keep the screen away from windows to cut glare.
- If you're using a laptop or tablet to follow the NBC News race pages, prop it on a sturdy stack of books at eye level.

If you have a small living room, angle the sofa slightly toward the screen instead of pushing everything flat against the walls. It makes the room feel more intentional and gives guests a better view.

## Build Flexible Seating on a Budget

You don't need a bigger sofa. You need more places to sit.

### Ideas that cost little or nothing
- Pull in dining chairs and turn them toward the screen.
- Stack floor cushions or couch pillows for casual seats.
- Use a sturdy storage ottoman or bench as extra seating.
- Borrow folding chairs from a neighbor and tuck them away afterward.

Add a throw blanket to the back of each seat. It looks tidy, and guests can grab one if the room cools off.

## Use Lighting to Set the Mood

Overhead lights are harsh for a long evening and can wash out the screen. Switch them off and layer in softer light instead.

1. Turn on two or three lamps at different heights.
2. Choose warm bulbs (look for "soft white" on the box) for a relaxed feel.
3. Place one lamp near the snack area so people can see what they're grabbing.
4. Avoid placing a bright lamp directly behind the screen.

A basic plug-in lamp or a string of lights costs only a few dollars at a thrift store or discount shop, and it makes a room feel finished.

## Create a Simple Snack Station

Keep food in one spot so the living room stays clear. A console table, a bookshelf shelf, or a tray on a side table all work.

- Use a cutting board or a thrifted tray as a base.
- Group items by height: tall drinks at the back, bowls in front.
- Put out a small trash bin and a stack of napkins.
- Add coasters so no one worries about your wood furniture.

Texas-inspired snacks are a fun touch if you like a theme, such as chips and salsa. Keep it simple and let the food do the decorating.

## Give Everyone a Spot to Put Things

People hate holding a drink for four hours. Small surfaces solve this fast.

- Tuck a stool or small stack of books beside each sofa end.
- Place a tray on the coffee table to corral remotes and phones.
- Add a power strip along the wall so devices can charge.

A tidy charging corner keeps cords off the floor and keeps people from hovering near the outlets.

## Keep the Decor Neutral and Welcoming

Guests may have different views, and a watch party is more fun when the room feels comfortable for everyone. Skip anything that looks like campaigning and lean on cozy basics: warm textures, a few candles or battery-powered flameless candles, and fresh greenery in a jar or pitcher.

A simple color palette helps. Pick two or three shades, such as cream, deep blue, and a warm wood tone, and repeat them across pillows, blankets, and tableware. It's an easy way to make a mismatched room look pulled together.

## Plan for the Long Haul

Results pages can take a while to settle, so build in comfort for a late night.

- Set out water alongside any other drinks.
- Keep a basket of extra blankets nearby.
- Open a window briefly before guests arrive to freshen the air.
- Decide in advance where coats and bags will go, such as hooks by the door or a bed in another room.

Small touches like these keep the room from feeling crowded as the night goes on.

## A Quick Setup Timeline

**The day before:** Clear surfaces, test your screen, and gather chairs, lamps, and blankets.

**Two hours before:** Arrange the seating, set up the snack station, and swap harsh overhead light for lamps.

**Thirty minutes before:** Bring out food, light any candles, and pull up the NBC News Texas midterms hub so the races are ready when guests arrive.

## The One Thing to Do First

Sit in each seat in your living room and check the view. If everyone can see the screen and reach a place to set a drink, the rest of the evening takes care of itself.
