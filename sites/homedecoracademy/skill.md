# Content Agent Instructions for homedecoracademy.com

## Target Audiences
General

## Tone
Engaging, informative, conversational

## Topics
- Budget-Friendly Makeovers
- Thrifty Decor Finds
- Everyday Style Lessons
- DIY Decor Projects

## Content Guidelines
Beginner-friendly decorating advice with clear, actionable steps.
Focus on room styling, small spaces, color, lighting, and budget updates.
Use short paragraphs, helpful lists, and realistic examples.
Keep the tone warm and approachable; avoid jargon and exaggerated promises.

## Schedule
- 4 article(s) per day
- Preferred days: Monday, Friday
