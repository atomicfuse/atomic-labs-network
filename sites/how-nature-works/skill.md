## Content Guidelines
Content Guidelines
Section	What to cover	Example headline
Animals & Wildlife	Familiar animal behaviors, habitats, and adaptations	Why Do Birds Fly in a V?
Plants & Trees	How plants grow, reproduce, and respond to the seasons	Why Do Some Trees Keep Their Leaves All Winter?
Insects & Backyard Life	Pollinators, garden insects, fungi, and other discoveries close to home	Why Do Fireflies Light Up?
Land & Water	How rivers, coastlines, rocks, and soil change over time	Why Do Rivers Bend?
Nature Through the Seasons	Migration, blooms, and seasonal changes readers can spot	Why Do Some Flowers Close at Night?


- Start with something readers can picture. Open with a familiar sight, give the short answer, then explain how it works in plain English.
- Give readers something to notice next time. A story on river bends could point out the sand or gravel that often collects on the inside of a bend.
- Be specific about species and regions. When a behavior varies by species, season, or location, say where it applies. Verify details with reliable sources such as museums, universities, or wildlife agencies.
