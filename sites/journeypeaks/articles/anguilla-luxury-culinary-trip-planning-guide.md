## How to Build a Food-Led Itinerary

I can't hand you a verified restaurant list from the announcement, since the coverage focuses on the awards themselves. What I can give you is a planning framework that works for any destination known for its dining.

### Step 1: Book the anchor meals first

On a small island with a strong food reputation, the best tables can fill up. Pick two or three dinners you'd be upset to miss and reserve them before you finalize flights.

### Step 2: Leave room for the unplanned

Don't schedule every meal. Keep at least two lunches or dinners open so you can follow local recommendations from your hotel staff, drivers, or the people at the next table.

### Step 3: Mix the formats

A luxury food trip shouldn't mean seven formal dinners in a row. Alternate a splurge night with a casual beachside lunch. You'll taste more of the island and your budget will thank you.

### Step 4: Choose lodging that supports your plan

Since Anguilla was honored for luxury, you'll likely find high-end stays. Decide whether you want a resort with on-site dining or a smaller property that leaves you free to roam. Check what's included before you book.
