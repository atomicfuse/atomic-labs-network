Apple cider is the drink you buy at a roadside orchard stand and then drink plain, straight from the jug. This season, try something a little more festive. By the end of this piece you'll know how to build a café-style mulled cider at home and what to put on the board beside it.

The ideas come from Bill Michaels of Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard, who shared autumn cider recipes on "Fox & Friends Weekend," per Fox News. His demonstration centered on a warm mulled cider with a caramel-rimmed glass, whipped cream, and mulling spices, paired with extra-sharp cheddar.

## The Drink: Mulled Cider With a Dessert Twist

According to Fox News, Michaels' version has three signature elements: mulling spices, a caramel-rimmed glass, and a whipped cream topping. That's the whole concept, and it's easy to reproduce.

### How to build it

The segment summary doesn't give exact measurements, so treat these as a sensible framework rather than a verbatim recipe:

1. **Start with good cider.** Fresh cider from a mill or orchard gives you the most flavor to work with.
2. **Warm it gently with mulling spices.** Keep it hot but don't let it boil hard, which can dull the aroma. Common mulling spices include cinnamon sticks, cloves, and allspice, though use whatever blend you like.
3. **Rim the glass with caramel.** Dip or drizzle the rim before you pour so it sets slightly.
4. **Pour and top with whipped cream.** This turns a simple warm drink into something closer to a café special.

The result reads as dessert, which makes it a natural finish to a dinner or a cozy weekend afternoon.

## The Pairing: Extra-Sharp Cheddar

Michaels also paired the cider with extra-sharp cheddar, which Fox News describes as a classic fall flavor combination. Here's our take on why it works: cider is sweet and slightly tart, while a sharp, salty cheddar gives the palate something savory to push against. Each makes the other taste better.

A few ways to use that idea:

- Serve cheddar cubes or thin slices alongside the warm mugs.
- Add apple slices to the plate to echo the cider.
- Keep the cheese the star of the savory side, so the sweet whipped cream and caramel don't compete with it.

## Turn It Into a Fall Drink Board

If you're hosting, you can combine both ideas into one easy setup:

- A pot of mulled cider kept warm on the stove
- A small bowl of caramel and a plate for rimming glasses
- Whipped cream on hand for guests who want the dessert version
- A board with extra-sharp cheddar

Letting guests top and rim their own mugs makes it interactive, and it keeps you out of the kitchen. It also works for holiday gatherings, since the caramel-rimmed glass looks festive without any real effort.

## Make It a Trip: Visit an Orchard

Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard is the source of these ideas, and orchards and cider mills are a classic autumn day trip. If you're planning one, here's how to make it count:

- **Buy cider where it's pressed.** Fresh from the mill beats anything off a supermarket shelf as a base for mulling.
- **Pick up local cheddar while you're there.** Many orchard stores stock regional cheese, so check before you leave.
- **Pack a cooler.** Fresh cider should stay cold until you're ready to warm it.
- **Call ahead.** Hours and offerings often change with the season, so confirm before you drive.

You come home with the makings of the drink and a souvenir that tastes like the place.

## Quick Tips for Better Results

- Warm cider in a pot, not a microwave, so the spices can infuse evenly.
- Taste before serving. If the cider is already sweet, go light on the caramel.
- Choose a sturdy mug or heatproof glass, since the drink is served hot.
- Serve the cheddar at room temperature for fuller flavor.

## The Takeaway

Next time you bring home a jug of orchard cider, warm it with spices, rim the glass with caramel, finish with whipped cream, and set out a plate of extra-sharp cheddar. That's the whole upgrade, and it takes about as long as heating a pot.
