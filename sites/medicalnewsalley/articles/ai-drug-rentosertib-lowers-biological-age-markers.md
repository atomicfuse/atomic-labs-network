## What Are Biological Age Markers?

Biological age clocks assess how well your body is functioning relative to your calendar age. Unlike chronological age, which simply measures the time since your birth, biological age looks at various biological markers to provide a more dynamic picture of health. These markers can include factors such as gene expression and protein levels, which can reflect how well our bodies are aging. The lowering of these markers indicates a potentially healthier biological state, but it does not confirm an actual reversal of aging.
