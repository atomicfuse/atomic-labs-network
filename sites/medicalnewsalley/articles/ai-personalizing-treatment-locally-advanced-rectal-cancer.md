In a groundbreaking study, researchers at University College London (UCL) have harnessed artificial intelligence (AI) to tailor cancer treatments for patients suffering from locally advanced rectal cancer. This innovative approach could enhance survival rates and reduce unnecessary side effects by determining which patients stand to benefit from intensified treatment.

## AI's Role in Cancer Treatment
The research extends upon traditional cancer treatment methods, blending the proven tactics of chemoradiotherapy with insights derived from AI analysis. Specifically, by analyzing tumor cell density, the researchers found that patients classified with high tumor cell density were significantly less likely to experience recurrence or death when treated with a combination of conventional therapies plus the chemotherapy drug irinotecan.

Conversely, patients with low tumor cell densities did not see similar benefits from the intensified treatment regimen. This critical finding underscores the importance of personalizing cancer care using innovative technologies, allowing healthcare providers to adopt a more strategic approach to managing locally advanced rectal cancer.
