## Longevity Bottlenecks: Neurons and Heart Cells
Why are these specific cells so important? Neurons and heart cells are among the few cell types in our body that do not regenerate effectively when damaged or aged. As we age, the cumulative effects of somatic mutations can compromise the functionality of these cells. While other cells can regenerate over time, the deterioration of neurons and heart cells suggests they may be our biological limits, determining the upper range of human longevity.
