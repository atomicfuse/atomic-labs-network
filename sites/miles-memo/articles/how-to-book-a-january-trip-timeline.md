January is one of the better months to find a deal, but only if you book in the right order. Per Yahoo's travel coverage, the basic rule is to book flights ahead, lock in Martin Luther King Jr. Day weekend as soon as you can, and choose refundable hotel and rental car rates so you can rebook if prices fall. Here's how to turn that into a plan you can follow.

## Planning details first

- **Time needed:** The booking work takes an evening or two. The trips themselves can be a long weekend or a full week.
- **Who it suits:** Budget-conscious travelers, deal hunters, and anyone eyeing a winter escape after the holiday rush.
- **Do you need a car?** That depends on the destination, but if you do, book a rental you can cancel (more on that below).

## Why January is a deal window

Holiday travel demand cools off once the new year starts, which is what opens up bargains. Yahoo notes that some things still sell out or spike in price anyway: warm-weather escapes, holiday weekends, and popular cruises. So the goal is not to wait on everything. It's to know what to grab now and what to watch.

## Book domestic flights 1 to 3 months out

For domestic January trips, the guidance is to book flights one to three months ahead. If you're reading this in early November, you're already inside that window for much of the month. Don't treat it as a reason to wait for a perfect fare. Pick your dates, check prices, and buy once you see a fare you'd be happy with.

A simple routine:

1. Decide on your travel dates and your flexibility (can you shift by a day?).
2. Check fares now to get a baseline.
3. Buy within the one-to-three-month window rather than waiting until the last minute.

## Lock in MLK weekend right away

MLK weekend is a holiday weekend, which makes it the exception to "wait for deals." Yahoo's advice is to book it as soon as possible. A three-day weekend draws demand, and prices and availability can get worse the longer you hold off. If you're planning a short getaway, this is the first thing on your list to book.

If your dates are flexible, a trip that avoids the holiday weekend may be easier to price. If they're not, commit early.

## Choose refundable hotels and rental cars

This is the most useful part of the strategy, because it lets you book early without betting against yourself. Yahoo recommends refundable rates on hotels and rental cars so you can rebook if prices drop.

Here's how it works in practice:

- Book a refundable room or car now to secure availability.
- Check the rate again every so often.
- If the price falls, cancel and rebook at the lower rate.

Before you click, confirm the cancellation deadline in your confirmation email. Note it on your calendar so you don't lose the refund by missing it. Refundable rates sometimes cost a bit more upfront, so compare that against the potential savings.

## Where January deals tend to hide

Yahoo points to several categories worth watching:

- **Theme parks:** Post-holiday demand dips can make this a good time to visit.
- **Ski resorts:** Winter sports destinations are among the January deal zones.
- **Hawaii:** A warm-weather option on the list, though warm-weather escapes can still sell out, so don't wait too long.
- **Cruises:** January is when to reserve.

## Cruises: reserve in January

Yahoo highlights wave season, the stretch of cruise-booking deals that makes January a good time to reserve a sailing. Popular cruises can sell out or climb in price, so this is a case where booking during the deal window beats waiting. Before you commit, check what's included and what the cancellation terms are.

## A simple what-to-book-when checklist

**Book now**
- MLK weekend travel
- Domestic flights, if you're inside the one-to-three-month window
- Popular cruises and warm-weather trips you can't flex on

**Book refundable, then monitor**
- Hotels
- Rental cars

**Watch for deals**
- Theme parks
- Ski resorts
- Hawaii

## The one move to make today

Pick your travel dates, then book your hotel and rental car on refundable rates. That locks in availability, keeps your options open, and lets you rebook if prices drop.
