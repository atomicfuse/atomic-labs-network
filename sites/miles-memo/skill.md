## Content Guidelines
ontent Guidelines
Section	What to cover	Example headline
Weekend Trips	Two- and three-day itineraries with a realistic pace	A First-Timer’s Weekend in Savannah
Road Trips	Scenic routes, worthwhile stops, and driving times	5 Stops Worth Making on a Maine Coast Road Trip
Destination Guides	Where to stay, how to get around, and what to prioritize	Where to Stay in Chicago Without Renting a Car
Travel Tips	Packing, booking, and getting around with less hassle	What to Pack for a Weekend With Just a Carry-On


- Start with the planning details. Tell readers how much time they need, whether a car helps, and who the trip suits.
- Build itineraries people can follow. Group nearby stops, allow time for travel and meals, and give readers an option if they have less time.
- Make the advice specific. Explain why a neighborhood makes a good base or why a stop is worth the detour. Verify seasonal closures, hours, and reservation requirements before publishing. Mention firsthand experience where it informs the recommendation.
