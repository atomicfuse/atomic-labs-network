As fall break approaches, Nashville International Airport (BNA) is gearing up for a busy travel season. With ongoing construction and expected congestion, it’s crucial to plan ahead to avoid delays or missed flights. Here’s your essential fall break travel checklist to help you sail smoothly through BNA this season.

## 1. Arrive Early
Given the anticipated surge in travelers and potential construction delays, plan to arrive at least **two hours prior** to your flight, especially if you're traveling with family. This extra buffer will help you navigate any unexpected situations, such as traffic or terminal congestion.

## 2. Parking Options
Consider your parking strategy before you head to the airport:
- **Economy Parking:** If you’re looking for budget-friendly options, choose the economy lot. It’s typically less crowded, but expect a shuttle ride to the terminal.
- **Daily Parking:** For short trips, the daily parking garage is closer to the terminal and convenient.
- **Airport Shuttle Services:** If you’re concerned about parking availability, think about using a local shuttle service to drop you off at the airport.

## 3. Plan Your Route
Ongoing construction around BNA can affect access roads. Check your route in advance:
- **Alternate Routes:** Familiarize yourself with alternate access points to BNA to avoid construction-related delays. Use navigation apps for live traffic updates, which can help you steer clear of congested areas.

## 4. TSA Preparation
Familiarize yourself with TSA screening requirements to ensure a smooth process:
- **ID Ready:** Have your identification handy and make sure it’s compliant with TSA standards. Children under 18 don’t need ID, but it’s good to confirm your travel plans.
- **Packing Efeciently:** Organize your carry-on luggage by separating out electronics, liquids, and other items that may require individual screening at the TSA checkpoint. Follow the 3-1-1 rule for liquids (3.4 ounces or less in a 1-quart bag).

## 5. Stay Updated
Keep an eye on your flight status and gate information:
- **Airline Apps:** Download your airline's app for real-time updates and notifications. This will help you stay informed about any delays or changes to your flight schedule.

## 6. Family Travel Tips
If traveling with kids, follow these tips for a smoother experience:
- **Involve Them in Planning:** Discuss the travel day with your children, so they know what to expect. Involve them in packing to make it more engaging.
- **Entertainment on the Go:** Pack snacks and activities to keep kids occupied while waiting at the terminal. This can alleviate stress and make the travel journey more enjoyable for everyone.

## Final Thoughts
Fall break travel at Nashville International Airport can be a breeze with proper planning. By following this checklist, you’ll be better prepared to handle the busy atmosphere and enjoy a stress-free journey. Make sure to factor in construction delays, prepare your parking and TSA strategies, and get an early start on travel day. Here’s to a fantastic fall break adventure!
