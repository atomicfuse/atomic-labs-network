As travel preferences shift and the conversation about overtourism gains momentum, 2027 is shaping up to be the year for lesser-known destinations. Intrepid Travel has released its annual Not Hot List, showcasing ten under-the-radar places that are poised to welcome more visitors in a responsible way. This list is not just about avoiding crowds; it’s about discovering places that are ready to offer unique experiences while benefiting local communities. Here’s a look at these hidden gems, perfect for those looking to travel thoughtfully.

## 1. Samburu National Reserve, Kenya
Nestled in the heart of Kenya, Samburu National Reserve is often overshadowed by more famous parks. However, it showcases a unique collection of wildlife, including endangered species such as the Grevy’s zebra and Somali ostrich. With limited crowds, you can enjoy peaceful game drives while supporting the local economy through sustainable tourism practices.

## 2. Mestia, Georgia
This mountainous village in the Svaneti region offers stunning views and rich cultural heritage. With its medieval architecture and unique traditions, Mestia is a fantastic alternative for those looking to experience the Caucasus in relative serenity. Discover the local lifestyle while enjoying activities like hiking and visiting ancient watchtowers.

## 3. Northern Highlands, Jordan
Embrace the beauty of the Northern Highlands, where landscapes of vast plateaus meet ancient history. Less frequented than Petra, this region offers archaeological sites and natural wonders that invite you to explore Jordan's lesser-known treasures. Enjoy hospitable locals and a chance to think outside the typical tourist experience.

## 4. Whitehorse, Yukon, Canada
Often referred to as the Gateway to the Arctic, Whitehorse is an ideal spot for adventurers. With incredible access to wilderness activities like hiking, canoeing, and dog sledding, this charming city provides ample opportunities to connect with nature. Plus, visit during winter for breathtaking Northern Lights that are often less crowded than other viewing spots.

## 5. Alonnisos, Greece
While many flock to popular islands like Santorini and Mykonos, Alonnisos remains a tranquil oasis. Part of the National Marine Park of Alonnisos, this island captures the essence of the Aegean with its vibrant marine life and picturesque landscapes. Enjoy secluded beaches and flavorful local cuisine while still supporting sustainable practices.

## 6. Dili, Timor-Leste
Dili, the capital of Timor-Leste, offers a mix of cultural experiences and stunning coastal scenery. Its vibrant markets and rich history create an authentic atmosphere for those seeking adventure and authentic local interaction. Travelers can enjoy diving and snorkeling in pristine waters while contributing to the local economy.

## 7. Pekoe Trail, Sri Lanka
For those who appreciate a good hike, the Pekoe Trail transports you through tea plantations and charming hill country. This less-traveled trail is ideal for capturing breathtaking scenery while engaging with local communities. Enjoy both nature and culture, all while reducing your carbon footprint.

## 8. Liepāja, Latvia
Known for its unique history and vibrant culture, Liepāja offers stunning architecture alongside some of the best beaches in Latvia. Experience the town's rich musical heritage, and don’t miss the lively local markets. With fewer tourists, Liepāja allows for a more intimate experience of Latvian life.

## 9. Carretera Austral, Chile
Carretera Austral is a scenic highway in Chile that spans over 1,200 kilometers of breathtaking landscapes. Ideal for road trips and outdoor adventures, this less-visited route showcases mountains, forests, and rivers, all while giving you a chance to connect with nature away from tourist crowds.

## 10. Dalsland, Sweden
Dalsland, often referred to as the “Lake District” of Sweden, is where you can hike, kayak, and escape into pristine nature. Enjoy exploring its many lakes and trails, all while engaging with eco-friendly lodges that promote sustainable tourism practices.

Traveling to these destinations isn’t just about exploring new places; it’s about embracing responsible tourism that benefits local communities. By choosing to visit sites on the Not Hot List, you’re helping to alleviate the pressure on overcrowded hotspots and ensuring that tourism has a positive impact.

As you plan your next adventure, consider these ten gems where you can experience the world in a more meaningful way before they become too popular. Happy travels!
