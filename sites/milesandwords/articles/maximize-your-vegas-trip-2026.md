Las Vegas is always an exciting destination, but 2026 brings new updates that every traveler should know. From unexpected fees to essential transport options, these 12 travel tips will help you navigate the city smarter and save money on your trip. 

## 1. Don't Plan Your Entire Vacation Around Walking the Strip
While the Las Vegas Strip is iconic, it can be deceiving. The distance between attractions is greater than it appears, and walking can be exhausting. Instead, consider planning activities that include a mix of walking and transportation to make the most of your time. 

## 2. Resort Fees Didn't Disappear – They're Just More Visible
Many travelers assume they can book a room for a certain price only to find additional resort fees added at checkout. In 2026, however, these fees are more clearly displayed during booking. Always check the total cost before confirming your stay. 

## 3. Don't Assume Your Hotel Includes Free Parking
Historically, Las Vegas hotels often included free parking, but that's been changing. By 2026, most hotels charge for parking, so factor this into your budget before you arrive. Look for properties that offer free parking to save money. 

## 4. Airport Rideshare Pickup Isn't Right Outside Baggage Claim
When arriving at Harry Reid International Airport, remember that rideshare pickups have moved to designated areas away from baggage claim. Looking for rideshare apps? Make sure you follow the signs to avoid confusion. 

## 5. The Monorail and Deuce Can Save a Lot of Rideshare Trips
Using the Monorail or the Deuce bus can be a cost-effective and convenient way to get around Las Vegas. The Monorail connects several key hotels, while the Deuce runs along the Strip and into downtown, helping you avoid multiple rideshare fees. 

## 6. Summer Heat Can Completely Rearrange Your Day
Las Vegas summers are notorious for their intense heat. Plan your outdoor activities for early morning or late evening to avoid the peak temperatures during the day. Stay hydrated and consider scheduling indoor activities during the hottest hours. 

## 7. Check the Event Calendar Before You Book Anything
Major events, like the Formula 1 Grand Prix, can greatly influence hotel pricing and availability. Always check the event calendar for any large gatherings that could affect your travel dates, leading to higher costs or difficulty finding accommodations. 

## 8. Don't Count on Buying Sphere Tickets at the Last Minute
With the popularity of venues like the Sphere, last-minute ticket availability can be hit or miss. If you're set on attending a show, book ahead of your trip to ensure you get a seat and the best prices. 

## 9. Give Downtown Las Vegas Its Own Afternoon or Evening
Downtown Las Vegas offers a different experience from the Strip, with unique attractions and a vibrant atmosphere. Dedicate at least one half-day to explore this area, tasting the local food and enjoying entertainment outside the casino circuit. 

## 10. Legal Cannabis Doesn't Mean You Can Use It on the Strip
While cannabis is legal in Nevada, smoking it on the Strip is not permitted. Make sure to enjoy responsibly and only in designated areas to avoid any fines or issues during your trip. 

## 11. Casino Floors Can Still Be Smoky
Despite some hotels having designated non-smoking areas, casino floors might still have a smoky atmosphere. If this is a concern for you, consider staying at resorts known for their non-smoking environments. 

## 12. You Don't Have to Pay for Every Hour of Your Vacation
In Las Vegas, there are countless free attractions worth exploring. Don't feel pressured to spend money at every venue. Check out the spectacular Bellagio Fountains, take photos at the Welcome sign, or stroll down Fremont Street for a vibrant experience at no cost. 

By keeping these 12 tips in mind, you can dodge hidden costs and enjoy a fulfilling Las Vegas experience in 2026. Plan wisely, and make sure your adventure is filled with unforgettable memories without breaking the bank.
