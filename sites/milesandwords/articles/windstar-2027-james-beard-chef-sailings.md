## How to evaluate a culinary sailing before booking

Since the announcement is light on specifics, here's a practical checklist to bring to Windstar or your travel advisor:

- **Which chef is on which sailing, and for how many days?** A chef aboard the whole voyage is a different experience than a single guest appearance.
- **What's included?** Ask whether special dinners, demonstrations or port excursions cost extra.
- **How does the itinerary support the theme?** The best food cruises connect ports to the menu.
- **What are the cancellation terms?** Booking a year or more ahead means flexibility matters.
- **How many guests will be aboard?** Small-ship access to a chef depends on a manageable group.
