Ever wonder how the dazzling showgirls of Las Vegas maintain their incredible energy and poise while performing night after night? ### The Importance of Pilates
At the heart of Kelsie’s fitness routine is Pilates, a discipline focusing on core strength, flexibility, and overall body awareness. Practicing Pilates regularly helps her improve her posture, prevent injuries, and maintain the strength necessary for six high-energy performances each week. If you're new to Pilates or haven't incorporated it into your routine yet, consider starting with these beginner-friendly moves:

#### 1. The Hundred  
Start lying on your back with your legs in a tabletop position. Raise your head and shoulders off the mat, extending your arms by your sides. Inhale for five counts while pumping your arms up and down, then exhale for five counts. Repeat this for 10 sets (100 pumps total). This classic exercise engages your core and elevates your heart rate, making it a fantastic opener for any workout.

#### 2. Roll-Up  
This move strengthens your abdominal muscles while enhancing flexibility in your spine. Lie flat on your back, legs extended, and arms reaching overhead. Inhale as you lift your arms toward the ceiling, then exhale to roll up one vertebra at a time, reaching toward your toes. Inhale to roll back down. Aim for 6-8 reps, controlling the movement to maximize effectiveness.

#### 3. Side Leg Lifts  
Lie on one side with your bottom leg bent for support. Extend your top leg straight out, flexing your foot. Lift your top leg to about a 45-degree angle, then lower back down. Perform this for 8-10 repetitions on each side. This exercise targets the hips and obliques, helping build a strong foundation for balanced movement.

### Daily Movement and Step Goals
Beyond Pilates, Kelsie emphasizes the importance of daily movement. With a personal goal of 10,000 steps a day, she finds ways to incorporate walking into her routine, whether it’s pacing around her home or taking strolls in her neighborhood. Here are some tips to boost your daily step count:
- **Take the Stairs**: Whenever possible, choose stairs over elevators or escalators.
- **Walk During Breaks**: Use work breaks to get up and walk around, even if it’s just for a few minutes.
- **Incorporate Walking Meetings**: If discussing something with a colleague, consider walking together instead of sitting in an office. 

### Hydration and Recovery
Staying hydrated is crucial, especially for those engaging in frequent physical activity. Kelsie focuses on maintaining optimum hydration levels to support her energy and muscle recovery. Here’s how you can make sure you’re drinking enough water:
- **Carry a Reusable Water Bottle**: Keep a water bottle handy to encourage regular sips, aiming for at least half your body weight in ounces each day.
- **Infuse Your Water**: If plain water doesn’t excite you, try adding slices of fruit or herbs to make it more appealing.
- **Set Reminders**: Use apps or alarms to remind you to drink water throughout the day.

### Building Confidence Through Fitness
Kelsie highlights that her fitness routine does more than keep her looking and feeling great for performances; it also serves as a means to build confidence in herself. Embracing a regular fitness routine can help you feel more empowered, both on and off the stage. To nurture confidence:

- **Join a Class or Buddy Up**: Engage in Pilates or any fitness class that interests you, and consider doing it with a friend for accountability and fun.
