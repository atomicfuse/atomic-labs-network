## What to Expect from a Pilates Class

For the uninitiated, the allure of Pilates comes from its dual focus on strength and flexibility, delivered in a low-impact format. This makes it suitable for a wide range of individuals, from athletes recovering from injuries to those simply wanting to enhance their core strength and posture. Many studios now offer reformer Pilates in addition to mat classes, providing options that cater to varying preferences and experiences. 

- **Mat Pilates** typically involves the use of a mat to perform a sequence of exercises, focusing on balance and breath control while building core strength.
- **Reformer Pilates** employs a special piece of equipment known as a reformer, adding resistance to workouts through springs, making movements more dynamic and adaptable.

These variations not only keep sessions interesting but also allow for personalized adjustments, ensuring that participants can progress according to their own fitness levels.
