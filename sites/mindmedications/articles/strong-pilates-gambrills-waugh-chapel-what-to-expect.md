## How to prepare for your first class

No schedule is published yet, but you can get ready now with a few simple steps.

### 1. Pick a goal
Decide what you want: strength, stamina, better posture, or a consistent habit. Having a goal helps you judge whether a studio is worth it after a few weeks.

### 2. Ask the right questions
When the studio opens, ask whether there are intro classes, how beginners are guided, and what modifications exist for injuries or limited mobility.

### 3. Start with a modest frequency
Two sessions a week is an easy, sustainable starting point for most people. You can build from there once your body adjusts.

### 4. Treat it as a community, not just a workout
