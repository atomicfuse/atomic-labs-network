## Content Guidelines
Content Guidelines
Section	What to cover	Example headline
Car Buying	New and used car shopping, trim levels, and features worth paying for	What Should You Check on a Used-Car Test Drive?
Car Comparisons	Practical trade-offs by budget, body style, and driving habits	Small SUV or Hatchback? Which Gives You More Usable Space?
Car Tech Explained	Hybrids, EV charging, dashboard symbols, and driver-assist features	What’s the Difference Between a Hybrid and a Plug-In Hybrid?
Car Ownership	Routine maintenance, running costs, and seasonal upkeep	What Does a Tire Pressure Warning Light Mean?


- Lead with the driver’s question. Give the short answer first, then explain what the difference means in daily use. A cargo-space comparison can show whether a folded stroller fits with the rear seats up.
- Be specific about the vehicle. Include model year, trim, and market when prices, features, or specifications vary. Verify these details against manufacturer specifications or owner’s manuals.
- Make the basis of each verdict clear. For hands-on reviews, say what you drove and under what conditions. For researched comparisons, show the specs and criteria behind the conclusion.
