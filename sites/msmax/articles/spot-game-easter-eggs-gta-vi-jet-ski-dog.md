A jet-skiing dog in "Grand Theft Auto VI" has fans pointing to a real pup from South Florida. You can use the same method to track down the real-world inspiration behind hidden details in any game. Here is what happened, then a simple routine for doing it yourself.

## What fans spotted

Per NBC Miami, eagle-eyed "Grand Theft Auto VI" fans noticed a familiar South Florida pet celebrity in the Miami-inspired game. An account dedicated to "GTA VI" updates posted on X: "GTA 6's jet-skiing dog Easter egg is a reference to Loosy, a real-life dog from Fort Lauderdale, Florida, who regularly rides jet skis Around her local area!"

Loosy is a viral Fort Lauderdale dog known for riding a personal watercraft while her owner rides a jet-propelled hoverboard, according to the report. The resemblance revived attention around her local fame.

One caveat: this is a fan connection. Nothing in the reporting says the game's developers confirmed Loosy as the inspiration, which is why NBC Miami's headline says the dog "may have been" inspired by her. Treat it as a strong guess, not a fact.

## Step 1: Capture the detail cleanly

Before you can compare anything, you need a clear look at it.

1. Pause the trailer or video at the exact frame. On YouTube, press the **period (.)** key while paused to step forward one frame, and the **comma (,)** key to step back.
2. Set playback quality to the highest option in the gear icon menu.
3. Take a screenshot. On Windows, press **Windows key + Shift + S**. On a Mac, press **Command + Shift + 4**. On an iPhone, press the side button and volume up together. On most Android phones, press power and volume down.
4. Note the timestamp so others can find the same moment.

## Step 2: Describe what's odd about it

Write down the specific, unusual features in plain words. Here that would be: a dog, riding a jet ski, in a Miami-style setting. A generic detail is hard to trace. An oddly specific one, like a dog on a watercraft, is much easier to match to something real.

The more unusual the combination, the better your odds. Fans clearly found this one because it is not something you see every day.

## Step 3: Search the real world for a match

Games set in real places often borrow from them, and "GTA VI" is built around a Miami-inspired setting. So search with the place name added.

- Try phrases like "dog riding jet ski Florida" in a regular search engine.
- Switch to video results or the search bar inside TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube, where local viral clips tend to live.
- Filter by the most popular results, then check whether the person or pet shows up in more than one clip.

That approach is how a regional internet star like Loosy can surface: she is known for repeatedly riding around her local area, so there is plenty of footage to find.

## Step 4: Compare side by side

Open the real footage and your screenshot next to each other. Look for matching traits: the pose, the type of vehicle, the setting. Keep your standards honest. A resemblance is not proof, and several real animals could look alike.

If you find a match, share both images together with the timestamp so others can check your work.

## Step 5: Check for an official word

Ask yourself whether the developer has said anything. If not, label your finding as a theory. Use wording like "fans think" or "looks like a nod to." That keeps you accurate if the answer changes later, and it is the standard NBC Miami's reporting follows here.

## Why local viral moments end up in games

It is my take, not a reported fact, but it makes sense: when a game is modeled on a real city, the surrounding culture comes with it. A dog on a personal watercraft fits a Florida waterfront. It also shows how a regional internet celebrity can cross over into mainstream entertainment, as NBC Miami's coverage frames it.

## Keep your detective tools handy

The same routine works for movies, shows, and other games: pause, capture, describe, search the real place, compare, then verify. Next time a trailer drops, pick one odd detail and trace it before you read anyone else's theory.
