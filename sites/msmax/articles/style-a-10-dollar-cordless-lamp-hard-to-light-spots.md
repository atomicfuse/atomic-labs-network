Some spots in your home never get good light because there's no outlet nearby. A cordless lamp fixes that, and a cheap one lets you try the idea without much risk. Per Homes & Gardens, ALDI is selling the Casalux Touch Battery Table Lamp for $9.99 as part of ALDI Finds. Below is how to put a lamp like it to work, step by step.

## What the ALDI lamp is

Here is what the report says about it. The Casalux Touch Battery Table Lamp is a cordless lamp with a metallic finish, sold in gold and silver. It runs on batteries, so no outlet is required, and it's touch-operated. It costs $9.99.

The report doesn't cover details like battery type, brightness, run time, or how long the lamp will be in stores, so check the box or ALDI's listing before you buy. ALDI Finds are typically limited-time items, so availability may vary by store.

## Step 1: Pick the spot that's too dark

The report points to overlooked areas where a cordless lamp makes sense. Walk through your home in the evening and look for these:

- **Shelves.** A small lamp gives a bookcase a warm glow.
- **Nightstands.** Handy if your outlet is behind the bed or already full.
- **Hallways.** A narrow console or ledge can hold a lamp with no cord running across the floor.
- **Entry tables.** A touch lamp is easy to switch on when you walk in.

Start with just one. Live with it for a few evenings before buying more.

## Step 2: Choose gold or silver

Both finishes are metallic, so the choice comes down to what's already in the room. This is my take, not ALDI's:

- **Gold** tends to suit warm tones: wood, cream, brass hardware, and rust or amber fall colors.
- **Silver** tends to suit cooler or neutral rooms: gray, white, black, or chrome fixtures.

Look at the hardware, frames, and other metal pieces near the spot. Match those, and the lamp will look intentional rather than random.

## Step 3: Place it like a styled object

A lamp looks better when it's part of a small arrangement. Try this:

1. Put the lamp on the surface first.
2. Add something taller or softer beside it, such as a stack of books or a framed print.
3. Add one small item with texture, like a candle holder, a ceramic dish, or a small plant.
4. Turn the lamp on and step back. If the surface looks crowded, remove one item.

The point is to give the glow somewhere to land, so it reads as ambient lighting and not just a bulb on a table.

## Step 4: Use it for ambiance, not for tasks

The pitch for this kind of lamp is warmth and mood. That's a fair use for it. For reading or working, keep your main lamp or overhead light. Use the cordless one in the evening to soften a room, especially as the days get shorter and cozy seasonal styling takes over.

A simple habit: touch it on when the sun goes down, and the room feels set for the evening without you rearranging anything.

## Why cordless suits renters

Per the report, cordless lamps are popular with renters and budget-conscious decorators because they need no installation. There's no drilling, no hardwiring, and no extension cord to hide. If you move, the lamp comes with you. That makes a $9.99 lamp a low-commitment way to test a lighting change before spending more on something pricier.

## Before you buy

- Check that your local ALDI has the item, since Finds rotate.
- Look at the batteries required and keep spares on hand.
- Pick the finish that matches the metal already in the room.
- Buy one first, place it, and see if you want a second.

The cheapest way to find out if a cordless lamp suits your home is to put one in your darkest corner this week and see how the room feels after dark.
