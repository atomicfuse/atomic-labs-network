## The Ethical Knot

The most interesting part of the film's premise is that it doesn't have a clean villain-and-hero setup. The BBC says the story explores the ethical tension between justice, journalism and exploitation. Those three things overlap in ways that make everyone uneasy:

- **Justice** wants consequences.
- **Journalism** wants the truth, ideally with rules about how it's gathered.
- **Entertainment** wants a gripping hour of television, and has its own incentives.

When one program tries to be all three, the seams show. A thriller is a natural place to put that on screen, because the audience gets to watch the machinery work and feel a little complicit in wanting to keep watching.

That's where Pattinson's 'terrifying' comment becomes useful context. We don't have the full interview in front of us, so it would be a stretch to say exactly what he was describing. But the line suggests he sees the subject as something bigger than a juicy role.
