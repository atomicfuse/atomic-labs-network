## The Main Event: *Coyote vs. Acme*

If you followed the saga at all — and if you're a movie fan with a Twitter account, you definitely did — then watching *Coyote vs. Acme* this weekend carries a little extra charge. This is the part of Vulture's list that carries the most emotional freight — and the one that reframes the whole weekend slightly.

The description Vulture chose — lifted from a Dolly lyric — says it plainly: *"Everything's gonna work out just fine."* That's not a flippant choice.
