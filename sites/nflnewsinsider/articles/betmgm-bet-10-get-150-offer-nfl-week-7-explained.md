## Picking a first bet: a sensible approach

This part is our analysis, not Fox News' or BetMGM's. Because the first wager must win, a few habits make sense:

- **Stay with games you follow.** Week 7 features teams such as the Chiefs, Packers, Lions, Rams, Raiders and Seahawks in featured windows, per Fox News. If you have watched one of those teams all season, you have a better read than on a team you've seen twice.
- **Don't chase a long shot because of the bonus.** A risky pick that pays big matters little if the goal is simply a winning first bet. Whether a safer line is worth it is your call.
- **Keep the stake at the minimum.** The requirement is $10 or more. Wagering more than the minimum raises your risk without changing the stated bonus.
