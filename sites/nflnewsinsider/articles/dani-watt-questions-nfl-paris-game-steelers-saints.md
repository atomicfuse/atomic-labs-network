## Why Leagues Rarely Cancel Showcase Games

This section is our analysis, not reported fact. Marquee international events involve months of planning, sponsor commitments, travel arrangements, and brand promotion. Leagues tend to lean on security assessments from authorities and contingency planning rather than pulling out early.
