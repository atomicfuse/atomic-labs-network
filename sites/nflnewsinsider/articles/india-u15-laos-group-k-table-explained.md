## What India need to climb the table

The path is straightforward even if the margins are not. India's next opponents are Cameroon and Grenada, per Khelnow. Here is how I'd read those two games (this is my analysis, not reporting):

1. **Cameroon is the direct test.** Cameroon sit above India in the table, so this match offers a chance to take points off a team they are chasing.

3. **Defensive tightness is the easy fix to name.** India scored four against Laos but allowed two. Cutting that down is the simplest way to improve their goal difference.

None of that is a prediction. Youth tournaments can swing fast, and one match is a small sample.
