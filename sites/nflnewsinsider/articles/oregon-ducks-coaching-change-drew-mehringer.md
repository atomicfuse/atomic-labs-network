1. **Outsized expectations.** The bar is set by the previous results, not by what's reasonable for a new voice.
2. **An existing system.** Keep it and you risk looking like an imitation. Change it and you risk breaking what worked.
3. **Little patience.** At a program aiming for championships, a slide gets noticed fast.

## Why a mid-season-style firing signals pressure

Program expectations explain the speed. Oregon's goals remain national-title level, so a fall from contention is treated as a crisis rather than a bump. Firing a coordinator is often the quickest lever a head coach or athletic department can pull when results slip. It's visible, it signals urgency, and it's less disruptive than changing a head coach.

It also raises the stakes for everyone left on the staff. When one high-profile job turns over this quickly, it suggests the bar isn't flexible.
