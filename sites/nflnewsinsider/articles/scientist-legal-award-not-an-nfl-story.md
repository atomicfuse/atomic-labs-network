## What the column actually says

## How to read an opinion column about a legal result

Football fans already know this skill. A postgame hot take and a box score are different things, and the same goes here.

### Separate the result from the interpretation

A jury verdict is a result. Why it happened and what it proves are interpretations. Columnists often move from one to the other in a single sentence, so slow down at that seam.

### Check who is making the call for action

The column urges a DOJ investigation. Calls for investigations are advocacy. Whether one happens is a separate, checkable fact.

### Look for the other side

An opinion piece rarely gives the institutions' full response. Look for statements from the people and organizations named before you form a view.
