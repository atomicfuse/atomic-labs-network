## What Meyer Said

According to Fox News, Meyer believes Manning has regressed after the promising showing against Ohio State. Meyer also argued that Texas may need to consider backup KJ Lacey Jr. at quarterback.

That's the extent of what we can attribute to him here. The specifics of his breakdown, including which throws or decisions he finds lacking, aren't covered in the reporting we have, so treat this as one prominent voice's opinion rather than a verdict.

That cuts two ways. It can make criticism feel harsher than the on-field evidence warrants. It can also make a real problem harder to ignore. Meyer's comments land in that gap, and you should weigh them as analysis from a former coach, not a measurement.
