However, the recent findings indicate that certain environmental factors can trigger epigenetic changes, particularly through DNA methylation, allowing some of these floaters to become formidable territorial males.

## Understanding Epigenetics

So, what is epigenetics? At its core, epigenetics involves changes in gene expression—essentially how genes are turned on or off—without altering the underlying DNA sequence itself. This can be influenced by various factors, including environmental conditions, which means that genetic traits can emerge or diminish based on interactions with surroundings.

For the cheetahs, this adaptation highlights a hidden layer of resilience. Instead of relying solely on genetic mutations over generations, these animals can exhibit significant changes within their lifetimes in response to environmental pressures. This adaptability is crucial as cheetahs face increasing threats from habitat changes and competition.
