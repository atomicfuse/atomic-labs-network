### The Recipe: Rosa's Cicatelli con Ciambutella

Rosa’s cicatelli con ciambutella is a representation of Italian culinary history that’s as simple as it is delicious. Here’s how you can recreate this tradition at home:

#### Ingredients
- Homemade or store-bought cicatelli pasta
- Ciambutella (a flavorful Italian sausage)
- Olive oil
- Garlic (to taste)
- Fresh parsley (for garnish)
- Salt and pepper (to taste)

#### Instructions
1. If making homemade cicatelli, prepare pasta dough and shape into small ovals. Allow to dry slightly.
2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic until golden.
3. Add sliced ciambutella to the skillet, cooking until browned.
4. Toss in the cooked cicatelli and mix thoroughly to incorporate flavors. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve warm, garnished with fresh parsley. Enjoy the flavors and stories each bite brings!

This recipe is a testament to the ethos of *Pasta Grannies*: *eat everything*, focusing on not just the food, but the experience it offers.

## Embracing a No-Waste Mindset

In following the Pasta Grannies' teachings, we also learn the importance of a no-waste philosophy. These women cook with what they have, using leftovers creatively and ensuring that food is appreciated and not discarded. The emphasis is on making the most out of ingredients without elaborate planning or packaging. They encourage you to experiment and adapt recipes based on what you already have on hand, leading not only to less waste but also to more flavorful dishes.

### Practical Tips to ‘Eat Everything’
- **Repurpose Ingredients:** Use the stems of herbs in sauces or soups, and save vegetable peels for homemade stocks. 
- **Plan for Leftovers:** When cooking, make a little extra to have for lunch or different meals throughout the week. 
- **Invite Community:** Share excess food with friends or neighbors, embracing a communal spirit around meals.
