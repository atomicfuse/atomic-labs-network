Apple cider is already a fall favorite, but a few small upgrades turn a jug from the store or orchard into something that feels like a café drink. You can also set it beside a block of cheese and call it hosting. Here is how to build that spread at home, based on a demonstration by Bill Michaels of Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard.

Michaels appeared on *Fox & Friends Weekend*, per Fox News, to share autumn cider ideas. The centerpiece was hot mulled cider in a caramel-rimmed glass, topped with whipped cream and mulling spices, served alongside extra-sharp cheddar cheese.

## The Dessert-Style Mulled Cider

The version Michaels showed has four visible parts: warm cider, mulling spices, a caramel-rimmed glass, and whipped cream. Fox News did not publish exact measurements in the segment summary, so treat the steps below as a framework and adjust to taste.

### How to build it

1. **Warm the cider with mulling spices.** Heat it gently on the stove. Keep it hot, not boiling hard, so the flavors stay bright. Many cooks use a pre-made mulling spice blend, or you can assemble your own from whole spices you like.
2. **Rim the glass with caramel.** Dip or brush the edge of a heatproof mug or glass in caramel sauce. Do this before pouring so the caramel can set slightly.
3. **Pour the hot cider.** Strain out the spices if you want a clean sip.
4. **Top with whipped cream.** This gives the drink its café look and a soft, dessert-like finish.

The result sits somewhere between a hot cider and a seasonal specialty drink, with no espresso machine required.

## Why Cheddar Belongs on the Table

The other half of Michaels' demonstration was pairing the cider with extra-sharp cheddar, which Fox News described as a classic fall flavor combination. My take on why it works: cider is sweet and fruity, and sharp cheddar is salty and tangy, so each makes the other taste more interesting. The richness of the cheese also balances the sweetness of a caramel and whipped cream topping.

### Serving tips

- Cut the cheddar into small blocks or thin slices so guests can grab a bite between sips.
- Set the cheese out a bit ahead of time so it is not fridge-cold. That is general serving practice, not something from the segment.
- Keep it simple: one good cheese is enough to make the point.

## Turn It Into a Fall Drink Board

If you are hosting a weekend gathering or a holiday get-together, this pairing scales easily:

- **A pot of mulled cider** kept warm on the stove
- **Caramel sauce** and **whipped cream** so guests can finish their own mugs
- **A cheddar plate** at the center of the table

That is a self-serve setup with very little last-minute work. Guests who want a plain warm cider can skip the toppings, and those who want the full café effect can add them.

## Make It Even More Seasonal

Michaels' orchard background is a reminder that cider tastes best when it is fresh and local. If an orchard or cider mill is within driving distance, a visit is a good way to pick up your jug and make the outing part of the tradition. Fresh cider will carry the spices well, so you need less fuss elsewhere.

## Quick Checklist Before Guests Arrive

- Cider heating gently with spices
- Mugs rimmed with caramel
- Whipped cream chilled and ready
- Extra-sharp cheddar sliced and on a board

Rim one glass and pour one hot mug this weekend. If the cheddar-and-cider bite wins you over, you have your fall hosting routine.
