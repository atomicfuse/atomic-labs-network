## Match the kit to your crowd

A kit built for a small table won't stretch to a big one without help. Two quick rules:

- **Small gathering (2 to 4):** A kit is likely all you need. Add one homemade side and call it done.
- **Larger gathering:** Buy the kit as your base, then pad it with cheap, filling pantry dishes. Rice, beans, potatoes, and stuffing-style bakes cost little per serving.

## The pantry add-on that makes a kit feel homemade: crispy breadcrumb topping

A store-bought casserole or mac and cheese looks flat straight from the oven. A crunchy topping fixes that in about ten minutes.

**Makes:** enough for one 9x13 dish, about 8 servings as a topping
**Prep:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** 5 to 8 minutes

**You'll need:**
- 1 cup dry breadcrumbs (or crushed crackers, or torn stale bread pulsed in a blender)
- 2 tablespoons butter or olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Pinch of salt and black pepper
- Optional: 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan

**Steps:**
1. Warm the butter in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the breadcrumbs and seasonings. Stir constantly.
3. Cook until deep golden and fragrant, 5 to 8 minutes.
4. Sprinkle over the hot dish right before serving.

**The step that makes the difference:** toast the crumbs in the pan first, rather than sprinkling them on raw and hoping the oven does it. Crumbs on a wet casserole steam instead of crisping. Pre-toasted crumbs go on at the end, so they stay crunchy.

**Doneness cue:** the crumbs should smell nutty and look the color of toast. Pull them a shade early, since they keep darkening in the hot pan.

**Substitutions:** panko works well, and crushed buttery crackers or cornflakes also do the job. Skip the Parmesan if you like.

## Crispy chickpeas as a snack or salad topper

While guests arrive, put out something crunchy that costs pennies per serving.

**Makes:** about 4 snack servings
**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 25 to 30 minutes at 425°F

1. Drain and rinse one 15-ounce can of chickpeas.
2. **Dry them thoroughly.** Roll them in a clean towel and let them air-dry a few minutes. Damp chickpeas steam rather than crisp.
3. Toss with 1 tablespoon oil, 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
4. Spread on a sheet pan in one layer and roast, shaking the pan halfway.

They're done when they rattle on the pan and feel crisp when you pinch one. They firm up more as they cool.

## Smart shopping habits for kit week

- **Check the list before you buy extras.** Most kits will leave gaps, and you may already own the fix: canned beans, rice, butter, spices, bread for crumbs.
- **Think cost per person, not sticker price.** A cheaper kit that feeds fewer people can cost more per plate.
- **Add one homemade dish.** It makes the whole table feel cooked, even if the turkey came in a box.
- **Plan leftovers.** Rice, beans, and extra crumbs turn leftover turkey into tomorrow's dinner.
