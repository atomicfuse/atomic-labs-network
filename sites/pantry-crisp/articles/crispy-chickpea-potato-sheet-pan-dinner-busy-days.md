Some days are too full to cook a dinner that needs your attention. Early voting, a research session before a big election, a late shift: you want something that is mostly hands-off and still tastes like you tried.

That's the idea behind this crispy chickpea and potato sheet-pan dinner. It uses canned chickpeas, potatoes, rice, and spices you likely already own. It takes 10 minutes of prep, about 35 minutes in the oven, and it holds up well if you prep it in advance.

The timing nudge comes from the Clarion Ledger, which reports that Mississippi ballots will be long in 2027, with a crowded field of candidates for governor and other statewide offices. Whatever state you live in, a long ballot means more reading, and reading goes better when dinner is already handled.

## Sheet-Pan Crispy Chickpeas and Potatoes with Rice

**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** 35 minutes

### What you need

- 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained
- 1 to 1¼ lb potatoes (any kind), cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil or olive oil
- 1½ teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ¾ teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 1½ cups uncooked rice (or about 4 cups cooked leftover rice)
- Optional to finish: lemon wedge, hot sauce, yogurt, or a spoonful of tahini

### The step that makes the difference: dry the chickpeas

Chickpeas straight from the can are wet, and wet food steams instead of crisping. Drain them, spread them on a clean kitchen towel, and pat the tops dry. Let them sit for 5 minutes while the oven heats. Loose skins will rub off, and you can leave them or discard them. Either way, the drier the chickpeas, the crunchier they finish.

### Steps

1. **Heat the oven to 425°F.** Put a large rimmed sheet pan in the oven while it heats if you want extra browning on the potatoes.
2. **Start the potatoes.** Toss the cubes with 1½ tablespoons of oil and ¼ teaspoon of the salt. Spread them in a single layer on the pan. Roast for 15 minutes.
3. **Season the chickpeas.** While the potatoes roast, toss the dried chickpeas with the remaining oil, the smoked paprika, garlic powder, and remaining salt.
4. **Combine.** Pull the pan out, stir the potatoes, and push them to one side. Add the chickpeas to the open space. Don't pile them up. Crowding is the second most common reason chickpeas turn soft.
5. **Roast 18 to 22 minutes more.** Stir once halfway through.
6. **Cook the rice** on the stove while everything roasts, following the package directions.
7. **Serve** the chickpeas and potatoes over rice with a squeeze of lemon and whatever sauce you like.

### How to tell it's done

- Chickpeas should look deep golden, feel dry on the surface, and rattle a little when you shake the pan. They will crisp further as they cool for a minute or two.
- Potatoes should have browned edges and a fork should slide in easily.
- If the chickpeas are golden but still soft in the middle, give them another 3 to 5 minutes. Check often, since smoked paprika can scorch.

## Easy swaps

- **No smoked paprika:** use regular paprika plus a pinch of cumin, or chili powder for a warmer flavor.
- **No fresh potatoes:** use a can of drained, dried black beans in place of one can of chickpeas, or add frozen cauliflower florets (no need to thaw) for the last 20 minutes.
- **No rice:** serve over pasta, couscous, or toasted bread.
- **Lentils instead of rice:** simmer 1 cup of dry brown or green lentils in salted water for 20 to 25 minutes until tender but not mushy.
- **Air fryer version:** cook the dried, seasoned chickpeas at 390°F for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket twice. Cook potatoes separately at 400°F for about 15 minutes.

## Prep it ahead for a busy day

This meal is forgiving, which is why it suits a day when you don't know when you'll be home.

- **Morning prep:** cube the potatoes and keep them in a container in the fridge. Drain and dry the chickpeas, then store them in a separate container with a paper towel on top.
- **Cook rice ahead:** it keeps in the fridge for a few days. Reheat it with a splash of water, covered, in the microwave.
- **Roast when you're ready:** the oven does the rest while you check your notes or your phone.

If you end up with leftovers, store the chickpeas apart from the rice and potatoes. Reheat them on a sheet pan at 400°F for 5 to 8 minutes to bring back the crunch. The microwave will make them soft.

## Make it feel like a full dinner

A plain pan of chickpeas and potatoes is good. A few finishing touches make it better with almost no work:

- A spoonful of plain yogurt or tahini thinned with lemon juice and water
- Chopped raw onion or scallions for freshness
- A fried egg on top if you have eggs
- Canned tuna stirred into the rice for extra protein

## The takeaway

When the day is going to be long, decide on dinner before it starts. Dry the chickpeas, get the pan in the oven, and let the heat do the work.
