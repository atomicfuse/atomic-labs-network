## Crispy Chickpeas with Smoked Paprika

**Serves:** 3 to 4 as a snack
**Prep:** 10 minutes
**Cook:** 25 to 30 minutes in the oven, or 12 to 15 minutes in an air fryer

### What you need

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas
- 1 to 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste

### The step that makes the difference: dry them

Crispy chickpeas depend on this. Drain and rinse the beans, then spread them on a clean towel and pat them dry. Let them sit for 10 minutes, then pat again. Any leftover surface moisture steams the chickpeas in the oven instead of roasting them, and you end up with chewy beans instead of crunchy ones.

If you have extra patience, rub them gently in the towel. Loose skins will fall off, and you can discard them or leave them on, since they crisp up too.

### Oven method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Toss the dry chickpeas with the oil on a sheet pan. Use just enough to coat them lightly.
3. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through.
4. Toss with the paprika, garlic powder, and salt while they're still hot, so the seasoning sticks.

### Air-fryer method

1. Heat the air fryer to 390°F.
2. Toss the chickpeas with 1 tablespoon of oil and cook in a single layer for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket every 4 minutes.
3. Season right after cooking.

### How to tell they're done

They should look deep golden, and a few should have split open. Pull one out and let it cool for a minute. It should crack when you bite it, with no soft center. If it's still tender, give the batch another 3 to 5 minutes.

They crisp further as they cool, so don't judge them straight off the pan.

## Easy substitutions

- **No smoked paprika:** Use regular paprika plus a pinch of cumin, or chili powder.
- **Sweet version:** Skip the savory spices and use 1 teaspoon cinnamon and 1 tablespoon sugar after roasting.
- **Different bean:** White beans work, though they come out a bit softer.
- **Less oil:** Go down to 1 tablespoon. The beans will be slightly drier but still crisp.

## Storing and using them

Chickpeas are crunchiest in the first few hours. Store leftovers uncovered or loosely covered at room temperature, since a sealed container traps moisture and softens them. If they go soft, a 5-minute return to a hot oven revives them.

Beyond snacking, scatter them over soup, grain bowls, or salads in place of croutons.

## The takeaway

Before you roast anything crispy, dry it first. Pat your chickpeas, wait 10 minutes, and pat again.
