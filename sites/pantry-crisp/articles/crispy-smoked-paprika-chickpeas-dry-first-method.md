A can of chickpeas can become a crunchy, salty snack in about 30 minutes. The difference between crisp and leathery comes down to one step: getting the chickpeas truly dry before they hit the heat. This guide covers that step, the timing, and how to tell when they're done.

**Makes:** about 2 servings as a snack (1½ cups)
**Prep:** 10 minutes
**Cook:** 25 to 35 minutes in the oven, or 12 to 15 minutes in an air fryer

## What You Need

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas
- 1 to 2 tablespoons olive oil (or any neutral oil)
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- Pinch of black pepper or cayenne, if you like heat

## The Step That Makes the Difference: Dry Them Completely

Moisture is what stands between a chickpea and a crunchy one. Wet chickpeas steam in the oven instead of roasting, and you end up with a chewy skin and a soft middle.

Here's how to avoid that:

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas well.
2. Spread them on a clean kitchen towel or a double layer of paper towels.
3. Pat the tops dry, then roll them around gently so the towel grabs the water clinging to the sides.
4. Let them air-dry for 5 to 10 minutes. Any loose skins that slip off can go in the trash, or stay on the pan if you don't mind them.

The chickpeas should feel dry to the touch, not tacky. If you have the time, leave them out for 20 minutes. Drier is better.

## Oven Method (Sheet Pan)

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Position a rack in the middle.
2. Toss the dry chickpeas with the oil on a rimmed sheet pan. Use just enough to coat them lightly. Too much oil makes them greasy rather than crisp.
3. Spread them in a single layer with space between them. Crowding causes steaming.
4. Roast for 15 minutes, then shake the pan.
5. Roast another 10 to 20 minutes, shaking every 10 minutes or so.
6. Pull the pan when the chickpeas look deep golden and sound like tiny maracas when you shake it.
7. Toss with the spices immediately while they're hot, so the seasoning clings.

### Doneness Cues

- **Sound:** a dry rattle against the pan.
- **Look:** deep golden, with cracked or split skins.
- **Test:** pull one out and let it cool for 30 seconds. It should crunch cleanly. If it's soft in the center, give the pan 5 more minutes.

Chickpeas keep crisping as they cool, so slightly less crunchy straight from the oven is normal.

## Air-Fryer Method

The air fryer circulates hot air tightly around the food, which makes it very good at this.

1. Heat the air fryer to 390°F (200°C) if yours preheats.
2. Toss the dry chickpeas with 1 tablespoon of oil.
3. Cook in a single layer in the basket for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket every 4 to 5 minutes.
4. Season hot, right out of the basket.

For a larger batch, cook in two rounds rather than piling them in. A deep stack of chickpeas won't crisp evenly.

## Add the Spices at the Right Time

Salt and spices go on after cooking in this recipe. Smoked paprika and garlic powder can scorch in a hot oven and turn bitter, and salt added before roasting can draw out moisture from the surface. Seasoning at the end keeps the flavor bright and the crunch intact.

## Easy Substitutions

- **No smoked paprika:** use regular paprika plus a pinch of cumin, or chili powder.
- **Sweet version:** skip the garlic and paprika, and toss the hot chickpeas with 1 teaspoon sugar and ½ teaspoon cinnamon.
- **Savory-cheesy:** toss with a spoonful of grated Parmesan or nutritional yeast right after roasting.
- **Other beans:** white beans work, but they can burst more easily. Roast them a few minutes shorter and check early.
- **Dried chickpeas:** cook them until just tender, drain, and dry thoroughly before roasting.

## Storing and Reviving

They are at their crunchiest the day they're made. Let them cool fully, then keep them in a bowl or a loosely covered container for up to 2 days. A tightly sealed container traps residual moisture and softens them. If they lose their snap, put them back in a 350°F oven for 5 to 8 minutes.

## Ways to Use Them

- Eat them by the handful as a snack.
- Sprinkle over soup or a rice bowl for crunch, the way you'd use croutons.
- Toss into a green salad or over yogurt with a drizzle of olive oil.
- Stir into a tuna rice bowl for a texture contrast.

## The Takeaway

Before you turn on the oven, dry the chickpeas with a towel and give them a few minutes to air out. That one habit does more for crunch than any seasoning blend will.
