## Content Guidelines
Content guidelines
- Pantry dinners. Focus on familiar ingredients like canned beans, rice, pasta, lentils, and tinned fish. Examples: Creamy White Bean Pasta and 15-Minute Tuna Rice Bowls.
- Crispy favorites. Cover oven, stovetop, and air-fryer recipes. Examples: Crispy Chickpeas with Smoked Paprika and Sheet-Pan Potato and Black Bean Tacos.
- Snacks and simple baking. Feature recipes like Peanut Butter Oat Cookies, Homemade Pita Chips, and No-Bake Chocolate Oat Bars.
- Make recipes easy to follow. Include servings, prep and cook times, common substitutions, and cues for doneness. For crispy recipes, explain the step that makes the difference, such as drying the chickpeas before roasting.
