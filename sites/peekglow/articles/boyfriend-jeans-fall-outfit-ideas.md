If you own one pair of boyfriend jeans, you already have the base for a full week of fall outfits. You just need a few formulas. Glamour recently shared the lessons from a decade of styling the look, and the throughline is simple: the slouchy, borrowed-from-the-boys fit goes almost anywhere when you change the top, the layer, and the shoes.

Below are ten ways to wear them, plus a quick way to tell whether the pair in your closet is even the right one.

## First, Know What You're Wearing

Boyfriend jeans are not the same as mom jeans. Per Glamour, the boyfriend cut is slouchy with a wide-leg fit, and that relaxed shape is what makes it so versatile. If your pair hangs loose and easy rather than sitting high and tapered, you're in boyfriend territory.

Why it's a good moment to pull them out: relaxed denim keeps leading as fashion leans toward comfort, '90s styling, and borrowed-from-the-boys silhouettes. Fall layering and preppy chic are also trending into 2026, so this fit feels current without much effort.

## The Everyday Looks

These are the outfits you'll reach for most often.

### 1. Preppy with a striped polo

A striped polo gives loose denim a neat, collegiate edge. It's one of the easiest ways to look put together on a weekend without trying hard.

### 2. With a model-off-duty leather jacket

A leather jacket adds structure on top while the jeans stay relaxed. Pair it with a simple tee and you have a classic casual outfit.

### 3. With a chic cardigan

A cardigan is the cozy option. It layers easily as temperatures drop and softens the whole look.

### 4. With a fall-appropriate turtleneck sweater

A turtleneck gives you a clean, streamlined top half. That contrast with the roomy jeans is what keeps the outfit from feeling shapeless.

### 5. The airport-chic look

Comfort is the point here. Boyfriend jeans are roomy enough for a long travel day, and the right top and layer keep them from looking like you gave up.

## The Dressed-Up Looks

Loose jeans can look polished too. These are the formulas for when the day asks more of you.

### 6. The office boyfriend jeans outfit

Glamour includes a dedicated office version. The idea is to let the jeans be the relaxed piece and make everything around them tidy: a crisp top, considered shoes, and simple accessories.

### 7. The power blazer

A blazer is the fastest way to make baggy denim look intentional. The sharp shoulders balance the slouch below, so the outfit reads as styled rather than sloppy.

### 8. The date-night look

Boyfriend jeans work after dark, too. Swap the everyday tee for a dressier top and upgrade your shoes and accessories. The jeans stay comfortable while the rest of the outfit does the dressing up.

## The Fun Ones

### 9. Denim-on-denim, the perfect pair

Glamour calls denim-on-denim a perfect pair with boyfriend jeans. If you're nervous about it, try a slightly different wash on top so the pieces look deliberate instead of mismatched.

### 10. With pops of color

Neutrals are easy, but color shows off the jeans' casual side. A bright top, scarf, or bag is enough. You don't need a full color-blocked outfit.

## The Pattern Behind All Ten

The jeans stay the same, and the outfit changes through proportions, shoes, and layers. That's the real lesson here, and it's why this works as a one-pair strategy. Some practical ways to apply it:

- **Balance the volume.** Roomy jeans pair well with something more fitted or structured on top, like a turtleneck, blazer, or leather jacket.
- **Let shoes set the mood.** The same jeans read differently with casual shoes than with dressier ones. Try swapping shoes before you swap anything else.
- **Use accessories as the finishing step.** For office and date-night looks, accessories are what push the outfit from casual to polished.

This is a judgment call, not a rule from the source, but a good test is to check the outfit in a mirror: if the top and the jeans are both shapeless, change one of them.

## Build Your Own Week

You don't need to try all ten. Pick three that fit your actual life, such as an errand outfit, a work outfit, and a weekend one. Write down the top, layer, and shoes for each, and you've built a small, affordable wardrobe around a single pair of jeans.

This week, pull your boyfriend jeans out and style them one new way: add a blazer or a striped polo, and see how much the pair can do.
