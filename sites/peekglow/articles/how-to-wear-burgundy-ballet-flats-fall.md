One pair of shoes can make a whole fall wardrobe feel more put together, and this season that pair is burgundy ballet flats. Below you'll find how to style them, what to pair them with, and where to look for options without paying luxury prices.

## What's Behind the Burgundy Flat Moment

Per InStyle, burgundy ballet flats are fall's biggest shoe and color trend, and Margot Robbie and Kendall Jenner have both been seen wearing them. InStyle also describes them as "rich-girl" shoes, a nod to how polished and expensive they look.

The appeal is easy to understand. Ballet flats are a practical, tidy alternative to boots and heels. Burgundy adds a seasonal color without being as loud as a bright shade. It's one change that makes an outfit look intentional.

## Where to Shop From $29

InStyle points shoppers to on-sale picks from brands including Steve Madden, Gap, and Madewell, with prices starting at $29. Prices and sales change quickly, so check the current price and your size before you get attached to a pair.

A few things to look for while you browse:

- **A true deep red.** Burgundy leans wine or oxblood. Compare product photos in natural light, since screens can shift the shade.
- **Comfortable construction.** Check the reviews for notes on fit, break-in time, and cushioning.
- **A finish you'll wear often.** Matte leather-look and soft suede-look styles read quietly. Patent reads dressier.

## Style Them Like a Capsule Piece

The easiest way to get your money's worth is to treat burgundy like a neutral. Here are outfit formulas that work with pieces you probably own already.

### With Jeans and a Knit

Straight or relaxed-fit jeans, a cream or gray sweater, and burgundy flats. It's simple, and the shoes are the only pop of color.

### With a Neutral Dress or Skirt

Black, camel, or chocolate brown all sit well next to burgundy. A midi skirt with a fitted top leaves the shoes in the spotlight.

### With Tailored Trousers

Black or charcoal trousers, a crisp shirt, and flats give you an office-ready look that's more comfortable than heels.

### Matching Without Overdoing It

If you want to echo the color, repeat it once in a small way, such as a bag, scarf, or nail polish. Matching everything can feel heavy, so stop at one repeat.

## Why a Color Swap Is the Smart Fall Upgrade

Trend pieces can be risky buys, but this one is forgiving. A classic silhouette means the shoes won't look dated once the trend moves on, and the color works with the darker, richer palette most people already wear in fall. If your wardrobe leans black, navy, gray, or tan, you likely own outfits that need only the shoes.

## A Quick Buying Checklist

1. Decide your budget first, then compare brands within it.
2. Pick the shade you'd happily wear with at least three outfits you own.
3. Read fit reviews, especially if you're between sizes.
4. Wear them around the house before committing to a full day out.

## The Takeaway

Pick one pair of burgundy flats, and build three outfits around them from clothes already in your closet. That's a fresher fall look for the price of one pair of shoes.
