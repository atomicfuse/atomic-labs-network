## The Water Bottle Avalanche

You open the cabinet and something rolls out. Water bottles, travel mugs, and shaker cups are awkward shapes, and they tend to pile up without a home.

BuzzFeed's roundup includes a product for organizing bottles. We don't have its name or details, so rather than guess, here's what to look for in any bottle organizer.

### What to look for

- **Fit for your actual collection.** Measure the widest bottle you own before buying anything. The chunky insulated one is usually the problem.
- **A spot you can reach.** Bottles you use daily should sit at eye or hand level, not behind the casserole dishes.
- **Lids stored separately or nearby.** Lids are the real clutter. A small bin next to the bottles keeps the pairs together.

### A free first step

Pull every bottle out and set them on the counter. Most households find a few they never use. Donate or recycle those, then organize only what's left. A smaller collection makes any organizer work better.

## Crumbs in the Oven

Burnt crumbs on the oven floor are easy to ignore until they smoke or smell during a bake. BuzzFeed's list features an oven crumb-cleaning product, though the description doesn't say what it is or how it works.

Whatever tool you choose, a few habits make oven cleanup less of a chore.

### Make it a small habit

- **Wipe up spills when the oven has cooled.** Fresh mess is far easier to remove than baked-on mess.
- **Check your manual before using any cleaner.** Oven finishes and self-cleaning features vary, and the manufacturer's instructions come first.
- **Use a tray for drips.** A sheet pan on a lower rack when you bake pies or casseroles catches what would otherwise land on the floor of the oven.

A tool that reaches into corners and lifts crumbs without scrubbing is a fair thing to want. Just read the product's directions and don't expect a miracle on years of buildup.

## The Garbage Disposal Smell

A smelly disposal can make an otherwise tidy kitchen feel off. The BuzzFeed post includes a garbage disposal deodorizing product. Again, specifics aren't in the description, so treat this as a category to shop thoughtfully.

### Keep expectations realistic

A deodorizer can freshen the drain, but odor usually starts with leftover food. Pair any product with simple routines:

- Run cold water for several seconds after using the disposal.
- Scrape plates into the trash or compost first when you can.
- Avoid sending large amounts of greasy or fibrous scraps down the drain.

If the smell sticks around after regular cleaning, it may be worth having the disposal looked at rather than buying more products.
