If your fall closet is a sea of beige, gray and oatmeal, one bold cardigan can change the whole mood without a full wardrobe overhaul. Here's how to wear a colorful chunky knit so it looks intentional, plus a budget-friendly option to start with.

## The Inspiration: Hilary Swank's Colorful Knit

Per Us Weekly, Hilary Swank's colorful chunky knit cardigan is a fun alternative to the neutral sweaters that dominate fall. The outlet pointed to a lookalike from Shenhe on Amazon, priced at $36. It has bold stripes and a cozy, textured knit, and it's the kind of piece that can sit at the center of an outfit.

I can't tell you how the Shenhe cardigan fits or holds up over time, since that's beyond what Us Weekly reported. Check recent reviews, sizing notes and the fabric details on the listing before you buy.

## Why One Colorful Piece Works Better Than a New Wardrobe

A statement cardigan does a lot of work for a small spend. Neutrals you already own become the supporting cast, and the knit supplies the personality. You get a fresh look from a single purchase instead of rebuilding your closet.

The chunky texture helps too. Thick knit reads cozy and relaxed, so even a loud stripe feels casual rather than costume-y.

## A Simple Outfit Formula

When the cardigan is the star, keep everything else quiet. Try this:

- **Base layer:** a plain tank in white, black or cream
- **Bottoms:** your everyday jeans
- **Shoes:** boots you already love
- **Top layer:** the colorful cardigan, worn open or loosely buttoned

That's four pieces, and three are probably already in your closet.

## Three Ways to Wear It

### For errands
Keep it easy: tank, straight or relaxed jeans, and flat boots. Throw the cardigan on open and you're done. The thick knit doubles as your jacket on cool mornings.

### For brunch
Swap in a slightly dressier tank, such as a fitted style in a solid color, and add a simple necklace or small hoops. Pick one of the stripe colors for your accessory so the outfit feels pulled together.

### For a casual day at home or out
Button it up and wear it as a sweater with a tank underneath for flexibility. If the room warms up, you can unbutton it without losing the look.

## How to Pick the Right Colors for Your Closet

Before you buy any striped knit, look at the shades in the stripes and ask whether you own at least two or three things that pair with them. Denim goes with nearly everything, so a cardigan that works with your jeans is a safe place to start. Then check your tanks and tees. If you have white, black or cream basics, you're set.

## Care Tips for Chunky Knits

Check the care label first, since knit fabrics vary. As general good practice, fold chunky knits rather than hanging them, because hanging can stretch the shoulders. Washing guidance depends on the fiber, so follow the tag.

## Shopping Smart for Under $40

A $36 price tag makes experimenting low-risk, but a few habits keep it that way:

1. Read reviews that mention fit and thickness, not just star ratings.
2. Check the fabric content so you know what you're getting.
3. Look at the return policy before you order.
4. Decide in advance what you'll wear it with. If you can name three outfits, it's a good buy.

## The Takeaway

You don't need a new fall wardrobe, just one piece that makes your basics feel new. Pull out your favorite tank, jeans and boots, find a striped knit in colors that suit them, and put the color on top.
