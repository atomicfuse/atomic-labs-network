If you own one pair of Chelsea boots, you already own the most flexible shoe in your fall closet. The trick is knowing how far it can stretch. Glamour recently rounded up ten ways to style the boot, and the range is wider than most of us try: polished, edgy, casual, or formal, depending on what you put around it.

Here are those ten ideas, grouped by the kind of day you're dressing for, with simple formulas you can copy this week.

Why this boot works in colder months: per Glamour's take, it balances comfort, weather practicality, and style. That makes it a solid anchor piece while fall fashion leans toward suede, tailored workwear, boho revival, and practical-luxe dressing.

## For Work and Polished Days

### With a suit
The Chelsea boot is a sharp alternative to a dress shoe. A tailored suit gives the boot a clean frame, and the boot gives the suit a little attitude. Keep the rest of the outfit simple and let the contrast do the work.

### With cigarette jeans
A slim, straight-leg cigarette jean shows off the boot's silhouette instead of bunching over it. It's an easy formula for a casual office or a weekend errand run: jeans, boots, a good knit.

### With all black
Black on black reads sleek and a bit edgy. If you wear a black leather boot, an all-black outfit is the most no-effort way to look pulled together. Mixing textures, like knit, denim, and leather, keeps it from looking flat.

## For Weekends and Cozy Layers

### With brown suede
Brown suede is one of the trends Glamour highlights for fall. It's softer and warmer-looking than black leather, so it suits earthy, relaxed outfits. If you're deciding between two pairs, black leather is the sharper pick and brown suede is the cozier one.

### With a barn jacket
A barn jacket and Chelsea boots make a practical, put-together pairing for cool days. Add jeans and you have a uniform you can repeat all season.

### With primary colors and winter gear
Maximalist winter color is part of the current mood. Try a bright coat, scarf, or hat in a primary shade over a simple base, and let the boots ground it. A neutral boot keeps a loud outfit from feeling chaotic.

## For Dresses, Skirts, and Evenings

### With a floral dress
Boho dresses are back in the fall conversation. A floral dress paired with a Chelsea boot balances sweet with sturdy, and the boot makes the look wearable on a chilly day.

### With a maxiskirt
A flowing maxiskirt over a sleek boot is a lovely contrast of soft and structured. Let the hem skim the top of the boot so the shoe stays visible.

### With royal purple
Royal purple is one of the season's colors to try. A purple piece, whether a sweater, a coat, or a skirt, looks rich beside a dark boot. If you're wary of color, start with one purple item and keep everything else neutral.

### With a gown
The most surprising idea on the list: a Chelsea boot with formal eveningwear. Dressing up a gown with a boot undercuts the formality in a way that feels modern and a little daring. It's a good one to try if you want a dressy outfit that's easier to walk in.

## How to Start With the Boots You Have

You don't need all ten looks. Pick two that feel like your life and try them first:

1. **If you work in an office:** start with the suit or cigarette jeans.
2. **If your days are casual:** try the barn jacket or a pop of color.
3. **If you love dresses:** go for the floral dress or the maxiskirt.

Take a quick photo of each outfit that works. It's an easy way to build a small library of go-to looks before the weather gets really cold.

The takeaway: pull your Chelsea boots to the front of the closet and wear them with something you wouldn't normally pair them with this week. One unexpected combination, like a maxiskirt or a bright coat, is often enough to make a familiar shoe feel new.
