Winter is a good time to change your hair, and this season's trends make it easy to pick a direction. Glamour rounded up the biggest winter hair looks for 2026-2027 and describes the season as one of transformation: bold color changes on one side, softer, romantasy-inspired styling on the other. Below are the seven trends it highlights, with practical ways to decide which one suits you and how to bring it up at your next appointment.

## Why Everything Looks a Little Fairy Tale Right Now

Per Glamour, romantasy and soft romance aesthetics are shaping beauty, fueled by pop culture moments like ACOTAR and Sense and Sensibility, along with Pinterest's "Romantic Reverie" prediction. Glamour also points to search spikes showing people are looking for both dramatic updates and low-commitment ones.

That's useful for you. You don't have to go all in. Some of these trends are a full reinvention; others are a small tweak.

## The Seven Trends

### 1. Every shade of pink

Pink is the headline color. Glamour's framing is "every shade," which suggests room for everything from subtle to bold. If you're nervous, start small: ask your stylist about a few pink streaks rather than all-over color. Streaks are easier to live with and let you test how much you like the look before you commit.

### 2. Heroine waves

This is the "romantasy waves" look: soft, romantic waves that feel storybook-ready. It's a styling trend, so it needs no cut or color at all. That makes it the lowest-commitment option on the list. Save a few photos of waves you like and note whether you want them loose and airy or more defined.

### 3. Curly cuts

Curls are getting attention through the cut itself. If you have natural curls, this is a good moment to ask for shaping designed around your texture. Bring photos of curly styles in your length range, and tell your stylist how you usually wear your hair day to day.

### 4. Auburn hues

Auburn is the warm, rich color pick for winter. It's a good middle path if pink feels like too much but you want a visible change. When you book, show a few reference images, since auburn can range from deeper to brighter, and let your stylist advise on what suits your base color.

### 5. Soft, easy bangs

The key words are soft and easy. This isn't a blunt, high-maintenance fringe. Tell your stylist you want bangs that blend in and are simple to style, and ask how they'll behave on your hair type and how often you'll need a trim.

### 6. All lengths of bobs

Glamour says bobs are in at all lengths, so there's no single correct version. That's good news if you're hesitant about cutting too much. Decide where you want the ends to land (chin, jaw, or a little longer) and bring a photo showing it.

### 7. Long layers

If you'd rather keep your length, long layers are the trend that lets you do it. They add movement without a dramatic chop. Ask for layers that keep your length, and be specific about whether you want them subtle or noticeable.

## Which Trend Fits You?

Here's a simple way to sort the list by how much change you want.

- **Barely any change:** heroine waves or long layers.
- **A small, reversible tweak:** a few pink streaks or soft bangs.
- **A noticeable color shift:** auburn or a fuller pink.
- **A real reinvention:** a bob, a curly cut, or a bolder pink.

If you can't decide, pick the lowest-commitment version of the one you keep coming back to. You can always go further next visit.

## Before You Book

A few habits make any of these go better:

1. **Save three to five photos.** Include at least one that shows what you don't want.
2. **Describe your daily routine.** Tell your stylist how much time you really spend on your hair.
3. **Ask about upkeep.** Bangs, bobs, and color all need maintenance, so ask what to expect and what it costs.
4. **Be honest about your comfort level.** Saying "I want pink but I'm nervous" gives your stylist useful direction.

## Your Next Step

Choose one trend from the seven, find three photos of it, and save them to your phone. That small bit of prep turns a vague wish for a winter change into a clear request.
