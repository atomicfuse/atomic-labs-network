The ballet flat and the sneaker have been dating since 2021, and this fall the relationship is officially a commitment. Per Glamour, the ballerina sneaker, nicknamed the "sneakerina," is back, with big names like Adidas and Puma releasing new versions alongside designer labels. Glamour also notes that Prime Day deals are live, which makes this a convenient moment to shop.

One caution on dates: the source describes this as the trend's return for fall 2024, so check current listings and prices before you buy anything.

My angle: the sneakerina is less a trend than a loophole. It lets you wear something that looks dressed up while your feet stay in sneaker mode. Here's how Glamour suggests styling it, with a few of my own observations.

## First, what is a sneakerina?

It's a hybrid of a ballet flat and a sneaker. Glamour traces the trend to its 2021 debut, when it was a viral moment, and says it has since grown into a wardrobe staple. The category now includes mainstream and luxury brands alike.

The shopping list Glamour rounds up is long, and the variety is the story. It includes:

- Adidas Barreda Mary Jane Sneakers and Adidas Tokyo Sneakers
- Puma Speedcat Go Ballet Sneakers and Puma Replicatch Ballet Sneakers
- Salomon RX Marie-Jeanne Sneakers
- Merrell Relay Tour Mary Janes
- Camper Peu Path Ballet Sneakers
- Vivaia Ciara Sneakerinas and Vivaia Yancy Sneakers
- Tigeridea Mary Jane Sneakerinas and Mary Jane Sneakers
- Iiimmu Ballet Sneakers, Anonymous Sneakerinas and Maguire Eliza Sneakers

It's hard not to read that roster as a sign of how far the trend has traveled. A shoe that began as a viral oddity now has a place in the catalogs of athletic giants and outdoor-gear brands.

## Formula one: the preppy version

Glamour's first formula pairs sneakerinas with pleated skirts, polos and crew socks for a sporty-feminine look. This is the easiest entry point. Everything in the outfit already says "campus," and the shoe simply fits in. The crew socks are the key detail, since they keep the sporty half of the hybrid in the picture.

## Formula two: the romantic contrast

The second approach is more playful. Glamour suggests bold-colored sneakerinas with flowy white skirts and balloon sleeves, which adds some unexpected edge to a soft outfit.

In my view, this is where the shoe earns its keep. A pastel-and-lace outfit can read sweet to the point of sugary. A saturated shoe breaks that up, and the sneaker silhouette keeps the look from feeling precious.

## Formula three: casual and comfortable

For everyday wear, Glamour points to oversized cardigans and baggy jeans, plus personal accessories, for a blank-canvas look. Here the shoe is a quiet supporting player. If your wardrobe is already built on relaxed layers, a sneakerina adds a little polish without asking you to change anything else.

## Formula four: transitional weather dressing

Fall temperatures are famously indecisive, and the fourth formula speaks to that. Glamour suggests lace-up sneakerinas layered with high socks and oversized jackets for cooler days. The high socks matter here, because a low-cut flat-style shoe is the first thing to feel the chill.

## Shopping notes: slim or thick soles?

Glamour's roundup compares slim-soled and thick-soled options, and that is probably the most useful choice you'll make. Going by the styles above, my own rule of thumb:

- **Slimmer soles** lean toward the ballet-flat side, which suits skirts and dressier outfits.
- **Thicker soles** lean sneaker, which suits baggy jeans and long walking days.

That's my read rather than anything the brands have said, so try pairs on if you can. The price range in the roundup is wide, so there are options at different budgets. With Prime Day deals live per Glamour, it's a reasonable time to compare, but prices and availability change quickly, and the sale won't last forever.

## The bottom line

The sneakerina has outlasted its viral debut, and the reason is simple. It solves a real problem: wanting to look put together without giving up comfort. Four outfits, one shoe, and the only hard decision is the sole thickness.
