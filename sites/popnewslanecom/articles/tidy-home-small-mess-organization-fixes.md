## A Starter Kit Mindset

If you're curious but not ready to scroll through all 38, think of the three highlighted problems as a starter kit for noticing what bugs you:

### 1. Pick the one you see daily

The car, the desk, and the entertainment center all get daily eyeballs. Fix the one that bothers you most, and the payoff multiplies because you see the result every day.

### 2. Go after the ignored surfaces

Baseboards are the classic example. Cleaning them once makes the whole room look sharper, in a way that's hard to explain until you've done it.

### 3. Tame the visual noise

Cables don't make a mess in the traditional sense. They make a mood. Bundling them is less about cleanliness and more about quieting a room.

Neat freaks are often teased for noticing everything, but that's the whole superpower.
