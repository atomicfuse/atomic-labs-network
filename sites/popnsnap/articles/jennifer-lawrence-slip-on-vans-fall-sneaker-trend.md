## How to Wear the Look

The appeal of a slip-on is that it plays well with almost anything, though I'll be upfront that this section is my styling take rather than something Lawrence or InStyle laid out:

### Casual
The default setting. Slip-ons with relaxed denim or a simple knit is the "ran out for coffee, looked great anyway" formula.

### Smart-casual
A clean slip-on can stand in for a loafer when you want something less formal than a dress shoe. Pair with tailored trousers and a sharper jacket, and the sneaker becomes the relaxed note in an otherwise polished outfit.

### Elevated
The sneaker-with-a-dressier-outfit trick is a street-style staple for a reason. The contrast is the whole point: a sleek coat or structured piece, plus a shoe that says you're not trying too hard.
