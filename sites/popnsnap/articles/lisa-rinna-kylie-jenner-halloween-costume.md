Lisa Rinna showed up on *Live With Kelly and Mark* for Halloween dressed as Kylie Jenner, and the review that matters most came from Kylie. Per The Cut, Jenner commented, "Thought this was me." When the person being impersonated does a double take, the costume has done its job.

## What Rinna Actually Pulled Off

According to The Cut, Rinna recreated Jenner's orange Chrome Hearts look, along with the glam and the signature pout. That's three separate things to get right, and a celebrity costume tends to fall apart when even one of them is off.

The outfit is the easy part to explain, since it's a specific, recognizable look. The glam and the pout are harder. Those are attitude, and they turn a costume into an impression.

## Why This One Landed

Here's my take: celebrity-as-celebrity costumes work best when the person wearing them commits to the details instead of winking at the audience. Rinna's version, as described by The Cut, reads as a full recreation rather than a quick nod. The pout is a good example. It's the sort of detail that could easily tip into caricature, yet the reaction from Jenner suggests it landed closer to homage.

If you're planning a celebrity look before the week is out, Rinna's version offers a decent blueprint based on what The Cut reported:

The lesson is simple: the details sell the impression, and the confidence sells the details.
