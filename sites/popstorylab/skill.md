# Content Agent Instructions for popstorylab

## Target Audiences
General

## Tone
Fun, curious, and conversational

## Topics
- Pop Culture, Celebrity News, TV Shows, Movies, Music, Viral Stories, Internet Trends, Behind the Scenes, Entertainment Nostalgia, Where Are They Now

## Content Guidelines
popstorylab.com sounds like a pop culture and entertainment site—celebrity stories, TV, movies, music, and viral internet moments.
I’d position it as “The stories behind what’s trending.” The “lab” gives it a curious angle: explain why something went viral, uncover behind-the-scenes details, and revisit nostalgic favorites.
Good content categories:
- Celebrity buzz: surprising stories and career updates.
- TV & movies: cast news, hidden details, and filming secrets.
- Viral culture: internet trends and the people behind them.
- Nostalgia: where-are-they-now stories and iconic pop culture moments.
For your publishing portfolio, entertainment with curiosity-driven headlines is the strongest fit.
Tagline: Pop culture. Unexpected stories.
