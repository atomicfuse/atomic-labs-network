## Build Your Visit Around the Pizzerias

The heart of the Fox News story is the city's legendary local pizzerias. The summary of the segment doesn't name each shop or describe each pie, so I won't invent a menu for you. What you can do is set up your own crawl with a simple structure:

1. **Pick two or three pizzerias** rather than trying to see everything. Pizza is filling, and a short list keeps the comparison meaningful.
2. **Order the same basic style at each stop** so you can taste the differences between kitchens.
3. **Talk to the people working there.** Traditions live with the people who keep them going.
4. **Take notes on the day.** Which crust, which char, which sauce stuck with you? You'll want that for the next trip.

If you're traveling with others, split pies. You'll taste more and stay hungry for the next stop.
