## Discover the Luxury of Tree House Hotels

If you’re on the hunt for an enchanting escape, these **17 luxury tree house hotels** offer a unique blend of nature and high-end design. From lush rainforests to snowy Arctic landscapes, each destination not only provides stunning views but also top-notch amenities such as spas, gourmet restaurants, and chic suites. Here’s a look at these elevated retreats that promise to turn your next getaway into an unforgettable adventure!

## 1. Acre - San José del Cabo, Mexico
Nestled in the Baja Peninsula, **Acre** boasts elegant tree houses surrounded by palm trees and lush gardens. Enjoy organic dining or relax by the pool while sipping craft cocktails. It’s a perfect blend of luxury and nature.

## 2. andBeyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge - Lake Manyara National Park, Tanzania
Immerse yourself in **andBeyond Lake Manyara Tree Lodge**, where you can fall asleep to the sounds of wildlife. With eight luxurious tree house suites, you can enjoy game drives, spa treatments, and breathtaking sunset views over the lake.

## 3. Arctic TreeHouse Hotel - Rovaniemi, Finland
For a true winter wonderland experience, stay at the **Arctic TreeHouse Hotel**. Surrounded by snow-covered forests, these stylish accommodations offer a chance to view the northern lights right from your window.

## 4. The Green O - Greenough, Montana
A secluded escape in Montana, **The Green O** combines modern design with nature. With six unique tree houses, each equipped with heated floors and private hot tubs, it's a luxurious base for exploring the great outdoors.

## 5. Hårtwood - Minden, Ontario, Canada
Experience rustic luxury at **Hårtwood**, where contemporary design meets the tranquility of nature. Nestled in a pristine forest, this retreat offers a sustainable stay with a focus on relaxation and rejuvenation.

## 6. Treehotel - Harads, Sweden
Known for its innovative design, **Treehotel** features rooms that are built to blend seamlessly with their surroundings. Unique themes like the Mirrorcube and the Cabin in the Tree make it a must-visit for design enthusiasts.

## 7. Post Ranch Inn - Big Sur, California 
Soak in panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean from your private tree house at **Post Ranch Inn**. This eco-luxury hotel embraces nature’s beauty with sustainable architecture that feels both luxurious and organic.

## 8. Kulu Safaris - Botswana
At **Kulu Safaris**, stay in luxurious tree houses overlooking the Okavango Delta. Here, enjoy piano trills of the bush or go on guided bush walks to see native wildlife.

## 9. The Whitepod Hotel - Les Cerniers, Switzerland
**The Whitepod Hotel** is unique to the Swiss Alps, featuring eco-friendly pods that rest in the trees. Experience winter sports during the day and come home to warm wood interiors and breathtaking mountain views.

## 10. Tsala Treetop Lodge - Plettenberg Bay, South Africa
Set in the stunning Garden Route, **Tsala Treetop Lodge** features five-star luxury in the treetops. The lodge offers a perfect base to explore wildlife and beaches while enjoying private plunge pools and fire pits.

## 11. The Treehouse Point - Issaquah, Washington
A quaint retreat just outside of Seattle, **Treehouse Point** offers rustic charm and a peaceful ambiance. Each tree house is uniquely designed, giving you that nostalgic feeling of childhood wonder.

## 12. Chewton Glen - New Milton, England
Stay in a tree house with luxury amenities at **Chewton Glen**. Each treetop suite features lush furnishings, a hot tub, and beautiful views over the New Forest National Park.

## 13. Finca Rosa Blanca - Costa Rica
Experience the tropical paradise of Costa Rica at **Finca Rosa Blanca**. This eco-friendly resort offers tree house accommodations with organic meals served on-site, perfect for immersing yourself in nature.

## 14. The Canopy Treehouse Hotel - Cairns, Australia
Explore the lush rainforests of Queensland at **The Canopy Treehouse Hotel**. Enjoy a unique stay high in the trees while having access to tours featuring local wildlife.

## 15. Tree House Lodge - Costa Rica
Located on the Caribbean coast, **Tree House Lodge** provides a unique experience with a focus on sustainability. Glide through the treetops and listen to the sounds of nature as you unwind in this eco-lodge.

## 16. Sky Lodge Adventure - Sacred Valley, Peru
For adventure seekers, **Sky Lodge** offers unique transparent pods that hang from a cliff, providing thrill-seeking views of the Sacred Valley below. This is an experience like no other.

## 17. Les Cabanes de Fiona - La Roque-Gageac, France
Enjoy the romantic atmosphere of **Les Cabanes de Fiona**, where tree houses sit atop beautiful hills and offer stunning views of the Dordogne River. It’s a perfect getaway for couples.
