One of the first things that visitors notice upon entering Dishoom is the incredible atmosphere. Each element is thoughtfully curated to transport diners to a different time and place, creating an experience that goes beyond just eating.

## A Menu Full of Flavor

Of course, the food is the main event in any restaurant, and Dishoom does not disappoint. Famous for its incredible breakfast, especially the bacon naan roll, the menu features a delightful blend of Indian and Persian cuisine. Signature dishes like the house black daal and the lamb chops are beloved by both locals and tourists, showcasing the rich flavors and culinary heritage of India. The attention to detail in each dish elevates the dining experience, earning rave reviews and shares online.
