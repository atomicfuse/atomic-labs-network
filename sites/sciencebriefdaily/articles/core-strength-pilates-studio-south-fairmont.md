## Understanding the Science of Pilates

Delving into the age-old practice's lineage can deepen your appreciation for what Pilates offers. Founded by Joseph Pilates during World War I as a method of rehabilitation, Pilates has evolved into a widely recognized fitness system. Modern reformers and techniques not only draw from this rich history but also integrate it into contemporary practices to cater to today's varied fitness landscape.
