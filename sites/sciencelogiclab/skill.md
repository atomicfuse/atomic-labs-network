## Content Guidelines
Make readers feel smarter without making them work hard. Accessible stories with a surprising takeaway, clear explanations, and strong visuals. Create accessible, engaging articles about science, nature, space, technology, and the human mind and body. Each article should answer a clear question, explain an interesting phenomenon, or explore a discovery.
