## How Quantum Materials Can Amplify Invisible Signals
Physicists have long faced challenges when it comes to detecting dark matter, particularly lighter candidates that interact very weakly with ordinary matter. Traditional detection methods often struggle to capture these extremely faint signals. However, researchers are now turning their attention to three promising quantum materials: titanium diselenide (TiSe₂), strontium ruthenate (Sr₂RuO₄), and hole-doped diamond.

These quantum materials can act as ultra-sensitive detectors by harnessing low-energy plasmons—collective excitations of electrons that can amplify tiny energy signals. This amplification could allow for the detection of light dark matter, which has been significantly elusive until now.
