## What Is a Super El Niño?
Super El Niño is a stronger version of the already well-known El Niño phenomenon, where warmer ocean waters in the Pacific Ocean significantly influence weather patterns worldwide. Typically occurring every few years, a Super El Niño generates even warmer sea surface temperatures, leading to dramatic shifts in weather patterns and climate.
