## The Rich-Looking Playbook

The angle I'm obsessed with is the "rich on a budget" one, and the lineup InStyle describes practically writes it for you. A few thoughts, clearly mine and not the outlet's:

### Let one hero piece do the talking
A Kate Spade bag is the kind of accessory that makes an outfit look intentional. Pair it with a Gap basic and nobody checks the tag.

### Matching sets are the ultimate cheat code
A matching set is a whole outfit in one decision. It reads polished, takes zero effort, and InStyle specifically flagged them as rich-looking. Fall layering, handled.

### Denim never gets old
Levi's is on the list, and InStyle also notes jeans loved by Oprah Winfrey. When a classic pair is discounted, it's hard not to wonder why anyone would wait for full price.

### Sneakers keep it real
Adidas sneakers are the sprinkle of off-duty cool that stops a look from trying too hard. Dress up, dress down, still the same shoes.

## Ella's Shopping Strategy

A few unsolicited, entirely affectionate tips:

- **Browse now, buy smart.** Build your list before tomorrow's launch so you're not deciding under pressure.
- **Start with the $4 finds.** If basics start that low, they're the easiest way to stretch a budget across more outfits.
- **Splurge on one thing.** One bag or pair of jeans, with cheap basics around it, is how "rich-looking" actually works.
- **Check the details.** Sizes and colors move fast, so confirm before you celebrate.
