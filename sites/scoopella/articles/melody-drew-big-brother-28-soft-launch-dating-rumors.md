## The Soft Launch: A Masterclass in Saying Nothing While Saying Everything

For the uninitiated — hi, welcome, glad you're here — a soft launch is the art of introducing a potential partner to your followers without actually *introducing* them. A hand in the corner of a photo. A second coffee cup. A reflection that catches the light just so. It's plausible deniability dressed in beachwear, and it has become the preferred relationship status update for anyone who wants the buzz without the commitment to a caption.

Melody's sunglasses photo is, frankly, textbook. If she'd wanted Drew invisible, she could have cropped differently. If she'd wanted him announced, she would have tagged him. Instead, she chose the exact middle ground that sends a fanbase into a tailspin — which is either a savvy media move or genuine ambiguity, and both are valid, and both are *fascinating*.

The Minions meme is a separate category of intrigue entirely. There is no reading of an unprompted, context-free Minions post that doesn't raise more questions than it answers. Ella is choosing to see it as a cryptic love letter and no one can stop her.
