Hello, Upper East Siders of the sneaker aisle. Ella here, and I've got a footwear scoop that's half ballet, half gym class, and fully back.

Per Glamour, the ballerina sneaker, better known as the "sneakerina," is returning for fall. It's a hybrid of the ballet flat and the sneaker, and the big names are in on it: Adidas, Puma, and designer labels are all releasing new versions, according to the report. Glamour also notes that Prime Day deals are live, which means your wallet may be about to make a very poor decision on your feet's behalf.

## From Viral Moment to Wardrobe Staple

Glamour traces the sneakerina back to its 2021 debut. Back then it looked like one of those fads that arrives, gets photographed, and quietly disappears. Instead, per the report, it has evolved into a wardrobe staple, with mainstream and luxury brands all-in on the category.

It's hard not to read that as the real story here. When Adidas, Puma, Salomon, and Camper are all making their own take on the same shoe, the trend isn't a whisper anymore. It's a roll call.

The shoes Glamour rounds up include:

- Adidas Barreda Mary Jane Sneakers and Adidas Tokyo Sneakers
- Puma Speedcat Go Ballet Sneakers and Puma Replicatch Ballet Sneakers
- Salomon RX Marie-Jeanne Sneakers
- Camper Peu Path Ballet Sneakers
- Merrell Relay Tour Mary Janes
- Vivaia's Ciara and Yancy styles
- Tigeridea, Iiimmu, Maguire Eliza, and Anonymous options

That's a lot of ballet-adjacent footwear, and gossip is my business, so I can tell you that nobody here is playing it shy.

## The Four Formulas

Glamour offers four ways to wear them this season. I'm judging them like I judge everyone: lovingly, and from a distance.

### 1. The Preppy One

Pleated skirt, polo, crew socks. Per Glamour, this is the sporty-feminine route. It's the outfit of someone who definitely has a tennis club membership and definitely does not discuss it.

### 2. The Romantic Contrast

This is my favorite, and not just because it has drama. Glamour suggests pairing bold-colored sneakerinas with a flowy white skirt and balloon sleeves, for what it describes as an unexpected edge. The soft, ethereal look gets a little attitude from the shoes. Think of it as a ballerina who just ditched rehearsal.

### 3. The Casual Comfort Look

Oversized cardigan, baggy jeans, and personal accessories. Glamour frames the sneakerina here as a blank-canvas shoe, and I'd say the take is that it lets the rest of your outfit do the talking. Low effort, high reward, and exactly the kind of look that says "I woke up like this" while very much not waking up like this.

### 4. The Transitional Layer

For cooler days, Glamour suggests lace-up sneakerinas with high socks and an oversized jacket. It's the sensible choice for the weeks when the weather can't make up its mind, and honestly, neither can the rest of us.

## Slim Soles, Thick Soles, and Prime Day

Here's where the shopping gets strategic. Glamour's roundup covers both slimmer and thicker-soled options, so you can pick your lane: the daintier, closer-to-a-ballet-flat end of the spectrum, or something chunkier and more sneaker-forward. The Puma Speedcat and Replicatch names, and the Salomon and Merrell entries, suggest the sportier brands are leaning into the hybrid, while labels like Vivaia and Camper keep it closer to the flat.

I'll be careful here: the details I have don't include prices, so I can't tell you which pair is the steal. What Glamour does say is that Prime Day deals are live, so check the listings yourself before you commit. A deal is only a deal if the shoes survive your actual commute.

## Ella's Take

Why does this shoe keep coming back? My theory, and it's only a theory, is that it solves a very modern dilemma. We want to look like we tried, and we want to be comfortable enough to run for the train. The sneakerina does both, and it does it with a little bow-tied wink.

The real tell is that it has outlived its viral moment. Fads get a week. Wardrobe staples get a fall season, a luxury collection, and a Prime Day banner.

So pick your formula, pick your sole, and try not to trip on your own hype. You know you love me.
