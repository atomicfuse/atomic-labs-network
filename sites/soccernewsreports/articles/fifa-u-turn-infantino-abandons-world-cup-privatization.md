## FIFA President Reverses Course on World Cup Privatization

Infantino's initial proposal aimed at generating significant revenue through private investment in the commercial rights of the World Cup, sparking fears of diluting the tournament's integrity. The U-turn signifies the ongoing complexities surrounding FIFA's governance, especially as the world looks forward to the 2026 World Cup.
