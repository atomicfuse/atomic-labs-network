A first charter is a pressure cooker. Everyone is learning the boat, the guests and each other at the same time. Under those conditions, a note delivered with a sharp edge can land as a personal attack. A note delivered gently can still get read as an insult by someone already on edge.

It's hard not to wonder whether this is less about the specific incident and more about two people sizing up who gets to set the tone in the interior. The recap also puts Alex's laid-back approach to running the deck under the microscope ahead of a difficult docking. Docking is where *Below Deck* leadership gets honest. That's the fair way to see it. A relaxed lead isn't automatically a weak one. Some of the best crews run on trust rather than barking. But the show tends to reward whoever can switch gears when the stakes rise, and this episode appears designed to find out whether Alex can.
