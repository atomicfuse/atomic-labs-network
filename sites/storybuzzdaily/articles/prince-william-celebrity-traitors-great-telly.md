Somewhere in a palace-adjacent living room, a royal family is reportedly doing what the rest of us do on a weeknight: yelling at the television about who the Traitors are.

That's the news. Here's the take: this is the most relatable thing a senior royal has said in a while, and it works because the show's whole premise is a royal-court fantasy in disguise.
