Winter hair has apparently been reading a lot of fantasy novels. Glamour's rundown of the 2026-2027 winter hair trends frames the season as one of transformation, with bold color changes on one side and softer, romantasy-inspired styling on the other. The list: every shade of pink, heroine waves, curly cuts, auburn hues, soft easy bangs, all lengths of bobs, and long layers.

Seven trends is a lot of options for one head, so here's the angle: think of this as a commitment ladder. Some of these are a cosmetic cliffhanger, others are a gentle chapter break. Pick your level of drama.

## Why Romantasy, Why Now

Per Glamour, soft romance aesthetics are shaping beauty right now, fueled by pop culture moments like *ACOTAR* and *Sense and Sensibility*, plus Pinterest's "Romantic Reverie" prediction. Glamour also points to search spikes showing people are looking for both dramatic and low-commitment winter hair updates.

That last bit is the useful one. It suggests (our read, not Glamour's) that readers aren't picking between "big change" and "small tweak" so much as shopping both aisles at once. Which makes a ladder a handy way to sort the list.

## Rung One: The Low-Stakes Refresh

### Soft, easy bangs

Glamour lists soft, easy bangs among the season's trends. The word "easy" is doing a lot of work, and it's the whole pitch: face-framing without a daily battle. If you're nervous, the practical move is to bring photos to your stylist and say exactly how soft you mean.

### Long layers

Long layers make the list too. They're the safest way to freshen things up without surrendering length, which is a fair pick for anyone who loves their hair but wants it to look like something happened.

### All lengths of bobs

Glamour's phrasing is "all lengths of bobs," which is refreshingly inclusive. Whether that means chin-grazing or longer, the point is that the bob is open to interpretation this season. It's a haircut with a built-in escape hatch: it grows out.

## Rung Two: The Mood Shift

### Heroine waves

This is the trend that gives the whole season its romantasy label. Glamour calls them "heroine waves," and the name does the storytelling for you: think of the soft, tousled look you'd imagine on the lead of a sweeping fantasy romance. Glamour's brief doesn't spell out a method here, so check with your stylist about what suits your texture and how to get the look at home.

### Curly cuts

Curly cuts are also on the list. It's a good reminder that romantic hair isn't one texture. If your natural pattern is the star, a cut designed around it is arguably the most direct way to get the vibe without fighting your hair every morning. That's our take, but it's hard to argue with the logic.

## Rung Three: The Plot Twist

### Auburn hues

Auburn is the warm, moody choice, and it fits the season's romance theme neatly. It's a color change that reads cozy rather than shocking, which makes it a decent first step for anyone curious about going darker or redder.

### Every shade of pink

The headliner. Glamour's phrasing is "every shade of pink," and its overview mentions pink streaks specifically, which is useful if you want the idea without the full commitment. Streaks give you the story beat; a full-head shift gives you the whole saga. Your stylist can help you pick the shade and the amount.

## How to Pick Your Chapter

A few ways to think about it, all of them our suggestions rather than Glamour's:

- **Want drama with an exit plan?** Pink streaks sound like the smart compromise.
- **Want romance without a big change?** Waves, long layers, or soft bangs.
- **Want a clean break?** A bob or an auburn shift.
- **Love your natural texture?** A curly cut built around it.

Whatever you choose, bring reference photos. The romantasy label is evocative, but "make me look like a fantasy heroine" is a tough brief for any stylist.

## The Real Appeal

The cleverest thing about this trend roundup is how little it asks of you ideologically. Fantasy romance has a reputation for big feelings and bigger gestures, yet the hair version ranges from a gentle wave to a pink overhaul. It's escapism you can book in an afternoon.

And with winter stretching on, that's not a bad deal: you may not get a faerie court, but you can at least get the hair.
