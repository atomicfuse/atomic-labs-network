House Beautiful has published a list of 20 elegant Halloween decorations, mostly from Pottery Barn plus a handful of other retailers, built around one promise: a home that looks ghoulishly glamorous rather than like a clearance aisle exploded. The picks run from wall mirrors and candle holders to tableware and planters.

It's a timely list. Halloween shopping is in full swing, and the pitch fits a broader appetite for seasonal decor that feels elevated and reusable instead of disposable. Call it quiet luxury, but make it haunted.

Here's our angle: the best pieces on this list work because they're good objects first and Halloween second. That's the whole trick to festive-not-tacky, and it's worth stealing.

## The Rule Hiding in the List

Look at what House Beautiful chose. An antique brass spider-web mirror. A smoked amber glass candle holder. Antique brushstroke birdcages. A sculptural golden snake. None of these shouts "HAPPY HALLOWEEN" at a volume that requires earplugs. They lean on materials (brass, glass, ceramic, enameled cast iron) and let the motif do the whispering.

That's the difference between decor and costume. A spider web etched into a mirror frame is a design choice. A plastic web stretched over your hedges is a cry for help (a lovable one, but still).

## Where to Spend Your Spooky Budget

### The wall: mirrors and hooks

Three items live here: the spider-web mirror, the serpentine wall hook, and a snake-wrapped mirror that House Beautiful describes as Nagini-inspired. If you're a Harry Potter person, you already know who Nagini is, and you can decide for yourself whether a mirror wearing a Voldemort-adjacent reptile counts as tasteful. We'd argue it does, as long as nobody in the room is a Slytherin about it.

### The table: where the list really shines

More than half the picks are for eating, drinking, or serving. The lineup includes:

- Scorpion-topped cocktail picks
- Autumn leaf inlay coasters
- A lidded snack bowl
- Linen napkins with an oak vine design
- Ghost-shaped pinch bowls
- A gold-decaled "Hex Mix" snack bowl
- A skull charcuterie board
- Pumpkin-shaped enameled cast iron cocottes
- A skeleton serving collection with wine glasses

It's hard not to read this as a quiet argument: the smartest way to decorate for Halloween is to throw a dinner party. Tableware does double duty, and a skull charcuterie board is a conversation piece that also holds brie. A "Hex Mix" bowl in gold decals is the rare pun that looks good on a sideboard.

A few of these are also the sneakiest picks for longevity. Autumn leaf coasters and oak vine napkins read as fall, not just Halloween, so they can stay out until Thanksgiving (our suggestion, not the list's).

### The glow: candles and incense

The smoked amber candle holder is the moody-lighting move, and the hanging taper candles are Hogwarts-inspired, per House Beautiful. Floating candles are a pop-culture shorthand for "wizard dining hall," and honestly the reference lands. Add a ghost incense smoker and you've covered sight and smell without a single fog machine.

### The soft stuff and the porch

Gus the Ghost throw pillow, topped with a bouclé pumpkin, is the cozy entry. Bouclé is the texture that made a thousand living rooms look expensive, so putting it on a pumpkin is a small act of genius. Outside, the list suggests a light blue ceramic pumpkin planter and a woodland critter doormat. Light blue on a pumpkin is a gentle break from orange, and the critters keep the welcome mat friendly rather than frightening.

## How to Make It Look Intentional

The list doesn't offer a styling formula, so here's ours, clearly labeled as opinion:

1. **Pick one hero.** A mirror or the golden snake can carry a room. Don't compete with it.
2. **Repeat a metal.** Brass and gold show up repeatedly in these picks. Sticking to one warm metal makes disparate spooky objects look like a set.
3. **Mix the cute with the creepy.** Gus the Ghost next to a serpent hook keeps the vibe playful instead of grim.
4. **Let the neutrals stay.** Birdcages, ceramics, and cast iron don't need a calendar.

## The Catch

This is a list heavily weighted toward Pottery Barn, so prices will likely trend upscale. The report ideas behind it travel well to thrift stores, though. Antique brass, amber glass, and old birdcages are exactly the things that turn up secondhand, and that's where this look gets cheaper and, arguably, more authentic.

## The Takeaway

The most telling thing about House Beautiful's 20 picks is how few of them are actually scary. Snakes, skulls, and scorpions show up, but they're rendered in gold, brass, and glass, which turns dread into décor. Tacky Halloween announces itself. Elegant Halloween lets guests notice the scorpion on their cocktail pick halfway through the second drink.

That's the better trick, and the better party.
