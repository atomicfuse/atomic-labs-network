Cyber Week is crowded, and deal fatigue is real. BuzzFeed's shopping team ran a post by a frequent traveler who sorted through the noise and picked the travel gear that earns its spot in a bag. The original post lists 28 deals. This guide covers seven of them, with the discounts as reported by BuzzFeed.

The common thread is that each pick solves a specific, annoying travel problem. If you have a holiday trip coming or you're planning 2026 travel, here's what's worth a look.

## Start With the Bag: Two Space-Savers

Packing well beats packing more. Two of the picks are about carrying less stuff, more cleverly.

### Beis travel backpack (25% off)

Backpacks like this tend to double as a plane personal item, which is how BuzzFeed's writer describes her favorite. If you're trying to skip a checked bag, a roomy personal item is the cheapest way to do it. Check your airline's personal item size limits before you buy, since they vary.

### Away Topsider suitcase (25% off)

This is the bigger purchase on the list. A suitcase is something you use for years, so a 25% discount is worth noticing if yours is already on its last zipper. Measure your storage spot at home too. A good suitcase that doesn't fit your closet is a small-space headache of its own.

## Comfort Upgrades for Long Days

### Trtl travel pillow (25% off)

Bulky neck pillows eat bag space. The Trtl is a wrap-style pillow, and it's on sale at 25% off. If you sleep upright on flights or trains, this is a low-cost way to make that tolerable.

### Garnet Hill cashmere wrap (40% off)

A wrap does triple duty: airplane blanket, scarf, and extra layer when the cabin is freezing. At 40% off, it's the splurgy-but-useful pick. It also works as a cozy throw on your couch once you're home, which makes it an easy gift.

## Small Items That Save Trips

### 2024 Kindle Paperwhite (35% off)

BuzzFeed notes this is a Kindle worth grabbing now because prices have gone up recently. A whole library in something thinner than one paperback is the ultimate space-saver, and it's easier on the eyes than a phone screen on a long flight.

### Tumella windproof travel umbrella (up to 50% off)

Travel umbrellas are small, but a wet trip without one is miserable. This one is billed as windproof and is up to 50% off. Toss it in your personal item and forget about it until you need it.

### Plackers Floss Picks, 600-count (49% off)

Not glamorous, but you'll use them. A 600-count pack at 49% off will outlast a lot of trips, and a few in a carry-on pouch beat hunting for a pharmacy in an unfamiliar airport.

## How to Decide What to Actually Buy

Deals only count if you'd buy the item anyway. A quick filter:

- **Replace before you add.** Buy the suitcase or backpack only if your current one is worn out or too big.
- **Weigh the daily use.** A Kindle or umbrella earns its price faster than something you'd use once a year.
- **Think gifts.** The pillow, wrap, and Kindle are easy picks for the frequent flyer in your life.
- **Check the price yourself.** Discounts are reported as percentages off, so compare against the item's usual price before you check out.

Sale windows are short and prices can change, so confirm the current deal on the retailer's page.

## The Takeaway

Pick the one item on this list that fixes your most annoying travel problem, whether that's cramped bags, bad sleep, or dead-battery reading, and skip the rest. One good purchase beats seven impulse buys.
