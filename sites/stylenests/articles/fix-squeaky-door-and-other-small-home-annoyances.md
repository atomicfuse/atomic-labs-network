You know the door. The one that announces every trip to the bathroom at 2 a.m., or makes you wince each time you walk into the bedroom. You've learned to live with it, which is exactly the problem.

Buzzfeed recently rounded up 45 products for the little home issues people keep putting off, and its headline pick for the squeaky door is a WD-40 pen. I can only speak to that one item from the roundup, so that's what we'll build on here. Below: how to quiet a squeaky door in a few minutes, and how to find the other small annoyances that deserve the same treatment.

## Start with the squeaky door

The pen format is the appeal. Per Buzzfeed, it's pitched as a fix for doors that make a horrible squeaking noise. A pen-style applicator is handy because you can put lubricant on a small spot rather than spraying a whole hinge and everything around it.

Here's a simple way to go about it:

1. **Find the noisy spot.** Open and close the door slowly and listen. Squeaks usually come from the hinges, so you can narrow it down by working the door back and forth.
2. **Apply a small amount.** Put the product right on the hinge, where the moving parts meet.
3. **Work the door.** Swing it open and shut several times so the lubricant spreads through the hinge.
4. **Wipe the extra.** Use a rag to catch drips so nothing ends up on your floor or paint.

If the noise sticks around after that, try another hinge. Doors often have two or three, and the loud one isn't always the obvious one.

## Why this kind of fix is worth your five minutes

Small irritations add up. A squeak, a sticky drawer, a wobbly handle: none of them is a real emergency, but together they make a home feel a bit more frustrating than it needs to be.

The nice part is that these fixes are cheap and fast. You don't need a weekend or a contractor. That makes them especially friendly for renters, since lubricating a hinge doesn't change anything about the space you'd need to answer to a landlord for.

## Do a 10-minute "stop ignoring this" walkthrough

The squeaky door is probably not your only tolerated annoyance. Here's a quick way to find the rest.

### Walk the house with a notepad

Go room by room and use your hands and ears, not just your eyes. Open every door and drawer. Turn every handle. Note anything that squeaks, sticks, rubs, or wobbles.

### Sort the list by effort

Mark each item as a two-minute fix or a "needs a trip to the store" fix. Do the two-minute ones that same day. Momentum matters, and crossing off three things feels great.

### Pick one product per problem

This is where roundups like Buzzfeed's are useful for browsing, since they show how many everyday annoyances have a purpose-built fix. Just match the product to your actual problem rather than buying a whole cart's worth. Start with the one thing that bugs you most.

## A few habits that keep the list short

- **Fix it when you notice it.** The squeak you hear today will still be there in six months, and you'll be more used to it.
- **Keep one small kit in a drawer.** A lubricant, a rag, and a screwdriver cover a surprising number of quick jobs.
- **Do a seasonal check.** When you swap your decor for a new season, take five minutes to run through doors and drawers again.

## The takeaway

Pick the one thing in your home you've stopped noticing because you've gotten used to it, and fix it this week. If it's the squeaky door, a few minutes with a lubricant pen and a rag is all it takes. Then enjoy the quiet.
