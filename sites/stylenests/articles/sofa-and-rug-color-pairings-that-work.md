A sofa and a rug are two of the biggest purchases in a living room, and they have to get along. Pick wrong and the room feels off, even if you can't say why. Here are five pairings that reliably work, per Livingetc's roundup, plus a simple way to think about color so you can adapt them to the sofa you already own.

## The Real Rule: Don't Match, Make Them Talk

The main idea behind all five pairings is that you don't need your rug and sofa to match exactly. A perfect match often looks flat. What matters more is:

- **Undertones:** Are both pieces leaning warm (yellow, red, cream) or cool (blue, gray)?
- **Warmth:** Do they feel like they belong in the same room's temperature?
- **Texture:** A nubby rug under a smooth sofa adds depth even when the colors are close.

Keep those three in mind and the pairings below become starting points, not strict recipes.

## 1. Red and Blue

This is the boldest pairing on the list. Think a blue sofa over a red-toned rug, or the reverse. The two colors contrast strongly, which gives the room energy and a confident, collected feel.

**Try it:** Let one piece lead. If the sofa is a deep blue, choose a rug where red is the main color, not an equal split. Then keep walls and other furniture quiet so the pairing is the star.

## 2. Earthy Brown and Muted Olive

This one is calm, grounded, and very much in step with the warmer, richer palettes showing up in interiors right now. A brown sofa with an olive-toned rug (or olive sofa, brown rug) feels like a room that's been lived in and loved.

**Keep it modern, not muddy:** Muted olive and brown are both low in brightness, so add some lift. Lighter wood, a pale throw, or a bit of texture in the rug keeps the room from feeling heavy.

## 3. Ivory and Umber

This is the timeless option. Pair an ivory sofa with a deep umber rug, or flip it with a dark sofa on a creamy rug. The strong light-dark contrast reads as classic and balanced.

**Try it:** If you have kids or pets, think about which piece takes the wear. A darker rug hides more, while a lighter sofa is easier to dress up with cushions. Choose based on which one you're more willing to fuss over.

## 4. Camel, Cognac, and Oatmeal

This is the tonal, cozy choice. All three shades live in the same warm family, so they blend instead of clash. A camel or cognac sofa on an oatmeal rug feels soft and layered.

Because the colors are so close, texture does the heavy lifting here. Look for a chunky weave, a wool-like pile, or a leather sofa against a woven rug. Without texture, a tonal room can fall flat.

## 5. Pink and Brown

This pairing is warmer and more unexpected than it sounds. A dusty pink sofa on a brown rug, or a brown sofa with a pink-toned rug, feels grown-up rather than sweet. The brown anchors the pink and keeps it from looking too sugary.

**Try it:** Go for dusty or muted pinks rather than bright ones. That's where the sophistication comes from.

## How to Choose Based on Mood

Not sure which pairing fits? Start with how you want the room to feel:

- **Bold:** red and blue
- **Calming:** brown and muted olive
- **Timeless:** ivory and umber
- **Tonal and cozy:** camel, cognac, and oatmeal
- **Soft but grown-up:** pink and brown

## Working With the Sofa You Already Have

Most of us are matching a rug to an existing sofa, so here's a quick routine:

1. **Find your sofa's undertone.** Hold a white sheet of paper next to it. Does the sofa look warm (creamy, yellow, red) or cool (gray, blue)?
2. **Pick a pairing above** that fits that family, or deliberately contrast it.
3. **Order a sample or small swatch** if the rug seller offers one, and look at it in your room at day and night. Light changes color a lot.
4. **Check texture.** If both pieces are smooth, choose a rug with more pile or weave.

Rugs are often the cheaper, easier piece to change, so if your sofa is staying put, the rug is where you have room to experiment on a budget.

## Start With One Pairing

Pick the pairing that matches the mood you want, then check undertones before you buy. If the two pieces feel like they belong in the same temperature, and one has some texture, you're most of the way there.
