## Content Guidelines
Write helpful, engaging articles about home décor, organization, small spaces, and everyday living. Use a warm, approachable tone with clear headings, short paragraphs, and practical tips. Include affordable ideas for different home sizes and budgets. Keep headlines accurate and specific, and ensure every article delivers on its promise. Create original content; verify factual claims and avoid invented expert quotes or unsupported product claims.
