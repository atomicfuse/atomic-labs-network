## Bring the Idea Home: Costa Rican-Style Rice and Beans

Gallo pinto, the rice-and-beans staple of Costa Rican cooking, is an easy way to taste the tradition behind the trend. This is a general home-style approach, not a recipe from the Forbes piece, and quantities are flexible.

### Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked rice, ideally day-old and cold
- 1 1/2 cups cooked black beans, with about 1/4 cup of their cooking liquid
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/2 small onion, finely diced
- 1/2 sweet pepper, finely diced
- A handful of chopped cilantro
- Salt, to taste
- Optional: a splash of Worcestershire-style sauce, or Costa Rica's popular bottled brown sauce if you can find it

### Steps

1. Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper for 3 to 4 minutes until soft.
2. Add the beans and their liquid. Simmer for 2 minutes.
3. Stir in the rice, breaking up clumps, and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until everything is evenly colored and the liquid is absorbed.
4. Season with salt and optional sauce. Fold in the cilantro just before serving.
5. Serve with eggs, plantains, or whatever you have on hand.

The point is the same one the La Fortuna trend makes: simple, local-rooted food, made with care, can be the main event.
