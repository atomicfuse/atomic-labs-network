## What's Happening on Nov. 19

According to Fox Business, the Original Pantry Cafe will reopen with all profits going to support Hope the Mission's homelessness programs. The reporting also says the diner will keep its classic recipes and bring back longtime union employees.

### Simple Diner-Style Pancakes

1. Whisk 1 1/2 cups flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, 1 tablespoon baking powder, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
2. In another bowl, beat 1 egg with 1 1/4 cups milk and 3 tablespoons melted butter.
3. Stir the wet mixture into the dry until just combined. Lumps are fine.
4. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
5. Cook 1/4 cup portions on a buttered griddle over medium heat until bubbles form, then flip and cook another minute or two.

Serve with butter and warm syrup. If you want to make it count, consider donating to a local homelessness service while you eat.
