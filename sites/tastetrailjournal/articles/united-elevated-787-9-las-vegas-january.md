## A Simple At-Home Recipe for the Trip Home

If you want to stretch the weekend, here is an approachable idea inspired by the classic steakhouse-style dinner many travelers associate with Vegas: a pan-seared steak with garlic butter.

**You'll need:** one steak (about 1 inch thick), salt, pepper, a neutral oil, 2 tablespoons butter, 2 smashed garlic cloves, and a few thyme sprigs.

1. Pat the steak dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a heavy skillet over high heat with a thin film of oil.
3. Sear the steak about 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare, adjusting for thickness.
4. Lower the heat, add butter, garlic, and thyme, and spoon the foaming butter over the steak for about a minute.
5. Rest the steak for 5 minutes before slicing.

Use a thermometer if you have one; about 130°F to 135°F is a common target for medium-rare.
