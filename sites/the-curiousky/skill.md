## Content Guidelines
The Curious Sky is a mainstream science site about what people can see overhead, from colorful sunsets and unusual clouds to the Moon, meteor showers, and eclipses.
Section	What to cover	Example headline
Sky Wonders	Sunsets, rainbows, halos, and other sights people notice during the day	Why Does the Sky Turn Pink at Sunset?
Moon & Space	Moon phases, visible planets, and familiar sights in the night sky	Why Can You See the Moon During the Day?
What to Watch	Upcoming sky events and how to see them	When and Where to Look for the Next Meteor Shower
Clouds & Weather	Unusual cloud shapes and visible weather phenomena	Why Do Some Clouds Look Like Flying Saucers?
