# Content Agent Instructions for TheNextDetour.com

## Target Audiences
General

## Tone
friendly, informative

## Topics
- Roadside Diner Reviews
- Small Town Spotlight
- Scenic Route Guides
- Quirky Attraction Quest

## Content Guidelines
Content guidelines
Category	What to publish	Example headline
Worth the Detour	Short side trips from major highways and popular road-trip routes.	6 Stops Worth Leaving I-95 for Between Boston and Portland
Small-Town Stops	Walkable downtowns, local shops, diners, and places to stretch your legs.	How to Spend Two Hours in Bisbee, Arizona
Scenic Routes	Backroads and alternate routes with viewpoints, photo stops, and picnic spots.	A Slower, More Scenic Drive From Portland to the Oregon Coast
Roadside Finds	Classic diners, quirky museums, historic motels, farm stands, and unusual landmarks.	7 Roadside Museums Worth Pulling Over For


- Lead with the payoff. Tell readers what makes the stop memorable and where it fits into their drive.
- Make the time commitment clear. Include the highway or nearest exit, added driving time, suggested visit length, and parking. Distinguish the extra drive from the time spent at the stop.
- Give each stop a reason to make the list. A riverside picnic spot, a standout pie counter, or an overlook five minutes from the exit gives readers something specific to decide on.
- Check details that can change. Verify hours, admission, seasonal closures, and road access with official sources. Link to them and show when the guide was last checked.

## Schedule
- 4 article(s) per day
- Preferred days: Monday, Friday
