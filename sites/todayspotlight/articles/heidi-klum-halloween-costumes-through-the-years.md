## Steal the Klum Method for Your Own Halloween

You don't need a team of artists to use her approach. Three habits show up across her costume history:

1. **Commit to one idea.** Every look on the list has a clear concept, from Betty Boop to a robot.
2. **Pick something with a strong visual identity.** Characters like Jessica Rabbit or Cleopatra are recognizable at a glance.
3. **Surprise people.** Going as an old lady or as yourself is funnier because it's unexpected.

Start with a costume idea you can explain in three words, then spend your effort on the details that make it unmistakable.
