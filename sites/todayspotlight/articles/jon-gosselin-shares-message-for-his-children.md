## Why a Public Message Is a Complicated Thing

Reaching out through a television interview is different from a private phone call or letter. Here's what that difference can mean. This section is our analysis, not anything Jon or his children have said.

- **It's one-sided by design.** A broadcast lets one person speak and gives the other no room to respond in the moment.
- **It puts family business in front of an audience.** Adult children who have set boundaries may not welcome being addressed in public.
- **It can read differently to different people.** Some viewers will hear sincerity. Others will hear a performance. Only the people involved know which, if either, is true.

None of that means the message was insincere. It simply shows why public gestures rarely replace the slow, private work of rebuilding trust.
