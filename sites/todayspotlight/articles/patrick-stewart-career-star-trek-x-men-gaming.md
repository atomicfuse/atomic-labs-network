## One Actor, Several Fan Communities

Most beloved actors are tied to one franchise. Stewart's career, as Nintendolife frames it, reached across sci-fi, superhero media and gaming, making him a cross-generational figure.

That means people are likely remembering him very differently, depending on how they met him:

- **Star Trek fans** know him as Jean-Luc Picard.
- **X-Men fans** know him as Professor X.
- **Gamers** may know him from his voice work, including the Elder Scrolls series, which Nintendolife specifically mentions.

A parent who grew up on one of those and a kid who found him through another can both claim him as theirs.
