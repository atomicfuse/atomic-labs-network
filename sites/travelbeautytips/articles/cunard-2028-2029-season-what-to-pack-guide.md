Cunard has unveiled its 2028-2029 season, and if you like to plan a wardrobe the way other people plan an itinerary, you now have plenty of runway. Per Travelpulse, the line announced 93 voyages across 104 destinations in 49 countries, led by Queen Mary 2's 500th Transatlantic Crossing. Also on the list: a World Voyage on Queen Elizabeth, South America sailings timed with Rio Carnival, and chances to visit 64 UNESCO sites.

This guide doesn't try to cover every sailing. It uses the three headline experiences to show you how to think about packing and personal style for a milestone voyage, so that when you book, your suitcase strategy is already half done.

## Start With the Voyage, Not the Closet

The three standout sailings ask very different things of your wardrobe:

- **Queen Mary 2's 500th Transatlantic Crossing:** a milestone sailing on a classic ocean-crossing route.
- **Queen Elizabeth's World Voyage:** a long itinerary touching many destinations.
- **South America during Rio Carnival:** a festive, high-energy stop on the calendar.

Pick the one that matches your travel personality, then build backward. Details like specific dates, ports, and onboard dress codes weren't part of the announcement coverage, so check Cunard's own materials before you finalize anything. What you can do now is set the style direction.

## The Transatlantic Crossing: Dress for the Occasion

A 500th crossing is a celebration, and the tone of a milestone like this suggests you'll want at least a few pieces that feel special. That's our read, not an announced dress code, so confirm the evening attire guidance for your sailing when you book.

A practical approach to a crossing:

1. **Build around one formal look you love.** One excellent outfit beats three so-so ones. Choose something that photographs well and doesn't wrinkle easily.
2. **Plan for layers.** Ocean crossings mean time on deck, so pack a wrap, a tailored coat, or a lightweight jacket that works over both day and evening outfits.
3. **Pack for a mix of sea days.** Expect long stretches onboard. Comfortable, polished daywear matters more than novelty pieces.
4. **Choose shoes in pairs that do double duty.** One dressy pair, one walking pair, and one deck-friendly option cover most situations.

## The World Voyage: Capsule Wardrobe Rules

A World Voyage on Queen Elizabeth means many ports, and that's where a capsule wardrobe earns its keep. Since we don't have the itinerary details from the announcement, treat this as a framework rather than a forecast.

### Pick a tight color palette
Choose two or three base colors plus one accent. Every top should work with every bottom. This is the single biggest space-saver for a long trip.

### Prioritize fabrics that recover
Knits, wrinkle-resistant blends, and fabrics that shake out after being folded will serve you better than delicate pieces that need pressing. Ask whether the ship offers laundry services before you decide how much to bring.

### Leave room for what you'll buy
A long voyage with many destinations means souvenirs. Pack your bag about 80 percent full on departure.

## Rio Carnival: Pack Color, Comfort, and Sun Protection

The South America sailings coincide with Rio Carnival, which changes the packing equation entirely. Think warm-weather, high-energy, and long days on your feet. Specific port schedules weren't detailed in the coverage, so verify timing with Cunard.

For a Carnival-timed trip, prioritize:

- **Breathable fabrics** in bold colors or prints that suit the festive mood.
- **Broken-in walking shoes.** Never debut new footwear on a trip built around events and crowds.
- **A crossbody bag with a secure closure.** Hands-free and zipped is the smart choice in busy settings.
- **Sun essentials:** a wide-brim hat, sunglasses, and sunscreen you've already tested on your skin.
- **Quick-dry pieces** for humid days and evening changes.

## Why Packing Early Pays Off

Cunard is announcing this season years ahead, which gives you time most trips never allow. Use it well:

- **Audit your formalwear now.** If you'd need to buy or tailor something, you can shop sales and book fittings without pressure.
- **Test your beauty routine.** Try travel-size versions of skincare and haircare at home, especially if you're heading for sun and humidity.
- **Break in shoes gradually.** Start wearing the pairs you plan to bring.
- **Build a running list.** Keep a note on your phone and add to it whenever you notice something you wished you'd packed on an earlier trip.

## A Note on the UNESCO Stops

The season offers opportunities to visit 64 UNESCO sites, per the announcement. Many heritage sites involve walking, uneven surfaces, and sometimes dress expectations, which vary by location. Pack at least one outfit that's comfortable for a full day of sightseeing and modest enough to adapt if you need it. A light scarf handles most of those situations and takes almost no space.

## Your Next Move

Pick the one voyage that makes you want to start planning, then write down the three outfits you'd wear for its biggest moment. Everything else in your suitcase can be built around those three.
