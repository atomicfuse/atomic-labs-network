Fall isn’t just about pumpkin spice; it’s the season for cozy gatherings and delicious apple cider cocktails. Impress your friends with three unique cocktails using a concentrated apple cider syrup, a homemade ingredient that elevates your drinks and pays homage to the essence of autumn. Let’s dive into how to prepare this syrup and the cocktails that you'll want to serve all season long.

## Making the Concentrated Apple Cider Syrup
To kick off your cocktail crafting journey, you'll need to whip up a batch of concentrated apple cider syrup. This syrup packs a punch of flavor that forms the backbone of your cocktails. Here’s how to make it:

### Ingredients:
- 4 cups fresh, unfiltered apple juice
- 1 cinnamon stick
- A pinch of salt

### Instructions:
1. **Reduce the Apple Juice**: In a saucepan, combine the apple juice, cinnamon stick, and salt. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
2. **Cook Down**: Allow the mixture to simmer for about 60 to 90 minutes, stirring occasionally. You’re looking for a 75% reduction, so you should end up with about 1 cup of syrup.
3. **Finish Up**: Once thickened and fragrant, let it cool. Remember, the unfiltered appearance is intentional, adding both flavor and depth to your cocktails.

## 1. Apple Old Fashioned
A delightful twist on the classic, this cocktail is perfect for bourbon lovers. The concentrated syrup nicely complements the spirit's richness.

### Ingredients:
- 2 oz bourbon
- 0.5 oz concentrated apple cider syrup
- A few dashes of bitters

### Instructions:
1. In a mixing glass, combine the bourbon, syrup, and bitters with ice.
2. Stir well until chilled.
3. Strain into a glass with fresh ice and garnish with an apple slice or orange twist.

## 2. Upstate Manhattan
This cocktail brings a hint of elegance to any fall soirée. The balance between the rye and syrup creates a rich flavor profile that's sure to please.

### Ingredients:
- 2 oz rye whiskey
- 0.75 oz sweet vermouth
- 0.25 oz concentrated apple cider syrup
- A few dashes of bitters

### Instructions:
1. Combine all the ingredients in a mixing glass with ice.
2. Stir well to chill before straining into a chilled cocktail glass.
3. Garnish with a cherry or a twist of lemon peel.

## 3. Kingston Windfall
Feeling adventurous? This cocktail mixes Jamaican rum with an apple twist for a tropical fall flavor that your guests won't forget.

### Ingredients:
- 1.5 oz Jamaican rum
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.75 oz concentrated apple cider syrup
- 0.25 oz honey syrup (made with a 3:1 ratio of honey to water)
- A few dashes of bitters

### Instructions:
1. Shake all ingredients with ice in a cocktail shaker until well chilled.
2. Strain into a glass filled with ice and garnish with a lemon wheel.

## Bonus Dessert Option
If you find yourself with extra syrup, drizzle it over vanilla ice cream for a delicious dessert alternative! The sweet and spicy notes will keep the autumn flavors alive right to the last bite.

With these three apple cider cocktails, you’ll turn any gathering into a festive fall affair. Whether you’re hosting friends over or spending a cozy evening at home, these beverages will surely impress. So, roll up your sleeves, brew that syrup, and toast to a delightful autumn season!
