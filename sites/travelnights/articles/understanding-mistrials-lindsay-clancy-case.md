### What Is a Mistrial?
A mistrial occurs when a court trial cannot reach a verdict due to various reasons, such as a hung jury—in this case, jurors unable to agree on a verdict. This situation is particularly intricate as it raises questions about the efficacy of the trial and the administration of justice. A hung jury does not imply that the accused is acquitted; it merely acknowledges that the prosecution couldn’t meet the evidential burden required for conviction.
