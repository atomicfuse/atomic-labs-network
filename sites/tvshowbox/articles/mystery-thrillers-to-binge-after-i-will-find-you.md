With the thrilling buzz surrounding Harlan Coben's *I Will Find You*, mystery lovers are scrambling for their next binge-worthy series. This summer's guilty pleasure has fans pondering the next edge-of-your-seat experience that can keep them guessing. Fear not! Here’s a list of the best thrillers that echo the suspenseful essence of Coben's work, complete with twisty plots, wrongly accused characters, and the secrets that bind families.

## 1. *Safe* (2018)
A gripping series that dives into the disappearance of a teenage girl, *Safe* finds our protagonist navigating a labyrinth of secrets in a seemingly perfect neighborhood. When you're done untangling the mystery in *I Will Find You*, this David E. Kelly gem will satisfy your craving for intrigue. A pinch of suspense with British flair? Yes, please!

## 2. *Defending Jacob* (2020)
Engaging, darkly emotional, and rife with courtroom drama, *Defending Jacob* explores the boundaries a parent would cross to protect their child. When a murder investigation puts a teenager at the center, the chilling journey will make you second-guess everything. It’s a courtroom thriller that sparks those self-reflective questions—how far would you go if your child were wrongly accused?

## 3. *Presumed Innocent* (2024)
This new adaptation of Scott Turow’s best-selling novel pulls viewers into a multi-layered mystery where a prosecutor is accused of murdering his colleague. It carries an enticing weight of suspense that will have viewers ensnared in the narrative, making you question the moral bounds of justice.

## 4. *Cape Fear* (2026)
A contemporary twist on the classic psychological thriller, *Cape Fear* explores themes of vengeance and obsession as a family is hunted down by a deranged ex-convict. The dark tonal shifts and unraveling mystery make it a thrilling ride that pairs well with the family-centered tension of *I Will Find You*.

## 5. *The Undoing* (2020)
Starring Nicole Kidman and Hugh Grant, *The Undoing* unfolds around a murder investigation that turns a couple's seemingly perfect life upside down. The suspense drips with clever plot twists, showcasing the fragility of relationships amidst uncovering dark truths—definitely a watch if you enjoyed the emotional stakes in Coben's narrative.

## 6. *Last Seen* (2026)
A timely release this year, *Last Seen* zeroes in on the search for a missing child, forcing characters to navigate their past and present connections as they uncover the fractured web of secrets around them. This series captures the essence of the gripping emotional pull that Coben fans adore.
