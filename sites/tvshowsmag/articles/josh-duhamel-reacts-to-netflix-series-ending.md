Netflix has put a brand on *Ransom Canyon*, and one of its stars isn't pretending to love it. Josh Duhamel, 53, said he was surprised and "a little upset" to learn the streamer won't renew the neo-Western romance for a third season, according to the Daily Mail's report.

The series wraps after two seasons, and Duhamel's reaction centers on a familiar streaming-era complaint: the show seemed to be doing fine, so why the exit?

## What Duhamel Said

Per the Daily Mail, Duhamel admitted he was caught off guard by the news, pointing to the show's strong early performance and the support from fans. That's the core of his frustration, as reported: a series that looked like it had momentum still got the axe.

The report also highlights how closely Duhamel connected with the cowboy role, and what a third season might have explored for his character, Staten, and Quinn. Fans see a show with buzz, a loyal audience and a recognizable star, and conclude it's a hit. Streaming platforms, by contrast, weigh viewership data against what a show costs and what it brings in. We don't have Netflix's internal math here, so it's hard to know where *Ransom Canyon* landed. ## The Yellowstone Shadow

The Daily Mail frames *Ransom Canyon* as a *Yellowstone* knock-off, and the label tells you something about why the show existed. *Yellowstone*'s cultural impact left a clear appetite for Western-style dramas, and streamers have been eager to feed it.

But *Ransom Canyon* leaned toward the romantic side of the genre, a cozier, relationship-driven take on cowboy country rather than a clone of the Dutton family's power struggles. Whether that made it a worthy successor or a pleasant side road is a debate fans can settle in the comments. The pitch, at least, was distinct enough that a third season felt like a fair ask.
