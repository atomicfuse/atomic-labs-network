Taylor Swift just became the youngest inductee ever into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame, and she used part of the moment to settle an old score. During her induction speech, Swift joked that she's been "estranged" from Brad Paisley for nearly 20 years, all because he filled her tour bus with live crickets during a 2007 tour prank, per TMZ.

Yes, crickets. Live ones. On a bus.

## The Prank, Explained for Newer Swifties

Here's what the reporting gives us: the incident dates to a 2007 tour, Paisley was the prankster, and the target was Swift's tour bus. That's the whole confirmed record, and honestly, it's enough. The details beyond that (how many crickets, how long the cleanup took, whether anyone screamed) weren't part of the report, so we'll leave those to your imagination, which is probably worse than reality anyway.

The "estrangement" is, to be clear, a bit. Swift framed it as a nearly two-decade rift, but the tone was humor, not heartbreak. TMZ's own framing leaned on the obvious pun, noting she's no stranger to "Bad Blood." It's hard not to admire the economy of that: a joke about a feud, delivered at a ceremony celebrating her songwriting, referencing a song title that is itself about a feud.

Induction speeches tend to run on gratitude and gravitas. Swift's detour into bus-based insect warfare did something smarter: it reminded the room where she came from. In 2007, she was a country artist on the road, learning the rituals of tour culture, which apparently include getting pranked by the established guys.

That's the contrast that makes this moment work. On one side, you have a petty, silly, very human tour story. On the other, you have the youngest inductee in the Hall's history.
