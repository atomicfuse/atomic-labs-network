## What to Look For in Satellite Imagery

When scouring satellite images for potential craters, you can look for specific features that could indicate an impact:

1. **Circular Patterns**: Many impact craters appear as distinct circular shapes. This is often due to the shockwaves traveling outward, which creates a perfect round formation.
   
2. **Surrounding Topography**: Check for raised areas or peaks around the perimeter of the circular pattern. These may represent the results of the material being displaced upon impact.
   
3. **Color Variation**: Sometimes, cratered terrain will show different color shades compared to surrounding areas, indicating alteration in soil composition or plant life due to the impact.
   
4. **Geological Context**: Understanding the geological history of the area can provide additional clues. Areas known for ancient volcanic activity or tectonic shifts may be more likely to host hidden craters.
