## Understanding Eclipse Layers in Plain English

This map isn't just for seasoned astronomers; it’s designed for anyone eager to witness the eclipse. Here's a breakdown of some of the layers you'll encounter:
- **Eclipse Path**: The line showing where the total eclipse can be observed—if you're outside this area, you'll see a partial eclipse.
- **Umbra**: The black area on the map signifies total darkness during the eclipse.
- **Penumbra**: Surrounding the umbra, this area denotes where a partial eclipse can be observed.
- **Shadow Views**: This layer presents a 3D model illustrating how the shadow travels across the Earth, enhancing visualization of the event.
