## What to Do If You Find Kittens
If you ever find kittens in a public place, here are a few steps you can take to ensure their safety:

1. **Assess the Situation**: Determine if the kittens appear to be abandoned or if a mother cat is nearby. If the mother is present, it’s usually best to leave the kittens alone, as she may be returning soon.
   
2. **Contact Local Rescue Groups**: If it's clear that the kittens need help, reach out to local animal rescues. Organizations often have the resources to foster the kittens and find them loving homes.
   
3. **Provide Immediate Care**: If you're able, you can place the kittens in a safe, warm space while waiting for help. Make sure they are protected from the elements and any potential dangers.

4. **Document Your Findings**: Taking pictures and documenting the location can help rescuers understand the context of the rescue.
