## 10. Masters of the Air
Released as a follow-up to the acclaimed *Band of Brothers*, *Masters of the Air* explores the harrowing experiences of American bomber crews during World War II. This limited series combines gripping combat sequences with a deep dive into the psychological toll of war, making it a must-watch for anyone interested in WWII narratives. 

## 9. Das Boot
Based on the novel by Lothar-Günther Buchheim, *Das Boot* delivers an intense portrayal of life aboard a German submarine during WWII. The series skillfully balances high-stakes naval battles with personal drama, effectively showcasing the harrowing experiences of its characters while complicating the viewer's perspective on heroism and duty. 

## 8. Rome
While not strictly a war show, *Rome* encapsulates the social and political intricacies of ancient warfare, offering insights into the lives of soldiers and leaders alike. Its rich storytelling intertwines battlefield action with the politics of power, making it not just a series about war, but about the lengths people will go to claim and maintain control. 

## 7. Tour of Duty
This series set during the Vietnam War focuses on the soldiers’ experiences both on the battlefield and during their downtime. *Tour of Duty* is remarkable for its character-driven narratives and its honest exploration of the moral complexities faced by soldiers in combat, providing a sobering look at the realities of war.
