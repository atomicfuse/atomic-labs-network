If you're in Morgantown for West Virginia's Big 12 game against Houston, you can fit in a hike before kickoff. Four spots stand out, per Fox News: Coopers Rock State Forest, Deckers Creek Rail Trail, Core Arboretum, and Valley Falls State Park. The right pick depends on how much time you have, how far you want to stray from campus, and what the sky is doing.

I can't give you a forecast for game day here. Check the National Weather Service forecast for Morgantown (weather.gov) close to your trip, and look at it again the morning you go. This guide covers which trail fits which situation, and what weather details should change your plan.

## Why Late Fall Works for Hiking Here

Fox News argues that late autumn is a good time to explore Morgantown's Appalachian trails, river gorges, waterfalls, and campus-accessible nature. The trade-off is that November weather in the mountains can change quickly. Plan for cool temperatures and the possibility of rain, and dress for the trail rather than the stadium.

## Coopers Rock State Forest: The Big-View Hike

This is the headliner. The Fox News piece points to Raven Rock Trail at Coopers Rock State Forest for views of the Cheat River Canyon.

**Best for:** Fans with a few hours who want the scenery they came for.

**Weather angle:** Overlook hikes depend on visibility. Fog or low cloud can wipe out the payoff, so check the forecast for sky cover before you drive out. A clear morning is the best case. If the forecast calls for fog or steady rain, consider a different stop from this list and save the canyon for another day.

## Deckers Creek Rail Trail: The Easy, Flexible Option

A rail trail is a former rail line converted to a path, which generally means a gentler walk than a mountain trail. It's a sensible choice if you want to move your legs without committing to a big outing.

**Best for:** Mixed groups, a shorter window before the game, or anyone who wants a low-effort walk.

**Weather angle:** With flexible mileage, you can turn around whenever clouds darken. Keep a light rain layer in your bag and you can usually stay out longer.

## Core Arboretum: Nature Close to Campus

Fox News lists Core Arboretum among the campus-accessible nature options. If your schedule is tight or you don't want to leave the WVU area, this is the low-friction choice.

**Best for:** A short walk, a quick break from tailgate logistics, or a backup when the weather turns.

**Weather angle:** Staying near campus means shelter, food, and your car are close if a shower rolls in.

## Valley Falls State Park: Waterfalls

Fox News highlights the park's waterfalls. Falls are a good fit for fall trips, but remember that wet weather affects them in two ways: more water can make the scene more dramatic, and it also makes rocks and trails slick.

**Best for:** Photo stops and fans who want something different from a ridge view.

**Weather angle:** After rain, assume footing is slippery near water. Wear shoes with real tread, not game-day sneakers.

## Match the Trail to Your Day

- **Half a day or more:** Coopers Rock State Forest.
- **A quick walk near town:** Core Arboretum.
- **Easy, adjustable mileage:** Deckers Creek Rail Trail.
- **Waterfalls and photos:** Valley Falls State Park.

This is my own sorting, not a ranking from the source. The Fox News article doesn't give mileage, difficulty ratings, or drive times in the material I have, so check each park's official page for trail conditions, hours, and any closures before you go.

## A Simple Weather Checklist Before You Go

1. **Check the NWS forecast** for Morgantown the night before and again that morning.
2. **Look at the hourly breakdown**, not just the daily high. Rain that clears by 10 a.m. is a very different day from rain at noon.
3. **Plan your hike earlier than you think.** Finish with a buffer before kickoff so you aren't rushing to the stadium muddy and late.
4. **Layer up.** Cool mornings can warm up, and a thin waterproof shell weighs little.
5. **Pick footwear for wet leaves and rock.** Fallen leaves hide slick spots.
6. **Tell someone your plan** if you're heading to a more remote trail, and check the sunset time so you're off the trail well before dark.

## The Takeaway

Pick your trail based on the forecast, not just the view. Check the Morgantown forecast the morning of the game, choose the spot that fits your time window, and keep Core Arboretum or Deckers Creek Rail Trail as your backup if the weather turns.
