## A quick useful answer while you're here

A 40% chance of rain doesn't mean it will rain 40% of the day. In US National Weather Service forecasts, it is the probability that any given point in the forecast area sees measurable rain during the forecast period. At 40%, bring the umbrella if you'll be outside for long stretches, but don't cancel the plan.

### How to check your own forecast

1. Use your national weather service for forecasts and alerts: the National Weather Service in the US, or the Met Office in the UK.
2. Look at the hourly view, not just the daily summary, to find dry windows.
3. Check for active warnings or advisories before travel, and note the time zone and when the forecast was last updated.
4. Recheck the morning of your plans, since forecasts shift.
