## Content Guidelines
Content Guidelines
Editorial focus: WeatherSidekick.com covers everyday weather and how it affects readers’ plans, travel, homes, and routines. Every article should answer a question people actually ask or help them make a decision.
Section	What to publish	Example headline
Weather News	Timely weather events and what they mean for affected areas	An Early Cold Snap Is Coming to the Midwest. Here’s What to Expect
Everyday Weather	Quick answers to common weather questions	What Does a 40% Chance of Rain Mean?
Travel & Outdoors	Weather considerations for road trips, beach days, and outdoor plans	How Wind Can Change Your Beach Day Plans
Home & Seasons	Practical, seasonal stories for the home and garden	When Should You Cover Your Plants Before a Frost?
Weather Explained	Plain-English explanations of familiar weather patterns	Why Does Humidity Make It Feel Hotter?


- Tone: Friendly, clear, and practical. Explain weather terms in plain English as they come up.
- Headlines: Be specific and deliver on the promise. Mention the location when a story applies to a particular region.
- Structure: Give the answer or main takeaway in the opening paragraph. Use short paragraphs, descriptive subheadings, and bullets for practical steps.
- Sources: Use the relevant official weather service for forecasts and alerts. For time-sensitive stories, include the affected area, time zone, and when the information was last checked. Link to the official advisory when one is active.
- Publishing mix: Build around seasonal guides and evergreen explainers, then add timely weather stories when they affect your readers.

## Schedule
