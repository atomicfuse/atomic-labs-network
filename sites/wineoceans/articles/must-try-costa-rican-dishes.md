Costa Rica’s culinary scene is rich with fresh ingredients and local traditions. If you’re planning a trip to this stunning country, there are several dishes you shouldn’t miss. Here’s a guide to seven must-try foods that capture the essence of Costa Rican cuisine.

## 1. Ceviche
Ceviche is a refreshing dish that showcases the fresh seafood available along Costa Rica’s coastlines. Typically made with fish like tilapia or snapper, it’s marinated in lime juice with onions, cilantro, and peppers. Best enjoyed at a local beachside restaurant, this dish embodies the ocean's freshness with every bite.

## 2. Gallo Pinto
No Costa Rican breakfast is complete without gallo pinto, a delicious blend of rice and black beans. This iconic dish is often seasoned with a splash of salsa Lizano, an essential condiment in many Costa Rican homes. Served with eggs, avocado, and sometimes fried plantains, gallo pinto is comfort food you’ll find at every soda (local diner).

## 3. Patacones
These twice-fried green plantains are a beloved snack in Costa Rica. Crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, patacones are usually served with a sprinkle of salt and a side of guacamole or salsa. Perfect as an appetizer or a quick bite on the go, they’re available in many beachfront eateries.

## 4. Heart of Palm Salad
Heart of palm salad showcases one of Costa Rica's unique ingredients. The hearts provide a refreshing crunch and mild flavor, typically tossed with fresh vegetables and a zesty vinaigrette. This salad is often seen on menus in fancier restaurants and is a great way to taste the country’s fresh produce.

## 5. Batidos
A batido is a must-try drink that combines fresh fruit, milk, and ice. These smoothies come in various flavors, from mango to passion fruit, providing a sweet and refreshing treat after a hot day of exploring. Grab one from a local fruit stand or a cafe to invigorate your palate!

## 6. Rice and Beans
Celebrated for its simplicity, Costa Rican rice and beans are often served as a side dish or as part of a larger meal. The beans are typically cooked with garlic, onions, and spices, creating a hearty accompaniment to many dishes. Don’t underestimate this dish; it’s a comforting staple that brings people together.

## 7. Casado
Casado is a plate that represents a little bit of everything, making it perfect for those wanting to sample various flavors. It typically includes rice, beans, a protein (like grilled chicken or fish), a salad, and fried plantains all served on one plate. This is a favorite among locals and is commonly served for lunch at sodas, embodying the farm-to-table ethos of Costa Rican cuisine.

Visiting Costa Rica not only exposes you to lush landscapes and friendly locals but immerses you in a vibrant food culture that emphasizes freshness, sustainability, and flavor. Make it a point to try these seven dishes to experience the country through its food. Your taste buds will thank you!
