You can enjoy Halloween without being scared once. By the end of this piece, you'll have a simple plan for a cozy, low-spook October built around two small, cheerful buys: a "Spooky Cutie" coloring book and a pair of embroidered Halloween socks.

The idea comes from a BuzzFeed shopping roundup titled "35 Cute Fall And Halloween Products For Anyone Who Does Not Care To Be Spooked, Thank You Very Much." The full roundup has 35 items. The details available to us cover two of them, so those are the two we'll focus on, plus a practical way to use them.

## The "Spooky Cutie" coloring book: the easiest cozy win

A coloring book is a good fit for a scaredy-cat Halloween because the activity itself is calm. You pick the colors, you set the pace, and nothing jumps out at you. The "Spooky Cutie" title signals the tone BuzzFeed is going for: seasonal imagery with a friendly, playful spin instead of horror.

How to get the most out of it:

- **Make it a ritual.** Pick one evening a week in October and treat it as your wind-down time.
- **Keep the setup simple.** A flat surface, a decent light, and whatever pencils or markers you already own are enough.
- **Share it.** Coloring pages work well as a low-pressure activity with friends or family who also prefer their Halloween gentle.

## Embroidered Halloween socks: seasonal spirit you can wear

The second item BuzzFeed highlights is embroidered Halloween socks. They're a small way to join in without committing to decorations, costumes, or anything that might startle you at the door.

Why socks work for a low-spook approach:

- **They're subtle.** You get a festive detail that's mostly for you.
- **They're cozy by nature.** They pair naturally with blankets and a warm drink.
- **They make easy gifts.** If someone in your life dislikes scary stuff, a cute pair is a safer bet than a gory prop.

## Put them together: a scaredy-cat self-care night

One of the angles around this niche is a Halloween self-care night, and these two items build one easily. This is our suggestion rather than anything from the original post:

1. Pull on the embroidered socks.
2. Set up your coloring book somewhere comfortable.
3. Choose something gentle to listen to or watch in the background.
4. Color until you feel done, not until the page is finished.

That's the whole plan. It's cheap, it takes minutes to set up, and it asks nothing of you except a bit of quiet.

Seasonal content tends to skew creepy once October arrives, and plenty of people simply don't enjoy that. A cute, cozy approach to Halloween is a perfectly valid way to celebrate fall. ## Shopping tips for a low-spook season

- **Check the vibe before you buy.** Look for words like "cute," "cozy," or "cutie" in product names and descriptions, and skim the images for anything gory.
- **Start small.** One coloring book and one pair of socks cost far less than redoing your whole home.
- **Buy early.** Seasonal items tend to be picked over as the holiday gets closer, so don't wait until the last week of October.
- **Open the full roundup.** BuzzFeed's list has 35 picks, so there are plenty more cozy options beyond these two.

## The takeaway

You don't have to be scared to have a good Halloween. Grab a coloring book and a pair of cute socks, set aside one quiet evening, and call it a season.
