With busy schedules demanding our attention and long hours often spent sitting down, incorporating movement into your day can feel challenging. That's where 'exercise snacks' come in — short bursts of exercise that can be seamlessly added to your routine. Pilates instructor Vanessa Michielon offers a fantastic five-minute workout that targets your core, shoulders, and glutes, designed specifically for those tight on time but wanting to stay active. Let's dive into this quick routine that you can do from anywhere!

## The 5-Minute Core Workout
This workout consists of four effective movements that will activate your core strength while also working your glutes and shoulders. Not only do these exercises require minimal space, but they also don’t need any special equipment. Here’s how to do each movement:

### 1. Plank Knee Drives  
**Reps:** 5 on each side  
Begin in a plank position with your arms straight and your body in a straight line from head to heels. Drive your right knee towards your chest, then return it to the starting position. Alternate sides for a total of five reps per leg. This movement is great for engaging the core and warming up the shoulders.

### 2. Kneeling Forearm Foldover with Leg Extension  
**Reps:** 10 on each side  
Come into a kneeling position and lower your forearms to the ground. Extend your right leg straight back while squeezing your glutes. Hold for a moment, then lower your leg back down. Repeat with the left leg. This exercise focuses on the glutes and core stability.

### 3. Kneeling Side Kick  
**Reps:** 8 sweeps, 10 pulses, 8 bicycles each side  
Stay in a kneeling position for this one. Extend your right leg to the side, sweeping it forward and back in a controlled motion for eight reps. Follow this with 10 small pulses upwards and finish with eight bicycle movements. Switch to the left side after completing the set. This dynamic movement targets the obliques and increases hip flexibility while engaging the core.

### 4. Side Plank with Rotation  
**Reps:** 8 on each side + an 8-second hold  
Transition into a side plank with your feet stacked or staggered for balance. From here, rotate your torso down and reach your top arm underneath your body, then return to the starting position. Once you finish eight repetitions, hold the side plank for an additional eight seconds to strengthen your obliques and shoulders.

## Why This Workout is Effective
In just five minutes, you’ll not only get a fantastic core workout but also break up your day’s sit-time, which has been shown to benefit overall health. Such movement breaks can improve focus, productivity, and energy — who wouldn’t want that?

## Making It Your Own
Feel free to modify these exercises to fit your fitness level. If you're a beginner, you might want to reduce the reps or take short breaks between movements. For a more challenging workout, speed up your movements or add in a few extra sets. Remember, the goal is to listen to your body and make adjustments as needed.
