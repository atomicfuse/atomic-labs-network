## What reformer Pilates is

A reformer is a sliding carriage with springs, straps, and a footbar. You push and pull against spring resistance while moving through controlled exercises. The springs can make an exercise easier or harder, which is why beginners and experienced movers can share the same equipment.

Expect a focus on slow, precise movement rather than speed. If you've done yoga, the mindset will feel familiar: move deliberately, breathe steadily, and notice what each muscle is doing.

## How to prepare for the Nov. 6 open house

An open house is a low-pressure way to test a studio before committing. A few steps will help you use it well.

### 1. Go with specific questions

Bring a short list. Good ones include:

- Do you offer a beginner or introductory class?
- How large are the classes?
- Can I try a session before buying a package?
- How do you handle injuries or limitations?
- What are the cancellation and booking policies?

### 2. Look at the space

Notice how the reformers are arranged. Is there room to move? Can the instructor see everyone? Does the room feel calm enough that you could relax into the work?

### 3. Tell the instructors about your body

If you have a bad back, sore knees, a past surgery, or are pregnant, say so. Good instructors will talk through modifications. For medical concerns, check with your healthcare provider before starting a new exercise program.

### 4. Dress for movement

If you get to try the equipment, wear fitted, comfortable clothing so the instructor can see your alignment. Grip socks are common in Pilates studios, but ask the studio what they require rather than assuming.

## Making the most of your first month

Once you start, a few habits help whether you're at JETSET or anywhere else.

- **Aim for consistency over intensity.** Two sessions a week that you actually attend beat an ambitious plan you drop.
- **Keep your yoga or walking routine.** Pilates fits well as one part of a varied week.
- **Focus on breath.** Coordinating movement with your exhale is a skill that carries over from yoga and meditation.
- **Go slow on springs.** Heavier isn't automatically better. Control is the point.
