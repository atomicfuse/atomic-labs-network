## How to Choose Between Mat Classes and Private Sessions

Since both formats are on the menu, here's a practical way to decide.

### Start with a group mat class if you...

- Are brand new to Pilates or yoga and want to learn the basics.
- Like the energy of practicing with others.
- Want a lower-cost way to test whether you enjoy it.

### Consider a private apparatus session if you...

- Want individualized attention to your form.
- Are managing a specific goal or limitation (check with your doctor first if you have an injury).
- Feel nervous about keeping up in a group.

Many people sensibly begin with one or two private sessions to learn fundamentals, then move to group classes. That's general advice, not a Tandem policy.

## Making Your First Class Count

Whether you go to Tandem or another local studio, these habits help:

1. **Arrive 10 minutes early.** Tell the instructor you're new and mention any injuries.
2. **Wear fitted, comfortable clothing.** It lets the instructor see your alignment.
3. **Focus on breath and control, not range of motion.** Pilates and yoga both reward steady, mindful movement over big stretches.
4. **Modify freely.** Needing a variation isn't a failure; it's how the practice works.
5. **Try three classes before deciding.** The first one often feels awkward for everyone.
