## Discovering the Unseen Work Behind Showgirl Glamour

Have you ever watched a Las Vegas show and found yourself mesmerized by the performers? While the glamour and glitz of the stage captivate audiences, the fitness routines that keep these showgirls stage-ready are often overshadowed. Kelsie McNutt, a dancer in the famed ‘FANTASY’ show at the Luxor Hotel & Casino, reveals how her Pilates-centric approach is essential for maintaining her energy and enthusiasm for six high-energy performances a week.

## Kelsie McNutt's Essential Fitness Routine

Kelsie’s routine emphasizes the importance of **Pilates** for building strength, enhancing flexibility, and preventing injuries. Here’s how you can incorporate her practical strategies into your own wellness plan.

### 1. Prioritize Your Pilates Practice

Pilates isn’t just for dancers or gym-goers; it’s accessible for everyone. Kelsie suggests starting with these foundational moves:

- **The Hundred**: This classic Pilates move targets your core while warming up your body. Lie on your back, lift your legs to a tabletop position, and pump your arms vigorously.
- **Roll-Up**: Great for spinal articulation and flexibility. Start lying down, arms overhead, and roll up to a sitting position, reaching toward your toes.
- **Side-Lying Leg Series**: Engage your hip muscles to support your pelvic floor. Lying on your side, lift your top leg toward the ceiling and lower it in controlled motions.

These moves are perfect for beginners and help strengthen the core, spine, and pelvic region, key areas that Kelsie relies on during her performances.

### 2. Strength and Stability through Core Work

A strong core is fundamental for any physical activity, especially in dance. Incorporate these exercises into your routine:

- **Plank Variations**: Start with a basic plank to build overall core strength. Challenge yourself with side planks or forearm planks as you progress.
- **Bridge**: This move strengthens the glutes and lower back while engaging the core. Lie on your back with knees bent and lift your hips toward the ceiling.

### 3. Daily Movement Goals

Kelsie emphasizes that staying active throughout the day is crucial. Aim for **10,000 steps** daily—this isn’t just a number but rather a simple way to ensure you’re getting ample movement. Instead of a gym session, consider these alternatives:

- Take a brisk walk during lunch. 
- Use the stairs instead of the elevator. 
- Stand and stretch while working or watching TV.

### 4. Hydration and Recovery

Hydration plays a key role in performance and recovery. Kelsie’s rule is simple: drink water consistently throughout the day. Utilize these tips for maintaining hydration:

- Keep a water bottle handy to remind you to sip regularly.
- Infuse your water with fruits or herbs for added flavor and motivation.

Additionally, prioritize rest and recovery. This could mean lower-intensity workouts on certain days or incorporating yoga to stretch those dancer muscles.
