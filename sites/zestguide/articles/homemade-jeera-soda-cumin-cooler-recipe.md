Cumin-flavored fizzy drinks are having a moment in India. Per the BBC, brands like Lahori Zeera, Campa Jeera and Jeeru are taking market share from Coca-Cola and Pepsi, as shoppers lean toward familiar, homegrown flavors at low prices. You don't need a bottle to see what the fuss is about. With a jar of cumin and a lemon, you can make a very good jeera soda in about five minutes.

The measurements below are my own working recipe for a home version, not the formula of any commercial brand. The brands' actual recipes aren't covered here.

## What a Jeera Soda Tastes Like

Think of a salty-sour lemonade with a toasted, earthy backbone. Cumin gives it warmth, lemon keeps it bright, and a pinch of salt makes the whole thing taste more refreshing rather than sweet. If you've had a savory lime soda, you're halfway there.

## The Quick Version: One Glass in 5 Minutes

**Makes:** 1 tall glass  
**Time:** 5 minutes (plus a minute to cool the cumin)

### Ingredients

- 1/2 teaspoon whole cumin seeds
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice (about half a lemon)
- 2 teaspoons sugar, or to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon black salt (kala namak), or regular salt if that's what you have
- 1 tablespoon water
- 3/4 to 1 cup cold sparkling water or club soda
- Ice
- Optional: a few mint leaves, a pinch of chaat masala

### Steps

1. **Toast the cumin.** Set a small dry pan over medium heat. Add the seeds and stir for 45 to 60 seconds, until fragrant and a shade darker. Don't walk away; cumin goes bitter fast.
2. **Grind it.** Tip the seeds onto a plate for a minute, then crush them with a mortar and pestle, or the back of a spoon in a small bowl, until mostly powdery.
3. **Make the base.** In your glass, stir the ground cumin, lemon juice, sugar, salt and 1 tablespoon water until the sugar dissolves.
4. **Add ice and fizz.** Fill the glass with ice, then pour the sparkling water slowly down the side to keep the bubbles.
5. **Stir once, gently, and taste.** Adjust with more lemon, salt or sugar.

## Make a Batch Syrup for the Week

If you'll drink this more than once, make a concentrate so each glass takes 30 seconds.

**Makes:** about 1 cup syrup, enough for 8 glasses  
**Time:** 10 minutes

- 2 tablespoons whole cumin seeds, toasted and ground
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon black salt

Simmer the sugar and water over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes until clear. Take it off the heat and stir in the cumin, lemon juice and salt. Let it steep for 10 minutes, then store it in a jar in the fridge for up to 5 days. For each drink, use 2 tablespoons of syrup and top with about 3/4 cup sparkling water. Shake or stir the jar before pouring, since the cumin settles.

## Four Easy Tweaks

- **More savory:** Add the chaat masala and an extra pinch of black salt. It tastes closer to a street-stall drink.
- **Fresher:** Muddle 5 or 6 mint leaves into the base before adding ice.
- **Spicier:** Add a thin slice of green chili or a pinch of black pepper.
- **Less sweet:** Cut the sugar to 1 teaspoon. The salt and lemon carry it.

## Troubleshooting

**It tastes flat.** Add salt, a few grains at a time. Salt is what makes this drink pop.

**It's gritty.** Cumin doesn't dissolve. Grind it finer, or strain the syrup through a fine sieve if you prefer a smoother glass.

**It tastes bitter.** The cumin was over-toasted. Pull it from the heat as soon as it smells nutty and a bit darker.

**It went flat quickly.** Use very cold sparkling water, pour gently, and don't stir more than once.

## Serving Ideas

Jeera soda works best alongside salty, fried or spiced food: samosas, pakoras, a cheese toastie, or a simple plate of rice and dal. The acidity and cumin cut through richness the way a squeeze of lime does.

Toast a spoonful of cumin tonight, squeeze a lemon, and pour yourself a glass. Salt is the part most people under-do, so add it first.
