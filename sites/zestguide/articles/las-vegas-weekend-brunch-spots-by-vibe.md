Las Vegas brunch has moved well past eggs and a mimosa. According to Las Vegas Weekly, which rounded up four weekend options (a sponsored roundup), the city's brunches now come with poolside Pilates, live DJs, arcade-style games and carving-station prime rib. The trick is picking the one that fits the morning you actually want.

This guide matches each of the four to a brunch personality, so you can book once and skip the scrolling. One note up front: the roundup as reported doesn't include prices, hours or reservation details, so check each venue directly before you plan around a budget or a start time.

## If You Want a Wellness Reset: Resorts World

Resorts World pairs movement with food. Per Las Vegas Weekly, Poolside Pulse Pilates at AYU Dayclub is followed by brunch at FUHU. That makes it the pick for anyone who wants to feel like they earned the meal, or who wants a calmer start to a weekend that will get louder later.

The resort also offers a Latin-inspired brunch at ¡VIVA!, a good alternative if you'd rather skip the workout and focus on the flavors.

**Best for:** a wellness-minded group, or a couple who want something active before eating.

**Plan ahead:** confirm the Pilates class schedule and whether brunch is booked separately or bundled. The roundup doesn't say.

## If You Want a Party Brunch: STK Las Vegas

STK's All You Can Brunch offers chef-driven bites, cocktails and live DJs, per the roundup. Think of it as the nightlife version of brunch: the music is part of the meal, so this isn't the spot for a quiet catch-up conversation.

**Best for:** birthdays, friend trips, and anyone who wants brunch to feel like an event.

**Plan ahead:** the "all you can" format usually rewards a longer, slower sitting, so give yourself time rather than squeezing it in before a tight afternoon schedule. Ask about time limits when you book.

## If You Want Drinks and Games: Play Playground Las Vegas

Play Playground Las Vegas combines a brunch menu with bottomless mimosas and Bloody Marys and more than 20 interactive games, according to Las Vegas Weekly. The games give the table something to do besides wait for the next round, which helps groups with mixed energy levels.

**Best for:** groups who like a competitive streak with their cocktails.

**Plan ahead:** ask whether the bottomless drinks have a time window or a food-order requirement. The roundup doesn't specify, and policies like that vary from place to place.

## If You Want a Classic Feast: South Point Garden Buffet

South Point's Garden Buffet serves a prime rib and Champagne brunch, with global buffet stations, per the roundup. This is the most traditional of the four: you walk the stations, build several plates, and settle in.

**Best for:** big appetites, groups where everyone wants something different, and anyone who values choice over spectacle.

**Plan ahead:** buffets reward a strategy. Do one lap before you load a plate, start with the prime rib while it's freshly carved, and save room for the stations you actually came for.

## How to Choose in 30 Seconds

- **Want to move first and eat after?** Resorts World.
- **Want music and a lively room?** STK.
- **Want games and a bottomless drink option?** Play Playground.
- **Want variety and prime rib?** South Point Garden Buffet.

A few practical habits apply to any of them. Book ahead for weekends, since brunch is a popular slot. Eat something before a bottomless-drink brunch if you're starting early. And decide what comes after: a pool afternoon, a show, or a nap.

## Bottomless vs. Buffet: A Quick Way to Think About Value

The roundup doesn't list prices, so it can't tell you which is the better deal. You can still judge by how you eat and drink. If you'll have two or three cocktails, a bottomless format may pay off. If you'd rather sample many dishes, the buffet's breadth is the draw. If you rarely drink at brunch, a food-focused format like the buffet or the Latin-inspired brunch will likely feel like better value.

## The Takeaway

Pick the vibe first, then the venue. Decide whether your ideal Saturday morning is calm, loud, playful or indulgent, call ahead for the details the roundup leaves out, and book the one that matches.
